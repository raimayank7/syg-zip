Where Hawaii County is

Its codes spread over about 81 miles from north to south and 69 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 96719 (Hawi) and 96772 (Naalehu), about 82 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Maui County (114 miles), Honolulu County (184 miles) and Kauai County (307 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Hawaii County starts with 967. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 967 is shared with 3 other counties, Honolulu County, Kauai County and Maui County, and has 87 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 28 of the 32 codes. Together they come to about 2,592 square miles of land (6,713 sq km). The largest is 96743 (Kamuela) with 292 square miles, and the smallest is 96780 (Papaaloa) with 1.8. Water adds about 241 square miles, roughly 9% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hawaii County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 96718 (Hawaii National Park), 96721 (Hilo), 96739 (Keauhou) and 96745 (Kailua Kona). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Hawaii County ranks 2nd among the 4 counties and county-level areas of Hawaii (equal counts share a rank), with 32 of the 137 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hawaii County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters HI and the ZIP code:

  • Hilo, HI 96720 (one of 2 codes for Hilo; the street decides which)
  • Kailua Kona, HI 96740 (one of 2 codes for Kailua Kona; the street decides which)
  • Captain Cook, HI 96704

Questions about Hawaii County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Hilo, HI?

Hilo has 2 ZIP codes in Hawaii County: 96720, 96721. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Hawaii County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 32 start with 967.

Which ZIP code in Hawaii County covers the most land?

96743 (Kamuela), at about 292 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
