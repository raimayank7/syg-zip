Where Maui County is

From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 31 miles, and from east to west about 76 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 96713 (Hana) and 96770 (Maunaloa), about 81 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Hawaii County (114 miles), Kauai County (201 miles) and Honolulu County (223 miles).

The first three digits

All 19 codes here begin with the same three digits, 967. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 967 is shared with 3 other counties, Hawaii County, Honolulu County and Kauai County, and has 87 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 15 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 1,104 square miles of land (2,858 sq km). The largest is 96790 (Kula) with 203 square miles, and the smallest is 96779 (Paia) with 11.6. Water adds about 121 square miles, roughly 10% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Maui County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 96733 (Kahului), 96767 (Lahaina), 96784 (Puunene) and 96788 (Pukalani). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Maui County ranks 3rd among the 4 counties and county-level areas of Hawaii (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 137 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Maui County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters HI and the ZIP code:

  • Kahului, HI 96732 (one of 2 codes for Kahului; the street decides which)
  • Lahaina, HI 96761 (one of 2 codes for Lahaina; the street decides which)
  • Haiku, HI 96708

Questions about Maui County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Kahului, HI?

Kahului has 2 ZIP codes in Maui County: 96732, 96733. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Maui County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 19 start with 967.

Which ZIP code in Maui County covers the most land?

96790 (Kula), at about 203 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
