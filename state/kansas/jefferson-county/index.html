Where Jefferson County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Jefferson County is in the north-east of Kansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 22 miles, and from east to west about 18 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 66060 (Nortonville) and 66429 (Grantville), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Douglas County (21 miles), Leavenworth County (21 miles), Atchison County (22 miles) and Shawnee County (22 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Jefferson County begin with 660 (7 codes), 664 (1 code) and 665 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 660 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Anderson County, Atchison County and Doniphan County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 560 square miles of land (1,449 sq km). The largest is 66088 (Valley Falls) with 120 square miles, and the smallest is 66429 (Grantville) with 12.8. The middle-sized code here covers 65.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. Water adds about 23.2 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jefferson County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Jefferson County ranks 14th among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jefferson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:

Questions about Jefferson County ZIP codes

Do all ZIP codes in Jefferson County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Jefferson County covers the most land?
