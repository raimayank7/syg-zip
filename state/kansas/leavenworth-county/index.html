Where Leavenworth County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Leavenworth County is in the north-east of Kansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 24 miles, and from east to west about 11 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 66027 (Fort Leavenworth) and 66052 (Linwood), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Platte County, Missouri (16 miles), Wyandotte County (17 miles), Jefferson County (21 miles) and Douglas County (23 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 660. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 660 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Anderson County, Atchison County and Doniphan County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 383 square miles of land (992 sq km). The largest is 66048 (Leavenworth) with 135 square miles, and the smallest is 66043 (Lansing) with 7.2. The middle-sized code here covers 40.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Leavenworth County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Leavenworth County ranks 33rd among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Leavenworth County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:
