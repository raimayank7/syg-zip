Where Pottawatomie County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Pottawatomie County is in the north-east of Kansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 29 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 66520 (Olsburg) and 66536 (Saint Marys), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Riley County (23 miles), Wabaunsee County (27 miles), Jackson County (29 miles) and Geary County (35 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Pottawatomie County begin with 665 (6 codes) and 664 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 665 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Brown County, Geary County and Jackson County, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 887 square miles of land (2,299 sq km). The largest is 66549 (Westmoreland) with 182 square miles, and the smallest is 66535 (Saint George) with 41.7. The middle-sized code here covers 69.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pottawatomie County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 66426 (Fostoria). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Pottawatomie County ranks 12th among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 748 codes in the state list.
