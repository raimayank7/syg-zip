Where Atchison County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Atchison County fall in the north-east of Kansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 7.8 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 66002 (Atchison) and 66058 (Muscotah), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Doniphan County (20 miles), Brown County (22 miles), Jefferson County (22 miles) and Jackson County (24 miles).

The first three digits

All 5 codes here begin with the same three digits, 660. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 660 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Anderson County, Doniphan County and Douglas County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 380 square miles of land (984 sq km). The largest is 66002 (Atchison) with 189 square miles, and the smallest is 66016 (Cummings) with 31.3. The middle-sized code here covers 53.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Atchison County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Atchison County ranks 57th among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Atchison County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:
