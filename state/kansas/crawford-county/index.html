Where Crawford County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Crawford County is in the south-east of Kansas. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 23 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 66746 (Hepler) and 66760 (Opolis), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cherokee County (24 miles), Barton County, Missouri (25 miles), Bourbon County (26 miles) and Neosho County (28 miles).

The first three digits

All 13 codes here begin with the same three digits, 667. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 667 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Allen County, Bourbon County and Cherokee County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 748 square miles of land (1,938 sq km). The largest is 66743 (Girard) with 144 square miles, and the smallest is 66760 (Opolis) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 35.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Crawford County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Crawford County ranks 7th among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Crawford County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:

  • Arcadia, KS 66711
  • Arma, KS 66712
  • Cherokee, KS 66724

Arcadia is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 13 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
