Where Marshall County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Marshall County is in the north-east of Kansas. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 66541 (Summerfield) and 66548 (Waterville), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Pawnee County, Nebraska (28 miles), Nemaha County (28 miles), Gage County, Nebraska (30 miles) and Washington County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Marshall County begin with 664 (6 codes) and 665 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 664 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Brown County, Geary County and Jackson County, and has 37 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,001 square miles of land (2,592 sq km). The largest is 66427 (Frankfort) with 211 square miles, and the smallest is 66438 (Home) with 35.4. The middle-sized code here covers 80.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Marshall County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Marshall County ranks 10th among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Marshall County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:

  • Axtell, KS 66403
  • Beattie, KS 66406
  • Blue Rapids, KS 66411

Axtell is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
