Where Seward County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Seward County is in the south-west of Kansas. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 13 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 67859 (Kismet) and 67905 (Liberal), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Beaver County, Oklahoma (26 miles), Stevens County (26 miles), Haskell County (27 miles) and Meade County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Seward County begin with 679 (2 codes) and 678 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 679 is shared with 2 other counties, Morton County and Stevens County, and has 7 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 2 of the 3 codes. Together they come to about 498 square miles of land (1,289 sq km). The largest is 67901 (Liberal) with 311 square miles, and the smallest is 67859 (Kismet) with 187. The middle-sized code here covers 249 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Seward County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 67905 (Liberal). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Seward County ranks 81st among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 3 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Seward County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:

  • Liberal, KS 67901 (one of 2 codes for Liberal; the street decides which)
  • Kismet, KS 67859

Questions about Seward County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Liberal, KS?

Liberal has 2 ZIP codes in Seward County: 67901, 67905. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Seward County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 679, on 2 of the 3 codes.

Which ZIP code in Seward County covers the most land?

67901 (Liberal), at about 311 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
