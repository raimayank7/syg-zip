Where Clay County is

The ZIP codes of Clay County are found in the north-east of Kansas. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 13 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 67447 (Green) and 67458 (Longford), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Riley County (23 miles), Geary County (24 miles), Cloud County (28 miles) and Washington County (31 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Clay County starts with 674. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 674 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Cloud County, Dickinson County and Ellsworth County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 649 square miles of land (1,681 sq km). The largest is 67432 (Clay Center) with 361 square miles, and the smallest is 67468 (Morganville) with 40.9. The middle-sized code here covers 77.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clay County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Clay County ranks 57th among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clay County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:

  • Clay Center, KS 67432
  • Green, KS 67447
  • Longford, KS 67458
