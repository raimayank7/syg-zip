Where Harvey County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Harvey County fall in the south of Kansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 67020 (Burrton) and 67151 (Walton), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Sedgwick County (24 miles), McPherson County (27 miles), Marion County (29 miles) and Butler County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Harvey County begin with 671 (4 codes) and 670 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 671 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Barber County, Butler County and Comanche County, and has 42 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 617 square miles of land (1,597 sq km). The largest is 67114 (Newton) with 237 square miles, and the smallest is 67117 (North Newton) with 1.3. The middle-sized code here covers 84.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Harvey County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Harvey County ranks 33rd among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Harvey County
