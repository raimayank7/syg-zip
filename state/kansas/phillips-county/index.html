Where Phillips County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Phillips County fall in the north-west of Kansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 24 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 67644 (Kirwin) and 67647 (Long Island), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Harlan County, Nebraska (27 miles), Smith County (28 miles), Rooks County (30 miles) and Norton County (31 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Phillips County starts with 676. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 676 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Decatur County, Ellis County and Graham County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 955 square miles of land (2,474 sq km). The largest is 67661 (Phillipsburg) with 372 square miles, and the smallest is 67621 (Agra) with 63.9. The middle-sized code here covers 91.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Phillips County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Phillips County ranks 33rd among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Phillips County
