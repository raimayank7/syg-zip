Where McPherson County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, McPherson County sits near the middle of Kansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 26 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 67107 (Moundridge) and 67464 (Marquette), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Saline County (25 miles), Harvey County (27 miles), Marion County (29 miles) and Rice County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of McPherson County begin with 674 (7 codes), 671 (1 code) and 675 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 674 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Clay County, Cloud County and Dickinson County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,016 square miles of land (2,631 sq km). The largest is 67460 (Mcpherson) with 227 square miles, and the smallest is 67476 (Roxbury) with 3.0. The middle-sized code here covers 115 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of McPherson County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, McPherson County ranks 14th among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in McPherson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:

  • Canton, KS 67428
  • Galva, KS 67443
  • Inman, KS 67546

Canton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 16 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
