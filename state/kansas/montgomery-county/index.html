Where Montgomery County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Montgomery County is in the south-east of Kansas. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 67333 (Caney) and 67335 (Cherryvale), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Nowata County, Oklahoma (21 miles), Chautauqua County (25 miles), Labette County (27 miles) and Wilson County (28 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 673. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 673 is shared with 3 other counties, Chautauqua County, Elk County and Labette County, and has 27 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 818 square miles of land (2,118 sq km). The largest is 67301 (Independence) with 215 square miles, and the smallest is 67364 (Tyro) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 58.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Montgomery County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Montgomery County ranks 12th among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Montgomery County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:
