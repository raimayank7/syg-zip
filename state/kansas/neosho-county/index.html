Where Neosho County is

The ZIP codes of Neosho County are found in the south-east of Kansas. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 66775 (Stark) and 66776 (Thayer), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Allen County (21 miles), Wilson County (23 miles), Labette County (27 miles) and Crawford County (28 miles).

The first three digits

All 6 codes here begin with the same three digits, 667. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 667 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Allen County, Bourbon County and Cherokee County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 568 square miles of land (1,471 sq km). The largest is 66720 (Chanute) with 199 square miles, and the smallest is 66775 (Stark) with 20.7. The middle-sized code here covers 86.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Neosho County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Neosho County ranks 49th among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Neosho County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:
