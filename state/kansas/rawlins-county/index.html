Where Rawlins County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Rawlins County fall in the north-west of Kansas. Its codes spread over about 7.7 miles from north to south and 27 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 67739 (Herndon) and 67745 (Mc Donald), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Hitchcock County, Nebraska (26 miles), Thomas County (30 miles), Decatur County (37 miles) and Cheyenne County (37 miles).

The first three digits

All 4 codes here begin with the same three digits, 677. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 677 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Cheyenne County, Decatur County and Gove County, and has 28 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 905 square miles of land (2,345 sq km). The largest is 67730 (Atwood) with 383 square miles, and the smallest is 67739 (Herndon) with 127. The middle-sized code here covers 198 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Rawlins County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Rawlins County ranks 71st among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Rawlins County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:

  • Atwood, KS 67730
  • Herndon, KS 67739
  • Ludell, KS 67744

Atwood is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
