Where Douglas County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Douglas County is in the north-east of Kansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 18 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 66006 (Baldwin City) and 66050 (Lecompton), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Jefferson County (21 miles), Leavenworth County (23 miles), Shawnee County (26 miles) and Johnson County (27 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Douglas County starts with 660. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 660 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Anderson County, Atchison County and Doniphan County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 433 square miles of land (1,122 sq km). The largest is 66006 (Baldwin City) with 109 square miles, and the smallest is 66045 (Lawrence) with 1.1. The middle-sized code here covers 51.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. Water adds about 18.5 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Douglas County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Douglas County ranks 23rd among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Douglas County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:

  • Lawrence, KS 66044 (one of 5 codes for Lawrence; the street decides which)
  • Baldwin City, KS 66006
  • Eudora, KS 66025

Lawrence is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
