Where Wyandotte County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Wyandotte County fall in the north-east of Kansas. Its codes spread over about 5.3 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 66012 (Bonner Springs) and 66115 (Kansas City), about 17 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Johnson County (11 miles), Jackson County, Missouri (13 miles), Platte County, Missouri (16 miles) and Leavenworth County (17 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Wyandotte County begin with 661 (16 codes) and 660 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 661, so those three digits alone point to Wyandotte County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 177 square miles of land (458 sq km). The largest is 66109 (Kansas City) with 45.9 square miles, and the smallest is 66160 (Kansas City) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 5.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wyandotte County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 66110 (Kansas City), 66113 (Edwardsville), 66117 (Kansas City) and 66119 (Kansas City). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Wyandotte County ranks 4th among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Wyandotte County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:

  • Kansas City, KS 66101 (one of 15 codes for Kansas City; the street decides which)
  • Bonner Springs, KS 66012
  • Edwardsville, KS 66113

Do all ZIP codes in Wyandotte County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Wyandotte County covers the most land?
