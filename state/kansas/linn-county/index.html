Where Linn County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Linn County is in the east of Kansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 67 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 66040 (Lacygne) and 66767 (Prescott), about 72 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Miami County (20 miles), Anderson County (21 miles), Franklin County (25 miles) and Bates County, Missouri (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Linn County begin with 660 (6 codes) and 667 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 660 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Anderson County, Atchison County and Doniphan County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 608 square miles of land (1,574 sq km). The largest is 66040 (Lacygne) with 134 square miles, and the smallest is 66767 (Prescott) with 46.0. The middle-sized code here covers 88.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Linn County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Linn County ranks 33rd among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Linn County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:

  • Blue Mound, KS 66010
  • Centerville, KS 66014
  • Lacygne, KS 66040
