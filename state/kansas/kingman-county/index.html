Where Kingman County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Kingman County fall in the south of Kansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 14 miles, and from east to west about 30 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 67112 (Nashville) and 67118 (Norwich), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Harper County (25 miles), Barber County (31 miles), Reno County (32 miles) and Pratt County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Kingman County begin with 671 (5 codes) and 670 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 671 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Barber County, Butler County and Comanche County, and has 42 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 923 square miles of land (2,392 sq km). The largest is 67068 (Kingman) with 282 square miles, and the smallest is 67111 (Murdock) with 62.4. The middle-sized code here covers 87.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Kingman County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Kingman County ranks 33rd among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Kingman County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:

  • Cunningham, KS 67035
  • Kingman, KS 67068
  • Murdock, KS 67111

Cunningham is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
