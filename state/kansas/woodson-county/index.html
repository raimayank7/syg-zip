Where Woodson County is

The ZIP codes of Woodson County are found in the south-east of Kansas. Its codes spread over about 12 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 66758 (Neosho Falls) and 66777 (Toronto), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Allen County (22 miles), Wilson County (23 miles), Coffey County (24 miles) and Greenwood County (25 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Woodson County starts with 667. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 667 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Allen County, Bourbon County and Cherokee County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 508 square miles of land (1,317 sq km). The largest is 66783 (Yates Center) with 285 square miles, and the smallest is 66761 (Piqua) with 43.4. The middle-sized code here covers 90.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Woodson County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Woodson County ranks 71st among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Woodson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:
