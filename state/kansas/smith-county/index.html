Where Smith County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Smith County is in the north of Kansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 11 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 66951 (Kensington) and 66952 (Lebanon), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Osborne County (22 miles), Phillips County (28 miles), Franklin County, Nebraska (32 miles) and Webster County, Nebraska (35 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Smith County begin with 669 (4 codes) and 676 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 669 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Cloud County, Jewell County and Republic County, and has 34 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 853 square miles of land (2,208 sq km). The largest is 66952 (Lebanon) with 244 square miles, and the smallest is 67628 (Cedar) with 43.9. The middle-sized code here covers 129 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Smith County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Smith County ranks 49th among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Smith County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:

  • Athol, KS 66932
  • Cedar, KS 67628
  • Gaylord, KS 67638

Athol is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
