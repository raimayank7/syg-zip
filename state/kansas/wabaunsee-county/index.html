Where Wabaunsee County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Wabaunsee County fall in the north-east of Kansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 28 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 66431 (Harveyville) and 66834 (Alta Vista), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Shawnee County (26 miles), Pottawatomie County (27 miles), Morris County (32 miles) and Osage County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Wabaunsee County begin with 664 (3 codes), 665 (3 codes) and 668 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 664 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Brown County, Geary County and Jackson County, and has 37 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 783 square miles of land (2,029 sq km). The largest is 66401 (Alma) with 241 square miles, and the smallest is 66501 (Mc Farland) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 92.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wabaunsee County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Wabaunsee County ranks 33rd among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Wabaunsee County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:

  • Alma, KS 66401
  • Alta Vista, KS 66834
  • Eskridge, KS 66423

Alma is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 10 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
