Where Labette County is

The ZIP codes of Labette County are found in the south-east of Kansas. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 67336 (Chetopa) and 67341 (Dennis), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Montgomery County (27 miles), Cherokee County (27 miles), Neosho County (27 miles) and Nowata County, Oklahoma (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 673. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 673 is shared with 3 other counties, Chautauqua County, Elk County and Montgomery County, and has 27 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 642 square miles of land (1,663 sq km). The largest is 67357 (Parsons) with 197 square miles, and the smallest is 67341 (Dennis) with 20.9. The middle-sized code here covers 64.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Labette County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Labette County ranks 23rd among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Labette County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:

  • Altamont, KS 67330
  • Bartlett, KS 67332
  • Chetopa, KS 67336

Altamont is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
