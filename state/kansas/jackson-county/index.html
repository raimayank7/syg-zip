Where Jackson County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Jackson County is in the north-east of Kansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 24 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 66418 (Delia) and 66552 (Whiting), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Atchison County (24 miles), Jefferson County (24 miles), Brown County (26 miles) and Shawnee County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Jackson County begin with 664 (5 codes) and 665 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 664 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Brown County, Geary County and Jefferson County, and has 37 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 670 square miles of land (1,737 sq km). The largest is 66436 (Holton) with 184 square miles, and the smallest is 66419 (Denison) with 20.1. The middle-sized code here covers 48.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jackson County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Jackson County ranks 14th among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jackson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:

  • Circleville, KS 66416
  • Delia, KS 66418
  • Denison, KS 66419

Circleville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
