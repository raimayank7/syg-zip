Where Shawnee County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Shawnee County is in the north-east of Kansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 66409 (Berryton) and 66533 (Rossville), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Jefferson County (22 miles), Osage County (24 miles), Douglas County (26 miles) and Wabaunsee County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Shawnee County begin with 666 (31 codes), 665 (4 codes) and 664 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 666, so those three digits alone point to Shawnee County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 24 of the 38 codes. Together they come to about 576 square miles of land (1,491 sq km). The largest is 66618 (Topeka) with 72.8 square miles, and the smallest is 66622 (Topeka) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 11.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Shawnee County; a code can reach across its border. 14 codes have no mapped area: 66420 (Dover), 66601 (Topeka), 66620 (Topeka), 66624 (Topeka), 66625 (Topeka) and 9 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Shawnee County ranks 3rd among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 38 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Shawnee County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:

  • Topeka, KS 66601 (one of 31 codes for Topeka; the street decides which)
  • Auburn, KS 66402
  • Berryton, KS 66409
