Where Doniphan County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Doniphan County is in the north-east of Kansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 66024 (Elwood) and 66094 (White Cloud), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Buchanan County, Missouri (17 miles), Holt County, Missouri (20 miles), Atchison County (20 miles) and Andrew County, Missouri (21 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Doniphan County starts with 660. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 660 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Anderson County, Atchison County and Douglas County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 365 square miles of land (945 sq km). The largest is 66087 (Troy) with 119 square miles, and the smallest is 66024 (Elwood) with 1.4. The middle-sized code here covers 44.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Doniphan County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Doniphan County ranks 33rd among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Doniphan County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:
