Where Reno County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Reno County fall in the south of Kansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 25 miles, and from east to west about 35 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 67522 (Buhler) and 67583 (Turon), about 39 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Rice County (29 miles), Kingman County (32 miles), Harvey County (35 miles) and Stafford County (37 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Reno County starts with 675. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 675 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Barton County, Edwards County and McPherson County, and has 55 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 1,206 square miles of land (3,123 sq km). The largest is 67583 (Turon) with 229 square miles, and the smallest is 67505 (South Hutchinson) with 2.8. The middle-sized code here covers 73.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Kansas is 80.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Reno County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 67504 (Hutchinson) and 67585 (Yoder). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Reno County ranks 5th among the 105 counties and county-level areas of Kansas (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 748 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Reno County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KS and the ZIP code:

  • Hutchinson, KS 67501 (one of 3 codes for Hutchinson; the street decides which)
  • Abbyville, KS 67510
  • Arlington, KS 67514

Questions about Reno County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Hutchinson, KS?

Hutchinson has 3 ZIP codes in Reno County: 67501, 67502, 67504. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Reno County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 15 start with 675.

Which ZIP code in Reno County covers the most land?

67583 (Turon), at about 229 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
