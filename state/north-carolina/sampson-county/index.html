Where Sampson County is

The ZIP codes of Sampson County are found in the east of North Carolina. Its codes spread over about 42 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 28366 (Newton Grove) and 28447 (Ivanhoe), about 43 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Duplin County (23 miles), Bladen County (28 miles), Cumberland County (29 miles) and Wayne County (35 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Sampson County begin with 283 (7 codes) and 284 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 283 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Bladen County, Cumberland County and Duplin County, and has 86 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 962 square miles of land (2,491 sq km). The largest is 28328 (Clinton) with 246 square miles, and the smallest is 28385 (Salemburg) with 39.9. The middle-sized code here covers 100 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sampson County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 28329 (Clinton). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Sampson County ranks 36th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Sampson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Clinton, NC 28328 (one of 2 codes for Clinton; the street decides which)
  • Autryville, NC 28318
  • Garland, NC 28441

Clinton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 25 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Sampson County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Clinton, NC?

Clinton has 2 ZIP codes in Sampson County: 28328, 28329. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Sampson County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 283, on 7 of the 10 codes.

Which ZIP code in Sampson County covers the most land?

28328 (Clinton), at about 246 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
