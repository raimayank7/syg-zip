Where Brunswick County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Brunswick County is in the south-east of North Carolina. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 36 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 28404 (Wilmington) and 28467 (Calabash), about 42 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are New Hanover County (26 miles), Columbus County (28 miles), Horry County, South Carolina (38 miles) and Pender County (41 miles).

The first three digits

All 14 codes here begin with the same three digits, 284. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 284 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Bladen County, Columbus County and Duplin County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 840 square miles of land (2,175 sq km). The largest is 28451 (Leland) with 172 square miles, and the smallest is 28452 (Longwood) with 5.0. The middle-sized code here covers 58.5 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. Water adds about 43.2 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Brunswick County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 28404 (Wilmington) and 28459 (Shallotte). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Brunswick County ranks 17th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Brunswick County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Shallotte, NC 28459 (one of 2 codes for Shallotte; the street decides which)
  • Ash, NC 28420
  • Bolivia, NC 28422

Wilmington shares a name with a place in another part of North Carolina; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
