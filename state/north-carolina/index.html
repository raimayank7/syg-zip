North Carolina ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 1,081 ZIP codes for North Carolina, about 2.6% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 11th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 100 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Mecklenburg County (83), Wake County (64), Guilford County (48), Forsyth County (38) and Buncombe County (25). At the other end, 4 have only one or two codes, for example Chowan County, Graham County and Hoke County.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. North Carolina uses 20 of these prefixes, from 270 to 289. The busiest are 278 (93 codes, most of them for Greenville), 286 (90 codes, most of them for Hickory), 287 (87 codes, most of them for Hendersonville) and 283 (86 codes, most of them for Fayetteville). The first digit 2 is shared with District of Columbia, Maryland, South Carolina, Virginia and West Virginia.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 853 of the 1,081 codes. The largest is 28719 (Cherokee, Swain County) at 400 square miles and the smallest is 28758 (Mountain Home) at 0.01; the middle value is 42.4 square miles. The other 228 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 28631 (Grassy Creek) and the southernmost 28468 (Sunset Beach); the one farthest east is 27982 (Waves) and farthest west 28903 (Culberson).

Place names and addresses in North Carolina

Of the 759 place names in the list, 694 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Charlotte (74), Raleigh (44), Greensboro (28) and Winston-Salem (25). 6 place names appear under more than one county, among them Charlotte, Clayton, Davidson and Rocky Mount: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters NC and the code, as in Charlotte, NC 28201. The name of the county is left out.
