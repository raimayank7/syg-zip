Where Davidson County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Davidson County is in the north of North Carolina. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 27239 (Denton) and 27373 (Wallburg), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Davie County (16 miles), Forsyth County (19 miles), Rowan County (20 miles) and Randolph County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Davidson County begin with 272 (6 codes) and 273 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 272 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Alamance County, Caswell County and Chatham County, and has 55 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 544 square miles of land (1,409 sq km). The largest is 27239 (Denton) with 156 square miles, and the smallest is 27351 (Southmont) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 88.5 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Davidson County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 27293 (Lexington), 27294 (Lexington), 27361 (Thomasville) and 27373 (Wallburg). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Davidson County ranks 30th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Davidson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Lexington, NC 27292 (one of 4 codes for Lexington; the street decides which)
  • Thomasville, NC 27360 (one of 2 codes for Thomasville; the street decides which)
  • Denton, NC 27239

Lexington is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 17 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
