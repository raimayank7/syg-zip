Where Carteret County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Carteret County fall in the south-east of North Carolina. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 46 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 28520 (Cedar Island) and 28582 (Stella), about 49 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Pamlico County (26 miles), Craven County (35 miles), Onslow County (44 miles) and Jones County (44 miles).

The first three digits

All 17 codes here begin with the same three digits, 285. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 285 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Craven County, Duplin County and Greene County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 468 square miles of land (1,213 sq km). The largest is 28570 (Newport) with 149 square miles, and the smallest is 28575 (Salter Path) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 8.2 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 242 square miles, roughly 34% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Carteret County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Carteret County ranks 9th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Carteret County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Atlantic, NC 28511
  • Atlantic Beach, NC 28512
  • Beaufort, NC 28516

Atlantic is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
