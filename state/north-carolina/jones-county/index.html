Where Jones County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Jones County is in the east of North Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 14 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 28555 (Maysville) and 28585 (Trenton), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Craven County (18 miles), Onslow County (21 miles), Lenoir County (23 miles) and Duplin County (34 miles).

The first three digits

All 4 codes here begin with the same three digits, 285. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 285 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Carteret County, Craven County and Duplin County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 3 of the 4 codes. Together they come to about 448 square miles of land (1,159 sq km). The largest is 28585 (Trenton) with 203 square miles, and the smallest is 28573 (Pollocksville) with 104. The middle-sized code here covers 141 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jones County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 28522 (Comfort). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Jones County ranks 82nd among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.
