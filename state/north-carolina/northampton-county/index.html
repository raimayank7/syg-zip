Where Northampton County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Northampton County fall in the north-east of North Carolina. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 37 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 27842 (Henrico) and 27897 (Woodland), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Halifax County (18 miles), Emporia (city), Virginia (19 miles), Greensville County, Virginia (19 miles) and Southampton County, Virginia (22 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Northampton County starts with 278. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 278 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Beaufort County, Bertie County and Edgecombe County, and has 93 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 538 square miles of land (1,394 sq km). The largest is 27845 (Jackson) with 97.8 square miles, and the smallest is 27877 (Severn) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 43.0 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Northampton County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 27867 (Potecasi). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Northampton County ranks 22nd among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Northampton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Conway, NC 27820
  • Garysburg, NC 27831
  • Gaston, NC 27832

Conway is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
