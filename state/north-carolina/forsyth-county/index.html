Where Forsyth County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Forsyth County fall in the north of North Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 27009 (Belews Creek) and 27102 (Winston-Salem), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Stokes County (19 miles), Davidson County (19 miles), Davie County (20 miles) and Guilford County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Forsyth County begin with 271 (25 codes), 270 (11 codes) and 272 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 271, so those three digits alone point to Forsyth County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 38 codes. Together they come to about 460 square miles of land (1,191 sq km). The largest is 27284 (Kernersville) with 78.8 square miles, and the smallest is 27110 (Winston-Salem) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 25.0 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Forsyth County; a code can reach across its border. 21 codes have no mapped area: 27010 (Bethania), 27094 (Rural Hall), 27098 (Rural Hall), 27099 (Rural Hall), 27102 (Winston-Salem) and 16 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Forsyth County ranks 4th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 38 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Forsyth County
