Where Gaston County is

The ZIP codes of Gaston County are found in the west of North Carolina. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 28 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 28021 (Cherryville) and 28035 (Davidson), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lincoln County (12 miles), Mecklenburg County (18 miles), York County, South Carolina (22 miles) and Cleveland County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Gaston County begin with 280 (14 codes) and 281 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 280 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Anson County, Cabarrus County and Cleveland County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 367 square miles of land (952 sq km). The largest is 28021 (Cherryville) with 58.8 square miles, and the smallest is 28101 (Mc Adenville) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 29.3 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Gaston County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 28035 (Davidson), 28053 (Gastonia) and 28055 (Gastonia). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Gaston County ranks 9th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Gaston County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Gastonia, NC 28052 (one of 5 codes for Gastonia; the street decides which)
  • Alexis, NC 28006
  • Belmont, NC 28012

Davidson shares a name with a place in another part of North Carolina; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Gaston County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Gastonia, NC?

Gastonia has 5 ZIP codes in Gaston County: 28052, 28053, 28054, 28055, 28056. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Gaston County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 280, on 14 of the 17 codes.

Which ZIP code in Gaston County covers the most land?

28021 (Cherryville), at about 58.8 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
