Where Caswell County is

The ZIP codes of Caswell County are found in the north of North Carolina. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 27311 (Pelham) and 27314 (Prospect Hill), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Danville (city), Virginia (13 miles), Person County (16 miles), Pittsylvania County, Virginia (26 miles) and Rockingham County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Caswell County begin with 273 (5 codes) and 272 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 273 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Alamance County, Chatham County and Davidson County, and has 42 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 315 square miles of land (817 sq km). The largest is 27379 (Yanceyville) with 88.7 square miles, and the smallest is 27314 (Prospect Hill) with 24.4. The middle-sized code here covers 49.1 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Caswell County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Caswell County ranks 61st among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.
