Where Edgecombe County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Edgecombe County fall in the north-east of North Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 18 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 27802 (Rocky Mount) and 27819 (Conetoe), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Nash County (17 miles), Wilson County (21 miles), Pitt County (24 miles) and Martin County (24 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 278. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 278 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Beaufort County, Bertie County and Greene County, and has 93 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 425 square miles of land (1,101 sq km). The largest is 27886 (Tarboro) with 191 square miles, and the smallest is 27881 (Speed) with 0.09. The middle-sized code here covers 48.1 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Edgecombe County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 27802 (Rocky Mount). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Edgecombe County ranks 52nd among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Edgecombe County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Rocky Mount, NC 27801 (one of 2 codes for Rocky Mount; the street decides which)
  • Battleboro, NC 27809
  • Conetoe, NC 27819

ZIP codes named Rocky Mount are also listed under Nash County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site.
