Where Cumberland County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Cumberland County sits near the middle of North Carolina. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 28342 (Falcon) and 28348 (Hope Mills), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hoke County (21 miles), Harnett County (21 miles), Sampson County (29 miles) and Lee County (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 21 codes here begin with the same three digits, 283. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 283 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Bladen County, Duplin County and Harnett County, and has 86 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 19 of the 21 codes. Together they come to about 678 square miles of land (1,757 sq km). The largest is 28312 (Fayetteville) with 164 square miles, and the smallest is 28331 (Cumberland) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 36.5 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cumberland County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 28302 (Fayetteville) and 28309 (Fayetteville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Cumberland County ranks 6th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 21 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cumberland County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Fayetteville, NC 28301 (one of 10 codes for Fayetteville; the street decides which)
  • Fort Bragg, NC 28307 (one of 2 codes for Fort Bragg; the street decides which)
  • Cumberland, NC 28331

Fayetteville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
