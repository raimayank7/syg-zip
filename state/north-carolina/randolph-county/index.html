Where Randolph County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Randolph County is in the north of North Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 24 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 27341 (Seagrove) and 27370 (Trinity), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Guilford County (21 miles), Chatham County (27 miles), Davidson County (27 miles) and Alamance County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Randolph County begin with 272 (6 codes) and 273 (6 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 272 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Alamance County, Caswell County and Chatham County, and has 55 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 724 square miles of land (1,874 sq km). The largest is 27205 (Asheboro) with 224 square miles, and the smallest is 27203 (Asheboro) with 22.5. The middle-sized code here covers 57.0 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Randolph County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 27204 (Asheboro) and 27230 (Cedar Falls). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Randolph County ranks 25th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Randolph County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Asheboro, NC 27203 (one of 3 codes for Asheboro; the street decides which)
  • Cedar Falls, NC 27230
  • Franklinville, NC 27248

Questions about Randolph County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Asheboro, NC?

Asheboro has 3 ZIP codes in Randolph County: 27203, 27204, 27205. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Randolph County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 272, on 6 of the 12 codes.

Which ZIP code in Randolph County covers the most land?

27205 (Asheboro), at about 224 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
