Where Cleveland County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Cleveland County is in the west of North Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 28020 (Casar) and 28073 (Grover), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cherokee County, South Carolina (18 miles), Rutherford County (20 miles), Lincoln County (22 miles) and Gaston County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Cleveland County begin with 280 (8 codes) and 281 (6 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 280 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Anson County, Cabarrus County and Gaston County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 513 square miles of land (1,328 sq km). The largest is 28150 (Shelby) with 109 square miles, and the smallest is 28136 (Polkville) with 0.08. The middle-sized code here covers 36.0 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cleveland County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 28038 (Earl) and 28151 (Shelby). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Cleveland County ranks 17th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cleveland County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Shelby, NC 28150 (one of 3 codes for Shelby; the street decides which)
  • Boiling Springs, NC 28017
  • Casar, NC 28020

Shelby is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Cleveland County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Shelby, NC?

Shelby has 3 ZIP codes in Cleveland County: 28150, 28151, 28152. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Cleveland County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 280, on 8 of the 14 codes.

Which ZIP code in Cleveland County covers the most land?

28150 (Shelby), at about 109 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
