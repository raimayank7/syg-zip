Where Rowan County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Rowan County is in the north of North Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 27013 (Cleveland) and 28071 (Gold Hill), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Cabarrus County (18 miles), Davie County (19 miles), Davidson County (20 miles) and Iredell County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Rowan County begin with 281 (7 codes), 280 (6 codes) and 270 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 281 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Anson County, Cabarrus County and Cleveland County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 494 square miles of land (1,279 sq km). The largest is 28146 (Salisbury) with 97.1 square miles, and the smallest is 28041 (Faith) with 0.05. The middle-sized code here covers 34.2 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Rowan County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 28145 (Salisbury). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Rowan County ranks 13th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Rowan County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Salisbury, NC 28144 (one of 4 codes for Salisbury; the street decides which)
  • China Grove, NC 28023
  • Cleveland, NC 27013

Salisbury is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Rowan County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Salisbury, NC?

Salisbury has 4 ZIP codes in Rowan County: 28144, 28145, 28146, 28147. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Rowan County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 281, on 7 of the 15 codes.

Which ZIP code in Rowan County covers the most land?

28146 (Salisbury), at about 97.1 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
