Where Lincoln County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Lincoln County fall in the west of North Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 6.8 miles, and from east to west about 26 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 28037 (Denver) and 28168 (Vale), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Gaston County (12 miles), Catawba County (13 miles), Cleveland County (22 miles) and Mecklenburg County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lincoln County begin with 280 (5 codes) and 281 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 280 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Anson County, Cabarrus County and Cleveland County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 308 square miles of land (798 sq km). The largest is 28092 (Lincolnton) with 120 square miles, and the smallest is 28033 (Crouse) with 16.1. The middle-sized code here covers 44.7 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. Water adds about 9.8 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lincoln County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 28093 (Lincolnton). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lincoln County ranks 68th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lincoln County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Lincolnton, NC 28092 (one of 2 codes for Lincolnton; the street decides which)
  • Crouse, NC 28033
  • Denver, NC 28037

Questions about Lincoln County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Lincolnton, NC?

Lincolnton has 2 ZIP codes in Lincoln County: 28092, 28093. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Lincoln County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 280, on 5 of the 6 codes.

Which ZIP code in Lincoln County covers the most land?

28092 (Lincolnton), at about 120 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
