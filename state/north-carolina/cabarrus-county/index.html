Where Cabarrus County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Cabarrus County fall close to the middle of North Carolina. Its codes spread over about 18 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 28081 (Kannapolis) and 28107 (Midland), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Stanly County (17 miles), Mecklenburg County (18 miles), Rowan County (18 miles) and Union County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Cabarrus County begin with 280 (7 codes) and 281 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 280 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Anson County, Cleveland County and Gaston County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 356 square miles of land (921 sq km). The largest is 28025 (Concord) with 108 square miles, and the smallest is 28083 (Kannapolis) with 20.0. The middle-sized code here covers 48.9 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cabarrus County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 28026 (Concord) and 28082 (Kannapolis). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Cabarrus County ranks 43rd among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cabarrus County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Concord, NC 28025 (one of 3 codes for Concord; the street decides which)
  • Kannapolis, NC 28081 (one of 3 codes for Kannapolis; the street decides which)
  • Harrisburg, NC 28075

Concord is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 12 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Cabarrus County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Concord, NC?

Concord has 3 ZIP codes in Cabarrus County: 28025, 28026, 28027. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Cabarrus County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 280, on 7 of the 9 codes.

Which ZIP code in Cabarrus County covers the most land?

28025 (Concord), at about 108 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
