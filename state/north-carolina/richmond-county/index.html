Where Richmond County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Richmond County sits near the middle of North Carolina. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 28345 (Hamlet) and 28367 (Norman), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Scotland County (18 miles), Anson County (21 miles), Moore County (23 miles) and Montgomery County (25 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 283. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 283 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Bladen County, Cumberland County and Duplin County, and has 86 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 483 square miles of land (1,250 sq km). The largest is 28379 (Rockingham) with 152 square miles, and the smallest is 28330 (Cordova) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 71.1 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Richmond County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 28380 (Rockingham). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Richmond County ranks 52nd among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Richmond County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Rockingham, NC 28379 (one of 2 codes for Rockingham; the street decides which)
  • Cordova, NC 28330
  • Ellerbe, NC 28338
