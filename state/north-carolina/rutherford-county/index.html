Where Rutherford County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Rutherford County fall in the west of North Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 28024 (Cliffside) and 28720 (Chimney Rock), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Polk County (19 miles), Cleveland County (20 miles), Cherokee County, South Carolina (25 miles) and McDowell County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Rutherford County begin with 280 (7 codes), 281 (3 codes) and 287 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 280 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Anson County, Cabarrus County and Cleveland County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 550 square miles of land (1,425 sq km). The largest is 28139 (Rutherfordton) with 182 square miles, and the smallest is 28019 (Caroleen) with 0.05. The middle-sized code here covers 27.1 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Rutherford County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Rutherford County ranks 25th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Rutherford County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

Questions about Rutherford County ZIP codes

Which ZIP code in Rutherford County covers the most land?
