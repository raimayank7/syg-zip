Where Pamlico County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Pamlico County fall in the east of North Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 28510 (Arapahoe) and 28552 (Lowland), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Craven County (21 miles), Beaufort County (24 miles), Carteret County (26 miles) and Hyde County (36 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Pamlico County starts with 285. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 285 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Carteret County, Craven County and Duplin County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 291 square miles of land (753 sq km). The largest is 28515 (Bayboro) with 76.8 square miles, and the smallest is 28509 (Alliance) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 22.2 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. Water adds about 117 square miles, roughly 29% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pamlico County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 28583 (Stonewall). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Pamlico County ranks 36th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pamlico County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Alliance, NC 28509
  • Arapahoe, NC 28510
  • Bayboro, NC 28515
