Where Lee County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Lee County sits near the middle of North Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 6.2 miles, and from east to west about 8.5 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 27331 (Sanford) and 27505 (Broadway), about 8.8 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Chatham County (17 miles), Harnett County (21 miles), Moore County (22 miles) and Cumberland County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lee County begin with 273 (3 codes), 272 (1 code), 275 (1 code) and 283 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 273 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Alamance County, Caswell County and Chatham County, and has 42 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 4 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 372 square miles of land (963 sq km). The largest is 27330 (Sanford) with 211 square miles, and the smallest is 28355 (Lemon Springs) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 80.5 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lee County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 27237 (Cumnock) and 27331 (Sanford). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lee County ranks 68th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lee County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Sanford, NC 27330 (one of 3 codes for Sanford; the street decides which)
  • Broadway, NC 27505
  • Cumnock, NC 27237

Sanford is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Lee County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Sanford, NC?

Sanford has 3 ZIP codes in Lee County: 27330, 27331, 27332. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Lee County start with the same digits?

No. They use 4 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 273, on 3 of the 6 codes.

Which ZIP code in Lee County covers the most land?

27330 (Sanford), at about 211 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
