Where Duplin County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Duplin County is in the east of North Carolina. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 28325 (Calypso) and 28521 (Chinquapin), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Sampson County (23 miles), Lenoir County (26 miles), Wayne County (28 miles) and Pender County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Duplin County begin with 283 (4 codes), 284 (4 codes) and 285 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 283 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Bladen County, Cumberland County and Harnett County, and has 86 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 830 square miles of land (2,150 sq km). The largest is 28466 (Wallace) with 179 square miles, and the smallest is 28325 (Calypso) with 0.9. The middle-sized code here covers 84.6 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Duplin County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Duplin County ranks 30th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.
