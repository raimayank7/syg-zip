Where Montgomery County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Montgomery County sits near the middle of North Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 12 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 27247 (Ether) and 27306 (Mount Gilead), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Moore County (22 miles), Stanly County (24 miles), Richmond County (25 miles) and Randolph County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Montgomery County begin with 272 (3 codes) and 273 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 272 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Alamance County, Caswell County and Chatham County, and has 55 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 524 square miles of land (1,357 sq km). The largest is 27306 (Mount Gilead) with 185 square miles, and the smallest is 27247 (Ether) with 2.1. The middle-sized code here covers 73.6 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Montgomery County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Montgomery County ranks 68th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Montgomery County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Biscoe, NC 27209
  • Candor, NC 27229
  • Ether, NC 27247

Questions about Montgomery County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Biscoe, NC?

27209 is the ZIP code listed for Biscoe in Montgomery County.

Do all ZIP codes in Montgomery County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 272, on 3 of the 6 codes.

Which ZIP code in Montgomery County covers the most land?
