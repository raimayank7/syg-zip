Where Transylvania County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Transylvania County is in the west of North Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 13 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 28766 (Penrose) and 28774 (Sapphire), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Henderson County (22 miles), Jackson County (23 miles), Haywood County (25 miles) and Pickens County, South Carolina (28 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Transylvania County starts with 287. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 287 is shared with 14 other counties, such as Buncombe County, Cherokee County and Graham County, and has 87 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 396 square miles of land (1,027 sq km). The largest is 28712 (Brevard) with 116 square miles, and the smallest is 28766 (Penrose) with 6.0. The middle-sized code here covers 48.6 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Transylvania County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Transylvania County ranks 52nd among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Transylvania County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:
