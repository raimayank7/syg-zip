Where Halifax County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Halifax County is in the north-east of North Carolina. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 29 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 27843 (Hobgood) and 27850 (Littleton), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Northampton County (18 miles), Edgecombe County (27 miles), Nash County (27 miles) and Emporia (city), Virginia (29 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 278. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 278 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Beaufort County, Bertie County and Edgecombe County, and has 93 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 839 square miles of land (2,172 sq km). The largest is 27850 (Littleton) with 191 square miles, and the smallest is 27890 (Weldon) with 9.2. The middle-sized code here covers 100 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Halifax County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 27887 (Tillery). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Halifax County ranks 43rd among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Halifax County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Enfield, NC 27823
  • Halifax, NC 27839
  • Hobgood, NC 27843

Enfield is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
