Where Craven County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Craven County is in the east of North Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 28 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 28526 (Dover) and 28532 (Havelock), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Jones County (18 miles), Pamlico County (21 miles), Beaufort County (27 miles) and Lenoir County (33 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Craven County starts with 285. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 285 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Carteret County, Duplin County and Greene County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 798 square miles of land (2,066 sq km). The largest is 28532 (Havelock) with 165 square miles, and the smallest is 28533 (Cherry Point) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 95.8 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 68.3 square miles, roughly 8% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Craven County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 28561 (New Bern), 28563 (New Bern) and 28564 (New Bern). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Craven County ranks 25th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Craven County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • New Bern, NC 28560 (one of 5 codes for New Bern; the street decides which)
  • Bridgeton, NC 28519
  • Cherry Point, NC 28533
