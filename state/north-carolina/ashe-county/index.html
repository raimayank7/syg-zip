Where Ashe County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Ashe County is in the north-west of North Carolina. Its codes spread over about 18 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 28615 (Creston) and 28644 (Laurel Springs), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Watauga County (19 miles), Grayson County, Virginia (21 miles), Johnson County, Tennessee (21 miles) and Alleghany County (21 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Ashe County starts with 286. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 286 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Alexander County, Alleghany County and Avery County, and has 90 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 484 square miles of land (1,253 sq km). The largest is 28644 (Laurel Springs) with 71.4 square miles, and the smallest is 28672 (Scottville) with 1.8. The middle-sized code here covers 36.1 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ashe County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Ashe County ranks 25th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Ashe County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Creston, NC 28615
  • Crumpler, NC 28617
  • Fleetwood, NC 28626

Creston is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
