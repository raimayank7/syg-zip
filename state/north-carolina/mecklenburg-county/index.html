Where Mecklenburg County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Mecklenburg County fall close to the middle of North Carolina. Its codes spread over about 30 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 28031 (Cornelius) and 28104 (Matthews), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cabarrus County (18 miles), Gaston County (18 miles), Union County (23 miles) and York County, South Carolina (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Mecklenburg County begin with 282 (73 codes), 281 (6 codes) and 280 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 282 is shared with 1 other county, Robeson County, and has 74 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 35 of the 83 codes. Together they come to about 566 square miles of land (1,465 sq km). The largest is 28078 (Huntersville) with 63.7 square miles, and the smallest is 28282 (Charlotte) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 14.0 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 22.8 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Mecklenburg County; a code can reach across its border. 48 codes have no mapped area: 28070 (Huntersville), 28106 (Matthews), 28126 (Newell), 28130 (Paw Creek), 28201 (Charlotte) and 43 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of North Carolina has more ZIP codes: Mecklenburg County holds 83 of the 1,081 in the state list, about 7.7%.

Writing an address in Mecklenburg County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Charlotte, NC 28201 (one of 73 codes for Charlotte; the street decides which)
  • Matthews, NC 28104 (one of 3 codes for Matthews; the street decides which)
  • Huntersville, NC 28070 (one of 2 codes for Huntersville; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named Charlotte are also listed under Robeson County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Charlotte is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
