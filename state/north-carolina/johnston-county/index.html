Where Johnston County is

The ZIP codes of Johnston County are found in the north-east of North Carolina. Its codes spread over about 25 miles from north to south and 23 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 27504 (Benson) and 27557 (Middlesex), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Wayne County (21 miles), Wilson County (24 miles), Wake County (26 miles) and Harnett County (30 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Johnston County starts with 275. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 275 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Chatham County, Durham County and Franklin County, and has 80 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 719 square miles of land (1,863 sq km). The largest is 27524 (Four Oaks) with 134 square miles, and the smallest is 27593 (Wilsons Mills) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 80.0 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Johnston County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Johnston County ranks 30th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Johnston County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Benson, NC 27504
  • Clayton, NC 27520
  • Four Oaks, NC 27524

Benson is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Clayton shares a name with a place in another part of North Carolina; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
