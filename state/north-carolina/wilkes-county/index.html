Where Wilkes County is

The ZIP codes of Wilkes County are found in the north-west of North Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 26 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 28624 (Ferguson) and 28683 (Thurmond), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Alleghany County (19 miles), Alexander County (21 miles), Ashe County (23 miles) and Yadkin County (26 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Wilkes County starts with 286. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 286 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Alexander County, Alleghany County and Ashe County, and has 90 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 735 square miles of land (1,904 sq km). The largest is 28659 (North Wilkesboro) with 101 square miles, and the smallest is 28683 (Thurmond) with 34.6. The middle-sized code here covers 56.8 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wilkes County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 28656 (North Wilkesboro). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Wilkes County ranks 17th among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Wilkes County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • North Wilkesboro, NC 28656 (one of 2 codes for North Wilkesboro; the street decides which)
  • Boomer, NC 28606
  • Ferguson, NC 28624

Which ZIP code in Wilkes County covers the most land?
