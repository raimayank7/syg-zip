Where Wake County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Wake County is in the north-east of North Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 30 miles, and from east to west about 35 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 27562 (New Hill) and 27597 (Zebulon), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Durham County (22 miles), Franklin County (23 miles), Granville County (25 miles) and Johnston County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Wake County begin with 276 (44 codes) and 275 (20 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 276, so those three digits alone point to Wake County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 35 of the 64 codes. Together they come to about 1,104 square miles of land (2,860 sq km). The largest is 27597 (Zebulon) with 118 square miles, and the smallest is 27697 (Raleigh) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 24.5 square miles; the middle value for all of North Carolina is 42.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wake County; a code can reach across its border. 29 codes have no mapped area: 27512 (Cary), 27588 (Wake Forest), 27602 (Raleigh), 27611 (Raleigh), 27619 (Raleigh) and 24 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Wake County ranks 2nd among the 100 counties and county-level areas of North Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 64 of the 1,081 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Wake County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NC and the ZIP code:

  • Raleigh, NC 27601 (one of 44 codes for Raleigh; the street decides which)
  • Cary, NC 27511 (one of 5 codes for Cary; the street decides which)
  • Apex, NC 27502 (one of 3 codes for Apex; the street decides which)

Raleigh is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
