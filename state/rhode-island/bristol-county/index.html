ZIP codes in Bristol County, Rhode Island

Bristol County, Rhode Island has 4 ZIP codes in this list, from 02806 to 02885. They cover 4 places, among them Barrington, Bristol, Prudence Island and Warren.

Where Bristol County is

Its codes spread over about 4.3 miles from north to south and 2.6 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 02806 (Barrington) and 02809 (Bristol), about 5.0 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Bristol County, Massachusetts (11 miles), Kent County (12 miles), Newport County (12 miles) and Providence County (15 miles).

The first three digits

All 4 codes here begin with the same three digits, 028. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 028 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Kent County, Newport County and Providence County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 30.1 square miles of land (77.9 sq km). The largest is 02809 (Bristol) with 9.8 square miles, and the smallest is 02872 (Prudence Island) with 5.9. Water adds about 24.7 square miles, roughly 45% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bristol County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Bristol County ranks 5th among the 5 counties and county-level areas of Rhode Island (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 90 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bristol County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters RI and the ZIP code:

  • Barrington, RI 02806
  • Bristol, RI 02809
  • Prudence Island, RI 02872

Barrington is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
