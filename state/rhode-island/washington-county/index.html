Where Washington County is

Its codes spread over about 29 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 02807 (Block Island) and 02852 (North Kingstown), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Kent County (18 miles), Newport County (20 miles), Southeastern Connecticut, Connecticut (24 miles) and Bristol County (25 miles).

The first three digits

All 23 codes here begin with the same three digits, 028. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 028 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Bristol County, Kent County and Newport County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 20 of the 23 codes. Together they come to about 329 square miles of land (853 sq km). The largest is 02822 (Exeter) with 54.4 square miles, and the smallest is 02836 (Kenyon) with 0.3. Water adds about 32.5 square miles, roughly 9% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Washington County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 02877 (Slocum), 02880 (Wakefield) and 02883 (Peace Dale). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Washington County ranks 2nd among the 5 counties and county-level areas of Rhode Island (equal counts share a rank), with 23 of the 90 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Washington County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters RI and the ZIP code:

  • Wakefield, RI 02879 (one of 2 codes for Wakefield; the street decides which)
  • Ashaway, RI 02804
  • Block Island, RI 02807

Wakefield is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
