Where Clackamas County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Clackamas County fall in the north-west of Oregon. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 50 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 97028 (Government Camp) and 97070 (Wilsonville), about 50 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Multnomah County (14 miles), Clark County, Washington (25 miles), Washington County (25 miles) and Marion County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Clackamas County begin with 970 (22 codes) and 972 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 970 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Columbia County, Hood River County and Marion County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 23 of the 26 codes. Together they come to about 1,077 square miles of land (2,789 sq km). The largest is 97023 (Estacada) with 196 square miles, and the smallest is 97027 (Gladstone) with 2.5. The middle-sized code here covers 23.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Oregon is 70.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clackamas County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 97036 (Marylhurst), 97268 (Portland) and 97269 (Portland). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Clackamas County ranks 5th among the 36 counties and county-level areas of Oregon (equal counts share a rank), with 26 of the 481 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clackamas County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OR and the ZIP code:

  • Portland, OR 97222 (one of 4 codes for Portland; the street decides which)
  • Lake Oswego, OR 97034 (one of 2 codes for Lake Oswego; the street decides which)
  • Beavercreek, OR 97004

ZIP codes named Portland are also listed under Multnomah County and Washington County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Portland is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 12 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Clackamas County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Portland, OR?

Portland has 4 ZIP codes in Clackamas County: 97222, 97267, 97268, 97269. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Clackamas County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 970, on 22 of the 26 codes.

Which ZIP code in Clackamas County covers the most land?

97023 (Estacada), at about 196 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
