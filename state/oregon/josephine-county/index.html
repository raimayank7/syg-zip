Where Josephine County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Josephine County is in the south-west of Oregon. Its codes spread over about 39 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 97497 (Wolf Creek) and 97534 (O Brien), about 41 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Jackson County (33 miles), Del Norte County, California (49 miles), Curry County (49 miles) and Douglas County (64 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Josephine County begin with 975 (11 codes) and 974 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 975 is shared with 1 other county, Jackson County, and has 26 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 1,206 square miles of land (3,123 sq km). The largest is 97523 (Cave Junction) with 250 square miles, and the smallest is 97531 (Kerby) with 3.7. The middle-sized code here covers 113 square miles; the middle value for all of Oregon is 70.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Josephine County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 97528 (Grants Pass) and 97533 (Murphy). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Josephine County ranks 14th among the 36 counties and county-level areas of Oregon (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 481 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Josephine County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OR and the ZIP code:

Questions about Josephine County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Grants Pass, OR?

Grants Pass has 3 ZIP codes in Josephine County: 97526, 97527, 97528. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Josephine County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 975, on 11 of the 12 codes.

Which ZIP code in Josephine County covers the most land?

97523 (Cave Junction), at about 250 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
