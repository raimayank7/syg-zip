Where Benton County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Benton County is in the west of Oregon. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 97326 (Blodgett) and 97456 (Monroe), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Linn County (25 miles), Polk County (29 miles), Lincoln County (31 miles) and Lane County (36 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Benton County begin with 973 (7 codes) and 974 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 973 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Lincoln County, Linn County and Marion County, and has 73 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 773 square miles of land (2,002 sq km). The largest is 97324 (Alsea) with 195 square miles, and the smallest is 97331 (Corvallis) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 112 square miles; the middle value for all of Oregon is 70.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Benton County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 97339 (Corvallis). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Benton County ranks 22nd among the 36 counties and county-level areas of Oregon (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 481 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Benton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OR and the ZIP code:

  • Corvallis, OR 97330 (one of 4 codes for Corvallis; the street decides which)
  • Alsea, OR 97324
  • Blodgett, OR 97326

Questions about Benton County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Corvallis, OR?

Corvallis has 4 ZIP codes in Benton County: 97330, 97331, 97333, 97339. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Benton County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 973, on 7 of the 8 codes.

Which ZIP code in Benton County covers the most land?

97324 (Alsea), at about 195 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
