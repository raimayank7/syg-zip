Where Wasco County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Wasco County is in the north of Oregon. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 53 miles, and from east to west about 31 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 97001 (Antelope) and 97040 (Mosier), about 60 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Sherman County (23 miles), Hood River County (35 miles), Klickitat County, Washington (38 miles) and Jefferson County (45 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 970. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 970 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Clackamas County, Columbia County and Hood River County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,600 square miles of land (4,144 sq km). The largest is 97037 (Maupin) with 525 square miles, and the smallest is 97057 (Shaniko) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 262 square miles; the middle value for all of Oregon is 70.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wasco County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Wasco County ranks 25th among the 36 counties and county-level areas of Oregon (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 481 codes in the state list.
