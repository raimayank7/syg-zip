Where Curry County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Curry County is in the south-west of Oregon. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 60 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 97415 (Brookings) and 97450 (Langlois), about 60 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Coos County (44 miles), Josephine County (49 miles), Del Norte County, California (56 miles) and Douglas County (67 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Curry County starts with 974. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 974 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Benton County, Coos County and Douglas County, and has 84 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 894 square miles of land (2,316 sq km). The largest is 97415 (Brookings) with 305 square miles, and the smallest is 97476 (Sixes) with 52.6. The middle-sized code here covers 138 square miles; the middle value for all of Oregon is 70.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Curry County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 97464 (Ophir) and 97491 (Wedderburn). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Curry County ranks 22nd among the 36 counties and county-level areas of Oregon (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 481 codes in the state list.
