Where Tillamook County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Tillamook County fall in the north-west of Oregon. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 43 miles, and from east to west about 7.1 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 97131 (Nehalem) and 97149 (Neskowin), about 43 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Yamhill County (38 miles), Clatsop County (39 miles), Polk County (46 miles) and Washington County (48 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Tillamook County starts with 971. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 971 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Clatsop County, Marion County and Washington County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 707 square miles of land (1,832 sq km). The largest is 97141 (Tillamook) with 260 square miles, and the smallest is 97134 (Oceanside) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 19.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Oregon is 70.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 40.9 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Tillamook County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 97143 (Netarts). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Tillamook County ranks 11th among the 36 counties and county-level areas of Oregon (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 481 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Tillamook County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OR and the ZIP code:

  • Bay City, OR 97107
  • Beaver, OR 97108
  • Cloverdale, OR 97112

Bay City is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
