Where Douglas County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Douglas County fall in the south-west of Oregon. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 68 miles, and from east to west about 61 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 97441 (Gardiner) and 97484 (Tiller), about 82 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Coos County (39 miles), Lane County (55 miles), Josephine County (64 miles) and Jackson County (64 miles).

The first three digits

All 26 codes here begin with the same three digits, 974. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 974 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Benton County, Coos County and Curry County, and has 84 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,681 square miles of land (6,943 sq km). The largest is 97467 (Reedsport) with 328 square miles, and the smallest is 97432 (Dillard) with 0.08. The middle-sized code here covers 87.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Oregon is 70.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Douglas County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Douglas County ranks 5th among the 36 counties and county-level areas of Oregon (equal counts share a rank), with 26 of the 481 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Douglas County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OR and the ZIP code:
