Where Wallowa County is

The ZIP codes of Wallowa County are found in the north-east of Oregon. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 34 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 97842 (Imnaha) and 97885 (Wallowa), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Union County (34 miles), Asotin County, Washington (53 miles), Baker County (58 miles) and Idaho County, Idaho (59 miles).

The first three digits

All 5 codes here begin with the same three digits, 978. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 978 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Baker County, Gilliam County and Grant County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,789 square miles of land (7,223 sq km). The largest is 97842 (Imnaha) with 1,075 square miles, and the smallest is 97857 (Lostine) with 256. The middle-sized code here covers 450 square miles; the middle value for all of Oregon is 70.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wallowa County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Wallowa County ranks 29th among the 36 counties and county-level areas of Oregon (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 481 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Wallowa County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OR and the ZIP code:

  • Enterprise, OR 97828
  • Imnaha, OR 97842
  • Joseph, OR 97846

Enterprise is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
