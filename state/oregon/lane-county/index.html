Where Lane County is

The ZIP codes of Lane County are found in the west of Oregon. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 32 miles, and from east to west about 98 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 97413 (Blue River) and 97439 (Florence), about 99 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Benton County (36 miles), Linn County (36 miles), Douglas County (55 miles) and Lincoln County (62 miles).

The first three digits

All 37 codes here begin with the same three digits, 974. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 974 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Benton County, Coos County and Curry County, and has 84 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 34 of the 37 codes. Together they come to about 2,945 square miles of land (7,627 sq km). The largest is 97413 (Blue River) with 474 square miles, and the smallest is 97403 (Eugene) with 4.2. The middle-sized code here covers 67.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Oregon is 70.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lane County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 97409 (Alvadore), 97440 (Eugene) and 97475 (Springfield). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lane County ranks 2nd among the 36 counties and county-level areas of Oregon (equal counts share a rank), with 37 of the 481 codes in the state list.
