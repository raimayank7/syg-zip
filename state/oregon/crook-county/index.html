Where Crook County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Crook County fall close to the middle of Oregon. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 61 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 97751 (Paulina) and 97753 (Powell Butte), about 61 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Deschutes County (41 miles), Wheeler County (45 miles), Jefferson County (45 miles) and Grant County (72 miles).

The first three digits

All 4 codes here begin with the same three digits, 977. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 977 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Deschutes County, Harney County and Jefferson County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,953 square miles of land (5,057 sq km). The largest is 97754 (Prineville) with 1,370 square miles, and the smallest is 97753 (Powell Butte) with 109. The middle-sized code here covers 237 square miles; the middle value for all of Oregon is 70.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Crook County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Crook County ranks 33rd among the 36 counties and county-level areas of Oregon (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 481 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Crook County
