Oregon ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 481 ZIP codes for Oregon, about 1.2% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 35th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 36 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Multnomah County (56), Lane County (37), Marion County (33), Washington County (31) and Clackamas County (26). The fewest are in Wheeler County, with 3.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Oregon uses 10 of these prefixes, from 970 to 979. The busiest are 974 (84 codes, most of them for Eugene), 973 (73 codes, most of them for Salem), 970 (69 codes, most of them for Beaverton) and 972 (60 codes, most of them for Portland). The first digit 9 is shared with Alaska, California, Hawaii and Washington.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 428 of the 481 codes. The largest is 97910 (Jordan Valley, Malheur County) at 3,931 square miles and the smallest is 97258 (Portland) at under 0.01; the middle value is 70.9 square miles. The other 53 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 97121 (Hammond) and the southernmost 97635 (New Pine Creek); the one farthest east is 97842 (Imnaha) and farthest west 97465 (Port Orford).

Place names and addresses in Oregon

Of the 373 place names in the list, 358 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Portland (60), Salem (14), Beaverton (10) and Eugene (7). 2 place names appear under more than one county, among them Portland and Salem: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters OR and the code, as in Portland, OR 97201. The name of the county is left out.

Questions about Oregon ZIP codes

What ZIP codes does Oregon use?

In this list, Oregon ZIP codes run from 97001 to 97920 and start with 9. A few codes inside that range can belong to a neighbouring state near the border.
