Where Baker County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Baker County fall in the north-east of Oregon. Its codes spread over about 41 miles from north to south and 66 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 97840 (Oxbow) and 97884 (Unity), about 75 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Washington County, Idaho (45 miles), Union County (49 miles), Wallowa County (58 miles) and Payette County, Idaho (59 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Baker County begin with 978 (9 codes) and 979 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 978 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Gilliam County, Grant County and Morrow County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,507 square miles of land (6,494 sq km). The largest is 97814 (Baker City) with 829 square miles, and the smallest is 97819 (Bridgeport) with 52.5. The middle-sized code here covers 141 square miles; the middle value for all of Oregon is 70.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Baker County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Baker County ranks 15th among the 36 counties and county-level areas of Oregon (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 481 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Baker County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OR and the ZIP code:

  • Baker City, OR 97814
  • Bridgeport, OR 97819
  • Durkee, OR 97905

Questions about Baker County ZIP codes

Do all ZIP codes in Baker County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Baker County covers the most land?
