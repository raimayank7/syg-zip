Where Washington County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Washington County fall in the north-west of Oregon. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 31 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 97062 (Tualatin) and 97144 (Timber), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Multnomah County (14 miles), Clark County, Washington (21 miles), Yamhill County (25 miles) and Clackamas County (25 miles).

Writing an address in Washington County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OR and the ZIP code:
