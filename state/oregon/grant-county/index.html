Where Grant County is

The ZIP codes of Grant County are found in the east of Oregon. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 47 miles, and from east to west about 47 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 97864 (Monument) and 97873 (Seneca), about 52 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Wheeler County (49 miles), Crook County (72 miles), Baker County (73 miles) and Morrow County (79 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Grant County starts with 978. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 978 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Baker County, Gilliam County and Morrow County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,709 square miles of land (7,017 sq km). The largest is 97856 (Long Creek) with 541 square miles, and the smallest is 97873 (Seneca) with 107. The middle-sized code here covers 257 square miles; the middle value for all of Oregon is 70.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Grant County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Grant County ranks 17th among the 36 counties and county-level areas of Oregon (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 481 codes in the state list.
