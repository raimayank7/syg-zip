Where Clatsop County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Clatsop County is in the north-west of Oregon. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 7.9 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 97102 (Arch Cape) and 97121 (Hammond), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Wahkiakum County, Washington (28 miles), Pacific County, Washington (34 miles), Tillamook County (39 miles) and Columbia County (47 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Clatsop County starts with 971. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 971 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Marion County, Tillamook County and Washington County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 360 square miles of land (931 sq km). The largest is 97103 (Astoria) with 163 square miles, and the smallest is 97145 (Tolovana Park) with 2.5. The middle-sized code here covers 8.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Oregon is 70.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 21.4 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clatsop County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Clatsop County ranks 25th among the 36 counties and county-level areas of Oregon (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 481 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clatsop County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OR and the ZIP code:
