Where Multnomah County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Multnomah County is in the north-west of Oregon. Its codes spread over about 14 miles from north to south and 36 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 97010 (Bridal Veil) and 97231 (Portland), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Clark County, Washington (13 miles), Washington County (14 miles), Clackamas County (14 miles) and Columbia County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Multnomah County begin with 972 (49 codes) and 970 (7 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 972 is shared with 2 other counties, Clackamas County and Washington County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 35 of the 56 codes. Together they come to about 341 square miles of land (883 sq km). The largest is 97019 (Corbett) with 78.8 square miles, and the smallest is 97258 (Portland) with under 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 6.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Oregon is 70.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 40.9 square miles, roughly 11% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Multnomah County; a code can reach across its border. 21 codes have no mapped area: 97010 (Bridal Veil), 97207 (Portland), 97228 (Portland), 97238 (Portland), 97240 (Portland) and 16 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Oregon has more ZIP codes: Multnomah County holds 56 of the 481 in the state list, about 11.6%.

Writing an address in Multnomah County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OR and the ZIP code:

  • Portland, OR 97201 (one of 49 codes for Portland; the street decides which)
  • Gresham, OR 97030 (one of 2 codes for Gresham; the street decides which)
  • Bridal Veil, OR 97010

ZIP codes named Portland are also listed under Clackamas County and Washington County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Portland is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 12 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
