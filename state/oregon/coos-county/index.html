Where Coos County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Coos County fall in the south-west of Oregon. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 49 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 97449 (Lakeside) and 97466 (Powers), about 49 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Douglas County (39 miles), Curry County (44 miles), Josephine County (70 miles) and Lane County (77 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Coos County starts with 974. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 974 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Benton County, Curry County and Douglas County, and has 84 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 1,138 square miles of land (2,948 sq km). The largest is 97458 (Myrtle Point) with 361 square miles, and the smallest is 97414 (Broadbent) with 45.2. The middle-sized code here covers 118 square miles; the middle value for all of Oregon is 70.9. Water adds about 53.6 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Coos County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 97407 (Allegany). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Coos County ranks 19th among the 36 counties and county-level areas of Oregon (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 481 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Coos County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OR and the ZIP code:
