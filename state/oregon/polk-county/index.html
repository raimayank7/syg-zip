Where Polk County is

The ZIP codes of Polk County are found in the north-west of Oregon. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 97304 (Salem) and 97347 (Grand Ronde), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Yamhill County (20 miles), Marion County (23 miles), Benton County (29 miles) and Linn County (36 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 973. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 973 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Benton County, Lincoln County and Linn County, and has 73 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 457 square miles of land (1,185 sq km). The largest is 97361 (Monmouth) with 133 square miles, and the smallest is 97344 (Falls City) with 11.5. The middle-sized code here covers 52.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Oregon is 70.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Polk County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Polk County ranks 25th among the 36 counties and county-level areas of Oregon (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 481 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Polk County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OR and the ZIP code:

  • Dallas, OR 97338
  • Falls City, OR 97344
  • Grand Ronde, OR 97347

Dallas is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Salem shares a name with a place in another part of Oregon; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
