Where Jackson County is

The ZIP codes of Jackson County are found in the south-west of Oregon. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 40 miles, and from east to west about 33 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 97530 (Jacksonville) and 97536 (Prospect), about 48 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Josephine County (33 miles), Siskiyou County, California (57 miles), Klamath County (60 miles) and Douglas County (64 miles).

The first three digits

All 15 codes here begin with the same three digits, 975. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 975 is shared with 1 other county, Josephine County, and has 26 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,034 square miles of land (5,268 sq km). The largest is 97530 (Jacksonville) with 464 square miles, and the smallest is 97535 (Phoenix) with 3.3. The middle-sized code here covers 81.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Oregon is 70.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jackson County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Jackson County ranks 10th among the 36 counties and county-level areas of Oregon (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 481 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jackson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OR and the ZIP code:

  • Medford, OR 97501 (one of 2 codes for Medford; the street decides which)
  • Ashland, OR 97520
  • Butte Falls, OR 97522

Medford is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
