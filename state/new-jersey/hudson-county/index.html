Where Hudson County is

The ZIP codes of Hudson County are found in the north-east of New Jersey. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 8.8 miles, and from east to west about 7.3 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 07002 (Bayonne) and 07093 (West New York), about 10 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are New York County, New York (4.7 miles), Essex County (8.4 miles), Kings County, New York (8.6 miles) and Richmond County, New York (11 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Hudson County begin with 070 (12 codes) and 073 (11 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 070 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Bergen County, Essex County and Middlesex County, and has 88 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 16 of the 23 codes. Together they come to about 46.2 square miles of land (120 sq km). The largest is 07032 (Kearny) with 8.8 square miles, and the smallest is 07311 (Jersey City) with 0.05. The middle-sized code here covers 1.7 square miles; the middle value for all of New Jersey is 4.8. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 16.2 square miles, roughly 26% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hudson County; a code can reach across its border. 7 codes have no mapped area: 07096 (Secaucus), 07097 (Jersey City), 07099 (Kearny), 07303 (Jersey City), 07308 (Jersey City) and 2 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Hudson County ranks 17th among the 21 counties and county-level areas of New Jersey (equal counts share a rank), with 23 of the 723 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hudson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NJ and the ZIP code:

  • Jersey City, NJ 07097 (one of 12 codes for Jersey City; the street decides which)
  • Kearny, NJ 07032 (one of 2 codes for Kearny; the street decides which)
  • Secaucus, NJ 07094 (one of 2 codes for Secaucus; the street decides which)
