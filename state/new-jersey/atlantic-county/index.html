Where Atlantic County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Atlantic County is in the south of New Jersey. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 22 miles, and from east to west about 31 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 08203 (Brigantine) and 08341 (Minotola), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Cape May County (24 miles), Cumberland County (26 miles), Gloucester County (35 miles) and Camden County (35 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Atlantic County begin with 082 (15 codes), 083 (10 codes), 084 (6 codes) and 080 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 082 is shared with 2 other counties, Burlington County and Cape May County, and has 37 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 26 of the 32 codes. Together they come to about 609 square miles of land (1,577 sq km). The largest is 08330 (Mays Landing) with 127 square miles, and the smallest is 08217 (Elwood) with 0.02. The middle-sized code here covers 6.0 square miles; the middle value for all of New Jersey is 4.8. Water adds about 51.8 square miles, roughly 8% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Atlantic County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 08213 (Cologne), 08220 (Leeds Point), 08231 (Oceanville), 08342 (Mizpah), 08404 (Atlantic City) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Atlantic County ranks 10th among the 21 counties and county-level areas of New Jersey (equal counts share a rank), with 32 of the 723 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Atlantic County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NJ and the ZIP code:

  • Atlantic City, NJ 08401 (one of 3 codes for Atlantic City; the street decides which)
  • Absecon, NJ 08201 (one of 2 codes for Absecon; the street decides which)
  • Brigantine, NJ 08203

Questions about Atlantic County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Atlantic City, NJ?

Atlantic City has 3 ZIP codes in Atlantic County: 08401, 08404, 08405. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Atlantic County start with the same digits?

No. They use 4 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 082, on 15 of the 32 codes.

Which ZIP code in Atlantic County covers the most land?

08330 (Mays Landing), at about 127 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
