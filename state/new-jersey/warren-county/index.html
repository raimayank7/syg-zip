Where Warren County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Warren County fall in the north-west of New Jersey. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 07820 (Allamuchy) and 08865 (Phillipsburg), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Hunterdon County (18 miles), Northampton County, Pennsylvania (20 miles), Monroe County, Pennsylvania (24 miles) and Sussex County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Warren County begin with 078 (15 codes) and 088 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 078 is shared with 3 other counties, Hunterdon County, Morris County and Sussex County, and has 56 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 368 square miles of land (953 sq km). The largest is 07825 (Blairstown) with 84.9 square miles, and the smallest is 07846 (Johnsonburg) with 0.08. The middle-sized code here covers 19.9 square miles; the middle value for all of New Jersey is 4.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Warren County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 07829 (Buttzville) and 08808 (Broadway). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Warren County ranks 20th among the 21 counties and county-level areas of New Jersey (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 723 codes in the state list.
