Where Gloucester County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Gloucester County is in the south-west of New Jersey. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 08014 (Bridgeport) and 08344 (Newfield), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Camden County (10 miles), Salem County (15 miles), Delaware County, Pennsylvania (17 miles) and Philadelphia County, Pennsylvania (17 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Gloucester County begin with 080 (23 codes) and 083 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 080 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Atlantic County, Burlington County and Camden County, and has 91 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 27 of the 28 codes. Together they come to about 339 square miles of land (879 sq km). The largest is 08094 (Williamstown) with 54.8 square miles, and the smallest is 08032 (Grenloch) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 5.5 square miles; the middle value for all of New Jersey is 4.8. Water adds about 15.2 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Gloucester County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 08025 (Ewan). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Gloucester County ranks 14th among the 21 counties and county-level areas of New Jersey (equal counts share a rank), with 28 of the 723 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Gloucester County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NJ and the ZIP code:

  • Bridgeport, NJ 08014
  • Clarksboro, NJ 08020
  • Clayton, NJ 08312

Bridgeport is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 13 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
