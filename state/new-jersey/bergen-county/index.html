Where Bergen County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Bergen County is in the north-east of New Jersey. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 07031 (North Arlington) and 07495 (Mahwah), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Passaic County (9.2 miles), Bronx County, New York (11 miles), New York County, New York (12 miles) and Hudson County (13 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Bergen County begin with 076 (42 codes), 074 (16 codes) and 070 (13 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 076, so those three digits alone point to Bergen County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 66 of the 71 codes. Together they come to about 233 square miles of land (603 sq km). The largest is 07430 (Mahwah) with 25.4 square miles, and the smallest is 07606 (South Hackensack) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 2.5 square miles; the middle value for all of New Jersey is 4.8. Water adds about 13.7 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bergen County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 07451 (Ridgewood), 07495 (Mahwah), 07602 (Hackensack), 07653 (Paramus) and 07699 (Teterboro). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of New Jersey has more ZIP codes: Bergen County holds 71 of the 723 in the state list, about 9.8%.

Writing an address in Bergen County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NJ and the ZIP code:

  • Hackensack, NJ 07601 (one of 2 codes for Hackensack; the street decides which)
  • Mahwah, NJ 07430 (one of 2 codes for Mahwah; the street decides which)
  • Paramus, NJ 07652 (one of 2 codes for Paramus; the street decides which)

Questions about Bergen County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Hackensack, NJ?

Hackensack has 2 ZIP codes in Bergen County: 07601, 07602. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Bergen County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 076, on 42 of the 71 codes.

Which ZIP code in Bergen County covers the most land?
