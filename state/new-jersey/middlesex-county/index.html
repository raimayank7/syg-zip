Where Middlesex County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Middlesex County is in the north-east of New Jersey. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 07008 (Carteret) and 08536 (Plainsboro), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Somerset County (13 miles), Union County (13 miles), Richmond County, New York (15 miles) and Monmouth County (19 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Middlesex County begin with 088 (29 codes), 070 (7 codes), 089 (7 codes) and 085 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 088 is shared with 3 other counties, Hunterdon County, Somerset County and Warren County, and has 67 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 36 of the 45 codes. Together they come to about 301 square miles of land (779 sq km). The largest is 08831 (Monroe Township) with 51.3 square miles, and the smallest is 08828 (Helmetta) with 0.8. The middle-sized code here covers 4.9 square miles; the middle value for all of New Jersey is 4.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Middlesex County; a code can reach across its border. 9 codes have no mapped area: 08818 (Edison), 08855 (Piscataway), 08862 (Perth Amboy), 08871 (Sayreville), 08899 (Edison) and 4 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Middlesex County ranks 4th among the 21 counties and county-level areas of New Jersey (equal counts share a rank), with 45 of the 723 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Middlesex County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NJ and the ZIP code:

  • Edison, NJ 08817 (one of 5 codes for Edison; the street decides which)
  • New Brunswick, NJ 08901 (one of 5 codes for New Brunswick; the street decides which)
  • Perth Amboy, NJ 08861 (one of 2 codes for Perth Amboy; the street decides which)

Edison is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Middlesex County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Edison, NJ?

Edison has 5 ZIP codes in Middlesex County: 08817, 08818, 08820, 08837, 08899. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Middlesex County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Middlesex County covers the most land?
