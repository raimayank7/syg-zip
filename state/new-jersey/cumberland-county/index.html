Where Cumberland County is

The ZIP codes of Cumberland County are found in the south-west of New Jersey. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 08313 (Deerfield Street) and 08314 (Delmont), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cape May County (22 miles), Salem County (23 miles), Atlantic County (26 miles) and Gloucester County (27 miles).

The first three digits

All 21 codes here begin with the same three digits, 083. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 083 is shared with 3 other counties, Atlantic County, Gloucester County and Salem County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 18 of the 21 codes. Together they come to about 483 square miles of land (1,251 sq km). The largest is 08302 (Bridgeton) with 143 square miles, and the smallest is 08352 (Rosenhayn) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 10.9 square miles; the middle value for all of New Jersey is 4.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 21.4 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cumberland County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 08313 (Deerfield Street), 08315 (Dividing Creek) and 08362 (Vineland). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Cumberland County ranks 18th among the 21 counties and county-level areas of New Jersey (equal counts share a rank), with 21 of the 723 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cumberland County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NJ and the ZIP code:
