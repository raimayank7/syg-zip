Where Mercer County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Mercer County fall close to the middle of New Jersey. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 08520 (Hightstown) and 08560 (Titusville), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Burlington County (19 miles), Bucks County, Pennsylvania (21 miles), Somerset County (21 miles) and Middlesex County (22 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Mercer County begin with 086 (28 codes) and 085 (11 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 086 is shared with 1 other county, Burlington County, and has 30 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 22 of the 39 codes. Together they come to about 247 square miles of land (639 sq km). The largest is 08540 (Princeton) with 51.7 square miles, and the smallest is 08544 (Princeton) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 7.6 square miles; the middle value for all of New Jersey is 4.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Mercer County; a code can reach across its border. 17 codes have no mapped area: 08541 (Princeton), 08543 (Princeton), 08601 (Trenton), 08602 (Trenton), 08603 (Trenton) and 12 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Mercer County ranks 6th among the 21 counties and county-level areas of New Jersey (equal counts share a rank), with 39 of the 723 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Mercer County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NJ and the ZIP code:

  • Trenton, NJ 08601 (one of 27 codes for Trenton; the street decides which)
  • Princeton, NJ 08540 (one of 5 codes for Princeton; the street decides which)
  • Hightstown, NJ 08520

Trenton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 15 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
