Where Essex County is

The ZIP codes of Essex County are found in the north-east of New Jersey. Its codes spread over about 12 miles from north to south and 8.9 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 07004 (Fairfield) and 07114 (Newark), about 13 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hudson County (8.4 miles), Union County (9.1 miles), Passaic County (13 miles) and New York County, New York (13 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Essex County begin with 071 (23 codes) and 070 (22 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 071, so those three digits alone point to Essex County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 31 of the 45 codes. Together they come to about 126 square miles of land (327 sq km). The largest is 07039 (Livingston) with 13.8 square miles, and the smallest is 07102 (Newark) with 1.1. The middle-sized code here covers 2.9 square miles; the middle value for all of New Jersey is 4.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Essex County; a code can reach across its border. 14 codes have no mapped area: 07007 (Caldwell), 07019 (East Orange), 07051 (Orange), 07101 (Newark), 07175 (Newark) and 9 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Essex County ranks 4th among the 21 counties and county-level areas of New Jersey (equal counts share a rank), with 45 of the 723 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Essex County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NJ and the ZIP code:

  • Newark, NJ 07101 (one of 20 codes for Newark; the street decides which)
  • East Orange, NJ 07017 (one of 3 codes for East Orange; the street decides which)
  • Caldwell, NJ 07006 (one of 2 codes for Caldwell; the street decides which)

Newark is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
