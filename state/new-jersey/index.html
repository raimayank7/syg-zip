New Jersey ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 723 ZIP codes for New Jersey, about 1.8% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 23rd among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 21 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Bergen County (71), Monmouth County (58), Morris County (55), Essex County (45) and Middlesex County (45). The fewest are in Salem County, with 11.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. New Jersey uses 20 of these prefixes, from 070 to 089. The busiest are 080 (91 codes, most of them for Cherry Hill), 070 (88 codes, most of them for Clifton), 088 (67 codes, most of them for Edison) and 078 (56 codes, most of them for Branchville). The first digit 0 is shared with Connecticut, Maine, Massachusetts, New Hampshire, New York, Rhode Island and Vermont.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 598 of the 723 codes. The largest is 08088 (Vincentown, Burlington County) at 151 square miles and the smallest is 08217 (Elwood) at 0.02; the middle value is 4.8 square miles. The other 125 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 07827 (Montague) and the southernmost 08212 (Cape May Point); the one farthest east is 07620 (Alpine) and farthest west 08070 (Pennsville).

Place names and addresses in New Jersey

Of the 582 place names in the list, 533 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Trenton (27), Newark (20), Paterson (14) and Jersey City (12). In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters NJ and the code, as in Trenton, NJ 08601. The name of the county is left out.
