Where Burlington County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Burlington County sits near the middle of New Jersey. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 43 miles, and from east to west about 30 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 08224 (New Gretna) and 08640 (Joint Base Mdl), about 46 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Camden County (17 miles), Mercer County (19 miles), Philadelphia County, Pennsylvania (19 miles) and Gloucester County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Burlington County begin with 080 (25 codes), 085 (6 codes), 086 (2 codes) and 082 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 080 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Atlantic County, Camden County and Gloucester County, and has 91 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 33 of the 34 codes. Together they come to about 673 square miles of land (1,743 sq km). The largest is 08088 (Vincentown) with 151 square miles, and the smallest is 08073 (Rancocas) with 0.09. The middle-sized code here covers 11.4 square miles; the middle value for all of New Jersey is 4.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Burlington County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 08076 (Riverton). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Burlington County ranks 9th among the 21 counties and county-level areas of New Jersey (equal counts share a rank), with 34 of the 723 codes in the state list.
