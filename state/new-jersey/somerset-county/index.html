Where Somerset County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Somerset County fall in the north of New Jersey. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 13 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 07934 (Gladstone) and 08528 (Kingston), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Middlesex County (13 miles), Hunterdon County (15 miles), Union County (17 miles) and Morris County (20 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Somerset County begin with 088 (14 codes), 079 (9 codes), 085 (5 codes) and 070 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 088 is shared with 3 other counties, Hunterdon County, Middlesex County and Warren County, and has 67 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 25 of the 29 codes. Together they come to about 291 square miles of land (754 sq km). The largest is 08844 (Hillsborough) with 55.2 square miles, and the smallest is 07939 (Lyons) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 4.5 square miles; the middle value for all of New Jersey is 4.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Somerset County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 07938 (Liberty Corner), 07978 (Pluckemin), 08504 (Blawenburg) and 08875 (Somerset). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Somerset County ranks 13th among the 21 counties and county-level areas of New Jersey (equal counts share a rank), with 29 of the 723 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Somerset County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NJ and the ZIP code:

  • Somerset, NJ 08873 (one of 2 codes for Somerset; the street decides which)
  • Basking Ridge, NJ 07920
  • Bedminster, NJ 07921

Somerset is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 11 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Somerset County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Somerset, NJ?

Somerset has 2 ZIP codes in Somerset County: 08873, 08875. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Somerset County start with the same digits?

No. They use 4 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 088, on 14 of the 29 codes.

Which ZIP code in Somerset County covers the most land?

08844 (Hillsborough), at about 55.2 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
