Where Vernon County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Vernon County is in the west of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 23 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 64728 (Bronaugh) and 64783 (Schell City), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Barton County (23 miles), Cedar County (25 miles), Bourbon County, Kansas (26 miles) and Bates County (29 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Vernon County starts with 647. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 647 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Barton County, Bates County and Cass County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 747 square miles of land (1,934 sq km). The largest is 64772 (Nevada) with 129 square miles, and the smallest is 64765 (Metz) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 65.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Vernon County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Vernon County ranks 26th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Vernon County
