Where Lincoln County is

The ZIP codes of Lincoln County are found in the north-east of Missouri. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 63369 (Old Monroe) and 63370 (Olney), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Calhoun County, Illinois (19 miles), Warren County (23 miles), Pike County (24 miles) and Saint Charles County (25 miles).

The first three digits

All 11 codes here begin with the same three digits, 633. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 633 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Audrain County, Callaway County and Montgomery County, and has 51 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 586 square miles of land (1,517 sq km). The largest is 63379 (Troy) with 136 square miles, and the smallest is 63387 (Whiteside) with 0.09. The middle-sized code here covers 37.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lincoln County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 63370 (Olney). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lincoln County ranks 26th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lincoln County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:
