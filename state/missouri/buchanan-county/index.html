Where Buchanan County is

The ZIP codes of Buchanan County are found in the north-west of Missouri. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 64443 (Easton) and 64484 (Rushville), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Doniphan County, Kansas (17 miles), Andrew County (18 miles), DeKalb County (24 miles) and Clinton County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Buchanan County begin with 645 (7 codes) and 644 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 645 is shared with 1 other county, Andrew County, and has 8 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 402 square miles of land (1,041 sq km). The largest is 64484 (Rushville) with 72.7 square miles, and the smallest is 64501 (Saint Joseph) with 3.7. The middle-sized code here covers 43.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Buchanan County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 64502 (Saint Joseph) and 64508 (Saint Joseph). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Buchanan County ranks 21st among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Buchanan County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Saint Joseph, MO 64501 (one of 7 codes for Saint Joseph; the street decides which)
  • Agency, MO 64401
  • De Kalb, MO 64440

ZIP codes named Saint Joseph are also listed under Andrew County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Saint Joseph is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Buchanan County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Saint Joseph, MO?

Saint Joseph has 7 ZIP codes in Buchanan County: 64501, 64502, 64503, 64504, 64505, 64507, 64508. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Buchanan County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Buchanan County covers the most land?
