Where Macon County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Macon County is in the north of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 26 miles from north to south and 23 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 63431 (Anabel) and 63539 (Ethel), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Shelby County (27 miles), Adair County (28 miles), Linn County (28 miles) and Randolph County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Macon County begin with 635 (8 codes), 634 (1 code) and 652 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 635 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Adair County, Knox County and Linn County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 817 square miles of land (2,115 sq km). The largest is 63549 (La Plata) with 135 square miles, and the smallest is 65247 (Excello) with 43.0. The middle-sized code here covers 66.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Macon County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Macon County ranks 31st among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.
