Where Texas County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Texas County is in the south of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 29 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 65552 (Plato) and 65571 (Summersville), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Wright County (28 miles), Dent County (31 miles), Shannon County (34 miles) and Phelps County (37 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Texas County begin with 655 (8 codes), 654 (6 codes) and 656 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 655 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Camden County, Crawford County and Dallas County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,232 square miles of land (3,191 sq km). The largest is 65542 (Licking) with 217 square miles, and the smallest is 65557 (Roby) with 8.3. The middle-sized code here covers 73.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Texas County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Texas County ranks 14th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Texas County
