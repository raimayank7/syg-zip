Where Audrain County is

The ZIP codes of Audrain County are found in the north of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 14 miles from north to south and 28 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 63382 (Vandalia) and 65285 (Thompson), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Montgomery County (23 miles), Monroe County (25 miles), Ralls County (27 miles) and Callaway County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Audrain County begin with 652 (5 codes) and 633 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 652 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Boone County, Callaway County and Chariton County, and has 52 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 635 square miles of land (1,646 sq km). The largest is 65265 (Mexico) with 224 square miles, and the smallest is 65280 (Rush Hill) with 19.0. The middle-sized code here covers 58.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Audrain County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Audrain County ranks 50th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.
