Where Reynolds County is

The ZIP codes of Reynolds County are found in the south-east of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 63625 (Black) and 63638 (Ellington), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Iron County (14 miles), Carter County (34 miles), Dent County (34 miles) and Wayne County (36 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 636. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 636 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Bollinger County, Iron County and Madison County, and has 37 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 919 square miles of land (2,380 sq km). The largest is 63638 (Ellington) with 295 square miles, and the smallest is 63654 (Lesterville) with 71.4. The middle-sized code here covers 90.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Reynolds County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 63666 (Reynolds). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Reynolds County ranks 69th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Reynolds County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Bunker, MO 63629
