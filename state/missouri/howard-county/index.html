Where Howard County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Howard County is in the north of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 9.3 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 65230 (Armstrong) and 65274 (New Franklin), about 16 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Cooper County (20 miles), Saline County (23 miles), Randolph County (24 miles) and Boone County (26 miles).

The first three digits

All 5 codes here begin with the same three digits, 652. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 652 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Audrain County, Boone County and Callaway County, and has 52 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 444 square miles of land (1,149 sq km). The largest is 65248 (Fayette) with 173 square miles, and the smallest is 65274 (New Franklin) with 49.6. The middle-sized code here covers 65.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Howard County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Howard County ranks 92nd among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Howard County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Armstrong, MO 65230
  • Fayette, MO 65248
  • Franklin, MO 65250

Armstrong is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
