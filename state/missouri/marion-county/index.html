Where Marion County is

The ZIP codes of Marion County are found in the north-east of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 63401 (Hannibal) and 63463 (Philadelphia), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lewis County (19 miles), Ralls County (21 miles), Adams County, Illinois (23 miles) and Shelby County (26 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Marion County starts with 634. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 634 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Clark County, Knox County and Lewis County, and has 42 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 401 square miles of land (1,038 sq km). The largest is 63461 (Palmyra) with 155 square miles, and the smallest is 63454 (Maywood) with 41.8. The middle-sized code here covers 46.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Marion County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Marion County ranks 92nd among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Marion County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Hannibal, MO 63401
  • Maywood, MO 63454
  • Palmyra, MO 63461

Hannibal is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
