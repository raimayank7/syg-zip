Where Camden County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Camden County fall close to the middle of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 65324 (Climax Springs) and 65567 (Stoutland), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Miller County (19 miles), Morgan County (25 miles), Dallas County (28 miles) and Laclede County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Camden County begin with 650 (5 codes), 655 (2 codes), 657 (2 codes) and 653 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 650 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Boone County, Callaway County and Cole County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 575 square miles of land (1,488 sq km). The largest is 65020 (Camdenton) with 124 square miles, and the smallest is 65065 (Osage Beach) with 16.5. The middle-sized code here covers 54.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. Water adds about 45.0 square miles, roughly 7% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Camden County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Camden County ranks 31st among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Camden County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Climax Springs, MO 65324
  • Lake Ozark, MO 65049

Questions about Camden County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Camdenton, MO?

65020 is the ZIP code listed for Camdenton in Camden County.

Do all ZIP codes in Camden County start with the same digits?

No. They use 4 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 650, on 5 of the 10 codes.

Which ZIP code in Camden County covers the most land?

65020 (Camdenton), at about 124 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
