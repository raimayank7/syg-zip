Where Pemiscot County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Pemiscot County is in the south-east of Missouri. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 15 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 63839 (Cooter) and 63879 (Wardell), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Dunklin County (16 miles), Lake County, Tennessee (22 miles), Dyer County, Tennessee (27 miles) and New Madrid County (31 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Pemiscot County starts with 638. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 638 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Dunklin County, Mississippi County and New Madrid County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 437 square miles of land (1,131 sq km). The largest is 63877 (Steele) with 148 square miles, and the smallest is 63840 (Deering) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 22.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 17.4 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pemiscot County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Pemiscot County ranks 31st among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.
