Where Washington County is

The ZIP codes of Washington County are found in the east of Missouri. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 11 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 63071 (Richwoods) and 63631 (Caledonia), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are St. Francois County (15 miles), Iron County (28 miles), Jefferson County (29 miles) and Crawford County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Washington County begin with 636 (7 codes) and 630 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 636 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Bollinger County, Iron County and Madison County, and has 37 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 550 square miles of land (1,425 sq km). The largest is 63664 (Potosi) with 288 square miles, and the smallest is 63674 (Tiff) with 2.9. The middle-sized code here covers 39.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Washington County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Washington County ranks 50th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Washington County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Belgrade, MO 63622
  • Cadet, MO 63630
  • Caledonia, MO 63631

Belgrade is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
