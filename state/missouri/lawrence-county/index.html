Where Lawrence County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Lawrence County is in the south-west of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 65664 (Halltown) and 65723 (Pierce City), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Dade County (26 miles), Barry County (27 miles), Newton County (29 miles) and Greene County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lawrence County begin with 657 (6 codes) and 656 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 657 is shared with 19 other counties, such as Barry County, Camden County and Cedar County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 629 square miles of land (1,629 sq km). The largest is 65605 (Aurora) with 163 square miles, and the smallest is 65654 (Freistatt) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 87.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lawrence County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lawrence County ranks 38th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lawrence County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Aurora, MO 65605
  • Freistatt, MO 65654
  • Halltown, MO 65664

Aurora is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 15 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
