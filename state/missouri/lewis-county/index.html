Where Lewis County is

The ZIP codes of Lewis County are found in the north of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 63447 (La Belle) and 63448 (La Grange), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Marion County (19 miles), Knox County (22 miles), Clark County (23 miles) and Adams County, Illinois (27 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 634. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 634 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Clark County, Knox County and Macon County, and has 42 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 582 square miles of land (1,506 sq km). The largest is 63435 (Canton) with 145 square miles, and the smallest is 63438 (Durham) with 13.2. The middle-sized code here covers 74.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lewis County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lewis County ranks 50th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lewis County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Canton, MO 63435
  • Durham, MO 63438
  • Ewing, MO 63440

Canton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 16 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
