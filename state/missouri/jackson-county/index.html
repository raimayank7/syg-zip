Where Jackson County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Jackson County fall in the north-west of Missouri. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 64066 (Levasy) and 64147 (Kansas City), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Wyandotte County, Kansas (13 miles), Johnson County, Kansas (16 miles), Clay County (16 miles) and Platte County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Jackson County begin with 641 (47 codes), 640 (27 codes) and 649 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 641 is shared with 2 other counties, Clay County and Platte County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 56 of the 75 codes. Together they come to about 619 square miles of land (1,603 sq km). The largest is 64075 (Oak Grove) with 49.3 square miles, and the smallest is 64102 (Kansas City) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 6.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jackson County; a code can reach across its border. 19 codes have no mapped area: 64002 (Lees Summit), 64013 (Blue Springs), 64051 (Independence), 64121 (Kansas City), 64141 (Kansas City) and 14 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Missouri has more ZIP codes: Jackson County holds 75 of the 1,157 in the state list, about 6.5%.

Writing an address in Jackson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Kansas City, MO 64101 (one of 48 codes for Kansas City; the street decides which)
  • Independence, MO 64050 (one of 9 codes for Independence; the street decides which)
  • Lees Summit, MO 64002 (one of 7 codes for Lees Summit; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named Kansas City are also listed under Clay County and Platte County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site.
