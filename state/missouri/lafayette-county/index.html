Where Lafayette County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Lafayette County fall in the north-west of Missouri. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 31 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 64074 (Napoleon) and 65327 (Emma), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Ray County (20 miles), Johnson County (25 miles), Carroll County (30 miles) and Saline County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lafayette County begin with 640 (12 codes) and 653 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 640 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Cass County, Clay County and Jackson County, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 665 square miles of land (1,722 sq km). The largest is 64076 (Odessa) with 124 square miles, and the smallest is 65327 (Emma) with 1.1. The middle-sized code here covers 35.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lafayette County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lafayette County ranks 18th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lafayette County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Alma, MO 64001
  • Bates City, MO 64011
  • Concordia, MO 64020

Alma is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 10 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
