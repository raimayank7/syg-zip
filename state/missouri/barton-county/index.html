Where Barton County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Barton County fall in the south-west of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 64748 (Golden City) and 64769 (Mindenmines), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Jasper County (23 miles), Vernon County (23 miles), Crawford County, Kansas (25 miles) and Dade County (29 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Barton County starts with 647. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 647 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Bates County, Cass County and Cedar County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 4 of the 5 codes. Together they come to about 542 square miles of land (1,405 sq km). The largest is 64759 (Lamar) with 266 square miles, and the smallest is 64769 (Mindenmines) with 40.6. The middle-sized code here covers 118 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Barton County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 64766 (Milford). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Barton County ranks 92nd among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.
