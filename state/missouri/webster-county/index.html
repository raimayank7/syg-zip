Where Webster County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Webster County is in the south of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 65713 (Niangua) and 65742 (Rogersville), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Greene County (23 miles), Christian County (25 miles), Wright County (25 miles) and Laclede County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Webster County begin with 657 (4 codes) and 656 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 657 is shared with 19 other counties, such as Barry County, Camden County and Cedar County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 748 square miles of land (1,937 sq km). The largest is 65746 (Seymour) with 185 square miles, and the smallest is 65652 (Fordland) with 71.3. The middle-sized code here covers 115 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Webster County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 65636 (Diggins). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Webster County ranks 69th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.
