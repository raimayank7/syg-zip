Where Callaway County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Callaway County sits near the middle of Missouri. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 26 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 65043 (Holts Summit) and 65231 (Auxvasse), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Osage County (25 miles), Cole County (25 miles), Boone County (25 miles) and Montgomery County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Callaway County begin with 650 (6 codes), 652 (3 codes) and 633 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 650 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Boone County, Camden County and Cole County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 741 square miles of land (1,919 sq km). The largest is 65251 (Fulton) with 213 square miles, and the smallest is 65077 (Steedman) with 26.9. The middle-sized code here covers 54.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Callaway County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Callaway County ranks 31st among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.
