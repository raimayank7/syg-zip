Where Ste. Genevieve County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Ste. Genevieve County is in the east of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 63627 (Bloomsdale) and 63673 (Saint Mary), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are St. Francois County (20 miles), Randolph County, Illinois (20 miles), Monroe County, Illinois (25 miles) and Perry County (29 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Ste. Genevieve County starts with 636. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 636 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Bollinger County, Iron County and Madison County, and has 37 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 439 square miles of land (1,138 sq km). The largest is 63670 (Sainte Genevieve) with 251 square miles, and the smallest is 63627 (Bloomsdale) with 73.0. The middle-sized code here covers 115 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ste. Genevieve County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Ste. Genevieve County ranks 113th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 3 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.
