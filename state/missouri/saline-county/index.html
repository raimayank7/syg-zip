Where Saline County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Saline County is in the north-west of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 65330 (Gilliam) and 65351 (Sweet Springs), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Howard County (23 miles), Carroll County (27 miles), Chariton County (28 miles) and Cooper County (28 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Saline County starts with 653. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 653 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Benton County, Camden County and Cooper County, and has 32 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 794 square miles of land (2,056 sq km). The largest is 65340 (Marshall) with 254 square miles, and the smallest is 65320 (Arrow Rock) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 77.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Saline County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Saline County ranks 38th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Saline County
