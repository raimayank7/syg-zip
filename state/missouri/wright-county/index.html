Where Wright County is

The ZIP codes of Wright County are found in the south of Missouri. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 18 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 65662 (Grovespring) and 65717 (Norwood), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Douglas County (21 miles), Webster County (25 miles), Texas County (28 miles) and Laclede County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Wright County begin with 657 (4 codes) and 656 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 657 is shared with 19 other counties, such as Barry County, Camden County and Cedar County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 862 square miles of land (2,234 sq km). The largest is 65711 (Mountain Grove) with 293 square miles, and the smallest is 65660 (Graff) with 26.6. The middle-sized code here covers 125 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wright County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Wright County ranks 69th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.
