Where Stoddard County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Stoddard County is in the south-east of Missouri. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 63730 (Advance) and 63822 (Bernie), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Scott County (23 miles), New Madrid County (24 miles), Butler County (27 miles) and Bollinger County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Stoddard County begin with 638 (5 codes), 637 (3 codes) and 639 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 638 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Dunklin County, Mississippi County and New Madrid County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 782 square miles of land (2,027 sq km). The largest is 63841 (Dexter) with 129 square miles, and the smallest is 63850 (Grayridge) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 93.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Stoddard County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Stoddard County ranks 31st among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.
