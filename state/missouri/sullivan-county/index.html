Where Sullivan County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Sullivan County is in the north of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 63566 (Winigan) and 64667 (Newtown), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Putnam County (18 miles), Grundy County (22 miles), Linn County (26 miles) and Mercer County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Sullivan County begin with 635 (5 codes) and 646 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 635 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Adair County, Knox County and Linn County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 737 square miles of land (1,910 sq km). The largest is 63556 (Milan) with 242 square miles, and the smallest is 63566 (Winigan) with 19.5. The middle-sized code here covers 74.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sullivan County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Sullivan County ranks 50th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.
