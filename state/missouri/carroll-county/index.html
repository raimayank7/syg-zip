Where Carroll County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Carroll County fall in the north-west of Missouri. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 18 miles, and from east to west about 29 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 64639 (De Witt) and 64680 (Stet), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Livingston County (22 miles), Chariton County (23 miles), Saline County (27 miles) and Caldwell County (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 646. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 646 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Caldwell County, Chariton County and Daviess County, and has 55 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 714 square miles of land (1,850 sq km). The largest is 64633 (Carrollton) with 239 square miles, and the smallest is 64639 (De Witt) with 36.1. The middle-sized code here covers 71.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Carroll County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 64680 (Stet). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Carroll County ranks 50th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Carroll County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:
