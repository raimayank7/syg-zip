Where Christian County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Christian County fall in the south of Missouri. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 33 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 65610 (Billings) and 65720 (Oldfield), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Stone County (21 miles), Greene County (21 miles), Taney County (22 miles) and Webster County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Christian County begin with 656 (7 codes), 657 (5 codes) and 726 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 656 is shared with 18 other counties, such as Barry County, Cedar County and Dade County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 546 square miles of land (1,415 sq km). The largest is 65721 (Ozark) with 95.9 square miles, and the smallest is 65630 (Chestnutridge) with 11.6. The middle-sized code here covers 41.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Christian County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 72643 (Lead Hill). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Christian County ranks 18th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Christian County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Billings, MO 65610
  • Bruner, MO 65620
  • Chadwick, MO 65629

Billings is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
