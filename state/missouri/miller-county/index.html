Where Miller County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Miller County fall close to the middle of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 65017 (Brumley) and 65064 (Olean), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Camden County (19 miles), Cole County (24 miles), Morgan County (25 miles) and Pulaski County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Miller County begin with 650 (7 codes) and 654 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 650 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Boone County, Callaway County and Camden County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 510 square miles of land (1,322 sq km). The largest is 65026 (Eldon) with 143 square miles, and the smallest is 65083 (Ulman) with 14.1. The middle-sized code here covers 55.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Miller County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Miller County ranks 50th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Miller County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Brumley, MO 65017
  • Eldon, MO 65026
  • Iberia, MO 65486
