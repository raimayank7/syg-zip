Where Saint Charles County is

The ZIP codes of Saint Charles County are found in the east of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 37 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 63332 (Augusta) and 63386 (West Alton), about 40 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are St. Louis County (16 miles), Warren County (25 miles), St. Louis (city) (25 miles) and Lincoln County (25 miles).

The first three digits

All 17 codes here begin with the same three digits, 633. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 633 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Audrain County, Callaway County and Lincoln County, and has 51 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 568 square miles of land (1,471 sq km). The largest is 63301 (Saint Charles) with 84.3 square miles, and the smallest is 63365 (New Melle) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 39.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. Water adds about 32.3 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Saint Charles County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 63302 (Saint Charles), 63338 (Cottleville) and 63346 (Flinthill). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Saint Charles County ranks 10th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Saint Charles County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Saint Charles, MO 63301 (one of 4 codes for Saint Charles; the street decides which)
  • O Fallon, MO 63366 (one of 2 codes for O Fallon; the street decides which)
  • Augusta, MO 63332

Saint Charles is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
