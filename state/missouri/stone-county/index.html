Where Stone County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Stone County is in the south-west of Missouri. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 26 miles, and from east to west about 13 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 65611 (Blue Eye) and 65633 (Crane), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Taney County (19 miles), Christian County (21 miles), Barry County (24 miles) and Carroll County, Arkansas (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Stone County begin with 656 (7 codes) and 657 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 656 is shared with 18 other counties, such as Barry County, Cedar County and Christian County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 417 square miles of land (1,081 sq km). The largest is 65656 (Galena) with 114 square miles, and the smallest is 65675 (Hurley) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 35.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. Water adds about 33.1 square miles, roughly 7% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Stone County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Stone County ranks 38th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Stone County
