Where Taney County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Taney County fall in the south of Missouri. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 24 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 65733 (Protem) and 65771 (Walnut Shade), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Stone County (19 miles), Christian County (22 miles), Carroll County, Arkansas (32 miles) and Marion County, Arkansas (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Taney County begin with 656 (9 codes) and 657 (8 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 656 is shared with 18 other counties, such as Barry County, Cedar County and Christian County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 15 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 631 square miles of land (1,633 sq km). The largest is 65614 (Bradleyville) with 107 square miles, and the smallest is 65726 (Point Lookout) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 47.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. Water adds about 28.8 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Taney County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 65615 (Branson) and 65673 (Hollister). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Taney County ranks 10th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Taney County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Hollister, MO 65672 (one of 2 codes for Hollister; the street decides which)
  • Bradleyville, MO 65614

Questions about Taney County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Branson, MO?

Branson has 2 ZIP codes in Taney County: 65615, 65616. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Taney County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 656, on 9 of the 17 codes.

Which ZIP code in Taney County covers the most land?

65614 (Bradleyville), at about 107 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
