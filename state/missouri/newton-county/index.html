Where Newton County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Newton County fall in the south-west of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 29 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 64865 (Seneca) and 64873 (Wentworth), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Jasper County (19 miles), McDonald County (20 miles), Barry County (28 miles) and Lawrence County (29 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Newton County starts with 648. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 648 is shared with 3 other counties, Barry County, Jasper County and McDonald County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 572 square miles of land (1,481 sq km). The largest is 64850 (Neosho) with 203 square miles, and the smallest is 64858 (Racine) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 53.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Newton County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 64853 (Newtonia) and 64864 (Saginaw). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Newton County ranks 26th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Newton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:
