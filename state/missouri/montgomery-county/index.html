Where Montgomery County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Montgomery County is in the north-east of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 63359 (Middletown) and 65069 (Rhineland), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Audrain County (23 miles), Warren County (24 miles), Lincoln County (26 miles) and Callaway County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Montgomery County begin with 633 (7 codes) and 650 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 633 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Audrain County, Callaway County and Lincoln County, and has 51 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 679 square miles of land (1,759 sq km). The largest is 63361 (Montgomery City) with 161 square miles, and the smallest is 63350 (High Hill) with 23.6. The middle-sized code here covers 77.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Montgomery County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Montgomery County ranks 50th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Montgomery County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:
