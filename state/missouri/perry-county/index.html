Where Perry County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Perry County fall in the south-east of Missouri. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 8.8 miles, and from east to west about 16 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 63732 (Altenburg) and 63775 (Perryville), about 17 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cape Girardeau County (19 miles), Jackson County, Illinois (21 miles), Randolph County, Illinois (27 miles) and Union County, Illinois (28 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 637. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 637 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Bollinger County, Cape Girardeau County and Scott County, and has 42 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 459 square miles of land (1,188 sq km). The largest is 63775 (Perryville) with 343 square miles, and the smallest is 63746 (Farrar) with 1.3. The middle-sized code here covers 33.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Perry County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 63737 (Brazeau) and 63776 (Mc Bride). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Perry County ranks 69th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.
