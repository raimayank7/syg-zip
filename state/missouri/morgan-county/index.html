Where Morgan County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Morgan County sits near the middle of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 31 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 65038 (Laurie) and 65354 (Syracuse), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Moniteau County (19 miles), Benton County (24 miles), Camden County (25 miles) and Miller County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Morgan County begin with 650 (6 codes) and 653 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 650 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Boone County, Callaway County and Camden County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 567 square miles of land (1,469 sq km). The largest is 65084 (Versailles) with 168 square miles, and the smallest is 65072 (Rocky Mount) with 19.1. The middle-sized code here covers 79.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. Water adds about 20.0 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Morgan County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 65038 (Laurie). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Morgan County ranks 50th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Morgan County
