Where Laclede County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Laclede County fall in the south of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 65463 (Eldridge) and 65543 (Lynchburg), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Dallas County (25 miles), Pulaski County (27 miles), Wright County (29 miles) and Webster County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Laclede County begin with 654 (2 codes), 655 (2 codes), 656 (1 code) and 657 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 654 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Crawford County, Dent County and Iron County, and has 29 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 799 square miles of land (2,069 sq km). The largest is 65536 (Lebanon) with 402 square miles, and the smallest is 65722 (Phillipsburg) with 49.5. The middle-sized code here covers 86.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Laclede County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Laclede County ranks 83rd among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Laclede County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Conway, MO 65632
  • Eldridge, MO 65463
  • Falcon, MO 65470

Conway is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
