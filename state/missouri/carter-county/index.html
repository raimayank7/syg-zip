Where Carter County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Carter County fall in the south-east of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 12 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 63937 (Ellsinore) and 63941 (Fremont), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Ripley County (22 miles), Wayne County (30 miles), Shannon County (31 miles) and Reynolds County (34 miles).

The first three digits

All 4 codes here begin with the same three digits, 639. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 639 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Butler County, Dunklin County and Ripley County, and has 32 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 617 square miles of land (1,598 sq km). The largest is 63965 (Van Buren) with 225 square miles, and the smallest is 63943 (Grandin) with 83.4. The middle-sized code here covers 154 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Carter County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Carter County ranks 106th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.
