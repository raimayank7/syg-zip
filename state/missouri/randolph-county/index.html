Where Randolph County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Randolph County is in the north of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 65243 (Clark) and 65260 (Jacksonville), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Howard County (24 miles), Monroe County (28 miles), Macon County (28 miles) and Boone County (31 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Randolph County starts with 652. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 652 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Audrain County, Boone County and Callaway County, and has 52 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 576 square miles of land (1,492 sq km). The largest is 65270 (Moberly) with 113 square miles, and the smallest is 65278 (Renick) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 77.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Randolph County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Randolph County ranks 50th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Randolph County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Cairo, MO 65239
  • Clark, MO 65243
  • Clifton Hill, MO 65244

Cairo is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
