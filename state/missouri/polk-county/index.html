Where Polk County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Polk County is in the south-west of Missouri. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 65674 (Humansville) and 65725 (Pleasant Hope), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Hickory County (23 miles), Dallas County (23 miles), Dade County (24 miles) and Greene County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Polk County begin with 656 (9 codes) and 657 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 656 is shared with 18 other counties, such as Barry County, Cedar County and Christian County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 736 square miles of land (1,906 sq km). The largest is 65613 (Bolivar) with 185 square miles, and the smallest is 65727 (Polk) with 10.0. The middle-sized code here covers 52.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Polk County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 65645 (Eudora). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Polk County ranks 21st among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Polk County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Bolivar, MO 65613
  • Brighton, MO 65617
