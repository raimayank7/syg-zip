Where St. Louis County is

The ZIP codes of St. Louis County are found in the east of Missouri. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 26 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 63025 (Eureka) and 63138 (Saint Louis), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are St. Louis (city) (9.4 miles), Saint Charles County (16 miles), St. Clair County, Illinois (24 miles) and Jefferson County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of St. Louis County begin with 631 (30 codes) and 630 (22 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 631 is shared with 1 other county-level area, St. Louis (city), and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 44 of the 52 codes. Together they come to about 515 square miles of land (1,334 sq km). The largest is 63025 (Eureka) with 41.7 square miles, and the smallest is 63140 (Saint Louis) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 10.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 17.4 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of St. Louis County; a code can reach across its border. 8 codes have no mapped area: 63006 (Chesterfield), 63022 (Ballwin), 63024 (Ballwin), 63032 (Florissant), 63099 (Fenton) and 3 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, St. Louis County ranks 2nd among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 52 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in St. Louis County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Saint Louis, MO 63105 (one of 30 codes for Saint Louis; the street decides which)
  • Ballwin, MO 63011 (one of 4 codes for Ballwin; the street decides which)
  • Florissant, MO 63031 (one of 4 codes for Florissant; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named Saint Louis are also listed under St. Louis (city), so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site.
