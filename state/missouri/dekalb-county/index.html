Where DeKalb County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of DeKalb County fall in the north-west of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 64494 (Union Star) and 64497 (Weatherby), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Clinton County (20 miles), Andrew County (20 miles), Gentry County (23 miles) and Buchanan County (24 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of DeKalb County starts with 644. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 644 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Andrew County, Atchison County and Buchanan County, and has 70 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 450 square miles of land (1,165 sq km). The largest is 64469 (Maysville) with 121 square miles, and the smallest is 64497 (Weatherby) with 41.1. The middle-sized code here covers 56.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of DeKalb County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, DeKalb County ranks 69th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in DeKalb County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Amity, MO 64422
  • Clarksdale, MO 64430
  • Maysville, MO 64469

Amity is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
