Where Phelps County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Phelps County sits near the middle of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 65461 (Duke) and 65559 (Saint James), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Pulaski County (19 miles), Dent County (22 miles), Maries County (22 miles) and Crawford County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Phelps County begin with 654 (6 codes) and 655 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 654 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Crawford County, Dent County and Iron County, and has 29 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 769 square miles of land (1,991 sq km). The largest is 65401 (Rolla) with 259 square miles, and the smallest is 65409 (Rolla) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 61.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Phelps County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 65402 (Rolla). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Phelps County ranks 38th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Phelps County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Rolla, MO 65401 (one of 3 codes for Rolla; the street decides which)
  • Beulah, MO 65436
  • Duke, MO 65461

Questions about Phelps County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Rolla, MO?

Rolla has 3 ZIP codes in Phelps County: 65401, 65402, 65409. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Phelps County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 654, on 6 of the 9 codes.

Which ZIP code in Phelps County covers the most land?

65401 (Rolla), at about 259 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
