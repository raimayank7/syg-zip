Where Boone County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Boone County fall in the north of Missouri. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 34 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 65039 (Hartsburg) and 65240 (Centralia), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Callaway County (25 miles), Howard County (26 miles), Cooper County (28 miles) and Moniteau County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Boone County begin with 652 (16 codes) and 650 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 652 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Audrain County, Callaway County and Chariton County, and has 52 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 18 codes. Together they come to about 828 square miles of land (2,145 sq km). The largest is 65240 (Centralia) with 180 square miles, and the smallest is 65215 (Columbia) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 59.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Boone County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 65205 (Columbia), 65211 (Columbia), 65212 (Columbia), 65217 (Columbia), 65218 (Columbia) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Boone County ranks 9th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Boone County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Columbia, MO 65201 (one of 10 codes for Columbia; the street decides which)
  • Ashland, MO 65010
  • Centralia, MO 65240

Columbia is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 16 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Boone County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Columbia, MO?

Columbia has 10 ZIP codes in Boone County: 65201, 65202, 65203, 65205, 65211, 65212, 65215, 65216, 65217, 65218. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Boone County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 652, on 16 of the 18 codes.

Which ZIP code in Boone County covers the most land?

65240 (Centralia), at about 180 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
