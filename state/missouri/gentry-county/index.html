Where Gentry County is

The ZIP codes of Gentry County are found in the north-west of Missouri. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 13 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 64453 (Gentry) and 64463 (King City), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Worth County (18 miles), DeKalb County (23 miles), Nodaway County (25 miles) and Harrison County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Gentry County begin with 644 (5 codes) and 646 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 644 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Andrew County, Atchison County and Buchanan County, and has 70 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 542 square miles of land (1,403 sq km). The largest is 64463 (King City) with 137 square miles, and the smallest is 64453 (Gentry) with 40.3. The middle-sized code here covers 94.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Gentry County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Gentry County ranks 83rd among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Gentry County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MO and the ZIP code:

  • Albany, MO 64402
  • Darlington, MO 64438
  • Gentry, MO 64453

Albany is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 14 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
