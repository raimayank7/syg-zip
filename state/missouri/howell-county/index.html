Where Howell County is

The ZIP codes of Howell County are found in the south of Missouri. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 32 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 65548 (Mountain View) and 65777 (Moody), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Fulton County, Arkansas (27 miles), Oregon County (28 miles), Shannon County (30 miles) and Ozark County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Howell County begin with 657 (6 codes), 656 (2 codes) and 655 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 657 is shared with 19 other counties, such as Barry County, Camden County and Cedar County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,163 square miles of land (3,012 sq km). The largest is 65775 (West Plains) with 442 square miles, and the smallest is 65688 (Brandsville) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 72.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Missouri is 51.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Howell County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Howell County ranks 38th among the 115 counties and county-level areas of Missouri (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,157 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Howell County
