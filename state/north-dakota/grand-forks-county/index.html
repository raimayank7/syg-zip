Where Grand Forks County is

The ZIP codes of Grand Forks County are found in the north-east of North Dakota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 31 miles, and from east to west about 37 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 58206 (Grand Forks) and 58266 (Niagara), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Traill County (33 miles), Walsh County (36 miles), Nelson County (40 miles) and Steele County (43 miles).

The first three digits

All 19 codes here begin with the same three digits, 582. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 582 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Cavalier County, Nelson County and Pembina County, and has 61 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 16 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 1,305 square miles of land (3,379 sq km). The largest is 58267 (Northwood) with 234 square miles, and the smallest is 58202 (Grand Forks) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 78.4 square miles; the middle value for all of North Dakota is 146. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Grand Forks County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 58206 (Grand Forks), 58207 (Grand Forks) and 58208 (Grand Forks). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Grand Forks County ranks 2nd among the 53 counties and county-level areas of North Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 407 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Grand Forks County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters ND and the ZIP code:
