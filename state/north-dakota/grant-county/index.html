Where Grant County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Grant County is in the south-west of North Dakota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 19 miles, and from east to west about 34 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 58562 (New Leipzig) and 58569 (Shields), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Morton County (29 miles), Sioux County (38 miles), Corson County, South Dakota (44 miles) and Hettinger County (49 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Grant County starts with 585. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 585 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Burleigh County, Emmons County and Logan County, and has 48 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,255 square miles of land (3,250 sq km). The largest is 58529 (Carson) with 394 square miles, and the smallest is 58564 (Raleigh) with 98.8. The middle-sized code here covers 290 square miles; the middle value for all of North Dakota is 146. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Grant County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Grant County ranks 32nd among the 53 counties and county-level areas of North Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 407 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Grant County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters ND and the ZIP code:

  • Carson, ND 58529
  • Elgin, ND 58533
  • New Leipzig, ND 58562

Carson is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
