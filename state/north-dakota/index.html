North Dakota ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 407 ZIP codes for North Dakota, about 1.0% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 38th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 53 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Cass County (34), Grand Forks County (19), Ward County (17), Burleigh County (14) and Richland County (14). At the other end, 5 have only one or two codes, for example Adams County, Billings County and Eddy County.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. North Dakota uses 9 of these prefixes, from 580 to 588. The busiest are 587 (64 codes, most of them for Minot), 584 (62 codes, most of them for Jamestown), 582 (61 codes, most of them for Grand Forks) and 580 (60 codes, most of them for Wahpeton). The first digit 5 is shared with District of Columbia, Iowa, Minnesota, Montana, South Dakota and Wisconsin.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 388 of the 407 codes. The largest is 58651 (Rhame, Bowman County) at 716 square miles and the smallest is 58402 (Jamestown) at 0.04; the middle value is 146 square miles. The other 19 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 58255 (Maida) and the southernmost 58043 (Havana); the one farthest east is 58074 (Wahpeton) and farthest west 58621 (Beach).

Place names and addresses in North Dakota

Of the 372 place names in the list, 362 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Fargo (13), Bismarck (7), Grand Forks (6) and Minot (4). In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters ND and the code, as in Fargo, ND 58102. The name of the county is left out.
