Where Burke County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Burke County fall in the north-west of North Dakota. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 23 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 58721 (Bowbells) and 58727 (Columbus), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Divide County (38 miles), Renville County (44 miles), Mountrail County (46 miles) and Williams County (53 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Burke County starts with 587. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 587 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Bottineau County, Divide County and McHenry County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,014 square miles of land (2,625 sq km). The largest is 58773 (Powers Lake) with 303 square miles, and the smallest is 58772 (Portal) with 56.6. The middle-sized code here covers 174 square miles; the middle value for all of North Dakota is 146. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Burke County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Burke County ranks 27th among the 53 counties and county-level areas of North Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 407 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Burke County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters ND and the ZIP code:

  • Bowbells, ND 58721
  • Columbus, ND 58727
  • Flaxton, ND 58737
