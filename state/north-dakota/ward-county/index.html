Where Ward County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Ward County fall in the north-west of North Dakota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 59 miles, and from east to west about 47 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 58735 (Douglas) and 58746 (Kenmare), about 65 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Renville County (33 miles), McHenry County (38 miles), Mountrail County (40 miles) and Bottineau County (45 miles).

The first three digits

All 17 codes here begin with the same three digits, 587. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 587 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Bottineau County, Burke County and Divide County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 16 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 2,519 square miles of land (6,524 sq km). The largest is 58746 (Kenmare) with 447 square miles, and the smallest is 58707 (Minot) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 133 square miles; the middle value for all of North Dakota is 146. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ward County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 58702 (Minot). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Ward County ranks 3rd among the 53 counties and county-level areas of North Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 407 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Ward County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters ND and the ZIP code:

  • Minot, ND 58701 (one of 4 codes for Minot; the street decides which)
  • Minot Afb, ND 58704 (one of 2 codes for Minot Afb; the street decides which)
  • Berthold, ND 58718
