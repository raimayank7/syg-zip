Where McKenzie County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of McKenzie County fall in the west of North Dakota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 34 miles, and from east to west about 59 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 58757 (Mandaree) and 58838 (Cartwright), about 59 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Williams County (43 miles), Richland County, Montana (47 miles), Billings County (47 miles) and Dunn County (51 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of McKenzie County begin with 588 (5 codes), 586 (1 code) and 587 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 588 is shared with 2 other counties, Divide County and Williams County, and has 17 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,474 square miles of land (6,408 sq km). The largest is 58854 (Watford City) with 666 square miles, and the smallest is 58847 (Keene) with 176. The middle-sized code here covers 342 square miles; the middle value for all of North Dakota is 146. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 109 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of McKenzie County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, McKenzie County ranks 22nd among the 53 counties and county-level areas of North Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 407 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in McKenzie County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters ND and the ZIP code:

  • Alexander, ND 58831
  • Arnegard, ND 58835
  • Cartwright, ND 58838

Alexander is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
