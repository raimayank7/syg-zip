Where Mercer County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Mercer County is in the west of North Dakota. Its codes spread over about 3.9 miles from north to south and 31 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 58541 (Golden Valley) and 58571 (Stanton), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Oliver County (30 miles), Dunn County (34 miles), McLean County (36 miles) and Morton County (41 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Mercer County starts with 585. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 585 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Burleigh County, Emmons County and Grant County, and has 48 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,041 square miles of land (2,696 sq km). The largest is 58545 (Hazen) with 315 square miles, and the smallest is 58571 (Stanton) with 115. The middle-sized code here covers 214 square miles; the middle value for all of North Dakota is 146. Water adds about 70.1 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Mercer County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Mercer County ranks 32nd among the 53 counties and county-level areas of North Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 407 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Mercer County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters ND and the ZIP code:

  • Beulah, ND 58523
  • Golden Valley, ND 58541
  • Hazen, ND 58545

Beulah is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
