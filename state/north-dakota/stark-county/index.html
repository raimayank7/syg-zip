Where Stark County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Stark County fall in the south-west of North Dakota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 18 miles, and from east to west about 39 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 58622 (Belfield) and 58652 (Richardton), about 39 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Hettinger County (27 miles), Dunn County (33 miles), Billings County (40 miles) and Adams County (52 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 586. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 586 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Adams County, Billings County and Bowman County, and has 32 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 2,046 square miles of land (5,299 sq km). The largest is 58601 (Dickinson) with 571 square miles, and the smallest is 58641 (Lefor) with 77.6. The middle-sized code here covers 183 square miles; the middle value for all of North Dakota is 146. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Stark County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 58602 (Dickinson). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Stark County ranks 18th among the 53 counties and county-level areas of North Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 407 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Stark County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters ND and the ZIP code:

  • Dickinson, ND 58601 (one of 2 codes for Dickinson; the street decides which)
  • Belfield, ND 58622
  • Gladstone, ND 58630

Questions about Stark County ZIP codes

Yes. All 8 start with 586.

Which ZIP code in Stark County covers the most land?
