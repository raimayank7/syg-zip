Where Ramsey County is

The ZIP codes of Ramsey County are found in the north-east of North Dakota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 30 miles, and from east to west about 36 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 58321 (Brocket) and 58325 (Churchs Ferry), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Benson County (29 miles), Cavalier County (35 miles), Nelson County (37 miles) and Towner County (38 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 583. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 583 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Benson County, Bottineau County and Cavalier County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,441 square miles of land (3,731 sq km). The largest is 58301 (Devils Lake) with 274 square miles, and the smallest is 58362 (Penn) with 41.1. The middle-sized code here covers 127 square miles; the middle value for all of North Dakota is 146. Water adds about 124 square miles, roughly 8% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ramsey County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Ramsey County ranks 15th among the 53 counties and county-level areas of North Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 407 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Ramsey County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters ND and the ZIP code:
