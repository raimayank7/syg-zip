Where Barnes County is

The ZIP codes of Barnes County are found in the south-east of North Dakota. Its codes spread over about 37 miles from north to south and 32 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 58062 (Nome) and 58492 (Wimbledon), about 44 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Steele County (36 miles), Ransom County (37 miles), Griggs County (39 miles) and Stutsman County (40 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Barnes County begin with 580 (6 codes) and 584 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 580 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Cass County, Ransom County and Richland County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,443 square miles of land (3,736 sq km). The largest is 58072 (Valley City) with 328 square miles, and the smallest is 58065 (Pillsbury) with 4.2. The middle-sized code here covers 124 square miles; the middle value for all of North Dakota is 146. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Barnes County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Barnes County ranks 11th among the 53 counties and county-level areas of North Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 407 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Barnes County
