Where Cass County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Cass County is in the south-east of North Dakota. Its codes spread over about 39 miles from north to south and 41 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 58047 (Horace) and 58064 (Page), about 45 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Clay County, Minnesota (22 miles), Norman County, Minnesota (35 miles), Traill County (38 miles) and Ransom County (39 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Cass County begin with 580 (21 codes) and 581 (13 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 580 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Barnes County, Ransom County and Richland County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 25 of the 34 codes. Together they come to about 1,859 square miles of land (4,815 sq km). The largest is 58064 (Page) with 176 square miles, and the smallest is 58105 (Fargo) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 70.2 square miles; the middle value for all of North Dakota is 146. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cass County; a code can reach across its border. 9 codes have no mapped area: 58106 (Fargo), 58107 (Fargo), 58108 (Fargo), 58109 (Fargo), 58121 (Fargo) and 4 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of North Dakota has more ZIP codes: Cass County holds 34 of the 407 in the state list, about 8.4%.

Writing an address in Cass County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters ND and the ZIP code:

  • Fargo, ND 58102 (one of 13 codes for Fargo; the street decides which)
  • Absaraka, ND 58002
  • Amenia, ND 58004
