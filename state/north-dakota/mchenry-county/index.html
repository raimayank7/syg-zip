Where McHenry County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of McHenry County fall in the north of North Dakota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 49 miles, and from east to west about 36 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 58710 (Anamoose) and 58789 (Upham), about 54 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Pierce County (37 miles), Ward County (38 miles), Bottineau County (42 miles) and McLean County (47 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 587. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 587 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Bottineau County, Burke County and Divide County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,960 square miles of land (5,077 sq km). The largest is 58788 (Towner) with 518 square miles, and the smallest is 58713 (Bantry) with 18.8. The middle-sized code here covers 166 square miles; the middle value for all of North Dakota is 146. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of McHenry County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, McHenry County ranks 8th among the 53 counties and county-level areas of North Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 407 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in McHenry County
