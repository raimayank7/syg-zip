Where Divide County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Divide County fall in the north-west of North Dakota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 4.8 miles, and from east to west about 33 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 58765 (Noonan) and 58844 (Fortuna), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Williams County (35 miles), Burke County (38 miles), Sheridan County, Montana (53 miles) and Mountrail County (66 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Divide County begin with 587 (2 codes) and 588 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 587 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Bottineau County, Burke County and McHenry County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 719 square miles of land (1,861 sq km). The largest is 58730 (Crosby) with 257 square miles, and the smallest is 58833 (Ambrose) with 97.6. The middle-sized code here covers 182 square miles; the middle value for all of North Dakota is 146. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Divide County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Divide County ranks 36th among the 53 counties and county-level areas of North Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 407 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Divide County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters ND and the ZIP code:
