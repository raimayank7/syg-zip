Where Door County is

The ZIP codes of Door County are found in the east of Wisconsin. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 47 miles, and from east to west about 35 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 54204 (Brussels) and 54246 (Washington Island), about 56 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Kewaunee County (36 miles), Menominee County, Michigan (39 miles), Marinette County (43 miles) and Oconto County (47 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Door County starts with 542. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 542 is shared with 3 other counties, Brown County, Kewaunee County and Manitowoc County, and has 30 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 469 square miles of land (1,216 sq km). The largest is 54235 (Sturgeon Bay) with 190 square miles, and the smallest is 54211 (Ephraim) with 3.6. The middle-sized code here covers 35.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. Water adds about 23.2 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Door County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 54226 (Maplewood). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Door County ranks 32nd among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Door County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Baileys Harbor, WI 54202
  • Brussels, WI 54204
  • Egg Harbor, WI 54209
