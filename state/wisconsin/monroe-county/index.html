Where Monroe County is

The ZIP codes of Monroe County are found in the south-west of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 54619 (Cashton) and 54666 (Warrens), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Juneau County (21 miles), Vernon County (27 miles), La Crosse County (27 miles) and Jackson County (31 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 546. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 546 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Adams County, Buffalo County and Crawford County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 984 square miles of land (2,548 sq km). The largest is 54656 (Sparta) with 282 square miles, and the smallest is 54670 (Wilton) with 52.7. The middle-sized code here covers 105 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Monroe County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 54620 (Cataract), 54649 (Oakdale) and 54662 (Tunnel City). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Monroe County ranks 39th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Monroe County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:
