Where Washington County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Washington County is in the south-east of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 53017 (Colgate) and 53040 (Kewaskum), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Ozaukee County (13 miles), Waukesha County (21 miles), Dodge County (22 miles) and Milwaukee County (26 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 530. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 530 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Calumet County, Dodge County and Fond du Lac County, and has 83 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 445 square miles of land (1,151 sq km). The largest is 53027 (Hartford) with 91.0 square miles, and the smallest is 53033 (Hubertus) with 16.0. The middle-sized code here covers 27.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Washington County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 53060 (Newburg). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Washington County ranks 27th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Washington County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • West Bend, WI 53090 (one of 2 codes for West Bend; the street decides which)
  • Allenton, WI 53002
  • Colgate, WI 53017
