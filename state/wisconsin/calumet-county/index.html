Where Calumet County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Calumet County fall in the south-east of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 11 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 53061 (New Holstein) and 54123 (Forest Junction), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Manitowoc County (19 miles), Winnebago County (22 miles), Outagamie County (23 miles) and Brown County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Calumet County begin with 541 (5 codes) and 530 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 541 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Brown County, Florence County and Forest County, and has 56 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 313 square miles of land (810 sq km). The largest is 53014 (Chilton) with 130 square miles, and the smallest is 53088 (Stockbridge) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 23.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Calumet County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 53062 (New Holstein). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Calumet County ranks 46th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Calumet County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • New Holstein, WI 53061 (one of 2 codes for New Holstein; the street decides which)
  • Brillion, WI 54110
  • Chilton, WI 53014

Questions about Calumet County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for New Holstein, WI?

New Holstein has 2 ZIP codes in Calumet County: 53061, 53062. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Calumet County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 541, on 5 of the 9 codes.

Which ZIP code in Calumet County covers the most land?

53014 (Chilton), at about 130 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
