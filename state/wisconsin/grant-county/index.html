Where Grant County is

The ZIP codes of Grant County are found in the south-west of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 49 miles from north to south and 32 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 53518 (Blue River) and 53824 (Sinsinawa), about 49 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Dubuque County, Iowa (28 miles), Iowa County (28 miles), Crawford County (30 miles) and Clayton County, Iowa (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Grant County begin with 538 (19 codes) and 535 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 538 is shared with 2 other counties, Crawford County and Lafayette County, and has 22 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 22 of the 23 codes. Together they come to about 1,407 square miles of land (3,643 sq km). The largest is 53818 (Platteville) with 154 square miles, and the smallest is 53817 (Patch Grove) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 56.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Grant County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 53824 (Sinsinawa). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Grant County ranks 4th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 23 of the 896 codes in the state list.
