Where Columbia County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Columbia County fall in the south of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 38 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 53925 (Columbus) and 53965 (Wisconsin Dells), about 43 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Marquette County (23 miles), Green Lake County (24 miles), Dane County (28 miles) and Dodge County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Columbia County begin with 539 (13 codes) and 535 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 539 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Adams County, Dodge County and Fond du Lac County, and has 51 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 915 square miles of land (2,369 sq km). The largest is 53965 (Wisconsin Dells) with 169 square miles, and the smallest is 53969 (Wyocena) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 75.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. Water adds about 34.4 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Columbia County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 53957 (Randolph). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Columbia County ranks 21st among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Columbia County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Arlington, WI 53911
  • Cambria, WI 53923
  • Columbus, WI 53925

Arlington is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 20 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Randolph shares a name with a place in another part of Wisconsin; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
