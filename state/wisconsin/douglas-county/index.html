Where Douglas County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Douglas County is in the north-west of Wisconsin. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 37 miles, and from east to west about 28 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 54880 (Superior) and 54890 (Wascott), about 39 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Bayfield County (33 miles), Carlton County, Minnesota (38 miles), Washburn County (44 miles) and Burnett County (51 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Douglas County starts with 548. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 548 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Ashland County, Barron County and Bayfield County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 1,299 square miles of land (3,364 sq km). The largest is 54873 (Solon Springs) with 265 square miles, and the smallest is 54842 (Hawthorne) with 4.9. The middle-sized code here covers 128 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 42.0 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Douglas County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 54890 (Wascott). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Douglas County ranks 32nd among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Douglas County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:
