Where Dunn County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Dunn County is in the west of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 33 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 54737 (Eau Galle) and 54765 (Sand Creek), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are St. Croix County (29 miles), Pepin County (30 miles), Eau Claire County (30 miles) and Pierce County (31 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 547. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 547 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Barron County, Buffalo County and Chippewa County, and has 55 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 779 square miles of land (2,018 sq km). The largest is 54751 (Menomonie) with 228 square miles, and the smallest is 54737 (Eau Galle) with 21.1. The middle-sized code here covers 60.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Dunn County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 54735 (Downsville), 54764 (Rock Falls) and 54765 (Sand Creek). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Dunn County ranks 27th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Dunn County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:
