Where Brown County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Brown County fall in the east of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 25 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 54162 (Pulaski) and 54208 (Denmark), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Outagamie County (22 miles), Kewaunee County (22 miles), Calumet County (27 miles) and Manitowoc County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Brown County begin with 543 (12 codes), 541 (5 codes) and 542 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 543, so those three digits alone point to Brown County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 638 square miles of land (1,652 sq km). The largest is 54115 (De Pere) with 124 square miles, and the smallest is 54307 (Green Bay) with 0.08. The middle-sized code here covers 26.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Brown County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 54305 (Green Bay), 54306 (Green Bay), 54308 (Green Bay), 54324 (Green Bay) and 54344 (Green Bay). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Brown County ranks 7th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Brown County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Green Bay, WI 54301 (one of 12 codes for Green Bay; the street decides which)
  • De Pere, WI 54115
  • Denmark, WI 54208
