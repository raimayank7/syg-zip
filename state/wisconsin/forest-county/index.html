Where Forest County is

The ZIP codes of Forest County are found in the north-east of Wisconsin. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 19 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 54103 (Armstrong Creek) and 54520 (Crandon), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Florence County (23 miles), Langlade County (28 miles), Iron County, Michigan (37 miles) and Marinette County (37 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Forest County begin with 545 (4 codes) and 541 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 545 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Ashland County, Florence County and Iron County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 750 square miles of land (1,942 sq km). The largest is 54511 (Argonne) with 277 square miles, and the smallest is 54103 (Armstrong Creek) with 77.0. The middle-sized code here covers 136 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Forest County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Forest County ranks 64th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Forest County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:
