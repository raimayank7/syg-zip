Where Barron County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Barron County fall in the north-west of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 26 miles from north to south and 28 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 54857 (Mikana) and 54889 (Turtle Lake), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Washburn County (27 miles), Polk County (29 miles), Dunn County (35 miles) and Burnett County (36 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Barron County begin with 548 (11 codes) and 547 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 548 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Ashland County, Bayfield County and Burnett County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 922 square miles of land (2,387 sq km). The largest is 54868 (Rice Lake) with 212 square miles, and the smallest is 54857 (Mikana) with 0.08. The middle-sized code here covers 53.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. Water adds about 32.0 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Barron County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 54818 (Brill). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Barron County ranks 21st among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Barron County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:
