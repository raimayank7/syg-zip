Where Sheboygan County is

The ZIP codes of Sheboygan County are found in the south-east of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 53023 (Glenbeulah) and 53082 (Sheboygan), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Ozaukee County (24 miles), Washington County (29 miles), Manitowoc County (30 miles) and Fond du Lac County (31 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Sheboygan County starts with 530. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 530 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Calumet County, Dodge County and Fond du Lac County, and has 83 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 502 square miles of land (1,299 sq km). The largest is 53073 (Plymouth) with 84.6 square miles, and the smallest is 53031 (Hingham) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 31.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. Water adds about 16.2 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sheboygan County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 53026 (Greenbush) and 53082 (Sheboygan). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Sheboygan County ranks 10th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Sheboygan County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Sheboygan, WI 53081 (one of 3 codes for Sheboygan; the street decides which)
  • Adell, WI 53001
  • Cascade, WI 53011

Questions about Sheboygan County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Sheboygan, WI?

Sheboygan has 3 ZIP codes in Sheboygan County: 53081, 53082, 53083. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Sheboygan County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 16 start with 530.

Which ZIP code in Sheboygan County covers the most land?

53073 (Plymouth), at about 84.6 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
