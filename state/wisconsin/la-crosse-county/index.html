Where La Crosse County is

The ZIP codes of La Crosse County are found in the south-west of Wisconsin. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 16 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 54601 (La Crosse) and 54644 (Mindoro), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Houston County, Minnesota (22 miles), Vernon County (25 miles), Monroe County (27 miles) and Jackson County (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 546. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 546 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Adams County, Buffalo County and Crawford County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 411 square miles of land (1,065 sq km). The largest is 54636 (Holmen) with 83.0 square miles, and the smallest is 54603 (La Crosse) with 8.9. The middle-sized code here covers 56.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of La Crosse County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 54602 (La Crosse). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, La Crosse County ranks 46th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in La Crosse County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • La Crosse, WI 54601 (one of 3 codes for La Crosse; the street decides which)
  • Bangor, WI 54614
  • Holmen, WI 54636

La Crosse is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about La Crosse County ZIP codes

La Crosse has 3 ZIP codes in La Crosse County: 54601, 54602, 54603. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Yes. All 9 start with 546.

Which ZIP code in La Crosse County covers the most land?
