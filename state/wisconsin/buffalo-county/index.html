Where Buffalo County is

The ZIP codes of Buffalo County are found in the west of Wisconsin. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 24 miles, and from east to west about 16 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 54629 (Fountain City) and 54756 (Nelson), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Pepin County (20 miles), Trempealeau County (20 miles), Wabasha County, Minnesota (24 miles) and Winona County, Minnesota (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Buffalo County begin with 546 (3 codes) and 547 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 546 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Adams County, Crawford County and Jackson County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 635 square miles of land (1,644 sq km). The largest is 54755 (Mondovi) with 259 square miles, and the smallest is 54622 (Cochrane) with 56.7. The middle-sized code here covers 112 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 32.7 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Buffalo County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 54743 (Gilmanton). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Buffalo County ranks 63rd among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Buffalo County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Alma, WI 54610
  • Cochrane, WI 54622
  • Fountain City, WI 54629

Alma is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 10 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
