Where Langlade County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Langlade County is in the north-east of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 54428 (Elcho) and 54464 (Phlox), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Menominee County (25 miles), Forest County (28 miles), Lincoln County (32 miles) and Shawano County (33 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Langlade County starts with 544. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 544 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Clark County, Lincoln County and Marathon County, and has 86 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 840 square miles of land (2,177 sq km). The largest is 54409 (Antigo) with 207 square miles, and the smallest is 54464 (Phlox) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 57.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Langlade County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Langlade County ranks 39th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 896 codes in the state list.
