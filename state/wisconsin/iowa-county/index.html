Where Iowa County is

The ZIP codes of Iowa County are found in the south of Wisconsin. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 24 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 53503 (Arena) and 53580 (Rewey), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lafayette County (24 miles), Richland County (24 miles), Grant County (28 miles) and Sauk County (32 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Iowa County starts with 535. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 535 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Columbia County, Dane County and Dodge County, and has 87 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 694 square miles of land (1,798 sq km). The largest is 53565 (Mineral Point) with 186 square miles, and the smallest is 53553 (Linden) with 6.8. The middle-sized code here covers 56.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Iowa County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 53535 (Edmund) and 53595 (Dodgeville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Iowa County ranks 24th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Iowa County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Dodgeville, WI 53533 (one of 2 codes for Dodgeville; the street decides which)
  • Arena, WI 53503
  • Avoca, WI 53506

Questions about Iowa County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Dodgeville, WI?

Dodgeville has 2 ZIP codes in Iowa County: 53533, 53595. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Iowa County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 13 start with 535.

Which ZIP code in Iowa County covers the most land?

53565 (Mineral Point), at about 186 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
