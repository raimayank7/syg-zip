Where Fond du Lac County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Fond du Lac County is in the south-east of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 34 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 53079 (Saint Cloud) and 53931 (Fairwater), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Winnebago County (21 miles), Dodge County (26 miles), Calumet County (28 miles) and Green Lake County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Fond du Lac County begin with 549 (7 codes), 530 (6 codes) and 539 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 549 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Green Lake County, Marquette County and Outagamie County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 684 square miles of land (1,770 sq km). The largest is 54937 (Fond Du Lac) with 120 square miles, and the smallest is 53931 (Fairwater) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 36.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Fond du Lac County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 54936 (Fond Du Lac). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Fond du Lac County ranks 16th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Fond du Lac County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Fond Du Lac, WI 54935 (one of 3 codes for Fond Du Lac; the street decides which)
  • Brandon, WI 53919
  • Campbellsport, WI 53010

Questions about Fond du Lac County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Fond Du Lac, WI?

Fond Du Lac has 3 ZIP codes in Fond du Lac County: 54935, 54936, 54937. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Fond du Lac County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 549, on 7 of the 15 codes.

Which ZIP code in Fond du Lac County covers the most land?

54937 (Fond Du Lac), at about 120 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
