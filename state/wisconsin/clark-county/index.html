Where Clark County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Clark County fall in the west of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 32 miles from north to south and 29 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 54425 (Dorchester) and 54746 (Humbird), about 41 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Taylor County (29 miles), Wood County (33 miles), Jackson County (38 miles) and Chippewa County (39 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Clark County begin with 544 (12 codes) and 547 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 544 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Langlade County, Lincoln County and Marathon County, and has 86 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,250 square miles of land (3,237 sq km). The largest is 54456 (Neillsville) with 225 square miles, and the smallest is 54405 (Abbotsford) with 29.8. The middle-sized code here covers 80.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clark County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Clark County ranks 21st among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clark County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:
