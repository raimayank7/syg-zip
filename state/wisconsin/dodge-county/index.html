Where Dodge County is

The ZIP codes of Dodge County are found in the south-east of Wisconsin. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 28 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 53003 (Ashippun) and 53956 (Randolph), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Washington County (22 miles), Fond du Lac County (26 miles), Jefferson County (26 miles) and Columbia County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Dodge County begin with 530 (15 codes), 539 (5 codes) and 535 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 530 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Calumet County, Fond du Lac County and Jefferson County, and has 83 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 870 square miles of land (2,252 sq km). The largest is 53916 (Beaver Dam) with 104 square miles, and the smallest is 53003 (Ashippun) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 33.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. Water adds about 30.8 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Dodge County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Dodge County ranks 5th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 22 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Dodge County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Ashippun, WI 53003
  • Beaver Dam, WI 53916
  • Brownsville, WI 53006

Randolph and Watertown share a name with a place in another part of Wisconsin; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
