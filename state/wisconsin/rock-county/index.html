Where Rock County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Rock County fall in the south-east of Wisconsin. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 53525 (Clinton) and 53536 (Evansville), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Green County (24 miles), Winnebago County, Illinois (24 miles), Boone County, Illinois (26 miles) and Walworth County (28 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Rock County starts with 535. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 535 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Columbia County, Dane County and Dodge County, and has 87 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 646 square miles of land (1,674 sq km). The largest is 53511 (Beloit) with 115 square miles, and the smallest is 53501 (Afton) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 62.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Rock County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 53512 (Beloit), 53542 (Hanover) and 53547 (Janesville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Rock County ranks 16th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Rock County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Janesville, WI 53545 (one of 4 codes for Janesville; the street decides which)
  • Beloit, WI 53511 (one of 2 codes for Beloit; the street decides which)
  • Afton, WI 53501

Janesville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
