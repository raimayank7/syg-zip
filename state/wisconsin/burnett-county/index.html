Where Burnett County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Burnett County is in the north-west of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 27 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 54840 (Grantsburg) and 54845 (Hertel), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Washburn County (27 miles), Pine County, Minnesota (29 miles), Polk County (30 miles) and Barron County (36 miles).

The first three digits

All 5 codes here begin with the same three digits, 548. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 548 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Ashland County, Barron County and Bayfield County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 831 square miles of land (2,151 sq km). The largest is 54830 (Danbury) with 353 square miles, and the smallest is 54845 (Hertel) with 5.2. The middle-sized code here covers 153 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 51.5 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Burnett County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Burnett County ranks 64th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Burnett County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Danbury, WI 54830
  • Grantsburg, WI 54840
  • Hertel, WI 54845

Danbury is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
