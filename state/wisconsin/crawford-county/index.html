Where Crawford County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Crawford County is in the south-west of Wisconsin. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 24 miles, and from east to west about 18 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 53821 (Prairie Du Chien) and 54655 (Soldiers Grove), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Allamakee County, Iowa (21 miles), Vernon County (25 miles), Grant County (30 miles) and Clayton County, Iowa (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Crawford County begin with 546 (8 codes) and 538 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 546 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Adams County, Buffalo County and Jackson County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 545 square miles of land (1,412 sq km). The largest is 54631 (Gays Mills) with 104 square miles, and the smallest is 54654 (Seneca) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 73.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Crawford County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 54640 (Lynxville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Crawford County ranks 39th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Crawford County
