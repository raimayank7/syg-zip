Where Jackson County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Jackson County is in the west of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 54635 (Hixton) and 54643 (Millston), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Trempealeau County (22 miles), La Crosse County (30 miles), Monroe County (31 miles) and Eau Claire County (37 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Jackson County begin with 546 (6 codes) and 547 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 546 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Adams County, Buffalo County and Crawford County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 676 square miles of land (1,750 sq km). The largest is 54615 (Black River Falls) with 316 square miles, and the smallest is 54643 (Millston) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 74.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jackson County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Jackson County ranks 57th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jackson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Black River Falls, WI 54615
  • Hixton, WI 54635
