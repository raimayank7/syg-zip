Where Waushara County is

The ZIP codes of Waushara County are found in the south of Wisconsin. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 13 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 54943 (Hancock) and 54967 (Poy Sippi), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Green Lake County (25 miles), Marquette County (26 miles), Waupaca County (27 miles) and Portage County (27 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Waushara County starts with 549. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 549 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Fond du Lac County, Green Lake County and Marquette County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 608 square miles of land (1,574 sq km). The largest is 54982 (Wautoma) with 128 square miles, and the smallest is 54967 (Poy Sippi) with 2.2. The middle-sized code here covers 79.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Waushara County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 54976 (Saxeville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Waushara County ranks 46th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 896 codes in the state list.
