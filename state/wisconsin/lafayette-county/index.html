Where Lafayette County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Lafayette County is in the south of Wisconsin. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 53516 (Blanchardville) and 53803 (Benton), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Jo Daviess County, Illinois (18 miles), Iowa County (24 miles), Green County (26 miles) and Stephenson County, Illinois (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lafayette County begin with 535 (8 codes) and 538 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 535 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Columbia County, Dane County and Dodge County, and has 87 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 586 square miles of land (1,519 sq km). The largest is 53530 (Darlington) with 130 square miles, and the smallest is 53599 (Woodford) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 63.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lafayette County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lafayette County ranks 46th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lafayette County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Argyle, WI 53504
  • Belmont, WI 53510
  • Benton, WI 53803

Argyle is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
