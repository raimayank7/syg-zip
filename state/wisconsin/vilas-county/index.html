Where Vilas County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Vilas County is in the north of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 39 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 54538 (Lac Du Flambeau) and 54554 (Phelps), about 39 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Oneida County (22 miles), Gogebic County, Michigan (30 miles), Iron County (44 miles) and Ontonagon County, Michigan (44 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Vilas County starts with 545. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 545 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Ashland County, Florence County and Forest County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 883 square miles of land (2,286 sq km). The largest is 54521 (Eagle River) with 188 square miles, and the smallest is 54560 (Sayner) with 31.8. The middle-sized code here covers 80.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. Water adds about 156 square miles, roughly 15% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Vilas County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Vilas County ranks 32nd among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Vilas County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:
