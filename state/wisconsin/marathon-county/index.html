Where Marathon County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Marathon County sits near the middle of Wisconsin. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 22 miles, and from east to west about 53 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 54429 (Elderon) and 54488 (Unity), about 53 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Portage County (29 miles), Wood County (30 miles), Lincoln County (35 miles) and Langlade County (41 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Marathon County starts with 544. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 544 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Clark County, Langlade County and Lincoln County, and has 86 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 16 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 1,330 square miles of land (3,445 sq km). The largest is 54455 (Mosinee) with 236 square miles, and the smallest is 54417 (Brokaw) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 79.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Marathon County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 54402 (Wausau), 54429 (Elderon) and 54432 (Galloway). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Marathon County ranks 7th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Marathon County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Wausau, WI 54401 (one of 3 codes for Wausau; the street decides which)
  • Aniwa, WI 54408
  • Athens, WI 54411
