Where Dane County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Dane County fall in the south of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 41 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 53517 (Blue Mounds) and 53523 (Cambridge), about 41 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Columbia County (28 miles), Green County (29 miles), Jefferson County (33 miles) and Rock County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Dane County begin with 537 (35 codes) and 535 (22 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 537, so those three digits alone point to Dane County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 35 of the 57 codes. Together they come to about 1,141 square miles of land (2,954 sq km). The largest is 53572 (Mount Horeb) with 99.1 square miles, and the smallest is 53792 (Madison) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 31.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Dane County; a code can reach across its border. 22 codes have no mapped area: 53596 (Sun Prairie), 53701 (Madison), 53702 (Madison), 53707 (Madison), 53708 (Madison) and 17 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Wisconsin has more ZIP codes: Dane County holds 57 of the 896 in the state list, about 6.4%.

Writing an address in Dane County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Madison, WI 53701 (one of 35 codes for Madison; the street decides which)
  • Sun Prairie, WI 53590 (one of 2 codes for Sun Prairie; the street decides which)
  • Belleville, WI 53508

Madison is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 25 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
