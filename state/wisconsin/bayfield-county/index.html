Where Bayfield County is

The ZIP codes of Bayfield County are found in the north-west of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 43 miles from north to south and 28 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 54814 (Bayfield) and 54821 (Cable), about 47 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Ashland County (26 miles), Douglas County (33 miles), Iron County (44 miles) and Lake County, Minnesota (51 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Bayfield County starts with 548. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 548 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Ashland County, Barron County and Burnett County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 1,118 square miles of land (2,896 sq km). The largest is 54856 (Mason) with 211 square miles, and the smallest is 54827 (Cornucopia) with 35.8. The middle-sized code here covers 83.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. Water adds about 39.1 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bayfield County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 54816 (Benoit). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Bayfield County ranks 32nd among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bayfield County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Benoit, WI 54816
  • Cable, WI 54821
