Where Sauk County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Sauk County is in the south of Wisconsin. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 28 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 53588 (Spring Green) and 53940 (Lake Delton), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Richland County (20 miles), Iowa County (32 miles), Juneau County (33 miles) and Columbia County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Sauk County begin with 539 (10 codes) and 535 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 539 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Adams County, Columbia County and Dodge County, and has 51 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 860 square miles of land (2,228 sq km). The largest is 53588 (Spring Green) with 140 square miles, and the smallest is 53940 (Lake Delton) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 51.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sauk County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 53958 (Reedsburg). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Sauk County ranks 16th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Sauk County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Reedsburg, WI 53958 (one of 2 codes for Reedsburg; the street decides which)
  • Baraboo, WI 53913
  • Hillpoint, WI 53937

Questions about Sauk County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Reedsburg, WI?

Reedsburg has 2 ZIP codes in Sauk County: 53958, 53959. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Sauk County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 539, on 10 of the 15 codes.

Which ZIP code in Sauk County covers the most land?

53588 (Spring Green), at about 140 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
