Where Manitowoc County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Manitowoc County fall in the south-east of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 53042 (Kiel) and 54240 (Tisch Mills), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Calumet County (19 miles), Brown County (28 miles), Sheboygan County (30 miles) and Kewaunee County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Manitowoc County begin with 542 (13 codes) and 530 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 542 is shared with 3 other counties, Brown County, Door County and Kewaunee County, and has 30 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 565 square miles of land (1,463 sq km). The largest is 54230 (Reedsville) with 109 square miles, and the smallest is 54215 (Kellnersville) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 39.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. Water adds about 19.7 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Manitowoc County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 54221 (Manitowoc) and 54240 (Tisch Mills). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Manitowoc County ranks 10th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Manitowoc County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Manitowoc, WI 54220 (one of 2 codes for Manitowoc; the street decides which)
  • Cleveland, WI 53015
  • Collins, WI 54207

Questions about Manitowoc County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Manitowoc, WI?

Manitowoc has 2 ZIP codes in Manitowoc County: 54220, 54221. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Manitowoc County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 542, on 13 of the 16 codes.

Which ZIP code in Manitowoc County covers the most land?

54230 (Reedsville), at about 109 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
