Where Waupaca County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Waupaca County fall in the east of Wisconsin. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 31 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 54933 (Embarrass) and 54946 (King), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Portage County (25 miles), Outagamie County (26 miles), Shawano County (26 miles) and Waushara County (27 miles).

The first three digits

All 15 codes here begin with the same three digits, 549. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 549 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Fond du Lac County, Green Lake County and Marquette County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 891 square miles of land (2,307 sq km). The largest is 54981 (Waupaca) with 132 square miles, and the smallest is 54946 (King) with 0.07. The middle-sized code here covers 81.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Waupaca County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 54969 (Readfield) and 54990 (Iola). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Waupaca County ranks 16th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Waupaca County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Big Falls, WI 54926
