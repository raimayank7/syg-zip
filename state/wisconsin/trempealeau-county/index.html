Where Trempealeau County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Trempealeau County fall in the west of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 40 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 54661 (Trempealeau) and 54738 (Eleva), about 40 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Buffalo County (20 miles), Jackson County (22 miles), Eau Claire County (30 miles) and Winona County, Minnesota (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Trempealeau County begin with 546 (6 codes) and 547 (6 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 546 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Adams County, Buffalo County and Crawford County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 978 square miles of land (2,532 sq km). The largest is 54758 (Osseo) with 157 square miles, and the smallest is 54760 (Pigeon Falls) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 83.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Trempealeau County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Trempealeau County ranks 27th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Trempealeau County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Arcadia, WI 54612
  • Blair, WI 54616
  • Dodge, WI 54625

Arcadia is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 13 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
