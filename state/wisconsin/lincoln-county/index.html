Where Lincoln County is

The ZIP codes of Lincoln County are found in the north of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 25 miles from north to south and 12 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 54452 (Merrill) and 54532 (Heafford Junction), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Oneida County (25 miles), Langlade County (32 miles), Marathon County (35 miles) and Price County (39 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lincoln County begin with 544 (4 codes) and 545 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 544 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Clark County, Langlade County and Marathon County, and has 86 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 4 of the 5 codes. Together they come to about 1,006 square miles of land (2,605 sq km). The largest is 54452 (Merrill) with 438 square miles, and the smallest is 54442 (Irma) with 75.5. The middle-sized code here covers 246 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 33.9 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lincoln County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 54532 (Heafford Junction). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lincoln County ranks 64th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 896 codes in the state list.
