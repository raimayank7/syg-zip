Where Outagamie County is

The ZIP codes of Outagamie County are found in the east of Wisconsin. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 27 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 54155 (Oneida) and 54931 (Dale), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Brown County (22 miles), Winnebago County (23 miles), Calumet County (23 miles) and Waupaca County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Outagamie County begin with 541 (10 codes) and 549 (10 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 541 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Brown County, Calumet County and Florence County, and has 56 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 20 codes. Together they come to about 664 square miles of land (1,720 sq km). The largest is 54170 (Shiocton) with 121 square miles, and the smallest is 54931 (Dale) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 19.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Wisconsin is 52.6. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Outagamie County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 54131 (Freedom), 54912 (Appleton) and 54919 (Appleton). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Outagamie County ranks 6th among the 72 counties and county-level areas of Wisconsin (equal counts share a rank), with 20 of the 896 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Outagamie County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WI and the ZIP code:

  • Appleton, WI 54911 (one of 6 codes for Appleton; the street decides which)
  • Bear Creek, WI 54922
  • Black Creek, WI 54106

Appleton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
