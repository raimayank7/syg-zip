Where Lincoln County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Lincoln County is in the south of New Mexico. Its codes spread over about 58 miles from north to south and 42 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 88318 (Corona) and 88343 (Picacho), about 61 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Otero County (49 miles), Chaves County (65 miles), Socorro County (88 miles) and Torrance County (89 miles).

The first three digits

All 15 codes here begin with the same three digits, 883. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 883 is shared with 3 other counties, Guadalupe County, Otero County and Torrance County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 4,307 square miles of land (11,155 sq km). The largest is 88318 (Corona) with 1,229 square miles, and the smallest is 88355 (Ruidoso) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 138 square miles; the middle value for all of New Mexico is 127. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lincoln County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lincoln County ranks 11th among the 33 counties and county-level areas of New Mexico (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 427 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lincoln County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NM and the ZIP code:

  • Ruidoso, NM 88345 (one of 2 codes for Ruidoso; the street decides which)
  • Alto, NM 88312
  • Capitan, NM 88316

Questions about Lincoln County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Ruidoso, NM?

Ruidoso has 2 ZIP codes in Lincoln County: 88345, 88355. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Lincoln County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 15 start with 883.

Which ZIP code in Lincoln County covers the most land?

88318 (Corona), at about 1,229 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
