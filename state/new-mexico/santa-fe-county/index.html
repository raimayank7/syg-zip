Where Santa Fe County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Santa Fe County is in the north of New Mexico. Its codes spread over about 63 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 87015 (Edgewood) and 87567 (Santa Cruz), about 63 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Los Alamos County (25 miles), Sandoval County (40 miles), San Miguel County (42 miles) and Bernalillo County (50 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Santa Fe County begin with 875 (15 codes) and 870 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 875 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Los Alamos County, Rio Arriba County and San Miguel County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 18 codes. Together they come to about 1,636 square miles of land (4,237 sq km). The largest is 87056 (Stanley) with 429 square miles, and the smallest is 87567 (Santa Cruz) with 2.8. The middle-sized code here covers 132 square miles; the middle value for all of New Mexico is 127. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Santa Fe County; a code can reach across its border. 7 codes have no mapped area: 87502 (Santa Fe), 87503 (Santa Fe), 87504 (Santa Fe), 87509 (Santa Fe), 87574 (Tesuque) and 2 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Santa Fe County ranks 6th among the 33 counties and county-level areas of New Mexico (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 427 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Santa Fe County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NM and the ZIP code:

  • Santa Fe, NM 87501 (one of 11 codes for Santa Fe; the street decides which)
  • Cerrillos, NM 87010
  • Edgewood, NM 87015

Santa Fe is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
