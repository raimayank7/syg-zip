Where Sandoval County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Sandoval County is in the north of New Mexico. Its codes spread over about 67 miles from north to south and 63 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 87001 (Algodones) and 87018 (Counselor), about 84 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Los Alamos County (28 miles), Bernalillo County (39 miles), Santa Fe County (40 miles) and Rio Arriba County (50 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Sandoval County begin with 870 (16 codes) and 871 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 870 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Bernalillo County, Cibola County and McKinley County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,740 square miles of land (7,097 sq km). The largest is 87013 (Cuba) with 1,132 square miles, and the smallest is 87083 (Cochiti Lake) with 10.8. The middle-sized code here covers 89.3 square miles; the middle value for all of New Mexico is 127. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sandoval County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Sandoval County ranks 6th among the 33 counties and county-level areas of New Mexico (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 427 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Sandoval County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NM and the ZIP code:

  • Rio Rancho, NM 87124 (one of 2 codes for Rio Rancho; the street decides which)
  • Algodones, NM 87001
  • Bernalillo, NM 87004

ZIP codes named Rio Rancho are also listed under Bernalillo County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site.

Questions about Sandoval County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Rio Rancho, NM?

Rio Rancho has 2 ZIP codes in Sandoval County: 87124, 87144. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Sandoval County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 870, on 16 of the 18 codes.

Which ZIP code in Sandoval County covers the most land?

87013 (Cuba), at about 1,132 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
