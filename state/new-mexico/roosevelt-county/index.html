Where Roosevelt County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Roosevelt County is in the east of New Mexico. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 45 miles, and from east to west about 39 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 88118 (Floyd) and 88125 (Milnesand), about 48 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Bailey County, Texas (33 miles), Cochran County, Texas (37 miles), Curry County (43 miles) and Parmer County, Texas (52 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 881. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 881 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Curry County, DeBaca County and Lea County, and has 23 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 2,899 square miles of land (7,507 sq km). The largest is 88116 (Elida) with 1,435 square miles, and the smallest is 88115 (Dora) with 1.0. The middle-sized code here covers 209 square miles; the middle value for all of New Mexico is 127. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Roosevelt County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 88122 (Kenna) and 88123 (Lingo). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Roosevelt County ranks 16th among the 33 counties and county-level areas of New Mexico (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 427 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Roosevelt County
