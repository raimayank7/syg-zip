Where Otero County is

The ZIP codes of Otero County are found in the south of New Mexico. Its codes spread over about 69 miles from north to south and 49 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 88081 (Chaparral) and 88340 (Mescalero), about 74 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Lincoln County (49 miles), Doña Ana County (67 miles), El Paso County, Texas (82 miles) and Chaves County (88 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Otero County begin with 883 (16 codes) and 880 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 883 is shared with 3 other counties, Guadalupe County, Lincoln County and Torrance County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 4,290 square miles of land (11,111 sq km). The largest is 88344 (Pinon) with 1,112 square miles, and the smallest is 88311 (Alamogordo) with 2.4. The middle-sized code here covers 107 square miles; the middle value for all of New Mexico is 127. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Otero County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Otero County ranks 9th among the 33 counties and county-level areas of New Mexico (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 427 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Otero County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NM and the ZIP code:

  • Alamogordo, NM 88310 (one of 2 codes for Alamogordo; the street decides which)
  • Bent, NM 88314
  • Chaparral, NM 88081

Questions about Otero County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Alamogordo, NM?

Alamogordo has 2 ZIP codes in Otero County: 88310, 88311. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Otero County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 883, on 16 of the 17 codes.

Which ZIP code in Otero County covers the most land?

88344 (Pinon), at about 1,112 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
