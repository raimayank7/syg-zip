Where Doña Ana County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Doña Ana County is in the south-west of New Mexico. Its codes spread over about 65 miles from north to south and 45 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 87936 (Garfield) and 88063 (Sunland Park), about 76 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are El Paso County, Texas (48 miles), Luna County (56 miles), Sierra County (62 miles) and Otero County (67 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Doña Ana County begin with 880 (25 codes) and 879 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 880 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Catron County, Grant County and Hidalgo County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 23 of the 29 codes. Together they come to about 2,604 square miles of land (6,743 sq km). The largest is 88044 (La Mesa) with 594 square miles, and the smallest is 88004 (Las Cruces) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 21.8 square miles; the middle value for all of New Mexico is 127. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Doña Ana County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 88006 (Las Cruces), 88013 (Las Cruces), 88032 (Dona Ana), 88033 (Fairacres), 88054 (Radium Springs) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Doña Ana County ranks 2nd among the 33 counties and county-level areas of New Mexico (equal counts share a rank), with 29 of the 427 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Doña Ana County
