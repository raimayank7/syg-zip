Where Torrance County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Torrance County sits near the middle of New Mexico. Its codes spread over about 44 miles from north to south and 48 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 87061 (Torreon) and 88321 (Encino), about 48 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Bernalillo County (45 miles), Valencia County (49 miles), Santa Fe County (60 miles) and Socorro County (61 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Torrance County begin with 870 (8 codes) and 883 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 870 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Bernalillo County, Cibola County and McKinley County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 3,438 square miles of land (8,905 sq km). The largest is 87036 (Mountainair) with 1,046 square miles, and the smallest is 87070 (Clines Corners) with 17.6. The middle-sized code here covers 370 square miles; the middle value for all of New Mexico is 127. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Torrance County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 87009 (Cedarvale). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Torrance County ranks 19th among the 33 counties and county-level areas of New Mexico (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 427 codes in the state list.
