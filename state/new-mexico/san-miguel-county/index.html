Where San Miguel County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of San Miguel County fall in the north-east of New Mexico. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 38 miles, and from east to west about 83 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 87573 (Tererro) and 88416 (Conchas Dam), about 87 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Mora County (33 miles), Santa Fe County (42 miles), Guadalupe County (44 miles) and Los Alamos County (62 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of San Miguel County begin with 875 (8 codes), 877 (4 codes) and 884 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 875 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Los Alamos County, Rio Arriba County and Santa Fe County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 3,396 square miles of land (8,796 sq km). The largest is 87701 (Las Vegas) with 1,602 square miles, and the smallest is 87583 (Villanueva) with 12.0. The middle-sized code here covers 83.2 square miles; the middle value for all of New Mexico is 127. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of San Miguel County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, San Miguel County ranks 11th among the 33 counties and county-level areas of New Mexico (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 427 codes in the state list.
