Where Curry County is

The ZIP codes of Curry County are found in the east of New Mexico. Its codes spread over about 29 miles from north to south and 33 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 88112 (Broadview) and 88124 (Melrose), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Parmer County, Texas (28 miles), Quay County (42 miles), Roosevelt County (43 miles) and Bailey County, Texas (46 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Curry County starts with 881. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 881 is shared with 4 other counties, such as DeBaca County, Lea County and Quay County, and has 23 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 2,026 square miles of land (5,247 sq km). The largest is 88124 (Melrose) with 749 square miles, and the smallest is 88103 (Cannon Afb) with 4.5. The middle-sized code here covers 295 square miles; the middle value for all of New Mexico is 127. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Curry County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 88102 (Clovis) and 88133 (Saint Vrain). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Curry County ranks 21st among the 33 counties and county-level areas of New Mexico (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 427 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Curry County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NM and the ZIP code:
