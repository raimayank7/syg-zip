Where Chaves County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Chaves County is in the south-east of New Mexico. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 44 miles, and from east to west about 11 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 88201 (Roswell) and 88253 (Lake Arthur), about 44 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Eddy County (52 miles), Lincoln County (65 miles), Lea County (69 miles) and Roosevelt County (74 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Chaves County starts with 882. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 882 is shared with 2 other counties, Eddy County and Lea County, and has 28 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 4,756 square miles of land (12,318 sq km). The largest is 88201 (Roswell) with 2,643 square miles, and the smallest is 88253 (Lake Arthur) with 218. The middle-sized code here covers 516 square miles; the middle value for all of New Mexico is 127. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Chaves County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 88202 (Roswell). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Chaves County ranks 27th among the 33 counties and county-level areas of New Mexico (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 427 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Chaves County
