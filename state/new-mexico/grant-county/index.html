Where Grant County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Grant County fall in the south-west of New Mexico. Its codes spread over about 84 miles from north to south and 55 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 88040 (Hachita) and 88051 (Mule Creek), about 90 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Hidalgo County (57 miles), Luna County (58 miles), Sierra County (59 miles) and Greenlee County, Arizona (61 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Grant County starts with 880. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 880 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Catron County, Doña Ana County and Hidalgo County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 16 of the 18 codes. Together they come to about 4,544 square miles of land (11,769 sq km). The largest is 88040 (Hachita) with 1,163 square miles, and the smallest is 88065 (Tyrone) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 154 square miles; the middle value for all of New Mexico is 127. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Grant County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 88036 (Fort Bayard) and 88062 (Silver City). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Grant County ranks 6th among the 33 counties and county-level areas of New Mexico (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 427 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Grant County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NM and the ZIP code:

  • Silver City, NM 88061 (one of 2 codes for Silver City; the street decides which)
  • Arenas Valley, NM 88022
  • Bayard, NM 88023

Silver City is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
