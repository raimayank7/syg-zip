Where Guadalupe County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Guadalupe County fall in the east of New Mexico. Its codes spread over about 40 miles from north to south and 54 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 88353 (Vaughn) and 88431 (Newkirk), about 63 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are San Miguel County (44 miles), DeBaca County (47 miles), Torrance County (70 miles) and Quay County (71 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Guadalupe County begin with 884 (3 codes), 877 (2 codes) and 883 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 884 is shared with 3 other counties, Quay County, San Miguel County and Union County, and has 21 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 3,157 square miles of land (8,176 sq km). The largest is 88435 (Santa Rosa) with 1,800 square miles, and the smallest is 87724 (La Loma) with 67.4. The middle-sized code here covers 319 square miles; the middle value for all of New Mexico is 127. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Guadalupe County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Guadalupe County ranks 27th among the 33 counties and county-level areas of New Mexico (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 427 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Guadalupe County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NM and the ZIP code:
