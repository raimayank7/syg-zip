Where Bernalillo County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Bernalillo County is in the north of New Mexico. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 31 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 87047 (Sandia Park) and 87151 (Albuquerque), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Valencia County (26 miles), Sandoval County (39 miles), Torrance County (45 miles) and Santa Fe County (50 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Bernalillo County begin with 871 (44 codes) and 870 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 871 is shared with 1 other county, Sandoval County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 24 of the 48 codes. Together they come to about 982 square miles of land (2,542 sq km). The largest is 87121 (Albuquerque) with 337 square miles, and the smallest is 87101 (Albuquerque) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 14.8 square miles; the middle value for all of New Mexico is 127. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bernalillo County; a code can reach across its border. 24 codes have no mapped area: 87103 (Albuquerque), 87115 (Albuquerque), 87119 (Albuquerque), 87125 (Albuquerque), 87131 (Albuquerque) and 19 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of New Mexico has more ZIP codes: Bernalillo County holds 48 of the 427 in the state list, about 11.2%.
