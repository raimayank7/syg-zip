Where Cibola County is

The ZIP codes of Cibola County are found in the north-west of New Mexico. Its codes spread over about 36 miles from north to south and 83 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 87026 (Laguna) and 87315 (Fence Lake), about 84 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are McKinley County (53 miles), Valencia County (58 miles), Bernalillo County (65 miles) and Sandoval County (73 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Cibola County begin with 870 (10 codes) and 873 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 870 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Bernalillo County, McKinley County and Rio Arriba County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,189 square miles of land (5,670 sq km). The largest is 87020 (Grants) with 902 square miles, and the smallest is 87021 (Milan) with 10.0. The middle-sized code here covers 41.5 square miles; the middle value for all of New Mexico is 127. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cibola County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Cibola County ranks 14th among the 33 counties and county-level areas of New Mexico (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 427 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cibola County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NM and the ZIP code:
