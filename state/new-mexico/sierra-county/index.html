Where Sierra County is

The ZIP codes of Sierra County are found in the south-west of New Mexico. Its codes spread over about 42 miles from north to south and 40 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 87654 (Spaceport City) and 87943 (Winston), about 46 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Grant County (59 miles), Doña Ana County (62 miles), Luna County (70 miles) and Socorro County (82 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Sierra County begin with 879 (8 codes), 876 (1 code) and 880 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 879 is shared with 1 other county, Doña Ana County, and has 12 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 3,855 square miles of land (9,984 sq km). The largest is 87943 (Winston) with 1,495 square miles, and the smallest is 87933 (Derry) with 1.1. The middle-sized code here covers 167 square miles; the middle value for all of New Mexico is 127. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sierra County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 87654 (Spaceport City). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Sierra County ranks 16th among the 33 counties and county-level areas of New Mexico (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 427 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Sierra County
