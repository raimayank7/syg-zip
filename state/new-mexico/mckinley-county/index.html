Where McKinley County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of McKinley County fall in the north-west of New Mexico. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 65 miles, and from east to west about 56 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 87325 (Tohatchi) and 87327 (Zuni), about 66 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Apache County, Arizona (51 miles), Cibola County (53 miles), San Juan County (79 miles) and Navajo County, Arizona (104 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of McKinley County begin with 873 (21 codes) and 870 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 873 is shared with 2 other counties, Cibola County and San Juan County, and has 24 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 21 of the 22 codes. Together they come to about 4,268 square miles of land (11,055 sq km). The largest is 87313 (Crownpoint) with 682 square miles, and the smallest is 87365 (Smith Lake) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 160 square miles; the middle value for all of New Mexico is 127. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of McKinley County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 87302 (Gallup). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, McKinley County ranks 4th among the 33 counties and county-level areas of New Mexico (equal counts share a rank), with 22 of the 427 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in McKinley County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NM and the ZIP code:

  • Gallup, NM 87301 (one of 3 codes for Gallup; the street decides which)
  • Brimhall, NM 87310
  • Church Rock, NM 87311

Questions about McKinley County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Gallup, NM?

Gallup has 3 ZIP codes in McKinley County: 87301, 87302, 87305. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in McKinley County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 873, on 21 of the 22 codes.

Which ZIP code in McKinley County covers the most land?

87313 (Crownpoint), at about 682 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
