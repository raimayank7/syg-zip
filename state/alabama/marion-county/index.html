Where Marion County is

The ZIP codes of Marion County are found in the north-west of Alabama. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 35564 (Hackleburg) and 35594 (Winfield), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Franklin County (23 miles), Winston County (24 miles), Fayette County (25 miles) and Lamar County (25 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Marion County starts with 355. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 355 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Fayette County, Franklin County and Lamar County, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 650 square miles of land (1,683 sq km). The largest is 35570 (Hamilton) with 234 square miles, and the smallest is 35543 (Bear Creek) with 37.0. The middle-sized code here covers 81.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Marion County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Marion County ranks 45th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Marion County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Bear Creek, AL 35543
  • Brilliant, AL 35548
  • Guin, AL 35563

Bear Creek is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
