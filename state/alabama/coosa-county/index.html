Where Coosa County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Coosa County is in the east of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 18 miles, and from east to west about 15 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 35183 (Weogufka) and 36026 (Equality), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Tallapoosa County (20 miles), Elmore County (26 miles), Talladega County (28 miles) and Clay County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Coosa County begin with 350 (2 codes), 351 (2 codes) and 360 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 350 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Bibb County, Blount County and Chilton County, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 627 square miles of land (1,624 sq km). The largest is 35136 (Rockford) with 217 square miles, and the smallest is 35089 (Kellyton) with 61.0. The middle-sized code here covers 92.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Coosa County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Coosa County ranks 55th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Coosa County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Equality, AL 36026
  • Goodwater, AL 35072
  • Kellyton, AL 35089
