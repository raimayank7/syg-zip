Where Marengo County is

The ZIP codes of Marengo County are found in the west of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 36738 (Faunsdale) and 36764 (Nanafalia), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Choctaw County (29 miles), Perry County (31 miles), Wilcox County (33 miles) and Hale County (33 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 367. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 367 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Autauga County, Bibb County and Chilton County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 764 square miles of land (1,979 sq km). The largest is 36782 (Sweet Water) with 205 square miles, and the smallest is 36764 (Nanafalia) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 73.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Marengo County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 36745 (Jefferson). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Marengo County ranks 24th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 811 codes in the state list.
