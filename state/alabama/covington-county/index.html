Where Covington County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Covington County is in the south of Alabama. Its codes spread over about 30 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 36455 (Lockhart) and 36474 (Red Level), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Coffee County (30 miles), Conecuh County (36 miles), Crenshaw County (36 miles) and Butler County (36 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Covington County begin with 364 (7 codes) and 360 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 364 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Butler County, Clarke County and Coffee County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 925 square miles of land (2,397 sq km). The largest is 36420 (Andalusia) with 268 square miles, and the smallest is 36038 (Gantt) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 133 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Covington County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Covington County ranks 33rd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Covington County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:
