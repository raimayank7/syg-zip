Where Tallapoosa County is

The ZIP codes of Tallapoosa County are found in the east of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 18 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 35011 (Alexander City) and 36850 (Camp Hill), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Coosa County (20 miles), Clay County (26 miles), Elmore County (30 miles) and Chambers County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Tallapoosa County begin with 368 (3 codes), 350 (2 codes), 360 (1 code) and 362 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 368 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Chambers County, Lee County and Macon County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 704 square miles of land (1,824 sq km). The largest is 35010 (Alexander City) with 223 square miles, and the smallest is 36861 (Jacksons Gap) with 50.1. The middle-sized code here covers 155 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 59.3 square miles, roughly 8% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Tallapoosa County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 35011 (Alexander City) and 36023 (East Tallassee). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Tallapoosa County ranks 39th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Tallapoosa County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Alexander City, AL 35010 (one of 2 codes for Alexander City; the street decides which)
  • Camp Hill, AL 36850
  • Dadeville, AL 36853

Questions about Tallapoosa County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Alexander City, AL?

Alexander City has 2 ZIP codes in Tallapoosa County: 35010, 35011. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Tallapoosa County start with the same digits?

No. They use 4 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 368, on 3 of the 7 codes.

Which ZIP code in Tallapoosa County covers the most land?

35010 (Alexander City), at about 223 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
