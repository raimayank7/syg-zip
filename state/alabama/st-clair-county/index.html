Where St. Clair County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, St. Clair County is in the north of Alabama. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 35054 (Cropwell) and 35987 (Steele), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Blount County (23 miles), Talladega County (24 miles), Etowah County (26 miles) and Calhoun County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of St. Clair County begin with 351 (8 codes), 350 (3 codes) and 359 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 351 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Bibb County, Blount County and Chilton County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 599 square miles of land (1,552 sq km). The largest is 35953 (Ashville) with 129 square miles, and the smallest is 35052 (Cook Springs) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 53.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. Water adds about 21.8 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of St. Clair County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 35182 (Wattsville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, St. Clair County ranks 13th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in St. Clair County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Pell City, AL 35125 (one of 2 codes for Pell City; the street decides which)
  • Ashville, AL 35953
  • Cook Springs, AL 35052

Questions about St. Clair County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Pell City, AL?

Pell City has 2 ZIP codes in St. Clair County: 35125, 35128. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in St. Clair County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 351, on 8 of the 13 codes.

Which ZIP code in St. Clair County covers the most land?

35953 (Ashville), at about 129 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
