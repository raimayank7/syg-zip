Where Blount County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Blount County is in the north of Alabama. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 35031 (Blountsville) and 35133 (Remlap), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cullman County (20 miles), St. Clair County (23 miles), Marshall County (29 miles) and Etowah County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Blount County begin with 350 (5 codes) and 351 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 350 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Bibb County, Chilton County and Clay County, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 483 square miles of land (1,250 sq km). The largest is 35121 (Oneonta) with 144 square miles, and the smallest is 35013 (Allgood) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 48.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Blount County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Blount County ranks 39th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Blount County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Allgood, AL 35013
  • Blountsville, AL 35031
  • Cleveland, AL 35049
