Where Houston County is

The ZIP codes of Houston County are found in the south-east of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 36305 (Dothan) and 36343 (Gordon), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Henry County (19 miles), Dale County (24 miles), Jackson County, Florida (27 miles) and Seminole County, Georgia (28 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Houston County starts with 363. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 363 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Coffee County, Dale County and Geneva County, and has 39 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 582 square miles of land (1,508 sq km). The largest is 36319 (Columbia) with 104 square miles, and the smallest is 36321 (Cowarts) with 6.3. The middle-sized code here covers 61.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Houston County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 36302 (Dothan) and 36304 (Dothan). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Houston County ranks 18th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Houston County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Dothan, AL 36301 (one of 5 codes for Dothan; the street decides which)
  • Ashford, AL 36312
  • Columbia, AL 36319

Questions about Houston County ZIP codes

Do all ZIP codes in Houston County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Houston County covers the most land?
