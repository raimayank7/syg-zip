Where Crenshaw County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Crenshaw County fall in the south-east of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 13 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 36028 (Dozier) and 36041 (Highland Home), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Pike County (21 miles), Butler County (26 miles), Coffee County (31 miles) and Lowndes County (34 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Crenshaw County starts with 360. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 360 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Autauga County, Barbour County and Bullock County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 679 square miles of land (1,759 sq km). The largest is 36009 (Brantley) with 161 square miles, and the smallest is 36071 (Rutledge) with 47.4. The middle-sized code here covers 79.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Crenshaw County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 36062 (Petrey). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Crenshaw County ranks 33rd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Crenshaw County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Dozier, AL 36028
  • Glenwood, AL 36034
