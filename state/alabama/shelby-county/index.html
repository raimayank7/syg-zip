Where Shelby County is

The ZIP codes of Shelby County are found in the north of Alabama. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 29 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 35178 (Vincent) and 35187 (Wilton), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Jefferson County (22 miles), Bibb County (28 miles), Talladega County (28 miles) and Chilton County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Shelby County begin with 351 (12 codes), 350 (6 codes) and 352 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 351 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Bibb County, Blount County and Chilton County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 16 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 817 square miles of land (2,116 sq km). The largest is 35115 (Montevallo) with 123 square miles, and the smallest is 35185 (Westover) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 46.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Shelby County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 35137 (Saginaw), 35144 (Siluria) and 35187 (Wilton). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Shelby County ranks 7th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Shelby County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Alabaster, AL 35007
  • Birmingham, AL 35242
  • Calera, AL 35040

Birmingham shares a name with a place in another part of Alabama; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
