Where Lee County is

The ZIP codes of Lee County are found in the east of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 29 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 36865 (Loachapoka) and 36877 (Smiths Station), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Chambers County (20 miles), Russell County (25 miles), Macon County (27 miles) and Muscogee County, Georgia (28 miles).

The first three digits

All 13 codes here begin with the same three digits, 368. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 368 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Chambers County, Macon County and Russell County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 692 square miles of land (1,793 sq km). The largest is 36804 (Opelika) with 197 square miles, and the smallest is 36865 (Loachapoka) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 78.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lee County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 36802 (Opelika), 36803 (Opelika), 36831 (Auburn) and 36872 (Valley). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lee County ranks 13th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lee County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Opelika, AL 36801 (one of 4 codes for Opelika; the street decides which)
  • Auburn, AL 36830 (one of 3 codes for Auburn; the street decides which)
  • Auburn University, AL 36849

Valley shares a name with a place in another part of Alabama; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
