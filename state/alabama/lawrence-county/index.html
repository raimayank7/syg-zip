Where Lawrence County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Lawrence County is in the north-west of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 13 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 35643 (Hillsboro) and 35651 (Mount Hope), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Colbert County (23 miles), Morgan County (24 miles), Limestone County (26 miles) and Lauderdale County (27 miles).

The first three digits

All 5 codes here begin with the same three digits, 356. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 356 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Colbert County, Franklin County and Lauderdale County, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 514 square miles of land (1,331 sq km). The largest is 35650 (Moulton) with 212 square miles, and the smallest is 35651 (Mount Hope) with 40.4. The middle-sized code here covers 80.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. Water adds about 26.4 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lawrence County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lawrence County ranks 55th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lawrence County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Courtland, AL 35618
  • Hillsboro, AL 35643
  • Moulton, AL 35650

Courtland is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
