Where Jackson County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Jackson County is in the north-east of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 31 miles, and from east to west about 39 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 35755 (Langston) and 35958 (Bryant), about 42 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are DeKalb County (22 miles), Franklin County, Tennessee (28 miles), Dade County, Georgia (31 miles) and Madison County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Jackson County begin with 357 (16 codes) and 359 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 357 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Limestone County, Madison County and Marshall County, and has 35 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,143 square miles of land (2,961 sq km). The largest is 35768 (Scottsboro) with 172 square miles, and the smallest is 35979 (Higdon) with 11.6. The middle-sized code here covers 52.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. Water adds about 63.4 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jackson County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Jackson County ranks 7th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jackson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Scottsboro, AL 35768 (one of 2 codes for Scottsboro; the street decides which)
  • Bridgeport, AL 35740
  • Bryant, AL 35958

Questions about Jackson County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Scottsboro, AL?

Scottsboro has 2 ZIP codes in Jackson County: 35768, 35769. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Jackson County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 357, on 16 of the 19 codes.

Which ZIP code in Jackson County covers the most land?

35768 (Scottsboro), at about 172 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
