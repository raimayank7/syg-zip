Where Calhoun County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Calhoun County fall in the north-east of Alabama. Its codes spread over about 18 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 36260 (Eastaboga) and 36272 (Piedmont), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Cleburne County (22 miles), Etowah County (23 miles), St. Clair County (29 miles) and Cherokee County (33 miles).

The first three digits

All 17 codes here begin with the same three digits, 362. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 362 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Cherokee County, Clay County and Cleburne County, and has 35 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 766 square miles of land (1,984 sq km). The largest is 36272 (Piedmont) with 318 square miles, and the smallest is 36277 (Weaver) with 7.7. The middle-sized code here covers 34.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Calhoun County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 36202 (Anniston), 36204 (Anniston), 36253 (Bynum), 36254 (Choccolocco) and 36257 (De Armanville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Calhoun County ranks 10th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Calhoun County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Anniston, AL 36201 (one of 6 codes for Anniston; the street decides which)
  • Alexandria, AL 36250
  • Bynum, AL 36253
