Where Washington County is

The ZIP codes of Washington County are found in the south-west of Alabama. Its codes spread over about 34 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 36513 (Calvert) and 36558 (Millry), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Clarke County (31 miles), Greene County, Mississippi (32 miles), Wayne County, Mississippi (32 miles) and George County, Mississippi (46 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Washington County starts with 365. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 365 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Baldwin County, Clarke County and Escambia County, and has 65 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,064 square miles of land (2,756 sq km). The largest is 36558 (Millry) with 236 square miles, and the smallest is 36556 (Malcolm) with 7.0. The middle-sized code here covers 64.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Washington County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Washington County ranks 11th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 811 codes in the state list.
