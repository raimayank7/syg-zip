Where Tuscaloosa County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Tuscaloosa County is in the north-west of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 35 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 35440 (Abernant) and 35480 (Ralph), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Bibb County (30 miles), Pickens County (33 miles), Greene County (36 miles) and Hale County (37 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Tuscaloosa County starts with 354. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 354 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Greene County, Hale County and Pickens County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 18 of the 27 codes. Together they come to about 1,081 square miles of land (2,799 sq km). The largest is 35475 (Northport) with 202 square miles, and the smallest is 35487 (Tuscaloosa) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 55.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Tuscaloosa County; a code can reach across its border. 9 codes have no mapped area: 35402 (Tuscaloosa), 35403 (Tuscaloosa), 35407 (Tuscaloosa), 35440 (Abernant), 35449 (Coaling) and 4 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Tuscaloosa County ranks 5th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 27 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Tuscaloosa County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Tuscaloosa, AL 35401 (one of 9 codes for Tuscaloosa; the street decides which)
  • Northport, AL 35473 (one of 3 codes for Northport; the street decides which)
  • Abernant, AL 35440
