Where Dallas County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Dallas County fall close to the middle of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 30 miles, and from east to west about 29 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 36758 (Plantersville) and 36773 (Safford), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Perry County (23 miles), Wilcox County (28 miles), Autauga County (31 miles) and Lowndes County (32 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 367. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 367 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Autauga County, Bibb County and Chilton County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 1,412 square miles of land (3,656 sq km). The largest is 36701 (Selma) with 277 square miles, and the smallest is 36773 (Safford) with 69.8. The middle-sized code here covers 148 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Dallas County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 36702 (Selma). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Dallas County ranks 24th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Dallas County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Selma, AL 36701 (one of 3 codes for Selma; the street decides which)
  • Marion Junction, AL 36759
  • Minter, AL 36761

Selma is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
