Where Lauderdale County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Lauderdale County fall in the north-west of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 8.7 miles, and from east to west about 39 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 35610 (Anderson) and 35677 (Waterloo), about 39 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Colbert County (13 miles), Lawrence County, Tennessee (21 miles), Wayne County, Tennessee (24 miles) and Lawrence County (27 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Lauderdale County starts with 356. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 356 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Colbert County, Franklin County and Lawrence County, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 682 square miles of land (1,765 sq km). The largest is 35633 (Florence) with 208 square miles, and the smallest is 35632 (Florence) with 0.09. The middle-sized code here covers 71.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. Water adds about 51.6 square miles, roughly 7% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lauderdale County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 35617 (Cloverdale) and 35631 (Florence). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lauderdale County ranks 20th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lauderdale County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Florence, AL 35630 (one of 5 codes for Florence; the street decides which)
  • Anderson, AL 35610
  • Cloverdale, AL 35617

Florence is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 16 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
