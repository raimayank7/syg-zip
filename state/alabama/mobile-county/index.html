Where Mobile County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Mobile County is in the south-west of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 74 miles, and from east to west about 109 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 36421 (Andalusia) and 36541 (Grand Bay), about 122 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Baldwin County (24 miles), George County, Mississippi (34 miles), Jackson County, Mississippi (35 miles) and Greene County, Mississippi (47 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Mobile County begin with 366 (35 codes), 365 (18 codes) and 364 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 366, so those three digits alone point to Mobile County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 39 of the 54 codes. Together they come to about 1,432 square miles of land (3,710 sq km). The largest is 36522 (Citronelle) with 233 square miles, and the smallest is 36652 (Mobile) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 12.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 79.7 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Mobile County; a code can reach across its border. 15 codes have no mapped area: 36590 (Theodore), 36601 (Mobile), 36616 (Mobile), 36628 (Mobile), 36633 (Mobile) and 10 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Mobile County ranks 2nd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 54 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Mobile County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Mobile, AL 36601 (one of 34 codes for Mobile; the street decides which)
  • Theodore, AL 36582 (one of 2 codes for Theodore; the street decides which)
  • Andalusia, AL 36421

Andalusia shares a name with a place in another part of Alabama; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
