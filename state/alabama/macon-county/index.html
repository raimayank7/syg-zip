Where Macon County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Macon County is in the east of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 13 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 36031 (Fort Davis) and 36866 (Notasulga), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Bullock County (18 miles), Lee County (27 miles), Elmore County (27 miles) and Montgomery County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Macon County begin with 360 (6 codes) and 368 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 360 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Autauga County, Barbour County and Bullock County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 453 square miles of land (1,173 sq km). The largest is 36083 (Tuskegee) with 223 square miles, and the smallest is 36088 (Tuskegee Institute) with 2.9. The middle-sized code here covers 64.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Macon County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 36087 (Tuskegee Institute). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Macon County ranks 39th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Macon County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Tuskegee Institute, AL 36087 (one of 2 codes for Tuskegee Institute; the street decides which)
  • Fort Davis, AL 36031
  • Hardaway, AL 36039

Questions about Macon County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Tuskegee Institute, AL?

Tuskegee Institute has 2 ZIP codes in Macon County: 36087, 36088. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Macon County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 360, on 6 of the 7 codes.

Which ZIP code in Macon County covers the most land?

36083 (Tuskegee), at about 223 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
