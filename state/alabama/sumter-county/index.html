Where Sumter County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Sumter County is in the west of Alabama. Its codes spread over about 32 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 35477 (Panola) and 36907 (Cuba), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Greene County (22 miles), Kemper County, Mississippi (26 miles), Lauderdale County, Mississippi (28 miles) and Hale County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Sumter County begin with 354 (5 codes) and 369 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 354 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Greene County, Hale County and Pickens County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 806 square miles of land (2,088 sq km). The largest is 35470 (Livingston) with 274 square miles, and the smallest is 35477 (Panola) with 9.8. The middle-sized code here covers 87.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sumter County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Sumter County ranks 33rd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Sumter County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

Questions about Sumter County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Bellamy, AL?

36901 is the ZIP code listed for Bellamy in Sumter County.

Do all ZIP codes in Sumter County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 354, on 5 of the 8 codes.

Which ZIP code in Sumter County covers the most land?
