Where Limestone County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Limestone County is in the north of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 24 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 35647 (Lester) and 35649 (Mooresville), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Morgan County (21 miles), Madison County (21 miles), Giles County, Tennessee (23 miles) and Lawrence County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Limestone County begin with 356 (9 codes) and 357 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 356 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Colbert County, Franklin County and Lauderdale County, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 467 square miles of land (1,208 sq km). The largest is 35611 (Athens) with 115 square miles, and the smallest is 35615 (Belle Mina) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 49.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. Water adds about 38.0 square miles, roughly 8% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Limestone County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 35612 (Athens) and 35742 (Capshaw). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Limestone County ranks 20th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Limestone County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Athens, AL 35611 (one of 4 codes for Athens; the street decides which)
  • Ardmore, AL 35739
  • Belle Mina, AL 35615

Athens is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 13 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Limestone County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Athens, AL?

Athens has 4 ZIP codes in Limestone County: 35611, 35612, 35613, 35614. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Limestone County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 356, on 9 of the 11 codes.

Which ZIP code in Limestone County covers the most land?

35611 (Athens), at about 115 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
