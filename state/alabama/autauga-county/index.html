Where Autauga County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Autauga County fall close to the middle of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 36066 (Prattville) and 36749 (Jones), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Chilton County (23 miles), Elmore County (24 miles), Lowndes County (27 miles) and Montgomery County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Autauga County begin with 360 (7 codes) and 367 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 360 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Barbour County, Bullock County and Butler County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 538 square miles of land (1,394 sq km). The largest is 36067 (Prattville) with 244 square miles, and the smallest is 36008 (Booth) with 0.9. The middle-sized code here covers 61.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Autauga County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 36068 (Prattville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Autauga County ranks 33rd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Autauga County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Prattville, AL 36066 (one of 3 codes for Prattville; the street decides which)
  • Autaugaville, AL 36003
  • Billingsley, AL 36006

Questions about Autauga County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Prattville, AL?

Prattville has 3 ZIP codes in Autauga County: 36066, 36067, 36068. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Autauga County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 360, on 7 of the 8 codes.

Which ZIP code in Autauga County covers the most land?

36067 (Prattville), at about 244 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
