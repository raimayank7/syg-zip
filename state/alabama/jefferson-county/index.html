Where Jefferson County is

The ZIP codes of Jefferson County are found in the north of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 39 miles, and from east to west about 34 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 35111 (Mc Calla) and 35180 (Warrior), about 43 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Shelby County (22 miles), Walker County (30 miles), St. Clair County (32 miles) and Blount County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Jefferson County begin with 352 (61 codes), 350 (17 codes) and 351 (14 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 352 is shared with 1 other county, Shelby County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 55 of the 92 codes. Together they come to about 1,177 square miles of land (3,049 sq km). The largest is 35006 (Adger) with 104 square miles, and the smallest is 35249 (Birmingham) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 12.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jefferson County; a code can reach across its border. 37 codes have no mapped area: 35015 (Alton), 35021 (Bessemer), 35048 (Clay), 35123 (Palmerdale), 35139 (Sayre) and 32 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Alabama has more ZIP codes: Jefferson County holds 92 of the 811 in the state list, about 11.3%.

Writing an address in Jefferson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Birmingham, AL 35201 (one of 61 codes for Birmingham; the street decides which)
  • Bessemer, AL 35020 (one of 4 codes for Bessemer; the street decides which)
  • Adamsville, AL 35005

ZIP codes named Birmingham are also listed under Shelby County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Birmingham is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
