Where Etowah County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Etowah County is in the north-east of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 35903 (Gadsden) and 35990 (Walnut Grove), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Calhoun County (23 miles), Marshall County (23 miles), St. Clair County (26 miles) and Cherokee County (29 miles).

The first three digits

All 11 codes here begin with the same three digits, 359. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 359 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Cherokee County, DeKalb County and Jackson County, and has 41 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 517 square miles of land (1,340 sq km). The largest is 35952 (Altoona) with 109 square miles, and the smallest is 35990 (Walnut Grove) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 45.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Etowah County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 35902 (Gadsden). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Etowah County ranks 20th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Etowah County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Gadsden, AL 35901 (one of 6 codes for Gadsden; the street decides which)
  • Altoona, AL 35952
  • Attalla, AL 35954

Gadsden is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
