Alabama ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 811 ZIP codes for Alabama, about 2.0% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 19th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 67 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Jefferson County (92), Mobile County (54), Montgomery County (42), Madison County (38) and Tuscaloosa County (27). At the other end, 1 has only one or two codes, for example Perry County.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Alabama uses 19 of these prefixes, from 350 to 369. The busiest are 360 (66 codes, most of them for Prattville), 365 (65 codes, most of them for Atmore), 350 (63 codes, most of them for Bessemer) and 352 (62 codes, most of them for Birmingham). The first digit 3 is shared with Florida, Georgia, Mississippi and Tennessee.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 656 of the 811 codes. The largest is 36426 (Brewton, Escambia County) at 498 square miles and the smallest is 36101 (Montgomery) at 0.01; the middle value is 60.2 square miles. The other 155 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 35739 (Ardmore) and the southernmost 36547 (Gulf Shores); the one farthest east is 36856 (Fort Mitchell) and farthest west 36913 (Melvin).

Place names and addresses in Alabama

Of the 575 place names in the list, 527 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Birmingham (62), Mobile (34), Montgomery (33) and Huntsville (24). 3 place names appear under more than one county, among them Andalusia, Birmingham and Valley: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters AL and the code, as in Birmingham, AL 35201. The name of the county is left out.

Questions about Alabama ZIP codes

What ZIP codes does Alabama use?

In this list, Alabama ZIP codes run from 35004 to 36925 and start with 3. A few codes inside that range can belong to a neighbouring state near the border.

Which place in Alabama has the most ZIP codes?

Birmingham, with 62 codes in this list.
