Where Madison County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Madison County is in the north of Alabama. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 35760 (New Hope) and 35773 (Toney), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Limestone County (21 miles), Morgan County (25 miles), Lincoln County, Tennessee (27 miles) and Jackson County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Madison County begin with 358 (24 codes) and 357 (14 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 358, so those three digits alone point to Madison County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 23 of the 38 codes. Together they come to about 891 square miles of land (2,309 sq km). The largest is 35761 (New Market) with 117 square miles, and the smallest is 35896 (Huntsville) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 28.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Madison County; a code can reach across its border. 15 codes have no mapped area: 35762 (Normal), 35767 (Ryland), 35804 (Huntsville), 35807 (Huntsville), 35809 (Huntsville) and 10 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Madison County ranks 4th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 38 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Madison County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Huntsville, AL 35801 (one of 24 codes for Huntsville; the street decides which)
  • Madison, AL 35756 (one of 3 codes for Madison; the street decides which)
  • Brownsboro, AL 35741

Huntsville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
