Where Elmore County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Elmore County is in the east of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 13 miles, and from east to west about 29 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 36022 (Deatsville) and 36078 (Tallassee), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Montgomery County (22 miles), Autauga County (24 miles), Coosa County (26 miles) and Macon County (27 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 360. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 360 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Autauga County, Barbour County and Bullock County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 674 square miles of land (1,745 sq km). The largest is 36078 (Tallassee) with 177 square miles, and the smallest is 36020 (Coosada) with 8.4. The middle-sized code here covers 78.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. Water adds about 27.3 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Elmore County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 36045 (Kent). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Elmore County ranks 24th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Elmore County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:
