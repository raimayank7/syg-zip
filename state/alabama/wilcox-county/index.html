Where Wilcox County is

The ZIP codes of Wilcox County are found in the south-west of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 35 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 36741 (Furman) and 36751 (Lower Peach Tree), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Dallas County (28 miles), Marengo County (33 miles), Monroe County (34 miles) and Clarke County (35 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Wilcox County begin with 367 (11 codes) and 364 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 367 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Autauga County, Bibb County and Chilton County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,038 square miles of land (2,689 sq km). The largest is 36726 (Camden) with 291 square miles, and the smallest is 36753 (Mc Williams) with 1.6. The middle-sized code here covers 49.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wilcox County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Wilcox County ranks 18th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Wilcox County
