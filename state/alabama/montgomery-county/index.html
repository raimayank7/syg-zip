Where Montgomery County is

The ZIP codes of Montgomery County are found in the east of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 32 miles, and from east to west about 24 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 36046 (Lapine) and 36110 (Montgomery), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Elmore County (22 miles), Lowndes County (23 miles), Autauga County (28 miles) and Macon County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Montgomery County begin with 361 (33 codes) and 360 (9 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 361, so those three digits alone point to Montgomery County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 24 of the 42 codes. Together they come to about 923 square miles of land (2,391 sq km). The largest is 36069 (Ramer) with 150 square miles, and the smallest is 36101 (Montgomery) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 13.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Montgomery County; a code can reach across its border. 18 codes have no mapped area: 36065 (Pine Level), 36102 (Montgomery), 36103 (Montgomery), 36118 (Montgomery), 36119 (Montgomery) and 13 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Montgomery County ranks 3rd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 42 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Montgomery County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Montgomery, AL 36101 (one of 33 codes for Montgomery; the street decides which)
  • Cecil, AL 36013
  • Grady, AL 36036

Montgomery is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 10 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
