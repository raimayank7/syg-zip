Where Butler County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Butler County fall in the south of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 13 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 36030 (Forest Home) and 36456 (Mc Kenzie), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Crenshaw County (26 miles), Conecuh County (30 miles), Lowndes County (32 miles) and Covington County (36 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Butler County begin with 360 (4 codes) and 364 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 360 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Autauga County, Barbour County and Bullock County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 4 of the 5 codes. Together they come to about 804 square miles of land (2,082 sq km). The largest is 36037 (Greenville) with 375 square miles, and the smallest is 36030 (Forest Home) with 74.5. The middle-sized code here covers 177 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Butler County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 36015 (Chapman). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Butler County ranks 55th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Butler County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Chapman, AL 36015
  • Forest Home, AL 36030
  • Georgiana, AL 36033
