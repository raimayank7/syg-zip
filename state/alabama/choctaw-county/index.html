Where Choctaw County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Choctaw County fall in the west of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 37 miles, and from east to west about 24 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 36919 (Silas) and 36922 (Ward), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Marengo County (29 miles), Clarke County, Mississippi (31 miles), Clarke County (35 miles) and Lauderdale County, Mississippi (37 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 369. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 369 is shared with 1 other county, Sumter County, and has 13 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 936 square miles of land (2,423 sq km). The largest is 36904 (Butler) with 231 square miles, and the smallest is 36910 (Jachin) with 1.6. The middle-sized code here covers 87.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Choctaw County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Choctaw County ranks 24th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Choctaw County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Butler, AL 36904
  • Gilbertown, AL 36908
  • Jachin, AL 36910

Butler is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 12 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
