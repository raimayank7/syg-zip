Where Baldwin County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Baldwin County is in the south-west of Alabama. Its codes spread over about 68 miles from north to south and 27 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 36547 (Gulf Shores) and 36550 (Little River), about 68 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Mobile County (24 miles), Escambia County, Florida (29 miles), Santa Rosa County, Florida (40 miles) and Escambia County (43 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Baldwin County starts with 365. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 365 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Clarke County, Escambia County and Mobile County, and has 65 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 23 of the 26 codes. Together they come to about 1,428 square miles of land (3,698 sq km). The largest is 36507 (Bay Minette) with 343 square miles, and the smallest is 36564 (Point Clear) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 37.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. Water adds about 79.4 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Baldwin County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 36533 (Fairhope), 36536 (Foley) and 36577 (Spanish Fort). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Baldwin County ranks 6th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 26 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Baldwin County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Fairhope, AL 36532 (one of 2 codes for Fairhope; the street decides which)
  • Foley, AL 36535 (one of 2 codes for Foley; the street decides which)
  • Gulf Shores, AL 36542 (one of 2 codes for Gulf Shores; the street decides which)
