Where Russell County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Russell County fall in the east of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 24 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 36860 (Hurtsboro) and 36867 (Phenix City), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Muscogee County, Georgia (17 miles), Chattahoochee County, Georgia (21 miles), Stewart County, Georgia (23 miles) and Lee County (25 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Russell County starts with 368. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 368 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Chambers County, Lee County and Macon County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 634 square miles of land (1,643 sq km). The largest is 36860 (Hurtsboro) with 153 square miles, and the smallest is 36851 (Cottonton) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 61.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Russell County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 36859 (Holy Trinity) and 36868 (Phenix City). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Russell County ranks 20th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Russell County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Phenix City, AL 36867 (one of 4 codes for Phenix City; the street decides which)
  • Cottonton, AL 36851
  • Fort Mitchell, AL 36856
