Where Clay County is

The ZIP codes of Clay County are found in the north-east of Alabama. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 24 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 35082 (Hollins) and 36258 (Delta), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Talladega County (22 miles), Randolph County (24 miles), Tallapoosa County (26 miles) and Coosa County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Clay County begin with 362 (5 codes) and 350 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 362 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Calhoun County, Cherokee County and Cleburne County, and has 35 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 523 square miles of land (1,355 sq km). The largest is 36266 (Lineville) with 197 square miles, and the smallest is 36267 (Millerville) with 1.0. The middle-sized code here covers 86.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Alabama is 60.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clay County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Clay County ranks 45th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Alabama (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 811 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clay County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AL and the ZIP code:

  • Ashland, AL 36251
  • Cragford, AL 36255
  • Delta, AL 36258

Ashland is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 17 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
