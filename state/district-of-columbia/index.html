District of Columbia ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 277 ZIP codes for District of Columbia, about 0.7% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 45th among the 51 state lists by size.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. District of Columbia uses 6 of these prefixes, from 200 to 569. The busiest are 200 (71 codes, most of them for Washington), 205 (70 codes, most of them for Washington), 202 (52 codes, most of them for Washington) and 204 (50 codes, most of them for Washington). By first digit, the codes split into 271 starting with 2 and 6 starting with 5. Those first digits are shared with Iowa, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, North Carolina, North Dakota, South Carolina, South Dakota, Virginia, West Virginia and Wisconsin.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 57 of the 277 codes. The largest is 20019 (Washington, District of Columbia) at 6.1 square miles and the smallest is 20390 (Washington) at under 0.01; the middle value is 0.1 square miles. The other 220 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 20012 (Washington) and the southernmost 20375 (Washington); the one farthest east is 20019 (Washington) and farthest west 20016 (Washington).

Place names and addresses in District of Columbia

Of the 3 place names in the list, 1 has a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Washington (271) and Washington Navy Yard (5). In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters DC and the code, as in Washington, DC 20001.
