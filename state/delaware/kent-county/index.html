Where Kent County is

Its codes spread over about 26 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 19954 (Houston) and 19977 (Smyrna), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Caroline County, Maryland (20 miles), Queen Anne's County, Maryland (26 miles), Kent County, Maryland (26 miles) and Cumberland County, New Jersey (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 21 codes here begin with the same three digits, 199. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 199 is shared with 1 other county, Sussex County, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 21 codes. Together they come to about 544 square miles of land (1,408 sq km). The largest is 19952 (Harrington) with 91.0 square miles, and the smallest is 19955 (Kenton) with 0.10. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Kent County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 19903 (Dover), 19905 (Dover), 19906 (Dover) and 19961 (Little Creek). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Kent County ranks 3rd among the 3 counties and county-level areas of Delaware (equal counts share a rank), with 21 of the 96 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Kent County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters DE and the ZIP code:

  • Dover, DE 19901 (one of 5 codes for Dover; the street decides which)
  • Camden Wyoming, DE 19934
  • Cheswold, DE 19936

Dover is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 17 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
