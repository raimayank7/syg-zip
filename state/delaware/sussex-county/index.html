Where Sussex County is

Its codes spread over about 32 miles from north to south and 31 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 19944 (Fenwick Island) and 19950 (Greenwood), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Wicomico County, Maryland (26 miles), Worcester County, Maryland (27 miles), Caroline County, Maryland (34 miles) and Kent County (35 miles).

The first three digits

All 23 codes here begin with the same three digits, 199. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 199 is shared with 1 other county, Kent County, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 22 of the 23 codes. Together they come to about 1,010 square miles of land (2,617 sq km). The largest is 19947 (Georgetown) with 119 square miles, and the smallest is 19931 (Bethel) with 1.2. Water adds about 58.2 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sussex County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 19969 (Nassau). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Sussex County ranks 2nd among the 3 counties and county-level areas of Delaware (equal counts share a rank), with 23 of the 96 codes in the state list.
