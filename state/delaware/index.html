Delaware ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 96 ZIP codes for Delaware, about 0.2% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 50th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 3 counties and county-level areas: New Castle County (52), Sussex County (23) and Kent County (21).

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Delaware uses 3 of these prefixes, from 197 to 199. The busiest are 199 (44 codes, most of them for Dover), 197 (27 codes, most of them for Newark) and 198 (25 codes, most of them for Wilmington). The first digit 1 is shared with New York and Pennsylvania.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 68 of the 96 codes. The largest is 19947 (Georgetown, Sussex County) at 119 square miles and the smallest is 19955 (Kenton) at 0.10; the middle value is 13.5 square miles. The other 28 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 19810 (Wilmington) and the southernmost 19975 (Selbyville); the one farthest east is 19944 (Fenwick Island) and farthest west 19716 (Newark).

Place names and addresses in Delaware

Of the 57 place names in the list, 53 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Wilmington (25), Newark (10), Dover (5) and New Castle (3). In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters DE and the code, as in Wilmington, DE 19801. The name of the county is left out.
