Where New Castle County is

Its codes spread over about 30 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 19734 (Townsend) and 19810 (Wilmington), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Salem County, New Jersey (13 miles), Cecil County, Maryland (19 miles), Delaware County, Pennsylvania (22 miles) and Chester County, Pennsylvania (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of New Castle County begin with 197 (27 codes) and 198 (25 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 197, so those three digits alone point to New Castle County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 29 of the 52 codes. Together they come to about 399 square miles of land (1,034 sq km). The largest is 19734 (Townsend) with 79.9 square miles, and the smallest is 19733 (Saint Georges) with 0.1. Water adds about 20.1 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of New Castle County; a code can reach across its border. 23 codes have no mapped area: 19708 (Kirkwood), 19712 (Newark), 19714 (Newark), 19715 (Newark), 19718 (Newark) and 18 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Delaware has more ZIP codes: New Castle County holds 52 of the 96 in the state list, about 54.2%.

Writing an address in New Castle County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters DE and the ZIP code:

  • Wilmington, DE 19801 (one of 25 codes for Wilmington; the street decides which)
  • Newark, DE 19702 (one of 10 codes for Newark; the street decides which)
  • New Castle, DE 19720 (one of 3 codes for New Castle; the street decides which)

Wilmington is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
