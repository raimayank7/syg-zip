Where Essex County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Essex County fall in the north-east of Vermont. Its codes spread over about 41 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 05824 (Concord) and 05902 (Beecher Falls), about 43 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Coos County, New Hampshire (19 miles), Caledonia County (26 miles), Orleans County (26 miles) and Lamoille County (46 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Essex County begin with 059 (7 codes) and 058 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 059, so those three digits alone point to Essex County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 698 square miles of land (1,807 sq km). The largest is 05905 (Guildhall) with 186 square miles, and the smallest is 05904 (Gilman) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 50.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Vermont is 31.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Essex County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Essex County ranks 13th among the 14 counties and county-level areas of Vermont (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 308 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Essex County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VT and the ZIP code:
