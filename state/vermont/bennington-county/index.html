Where Bennington County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Bennington County is in the south-west of Vermont. Its codes spread over about 35 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 05152 (Peru) and 05261 (Pownal), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Windham County (22 miles), Washington County, New York (25 miles), Rensselaer County, New York (32 miles) and Saratoga County, New York (35 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Bennington County begin with 052 (11 codes), 053 (3 codes), 057 (2 codes) and 051 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 052, so those three digits alone point to Bennington County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 634 square miles of land (1,643 sq km). The largest is 05201 (Bennington) with 99.1 square miles, and the smallest is 05254 (Manchester) with 2.5. The middle-sized code here covers 36.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Vermont is 31.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bennington County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Bennington County ranks 11th among the 14 counties and county-level areas of Vermont (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 308 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bennington County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VT and the ZIP code:

  • Arlington, VT 05250
  • Bennington, VT 05201
  • Bondville, VT 05340

Arlington is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 20 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
