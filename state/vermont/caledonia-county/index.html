Where Caledonia County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Caledonia County is in the north-east of Vermont. Its codes spread over about 32 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 05046 (Groton) and 05871 (West Burke), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Essex County (26 miles), Orleans County (27 miles), Washington County (27 miles) and Lamoille County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Caledonia County begin with 058 (18 codes) and 050 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 058 is shared with 2 other counties, Essex County and Orleans County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 22 codes. Together they come to about 628 square miles of land (1,626 sq km). The largest is 05819 (Saint Johnsbury) with 88.8 square miles, and the smallest is 05050 (Mc Indoe Falls) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 32.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Vermont is 31.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Caledonia County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 05838 (East Saint Johnsbury), 05848 (Lower Waterford), 05849 (Lyndon), 05861 (Passumpsic) and 05863 (Saint Johnsbury Center). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Caledonia County ranks 8th among the 14 counties and county-level areas of Vermont (equal counts share a rank), with 22 of the 308 codes in the state list.
