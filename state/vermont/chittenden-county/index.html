Where Chittenden County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Chittenden County fall in the north-west of Vermont. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 05445 (Charlotte) and 05494 (Westford), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Grand Isle County (20 miles), Lamoille County (24 miles), Franklin County (29 miles) and Washington County (29 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Chittenden County starts with 054. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 054 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Addison County, Franklin County and Grand Isle County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 18 of the 26 codes. Together they come to about 526 square miles of land (1,362 sq km). The largest is 05468 (Milton) with 62.5 square miles, and the smallest is 05439 (Colchester) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 33.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Vermont is 31.4. Water adds about 83.2 square miles, roughly 14% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Chittenden County; a code can reach across its border. 8 codes have no mapped area: 05402 (Burlington), 05406 (Burlington), 05407 (South Burlington), 05449 (Colchester), 05451 (Essex) and 3 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Chittenden County ranks 5th among the 14 counties and county-level areas of Vermont (equal counts share a rank), with 26 of the 308 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Chittenden County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VT and the ZIP code:

  • Burlington, VT 05401 (one of 5 codes for Burlington; the street decides which)
  • Colchester, VT 05439 (one of 3 codes for Colchester; the street decides which)
  • Essex Junction, VT 05452 (one of 2 codes for Essex Junction; the street decides which)

Burlington is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 20 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Chittenden County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Burlington, VT?

Burlington has 5 ZIP codes in Chittenden County: 05401, 05402, 05405, 05406, 05408. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Chittenden County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 26 start with 054.

Which ZIP code in Chittenden County covers the most land?

05468 (Milton), at about 62.5 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
