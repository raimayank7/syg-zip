Where Windsor County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Windsor County is in the south of Vermont. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 41 miles, and from east to west about 26 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 05156 (Springfield) and 05767 (Rochester), about 43 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Sullivan County, New Hampshire (22 miles), Rutland County (26 miles), Orange County (28 miles) and Grafton County, New Hampshire (37 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Windsor County begin with 050 (26 codes), 051 (8 codes) and 057 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 050 is shared with 2 other counties, Caledonia County and Orange County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 35 of the 37 codes. Together they come to about 988 square miles of land (2,558 sq km). The largest is 05143 (Chester) with 114 square miles, and the smallest is 05009 (White River Junction) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 26.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Vermont is 31.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Windsor County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 05047 (Hartford) and 05049 (Hartland Four Corners). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Vermont has more ZIP codes: Windsor County holds 37 of the 308 in the state list, about 12%.

Writing an address in Windsor County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VT and the ZIP code:

  • White River Junction, VT 05001 (one of 2 codes for White River Junction; the street decides which)
  • Ascutney, VT 05030
  • Barnard, VT 05031

Questions about Windsor County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for White River Junction, VT?

White River Junction has 2 ZIP codes in Windsor County: 05001, 05009. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Windsor County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 050, on 26 of the 37 codes.

Which ZIP code in Windsor County covers the most land?

05143 (Chester), at about 114 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
