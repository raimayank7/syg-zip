Where Washington County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Washington County is in the north of Vermont. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 24 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 05647 (Cabot) and 05674 (Warren), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Orange County (24 miles), Lamoille County (24 miles), Caledonia County (27 miles) and Chittenden County (29 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Washington County starts with 056. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 056 is shared with 3 other counties, Addison County, Lamoille County and Orange County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 23 of the 30 codes. Together they come to about 694 square miles of land (1,798 sq km). The largest is 05676 (Waterbury) with 93.8 square miles, and the smallest is 05664 (Northfield Falls) with 0.02. The middle-sized code here covers 24.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Vermont is 31.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Washington County; a code can reach across its border. 7 codes have no mapped area: 05601 (Montpelier), 05603 (Montpelier), 05604 (Montpelier), 05609 (Montpelier), 05620 (Montpelier) and 2 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Washington County ranks 2nd among the 14 counties and county-level areas of Vermont (equal counts share a rank), with 30 of the 308 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Washington County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VT and the ZIP code:

  • Montpelier, VT 05601 (one of 7 codes for Montpelier; the street decides which)
  • Waterbury, VT 05671 (one of 2 codes for Waterbury; the street decides which)
  • Adamant, VT 05640

Montpelier is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
