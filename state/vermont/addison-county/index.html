Where Addison County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Addison County is in the west of Vermont. Its codes spread over about 33 miles from north to south and 31 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 05669 (Roxbury) and 05760 (Orwell), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Essex County, New York (29 miles), Chittenden County (30 miles), Washington County (32 miles) and Rutland County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Addison County begin with 057 (10 codes), 054 (7 codes) and 056 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 057 is shared with 3 other counties, Bennington County, Rutland County and Windsor County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 16 of the 18 codes. Together they come to about 765 square miles of land (1,982 sq km). The largest is 05443 (Bristol) with 108 square miles, and the smallest is 05473 (North Ferrisburgh) with 19.6. The middle-sized code here covers 42.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Vermont is 31.4. Water adds about 40.4 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Addison County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 05469 (Monkton) and 05740 (East Middlebury). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Addison County ranks 9th among the 14 counties and county-level areas of Vermont (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 308 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Addison County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VT and the ZIP code:
