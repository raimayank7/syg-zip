Where Orange County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Orange County sits near the middle of Vermont. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 24 miles, and from east to west about 31 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 05060 (Randolph) and 05081 (Wells River), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Grafton County, New Hampshire (22 miles), Washington County (24 miles), Windsor County (28 miles) and Caledonia County (36 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Orange County begin with 050 (24 codes) and 056 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 050 is shared with 2 other counties, Caledonia County and Windsor County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 23 of the 26 codes. Together they come to about 654 square miles of land (1,693 sq km). The largest is 05060 (Randolph) with 76.3 square miles, and the smallest is 05083 (West Fairlee) with 0.8. The middle-sized code here covers 30.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Vermont is 31.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Orange County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 05054 (North Thetford), 05074 (Thetford) and 05085 (West Newbury). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Orange County ranks 5th among the 14 counties and county-level areas of Vermont (equal counts share a rank), with 26 of the 308 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Orange County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VT and the ZIP code:

  • Bradford, VT 05033
  • Brookfield, VT 05036
  • Chelsea, VT 05038

Bradford is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 11 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
