Where Rutland County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Rutland County fall in the south-west of Vermont. Its codes spread over about 32 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 05762 (Pittsfield) and 05775 (West Pawlet), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Washington County, New York (25 miles), Windsor County (26 miles), Addison County (32 miles) and Bennington County (37 miles).

The first three digits

All 30 codes here begin with the same three digits, 057. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 057 is shared with 3 other counties, Addison County, Bennington County and Windsor County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 26 of the 30 codes. Together they come to about 980 square miles of land (2,537 sq km). The largest is 05733 (Brandon) with 108 square miles, and the smallest is 05731 (Benson) with 1.4. The middle-sized code here covers 32.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Vermont is 31.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Rutland County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 05702 (Rutland), 05741 (East Poultney), 05745 (Forest Dale) and 05750 (Hydeville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Rutland County ranks 2nd among the 14 counties and county-level areas of Vermont (equal counts share a rank), with 30 of the 308 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Rutland County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VT and the ZIP code:

  • Rutland, VT 05701 (one of 2 codes for Rutland; the street decides which)
  • Belmont, VT 05730
  • Benson, VT 05731

Rutland is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
