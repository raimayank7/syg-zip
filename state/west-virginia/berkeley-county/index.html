Where Berkeley County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Berkeley County is in the north-east of West Virginia. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 25419 (Falling Waters) and 25440 (Ridgeway), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Jefferson County (12 miles), Morgan County (18 miles), Washington County, Maryland (19 miles) and Winchester (city), Virginia (20 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 254. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 254 is shared with 3 other counties, Hampshire County, Jefferson County and Morgan County, and has 29 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 330 square miles of land (854 sq km). The largest is 25427 (Hedgesville) with 131 square miles, and the smallest is 25421 (Glengary) with 1.0. The middle-sized code here covers 22.4 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Berkeley County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 25402 (Martinsburg) and 25440 (Ridgeway). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Berkeley County ranks 24th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 851 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Berkeley County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WV and the ZIP code:

  • Martinsburg, WV 25401 (one of 5 codes for Martinsburg; the street decides which)
  • Bunker Hill, WV 25413
  • Falling Waters, WV 25419

Martinsburg is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
