Where Kanawha County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Kanawha County is in the south-west of West Virginia. Its codes spread over about 34 miles from north to south and 30 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 25075 (Eskdale) and 25320 (Charleston), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Boone County (20 miles), Putnam County (22 miles), Lincoln County (28 miles) and Roane County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Kanawha County begin with 253 (47 codes), 250 (16 codes), 251 (13 codes) and 252 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 253, so those three digits alone point to Kanawha County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 44 of the 79 codes. Together they come to about 969 square miles of land (2,510 sq km). The largest is 25045 (Clendenin) with 131 square miles, and the smallest is 25156 (Pinch) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 14.0 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Kanawha County; a code can reach across its border. 35 codes have no mapped area: 25026 (Blue Creek), 25079 (Falling Rock), 25317 (Charleston), 25321 (Charleston), 25322 (Charleston) and 30 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of West Virginia has more ZIP codes: Kanawha County holds 79 of the 851 in the state list, about 9.3%.

Writing an address in Kanawha County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WV and the ZIP code:

  • Charleston, WV 25301 (one of 45 codes for Charleston; the street decides which)
  • South Charleston, WV 25303 (one of 2 codes for South Charleston; the street decides which)
  • Alum Creek, WV 25003

Charleston is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
