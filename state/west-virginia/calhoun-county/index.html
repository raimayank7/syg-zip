Where Calhoun County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Calhoun County fall in the west of West Virginia. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 4.5 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 25235 (Chloe) and 26137 (Big Springs), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Gilmer County (15 miles), Wirt County (15 miles), Roane County (16 miles) and Ritchie County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Calhoun County begin with 252 (4 codes) and 261 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 252 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Boone County, Clay County and Gilmer County, and has 40 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 274 square miles of land (710 sq km). The largest is 26137 (Big Springs) with 51.1 square miles, and the smallest is 25261 (Millstone) with 10.0. The middle-sized code here covers 39.7 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Calhoun County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Calhoun County ranks 37th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 851 codes in the state list.
