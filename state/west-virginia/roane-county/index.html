Where Roane County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Roane County is in the west of West Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 22 miles, and from east to west about 15 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 25266 (Newton) and 25270 (Reedy), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Calhoun County (16 miles), Clay County (19 miles), Wirt County (22 miles) and Jackson County (22 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Roane County begin with 252 (7 codes) and 250 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 252 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Boone County, Calhoun County and Clay County, and has 40 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 420 square miles of land (1,089 sq km). The largest is 25276 (Spencer) with 165 square miles, and the smallest is 25005 (Amma) with 7.4. The middle-sized code here covers 42.2 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Roane County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Roane County ranks 37th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 851 codes in the state list.
