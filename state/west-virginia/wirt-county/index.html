Where Wirt County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Wirt County fall in the west of West Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 8.2 miles, and from east to west about 13 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 26138 (Brohard) and 26160 (Palestine), about 14 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Calhoun County (15 miles), Ritchie County (17 miles), Wood County (20 miles) and Roane County (22 miles).

The first three digits

All 5 codes here begin with the same three digits, 261. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 261 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Calhoun County, Jackson County and Pleasants County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 262 square miles of land (679 sq km). The largest is 26143 (Elizabeth) with 134 square miles, and the smallest is 26152 (Munday) with 9.9. The middle-sized code here covers 38.6 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wirt County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Wirt County ranks 48th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 851 codes in the state list.
