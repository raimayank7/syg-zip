Where Randolph County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Randolph County sits near the middle of West Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 39 miles, and from east to west about 42 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 26230 (Pickens) and 26263 (Dryfork), about 46 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Upshur County (21 miles), Barbour County (21 miles), Tucker County (25 miles) and Pendleton County (29 miles).

The first three digits

All 21 codes here begin with the same three digits, 262. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 262 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Barbour County, Nicholas County and Pocahontas County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,102 square miles of land (2,854 sq km). The largest is 26254 (Bowden) with 119 square miles, and the smallest is 26293 (Valley Bend) with 5.8. The middle-sized code here covers 52.5 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Randolph County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Randolph County ranks 10th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 21 of the 851 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Randolph County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WV and the ZIP code:

Beverly is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
