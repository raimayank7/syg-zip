Where Monroe County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Monroe County fall in the south of West Virginia. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 23 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 24941 (Gap Mills) and 24963 (Peterstown), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Giles County, Virginia (17 miles), Summers County (17 miles), Craig County, Virginia (20 miles) and Greenbrier County (24 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Monroe County starts with 249. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 249 is shared with 3 other counties, Greenbrier County, Pocahontas County and Summers County, and has 36 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 444 square miles of land (1,150 sq km). The largest is 24983 (Union) with 99.6 square miles, and the smallest is 24993 (Wolfcreek) with 2.0. The middle-sized code here covers 37.5 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Monroe County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Monroe County ranks 28th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 851 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Monroe County
