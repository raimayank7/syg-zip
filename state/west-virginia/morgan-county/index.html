Where Morgan County is

The ZIP codes of Morgan County are found in the north-east of West Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 6.3 miles, and from east to west about 14 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 25411 (Berkeley Springs) and 25434 (Paw Paw), about 15 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Berkeley County (18 miles), Hampshire County (21 miles), Frederick County, Virginia (24 miles) and Winchester (city), Virginia (27 miles).

The first three digits

All 3 codes here begin with the same three digits, 254. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 254 is shared with 3 other counties, Berkeley County, Hampshire County and Jefferson County, and has 29 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 280 square miles of land (726 sq km). The largest is 25411 (Berkeley Springs) with 140 square miles, and the smallest is 25422 (Great Cacapon) with 66.1. The middle-sized code here covers 74.6 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Morgan County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Morgan County ranks 54th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 3 of the 851 codes in the state list.
