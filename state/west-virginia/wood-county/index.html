Where Wood County is

The ZIP codes of Wood County are found in the west of West Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 26169 (Rockport) and 26187 (Williamstown), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Washington County, Ohio (16 miles), Pleasants County (19 miles), Wirt County (20 miles) and Ritchie County (25 miles).

The first three digits

All 16 codes here begin with the same three digits, 261. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 261 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Calhoun County, Jackson County and Pleasants County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 367 square miles of land (951 sq km). The largest is 26180 (Walker) with 67.0 square miles, and the smallest is 26142 (Davisville) with 10.6. The middle-sized code here covers 37.0 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wood County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 26102 (Parkersburg), 26103 (Parkersburg), 26106 (Parkersburg), 26120 (Mineral Wells) and 26121 (Mineral Wells). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Wood County ranks 16th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 851 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Wood County
