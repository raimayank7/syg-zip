Where Cabell County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Cabell County is in the south-west of West Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 10 miles, and from east to west about 24 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 25510 (Culloden) and 25704 (Huntington), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lawrence County, Ohio (14 miles), Wayne County (16 miles), Boyd County, Kentucky (20 miles) and Lincoln County (21 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Cabell County begin with 257 (39 codes) and 255 (6 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 257 is shared with 1 other county, Wayne County, and has 40 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 45 codes. Together they come to about 318 square miles of land (824 sq km). The largest is 25541 (Milton) with 70.1 square miles, and the smallest is 25755 (Huntington) with 0.09. The middle-sized code here covers 24.8 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cabell County; a code can reach across its border. 33 codes have no mapped area: 25706 (Huntington), 25707 (Huntington), 25708 (Huntington), 25710 (Huntington), 25711 (Huntington) and 28 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Cabell County ranks 2nd among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 45 of the 851 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cabell County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WV and the ZIP code:

  • Huntington, WV 25701 (one of 39 codes for Huntington; the street decides which)
  • Barboursville, WV 25504
  • Culloden, WV 25510

ZIP codes named Huntington are also listed under Wayne County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Huntington is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
