This list holds 851 ZIP codes for West Virginia, about 2.1% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 18th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 55 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Kanawha County (79), Cabell County (45), Raleigh County (44), Fayette County (42) and McDowell County (36). At the other end, 1 has only one or two codes, for example Pleasants County.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. West Virginia uses 22 of these prefixes, from 247 to 268. The busiest are 248 (57 codes, most of them for Anawalt), 262 (54 codes, most of them for Adrian), 250 (52 codes, most of them for Alloy) and 251 (52 codes, most of them for Handley). The first digit 2 is shared with District of Columbia, Maryland, North Carolina, South Carolina and Virginia.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 738 of the 851 codes. The largest is 24954 (Marlinton, Pocahontas County) at 291 square miles and the smallest is 25156 (Pinch) at 0.03; the middle value is 20.4 square miles. The other 113 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 26050 (Newell) and the southernmost 24884 (Squire); the one farthest east is 25425 (Harpers Ferry) and farthest west 25555 (Prichard).

Place names and addresses in West Virginia

Of the 745 place names in the list, 733 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Charleston (45), Huntington (40), Morgantown (7) and Martinsburg (5). 1 place name appears under more than one county, among them Huntington: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters WV and the code, as in Charleston, WV 25301. The name of the county is left out.
