Where Wetzel County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Wetzel County is in the north of West Virginia. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 27 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 26159 (Paden City) and 26562 (Burton), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Tyler County (16 miles), Doddridge County (21 miles), Marshall County (22 miles) and Marion County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Wetzel County begin with 261 (4 codes), 265 (4 codes), 263 (2 codes) and 264 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 261 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Calhoun County, Jackson County and Pleasants County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 383 square miles of land (991 sq km). The largest is 26155 (New Martinsville) with 114 square miles, and the smallest is 26159 (Paden City) with 1.0. The middle-sized code here covers 29.2 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wetzel County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 26561 (Big Run). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Wetzel County ranks 24th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 851 codes in the state list.
