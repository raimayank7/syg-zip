Where Putnam County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Putnam County fall in the south-west of West Virginia. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 13 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 25124 (Liberty) and 25526 (Hurricane), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Mason County (22 miles), Jackson County (22 miles), Kanawha County (22 miles) and Lincoln County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Putnam County begin with 250 (4 codes), 251 (4 codes), 255 (3 codes) and 252 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 250 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Boone County, Clay County and Fayette County, and has 52 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 330 square miles of land (854 sq km). The largest is 25526 (Hurricane) with 91.3 square miles, and the smallest is 25109 (Hometown) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 31.9 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Putnam County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 25569 (Teays). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Putnam County ranks 24th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 851 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Putnam County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WV and the ZIP code:

  • Bancroft, WV 25011
  • Buffalo, WV 25033
  • Eleanor, WV 25070

Bancroft is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
