Where Taylor County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Taylor County sits near the middle of West Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 5.2 miles, and from east to west about 14 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 26424 (Rosemont) and 26440 (Thornton), about 14 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Barbour County (13 miles), Harrison County (15 miles), Marion County (18 miles) and Preston County (22 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Taylor County begin with 264 (3 codes) and 263 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 264 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Barbour County, Doddridge County and Gilmer County, and has 30 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 178 square miles of land (460 sq km). The largest is 26354 (Grafton) with 92.8 square miles, and the smallest is 26435 (Simpson) with 0.10. The middle-sized code here covers 40.2 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Taylor County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Taylor County ranks 48th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 851 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Taylor County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WV and the ZIP code:
