Where Nicholas County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Nicholas County is in the south-west of West Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 35 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 25059 (Dixie) and 26261 (Richwood), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Clay County (20 miles), Fayette County (23 miles), Webster County (25 miles) and Greenbrier County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Nicholas County begin with 266 (15 codes), 262 (3 codes) and 250 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 266 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Braxton County, Clay County and Fayette County, and has 29 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 18 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 726 square miles of land (1,882 sq km). The largest is 26651 (Summersville) with 200 square miles, and the smallest is 26684 (Pool) with 2.3. The middle-sized code here covers 17.0 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Nicholas County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 26675 (Keslers Cross Lanes). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Nicholas County ranks 13th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 851 codes in the state list.
