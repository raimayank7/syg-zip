Where Monongalia County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Monongalia County is in the north of West Virginia. Its codes spread over about 9.4 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 26507 (Morgantown) and 26590 (Wana), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Marion County (14 miles), Greene County, Pennsylvania (17 miles), Preston County (21 miles) and Taylor County (24 miles).

The first three digits

All 16 codes here begin with the same three digits, 265. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 265 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Harrison County, Marion County and Preston County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 265 square miles of land (686 sq km). The largest is 26508 (Morgantown) with 123 square miles, and the smallest is 26506 (Morgantown) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 1.9 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Monongalia County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 26502 (Morgantown), 26504 (Morgantown), 26507 (Morgantown), 26527 (Cassville) and 26531 (Dellslow). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Monongalia County ranks 16th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 851 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Monongalia County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WV and the ZIP code:

  • Morgantown, WV 26501 (one of 7 codes for Morgantown; the street decides which)

Morgantown is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
