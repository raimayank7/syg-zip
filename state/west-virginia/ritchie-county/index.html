Where Ritchie County is

The ZIP codes of Ritchie County are found in the west of West Virginia. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 26161 (Petroleum) and 26325 (Auburn), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Pleasants County (17 miles), Wirt County (17 miles), Doddridge County (20 miles) and Gilmer County (21 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Ritchie County begin with 263 (5 codes), 261 (3 codes) and 264 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 263 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Barbour County, Braxton County and Doddridge County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 452 square miles of land (1,171 sq km). The largest is 26362 (Harrisville) with 106 square miles, and the smallest is 26327 (Berea) with 10.2. The middle-sized code here covers 30.7 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ritchie County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Ritchie County ranks 31st among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 851 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Ritchie County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WV and the ZIP code:

  • Auburn, WV 26325
  • Berea, WV 26327
  • Cairo, WV 26337

Auburn is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 17 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
