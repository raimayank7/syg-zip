Where Braxton County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Braxton County fall close to the middle of West Virginia. Its codes spread over about 18 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 26623 (Frametown) and 26631 (Napier), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Gilmer County (15 miles), Lewis County (20 miles), Webster County (20 miles) and Calhoun County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Braxton County begin with 266 (9 codes) and 263 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 266 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Clay County, Fayette County and Gilmer County, and has 29 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 425 square miles of land (1,101 sq km). The largest is 26601 (Sutton) with 127 square miles, and the smallest is 26615 (Copen) with 6.5. The middle-sized code here covers 29.9 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Braxton County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Braxton County ranks 31st among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 851 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Braxton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WV and the ZIP code:

  • Burnsville, WV 26335
  • Copen, WV 26615
  • Exchange, WV 26619

Burnsville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
