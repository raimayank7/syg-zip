Where Jackson County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Jackson County fall in the west of West Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 25231 (Advent) and 26164 (Ravenswood), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Mason County (19 miles), Putnam County (22 miles), Roane County (22 miles) and Meigs County, Ohio (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Jackson County begin with 252 (11 codes) and 261 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 252 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Boone County, Calhoun County and Clay County, and has 40 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 522 square miles of land (1,352 sq km). The largest is 26164 (Ravenswood) with 102 square miles, and the smallest is 25262 (Millwood) with 3.9. The middle-sized code here covers 32.5 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jackson County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Jackson County ranks 24th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 851 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jackson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WV and the ZIP code:
