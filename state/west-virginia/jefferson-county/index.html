Where Jefferson County is

The ZIP codes of Jefferson County are found in the east of West Virginia. Its codes spread over about 14 miles from north to south and 10 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 25443 (Shepherdstown) and 25446 (Summit Point), about 16 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Berkeley County (12 miles), Clarke County, Virginia (19 miles), Winchester (city), Virginia (20 miles) and Washington County, Maryland (20 miles).

The first three digits

All 11 codes here begin with the same three digits, 254. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 254 is shared with 3 other counties, Berkeley County, Hampshire County and Morgan County, and has 29 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 216 square miles of land (560 sq km). The largest is 25414 (Charles Town) with 56.6 square miles, and the smallest is 25432 (Millville) with 0.07. The middle-sized code here covers 22.7 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jefferson County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 25410 (Bakerton), 25423 (Halltown) and 25441 (Rippon). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Jefferson County ranks 28th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 851 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jefferson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WV and the ZIP code:
