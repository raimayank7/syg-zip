Where Hampshire County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Hampshire County is in the east of West Virginia. Its codes spread over about 25 miles from north to south and 27 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 26817 (Bloomery) and 26852 (Purgitsville), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Mineral County (20 miles), Frederick County, Virginia (21 miles), Morgan County (21 miles) and Allegany County, Maryland (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Hampshire County begin with 267 (8 codes), 268 (5 codes) and 254 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 267 is shared with 3 other counties, Grant County, Mineral County and Preston County, and has 23 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 608 square miles of land (1,576 sq km). The largest is 26757 (Romney) with 146 square miles, and the smallest is 25431 (Levels) with 8.7. The middle-sized code here covers 23.8 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hampshire County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Hampshire County ranks 16th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 851 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hampshire County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WV and the ZIP code:

  • Augusta, WV 26704
  • Bloomery, WV 26817
  • Capon Bridge, WV 26711

Augusta is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 12 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
