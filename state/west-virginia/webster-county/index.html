Where Webster County is

The ZIP codes of Webster County are found in the south of West Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 18 miles, and from east to west about 16 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 26208 (Camden On Gauley) and 26222 (Hacker Valley), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Braxton County (20 miles), Nicholas County (25 miles), Upshur County (27 miles) and Pocahontas County (29 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Webster County starts with 262. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 262 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Barbour County, Nicholas County and Pocahontas County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 554 square miles of land (1,434 sq km). The largest is 26288 (Webster Springs) with 235 square miles, and the smallest is 26266 (Upperglade) with 4.0. The middle-sized code here covers 39.2 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Webster County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Webster County ranks 37th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 851 codes in the state list.
