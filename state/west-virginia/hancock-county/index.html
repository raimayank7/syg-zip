Where Hancock County is

The ZIP codes of Hancock County are found in the north of West Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 14 miles, and from east to west about 2.2 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 26050 (Newell) and 26062 (Weirton), about 14 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Jefferson County, Ohio (15 miles), Beaver County, Pennsylvania (17 miles), Columbiana County, Ohio (20 miles) and Brooke County (20 miles).

The first three digits

All 5 codes here begin with the same three digits, 260. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 260 is shared with 3 other counties, Brooke County, Marshall County and Ohio County, and has 24 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 89.1 square miles of land (231 sq km). The largest is 26047 (New Cumberland) with 41.6 square miles, and the smallest is 26056 (New Manchester) with 2.3. The middle-sized code here covers 15.1 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. Water adds about 5.7 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hancock County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Hancock County ranks 48th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 851 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hancock County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WV and the ZIP code:

  • Chester, WV 26034
  • New Cumberland, WV 26047
  • New Manchester, WV 26056

Chester is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 23 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
