Where Logan County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Logan County is in the south-west of West Virginia. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 25505 (Big Creek) and 25611 (Bruno), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Mingo County (14 miles), Boone County (19 miles), Wyoming County (26 miles) and Lincoln County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Logan County begin with 256 (24 codes), 250 (3 codes), 255 (3 codes) and 251 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 256 is shared with 2 other counties, Mingo County and Wayne County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 31 of the 32 codes. Together they come to about 436 square miles of land (1,129 sq km). The largest is 25508 (Chapmanville) with 74.2 square miles, and the smallest is 25653 (Wilkinson) with 1.1. The middle-sized code here covers 11.6 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Logan County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 25614 (Cora). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Logan County ranks 7th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 32 of the 851 codes in the state list.
