Where Pendleton County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Pendleton County fall in the east of West Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 24 miles, and from east to west about 18 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 26815 (Sugar Grove) and 26884 (Seneca Rocks), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Highland County, Virginia (22 miles), Randolph County (29 miles), Tucker County (30 miles) and Rockingham County, Virginia (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 268. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 268 is shared with 3 other counties, Grant County, Hampshire County and Hardy County, and has 24 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 696 square miles of land (1,803 sq km). The largest is 26807 (Franklin) with 162 square miles, and the smallest is 26886 (Onego) with 30.3. The middle-sized code here covers 80.6 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pendleton County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Pendleton County ranks 37th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 851 codes in the state list.
