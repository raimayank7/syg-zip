Where Mercer County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Mercer County fall in the south-west of West Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 19 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 24751 (Wolfe) and 25971 (Lerona), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Bland County, Virginia (18 miles), Summers County (24 miles), Wyoming County (25 miles) and McDowell County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Mercer County begin with 247 (16 codes), 258 (2 codes) and 259 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 247 is shared with 1 other county, Wyoming County, and has 19 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 18 of the 20 codes. Together they come to about 428 square miles of land (1,110 sq km). The largest is 24739 (Princeton) with 125 square miles, and the smallest is 24738 (Nemours) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 12.7 square miles; the middle value for all of West Virginia is 20.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Mercer County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 24732 (Kellysville) and 24751 (Wolfe). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Mercer County ranks 11th among the 55 counties and county-level areas of West Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 20 of the 851 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Mercer County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WV and the ZIP code:

  • Princeton, WV 24739 (one of 2 codes for Princeton; the street decides which)

Princeton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 18 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
