Where Twin Falls County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Twin Falls County is in the south of Idaho. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 26 miles, and from east to west about 36 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 83321 (Castleford) and 83344 (Murtaugh), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Jerome County (14 miles), Gooding County (29 miles), Lincoln County (35 miles) and Minidoka County (44 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 833. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 833 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Blaine County, Camas County and Cassia County, and has 39 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 1,538 square miles of land (3,985 sq km). The largest is 83302 (Rogerson) with 352 square miles, and the smallest is 83328 (Filer) with 67.3. The middle-sized code here covers 178 square miles; the middle value for all of Idaho is 111. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Twin Falls County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 83303 (Twin Falls). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Twin Falls County ranks 12th among the 44 counties and county-level areas of Idaho (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 320 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Twin Falls County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters ID and the ZIP code:
