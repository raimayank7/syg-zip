Where Bonneville County is

The ZIP codes of Bonneville County are found in the south-east of Idaho. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 14 miles, and from east to west about 40 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 83402 (Idaho Falls) and 83428 (Irwin), about 41 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Jefferson County (22 miles), Madison County (24 miles), Bingham County (36 miles) and Teton County (40 miles).

The first three digits

All 11 codes here begin with the same three digits, 834. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 834 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Clark County, Fremont County and Jefferson County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 986 square miles of land (2,553 sq km). The largest is 83428 (Irwin) with 249 square miles, and the smallest is 83454 (Ucon) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 127 square miles; the middle value for all of Idaho is 111. Water adds about 30.7 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bonneville County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 83403 (Idaho Falls), 83405 (Idaho Falls) and 83415 (Idaho Falls). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Bonneville County ranks 9th among the 44 counties and county-level areas of Idaho (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 320 codes in the state list.
