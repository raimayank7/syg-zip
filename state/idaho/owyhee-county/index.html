Where Owyhee County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Owyhee County fall in the south-west of Idaho. Its codes spread over about 69 miles from north to south and 56 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 83604 (Bruneau) and 83628 (Homedale), about 89 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Ada County (32 miles), Canyon County (38 miles), Elmore County (47 miles) and Gem County (61 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Owyhee County starts with 836. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 836 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Ada County, Adams County and Boise County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,824 square miles of land (7,313 sq km). The largest is 83650 (Murphy) with 1,456 square miles, and the smallest is 83628 (Homedale) with 63.1. The middle-sized code here covers 265 square miles; the middle value for all of Idaho is 111. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Owyhee County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Owyhee County ranks 23rd among the 44 counties and county-level areas of Idaho (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 320 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Owyhee County
