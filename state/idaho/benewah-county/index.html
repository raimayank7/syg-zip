Where Benewah County is

The ZIP codes of Benewah County are found in the north-west of Idaho. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 24 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 83830 (Fernwood) and 83851 (Plummer), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Latah County (30 miles), Shoshone County (34 miles), Kootenai County (36 miles) and Whitman County, Washington (38 miles).

The first three digits

All 6 codes here begin with the same three digits, 838. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 838 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Bonner County, Boundary County and Clearwater County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 843 square miles of land (2,184 sq km). The largest is 83861 (Saint Maries) with 456 square miles, and the smallest is 83830 (Fernwood) with 60.8. The middle-sized code here covers 68.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Idaho is 111. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Benewah County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Benewah County ranks 18th among the 44 counties and county-level areas of Idaho (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 320 codes in the state list.
