Where Boundary County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Boundary County is in the north-west of Idaho. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 83826 (Eastport) and 83847 (Naples), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Bonner County (42 miles), Pend Oreille County, Washington (50 miles), Lincoln County, Montana (52 miles) and Kootenai County (79 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Boundary County starts with 838. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 838 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Benewah County, Bonner County and Clearwater County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 4 of the 5 codes. Together they come to about 1,076 square miles of land (2,788 sq km). The largest is 83805 (Bonners Ferry) with 880 square miles, and the smallest is 83826 (Eastport) with 11.8. The middle-sized code here covers 92.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Idaho is 111. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Boundary County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 83853 (Porthill). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Boundary County ranks 23rd among the 44 counties and county-level areas of Idaho (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 320 codes in the state list.
