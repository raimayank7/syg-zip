Where Kootenai County is

The ZIP codes of Kootenai County are found in the north-west of Idaho. Its codes spread over about 38 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 83803 (Bayview) and 83876 (Worley), about 42 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Spokane County, Washington (29 miles), Benewah County (36 miles), Bonner County (37 miles) and Shoshone County (38 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Kootenai County starts with 838. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 838 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Benewah County, Bonner County and Boundary County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 1,265 square miles of land (3,277 sq km). The largest is 83814 (Coeur d'Alene) with 194 square miles, and the smallest is 83815 (Coeur d'Alene) with 15.6. The middle-sized code here covers 109 square miles; the middle value for all of Idaho is 111. Water adds about 122 square miles, roughly 9% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Kootenai County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 83816 (Coeur d'Alene) and 83877 (Post Falls). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Kootenai County ranks 4th among the 44 counties and county-level areas of Idaho (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 320 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Kootenai County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters ID and the ZIP code:

  • Coeur d'Alene, ID 83814 (one of 3 codes for Coeur d'Alene; the street decides which)
  • Post Falls, ID 83854 (one of 2 codes for Post Falls; the street decides which)
  • Athol, ID 83801
