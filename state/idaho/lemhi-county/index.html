Where Lemhi County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Lemhi County sits near the middle of Idaho. Its codes spread over about 67 miles from north to south and 38 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 83253 (May) and 83463 (Gibbonsville), about 67 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Beaverhead County, Montana (49 miles), Custer County (54 miles), Ravalli County, Montana (79 miles) and Silver Bow County, Montana (82 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lemhi County begin with 834 (8 codes) and 832 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 834 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Bonneville County, Clark County and Fremont County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 4,537 square miles of land (11,751 sq km). The largest is 83469 (Shoup) with 1,336 square miles, and the smallest is 83462 (Carmen) with 110. The middle-sized code here covers 236 square miles; the middle value for all of Idaho is 111. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lemhi County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 83229 (Cobalt). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lemhi County ranks 10th among the 44 counties and county-level areas of Idaho (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 320 codes in the state list.
