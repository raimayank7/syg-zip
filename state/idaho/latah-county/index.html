Where Latah County is

The ZIP codes of Latah County are found in the north-west of Idaho. Its codes spread over about 26 miles from north to south and 29 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 83806 (Bovill) and 83832 (Genesee), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Nez Perce County (26 miles), Benewah County (30 miles), Whitman County, Washington (31 miles) and Clearwater County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Latah County begin with 838 (10 codes) and 835 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 838 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Benewah County, Bonner County and Boundary County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,095 square miles of land (2,836 sq km). The largest is 83537 (Kendrick) with 176 square miles, and the smallest is 83844 (Moscow) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 101 square miles; the middle value for all of Idaho is 111. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Latah County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Latah County ranks 6th among the 44 counties and county-level areas of Idaho (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 320 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Latah County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters ID and the ZIP code:

  • Moscow, ID 83843 (one of 2 codes for Moscow; the street decides which)
  • Bovill, ID 83806
  • Deary, ID 83823

Moscow is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Latah County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Moscow, ID?

Moscow has 2 ZIP codes in Latah County: 83843, 83844. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Latah County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 838, on 10 of the 12 codes.

Which ZIP code in Latah County covers the most land?

83537 (Kendrick), at about 176 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
