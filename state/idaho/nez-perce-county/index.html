Where Nez Perce County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Nez Perce County is in the north-west of Idaho. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 8.0 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 83501 (Lewiston) and 83545 (Peck), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lewis County (18 miles), Asotin County, Washington (22 miles), Latah County (26 miles) and Clearwater County (26 miles).

The first three digits

All 4 codes here begin with the same three digits, 835. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 835 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Clearwater County, Idaho County and Latah County, and has 28 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 512 square miles of land (1,325 sq km). The largest is 83501 (Lewiston) with 244 square miles, and the smallest is 83545 (Peck) with 43.1. The middle-sized code here covers 112 square miles; the middle value for all of Idaho is 111. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Nez Perce County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Nez Perce County ranks 29th among the 44 counties and county-level areas of Idaho (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 320 codes in the state list.
