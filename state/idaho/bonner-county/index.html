Where Bonner County is

The ZIP codes of Bonner County are found in the north-west of Idaho. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 42 miles, and from east to west about 38 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 83811 (Clark Fork) and 83848 (Nordman), about 49 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Pend Oreille County, Washington (37 miles), Kootenai County (37 miles), Boundary County (42 miles) and Spokane County, Washington (53 miles).

The first three digits

All 16 codes here begin with the same three digits, 838. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 838 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Benewah County, Boundary County and Clearwater County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 15 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 1,394 square miles of land (3,610 sq km). The largest is 83864 (Sandpoint) with 402 square miles, and the smallest is 83840 (Kootenai) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 49.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Idaho is 111. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 134 square miles, roughly 9% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bonner County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 83865 (Colburn). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.
