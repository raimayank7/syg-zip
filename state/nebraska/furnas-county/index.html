Where Furnas County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Furnas County fall in the south of Nebraska. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 14 miles, and from east to west about 28 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 68967 (Oxford) and 69022 (Cambridge), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Gosper County (26 miles), Red Willow County (26 miles), Harlan County (29 miles) and Phelps County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Furnas County begin with 689 (6 codes) and 690 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 689 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Adams County, Clay County and Franklin County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,140 square miles of land (2,953 sq km). The largest is 69022 (Cambridge) with 300 square miles, and the smallest is 68946 (Hendley) with 19.7. The middle-sized code here covers 133 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Furnas County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Furnas County ranks 20th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Furnas County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Arapahoe, NE 68922
  • Beaver City, NE 68926
  • Cambridge, NE 69022

Arapahoe is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
