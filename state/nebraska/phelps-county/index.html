Where Phelps County is

The ZIP codes of Phelps County are found in the south of Nebraska. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 68927 (Bertrand) and 68940 (Funk), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Gosper County (20 miles), Harlan County (22 miles), Kearney County (23 miles) and Buffalo County (29 miles).

The first three digits

All 5 codes here begin with the same three digits, 689. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 689 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Adams County, Clay County and Franklin County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 516 square miles of land (1,338 sq km). The largest is 68949 (Holdrege) with 218 square miles, and the smallest is 68923 (Atlanta) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 73.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Phelps County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Phelps County ranks 46th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Phelps County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Atlanta, NE 68923
  • Bertrand, NE 68927
  • Funk, NE 68940

Atlanta is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 10 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
