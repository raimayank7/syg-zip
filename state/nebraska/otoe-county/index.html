Where Otoe County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Otoe County fall in the south-east of Nebraska. Its codes spread over about 12 miles from north to south and 28 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 68344 (Douglas) and 68410 (Nebraska City), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Johnson County (18 miles), Cass County (19 miles), Nemaha County (23 miles) and Lancaster County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Otoe County begin with 684 (6 codes) and 683 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 684 is shared with 14 other counties, such as Cass County, Clay County and Fillmore County, and has 56 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 554 square miles of land (1,434 sq km). The largest is 68410 (Nebraska City) with 143 square miles, and the smallest is 68382 (Lorton) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 51.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Otoe County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Otoe County ranks 12th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Otoe County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Burr, NE 68324
  • Douglas, NE 68344
  • Dunbar, NE 68346

Questions about Otoe County ZIP codes
