Where Lancaster County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Lancaster County is in the south-east of Nebraska. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 68336 (Davey) and 68358 (Firth), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Seward County (25 miles), Cass County (28 miles), Otoe County (30 miles) and Saunders County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lancaster County begin with 685 (29 codes), 684 (8 codes) and 683 (6 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 685, so those three digits alone point to Lancaster County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 37 of the 43 codes. Together they come to about 755 square miles of land (1,955 sq km). The largest is 68428 (Raymond) with 57.2 square miles, and the smallest is 68588 (Lincoln) with 0.07. The middle-sized code here covers 13.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lancaster County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 68501 (Lincoln), 68509 (Lincoln), 68529 (Lincoln), 68542 (Lincoln), 68544 (Lincoln) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lancaster County ranks 2nd among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 43 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lancaster County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Lincoln, NE 68501 (one of 29 codes for Lincoln; the street decides which)
  • Bennet, NE 68317
  • Davey, NE 68336

Lincoln is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 18 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
