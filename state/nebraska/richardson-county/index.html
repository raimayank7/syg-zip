Where Richardson County is

The ZIP codes of Richardson County are found in the south-east of Nebraska. Its codes spread over about 12 miles from north to south and 27 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 68376 (Humboldt) and 68431 (Rulo), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Nemaha County (21 miles), Brown County, Kansas (25 miles), Atchison County, Missouri (25 miles) and Pawnee County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Richardson County begin with 684 (5 codes) and 683 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 684 is shared with 14 other counties, such as Cass County, Clay County and Fillmore County, and has 56 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 588 square miles of land (1,522 sq km). The largest is 68376 (Humboldt) with 155 square miles, and the smallest is 68442 (Stella) with 36.1. The middle-sized code here covers 51.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Richardson County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Richardson County ranks 20th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Richardson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Dawson, NE 68337
  • Falls City, NE 68355
  • Humboldt, NE 68376

Dawson is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
