Where Thayer County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Thayer County is in the south-east of Nebraska. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 68335 (Davenport) and 68429 (Reynolds), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Nuckolls County (23 miles), Republic County, Kansas (23 miles), Fillmore County (24 miles) and Jefferson County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Thayer County begin with 683 (11 codes) and 684 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 683 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Cass County, Fillmore County and Gage County, and has 70 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 669 square miles of land (1,732 sq km). The largest is 68370 (Hebron) with 163 square miles, and the smallest is 68362 (Gilead) with 2.3. The middle-sized code here covers 51.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Thayer County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Thayer County ranks 7th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Thayer County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Alexandria, NE 68303
  • Belvidere, NE 68315
  • Bruning, NE 68322

Alexandria is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 11 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
