Where Holt County is

The ZIP codes of Holt County are found in the north of Nebraska. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 38 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 68735 (Ewing) and 68780 (Stuart), about 46 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Antelope County (36 miles), Boyd County (36 miles), Wheeler County (39 miles) and Rock County (43 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 687. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 687 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Antelope County, Boyd County and Cedar County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,365 square miles of land (6,125 sq km). The largest is 68713 (Atkinson) with 592 square miles, and the smallest is 68742 (Inman) with 39.8. The middle-sized code here covers 210 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Holt County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Holt County ranks 15th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Holt County
