Where Pawnee County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Pawnee County is in the south-east of Nebraska. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 68345 (Du Bois) and 68380 (Lewiston), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Johnson County (16 miles), Gage County (24 miles), Richardson County (27 miles) and Nemaha County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Pawnee County begin with 683 (3 codes) and 684 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 683 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Cass County, Fillmore County and Gage County, and has 70 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 410 square miles of land (1,062 sq km). The largest is 68420 (Pawnee City) with 130 square miles, and the smallest is 68380 (Lewiston) with 7.0. The middle-sized code here covers 60.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pawnee County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Pawnee County ranks 38th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pawnee County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Burchard, NE 68323
  • Du Bois, NE 68345
  • Lewiston, NE 68380
