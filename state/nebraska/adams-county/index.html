Where Adams County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Adams County is in the south-east of Nebraska. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 14 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 68925 (Ayr) and 68956 (Kenesaw), about 17 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Clay County (24 miles), Hall County (24 miles), Webster County (25 miles) and Kearney County (26 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 689. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 689 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Clay County, Franklin County and Furnas County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 496 square miles of land (1,285 sq km). The largest is 68901 (Hastings) with 150 square miles, and the smallest is 68973 (Roseland) with 47.3. The middle-sized code here covers 77.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Adams County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 68902 (Hastings). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Adams County ranks 28th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Adams County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Hastings, NE 68901 (one of 2 codes for Hastings; the street decides which)
  • Ayr, NE 68925
  • Holstein, NE 68950

Hastings is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
