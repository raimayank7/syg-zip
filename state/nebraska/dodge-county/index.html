Where Dodge County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Dodge County fall in the east of Nebraska. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 68026 (Fremont) and 68633 (Dodge), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Washington County (22 miles), Burt County (23 miles), Colfax County (24 miles) and Saunders County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Dodge County begin with 680 (7 codes) and 686 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 680 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Burt County, Butler County and Cass County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 640 square miles of land (1,658 sq km). The largest is 68057 (Scribner) with 128 square miles, and the smallest is 68072 (Winslow) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 66.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Dodge County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 68026 (Fremont). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Dodge County ranks 9th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Dodge County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Fremont, NE 68025 (one of 2 codes for Fremont; the street decides which)
  • Ames, NE 68621
  • Dodge, NE 68633

Fremont is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Dodge County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Fremont, NE?

Fremont has 2 ZIP codes in Dodge County: 68025, 68026. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Dodge County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 680, on 7 of the 11 codes.

Which ZIP code in Dodge County covers the most land?

68057 (Scribner), at about 128 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
