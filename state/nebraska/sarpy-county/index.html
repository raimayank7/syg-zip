Where Sarpy County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Sarpy County fall in the south-east of Nebraska. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 68028 (Gretna) and 68113 (Offutt Afb), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Douglas County (8.0 miles), Cass County (18 miles), Mills County, Iowa (24 miles) and Washington County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Sarpy County begin with 681 (8 codes) and 680 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 681 is shared with 1 other county, Douglas County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 235 square miles of land (608 sq km). The largest is 68028 (Gretna) with 63.0 square miles, and the smallest is 68056 (St Columbans) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 11.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 8.8 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sarpy County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Sarpy County ranks 5th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Sarpy County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Bellevue, NE 68005 (one of 3 codes for Bellevue; the street decides which)
  • Omaha, NE 68136 (one of 3 codes for Omaha; the street decides which)
  • Papillion, NE 68046 (one of 2 codes for Papillion; the street decides which)

Bellevue is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Omaha shares a name with a place in another part of Nebraska; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
