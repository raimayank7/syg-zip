Where Lincoln County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Lincoln County fall in the south-west of Nebraska. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 42 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 69123 (Brady) and 69169 (Wallace), about 44 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Hayes County (35 miles), Frontier County (37 miles), Logan County (38 miles) and McPherson County (40 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 691. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 691 is shared with 16 other counties, such as Arthur County, Blaine County and Cherry County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 2,471 square miles of land (6,401 sq km). The largest is 69101 (North Platte) with 586 square miles, and the smallest is 69132 (Dickens) with 126. The middle-sized code here covers 259 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lincoln County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 69103 (North Platte). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lincoln County ranks 15th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lincoln County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • North Platte, NE 69101 (one of 2 codes for North Platte; the street decides which)
  • Brady, NE 69123
  • Dickens, NE 69132

Questions about Lincoln County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for North Platte, NE?

North Platte has 2 ZIP codes in Lincoln County: 69101, 69103. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Lincoln County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 9 start with 691.

Which ZIP code in Lincoln County covers the most land?

69101 (North Platte), at about 586 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
