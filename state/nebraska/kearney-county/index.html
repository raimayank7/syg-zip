Where Kearney County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Kearney County is in the south of Nebraska. Its codes spread over about 14 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 68945 (Heartwell) and 68982 (Wilcox), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Franklin County (21 miles), Phelps County (23 miles), Buffalo County (23 miles) and Adams County (26 miles).

The first three digits

All 4 codes here begin with the same three digits, 689. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 689 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Adams County, Clay County and Franklin County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 491 square miles of land (1,273 sq km). The largest is 68959 (Minden) with 251 square miles, and the smallest is 68945 (Heartwell) with 45.9. The middle-sized code here covers 97.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Kearney County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Kearney County ranks 59th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Kearney County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Axtell, NE 68924
  • Heartwell, NE 68945
  • Minden, NE 68959

Axtell is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
