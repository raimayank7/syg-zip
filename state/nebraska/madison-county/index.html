Where Madison County is

The ZIP codes of Madison County are found in the north-east of Nebraska. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 68701 (Norfolk) and 68758 (Newman Grove), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Pierce County (22 miles), Stanton County (24 miles), Wayne County (28 miles) and Platte County (29 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Madison County starts with 687. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 687 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Antelope County, Boyd County and Cedar County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 788 square miles of land (2,041 sq km). The largest is 68701 (Norfolk) with 189 square miles, and the smallest is 68715 (Battle Creek) with 51.4. The middle-sized code here covers 131 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Madison County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 68702 (Norfolk). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Madison County ranks 28th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Madison County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Norfolk, NE 68701 (one of 2 codes for Norfolk; the street decides which)
  • Battle Creek, NE 68715
  • Madison, NE 68748

Norfolk is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
