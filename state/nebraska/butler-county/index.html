Where Butler County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Butler County fall in the east of Nebraska. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 68036 (Linwood) and 68667 (Surprise), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Seward County (25 miles), Saunders County (26 miles), Polk County (26 miles) and Colfax County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Butler County begin with 686 (7 codes) and 680 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 686 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Antelope County, Boone County and Colfax County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 589 square miles of land (1,526 sq km). The largest is 68632 (David City) with 154 square miles, and the smallest is 68001 (Abie) with 1.5. The middle-sized code here covers 61.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Butler County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Butler County ranks 12th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Butler County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Abie, NE 68001
  • Bellwood, NE 68624
  • Brainard, NE 68626
