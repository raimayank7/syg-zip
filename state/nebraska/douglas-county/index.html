Where Douglas County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Douglas County fall in the east of Nebraska. Its codes spread over about 11 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 68064 (Valley) and 68108 (Omaha), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Sarpy County (8.0 miles), Washington County (18 miles), Pottawattamie County, Iowa (23 miles) and Cass County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Douglas County begin with 681 (45 codes) and 680 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 681 is shared with 1 other county, Sarpy County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 35 of the 50 codes. Together they come to about 363 square miles of land (940 sq km). The largest is 68064 (Valley) with 51.2 square miles, and the smallest is 68183 (Omaha) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 6.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 14.6 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Douglas County; a code can reach across its border. 15 codes have no mapped area: 68101 (Omaha), 68103 (Omaha), 68109 (Omaha), 68119 (Omaha), 68120 (Omaha) and 10 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Nebraska has more ZIP codes: Douglas County holds 50 of the 620 in the state list, about 8.1%.

Writing an address in Douglas County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Omaha, NE 68101 (one of 45 codes for Omaha; the street decides which)
  • Bennington, NE 68007
  • Boys Town, NE 68010

ZIP codes named Omaha are also listed under Sarpy County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Omaha is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
