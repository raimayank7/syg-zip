Where Franklin County is

The ZIP codes of Franklin County are found in the south of Nebraska. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 68932 (Campbell) and 68960 (Naponee), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Kearney County (21 miles), Webster County (23 miles), Harlan County (24 miles) and Adams County (32 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Franklin County starts with 689. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 689 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Adams County, Clay County and Furnas County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 634 square miles of land (1,642 sq km). The largest is 68939 (Franklin) with 134 square miles, and the smallest is 68960 (Naponee) with 45.3. The middle-sized code here covers 93.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Franklin County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Franklin County ranks 28th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Franklin County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Bloomington, NE 68929
  • Campbell, NE 68932
  • Franklin, NE 68939

Bloomington is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
