Where Chase County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Chase County is in the south-west of Nebraska. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 5.8 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 69023 (Champion) and 69045 (Wauneta), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Dundy County (24 miles), Perkins County (26 miles), Hayes County (29 miles) and Hitchcock County (33 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Chase County starts with 690. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 690 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Dawson County, Dundy County and Frontier County, and has 26 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 986 square miles of land (2,554 sq km). The largest is 69033 (Imperial) with 331 square miles, and the smallest is 69027 (Enders) with 99.9. The middle-sized code here covers 277 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Chase County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Chase County ranks 59th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Chase County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Enders, NE 69027
