Nebraska ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 620 ZIP codes for Nebraska, about 1.5% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 29th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 93 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Douglas County (50), Lancaster County (43), Cass County (15), Saunders County (14) and Buffalo County (13). At the other end, 15 have only one or two codes, for example Arthur County, Banner County and Box Butte County.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Nebraska uses 13 of these prefixes, from 680 to 693. The busiest are 687 (75 codes, most of them for Norfolk), 683 (70 codes, most of them for Adams), 688 (66 codes, most of them for Kearney) and 680 (57 codes, most of them for Blair). The first digit 6 is shared with Illinois, Kansas and Missouri.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 586 of the 620 codes. The largest is 69201 (Valentine, Cherry County) at 1,606 square miles and the smallest is 68183 (Omaha) at 0.01; the middle value is 78.2 square miles. The other 34 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 69365 (Whiteclay) and the southernmost 68325 (Byron); the one farthest east is 68431 (Rulo) and farthest west 69352 (Lyman).

Place names and addresses in Nebraska

Of the 528 place names in the list, 513 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Omaha (48), Lincoln (29), Kearney (4) and Bellevue (3). 1 place name appears under more than one county, among them Omaha: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters NE and the code, as in Omaha, NE 68101. The name of the county is left out.
