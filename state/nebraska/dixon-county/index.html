Where Dixon County is

The ZIP codes of Dixon County are found in the north-east of Nebraska. Its codes spread over about 29 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 68751 (Maskell) and 68784 (Wakefield), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Dakota County (19 miles), Cedar County (21 miles), Union County, South Dakota (25 miles) and Wayne County (25 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Dixon County starts with 687. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 687 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Antelope County, Boyd County and Cedar County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 512 square miles of land (1,325 sq km). The largest is 68757 (Newcastle) with 124 square miles, and the smallest is 68751 (Maskell) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 60.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Dixon County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Dixon County ranks 20th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Dixon County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Allen, NE 68710
  • Concord, NE 68728
  • Dixon, NE 68732

Allen is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
