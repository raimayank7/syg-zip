Where Buffalo County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Buffalo County fall in the south of Nebraska. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 33 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 68858 (Miller) and 68876 (Shelton), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Kearney County (23 miles), Sherman County (27 miles), Phelps County (29 miles) and Hall County (34 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Buffalo County starts with 688. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 688 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Blaine County, Custer County and Dawson County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 1,175 square miles of land (3,044 sq km). The largest is 68869 (Ravenna) with 211 square miles, and the smallest is 68849 (Kearney) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 110 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Buffalo County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 68848 (Kearney). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Buffalo County ranks 5th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Buffalo County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Kearney, NE 68845 (one of 4 codes for Kearney; the street decides which)
  • Amherst, NE 68812
  • Elm Creek, NE 68836

Questions about Buffalo County ZIP codes

Do all ZIP codes in Buffalo County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Buffalo County covers the most land?
