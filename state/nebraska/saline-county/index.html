Where Saline County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Saline County is in the south-east of Nebraska. Its codes spread over about 18 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 68341 (De Witt) and 68359 (Friend), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Jefferson County (23 miles), Fillmore County (24 miles), Seward County (25 miles) and Gage County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Saline County begin with 683 (4 codes) and 684 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 683 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Cass County, Fillmore County and Gage County, and has 70 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 694 square miles of land (1,798 sq km). The largest is 68359 (Friend) with 120 square miles, and the smallest is 68445 (Swanton) with 21.5. The middle-sized code here covers 93.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Nebraska is 78.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Saline County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Saline County ranks 20th among the 93 counties and county-level areas of Nebraska (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 620 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Saline County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NE and the ZIP code:

  • Crete, NE 68333
  • De Witt, NE 68341
  • Dorchester, NE 68343
