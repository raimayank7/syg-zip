Where Tioga County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Tioga County is in the north of Pennsylvania. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 30 miles, and from east to west about 35 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 16943 (Sabinsville) and 17765 (Roaring Branch), about 39 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Chemung County, New York (32 miles), Potter County (33 miles), Steuben County, New York (34 miles) and Lycoming County (36 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Tioga County begin with 169 (20 codes) and 177 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 169 is shared with 2 other counties, Bradford County and Potter County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,203 square miles of land (3,115 sq km). The largest is 16901 (Wellsboro) with 230 square miles, and the smallest is 16939 (Morris Run) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 47.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Tioga County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Tioga County ranks 39th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 21 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.
