Where Washington County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Washington County fall in the south-west of Pennsylvania. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 34 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 15021 (Burgettstown) and 15483 (Stockdale), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Allegheny County (20 miles), Greene County (23 miles), Ohio County, West Virginia (25 miles) and Brooke County, West Virginia (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Washington County begin with 153 (34 codes), 150 (14 codes) and 154 (9 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 153 is shared with 1 other county, Greene County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 55 of the 57 codes. Together they come to about 899 square miles of land (2,329 sq km). The largest is 15301 (Washington) with 122 square miles, and the smallest is 15378 (Westland) with 0.07. The middle-sized code here covers 1.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Washington County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 15336 (Gastonville) and 15339 (Hendersonville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.
