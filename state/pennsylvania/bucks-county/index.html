Where Bucks County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Bucks County is in the south-east of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 34 miles from north to south and 32 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 18981 (Zionhill) and 19007 (Bristol), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Montgomery County (15 miles), Hunterdon County, New Jersey (20 miles), Mercer County, New Jersey (21 miles) and Philadelphia County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Bucks County begin with 189 (50 codes), 190 (14 codes) and 180 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 189 is shared with 1 other county, Montgomery County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 48 of the 67 codes. Together they come to about 579 square miles of land (1,498 sq km). The largest is 18951 (Quakertown) with 71.7 square miles, and the smallest is 18962 (Silverdale) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 5.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bucks County; a code can reach across its border. 19 codes have no mapped area: 18039 (Durham), 18910 (Bedminster), 18911 (Blooming Glen), 18916 (Danboro), 18922 (Forest Grove) and 14 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Bucks County ranks 6th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 67 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bucks County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

Questions about Bucks County ZIP codes

Do all ZIP codes in Bucks County start with the same digits?
