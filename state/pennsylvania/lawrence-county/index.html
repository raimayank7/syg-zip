Where Lawrence County is

The ZIP codes of Lawrence County are found in the west of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 18 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 16117 (Ellwood City) and 16140 (New Bedford), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Mahoning County, Ohio (18 miles), Mercer County (21 miles), Beaver County (22 miles) and Butler County (25 miles).

The first three digits

All 19 codes here begin with the same three digits, 161. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 161 is shared with 3 other counties, Beaver County, Crawford County and Mercer County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 16 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 370 square miles of land (959 sq km). The largest is 16101 (New Castle) with 69.2 square miles, and the smallest is 16172 (New Wilmington) with 0.08. The middle-sized code here covers 24.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lawrence County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 16103 (New Castle), 16107 (New Castle) and 16108 (New Castle). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lawrence County ranks 45th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lawrence County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

  • New Castle, PA 16101 (one of 6 codes for New Castle; the street decides which)
  • New Wilmington, PA 16142 (one of 2 codes for New Wilmington; the street decides which)
  • Bessemer, PA 16112

New Castle is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
