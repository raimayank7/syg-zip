Where Berks County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Berks County fall in the south-east of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 33 miles from north to south and 39 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 18056 (Hereford) and 19544 (Mount Aetna), about 39 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lehigh County (23 miles), Lebanon County (28 miles), Schuylkill County (29 miles) and Lancaster County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Berks County begin with 195 (40 codes), 196 (12 codes) and 180 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 195 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Chester County, Lancaster County and Montgomery County, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 49 of the 53 codes. Together they come to about 845 square miles of land (2,188 sq km). The largest is 19506 (Bernville) with 63.3 square miles, and the smallest is 19545 (New Berlinville) with 0.02. The middle-sized code here covers 11.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Berks County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 19519 (Earlville), 19548 (Pine Forge), 19603 (Reading) and 19612 (Reading). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Berks County ranks 13th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 53 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Berks County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

  • Reading, PA 19601 (one of 12 codes for Reading; the street decides which)
  • Bally, PA 19503
  • Barto, PA 19504

Reading is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
