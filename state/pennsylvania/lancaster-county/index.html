Where Lancaster County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Lancaster County is in the south-east of Pennsylvania. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 34 miles, and from east to west about 37 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 17502 (Bainbridge) and 17555 (Narvon), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Lebanon County (21 miles), York County (27 miles), Chester County (29 miles) and Berks County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lancaster County begin with 175 (60 codes), 176 (11 codes), 170 (1 code) and 195 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 175, so those three digits alone point to Lancaster County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 53 of the 73 codes. Together they come to about 942 square miles of land (2,440 sq km). The largest is 17545 (Manheim) with 69.8 square miles, and the smallest is 17504 (Bausman) with 0.02. The middle-sized code here covers 14.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Water adds about 39.6 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lancaster County; a code can reach across its border. 20 codes have no mapped area: 17503 (Bart), 17521 (Elm), 17528 (Goodville), 17533 (Hopeland), 17537 (Lampeter) and 15 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lancaster County ranks 5th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 73 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lancaster County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

  • Lancaster, PA 17601 (one of 11 codes for Lancaster; the street decides which)
  • Ronks, PA 17572 (one of 2 codes for Ronks; the street decides which)
  • Adamstown, PA 19501

Lancaster is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 15 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
