Where Sullivan County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Sullivan County is in the north-east of Pennsylvania. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 17768 (Shunk) and 18628 (Lopez), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Bradford County (23 miles), Lycoming County (29 miles), Wyoming County (31 miles) and Columbia County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Sullivan County begin with 186 (6 codes) and 177 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 186 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Carbon County, Columbia County and Lackawanna County, and has 41 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 453 square miles of land (1,175 sq km). The largest is 18614 (Dushore) with 153 square miles, and the smallest is 18632 (Mildred) with 7.2. The middle-sized code here covers 30.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sullivan County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Sullivan County ranks 63rd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.
