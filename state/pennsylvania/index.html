Pennsylvania ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 2,176 ZIP codes for Pennsylvania, about 5.3% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 3rd among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 67 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Allegheny County (142), Philadelphia County (85), Westmoreland County (85), Montgomery County (83) and Lancaster County (73). The fewest are in Montour County, with 3.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Pennsylvania uses 47 of these prefixes, from 150 to 196. The busiest are 170 (92 codes, most of them for Camp Hill), 190 (85 codes, most of them for Levittown), 191 (81 codes, most of them for Philadelphia) and 156 (77 codes, most of them for Greensburg). The first digit 1 is shared with Delaware and New York.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 1,833 of the 2,176 codes. The largest is 16720 (Austin, Potter County) at 269 square miles and the smallest is 19109 (Philadelphia) at under 0.01; the middle value is 11.9 square miles. The other 343 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 16428 (North East) and the southernmost 19351 (Lewisville); the one farthest east is 18336 (Matamoras) and farthest west 16155 (Villa Maria).

Place names and addresses in Pennsylvania

Of the 1,786 place names in the list, 1,713 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Philadelphia (87), Pittsburgh (75), Erie (26) and Harrisburg (26). 5 place names appear under more than one county, among them Philadelphia, Bethlehem, East Stroudsburg and Pottstown: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters PA and the code, as in Philadelphia, PA 19019. The name of the county is left out.
