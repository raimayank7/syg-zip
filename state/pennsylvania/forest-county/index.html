Where Forest County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Forest County fall in the north-west of Pennsylvania. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 18 miles, and from east to west about 14 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 15828 (Clarington) and 16321 (East Hickory), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Clarion County (20 miles), Warren County (24 miles), Venango County (25 miles) and Jefferson County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Forest County begin with 163 (4 codes), 162 (2 codes) and 158 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 163 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Clarion County, Crawford County and McKean County, and has 49 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 383 square miles of land (992 sq km). The largest is 16239 (Marienville) with 190 square miles, and the smallest is 16321 (East Hickory) with 1.0. The middle-sized code here covers 7.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Forest County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Forest County ranks 65th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Forest County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:
