Where Northumberland County is

The ZIP codes of Northumberland County are found in the east of Pennsylvania. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 32 miles, and from east to west about 24 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 17017 (Dalmatia) and 17772 (Turbotville), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Montour County (9.4 miles), Union County (19 miles), Columbia County (19 miles) and Snyder County (20 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Northumberland County begin with 178 (20 codes), 177 (4 codes) and 170 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 178 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Columbia County, Montour County and Snyder County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 24 of the 25 codes. Together they come to about 447 square miles of land (1,159 sq km). The largest is 17801 (Sunbury) with 66.1 square miles, and the smallest is 17730 (Dewart) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 19.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Water adds about 24.2 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Northumberland County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 17877 (Snydertown). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Northumberland County ranks 33rd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 25 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.
