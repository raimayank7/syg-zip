Where Allegheny County is

The ZIP codes of Allegheny County are found in the south-west of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 29 miles from north to south and 32 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 15026 (Clinton) and 15065 (Natrona Heights), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Washington County (20 miles), Beaver County (25 miles), Westmoreland County (25 miles) and Butler County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Allegheny County begin with 152 (75 codes), 150 (37 codes) and 151 (30 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 152, so those three digits alone point to Allegheny County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 104 of the 142 codes. Together they come to about 709 square miles of land (1,837 sq km). The largest is 15108 (Coraopolis) with 39.5 square miles, and the smallest is 15075 (Rural Ridge) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 2.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Allegheny County; a code can reach across its border. 38 codes have no mapped area: 15032 (Curtisville), 15091 (Wildwood), 15095 (Warrendale), 15096 (Warrendale), 15127 (Ingomar) and 33 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Pennsylvania has more ZIP codes: Allegheny County holds 142 of the 2,176 in the state list, about 6.5%.

Writing an address in Allegheny County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

  • Mckeesport, PA 15131 (one of 5 codes for Mckeesport; the street decides which)
  • Warrendale, PA 15086 (one of 3 codes for Warrendale; the street decides which)
