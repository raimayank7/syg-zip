Where Pike County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Pike County is in the north-east of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 29 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 18336 (Matamoras) and 18426 (Greentown), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Sussex County, New Jersey (22 miles), Wayne County (25 miles), Monroe County (26 miles) and Sullivan County, New York (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Pike County begin with 183 (7 codes) and 184 (7 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 183 is shared with 2 other counties, Monroe County and Northampton County, and has 40 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 461 square miles of land (1,195 sq km). The largest is 18328 (Dingmans Ferry) with 112 square miles, and the smallest is 18457 (Rowland) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 17.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Water adds about 18.6 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pike County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Pike County ranks 52nd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pike County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

  • Bushkill, PA 18324
  • Dingmans Ferry, PA 18328
  • East Stroudsburg, PA 18302

East Stroudsburg shares a name with a place in another part of Pennsylvania; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
