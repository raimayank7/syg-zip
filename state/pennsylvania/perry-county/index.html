Where Perry County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Perry County fall in the south of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 18 miles from north to south and 32 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 17045 (Liverpool) and 17071 (New Germantown), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Juniata County (12 miles), Cumberland County (16 miles), Dauphin County (22 miles) and Mifflin County (26 miles).

The first three digits

All 14 codes here begin with the same three digits, 170. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 170 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Cumberland County, Dauphin County and Huntingdon County, and has 92 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 575 square miles of land (1,489 sq km). The largest is 17062 (Millerstown) with 82.5 square miles, and the smallest is 17069 (New Buffalo) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 35.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Perry County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Perry County ranks 52nd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.
