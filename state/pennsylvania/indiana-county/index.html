Where Indiana County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Indiana County fall in the south-west of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 35 miles from north to south and 29 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 15725 (Clarksburg) and 15742 (Glen Campbell), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cambria County (22 miles), Armstrong County (23 miles), Jefferson County (28 miles) and Westmoreland County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Indiana County begin with 157 (37 codes), 159 (4 codes), 162 (3 codes) and 156 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 157 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Armstrong County, Cambria County and Clearfield County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 44 of the 45 codes. Together they come to about 793 square miles of land (2,053 sq km). The largest is 15701 (Indiana) with 112 square miles, and the smallest is 15752 (Kent) with 0.07. The middle-sized code here covers 1.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Indiana County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 15758 (Marchand). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Indiana County ranks 18th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 45 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Indiana County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

Questions about Indiana County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Indiana, PA?

Do all ZIP codes in Indiana County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Indiana County covers the most land?
