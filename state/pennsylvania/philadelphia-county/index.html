Where Philadelphia County is

The ZIP codes of Philadelphia County are found in the south-east of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 19153 (Philadelphia) and 19154 (Philadelphia), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Camden County, New Jersey (11 miles), Delaware County (13 miles), Gloucester County, New Jersey (17 miles) and Montgomery County (17 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Philadelphia County begin with 191 (79 codes), 190 (4 codes) and 192 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 191 is shared with 2 other counties, Delaware County and Montgomery County, and has 81 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 48 of the 85 codes. Together they come to about 136 square miles of land (353 sq km). The largest is 19153 (Philadelphia) with 10.1 square miles, and the smallest is 19109 (Philadelphia) with under 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 2.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 9.4 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Philadelphia County; a code can reach across its border. 37 codes have no mapped area: 19019, 19092, 19093, 19099, 19101 and 32 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Philadelphia County ranks 2nd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 85 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Philadelphia County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

  • Philadelphia, PA 19019 (one of 85 codes for Philadelphia; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named Philadelphia are also listed under Delaware County and Montgomery County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Philadelphia is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
