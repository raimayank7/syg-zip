Where Montour County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Montour County fall in the east of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 5.8 miles from north to south and 3.6 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 17822 (Danville) and 17884 (Washingtonville), about 6.9 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Northumberland County (9.4 miles), Columbia County (13 miles), Union County (21 miles) and Snyder County (26 miles).

The first three digits

All 3 codes here begin with the same three digits, 178. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 178 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Columbia County, Northumberland County and Snyder County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 135 square miles of land (349 sq km). The largest is 17821 (Danville) with 134 square miles, and the smallest is 17884 (Washingtonville) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 0.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Montour County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Montour County ranks 66th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 3 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Montour County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

  • Danville, PA 17821 (one of 2 codes for Danville; the street decides which)
  • Washingtonville, PA 17884

Danville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 16 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
