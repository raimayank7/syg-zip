Where Cumberland County is

The ZIP codes of Cumberland County are found in the south of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 18 miles from north to south and 37 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 17070 (New Cumberland) and 17240 (Newburg), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Perry County (16 miles), Dauphin County (18 miles), Adams County (21 miles) and Juniata County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Cumberland County begin with 170 (17 codes) and 172 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 170 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Dauphin County, Huntingdon County and Juniata County, and has 92 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 21 codes. Together they come to about 580 square miles of land (1,503 sq km). The largest is 17257 (Shippensburg) with 115 square miles, and the smallest is 17093 (Summerdale) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 15.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cumberland County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 17001 (Camp Hill), 17012 (Camp Hill), 17072 (New Kingstown) and 17089 (Camp Hill). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Cumberland County ranks 39th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 21 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cumberland County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

  • Camp Hill, PA 17001 (one of 4 codes for Camp Hill; the street decides which)
  • Carlisle, PA 17013 (one of 2 codes for Carlisle; the street decides which)
  • Mechanicsburg, PA 17050 (one of 2 codes for Mechanicsburg; the street decides which)

Questions about Cumberland County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Camp Hill, PA?

Camp Hill has 4 ZIP codes in Cumberland County: 17001, 17011, 17012, 17089. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Cumberland County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 170, on 17 of the 21 codes.

Which ZIP code in Cumberland County covers the most land?

17257 (Shippensburg), at about 115 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
