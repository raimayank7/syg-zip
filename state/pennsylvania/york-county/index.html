Where York County is

The ZIP codes of York County are found in the south-east of Pennsylvania. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 28 miles, and from east to west about 37 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 17019 (Dillsburg) and 17314 (Delta), about 44 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Adams County (25 miles), Lancaster County (27 miles), Cumberland County (28 miles) and Carroll County, Maryland (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of York County begin with 173 (40 codes), 174 (8 codes) and 170 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 173 is shared with 1 other county, Adams County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 41 of the 49 codes. Together they come to about 898 square miles of land (2,326 sq km). The largest is 17331 (Hanover) with 74.4 square miles, and the smallest is 17311 (Codorus) with 0.05. The middle-sized code here covers 18.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of York County; a code can reach across its border. 8 codes have no mapped area: 17312 (Craley), 17332 (Hanover), 17333 (Hanover), 17334 (Hanover), 17335 (Hanover) and 3 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, York County ranks 15th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 49 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in York County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

  • York, PA 17401 (one of 8 codes for York; the street decides which)
  • Hanover, PA 17331 (one of 5 codes for Hanover; the street decides which)
  • Airville, PA 17302
