Where Warren County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Warren County is in the north-west of Pennsylvania. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 19 miles, and from east to west about 28 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 16347 (Sheffield) and 16405 (Columbus), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Forest County (24 miles), Chautauqua County, New York (29 miles), McKean County (38 miles) and Venango County (39 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Warren County begin with 163 (15 codes) and 164 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 163 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Clarion County, Crawford County and Forest County, and has 49 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 16 of the 20 codes. Together they come to about 882 square miles of land (2,284 sq km). The largest is 16365 (Warren) with 139 square miles, and the smallest is 16312 (Chandlers Valley) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 49.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Warren County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 16366 (Warren), 16367 (Warren), 16368 (Irvine) and 16369 (Irvine). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Warren County ranks 42nd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 20 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.
