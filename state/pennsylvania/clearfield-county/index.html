Where Clearfield County is

The ZIP codes of Clearfield County are found in the west of Pennsylvania. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 32 miles, and from east to west about 39 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 15721 (Burnside) and 16871 (Pottersdale), about 47 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Elk County (32 miles), Jefferson County (33 miles), Centre County (33 miles) and Cameron County (35 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Clearfield County begin with 168 (26 codes), 166 (14 codes), 158 (5 codes) and 157 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 168 is shared with 3 other counties, Centre County, Clinton County and Huntingdon County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 43 of the 48 codes. Together they come to about 1,196 square miles of land (3,097 sq km). The largest is 16830 (Clearfield) with 132 square miles, and the smallest is 16840 (Hawk Run) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 19.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clearfield County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 16663 (Morann), 16681 (Smokerun), 16698 (Houtzdale), 16850 (Lecontes Mills) and 16873 (Shawville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Clearfield County ranks 16th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 48 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clearfield County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:
