Where Greene County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Greene County is in the south-west of Pennsylvania. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 29 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 15351 (Nemacolin) and 15352 (New Freeport), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Monongalia County, West Virginia (17 miles), Fayette County (22 miles), Washington County (23 miles) and Marion County, West Virginia (24 miles).

The first three digits

All 23 codes here begin with the same three digits, 153. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 153 is shared with 1 other county, Washington County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 553 square miles of land (1,433 sq km). The largest is 15370 (Waynesburg) with 148 square miles, and the smallest is 15353 (Nineveh) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 15.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Greene County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Greene County ranks 36th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 23 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.
