Where Butler County is

The ZIP codes of Butler County are found in the west of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 33 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 16030 (Eau Claire) and 16066 (Cranberry Township), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Armstrong County (24 miles), Lawrence County (25 miles), Beaver County (27 miles) and Allegheny County (32 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Butler County starts with 160. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 160 is shared with 2 other counties, Armstrong County and Clarion County, and has 42 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 31 of the 37 codes. Together they come to about 822 square miles of land (2,130 sq km). The largest is 16057 (Slippery Rock) with 88.7 square miles, and the smallest is 16024 (Callery) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 22.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Butler County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 16003 (Butler), 16016 (Boyers), 16017 (Boyers), 16018 (Boyers), 16021 (Branchton) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Butler County ranks 21st among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 37 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Butler County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

  • Boyers, PA 16016 (one of 4 codes for Boyers; the street decides which)
  • Butler, PA 16001 (one of 3 codes for Butler; the street decides which)
  • Branchton, PA 16021
