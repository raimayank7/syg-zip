Where Union County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Union County fall close to the middle of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 17885 (Weikert) and 17887 (White Deer), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Snyder County (12 miles), Northumberland County (19 miles), Montour County (21 miles) and Lycoming County (25 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Union County starts with 178. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 178 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Columbia County, Montour County and Northumberland County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 370 square miles of land (959 sq km). The largest is 17844 (Mifflinburg) with 107 square miles, and the smallest is 17880 (Swengel) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 12.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Union County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 17883 (Vicksburg). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Union County ranks 52nd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.
