Where Bradford County is

The ZIP codes of Bradford County are found in the north-east of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 36 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 17735 (Grover) and 18851 (Warren Center), about 41 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Sullivan County (23 miles), Tioga County, New York (27 miles), Chemung County, New York (27 miles) and Wyoming County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Bradford County begin with 188 (17 codes), 169 (6 codes) and 177 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 188 is shared with 1 other county, Susquehanna County, and has 37 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 22 of the 25 codes. Together they come to about 1,155 square miles of land (2,991 sq km). The largest is 16947 (Troy) with 112 square miles, and the smallest is 16910 (Alba) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 44.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bradford County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 16945 (Sylvania), 17735 (Grover) and 18815 (Camptown). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Bradford County ranks 33rd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 25 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bradford County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

  • Alba, PA 16910
  • Athens, PA 18810
  • Burlington, PA 18814

Alba is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
