Where Adams County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Adams County is in the south of Pennsylvania. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 17316 (East Berlin) and 17320 (Fairfield), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cumberland County (21 miles), York County (25 miles), Franklin County (25 miles) and Carroll County, Maryland (27 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Adams County starts with 173. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 173 is shared with 1 other county, York County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 15 of the 18 codes. Together they come to about 531 square miles of land (1,374 sq km). The largest is 17325 (Gettysburg) with 126 square miles, and the smallest is 17303 (Arendtsville) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 38.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Adams County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 17310 (Cashtown), 17337 (Idaville) and 17375 (Peach Glen). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Adams County ranks 47th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.
