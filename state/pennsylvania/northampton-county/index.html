Where Northampton County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Northampton County is in the east of Pennsylvania. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 18088 (Walnutport) and 18351 (Portland), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Lehigh County (14 miles), Warren County, New Jersey (20 miles), Monroe County (22 miles) and Carbon County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Northampton County begin with 180 (27 codes) and 183 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 180 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Berks County, Bucks County and Carbon County, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 23 of the 29 codes. Together they come to about 381 square miles of land (988 sq km). The largest is 18013 (Bangor) with 63.0 square miles, and the smallest is 18086 (Treichlers) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 16.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Northampton County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 18001 (Lehigh Valley), 18002 (Lehigh Valley), 18003 (Lehigh Valley), 18016 (Bethlehem), 18043 (Easton) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Northampton County ranks 27th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 29 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Northampton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

  • Bethlehem, PA 18015 (one of 5 codes for Bethlehem; the street decides which)
  • Easton, PA 18040 (one of 5 codes for Easton; the street decides which)
  • Lehigh Valley, PA 18001 (one of 3 codes for Lehigh Valley; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named Bethlehem are also listed under Lehigh County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Bethlehem is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Northampton County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Bethlehem, PA?

Bethlehem has 5 ZIP codes in Northampton County: 18015, 18016, 18017, 18018, 18020. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Northampton County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Northampton County covers the most land?
