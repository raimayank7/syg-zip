Where Clarion County is

The ZIP codes of Clarion County are found in the west of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 29 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 16028 (East Brady) and 16260 (Vowinckel), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Forest County (20 miles), Venango County (21 miles), Jefferson County (24 miles) and Armstrong County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Clarion County begin with 162 (19 codes), 163 (6 codes) and 160 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 162 is shared with 3 other counties, Armstrong County, Forest County and Indiana County, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 23 of the 29 codes. Together they come to about 581 square miles of land (1,504 sq km). The largest is 16242 (New Bethlehem) with 93.6 square miles, and the smallest is 16213 (Callensburg) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 15.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clarion County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 16058 (Turkey City), 16221 (Curllsville), 16225 (Fisher), 16234 (Limestone), 16257 (Snydersburg) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Clarion County ranks 27th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 29 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clarion County
