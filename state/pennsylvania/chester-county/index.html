Where Chester County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Chester County is in the south-east of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 33 miles from north to south and 32 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 19362 (Nottingham) and 19457 (Parker Ford), about 40 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Delaware County (18 miles), New Castle County, Delaware (23 miles), Montgomery County (25 miles) and Lancaster County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Chester County begin with 193 (46 codes), 194 (16 codes) and 195 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 193 is shared with 1 other county, Delaware County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 37 of the 63 codes. Together they come to about 755 square miles of land (1,955 sq km). The largest is 19320 (Coatesville) with 81.6 square miles, and the smallest is 19369 (Sadsburyville) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 16.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Chester County; a code can reach across its border. 26 codes have no mapped area: 19318 (Chatham), 19346 (Kelton), 19347 (Kemblesville), 19351 (Lewisville), 19353 (Lionville) and 21 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Chester County ranks 7th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 63 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Chester County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:
