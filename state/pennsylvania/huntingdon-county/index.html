Where Huntingdon County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Huntingdon County is in the south of Pennsylvania. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 42 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 16877 (Warriors Mark) and 17239 (Neelyton), about 43 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Blair County (20 miles), Mifflin County (24 miles), Fulton County (26 miles) and Bedford County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Huntingdon County begin with 166 (15 codes), 172 (8 codes), 170 (3 codes) and 168 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 166 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Bedford County, Blair County and Cambria County, and has 74 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 26 of the 27 codes. Together they come to about 838 square miles of land (2,171 sq km). The largest is 16652 (Huntingdon) with 174 square miles, and the smallest is 16654 (Huntingdon) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 22.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Huntingdon County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 16660 (Mc Connellstown). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Huntingdon County ranks 29th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 27 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Huntingdon County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

  • Huntingdon, PA 16652 (one of 2 codes for Huntingdon; the street decides which)
  • Alexandria, PA 16611
  • Blairs Mills, PA 17213

Questions about Huntingdon County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Huntingdon, PA?

Huntingdon has 2 ZIP codes in Huntingdon County: 16652, 16654. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Huntingdon County start with the same digits?

No. They use 4 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 166, on 15 of the 27 codes.

Which ZIP code in Huntingdon County covers the most land?

16652 (Huntingdon), at about 174 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
