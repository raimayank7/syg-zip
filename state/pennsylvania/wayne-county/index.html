Where Wayne County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Wayne County fall in the north-east of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 47 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 18424 (Gouldsboro) and 18461 (Starlight), about 48 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lackawanna County (21 miles), Pike County (25 miles), Susquehanna County (26 miles) and Sullivan County, New York (28 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Wayne County starts with 184. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 184 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Lackawanna County, Monroe County and Pike County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 23 of the 26 codes. Together they come to about 878 square miles of land (2,275 sq km). The largest is 18428 (Hawley) with 151 square miles, and the smallest is 18473 (White Mills) with 0.8. The middle-sized code here covers 28.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 29.8 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wayne County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 18427 (Hamlin), 18449 (Orson) and 18459 (South Canaan). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Wayne County ranks 31st among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 26 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.
