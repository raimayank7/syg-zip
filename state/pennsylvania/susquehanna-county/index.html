Where Susquehanna County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Susquehanna County fall in the north-east of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 34 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 18421 (Forest City) and 18830 (Little Meadows), about 40 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Wyoming County (20 miles), Broome County, New York (25 miles), Lackawanna County (26 miles) and Wayne County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Susquehanna County begin with 188 (20 codes) and 184 (6 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 188 is shared with 1 other county, Bradford County, and has 37 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 24 of the 26 codes. Together they come to about 797 square miles of land (2,065 sq km). The largest is 18801 (Montrose) with 180 square miles, and the smallest is 18413 (Clifford) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 25.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Susquehanna County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 18813 (Brooklyn) and 18827 (Lanesboro). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Susquehanna County ranks 31st among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 26 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.
