Where Delaware County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Delaware County fall in the south-east of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 19082 (Upper Darby) and 19317 (Chadds Ford), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Philadelphia County (13 miles), Gloucester County, New Jersey (17 miles), Chester County (18 miles) and Camden County, New Jersey (19 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Delaware County begin with 190 (46 codes), 193 (7 codes) and 191 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 190 is shared with 3 other counties, Bucks County, Montgomery County and Philadelphia County, and has 85 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 41 of the 54 codes. Together they come to about 216 square miles of land (558 sq km). The largest is 19063 (Media) with 25.1 square miles, and the smallest is 19052 (Lenni) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 2.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Delaware County; a code can reach across its border. 13 codes have no mapped area: 19016 (Chester), 19028 (Edgemont), 19037 (Glen Riddle Lima), 19039 (Gradyville), 19065 (Media) and 8 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Delaware County ranks 12th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 54 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Delaware County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

  • Wayne, PA 19080 (one of 4 codes for Wayne; the street decides which)
  • Concordville, PA 19331 (one of 3 codes for Concordville; the street decides which)
  • Media, PA 19063 (one of 3 codes for Media; the street decides which)

Wayne is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Philadelphia shares a name with a place in another part of Pennsylvania; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Delaware County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Wayne, PA?

Wayne has 4 ZIP codes in Delaware County: 19080, 19087, 19088, 19089. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Delaware County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 190, on 46 of the 54 codes.

Which ZIP code in Delaware County covers the most land?

19063 (Media), at about 25.1 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
