Where Cambria County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Cambria County fall in the south-west of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 30 miles from north to south and 28 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 15905 (Johnstown) and 16619 (Blandburg), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Blair County (19 miles), Indiana County (22 miles), Bedford County (32 miles) and Clearfield County (36 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Cambria County begin with 159 (30 codes), 166 (14 codes) and 157 (8 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 159 is shared with 3 other counties, Indiana County, Somerset County and Westmoreland County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 48 of the 52 codes. Together they come to about 727 square miles of land (1,884 sq km). The largest is 15931 (Ebensburg) with 81.3 square miles, and the smallest is 16699 (Cresson) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 8.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cambria County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 15907 (Johnstown), 15915 (Johnstown), 16629 (Coupon) and 16644 (Glasgow). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Cambria County ranks 14th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 52 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cambria County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

  • Johnstown, PA 15901 (one of 8 codes for Johnstown; the street decides which)
  • Cresson, PA 16630 (one of 2 codes for Cresson; the street decides which)
  • Ashville, PA 16613

Johnstown is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Cambria County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Johnstown, PA?

Johnstown has 8 ZIP codes in Cambria County: 15901, 15902, 15904, 15905, 15906, 15907, 15909, 15915. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Cambria County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Cambria County covers the most land?
