Where Luzerne County is

The ZIP codes of Luzerne County are found in the east of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 29 miles from north to south and 31 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 18246 (Rock Glen) and 18641 (Pittston), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Carbon County (24 miles), Columbia County (25 miles), Lackawanna County (25 miles) and Wyoming County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Luzerne County begin with 186 (23 codes), 187 (18 codes) and 182 (15 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 186 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Carbon County, Columbia County and Lackawanna County, and has 41 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 40 of the 56 codes. Together they come to about 898 square miles of land (2,325 sq km). The largest is 18661 (White Haven) with 121 square miles, and the smallest is 18627 (Lehman) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 14.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Luzerne County; a code can reach across its border. 16 codes have no mapped area: 18247 (Saint Johns), 18251 (Sybertsville), 18601 (Beach Haven), 18611 (Cambra), 18654 (Shawanese) and 11 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Luzerne County ranks 11th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 56 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Luzerne County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:
