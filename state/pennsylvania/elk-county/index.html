Where Elk County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Elk County is in the north-west of Pennsylvania. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 25 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 15821 (Benezett) and 16734 (James City), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cameron County (24 miles), Jefferson County (31 miles), McKean County (32 miles) and Clearfield County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Elk County begin with 158 (12 codes) and 167 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 158 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Cameron County, Clearfield County and Forest County, and has 31 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 672 square miles of land (1,742 sq km). The largest is 15853 (Ridgway) with 150 square miles, and the smallest is 15831 (Dagus Mines) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 37.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Elk County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 15822 (Brandy Camp) and 16728 (De Young). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Elk County ranks 52nd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.
