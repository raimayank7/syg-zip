Where Mercer County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Mercer County is in the north-west of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 16161 (Wheatland) and 16311 (Carlton), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Lawrence County (21 miles), Trumbull County, Ohio (23 miles), Crawford County (27 miles) and Venango County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Mercer County begin with 161 (19 codes) and 163 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 161 is shared with 3 other counties, Beaver County, Crawford County and Lawrence County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 652 square miles of land (1,689 sq km). The largest is 16137 (Mercer) with 109 square miles, and the smallest is 16151 (Sheakleyville) with 0.08. The middle-sized code here covers 27.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Mercer County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Mercer County ranks 42nd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 20 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Mercer County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

  • Carlton, PA 16311
  • Clark, PA 16113
  • Clarks Mills, PA 16114

Carlton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
