Where Somerset County is

The ZIP codes of Somerset County are found in the south-west of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 37 miles from north to south and 31 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 15411 (Addison) and 15963 (Windber), about 43 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Allegany County, Maryland (29 miles), Bedford County (32 miles), Garrett County, Maryland (34 miles) and Westmoreland County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Somerset County begin with 155 (28 codes), 159 (9 codes) and 154 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 155 is shared with 2 other counties, Bedford County and Fulton County, and has 40 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 35 of the 40 codes. Together they come to about 1,080 square miles of land (2,798 sq km). The largest is 15501 (Somerset) with 132 square miles, and the smallest is 15560 (Shanksville) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 22.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Somerset County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 15485 (Ursina), 15548 (Kantner), 15549 (Listie), 15553 (New Baltimore) and 15959 (Tire Hill). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Somerset County ranks 20th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 40 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Somerset County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

  • Somerset, PA 15501 (one of 2 codes for Somerset; the street decides which)
  • Acosta, PA 15520
  • Addison, PA 15411

Somerset is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 11 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
