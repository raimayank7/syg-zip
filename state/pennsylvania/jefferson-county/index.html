Where Jefferson County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Jefferson County is in the west of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 15778 (Timblin) and 15824 (Brockway), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Clarion County (24 miles), Indiana County (28 miles), Armstrong County (29 miles) and Elk County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Jefferson County begin with 157 (13 codes) and 158 (10 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 157 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Armstrong County, Cambria County and Clearfield County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 722 square miles of land (1,871 sq km). The largest is 15767 (Punxsutawney) with 160 square miles, and the smallest is 15847 (Knox Dale) with 0.02. The middle-sized code here covers 2.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jefferson County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Jefferson County ranks 36th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 23 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.
