Where Armstrong County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Armstrong County is in the west of Pennsylvania. Its codes spread over about 35 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 15774 (Shelocta) and 16049 (Parker), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Indiana County (23 miles), Butler County (24 miles), Clarion County (28 miles) and Jefferson County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Armstrong County begin with 162 (20 codes), 156 (4 codes), 157 (2 codes) and 160 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 162 is shared with 3 other counties, Clarion County, Forest County and Indiana County, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 26 of the 27 codes. Together they come to about 620 square miles of land (1,607 sq km). The largest is 16201 (Kittanning) with 112 square miles, and the smallest is 16236 (Mc Grann) with 0.07. The middle-sized code here covers 7.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Pennsylvania is 11.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Armstrong County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 16261 (Widnoon). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Armstrong County ranks 29th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Pennsylvania (equal counts share a rank), with 27 of the 2,176 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Armstrong County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters PA and the ZIP code:

  • Adrian, PA 16210
  • Cadogan, PA 16212
  • Cowansville, PA 16218

Adrian is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
