Where McCook County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of McCook County fall in the south-east of South Dakota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 14 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 57048 (Montrose) and 57374 (Spencer), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Hanson County (19 miles), Turner County (27 miles), Miner County (27 miles) and Lake County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of McCook County begin with 570 (3 codes) and 573 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 570 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Bon Homme County, Brookings County and Clay County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 597 square miles of land (1,547 sq km). The largest is 57058 (Salem) with 159 square miles, and the smallest is 57374 (Spencer) with 77.8. The middle-sized code here covers 130 square miles; the middle value for all of South Dakota is 136. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of McCook County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, McCook County ranks 31st among the 66 counties and county-level areas of South Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 385 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in McCook County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SD and the ZIP code:

  • Bridgewater, SD 57319
  • Canistota, SD 57012
  • Montrose, SD 57048

Bridgewater is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
