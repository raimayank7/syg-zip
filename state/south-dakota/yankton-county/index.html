Where Yankton County is

The ZIP codes of Yankton County are found in the south-east of South Dakota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 11 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 57031 (Gayville) and 57040 (Lesterville), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Clay County (16 miles), Bon Homme County (26 miles), Cedar County, Nebraska (28 miles) and Turner County (28 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Yankton County starts with 570. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 570 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Bon Homme County, Brookings County and Clay County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 402 square miles of land (1,042 sq km). The largest is 57078 (Yankton) with 111 square miles, and the smallest is 57031 (Gayville) with 39.4. The middle-sized code here covers 63.4 square miles; the middle value for all of South Dakota is 136. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Yankton County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Yankton County ranks 22nd among the 66 counties and county-level areas of South Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 385 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Yankton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SD and the ZIP code:
