Where Brown County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Brown County fall in the north-east of South Dakota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 40 miles, and from east to west about 26 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 57439 (Ferney) and 57441 (Frederick), about 42 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Dickey County, North Dakota (35 miles), Day County (36 miles), Marshall County (37 miles) and Spink County (39 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Brown County starts with 574. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 574 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Day County, Edmunds County and Faulk County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 1,655 square miles of land (4,287 sq km). The largest is 57401 (Aberdeen) with 279 square miles, and the smallest is 57439 (Ferney) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 112 square miles; the middle value for all of South Dakota is 136. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Brown County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 57402 (Aberdeen). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Brown County ranks 2nd among the 66 counties and county-level areas of South Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 385 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Brown County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SD and the ZIP code:

  • Aberdeen, SD 57401 (one of 2 codes for Aberdeen; the street decides which)
  • Barnard, SD 57426
  • Bath, SD 57427

Aberdeen is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
