Where Kingsbury County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Kingsbury County is in the east of South Dakota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 39 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 57212 (Arlington) and 57353 (Iroquois), about 39 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Hamlin County (21 miles), Miner County (29 miles), Lake County (29 miles) and Brookings County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Kingsbury County begin with 572 (5 codes), 570 (1 code) and 573 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 572 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Brookings County, Clark County and Codington County, and has 56 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,043 square miles of land (2,700 sq km). The largest is 57353 (Iroquois) with 299 square miles, and the smallest is 57214 (Badger) with 5.6. The middle-sized code here covers 146 square miles; the middle value for all of South Dakota is 136. Water adds about 43.2 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Kingsbury County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Kingsbury County ranks 12th among the 66 counties and county-level areas of South Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 385 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Kingsbury County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SD and the ZIP code:

  • Arlington, SD 57212
  • Badger, SD 57214
  • De Smet, SD 57231

Arlington is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 20 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
