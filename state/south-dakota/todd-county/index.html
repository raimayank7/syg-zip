Where Todd County is

The ZIP codes of Todd County are found in the south of South Dakota. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 32 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 57563 (Okreek) and 57566 (Parmelee), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Mellette County (18 miles), Cherry County, Nebraska (35 miles), Jones County (44 miles) and Tripp County (47 miles).

The first three digits

All 5 codes here begin with the same three digits, 575. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 575 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Bennett County, Gregory County and Haakon County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 794 square miles of land (2,056 sq km). The largest is 57555 (Mission) with 340 square miles, and the smallest is 57563 (Okreek) with 29.7. The middle-sized code here covers 153 square miles; the middle value for all of South Dakota is 136. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Todd County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Todd County ranks 31st among the 66 counties and county-level areas of South Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 385 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Todd County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SD and the ZIP code:

  • Mission, SD 57555
  • Okreek, SD 57563
  • Parmelee, SD 57566
