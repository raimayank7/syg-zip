Where Brookings County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Brookings County fall in the east of South Dakota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 57026 (Elkton) and 57061 (Sinai), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Moody County (25 miles), Lake County (25 miles), Lincoln County, Minnesota (26 miles) and Deuel County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Brookings County begin with 570 (6 codes) and 572 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 570 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Bon Homme County, Clay County and Hutchinson County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 746 square miles of land (1,932 sq km). The largest is 57026 (Elkton) with 178 square miles, and the smallest is 57007 (Brookings) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 114 square miles; the middle value for all of South Dakota is 136. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Brookings County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Brookings County ranks 8th among the 66 counties and county-level areas of South Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 385 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Brookings County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SD and the ZIP code:

  • Brookings, SD 57006 (one of 2 codes for Brookings; the street decides which)
  • Aurora, SD 57002
  • Bruce, SD 57220

Questions about Brookings County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Brookings, SD?

Brookings has 2 ZIP codes in Brookings County: 57006, 57007. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Brookings County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 570, on 6 of the 8 codes.

Which ZIP code in Brookings County covers the most land?

57026 (Elkton), at about 178 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
