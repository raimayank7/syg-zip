Where Davison County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Davison County fall in the south-east of South Dakota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 14 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 57334 (Ethan) and 57363 (Mount Vernon), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Hanson County (17 miles), Sanborn County (23 miles), Aurora County (24 miles) and Douglas County (25 miles).

The first three digits

All 3 codes here begin with the same three digits, 573. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 573 is shared with 19 other counties, such as Aurora County, Beadle County and Bon Homme County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 534 square miles of land (1,384 sq km). The largest is 57301 (Mitchell) with 238 square miles, and the smallest is 57334 (Ethan) with 106. The middle-sized code here covers 190 square miles; the middle value for all of South Dakota is 136. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Davison County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Davison County ranks 50th among the 66 counties and county-level areas of South Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 3 of the 385 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Davison County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SD and the ZIP code:

  • Ethan, SD 57334
  • Mitchell, SD 57301
  • Mount Vernon, SD 57363
