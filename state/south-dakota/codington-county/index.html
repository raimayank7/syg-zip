Where Codington County is

The ZIP codes of Codington County are found in the north-east of South Dakota. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 57245 (Kranzburg) and 57272 (Wallace), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hamlin County (23 miles), Grant County (26 miles), Clark County (27 miles) and Deuel County (33 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Codington County starts with 572. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 572 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Brookings County, Clark County and Day County, and has 56 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 721 square miles of land (1,866 sq km). The largest is 57201 (Watertown) with 319 square miles, and the smallest is 57245 (Kranzburg) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 113 square miles; the middle value for all of South Dakota is 136. Water adds about 31.5 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Codington County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Codington County ranks 22nd among the 66 counties and county-level areas of South Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 385 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Codington County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SD and the ZIP code:

  • Florence, SD 57235
  • Henry, SD 57243
  • Kranzburg, SD 57245

Florence is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 16 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
