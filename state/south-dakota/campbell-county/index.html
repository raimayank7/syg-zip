Where Campbell County is

The ZIP codes of Campbell County are found in the north of South Dakota. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 12 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 57646 (Mound City) and 57648 (Pollock), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Walworth County (23 miles), Emmons County, North Dakota (36 miles), McIntosh County, North Dakota (37 miles) and Sioux County, North Dakota (43 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Campbell County starts with 576. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 576 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Corson County, Dewey County and Harding County, and has 32 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 566 square miles of land (1,466 sq km). The largest is 57648 (Pollock) with 237 square miles, and the smallest is 57646 (Mound City) with 128. The middle-sized code here covers 200 square miles; the middle value for all of South Dakota is 136. Water adds about 25.8 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Campbell County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Campbell County ranks 50th among the 66 counties and county-level areas of South Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 3 of the 385 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Campbell County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SD and the ZIP code:

  • Mound City, SD 57646
  • Pollock, SD 57648
