Where Douglas County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Douglas County fall in the south-east of South Dakota. Its codes spread over about 12 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 57330 (Delmont) and 57364 (New Holland), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Charles Mix County (17 miles), Aurora County (24 miles), Davison County (25 miles) and Hutchinson County (31 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Douglas County starts with 573. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 573 is shared with 19 other counties, such as Aurora County, Beadle County and Bon Homme County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 493 square miles of land (1,278 sq km). The largest is 57313 (Armour) with 181 square miles, and the smallest is 57364 (New Holland) with 1.1. The middle-sized code here covers 116 square miles; the middle value for all of South Dakota is 136. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Douglas County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Douglas County ranks 31st among the 66 counties and county-level areas of South Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 385 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Douglas County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SD and the ZIP code:

  • Armour, SD 57313
  • Corsica, SD 57328
  • Delmont, SD 57330
