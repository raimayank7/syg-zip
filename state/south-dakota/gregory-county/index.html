Where Gregory County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Gregory County fall in the south-east of South Dakota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 14 miles, and from east to west about 34 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 57335 (Fairfax) and 57529 (Dallas), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Boyd County, Nebraska (28 miles), Keya Paha County, Nebraska (29 miles), Charles Mix County (34 miles) and Tripp County (39 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Gregory County begin with 575 (5 codes) and 573 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 575 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Bennett County, Haakon County and Hughes County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,271 square miles of land (3,292 sq km). The largest is 57533 (Gregory) with 377 square miles, and the smallest is 57571 (Saint Charles) with 10.5. The middle-sized code here covers 159 square miles; the middle value for all of South Dakota is 136. Water adds about 45.1 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Gregory County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Gregory County ranks 12th among the 66 counties and county-level areas of South Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 385 codes in the state list.
