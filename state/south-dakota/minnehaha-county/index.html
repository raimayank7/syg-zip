Where Minnehaha County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Minnehaha County fall in the south-east of South Dakota. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 29 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 57035 (Humboldt) and 57068 (Valley Springs), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Lincoln County (22 miles), Moody County (24 miles), Turner County (27 miles) and Rock County, Minnesota (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Minnehaha County begin with 571 (15 codes) and 570 (11 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 571, so those three digits alone point to Minnehaha County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 20 of the 26 codes. Together they come to about 925 square miles of land (2,396 sq km). The largest is 57022 (Dell Rapids) with 141 square miles, and the smallest is 57197 (Sioux Falls) with 0.08. The middle-sized code here covers 30.4 square miles; the middle value for all of South Dakota is 136. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Minnehaha County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 57101 (Sioux Falls), 57109 (Sioux Falls), 57118 (Sioux Falls), 57186 (Sioux Falls), 57193 (Sioux Falls) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of South Dakota has more ZIP codes: Minnehaha County holds 26 of the 385 in the state list, about 6.8%.

Writing an address in Minnehaha County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SD and the ZIP code:

  • Sioux Falls, SD 57101 (one of 15 codes for Sioux Falls; the street decides which)
  • Baltic, SD 57003
  • Brandon, SD 57005
