Where Hamlin County is

The ZIP codes of Hamlin County are found in the north-east of South Dakota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 28 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 57221 (Bryant) and 57234 (Estelline), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Kingsbury County (21 miles), Codington County (23 miles), Deuel County (26 miles) and Clark County (29 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Hamlin County starts with 572. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 572 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Brookings County, Clark County and Codington County, and has 56 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 602 square miles of land (1,560 sq km). The largest is 57223 (Castlewood) with 125 square miles, and the smallest is 57248 (Lake Norden) with 68.0. The middle-sized code here covers 102 square miles; the middle value for all of South Dakota is 136. Water adds about 23.6 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hamlin County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Hamlin County ranks 22nd among the 66 counties and county-level areas of South Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 385 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hamlin County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SD and the ZIP code:

  • Bryant, SD 57221
  • Castlewood, SD 57223
  • Estelline, SD 57234

Bryant is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
