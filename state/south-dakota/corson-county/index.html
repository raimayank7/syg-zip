Where Corson County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Corson County is in the north of South Dakota. Its codes spread over about 32 miles from north to south and 65 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 57634 (Keldron) and 57658 (Wakpala), about 65 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Sioux County, North Dakota (35 miles), Dewey County (36 miles), Grant County, North Dakota (44 miles) and Campbell County (49 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Corson County starts with 576. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 576 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Campbell County, Dewey County and Harding County, and has 32 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,147 square miles of land (5,561 sq km). The largest is 57642 (Mc Laughlin) with 693 square miles, and the smallest is 57639 (Little Eagle) with 2.0. The middle-sized code here covers 194 square miles; the middle value for all of South Dakota is 136. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Corson County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Corson County ranks 6th among the 66 counties and county-level areas of South Dakota (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 385 codes in the state list.
