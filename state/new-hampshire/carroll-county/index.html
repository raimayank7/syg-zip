Where Carroll County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Carroll County fall in the east of New Hampshire. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 72 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 03846 (Jackson) and 03861 (Lee), about 73 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Belknap County (25 miles), Grafton County (36 miles), York County, Maine (36 miles) and Strafford County (38 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Carroll County begin with 038 (28 codes) and 032 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 038 is shared with 3 other counties, Belknap County, Rockingham County and Strafford County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 30 of the 31 codes. Together they come to about 951 square miles of land (2,462 sq km). The largest is 03813 (Center Conway) with 98.3 square miles, and the smallest is 03847 (Kearsarge) with 0.8. The middle-sized code here covers 25.4 square miles; the middle value for all of New Hampshire is 28.8. Water adds about 61.8 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Carroll County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 03896 (Wolfeboro Falls). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Carroll County ranks 4th among the 10 counties and county-level areas of New Hampshire (equal counts share a rank), with 31 of the 281 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Carroll County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NH and the ZIP code:

  • Bartlett, NH 03812
  • Center Conway, NH 03813
  • Center Ossipee, NH 03814

Bartlett is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
