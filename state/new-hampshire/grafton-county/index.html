Where Grafton County is

The ZIP codes of Grafton County are found in the west of New Hampshire. Its codes spread over about 74 miles from north to south and 44 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 03258 (Chichester) and 03771 (Monroe), about 77 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Orange County, Vermont (22 miles), Belknap County (35 miles), Carroll County (36 miles) and Windsor County, Vermont (37 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Grafton County begin with 037 (18 codes), 032 (17 codes) and 035 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 037 is shared with 1 other county, Sullivan County, and has 29 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 37 of the 40 codes. Together they come to about 1,731 square miles of land (4,483 sq km). The largest is 03215 (Waterville Valley) with 192 square miles, and the smallest is 03238 (Glencliff) with 1.0. The middle-sized code here covers 41.5 square miles; the middle value for all of New Hampshire is 28.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Grafton County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 03749 (Enfield Center), 03756 (Lebanon) and 03769 (Lyme Center). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Grafton County ranks 2nd among the 10 counties and county-level areas of New Hampshire (equal counts share a rank), with 40 of the 281 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Grafton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NH and the ZIP code:

  • Lebanon, NH 03756 (one of 2 codes for Lebanon; the street decides which)
  • Ashland, NH 03217
  • Bath, NH 03740

Lebanon is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 18 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
