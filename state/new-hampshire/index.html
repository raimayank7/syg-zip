New Hampshire ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 281 ZIP codes for New Hampshire, about 0.7% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 44th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 10 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Rockingham County (44), Grafton County (40), Hillsborough County (39), Carroll County (31) and Merrimack County (29). The fewest are in Coos County, with 16.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. New Hampshire uses 9 of these prefixes, from 030 to 038. The busiest are 038 (77 codes, most of them for Portsmouth), 032 (64 codes, most of them for Tilton), 030 (38 codes, most of them for Nashua) and 037 (29 codes, most of them for Lebanon). The first digit 0 is shared with Connecticut, Maine, Massachusetts, New Jersey, New York, Rhode Island and Vermont.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 247 of the 281 codes. The largest is 03579 (Errol, Coos County) at 562 square miles and the smallest is 03604 (Drewsville) at 0.05; the middle value is 28.8 square miles. The other 34 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 03592 (Pittsburg) and the southernmost 03062 (Nashua); the one farthest east is 03854 (New Castle) and farthest west 03466 (West Chesterfield).

Place names and addresses in New Hampshire

Of the 253 place names in the list, 242 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Manchester (8), Nashua (5), Concord (4) and Portsmouth (4). In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters NH and the code, as in Manchester, NH 03101. The name of the county is left out.
