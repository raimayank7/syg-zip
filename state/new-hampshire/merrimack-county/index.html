Where Merrimack County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Merrimack County fall in the south of New Hampshire. Its codes spread over about 32 miles from north to south and 34 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 03106 (Hooksett) and 03230 (Danbury), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Belknap County (19 miles), Sullivan County (25 miles), Hillsborough County (29 miles) and Strafford County (37 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Merrimack County begin with 032 (21 codes), 033 (6 codes), 030 (1 code) and 031 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 032 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Belknap County, Carroll County and Grafton County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 26 of the 29 codes. Together they come to about 886 square miles of land (2,293 sq km). The largest is 03303 (Concord) with 67.8 square miles, and the smallest is 03231 (East Andover) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 35.1 square miles; the middle value for all of New Hampshire is 28.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Merrimack County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 03272 (South Newbury), 03302 (Concord) and 03305 (Concord). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Merrimack County ranks 5th among the 10 counties and county-level areas of New Hampshire (equal counts share a rank), with 29 of the 281 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Merrimack County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NH and the ZIP code:

  • Concord, NH 03301 (one of 4 codes for Concord; the street decides which)
  • Andover, NH 03216
  • Bow, NH 03304

Concord is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 12 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Merrimack County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Concord, NH?

Concord has 4 ZIP codes in Merrimack County: 03301, 03302, 03303, 03305. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Merrimack County start with the same digits?

No. They use 4 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 032, on 21 of the 29 codes.

Which ZIP code in Merrimack County covers the most land?

03303 (Concord), at about 67.8 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
