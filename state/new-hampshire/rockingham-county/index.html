Where Rockingham County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Rockingham County fall in the south-east of New Hampshire. Its codes spread over about 29 miles from north to south and 33 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 03053 (Londonderry) and 03854 (New Castle), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Strafford County (22 miles), Essex County, Massachusetts (24 miles), Hillsborough County (30 miles) and York County, Maine (38 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Rockingham County begin with 038 (27 codes), 030 (14 codes) and 032 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 038 is shared with 3 other counties, Belknap County, Carroll County and Strafford County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 36 of the 44 codes. Together they come to about 695 square miles of land (1,801 sq km). The largest is 03037 (Deerfield) with 48.6 square miles, and the smallest is 03291 (West Nottingham) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 14.5 square miles; the middle value for all of New Hampshire is 28.8. Water adds about 32.5 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Rockingham County; a code can reach across its border. 8 codes have no mapped area: 03040 (East Candia), 03041 (East Derry), 03073 (North Salem), 03802 (Portsmouth), 03803 (Portsmouth) and 3 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of New Hampshire has more ZIP codes: Rockingham County holds 44 of the 281 in the state list, about 15.7%.

Writing an address in Rockingham County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NH and the ZIP code:

  • Portsmouth, NH 03801 (one of 4 codes for Portsmouth; the street decides which)
  • Hampton, NH 03842 (one of 2 codes for Hampton; the street decides which)
  • Atkinson, NH 03811

Portsmouth is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Rockingham County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Portsmouth, NH?

Portsmouth has 4 ZIP codes in Rockingham County: 03801, 03802, 03803, 03804. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Rockingham County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 038, on 27 of the 44 codes.

Which ZIP code in Rockingham County covers the most land?

03037 (Deerfield), at about 48.6 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
