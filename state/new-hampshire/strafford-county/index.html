Where Strafford County is

The ZIP codes of Strafford County are found in the south-east of New Hampshire. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 03824 (Durham) and 03852 (Milton Mills), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are York County, Maine (19 miles), Rockingham County (22 miles), Belknap County (27 miles) and Merrimack County (37 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Strafford County starts with 038. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 038 is shared with 3 other counties, Belknap County, Carroll County and Rockingham County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 15 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 349 square miles of land (903 sq km). The largest is 03884 (Strafford) with 49.0 square miles, and the smallest is 03869 (Rollinsford) with 6.7. The middle-sized code here covers 22.4 square miles; the middle value for all of New Hampshire is 28.8. Water adds about 14.9 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Strafford County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 03815 (Center Strafford), 03821 (Dover), 03822 (Dover) and 03866 (Rochester). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Strafford County ranks 7th among the 10 counties and county-level areas of New Hampshire (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 281 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Strafford County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NH and the ZIP code:

  • Rochester, NH 03839 (one of 4 codes for Rochester; the street decides which)
  • Dover, NH 03820 (one of 3 codes for Dover; the street decides which)
  • Barrington, NH 03825

Rochester is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 12 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
