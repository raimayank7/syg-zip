Where Belknap County is

The ZIP codes of Belknap County are found in the south of New Hampshire. Its codes spread over about 26 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 03218 (Barnstead) and 03226 (Center Harbor), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Merrimack County (19 miles), Carroll County (25 miles), Strafford County (27 miles) and Grafton County (35 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Belknap County begin with 032 (16 codes) and 038 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 032 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Carroll County, Grafton County and Hillsborough County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 430 square miles of land (1,115 sq km). The largest is 03269 (Sanbornton) with 49.5 square miles, and the smallest is 03226 (Center Harbor) with 13.7. The middle-sized code here covers 33.5 square miles; the middle value for all of New Hampshire is 28.8. Water adds about 68.5 square miles, roughly 14% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Belknap County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 03247 (Laconia), 03252 (Lochmere), 03289 (Winnisquam), 03298 (Tilton) and 03299 (Tilton). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Belknap County ranks 7th among the 10 counties and county-level areas of New Hampshire (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 281 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Belknap County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NH and the ZIP code:

  • Tilton, NH 03276 (one of 3 codes for Tilton; the street decides which)
  • Laconia, NH 03246 (one of 2 codes for Laconia; the street decides which)
  • Alton, NH 03809

Questions about Belknap County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Tilton, NH?

Tilton has 3 ZIP codes in Belknap County: 03276, 03298, 03299. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Belknap County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 032, on 16 of the 19 codes.

Which ZIP code in Belknap County covers the most land?

03269 (Sanbornton), at about 49.5 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
