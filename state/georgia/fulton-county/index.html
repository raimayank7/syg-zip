Where Fulton County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Fulton County is in the north-west of Georgia. Its codes spread over about 41 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 30005 (Alpharetta) and 30268 (Palmetto), about 46 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Cobb County (12 miles), DeKalb County (13 miles), Clayton County (19 miles) and Douglas County (20 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Fulton County begin with 303 (61 codes), 311 (12 codes), 300 (8 codes) and 302 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 303 is shared with 1 other county, DeKalb County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 41 of the 85 codes. Together they come to about 589 square miles of land (1,525 sq km). The largest is 30268 (Palmetto) with 69.3 square miles, and the smallest is 30334 (Atlanta) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 9.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Fulton County; a code can reach across its border. 44 codes have no mapped area: 30023 (Alpharetta), 30077 (Roswell), 30301 (Atlanta), 30302 (Atlanta), 30304 (Atlanta) and 39 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Georgia has more ZIP codes: Fulton County holds 85 of the 952 in the state list, about 8.9%.

Writing an address in Fulton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Atlanta, GA 30301 (one of 73 codes for Atlanta; the street decides which)
  • Alpharetta, GA 30004 (one of 5 codes for Alpharetta; the street decides which)
  • Roswell, GA 30075 (one of 3 codes for Roswell; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named Atlanta are also listed under DeKalb County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Atlanta is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 10 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
