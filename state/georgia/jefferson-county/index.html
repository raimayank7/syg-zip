Where Jefferson County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Jefferson County is in the east of Georgia. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 11 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 30413 (Bartow) and 30818 (Matthews), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Columbia County (15 miles), Glascock County (16 miles), Burke County (24 miles) and Washington County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Jefferson County begin with 308 (4 codes) and 304 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 308 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Burke County, Columbia County and Glascock County, and has 25 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 633 square miles of land (1,640 sq km). The largest is 30434 (Louisville) with 204 square miles, and the smallest is 30818 (Matthews) with 30.1. The middle-sized code here covers 71.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jefferson County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Jefferson County ranks 29th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jefferson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Avera, GA 30803
  • Bartow, GA 30413
  • Louisville, GA 30434

Questions about Jefferson County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Avera, GA?

30803 is the ZIP code listed for Avera in Jefferson County.

Do all ZIP codes in Jefferson County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 308, on 4 of the 7 codes.

Which ZIP code in Jefferson County covers the most land?
