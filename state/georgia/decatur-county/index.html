Where Decatur County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Decatur County is in the south-west of Georgia. Its codes spread over about 11 miles from north to south and 5.0 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 39815 (Attapulgus) and 39819 (Bainbridge), about 12 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Miller County (7.8 miles), Grady County (16 miles), Gadsden County, Florida (17 miles) and Early County (18 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Decatur County starts with 398. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 398 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Baker County, Ben Hill County and Calhoun County, and has 34 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 2 of the 4 codes. Together they come to about 205 square miles of land (530 sq km). The largest is 39819 (Bainbridge) with 150 square miles, and the smallest is 39815 (Attapulgus) with 54.7. The middle-sized code here covers 102 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. Water adds about 8.8 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Decatur County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 39818 (Bainbridge) and 39852 (Fowlstown). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Decatur County ranks 79th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Decatur County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Bainbridge, GA 39818 (one of 2 codes for Bainbridge; the street decides which)
  • Attapulgus, GA 39815
  • Fowlstown, GA 39852

ZIP codes named Bainbridge are also listed under Miller County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Bainbridge is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
