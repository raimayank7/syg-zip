Where White County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of White County fall in the north of Georgia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 9.1 miles, and from east to west about 3.2 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 30528 (Cleveland) and 30571 (Sautee Nacoochee), about 9.7 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Habersham County (13 miles), Lumpkin County (17 miles), Towns County (19 miles) and Union County (19 miles).

The first three digits

All 3 codes here begin with the same three digits, 305. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 305 is shared with 15 other counties, such as Banks County, Dawson County and Fannin County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 256 square miles of land (664 sq km). The largest is 30528 (Cleveland) with 166 square miles, and the smallest is 30571 (Sautee Nacoochee) with 43.7. The middle-sized code here covers 46.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of White County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, White County ranks 99th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 3 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in White County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Cleveland, GA 30528
  • Helen, GA 30545
  • Sautee Nacoochee, GA 30571

Cleveland is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 18 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
