Where Columbia County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Columbia County sits near the middle of Georgia. Its codes spread over about 151 miles from north to south and 149 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 30809 (Evans) and 39813 (Arlington), about 209 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Glascock County (4.2 miles), Jefferson County (15 miles), Warren County (17 miles) and Washington County (18 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Columbia County begin with 308 (4 codes), 309 (1 code) and 398 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 308 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Burke County, Glascock County and Jefferson County, and has 25 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 413 square miles of land (1,070 sq km). The largest is 39813 (Arlington) with 133 square miles, and the smallest is 30809 (Evans) with 44.2. The middle-sized code here covers 53.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. Water adds about 18.1 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Columbia County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 30917 (Augusta). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Columbia County ranks 36th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Columbia County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Appling, GA 30802
  • Arlington, GA 39813
  • Augusta, GA 30917

Augusta shares a name with a place in another part of Georgia; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
