Where Clayton County is

The ZIP codes of Clayton County are found in the north-west of Georgia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 13 miles, and from east to west about 9.1 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 30250 (Lovejoy) and 30288 (Conley), about 13 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Henry County (13 miles), Fayette County (14 miles), Fulton County (19 miles) and Spalding County (19 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Clayton County starts with 302. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 302 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Butts County, Coweta County and Fayette County, and has 65 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 139 square miles of land (360 sq km). The largest is 30294 (Ellenwood) with 32.8 square miles, and the smallest is 30250 (Lovejoy) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 10.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clayton County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 30237 (Jonesboro), 30287 (Morrow) and 30298 (Forest Park). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Clayton County ranks 9th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clayton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Jonesboro, GA 30236 (one of 3 codes for Jonesboro; the street decides which)
  • Forest Park, GA 30297 (one of 2 codes for Forest Park; the street decides which)
  • Morrow, GA 30260 (one of 2 codes for Morrow; the street decides which)

Jonesboro is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
