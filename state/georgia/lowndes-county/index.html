Where Lowndes County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Lowndes County fall in the south of Georgia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 15 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 31632 (Hahira) and 31636 (Lake Park), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lanier County (17 miles), Echols County (17 miles), Brooks County (21 miles) and Cook County (23 miles).

The first three digits

All 11 codes here begin with the same three digits, 316. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 316 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Atkinson County, Berrien County and Brooks County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 684 square miles of land (1,771 sq km). The largest is 31636 (Lake Park) with 186 square miles, and the smallest is 31698 (Valdosta) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 48.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lowndes County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 31603 (Valdosta) and 31604 (Valdosta). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lowndes County ranks 13th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lowndes County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Valdosta, GA 31601 (one of 7 codes for Valdosta; the street decides which)
  • Hahira, GA 31632
  • Lake Park, GA 31636
