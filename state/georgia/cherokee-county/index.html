Where Cherokee County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Cherokee County is in the north-west of Georgia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 19 miles, and from east to west about 11 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 30151 (Nelson) and 30189 (Woodstock), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Pickens County (16 miles), Forsyth County (19 miles), Bartow County (21 miles) and Cobb County (21 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 301. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 301 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Bartow County, Carroll County and Cobb County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 402 square miles of land (1,042 sq km). The largest is 30107 (Ball Ground) with 108 square miles, and the smallest is 30189 (Woodstock) with 19.2. The middle-sized code here covers 68.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cherokee County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 30142 (Holly Springs), 30146 (Lebanon), 30151 (Nelson) and 30169 (Canton). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Cherokee County ranks 17th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cherokee County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Canton, GA 30114 (one of 3 codes for Canton; the street decides which)
  • Woodstock, GA 30188 (one of 2 codes for Woodstock; the street decides which)
  • Ball Ground, GA 30107

Canton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 16 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
