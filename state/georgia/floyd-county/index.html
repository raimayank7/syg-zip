Where Floyd County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Floyd County fall in the north-west of Georgia. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 11 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 30105 (Armuchee) and 30124 (Cave Spring), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Chattooga County (19 miles), Polk County (19 miles), Bartow County (22 miles) and Cherokee County, Alabama (24 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Floyd County starts with 301. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 301 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Bartow County, Carroll County and Cherokee County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 485 square miles of land (1,256 sq km). The largest is 30165 (Rome) with 167 square miles, and the smallest is 30149 (Mount Berry) with 6.7. The middle-sized code here covers 54.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Floyd County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 30129 (Coosa), 30162 (Rome), 30163 (Rome), 30164 (Rome) and 30172 (Shannon). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Floyd County ranks 10th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Floyd County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Rome, GA 30161 (one of 5 codes for Rome; the street decides which)
  • Armuchee, GA 30105
  • Cave Spring, GA 30124

Rome is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Floyd County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Rome, GA?

Rome has 5 ZIP codes in Floyd County: 30161, 30162, 30163, 30164, 30165. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Floyd County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 12 start with 301.

Which ZIP code in Floyd County covers the most land?

30165 (Rome), at about 167 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
