Where Colquitt County is

The ZIP codes of Colquitt County are found in the south-west of Georgia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 31722 (Berlin) and 31744 (Doerun), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cook County (20 miles), Mitchell County (21 miles), Tift County (22 miles) and Thomas County (22 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 317. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 317 is shared with 14 other counties, such as Baker County, Ben Hill County and Berrien County, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 551 square miles of land (1,427 sq km). The largest is 31768 (Moultrie) with 142 square miles, and the smallest is 31753 (Funston) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 77.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Colquitt County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 31776 (Moultrie). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Colquitt County ranks 18th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Colquitt County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Moultrie, GA 31768 (one of 3 codes for Moultrie; the street decides which)
  • Berlin, GA 31722
  • Doerun, GA 31744
