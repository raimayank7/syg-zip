Where Barrow County is

The ZIP codes of Barrow County are found in the north of Georgia. Its codes spread over about 6.4 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 30011 (Auburn) and 30666 (Statham), about 14 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Jackson County (14 miles), Walton County (15 miles), Oconee County (18 miles) and Clarke County (19 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Barrow County begin with 306 (3 codes) and 300 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 306 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Clarke County, Elbert County and Franklin County, and has 52 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 180 square miles of land (465 sq km). The largest is 30680 (Winder) with 95.0 square miles, and the smallest is 30620 (Bethlehem) with 22.2. The middle-sized code here covers 31.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Barrow County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Barrow County ranks 79th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Barrow County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Auburn, GA 30011
  • Bethlehem, GA 30620
  • Statham, GA 30666

Auburn is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 17 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
