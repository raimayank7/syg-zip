Where DeKalb County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, DeKalb County is in the north-west of Georgia. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 30038 (Lithonia) and 30350 (Atlanta), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Gwinnett County (12 miles), Fulton County (13 miles), Rockdale County (16 miles) and Clayton County (21 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of DeKalb County begin with 300 (21 codes), 303 (14 codes), 311 (4 codes) and 399 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 300 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Barrow County, Cobb County and Forsyth County, and has 89 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 24 of the 40 codes. Together they come to about 245 square miles of land (634 sq km). The largest is 30058 (Lithonia) with 30.5 square miles, and the smallest is 30072 (Pine Lake) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 8.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of DeKalb County; a code can reach across its border. 16 codes have no mapped area: 30031 (Decatur), 30036 (Decatur), 30037 (Decatur), 30074 (Redan), 30085 (Tucker) and 11 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, DeKalb County ranks 2nd among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 40 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in DeKalb County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Atlanta, GA 30319 (one of 19 codes for Atlanta; the street decides which)
  • Decatur, GA 30030 (one of 8 codes for Decatur; the street decides which)
  • Stone Mountain, GA 30083 (one of 4 codes for Stone Mountain; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named Atlanta are also listed under Fulton County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Atlanta is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 10 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
