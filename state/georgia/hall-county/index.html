Where Hall County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Hall County is in the north-west of Georgia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 16 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 30527 (Clermont) and 30542 (Flowery Branch), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lumpkin County (17 miles), Jackson County (18 miles), Banks County (18 miles) and Forsyth County (19 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Hall County starts with 305. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 305 is shared with 15 other counties, such as Banks County, Dawson County and Fannin County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 429 square miles of land (1,111 sq km). The largest is 30506 (Gainesville) with 100 square miles, and the smallest is 30566 (Oakwood) with 8.0. The middle-sized code here covers 35.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. Water adds about 42.6 square miles, roughly 9% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hall County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 30502 (Chestnut Mountain) and 30503 (Gainesville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Hall County ranks 10th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hall County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Gainesville, GA 30501 (one of 5 codes for Gainesville; the street decides which)
  • Chestnut Mountain, GA 30502
  • Clermont, GA 30527

Gainesville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Hall County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Gainesville, GA?

Gainesville has 5 ZIP codes in Hall County: 30501, 30503, 30504, 30506, 30507. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Hall County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 12 start with 305.

Which ZIP code in Hall County covers the most land?

30506 (Gainesville), at about 100 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
