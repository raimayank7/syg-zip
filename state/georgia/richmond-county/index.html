Where Richmond County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Richmond County fall in the north-east of Georgia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 13 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 30805 (Blythe) and 30901 (Augusta), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Aiken County, South Carolina (21 miles), McDuffie County (23 miles), Burke County (25 miles) and Edgefield County, South Carolina (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Richmond County begin with 309 (12 codes) and 308 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 309 is shared with 1 other county, Columbia County, and has 13 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 350 square miles of land (907 sq km). The largest is 30815 (Hephzibah) with 127 square miles, and the smallest is 30912 (Augusta) with 0.07. The middle-sized code here covers 21.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Richmond County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 30903 (Augusta), 30914 (Augusta), 30916 (Augusta), 30919 (Augusta) and 30999 (Augusta). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Richmond County ranks 8th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Richmond County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Augusta, GA 30901 (one of 12 codes for Augusta; the street decides which)
  • Blythe, GA 30805
  • Gracewood, GA 30812

ZIP codes named Augusta are also listed under Columbia County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Augusta is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 12 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
