Where Clarke County is

The ZIP codes of Clarke County are found in the north of Georgia. Its codes spread over about 5.2 miles from north to south and 7.9 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 30607 (Athens) and 30683 (Winterville), about 8.7 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Oconee County (9.1 miles), Madison County (13 miles), Oglethorpe County (15 miles) and Jackson County (18 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Clarke County starts with 306. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 306 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Barrow County, Elbert County and Franklin County, and has 52 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 169 square miles of land (438 sq km). The largest is 30683 (Winterville) with 38.8 square miles, and the smallest is 30609 (Athens) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 30.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clarke County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 30603 (Athens), 30604 (Athens), 30608 (Athens) and 30612 (Athens). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Clarke County ranks 13th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clarke County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Athens, GA 30601 (one of 10 codes for Athens; the street decides which)
  • Winterville, GA 30683

Athens is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 13 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
