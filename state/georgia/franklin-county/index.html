Where Franklin County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Franklin County is in the north of Georgia. Its codes spread over about 12 miles from north to south and 8.1 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 30553 (Lavonia) and 30639 (Franklin Springs), about 12 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Hart County (9.9 miles), Stephens County (15 miles), Elbert County (18 miles) and Madison County (18 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Franklin County begin with 305 (3 codes) and 306 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 305 is shared with 15 other counties, such as Banks County, Dawson County and Fannin County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 291 square miles of land (753 sq km). The largest is 30521 (Carnesville) with 92.0 square miles, and the smallest is 30639 (Franklin Springs) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 59.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Franklin County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Franklin County ranks 55th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Franklin County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Canon, GA 30520
  • Carnesville, GA 30521
  • Franklin Springs, GA 30639
