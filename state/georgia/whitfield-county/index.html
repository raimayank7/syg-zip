Where Whitfield County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Whitfield County fall in the north-west of Georgia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 106 miles, and from east to west about 26 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 30270 (Peachtree City) and 30710 (Cohutta), about 108 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Gordon County (11 miles), Murray County (20 miles), Catoosa County (24 miles) and Walker County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Whitfield County begin with 307 (8 codes) and 302 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 307 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Catoosa County, Chattooga County and Dade County, and has 36 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 309 square miles of land (801 sq km). The largest is 30721 (Dalton) with 127 square miles, and the smallest is 30756 (Varnell) with 0.05. The middle-sized code here covers 47.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Whitfield County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 30270 (Peachtree City), 30719 (Dalton) and 30722 (Dalton). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Whitfield County ranks 18th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Whitfield County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Dalton, GA 30719 (one of 4 codes for Dalton; the street decides which)
  • Cohutta, GA 30710
  • Peachtree City, GA 30270

Dalton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Peachtree City shares a name with a place in another part of Georgia; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Whitfield County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Dalton, GA?
