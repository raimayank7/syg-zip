Where Bibb County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Bibb County fall close to the middle of Georgia. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 31052 (Lizella) and 31217 (Macon), about 15 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Monroe County (18 miles), Jones County (19 miles), Peach County (19 miles) and Twiggs County (20 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Bibb County begin with 312 (20 codes) and 310 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 312, so those three digits alone point to Bibb County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 21 codes. Together they come to about 421 square miles of land (1,091 sq km). The largest is 31217 (Macon) with 108 square miles, and the smallest is 31213 (Macon) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 41.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bibb County; a code can reach across its border. 10 codes have no mapped area: 31202 (Macon), 31203 (Macon), 31205 (Macon), 31208 (Macon), 31209 (Macon) and 5 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Bibb County ranks 6th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 21 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bibb County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Macon, GA 31201 (one of 20 codes for Macon; the street decides which)
  • Lizella, GA 31052

Macon is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
