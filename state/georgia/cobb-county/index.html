Where Cobb County is

The ZIP codes of Cobb County are found in the north-west of Georgia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 16 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 30101 (Acworth) and 30168 (Austell), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Fulton County (12 miles), Paulding County (14 miles), Douglas County (18 miles) and Cherokee County (21 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Cobb County begin with 300 (17 codes), 301 (11 codes) and 311 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 300 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Barrow County, DeKalb County and Forsyth County, and has 89 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 19 of the 29 codes. Together they come to about 375 square miles of land (971 sq km). The largest is 30127 (Powder Springs) with 51.3 square miles, and the smallest is 30090 (Marietta) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 15.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cobb County; a code can reach across its border. 10 codes have no mapped area: 30006 (Marietta), 30007 (Marietta), 30061 (Marietta), 30063 (Marietta), 30065 (Marietta) and 5 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Cobb County ranks 4th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 29 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cobb County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Marietta, GA 30006 (one of 14 codes for Marietta; the street decides which)
  • Kennesaw, GA 30144 (one of 5 codes for Kennesaw; the street decides which)
  • Smyrna, GA 30080 (one of 3 codes for Smyrna; the street decides which)

Marietta is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 10 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
