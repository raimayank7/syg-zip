Where Bulloch County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Bulloch County fall in the east of Georgia. Its codes spread over about 18 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 30415 (Brooklet) and 30450 (Portal), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Candler County (13 miles), Screven County (17 miles), Evans County (20 miles) and Effingham County (27 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Bulloch County starts with 304. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 304 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Burke County, Candler County and Emanuel County, and has 49 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 579 square miles of land (1,499 sq km). The largest is 30461 (Statesboro) with 163 square miles, and the smallest is 30460 (Statesboro) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 99.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bulloch County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 30459 (Statesboro). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Bulloch County ranks 29th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bulloch County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Statesboro, GA 30458 (one of 4 codes for Statesboro; the street decides which)
  • Brooklet, GA 30415
  • Portal, GA 30450
