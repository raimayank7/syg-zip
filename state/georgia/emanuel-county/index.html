Where Emanuel County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Emanuel County is in the east of Georgia. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 27 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 30425 (Garfield) and 31002 (Adrian), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Treutlen County (19 miles), Johnson County (21 miles), Candler County (21 miles) and Toombs County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Emanuel County begin with 304 (6 codes) and 310 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 304 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Bulloch County, Burke County and Candler County, and has 49 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 619 square miles of land (1,603 sq km). The largest is 30401 (Swainsboro) with 260 square miles, and the smallest is 30464 (Stillmore) with 0.8. The middle-sized code here covers 103 square miles; the middle value for all of Georgia is 53.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Emanuel County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 30447 (Norristown). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Emanuel County ranks 29th among the 157 counties and county-level areas of Georgia (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 952 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Emanuel County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters GA and the ZIP code:

  • Adrian, GA 31002
  • Garfield, GA 30425
  • Norristown, GA 30447

Adrian is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
