Where Clearwater County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Clearwater County fall in the north-west of Minnesota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 18 miles, and from east to west about 12 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 56644 (Gonvick) and 56676 (Shevlin), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Beltrami County (27 miles), Mahnomen County (32 miles), Red Lake County (35 miles) and Polk County (43 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Clearwater County starts with 566. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 566 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Beltrami County, Cass County and Itasca County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 821 square miles of land (2,128 sq km). The largest is 56621 (Bagley) with 321 square miles, and the smallest is 56652 (Leonard) with 65.5. The middle-sized code here covers 120 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Water adds about 26.1 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clearwater County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Clearwater County ranks 71st among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clearwater County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Clearbrook, MN 56634
  • Gonvick, MN 56644
