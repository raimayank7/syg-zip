Where Itasca County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Itasca County is in the north of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 53 miles from north to south and 50 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 55784 (Swan River) and 56681 (Squaw Lake), about 59 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Cass County (48 miles), Aitkin County (50 miles), St. Louis County (53 miles) and Beltrami County (62 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Itasca County begin with 557 (15 codes) and 566 (10 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 557 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Aitkin County, Carlton County and Pine County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 22 of the 25 codes. Together they come to about 2,422 square miles of land (6,273 sq km). The largest is 56636 (Deer River) with 437 square miles, and the smallest is 55753 (Keewatin) with 0.9. The middle-sized code here covers 58.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Water adds about 250 square miles, roughly 9% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Itasca County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 55730 (Grand Rapids), 55745 (Grand Rapids) and 56631 (Bowstring). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Itasca County ranks 5th among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 25 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Itasca County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Grand Rapids, MN 55730 (one of 3 codes for Grand Rapids; the street decides which)
  • Bigfork, MN 56628
  • Bovey, MN 55709

Questions about Itasca County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Grand Rapids, MN?

Grand Rapids has 3 ZIP codes in Itasca County: 55730, 55744, 55745. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Itasca County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 557, on 15 of the 25 codes.

Which ZIP code in Itasca County covers the most land?

56636 (Deer River), at about 437 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
