Where Brown County is

The ZIP codes of Brown County are found in the south-west of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 27 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 56084 (Searles) and 56087 (Springfield), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Watonwan County (17 miles), Nicollet County (23 miles), Cottonwood County (30 miles) and Redwood County (32 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 560. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 560 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Blue Earth County, Faribault County and Freeborn County, and has 74 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 724 square miles of land (1,876 sq km). The largest is 56085 (Sleepy Eye) with 206 square miles, and the smallest is 56030 (Essig) with 0.02. The middle-sized code here covers 123 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Brown County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 56084 (Searles). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Brown County ranks 51st among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Brown County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:
