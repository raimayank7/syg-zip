Where Le Sueur County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Le Sueur County fall in the south of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 56050 (Kasota) and 56071 (New Prague), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Rice County (20 miles), Waseca County (24 miles), Scott County (25 miles) and Nicollet County (25 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 560. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 560 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Blue Earth County, Brown County and Faribault County, and has 74 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 546 square miles of land (1,413 sq km). The largest is 56058 (Le Sueur) with 127 square miles, and the smallest is 56028 (Elysian) with 17.9. The middle-sized code here covers 55.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Water adds about 29.2 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Le Sueur County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Le Sueur County ranks 41st among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Le Sueur County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Cleveland, MN 56017
  • Elysian, MN 56028
  • Kasota, MN 56050

Cleveland is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 18 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
