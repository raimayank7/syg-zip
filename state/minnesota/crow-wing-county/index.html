Where Crow Wing County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Crow Wing County fall close to the middle of Minnesota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 37 miles, and from east to west about 24 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 56447 (Emily) and 56449 (Fort Ripley), about 40 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Aitkin County (34 miles), Cass County (36 miles), Morrison County (39 miles) and Wadena County (44 miles).

The first three digits

All 15 codes here begin with the same three digits, 564. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 564 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Aitkin County, Cass County and Hubbard County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 1,031 square miles of land (2,669 sq km). The largest is 56401 (Brainerd) with 323 square miles, and the smallest is 56425 (Baxter) with 18.3. The middle-sized code here covers 49.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Water adds about 162 square miles, roughly 14% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Crow Wing County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 56456 (Jenkins) and 56459 (Lake Hubert). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Crow Wing County ranks 12th among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Crow Wing County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Baxter, MN 56425
  • Brainerd, MN 56401
  • Crosby, MN 56441

Baxter is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
