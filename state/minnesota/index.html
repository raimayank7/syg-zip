Minnesota ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 992 ZIP codes for Minnesota, about 2.4% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 13th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 87 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Hennepin County (104), St. Louis County (53), Stearns County (34), Ramsey County (32) and Itasca County (25). At the other end, 1 has only one or two codes, for example Kanabec County.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Minnesota uses 17 of these prefixes, from 550 to 567. The busiest are 553 (93 codes, most of them for Eden Prairie), 563 (83 codes, most of them for Saint Cloud), 565 (76 codes, most of them for Moorhead) and 557 (75 codes, most of them for Grand Rapids). The first digit 5 is shared with District of Columbia, Iowa, Montana, North Dakota, South Dakota and Wisconsin.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 881 of the 992 codes. The largest is 55731 (Ely, St. Louis County) at 1,607 square miles and the smallest is 56030 (Essig) at 0.02; the middle value is 62.9 square miles. The other 111 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 56711 (Angle Inlet) and the southernmost 55931 (Eitzen); the one farthest east is 55605 (Grand Portage) and farthest west 56740 (Noyes).

Place names and addresses in Minnesota

Of the 806 place names in the list, 778 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Minneapolis (69), Saint Paul (43), Duluth (14) and Maple Plain (11). 8 place names appear under more than one county, among them Saint Paul, Minneapolis, Howard Lake and Mankato: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters MN and the code, as in Minneapolis, MN 55401. The name of the county is left out.

Questions about Minnesota ZIP codes

What ZIP codes does Minnesota use?

In this list, Minnesota ZIP codes run from 55001 to 56763 and start with 5. A few codes inside that range can belong to a neighbouring state near the border.

Which place in Minnesota has the most ZIP codes?

Minneapolis, with 69 codes in this list.
