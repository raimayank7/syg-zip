Where Koochiching County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Koochiching County fall in the north of Minnesota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 54 miles, and from east to west about 39 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 56661 (Northome) and 56668 (Ranier), about 66 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Beltrami County (63 miles), Itasca County (64 miles), Lake of the Woods County (70 miles) and Clearwater County (88 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Koochiching County starts with 566. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 566 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Beltrami County, Cass County and Clearwater County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 2,875 square miles of land (7,446 sq km). The largest is 56627 (Big Falls) with 939 square miles, and the smallest is 56668 (Ranier) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 268 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 136 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Koochiching County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 56679 (South International Falls). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Koochiching County ranks 26th among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 992 codes in the state list.
