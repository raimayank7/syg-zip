Where Polk County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Polk County fall in the north-west of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 32 miles from north to south and 64 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 56651 (Lengby) and 56721 (East Grand Forks), about 71 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Red Lake County (16 miles), Pennington County (28 miles), Norman County (31 miles) and Mahnomen County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Polk County begin with 565 (8 codes), 567 (5 codes) and 566 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 565 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Becker County, Clay County and Grant County, and has 76 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,964 square miles of land (5,087 sq km). The largest is 56716 (Crookston) with 325 square miles, and the smallest is 56568 (Nielsville) with 29.7. The middle-sized code here covers 107 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Polk County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Polk County ranks 11th among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Polk County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:
