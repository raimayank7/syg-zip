Where Hubbard County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Hubbard County is in the west of Minnesota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 16 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 56461 (Laporte) and 56470 (Park Rapids), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Cass County (24 miles), Wadena County (38 miles), Becker County (43 miles) and Clearwater County (45 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Hubbard County starts with 564. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 564 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Aitkin County, Cass County and Crow Wing County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 925 square miles of land (2,396 sq km). The largest is 56470 (Park Rapids) with 382 square miles, and the smallest is 56458 (Lake George) with 21.3. The middle-sized code here covers 125 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Water adds about 74.8 square miles, roughly 7% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hubbard County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 56436 (Benedict). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Hubbard County ranks 63rd among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 992 codes in the state list.
