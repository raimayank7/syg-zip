Where Washington County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Washington County is in the south of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 30 miles from north to south and 11 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 55071 (Saint Paul Park) and 55073 (Scandia), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Ramsey County (10 miles), Dakota County (21 miles), St. Croix County, Wisconsin (22 miles) and Anoka County (22 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Washington County begin with 550 (14 codes) and 551 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 550 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Anoka County, Chisago County and Dakota County, and has 74 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 18 codes. Together they come to about 413 square miles of land (1,069 sq km). The largest is 55082 (Stillwater) with 79.2 square miles, and the smallest is 55090 (Willernie) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 19.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 38.7 square miles, roughly 9% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Washington County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 55083 (Stillwater). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Washington County ranks 9th among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Washington County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Saint Paul, MN 55115 (one of 4 codes for Saint Paul; the street decides which)
  • Stillwater, MN 55082 (one of 2 codes for Stillwater; the street decides which)
  • Afton, MN 55001

ZIP codes named Saint Paul are also listed under Dakota County, Hennepin County and Ramsey County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Saint Paul is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Washington County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Saint Paul, MN?

Saint Paul has 4 ZIP codes in Washington County: 55115, 55125, 55128, 55129. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Washington County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 550, on 14 of the 18 codes.

Which ZIP code in Washington County covers the most land?

55082 (Stillwater), at about 79.2 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
