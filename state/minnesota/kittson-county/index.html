Where Kittson County is

The ZIP codes of Kittson County are found in the north-west of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 29 miles from north to south and 30 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 56732 (Karlstad) and 56740 (Noyes), about 41 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Pembina County, North Dakota (31 miles), Marshall County (36 miles), Walsh County, North Dakota (49 miles) and Roseau County (55 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 567. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 567 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Lake of the Woods County, Marshall County and Pennington County, and has 42 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 1,177 square miles of land (3,048 sq km). The largest is 56735 (Lancaster) with 367 square miles, and the smallest is 56720 (Donaldson) with 8.1. The middle-sized code here covers 152 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Kittson County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 56740 (Noyes). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Kittson County ranks 33rd among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Kittson County
