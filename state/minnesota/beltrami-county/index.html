Where Beltrami County is

The ZIP codes of Beltrami County are found in the north-west of Minnesota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 49 miles, and from east to west about 30 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 56678 (Solway) and 56685 (Waskish), about 52 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Clearwater County (27 miles), Hubbard County (45 miles), Cass County (55 miles) and Mahnomen County (57 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Beltrami County starts with 566. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 566 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Cass County, Clearwater County and Itasca County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 1,782 square miles of land (4,615 sq km). The largest is 56601 (Bemidji) with 371 square miles, and the smallest is 56670 (Redby) with 28.3. The middle-sized code here covers 111 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Water adds about 93.6 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Beltrami County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 56619 (Bemidji) and 56687 (Wilton). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Beltrami County ranks 13th among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Beltrami County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Bemidji, MN 56601 (one of 2 codes for Bemidji; the street decides which)
  • Blackduck, MN 56630
  • Hines, MN 56647
