Where Renville County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Renville County fall in the south-west of Minnesota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 18 miles, and from east to west about 37 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 55314 (Buffalo Lake) and 56285 (Sacred Heart), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Redwood County (27 miles), Kandiyohi County (29 miles), Brown County (35 miles) and Meeker County (37 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Renville County begin with 553 (5 codes) and 562 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 553 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Anoka County, Carver County and Dakota County, and has 93 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 971 square miles of land (2,514 sq km). The largest is 55342 (Hector) with 162 square miles, and the smallest is 56270 (Morton) with 48.2. The middle-sized code here covers 98.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Renville County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Renville County ranks 33rd among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 992 codes in the state list.
