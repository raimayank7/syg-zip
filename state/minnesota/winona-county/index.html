Where Winona County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Winona County fall in the south-east of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 33 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 55925 (Dakota) and 55972 (Saint Charles), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Buffalo County, Wisconsin (24 miles), Fillmore County (27 miles), Wabasha County (28 miles) and Houston County (29 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 559. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 559 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Dodge County, Fillmore County and Goodhue County, and has 73 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 556 square miles of land (1,441 sq km). The largest is 55987 (Winona) with 157 square miles, and the smallest is 55969 (Rollingstone) with 29.1. The middle-sized code here covers 62.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Winona County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 55942 (Homer) and 55988 (Stockton). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Winona County ranks 33rd among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Winona County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:
