Where Pine County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Pine County fall close to the middle of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 43 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 55067 (Rock Creek) and 55756 (Kerrick), about 47 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Kanabec County (29 miles), Burnett County, Wisconsin (29 miles), Carlton County (37 miles) and Isanti County (38 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Pine County begin with 550 (7 codes) and 557 (6 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 550 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Anoka County, Chisago County and Dakota County, and has 74 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 1,589 square miles of land (4,116 sq km). The largest is 55072 (Sandstone) with 353 square miles, and the smallest is 55036 (Henriette) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 122 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pine County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 55067 (Rock Creek). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Pine County ranks 18th among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pine County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Brook Park, MN 55007
