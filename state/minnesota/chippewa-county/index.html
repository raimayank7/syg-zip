Where Chippewa County is

The ZIP codes of Chippewa County are found in the south-west of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 56222 (Clara City) and 56262 (Milan), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Swift County (18 miles), Yellow Medicine County (22 miles), Lac qui Parle County (26 miles) and Kandiyohi County (31 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Chippewa County starts with 562. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 562 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Big Stone County, Grant County and Kandiyohi County, and has 74 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 548 square miles of land (1,419 sq km). The largest is 56265 (Montevideo) with 264 square miles, and the smallest is 56295 (Watson) with 22.6. The middle-sized code here covers 94.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Chippewa County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Chippewa County ranks 71st among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Chippewa County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Clara City, MN 56222
  • Maynard, MN 56260
  • Milan, MN 56262
