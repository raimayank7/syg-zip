Where Goodhue County is

The ZIP codes of Goodhue County are found in the south of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 33 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 55026 (Frontenac) and 55946 (Kenyon), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Wabasha County (25 miles), Pierce County, Wisconsin (26 miles), Dodge County (26 miles) and Rice County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Goodhue County begin with 550 (6 codes) and 559 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 550 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Anoka County, Chisago County and Dakota County, and has 74 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 798 square miles of land (2,067 sq km). The largest is 55946 (Kenyon) with 131 square miles, and the smallest is 55026 (Frontenac) with 3.6. The middle-sized code here covers 76.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Goodhue County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Goodhue County ranks 33rd among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Goodhue County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:
