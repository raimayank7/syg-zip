Where McLeod County is

The ZIP codes of McLeod County are found in the south of Minnesota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 55385 (Stewart) and 55395 (Winsted), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Sibley County (16 miles), Carver County (22 miles), Meeker County (27 miles) and Wright County (29 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 553. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 553 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Anoka County, Carver County and Dakota County, and has 93 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 588 square miles of land (1,524 sq km). The largest is 55350 (Hutchinson) with 160 square miles, and the smallest is 55370 (Plato) with 20.7. The middle-sized code here covers 58.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Water adds about 18.4 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of McLeod County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, McLeod County ranks 45th among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in McLeod County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Brownton, MN 55312
  • Glencoe, MN 55336
  • Hutchinson, MN 55350
