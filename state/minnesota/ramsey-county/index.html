Where Ramsey County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Ramsey County fall in the south of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 11 miles from north to south and 9.2 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 55110 (Saint Paul) and 55116 (Saint Paul), about 14 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Washington County (10 miles), Hennepin County (14 miles), Dakota County (16 miles) and Anoka County (18 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Ramsey County starts with 551. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 551 is shared with 3 other counties, Dakota County, Hennepin County and Washington County, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 20 of the 32 codes. Together they come to about 160 square miles of land (416 sq km). The largest is 55110 (Saint Paul) with 24.7 square miles, and the smallest is 55155 (Saint Paul) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 5.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 20.8 square miles, roughly 11% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ramsey County; a code can reach across its border. 12 codes have no mapped area: 55131, 55133, 55144, 55145, 55146 and 7 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Ramsey County ranks 4th among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 32 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Ramsey County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Saint Paul, MN 55101 (one of 32 codes for Saint Paul; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named Saint Paul are also listed under Dakota County, Hennepin County and Washington County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Saint Paul is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
