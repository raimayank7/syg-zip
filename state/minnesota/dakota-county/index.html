Where Dakota County is

The ZIP codes of Dakota County are found in the south of Minnesota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 26 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 55065 (Randolph) and 55118 (Saint Paul), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Ramsey County (16 miles), Scott County (18 miles), Hennepin County (21 miles) and Washington County (21 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Dakota County begin with 550 (11 codes), 551 (7 codes) and 553 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 550 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Anoka County, Chisago County and Goodhue County, and has 74 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 19 of the 20 codes. Together they come to about 538 square miles of land (1,393 sq km). The largest is 55033 (Hastings) with 144 square miles, and the smallest is 55150 (Mendota) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 10.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 23.7 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Dakota County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 55010 (Castle Rock). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Dakota County ranks 7th among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 20 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Dakota County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Saint Paul, MN 55118 (one of 6 codes for Saint Paul; the street decides which)
  • Burnsville, MN 55306 (one of 2 codes for Burnsville; the street decides which)
  • Inver Grove Heights, MN 55076 (one of 2 codes for Inver Grove Heights; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named Saint Paul are also listed under Hennepin County, Ramsey County and Washington County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Saint Paul is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Dakota County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Saint Paul, MN?

Saint Paul has 6 ZIP codes in Dakota County: 55118, 55120, 55121, 55122, 55123, 55124. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Dakota County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 550, on 11 of the 20 codes.

Which ZIP code in Dakota County covers the most land?

55033 (Hastings), at about 144 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
