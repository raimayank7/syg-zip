Where Marshall County is

The ZIP codes of Marshall County are found in the north-west of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 68 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 56727 (Grygla) and 56744 (Oslo), about 68 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Pennington County (27 miles), Kittson County (36 miles), Red Lake County (39 miles) and Polk County (46 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Marshall County starts with 567. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 567 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Kittson County, Lake of the Woods County and Pennington County, and has 42 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,246 square miles of land (5,816 sq km). The largest is 56727 (Grygla) with 622 square miles, and the smallest is 56710 (Alvarado) with 44.4. The middle-sized code here covers 182 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Marshall County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Marshall County ranks 26th among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 992 codes in the state list.
