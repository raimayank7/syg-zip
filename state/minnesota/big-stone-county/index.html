Where Big Stone County is

The ZIP codes of Big Stone County are found in the south-west of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 56211 (Beardsley) and 56227 (Correll), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Grant County, South Dakota (23 miles), Stevens County (24 miles), Traverse County (24 miles) and Roberts County, South Dakota (27 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 562. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 562 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Chippewa County, Grant County and Kandiyohi County, and has 74 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 602 square miles of land (1,558 sq km). The largest is 56240 (Graceville) with 149 square miles, and the smallest is 56210 (Barry) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 102 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Water adds about 37.4 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Big Stone County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Big Stone County ranks 51st among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Big Stone County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:
