Where Swift County is

The ZIP codes of Swift County are found in the south-west of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 12 miles from north to south and 33 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 56208 (Appleton) and 56252 (Kerkhoven), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Chippewa County (18 miles), Stevens County (27 miles), Pope County (29 miles) and Kandiyohi County (32 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 562. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 562 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Big Stone County, Chippewa County and Grant County, and has 74 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 828 square miles of land (2,144 sq km). The largest is 56215 (Benson) with 211 square miles, and the smallest is 56226 (Clontarf) with 35.8. The middle-sized code here covers 91.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Swift County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Swift County ranks 51st among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Swift County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Appleton, MN 56208
  • Benson, MN 56215
  • Clontarf, MN 56226

Appleton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
