Where Meeker County is

The ZIP codes of Meeker County are found in the south-west of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 55389 (Watkins) and 56228 (Cosmos), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Kandiyohi County (25 miles), McLeod County (27 miles), Stearns County (28 miles) and Wright County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Meeker County begin with 553 (5 codes) and 562 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 553 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Anoka County, Carver County and Dakota County, and has 93 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 559 square miles of land (1,448 sq km). The largest is 55355 (Litchfield) with 172 square miles, and the smallest is 55324 (Darwin) with 35.2. The middle-sized code here covers 74.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Water adds about 33.9 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Meeker County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Meeker County ranks 51st among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Meeker County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Cosmos, MN 56228
  • Darwin, MN 55324
  • Dassel, MN 55325

Questions about Meeker County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Cosmos, MN?

56228 is the ZIP code listed for Cosmos in Meeker County.

Do all ZIP codes in Meeker County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 553, on 5 of the 7 codes.

Which ZIP code in Meeker County covers the most land?

55355 (Litchfield), at about 172 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
