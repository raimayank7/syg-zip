Where Douglas County is

The ZIP codes of Douglas County are found in the west of Minnesota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 56326 (Evansville) and 56360 (Osakis), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Pope County (18 miles), Grant County (26 miles), Todd County (27 miles) and Otter Tail County (34 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 563. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 563 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Aitkin County, Benton County and Grant County, and has 83 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 687 square miles of land (1,780 sq km). The largest is 56308 (Alexandria) with 161 square miles, and the smallest is 56355 (Nelson) with 25.6. The middle-sized code here covers 68.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Water adds about 87.5 square miles, roughly 11% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Douglas County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 56341 (Holmes City). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Douglas County ranks 33rd among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Douglas County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Alexandria, MN 56308
  • Brandon, MN 56315
  • Carlos, MN 56319

Alexandria is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 11 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
