Where Lake County is

The ZIP codes of Lake County are found in the north-east of Minnesota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 49 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 55607 (Isabella) and 55609 (Knife River), about 51 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are St. Louis County (45 miles), Bayfield County, Wisconsin (51 miles), Cook County (58 miles) and Douglas County, Wisconsin (58 miles).

The first three digits

All 6 codes here begin with the same three digits, 556. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 556 is shared with 2 other counties, Cook County and St. Louis County, and has 13 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,326 square miles of land (3,435 sq km). The largest is 55616 (Two Harbors) with 480 square miles, and the smallest is 55609 (Knife River) with 2.5. The middle-sized code here covers 260 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 53.8 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lake County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lake County ranks 63rd among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 992 codes in the state list.
