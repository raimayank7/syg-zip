Where Becker County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Becker County is in the west of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 39 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 56554 (Lake Park) and 56570 (Osage), about 40 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Mahnomen County (28 miles), Otter Tail County (36 miles), Clay County (41 miles) and Wadena County (42 miles).

The first three digits

All 13 codes here begin with the same three digits, 565. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 565 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Clay County, Grant County and Mahnomen County, and has 76 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 1,082 square miles of land (2,802 sq km). The largest is 56544 (Frazee) with 198 square miles, and the smallest is 56591 (White Earth) with 2.7. The middle-sized code here covers 106 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Water adds about 129 square miles, roughly 11% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Becker County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 56502 (Detroit Lakes), 56577 (Richwood) and 56593 (Wolf Lake). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Becker County ranks 18th among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Becker County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Detroit Lakes, MN 56501 (one of 2 codes for Detroit Lakes; the street decides which)
  • Audubon, MN 56511
  • Callaway, MN 56521

Questions about Becker County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Detroit Lakes, MN?

Detroit Lakes has 2 ZIP codes in Becker County: 56501, 56502. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Becker County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 13 start with 565.

Which ZIP code in Becker County covers the most land?
