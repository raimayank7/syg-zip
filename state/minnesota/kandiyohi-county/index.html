Where Kandiyohi County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Kandiyohi County is in the south-west of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 29 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 56253 (Lake Lillian) and 56289 (Sunburg), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Meeker County (25 miles), Renville County (29 miles), Chippewa County (31 miles) and Swift County (32 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Kandiyohi County starts with 562. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 562 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Big Stone County, Chippewa County and Grant County, and has 74 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 776 square miles of land (2,009 sq km). The largest is 56201 (Willmar) with 118 square miles, and the smallest is 56281 (Prinsburg) with 12.9. The middle-sized code here covers 61.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Water adds about 65.7 square miles, roughly 8% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Kandiyohi County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Kandiyohi County ranks 26th among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Kandiyohi County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Atwater, MN 56209
  • Blomkest, MN 56216
  • Kandiyohi, MN 56251
