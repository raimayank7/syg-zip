Where Aitkin County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Aitkin County fall close to the middle of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 55 miles from north to south and 29 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 55748 (Hill City) and 56350 (Mc Grath), about 56 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Crow Wing County (34 miles), Carlton County (40 miles), Cass County (47 miles) and Itasca County (50 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Aitkin County begin with 557 (5 codes), 564 (2 codes) and 563 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 557 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Carlton County, Itasca County and Pine County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,826 square miles of land (4,729 sq km). The largest is 56431 (Aitkin) with 463 square miles, and the smallest is 55785 (Swatara) with 113. The middle-sized code here covers 201 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 172 square miles, roughly 9% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Aitkin County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Aitkin County ranks 45th among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 992 codes in the state list.
