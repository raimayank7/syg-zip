Where Carver County is

The ZIP codes of Carver County are found in the south of Minnesota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 18 miles, and from east to west about 35 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 55339 (Hamburg) and 55473 (Minneapolis), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Scott County (18 miles), Hennepin County (21 miles), McLeod County (22 miles) and Wright County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Carver County begin with 553 (13 codes), 555 (8 codes) and 554 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 553 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Anoka County, Dakota County and Hennepin County, and has 93 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 22 codes. Together they come to about 348 square miles of land (902 sq km). The largest is 55322 (Cologne) with 57.2 square miles, and the smallest is 55386 (Victoria) with 6.4. The middle-sized code here covers 29.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 19.3 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Carver County; a code can reach across its border. 10 codes have no mapped area: 55383 (Norwood), 55473 (Minneapolis), 55550 (Young America), 55551 (Young America), 55553 (Young America) and 5 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Carver County ranks 6th among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 22 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Carver County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:
