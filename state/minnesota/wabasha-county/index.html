Where Wabasha County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Wabasha County fall in the south-east of Minnesota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 24 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 55956 (Mazeppa) and 55981 (Wabasha), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Olmsted County (20 miles), Pepin County, Wisconsin (20 miles), Buffalo County, Wisconsin (24 miles) and Goodhue County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Wabasha County begin with 559 (8 codes) and 550 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 559 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Dodge County, Fillmore County and Goodhue County, and has 73 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 590 square miles of land (1,528 sq km). The largest is 55041 (Lake City) with 144 square miles, and the smallest is 55968 (Reads Landing) with 1.6. The middle-sized code here covers 54.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Water adds about 30.7 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wabasha County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Wabasha County ranks 41st among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Wabasha County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Elgin, MN 55932
  • Kellogg, MN 55945
  • Lake City, MN 55041

Elgin is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 12 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
