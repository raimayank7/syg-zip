Where Chisago County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Chisago County is in the south of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 55069 (Rush City) and 55074 (Shafer), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Isanti County (21 miles), Polk County, Wisconsin (21 miles), Anoka County (24 miles) and Washington County (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 550. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 550 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Anoka County, Dakota County and Goodhue County, and has 74 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 484 square miles of land (1,253 sq km). The largest is 55056 (North Branch) with 99.5 square miles, and the smallest is 55012 (Center City) with 23.7. The middle-sized code here covers 34.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Water adds about 32.5 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Chisago County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 55002 (Almelund) and 55078 (Stacy). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Chisago County ranks 20th among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Chisago County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Stacy, MN 55078 (one of 2 codes for Stacy; the street decides which)
  • Almelund, MN 55002
  • Center City, MN 55012
