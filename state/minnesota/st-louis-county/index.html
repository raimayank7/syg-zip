Where St. Louis County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of St. Louis County fall in the north-east of Minnesota. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 109 miles, and from east to west about 61 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 55725 (Crane Lake) and 55808 (Duluth), about 110 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lake County (45 miles), Carlton County (49 miles), Itasca County (53 miles) and Douglas County, Wisconsin (62 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of St. Louis County begin with 557 (38 codes), 558 (14 codes) and 556 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 557 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Aitkin County, Carlton County and Itasca County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 46 of the 53 codes. Together they come to about 7,345 square miles of land (19,025 sq km). The largest is 55731 (Ely) with 1,607 square miles, and the smallest is 55814 (Duluth) with 0.08. The middle-sized code here covers 85.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Water adds about 589 square miles, roughly 7% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of St. Louis County; a code can reach across its border. 7 codes have no mapped area: 55747 (Hibbing), 55766 (Melrude), 55777 (Virginia), 55791 (Twig), 55801 (Duluth) and 2 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, St. Louis County ranks 2nd among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 53 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in St. Louis County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Duluth, MN 55801 (one of 14 codes for Duluth; the street decides which)
  • Hibbing, MN 55746 (one of 2 codes for Hibbing; the street decides which)
  • Virginia, MN 55777 (one of 2 codes for Virginia; the street decides which)
