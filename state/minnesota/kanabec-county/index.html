Where Kanabec County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Kanabec County sits near the middle of Minnesota. Its codes spread over about 3.3 miles from north to south and 6.8 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 55051 (Mora) and 56358 (Ogilvie), about 7.6 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Mille Lacs County (11 miles), Isanti County (18 miles), Pine County (29 miles) and Benton County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Kanabec County begin with 550 (1 code) and 563 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 550 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Anoka County, Chisago County and Dakota County, and has 74 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 343 square miles of land (888 sq km). The largest is 55051 (Mora) with 218 square miles, and the smallest is 56358 (Ogilvie) with 125. The middle-sized code here covers 171 square miles; the middle value for all of Minnesota is 62.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Kanabec County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Kanabec County ranks 87th among the 87 counties and county-level areas of Minnesota (equal counts share a rank), with 2 of the 992 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Kanabec County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MN and the ZIP code:

  • Mora, MN 55051
  • Ogilvie, MN 56358

Mora is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
