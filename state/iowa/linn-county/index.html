Where Linn County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Linn County is in the east of Iowa. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 52253 (Lisbon) and 52352 (Walker), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Benton County (21 miles), Jones County (24 miles), Johnson County (28 miles) and Delaware County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Linn County begin with 524 (14 codes), 522 (9 codes) and 523 (8 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 524, so those three digits alone point to Linn County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 23 of the 31 codes. Together they come to about 832 square miles of land (2,155 sq km). The largest is 52214 (Central City) with 90.2 square miles, and the smallest is 52344 (Troy Mills) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 29.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Linn County; a code can reach across its border. 8 codes have no mapped area: 52406 (Cedar Rapids), 52407 (Cedar Rapids), 52408 (Cedar Rapids), 52409 (Cedar Rapids), 52410 (Cedar Rapids) and 3 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Linn County ranks 2nd among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 31 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Linn County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Cedar Rapids, IA 52401 (one of 14 codes for Cedar Rapids; the street decides which)
  • Alburnett, IA 52202
  • Center Point, IA 52213
