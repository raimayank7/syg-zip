Where Story County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Story County sits near the middle of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 50244 (Slater) and 50278 (Zearing), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Boone County (22 miles), Hamilton County (25 miles), Polk County (26 miles) and Marshall County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Story County begin with 500 (8 codes), 501 (5 codes) and 502 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 500 is shared with 23 other counties, such as Adair County, Audubon County and Boone County, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 18 codes. Together they come to about 733 square miles of land (1,898 sq km). The largest is 50201 (Nevada) with 117 square miles, and the smallest is 50012 (Ames) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 46.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Story County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 50011 (Ames). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Story County ranks 6th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Story County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Ames, IA 50010 (one of 5 codes for Ames; the street decides which)
  • Cambridge, IA 50046
  • Collins, IA 50055

Questions about Story County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Ames, IA?

Ames has 5 ZIP codes in Story County: 50010, 50011, 50012, 50013, 50014. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Story County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 500, on 8 of the 18 codes.

Which ZIP code in Story County covers the most land?

50201 (Nevada), at about 117 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
