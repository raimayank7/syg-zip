Where Dubuque County is

The ZIP codes of Dubuque County are found in the north-east of Iowa. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 52065 (New Vienna) and 52079 (Zwingle), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Delaware County (25 miles), Grant County, Wisconsin (28 miles), Jackson County (29 miles) and Jones County (30 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Dubuque County starts with 520. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 520 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Clayton County, Clinton County and Delaware County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 16 of the 18 codes. Together they come to about 805 square miles of land (2,085 sq km). The largest is 52032 (Bernard) with 102 square miles, and the smallest is 52056 (Luxemburg) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 48.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Dubuque County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 52004 (Dubuque) and 52099 (Dubuque). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Dubuque County ranks 6th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Dubuque County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Dubuque, IA 52001 (one of 5 codes for Dubuque; the street decides which)
  • Bernard, IA 52032
  • Cascade, IA 52033
