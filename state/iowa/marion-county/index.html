Where Marion County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Marion County fall in the south of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 50044 (Bussey) and 50252 (Swan), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Monroe County (20 miles), Lucas County (24 miles), Jasper County (25 miles) and Mahaska County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Marion County begin with 502 (5 codes), 501 (4 codes) and 500 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 502 is shared with 21 other counties, such as Boone County, Cass County and Clarke County, and has 65 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 580 square miles of land (1,502 sq km). The largest is 50138 (Knoxville) with 168 square miles, and the smallest is 50163 (Melcher Dallas) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 29.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Marion County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Marion County ranks 23rd among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Marion County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Melcher Dallas, IA 50062 (one of 2 codes for Melcher Dallas; the street decides which)
  • Bussey, IA 50044
  • Columbia, IA 50057

Questions about Marion County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Melcher Dallas, IA?

Melcher Dallas has 2 ZIP codes in Marion County: 50062, 50163. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Marion County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 502, on 5 of the 12 codes.

Which ZIP code in Marion County covers the most land?

50138 (Knoxville), at about 168 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
