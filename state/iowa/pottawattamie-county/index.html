Where Pottawattamie County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Pottawattamie County is in the south-west of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 36 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 51510 (Carter Lake) and 51577 (Walnut), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Mills County (20 miles), Douglas County, Nebraska (23 miles), Harrison County (27 miles) and Sarpy County, Nebraska (28 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Pottawattamie County starts with 515. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 515 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Audubon County, Cass County and Crawford County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 951 square miles of land (2,464 sq km). The largest is 51503 (Council Bluffs) with 122 square miles, and the smallest is 51510 (Carter Lake) with 1.8. The middle-sized code here covers 47.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pottawattamie County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 51502 (Council Bluffs) and 51593 (Harlan). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Pottawattamie County ranks 5th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pottawattamie County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Council Bluffs, IA 51501 (one of 3 codes for Council Bluffs; the street decides which)
  • Avoca, IA 51521
  • Carson, IA 51525

Harlan shares a name with a place in another part of Iowa; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
