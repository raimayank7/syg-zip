Where Clayton County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Clayton County is in the north-east of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 30 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 52066 (North Buena Vista) and 52156 (Luana), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Delaware County (25 miles), Fayette County (27 miles), Allamakee County (29 miles) and Grant County, Wisconsin (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Clayton County begin with 520 (11 codes) and 521 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 520 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Clinton County, Delaware County and Dubuque County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 747 square miles of land (1,935 sq km). The largest is 52076 (Strawberry Point) with 102 square miles, and the smallest is 52158 (Marquette) with 1.3. The middle-sized code here covers 47.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clayton County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Clayton County ranks 9th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clayton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Edgewood, IA 52042
  • Elkader, IA 52043
  • Elkport, IA 52044

Edgewood is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
