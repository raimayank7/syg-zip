Where Tama County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Tama County is in the east of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 50652 (Lincoln) and 52215 (Chelsea), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Grundy County (24 miles), Benton County (25 miles), Marshall County (26 miles) and Poweshiek County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Tama County begin with 506 (5 codes), 522 (4 codes), 523 (3 codes) and 501 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 506 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Black Hawk County, Bremer County and Buchanan County, and has 70 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 757 square miles of land (1,961 sq km). The largest is 50675 (Traer) with 112 square miles, and the smallest is 52348 (Vining) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 54.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Tama County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Tama County ranks 16th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Tama County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Buckingham, IA 50612
  • Chelsea, IA 52215
  • Clutier, IA 52217

Buckingham is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
