Where Marshall County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Marshall County fall close to the middle of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 50106 (Gilman) and 50239 (Saint Anthony), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Jasper County (24 miles), Tama County (26 miles), Story County (27 miles) and Hardin County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Marshall County begin with 501 (7 codes), 500 (3 codes) and 502 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 501 is shared with 25 other counties, such as Audubon County, Boone County and Clarke County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 586 square miles of land (1,519 sq km). The largest is 50158 (Marshalltown) with 212 square miles, and the smallest is 50078 (Ferguson) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 27.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Marshall County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Marshall County ranks 16th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Marshall County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Albion, IA 50005
  • Clemons, IA 50051
  • Ferguson, IA 50078

Albion is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 12 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Do all ZIP codes in Marshall County start with the same digits?
