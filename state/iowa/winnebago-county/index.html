Where Winnebago County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Winnebago County is in the north of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 14 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 50424 (Buffalo Center) and 50450 (Lake Mills), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hancock County (22 miles), Worth County (23 miles), Faribault County, Minnesota (23 miles) and Freeborn County, Minnesota (26 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Winnebago County starts with 504. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 504 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Cerro Gordo County, Floyd County and Franklin County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 517 square miles of land (1,340 sq km). The largest is 50424 (Buffalo Center) with 139 square miles, and the smallest is 50465 (Rake) with 3.3. The middle-sized code here covers 76.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Winnebago County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Winnebago County ranks 75th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Winnebago County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Buffalo Center, IA 50424
  • Forest City, IA 50436
  • Lake Mills, IA 50450
