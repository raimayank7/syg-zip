Where Fayette County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Fayette County is in the north-east of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 50664 (Oran) and 52135 (Clermont), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Buchanan County (25 miles), Bremer County (25 miles), Winneshiek County (25 miles) and Clayton County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Fayette County begin with 521 (9 codes) and 506 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 521 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Allamakee County, Chickasaw County and Clayton County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 681 square miles of land (1,765 sq km). The largest is 52175 (West Union) with 85.7 square miles, and the smallest is 52166 (Saint Lucas) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 51.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Fayette County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Fayette County ranks 13th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Fayette County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Arlington, IA 50606
  • Clermont, IA 52135
  • Elgin, IA 52141

Arlington is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 20 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
