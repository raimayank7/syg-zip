Where Muscatine County is

The ZIP codes of Muscatine County are found in the south-east of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 28 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 52739 (Conesville) and 52769 (Stockton), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Louisa County (20 miles), Cedar County (20 miles), Johnson County (27 miles) and Scott County (27 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Muscatine County starts with 527. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 527 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Cedar County, Clinton County and Johnson County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 530 square miles of land (1,373 sq km). The largest is 52761 (Muscatine) with 178 square miles, and the smallest is 52759 (Montpelier) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 41.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Muscatine County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Muscatine County ranks 35th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.
