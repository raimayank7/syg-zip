Where Wapello County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Wapello County is in the south-east of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 52553 (Eddyville) and 52554 (Eldon), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Davis County (19 miles), Mahaska County (21 miles), Monroe County (24 miles) and Jefferson County (25 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 525. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 525 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Appanoose County, Davis County and Jefferson County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 426 square miles of land (1,103 sq km). The largest is 52501 (Ottumwa) with 225 square miles, and the smallest is 52548 (Chillicothe) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 37.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wapello County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Wapello County ranks 75th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Wapello County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:
