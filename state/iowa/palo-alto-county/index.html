Where Palo Alto County is

The ZIP codes of Palo Alto County are found in the north-west of Iowa. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 50597 (West Bend) and 51358 (Ruthven), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Emmet County (22 miles), Pocahontas County (22 miles), Clay County (23 miles) and Kossuth County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Palo Alto County begin with 505 (6 codes) and 513 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 505 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Buena Vista County, Calhoun County and Emmet County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 609 square miles of land (1,576 sq km). The largest is 50536 (Emmetsburg) with 142 square miles, and the smallest is 50515 (Ayrshire) with 38.4. The middle-sized code here covers 77.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Palo Alto County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Palo Alto County ranks 56th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Palo Alto County
