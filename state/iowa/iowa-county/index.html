Where Iowa County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Iowa County fall in the east of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 52203 (Amana) and 52316 (North English), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Benton County (22 miles), Johnson County (23 miles), Poweshiek County (25 miles) and Keokuk County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Iowa County begin with 523 (8 codes) and 522 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 523 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Benton County, Buchanan County and Cedar County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 566 square miles of land (1,466 sq km). The largest is 52361 (Williamsburg) with 130 square miles, and the smallest is 52308 (Millersburg) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 39.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Iowa County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 52204 (Amana). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Iowa County ranks 16th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Iowa County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Amana, IA 52203 (one of 2 codes for Amana; the street decides which)
  • Conroy, IA 52220
  • Homestead, IA 52236

Questions about Iowa County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Amana, IA?

Amana has 2 ZIP codes in Iowa County: 52203, 52204. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Iowa County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 523, on 8 of the 13 codes.

Which ZIP code in Iowa County covers the most land?

52361 (Williamsburg), at about 130 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
