Where Buena Vista County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Buena Vista County is in the north-west of Iowa. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 50568 (Newell) and 51033 (Linn Grove), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Clay County (20 miles), Pocahontas County (22 miles), Cherokee County (25 miles) and Sac County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Buena Vista County begin with 505 (7 codes) and 510 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 505 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Calhoun County, Emmet County and Hamilton County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 625 square miles of land (1,619 sq km). The largest is 50588 (Storm Lake) with 139 square miles, and the smallest is 50592 (Truesdale) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 66.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Buena Vista County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Buena Vista County ranks 42nd among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Buena Vista County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:
