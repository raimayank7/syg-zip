Where Johnson County is

The ZIP codes of Johnson County are found in the east of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 52338 (Swisher) and 52755 (Lone Tree), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Washington County (23 miles), Iowa County (23 miles), Cedar County (26 miles) and Muscatine County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Johnson County begin with 522 (8 codes), 523 (5 codes) and 527 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 522 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Benton County, Buchanan County and Cedar County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 501 square miles of land (1,298 sq km). The largest is 52240 (Iowa City) with 142 square miles, and the smallest is 52242 (Iowa City) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 26.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Johnson County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 52243 (Iowa City) and 52244 (Iowa City). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Johnson County ranks 13th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Johnson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Iowa City, IA 52240 (one of 6 codes for Iowa City; the street decides which)
  • Coralville, IA 52241
  • Hills, IA 52235

Questions about Johnson County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Iowa City, IA?

Iowa City has 6 ZIP codes in Johnson County: 52240, 52242, 52243, 52244, 52245, 52246. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Johnson County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 522, on 8 of the 14 codes.

Which ZIP code in Johnson County covers the most land?

52240 (Iowa City), at about 142 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
