Where Mitchell County is

The ZIP codes of Mitchell County are found in the north of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 50426 (Carpenter) and 50455 (Mc Intire), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Floyd County (19 miles), Mower County, Minnesota (21 miles), Howard County (24 miles) and Worth County (25 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 504. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 504 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Cerro Gordo County, Floyd County and Franklin County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 422 square miles of land (1,093 sq km). The largest is 50461 (Osage) with 178 square miles, and the smallest is 50426 (Carpenter) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 35.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Mitchell County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 50481 (Toeterville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Mitchell County ranks 56th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Mitchell County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Little Cedar, IA 50454
  • Mc Intire, IA 50455
