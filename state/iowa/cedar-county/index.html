Where Cedar County is

The ZIP codes of Cedar County are found in the east of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 52306 (Mechanicsville) and 52747 (Durant), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Jones County (20 miles), Muscatine County (20 miles), Johnson County (26 miles) and Scott County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Cedar County begin with 523 (3 codes), 527 (3 codes) and 522 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 523 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Benton County, Buchanan County and Delaware County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 486 square miles of land (1,258 sq km). The largest is 52772 (Tipton) with 130 square miles, and the smallest is 52337 (Stanwood) with 30.0. The middle-sized code here covers 50.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cedar County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Cedar County ranks 56th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cedar County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Bennett, IA 52721
  • Clarence, IA 52216
  • Durant, IA 52747

Questions about Cedar County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Bennett, IA?

52721 is the ZIP code listed for Bennett in Cedar County.

Do all ZIP codes in Cedar County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 523, on 3 of the 8 codes.

Which ZIP code in Cedar County covers the most land?

52772 (Tipton), at about 130 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
