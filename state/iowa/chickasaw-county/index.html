Where Chickasaw County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Chickasaw County is in the north-east of Iowa. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 14 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 50658 (Nashua) and 52154 (Lawler), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Bremer County (19 miles), Howard County (21 miles), Floyd County (23 miles) and Fayette County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Chickasaw County begin with 506 (6 codes) and 521 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 506 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Black Hawk County, Bremer County and Buchanan County, and has 70 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 518 square miles of land (1,343 sq km). The largest is 50659 (New Hampton) with 153 square miles, and the smallest is 50603 (Alta Vista) with 45.1. The middle-sized code here covers 81.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Chickasaw County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 50661 (North Washington). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Chickasaw County ranks 75th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Chickasaw County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:
