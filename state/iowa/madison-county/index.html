Where Madison County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Madison County fall in the south of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 18 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 50072 (Earlham) and 50257 (Truro), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Warren County (20 miles), Clarke County (22 miles), Union County (25 miles) and Dallas County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Madison County begin with 502 (5 codes), 500 (2 codes) and 501 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 502 is shared with 21 other counties, such as Boone County, Cass County and Clarke County, and has 65 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 477 square miles of land (1,234 sq km). The largest is 50273 (Winterset) with 195 square miles, and the smallest is 50033 (Bevington) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 42.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Madison County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Madison County ranks 56th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Madison County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Bevington, IA 50033
  • Earlham, IA 50072
  • Macksburg, IA 50155

Questions about Madison County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Bevington, IA?

50033 is the ZIP code listed for Bevington in Madison County.

Do all ZIP codes in Madison County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 502, on 5 of the 8 codes.

Which ZIP code in Madison County covers the most land?

50273 (Winterset), at about 195 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
