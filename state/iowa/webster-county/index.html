Where Webster County is

The ZIP codes of Webster County are found in the north-west of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 25 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 50524 (Clare) and 50530 (Dayton), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Calhoun County (22 miles), Humboldt County (25 miles), Hamilton County (25 miles) and Greene County (27 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Webster County starts with 505. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 505 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Buena Vista County, Calhoun County and Emmet County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 688 square miles of land (1,781 sq km). The largest is 50501 (Fort Dodge) with 152 square miles, and the smallest is 50521 (Burnside) with 1.2. The middle-sized code here covers 39.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Webster County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Webster County ranks 13th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Webster County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Badger, IA 50516
  • Barnum, IA 50518
  • Burnside, IA 50521

Badger is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
