Where Wayne County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Wayne County fall in the south of Iowa. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 50123 (Humeston) and 52590 (Seymour), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lucas County (19 miles), Mercer County, Missouri (22 miles), Decatur County (24 miles) and Putnam County, Missouri (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Wayne County begin with 500 (3 codes), 501 (3 codes) and 525 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 500 is shared with 23 other counties, such as Adair County, Audubon County and Boone County, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 553 square miles of land (1,433 sq km). The largest is 50060 (Corydon) with 173 square miles, and the smallest is 50165 (Millerton) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 73.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wayne County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Wayne County ranks 56th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Wayne County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Allerton, IA 50008
  • Clio, IA 50052
  • Corydon, IA 50060

Questions about Wayne County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Allerton, IA?

50008 is the ZIP code listed for Allerton in Wayne County.

Do all ZIP codes in Wayne County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 500, on 3 of the 8 codes.

Which ZIP code in Wayne County covers the most land?

50060 (Corydon), at about 173 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
