Where Keokuk County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Keokuk County is in the south-east of Iowa. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 50104 (Gibson) and 52585 (Richland), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Mahaska County (20 miles), Wapello County (25 miles), Jefferson County (25 miles) and Washington County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Keokuk County begin with 525 (7 codes), 501 (2 codes), 502 (2 codes), 522 (2 codes) and 523 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 525 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Appanoose County, Davis County and Jefferson County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 704 square miles of land (1,824 sq km). The largest is 52591 (Sigourney) with 124 square miles, and the smallest is 52562 (Hayesville) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 39.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Keokuk County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Keokuk County ranks 9th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Keokuk County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Delta, IA 52550
  • Gibson, IA 50104
  • Harper, IA 52231

Delta is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
