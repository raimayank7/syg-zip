Where Delaware County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Delaware County is in the north-east of Iowa. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 52035 (Colesburg) and 52330 (Ryan), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Buchanan County (25 miles), Dubuque County (25 miles), Clayton County (25 miles) and Jones County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Delaware County begin with 520 (6 codes), 522 (2 codes), 506 (1 code) and 523 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 520 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Clayton County, Clinton County and Dubuque County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 540 square miles of land (1,398 sq km). The largest is 52057 (Manchester) with 139 square miles, and the smallest is 52036 (Delaware) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 48.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Delaware County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Delaware County ranks 35th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Delaware County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

Questions about Delaware County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Colesburg, IA?

52035 is the ZIP code listed for Colesburg in Delaware County.

Do all ZIP codes in Delaware County start with the same digits?

No. They use 4 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 520, on 6 of the 10 codes.

Which ZIP code in Delaware County covers the most land?

52057 (Manchester), at about 139 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
