Where Des Moines County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Des Moines County is in the south-east of Iowa. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 14 miles, and from east to west about 10 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 52601 (Burlington) and 52660 (Yarmouth), about 15 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Henderson County, Illinois (16 miles), Henry County (20 miles), Lee County (21 miles) and Louisa County (22 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 526. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 526 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Henry County, Jefferson County and Lee County, and has 36 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 349 square miles of land (905 sq km). The largest is 52601 (Burlington) with 109 square miles, and the smallest is 52655 (West Burlington) with 17.5. The middle-sized code here covers 38.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Des Moines County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Des Moines County ranks 75th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Des Moines County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Burlington, IA 52601
  • Danville, IA 52623
  • Mediapolis, IA 52637

Burlington is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 20 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
