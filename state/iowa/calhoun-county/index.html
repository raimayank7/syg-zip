Where Calhoun County is

The ZIP codes of Calhoun County are found in the north-west of Iowa. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 50538 (Farnhamville) and 50561 (Lytton), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Webster County (22 miles), Pocahontas County (25 miles), Sac County (25 miles) and Greene County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Calhoun County begin with 505 (8 codes) and 514 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 505 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Buena Vista County, Emmet County and Hamilton County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 616 square miles of land (1,597 sq km). The largest is 50579 (Rockwell City) with 141 square miles, and the smallest is 50552 (Knierim) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 55.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Calhoun County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Calhoun County ranks 35th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.
