Where Crawford County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Crawford County is in the west of Iowa. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 24 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 51432 (Aspinwall) and 51460 (Ricketts), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Shelby County (23 miles), Ida County (26 miles), Carroll County (26 miles) and Sac County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Crawford County begin with 514 (10 codes) and 515 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 514 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Calhoun County, Carroll County and Greene County, and has 32 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 689 square miles of land (1,784 sq km). The largest is 51442 (Denison) with 160 square miles, and the smallest is 51432 (Aspinwall) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 57.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Crawford County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Crawford County ranks 23rd among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.
