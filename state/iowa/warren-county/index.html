Where Warren County is

The ZIP codes of Warren County are found in the south of Iowa. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 50061 (Cumming) and 50139 (Lacona), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Madison County (20 miles), Polk County (21 miles), Clarke County (25 miles) and Marion County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Warren County begin with 501 (6 codes), 502 (4 codes) and 500 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 501 is shared with 25 other counties, such as Audubon County, Boone County and Clarke County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 619 square miles of land (1,602 sq km). The largest is 50125 (Indianola) with 162 square miles, and the smallest is 50241 (Saint Marys) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 42.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Warren County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Warren County ranks 16th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Warren County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Ackworth, IA 50001
  • Carlisle, IA 50047
  • Cumming, IA 50061
