Where Polk County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Polk County sits near the middle of Iowa. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 22 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 50237 (Runnells) and 50243 (Sheldahl), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Warren County (21 miles), Dallas County (22 miles), Story County (26 miles) and Jasper County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Polk County begin with 503 (55 codes), 509 (8 codes), 500 (7 codes), 502 (5 codes) and 501 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 503, so those three digits alone point to Polk County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 32 of the 78 codes. Together they come to about 519 square miles of land (1,345 sq km). The largest is 50237 (Runnells) with 48.5 square miles, and the smallest is 50319 (Des Moines) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 13.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Polk County; a code can reach across its border. 46 codes have no mapped area: 50301 (Des Moines), 50302 (Des Moines), 50303 (Des Moines), 50304 (Des Moines), 50305 (Des Moines) and 41 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Iowa has more ZIP codes: Polk County holds 78 of the 1,055 in the state list, about 7.4%.

Writing an address in Polk County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Des Moines, IA 50301 (one of 57 codes for Des Moines; the street decides which)
  • Urbandale, IA 50322 (one of 3 codes for Urbandale; the street decides which)
  • Ankeny, IA 50021 (one of 2 codes for Ankeny; the street decides which)
