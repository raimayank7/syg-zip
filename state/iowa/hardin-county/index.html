Where Hardin County is

The ZIP codes of Hardin County are found in the north of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 50102 (Garden City) and 50601 (Ackley), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Grundy County (24 miles), Hamilton County (24 miles), Franklin County (26 miles) and Marshall County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Hardin County begin with 502 (5 codes), 501 (3 codes), 506 (3 codes) and 500 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 502 is shared with 21 other counties, such as Boone County, Cass County and Clarke County, and has 65 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 817 square miles of land (2,116 sq km). The largest is 50601 (Ackley) with 139 square miles, and the smallest is 50102 (Garden City) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 84.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hardin County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 50043 (Buckeye) and 50259 (Gifford). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Hardin County ranks 16th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hardin County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Ackley, IA 50601
  • Alden, IA 50006
  • Buckeye, IA 50043
