Where O'Brien County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, O'Brien County is in the north-west of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 51058 (Sutherland) and 51201 (Sheldon), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Osceola County (20 miles), Cherokee County (23 miles), Clay County (25 miles) and Sioux County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of O'Brien County begin with 512 (4 codes), 510 (3 codes) and 513 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 512 is shared with 3 other counties, Lyon County, Osceola County and Sioux County, and has 20 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 622 square miles of land (1,610 sq km). The largest is 51346 (Hartley) with 146 square miles, and the smallest is 51009 (Calumet) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 82.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of O'Brien County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, O'Brien County ranks 56th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in O'Brien County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

Questions about O'Brien County ZIP codes

Do all ZIP codes in O'Brien County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in O'Brien County covers the most land?
