Where Dallas County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Dallas County sits near the middle of Iowa. Its codes spread over about 26 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 50066 (Dawson) and 50261 (Van Meter), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Guthrie County (20 miles), Polk County (22 miles), Madison County (25 miles) and Boone County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Dallas County begin with 500 (7 codes), 502 (5 codes) and 501 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 500 is shared with 23 other counties, such as Adair County, Audubon County and Boone County, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 721 square miles of land (1,867 sq km). The largest is 50003 (Adel) with 115 square miles, and the smallest is 50038 (Booneville) with 3.9. The middle-sized code here covers 41.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Iowa is 48.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Dallas County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Dallas County ranks 9th among the 99 counties and county-level areas of Iowa (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 1,055 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Dallas County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IA and the ZIP code:

  • Adel, IA 50003
  • Booneville, IA 50038
  • Bouton, IA 50039

Questions about Dallas County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Adel, IA?

50003 is the ZIP code listed for Adel in Dallas County.

Do all ZIP codes in Dallas County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 500, on 7 of the 15 codes.

Which ZIP code in Dallas County covers the most land?

50003 (Adel), at about 115 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
