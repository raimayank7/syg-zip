Where Southeastern Connecticut is

The ZIP codes of Southeastern Connecticut are found in the east of Connecticut. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 29 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 06379 (Pawcatuck) and 06474 (North Westchester), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are New London (6.4 miles), Lower Connecticut River Valley (22 miles), Northeastern Connecticut (24 miles) and Washington County, Rhode Island (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Southeastern Connecticut begin with 063 (27 codes), 062 (6 codes) and 064 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 063 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Lower Connecticut River Valley, New London and Northeastern Connecticut, and has 40 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 31 of the 36 codes. Together they come to about 573 square miles of land (1,483 sq km). The largest is 06415 (Colchester) with 56.0 square miles, and the smallest is 06353 (Montville) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 15.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Connecticut is 14.6. Water adds about 33.2 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Southeastern Connecticut; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 06349 (Groton), 06372 (Old Mystic), 06383 (Versailles), 06388 (West Mystic) and 06474 (North Westchester). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Southeastern Connecticut ranks 5th among the 15 counties and county-level areas of Connecticut (equal counts share a rank), with 36 of the 429 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Southeastern Connecticut

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CT and the ZIP code:

  • Groton, CT 06340 (one of 2 codes for Groton; the street decides which)
  • Baltic, CT 06330
  • Colchester, CT 06415

Groton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
