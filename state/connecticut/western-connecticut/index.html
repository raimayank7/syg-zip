Where Western Connecticut is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Western Connecticut fall in the south-west of Connecticut. Its codes spread over about 43 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 06755 (Gaylordsville) and 06836 (Greenwich), about 44 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Fairfield (8.5 miles), Connecticut Metropolitan (13 miles), Westchester County, New York (18 miles) and Putnam County, New York (21 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Western Connecticut begin with 068 (39 codes), 069 (17 codes), 064 (4 codes) and 067 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 068 is shared with 2 other counties, Connecticut Metropolitan and Fairfield, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 34 of the 64 codes. Together they come to about 532 square miles of land (1,379 sq km). The largest is 06776 (New Milford) with 56.2 square miles, and the smallest is 06901 (Stamford) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 16.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Connecticut is 14.6. Water adds about 41.0 square miles, roughly 7% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Western Connecticut; a code can reach across its border. 30 codes have no mapped area: 06404 (Botsford), 06440 (Hawleyville), 06813 (Danbury), 06814 (Danbury), 06816 (Danbury) and 25 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Western Connecticut ranks 2nd among the 15 counties and county-level areas of Connecticut (equal counts share a rank), with 64 of the 429 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Western Connecticut

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CT and the ZIP code:

  • Stamford, CT 06901 (one of 17 codes for Stamford; the street decides which)
  • Norwalk, CT 06850 (one of 10 codes for Norwalk; the street decides which)
  • Danbury, CT 06810 (one of 6 codes for Danbury; the street decides which)

Stamford is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
