Where Northwest Hills is

The ZIP codes of Northwest Hills are found in the north-west of Connecticut. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 33 miles, and from east to west about 29 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 06027 (East Hartland) and 06783 (Roxbury), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Litchfield (14 miles), Naugatuck Valley (23 miles), Capitol Region (28 miles) and Dutchess County, New York (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Northwest Hills begin with 060 (18 codes) and 067 (16 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 060 is shared with 3 other counties, Capitol Region, Naugatuck Valley and Tolland, and has 76 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 32 of the 34 codes. Together they come to about 756 square miles of land (1,959 sq km). The largest is 06759 (Litchfield) with 53.7 square miles, and the smallest is 06061 (Pine Meadow) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 27.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Connecticut is 14.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Northwest Hills; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 06094 (Winchester Center) and 06792 (Torrington). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Northwest Hills ranks 6th among the 15 counties and county-level areas of Connecticut (equal counts share a rank), with 34 of the 429 codes in the state list.
