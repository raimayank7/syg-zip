Connecticut ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 429 ZIP codes for Connecticut, about 1.0% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 36th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 15 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Capitol Region (119), Western Connecticut (64), South Central Regional (48), Naugatuck Valley (41) and Southeastern Connecticut (36). At the other end, 5 have only one or two codes, for example Fairfield, Litchfield and Middlesex.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Connecticut uses 10 of these prefixes, from 060 to 069. The busiest are 060 (76 codes, most of them for Manchester), 064 (64 codes, most of them for Wallingford), 061 (51 codes, most of them for Hartford) and 067 (50 codes, most of them for Waterbury). The first digit 0 is shared with Maine, Massachusetts, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, Rhode Island and Vermont.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 288 of the 429 codes. The largest is 06076 (Stafford Springs, Capitol Region) at 86.6 square miles and the smallest is 06376 (South Lyme) at 0.03; the middle value is 14.6 square miles. The other 141 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 06079 (Taconic) and the southernmost 06836 (Greenwich); the one farthest east is 06243 (East Killingly) and farthest west 06831 (Greenwich).

Place names and addresses in Connecticut

Of the 267 place names in the list, 234 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Hartford (36), New Haven (28), Stamford (17) and Waterbury (16). In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters CT and the code, as in Hartford, CT 06101. The name of the county is left out.
