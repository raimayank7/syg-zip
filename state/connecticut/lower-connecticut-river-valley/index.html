Where Lower Connecticut River Valley is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Lower Connecticut River Valley is in the east of Connecticut. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 22 miles, and from east to west about 24 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 06376 (South Lyme) and 06416 (Cromwell), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Middlesex (12 miles), New London (16 miles), Southeastern Connecticut (22 miles) and South Central Regional (22 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lower Connecticut River Valley begin with 064 (23 codes) and 063 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 064 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Capitol Region, Connecticut Metropolitan and Middlesex, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 415 square miles of land (1,074 sq km). The largest is 06371 (Old Lyme) with 54.0 square miles, and the smallest is 06376 (South Lyme) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 14.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Connecticut is 14.6. Water adds about 28.6 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lower Connecticut River Valley; a code can reach across its border.
