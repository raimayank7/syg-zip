Where Capitol Region is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Capitol Region fall in the north of Connecticut. Its codes spread over about 32 miles from north to south and 37 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 06077 (Staffordville) and 06444 (Marion), about 45 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Middlesex (18 miles), Tolland (19 miles), Hampden County, Massachusetts (24 miles) and Lower Connecticut River Valley (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Capitol Region begin with 060 (55 codes), 061 (51 codes), 062 (8 codes) and 064 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 060 is shared with 3 other counties, Naugatuck Valley, Northwest Hills and Tolland, and has 76 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 67 of the 119 codes. Together they come to about 976 square miles of land (2,529 sq km). The largest is 06076 (Stafford Springs) with 86.6 square miles, and the smallest is 06072 (Somersville) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 12.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Connecticut is 14.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Capitol Region; a code can reach across its border. 52 codes have no mapped area: 06006 (Windsor), 06025 (East Glastonbury), 06028 (East Windsor Hill), 06030 (Farmington), 06034 (Farmington) and 47 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Connecticut has more ZIP codes: Capitol Region holds 119 of the 429 in the state list, about 27.7%.

Writing an address in Capitol Region

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CT and the ZIP code:

  • Hartford, CT 06101 (one of 36 codes for Hartford; the street decides which)
  • West Hartford, CT 06107 (one of 7 codes for West Hartford; the street decides which)
  • East Hartford, CT 06108 (one of 4 codes for East Hartford; the street decides which)

Hartford is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 14 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
