Where Miami-Dade County is

The ZIP codes of Miami-Dade County are found in the south-east of Florida. Its codes spread over about 47 miles from north to south and 31 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 33034 (Homestead) and 33180 (Miami), about 56 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Broward County (26 miles), Palm Beach County (58 miles), Collier County (89 miles) and Monroe County (89 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Miami-Dade County begin with 331 (82 codes), 330 (22 codes) and 332 (22 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 331 is shared with 1 other county, Putnam County, and has 83 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 80 of the 126 codes. Together they come to about 1,996 square miles of land (5,169 sq km). The largest is 33034 (Homestead) with 1,173 square miles, and the smallest is 33101 (Miami) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 5.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 156 square miles, roughly 7% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Miami-Dade County; a code can reach across its border. 46 codes have no mapped area: 33002 (Hialeah), 33011 (Hialeah), 33017 (Hialeah), 33090 (Homestead), 33092 (Homestead) and 41 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Florida has more ZIP codes: Miami-Dade County holds 126 of the 1,473 in the state list, about 8.6%.

Writing an address in Miami-Dade County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • Miami, FL 33101 (one of 94 codes for Miami; the street decides which)
  • Hialeah, FL 33002 (one of 10 codes for Hialeah; the street decides which)
  • Homestead, FL 33030 (one of 9 codes for Homestead; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named Miami are also listed under Putnam County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Miami is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
