Where Polk County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Polk County is in the east of Florida. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 134 miles, and from east to west about 51 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 33967 (Fort Myers) and 34714 (Clermont), about 134 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Osceola County (31 miles), Hardee County (33 miles), Hillsborough County (40 miles) and Highlands County (44 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Polk County begin with 338 (54 codes), 347 (2 codes) and 339 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 338 is shared with 3 other counties, Hardee County, Highlands County and Osceola County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 43 of the 57 codes. Together they come to about 1,928 square miles of land (4,994 sq km). The largest is 33898 (Lake Wales) with 297 square miles, and the smallest is 33840 (Eaton Park) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 21.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Water adds about 219 square miles, roughly 10% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Polk County; a code can reach across its border. 14 codes have no mapped area: 33802 (Lakeland), 33804 (Lakeland), 33806 (Lakeland), 33807 (Lakeland), 33820 (Alturas) and 9 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Polk County ranks 7th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 57 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Polk County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • Lakeland, FL 33801 (one of 13 codes for Lakeland; the street decides which)
  • Winter Haven, FL 33880 (one of 7 codes for Winter Haven; the street decides which)
  • Davenport, FL 33836 (one of 4 codes for Davenport; the street decides which)

Lakeland is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Clermont, Fort Myers and Kissimmee share a name with a place in another part of Florida; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
