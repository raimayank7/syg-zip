Where Saint Johns County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Saint Johns County fall in the north-east of Florida. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 37 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 32004 (Ponte Vedra Beach) and 32145 (Hastings), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Clay County (24 miles), Duval County (28 miles), Flagler County (33 miles) and Putnam County (40 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Saint Johns County begin with 320 (10 codes), 322 (2 codes) and 321 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 320 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Baker County, Bradford County and Clay County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 601 square miles of land (1,557 sq km). The largest is 32092 (Saint Augustine) with 124 square miles, and the smallest is 32080 (Saint Augustine) with 15.7. The middle-sized code here covers 57.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 83.9 square miles, roughly 12% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Saint Johns County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 32004 (Ponte Vedra Beach), 32085 (Saint Augustine) and 32260 (Jacksonville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Saint Johns County ranks 31st among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Saint Johns County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • Saint Augustine, FL 32080 (one of 6 codes for Saint Augustine; the street decides which)
  • Ponte Vedra Beach, FL 32004 (one of 2 codes for Ponte Vedra Beach; the street decides which)
  • Elkton, FL 32033

Jacksonville shares a name with a place in another part of Florida; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Saint Johns County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Saint Augustine, FL?

Saint Augustine has 6 ZIP codes in Saint Johns County: 32080, 32084, 32085, 32086, 32092, 32095. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Saint Johns County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 320, on 10 of the 13 codes.

Which ZIP code in Saint Johns County covers the most land?

32092 (Saint Augustine), at about 124 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
