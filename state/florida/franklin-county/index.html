Where Franklin County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Franklin County is in the north-west of Florida. Its codes spread over about 49 miles from north to south and 43 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 32318 (Tallahassee) and 32320 (Apalachicola), about 66 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Wakulla County (27 miles), Liberty County (29 miles), Gulf County (33 miles) and Calhoun County (45 miles).

The first three digits

All 6 codes here begin with the same three digits, 323. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 323 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Gadsden County, Jefferson County and Leon County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 4 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 201 square miles of land (519 sq km). The largest is 32328 (Eastpoint) with 85.8 square miles, and the smallest is 32323 (Lanark Village) with 7.3. The middle-sized code here covers 53.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 157 square miles, roughly 44% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Franklin County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 32318 (Tallahassee) and 32329 (Apalachicola). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Franklin County ranks 44th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Franklin County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • Apalachicola, FL 32320 (one of 2 codes for Apalachicola; the street decides which)
  • Carrabelle, FL 32322
  • Eastpoint, FL 32328

Tallahassee shares a name with a place in another part of Florida; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
