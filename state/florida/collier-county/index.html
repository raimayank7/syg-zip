Where Collier County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Collier County is in the south-east of Florida. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 45 miles, and from east to west about 40 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 34108 (Naples) and 34141 (Ochopee), about 48 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Lee County (35 miles), Hendry County (45 miles), Glades County (59 miles) and Charlotte County (64 miles).

The first three digits

All 26 codes here begin with the same three digits, 341. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 341 is shared with 1 other county, Lee County, and has 30 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 22 of the 26 codes. Together they come to about 2,346 square miles of land (6,075 sq km). The largest is 34141 (Ochopee) with 988 square miles, and the smallest is 34138 (Chokoloskee) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 13.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Water adds about 94.2 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Collier County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 34106 (Naples), 34107 (Naples), 34143 (Immokalee) and 34146 (Marco Island). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Collier County ranks 19th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 26 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Collier County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • Naples, FL 34101 (one of 17 codes for Naples; the street decides which)
  • Immokalee, FL 34142 (one of 2 codes for Immokalee; the street decides which)
  • Marco Island, FL 34145 (one of 2 codes for Marco Island; the street decides which)

Naples is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
