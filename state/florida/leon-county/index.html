Where Leon County is

The ZIP codes of Leon County are found in the north of Florida. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 32309 (Tallahassee) and 32362 (Woodville), about 17 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Gadsden County (22 miles), Wakulla County (23 miles), Grady County, Georgia (28 miles) and Decatur County, Georgia (31 miles).

The first three digits

All 20 codes here begin with the same three digits, 323. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 323 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Franklin County, Gadsden County and Jefferson County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 20 codes. Together they come to about 573 square miles of land (1,483 sq km). The largest is 32309 (Tallahassee) with 122 square miles, and the smallest is 32306 (Tallahassee) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 25.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Water adds about 33.1 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Leon County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 32302 (Tallahassee), 32314 (Tallahassee), 32315 (Tallahassee), 32316 (Tallahassee), 32362 (Woodville) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Leon County ranks 22nd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 20 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Leon County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • Tallahassee, FL 32301 (one of 19 codes for Tallahassee; the street decides which)
  • Woodville, FL 32362

ZIP codes named Tallahassee are also listed under Franklin County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site.
