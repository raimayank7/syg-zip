Where Pinellas County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Pinellas County is in the east of Florida. Its codes spread over about 33 miles from north to south and 13 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 33715 (Saint Petersburg) and 34688 (Tarpon Springs), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Hillsborough County (20 miles), Pasco County (31 miles), Manatee County (32 miles) and Hernando County (48 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Pinellas County begin with 337 (61 codes) and 346 (12 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 337, so those three digits alone point to Pinellas County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 48 of the 73 codes. Together they come to about 273 square miles of land (707 sq km). The largest is 34688 (Tarpon Springs) with 15.8 square miles, and the smallest is 33786 (Belleair Beach) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 5.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 86.2 square miles, roughly 24% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pinellas County; a code can reach across its border. 25 codes have no mapped area: 33729 (Saint Petersburg), 33730 (Saint Petersburg), 33731 (Saint Petersburg), 33732 (Saint Petersburg), 33733 (Saint Petersburg) and 20 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Pinellas County ranks 6th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 73 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pinellas County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:
