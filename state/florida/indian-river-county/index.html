Where Indian River County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Indian River County fall in the east of Florida. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 32957 (Roseland) and 32962 (Vero Beach), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are St. Lucie County (22 miles), Okeechobee County (38 miles), Martin County (39 miles) and Brevard County (40 miles).

The first three digits

All 16 codes here begin with the same three digits, 329. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 329 is shared with 1 other county, Brevard County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 527 square miles of land (1,364 sq km). The largest is 32966 (Vero Beach) with 260 square miles, and the smallest is 32970 (Wabasso) with 1.0. The middle-sized code here covers 27.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Water adds about 43.7 square miles, roughly 8% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Indian River County; a code can reach across its border. 7 codes have no mapped area: 32957 (Roseland), 32961 (Vero Beach), 32964 (Vero Beach), 32965 (Vero Beach), 32969 (Vero Beach) and 2 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Indian River County ranks 27th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Indian River County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:
