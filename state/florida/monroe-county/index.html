Where Monroe County is

The ZIP codes of Monroe County are found in the south-east of Florida. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 37 miles, and from east to west about 84 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 33037 (Key Largo) and 33040 (Key West), about 91 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Miami-Dade County (89 miles), Collier County (99 miles), Broward County (112 miles) and Lee County (134 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 330. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 330 is shared with 2 other counties, Broward County and Miami-Dade County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 116 square miles of land (301 sq km). The largest is 33037 (Key Largo) with 32.9 square miles, and the smallest is 33051 (Key Colony Beach) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 9.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Water adds about 497 square miles, roughly 81% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Monroe County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 33041 (Key West), 33045 (Key West) and 33052 (Marathon Shores). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Monroe County ranks 33rd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Monroe County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • Key West, FL 33040 (one of 3 codes for Key West; the street decides which)
  • Big Pine Key, FL 33043
  • Islamorada, FL 33036

Questions about Monroe County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Key West, FL?

Key West has 3 ZIP codes in Monroe County: 33040, 33041, 33045. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Monroe County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 12 start with 330.

Which ZIP code in Monroe County covers the most land?

33037 (Key Largo), at about 32.9 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
