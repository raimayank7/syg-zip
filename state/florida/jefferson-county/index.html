Where Jefferson County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Jefferson County fall in the north of Florida. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 115 miles, and from east to west about 162 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 32337 (Lloyd) and 32753 (Debary), about 197 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Taylor County (15 miles), Lafayette County (17 miles), Madison County (20 miles) and Suwannee County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Jefferson County begin with 323 (5 codes) and 327 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 323 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Franklin County, Gadsden County and Leon County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 4 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 671 square miles of land (1,737 sq km). The largest is 32344 (Monticello) with 445 square miles, and the smallest is 32337 (Lloyd) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 113 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jefferson County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 32345 (Monticello) and 32753 (Debary). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Jefferson County ranks 44th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jefferson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • Monticello, FL 32344 (one of 2 codes for Monticello; the street decides which)
  • Debary, FL 32753
  • Lamont, FL 32336

Monticello is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 14 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Debary shares a name with a place in another part of Florida; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Jefferson County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Monticello, FL?

Monticello has 2 ZIP codes in Jefferson County: 32344, 32345. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Jefferson County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 323, on 5 of the 6 codes.

Which ZIP code in Jefferson County covers the most land?

32344 (Monticello), at about 445 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
