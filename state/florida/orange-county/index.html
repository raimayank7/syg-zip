Where Orange County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Orange County is in the north-east of Florida. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 39 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 32709 (Christmas) and 32777 (Tangerine), about 40 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Seminole County (13 miles), Osceola County (21 miles), Lake County (28 miles) and Volusia County (40 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Orange County begin with 328 (57 codes), 327 (14 codes) and 347 (8 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 328 is shared with 1 other county, Brevard County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 46 of the 79 codes. Together they come to about 859 square miles of land (2,225 sq km). The largest is 32709 (Christmas) with 148 square miles, and the smallest is 32768 (Plymouth) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 11.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Water adds about 68.8 square miles, roughly 7% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Orange County; a code can reach across its border. 33 codes have no mapped area: 32704 (Apopka), 32710 (Clarcona), 32777 (Tangerine), 32790 (Winter Park), 32793 (Winter Park) and 28 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Orange County ranks 4th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 79 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Orange County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • Orlando, FL 32801 (one of 57 codes for Orlando; the street decides which)
  • Winter Park, FL 32789 (one of 4 codes for Winter Park; the street decides which)
  • Apopka, FL 32703 (one of 3 codes for Apopka; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named Orlando are also listed under Brevard County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Orlando is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
