Where Osceola County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Osceola County is in the east of Florida. Its codes spread over about 31 miles from north to south and 35 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 34739 (Kenansville) and 34747 (Kissimmee), about 44 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Orange County (21 miles), Polk County (31 miles), Seminole County (34 miles) and Brevard County (41 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Osceola County begin with 347 (14 codes) and 338 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 347 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Lake County, Orange County and Polk County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 1,133 square miles of land (2,935 sq km). The largest is 34739 (Kenansville) with 396 square miles, and the smallest is 34743 (Kissimmee) with 6.9. The middle-sized code here covers 38.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 148 square miles, roughly 12% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Osceola County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 34742 (Kissimmee), 34745 (Kissimmee) and 34770 (Saint Cloud). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Osceola County ranks 29th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Osceola County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • Kissimmee, FL 34741 (one of 8 codes for Kissimmee; the street decides which)
  • Saint Cloud, FL 34769 (one of 5 codes for Saint Cloud; the street decides which)
  • Intercession City, FL 33848

ZIP codes named Kissimmee are also listed under Polk County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site.
