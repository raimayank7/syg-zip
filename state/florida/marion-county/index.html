Where Marion County is

The ZIP codes of Marion County are found in the north-east of Florida. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 35 miles, and from east to west about 41 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 32134 (Fort Mc Coy) and 34431 (Dunnellon), about 44 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Sumter County (29 miles), Citrus County (31 miles), Putnam County (32 miles) and Alachua County (35 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Marion County begin with 344 (23 codes), 321 (9 codes) and 326 (6 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 344 is shared with 3 other counties, Citrus County, Levy County and Sumter County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 28 of the 38 codes. Together they come to about 1,326 square miles of land (3,434 sq km). The largest is 32134 (Fort Mc Coy) with 268 square miles, and the smallest is 32634 (Fairfield) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 32.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 77.5 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Marion County; a code can reach across its border. 10 codes have no mapped area: 32111 (Candler), 32183 (Ocklawaha), 32663 (Lowell), 34421 (Belleview), 34430 (Dunnellon) and 5 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Marion County ranks 12th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 38 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Marion County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • Ocala, FL 34470 (one of 14 codes for Ocala; the street decides which)
  • Dunnellon, FL 34430 (one of 3 codes for Dunnellon; the street decides which)
  • Belleview, FL 34420 (one of 2 codes for Belleview; the street decides which)

Dunnellon shares a name with a place in another part of Florida; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
