Florida ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 1,473 ZIP codes for Florida, about 3.6% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 6th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 67 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Miami-Dade County (126), Hillsborough County (94), Broward County (89), Orange County (79) and Palm Beach County (75). At the other end, 3 have only one or two codes, for example Gilchrist County, Glades County and Lafayette County.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Florida uses 26 of these prefixes, from 320 to 349. The busiest are 331 (83 codes, most of them for Miami), 334 (82 codes, most of them for West Palm Beach), 327 (74 codes, most of them for Altamonte Springs) and 330 (71 codes, most of them for Pompano Beach). The first digit 3 is shared with Alabama, Georgia, Mississippi and Tennessee.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 1,013 of the 1,473 codes. The largest is 33034 (Homestead, Miami-Dade County) at 1,173 square miles and the smallest is 34487 (Homosassa) at 0.01; the middle value is 15.2 square miles. The other 460 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 32452 (Noma) and the southernmost 33040 (Key West); the one farthest east is 33480 (Palm Beach) and farthest west 32568 (Mc David).

Place names and addresses in Florida

Of the 528 place names in the list, 355 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Miami (95), Orlando (59), Tampa (58) and Jacksonville (48). 17 place names appear under more than one county, among them Clermont, Debary, Dunnellon and Fort Myers: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters FL and the code, as in Miami, FL 33101. The name of the county is left out.

Questions about Florida ZIP codes

What ZIP codes does Florida use?

In this list, Florida ZIP codes run from 32003 to 34997 and start with 3. A few codes inside that range can belong to a neighbouring state near the border.

Which place in Florida has the most ZIP codes?

Miami, with 95 codes in this list.
