Where St. Lucie County is

The ZIP codes of St. Lucie County are found in the east of Florida. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 34951 (Fort Pierce) and 34987 (Port Saint Lucie), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Martin County (17 miles), Indian River County (22 miles), Okeechobee County (31 miles) and Palm Beach County (55 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of St. Lucie County starts with 349. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 349 is shared with 2 other counties, Martin County and Okeechobee County, and has 32 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 514 square miles of land (1,331 sq km). The largest is 34945 (Fort Pierce) with 217 square miles, and the smallest is 34950 (Fort Pierce) with 5.2. The middle-sized code here covers 15.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Water adds about 30.3 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of St. Lucie County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 34948 (Fort Pierce), 34954 (Fort Pierce), 34979 (Fort Pierce), 34985 (Port Saint Lucie) and 34988 (Port Saint Lucie). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, St. Lucie County ranks 23rd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in St. Lucie County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • Fort Pierce, FL 34945 (one of 11 codes for Fort Pierce; the street decides which)
  • Port Saint Lucie, FL 34952 (one of 8 codes for Port Saint Lucie; the street decides which)
