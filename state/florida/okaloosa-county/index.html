Where Okaloosa County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Okaloosa County is in the north-west of Florida. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 39 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 32567 (Laurel Hill) and 32569 (Mary Esther), about 43 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Walton County (23 miles), Santa Rosa County (29 miles), Escambia County (41 miles) and Covington County, Alabama (46 miles).

The first three digits

All 18 codes here begin with the same three digits, 325. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 325 is shared with 3 other counties, Escambia County, Santa Rosa County and Walton County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 18 codes. Together they come to about 831 square miles of land (2,153 sq km). The largest is 32531 (Baker) with 228 square miles, and the smallest is 32579 (Shalimar) with 3.5. The middle-sized code here covers 26.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Water adds about 37.5 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Okaloosa County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 32537 (Milligan), 32540 (Destin), 32549 (Fort Walton Beach) and 32588 (Niceville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Okaloosa County ranks 25th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Okaloosa County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • Fort Walton Beach, FL 32547 (one of 3 codes for Fort Walton Beach; the street decides which)
  • Crestview, FL 32536 (one of 2 codes for Crestview; the street decides which)
  • Destin, FL 32540 (one of 2 codes for Destin; the street decides which)
