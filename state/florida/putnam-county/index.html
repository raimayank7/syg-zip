Where Putnam County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Putnam County is in the north-east of Florida. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 277 miles, and from east to west about 99 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 32666 (Melrose) and 33112 (Miami), about 290 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Flagler County (26 miles), Marion County (32 miles), Volusia County (39 miles) and Saint Johns County (40 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Putnam County begin with 321 (16 codes), 320 (1 code), 326 (1 code) and 331 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 321 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Clay County, Flagler County and Lake County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 609 square miles of land (1,577 sq km). The largest is 32177 (Palatka) with 227 square miles, and the smallest is 32157 (Lake Como) with 2.5. The middle-sized code here covers 29.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Water adds about 86.7 square miles, roughly 12% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Putnam County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 32007 (Bostwick), 32138 (Grandin), 32149 (Interlachen), 32178 (Palatka), 32185 (Putnam Hall) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Putnam County ranks 23rd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Putnam County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

Questions about Putnam County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Interlachen, FL?

Interlachen has 2 ZIP codes in Putnam County: 32148, 32149. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Putnam County start with the same digits?

No. They use 4 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 321, on 16 of the 19 codes.

Which ZIP code in Putnam County covers the most land?

32177 (Palatka), at about 227 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
