Where Hillsborough County is

The ZIP codes of Hillsborough County are found in the east of Florida. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 32 miles, and from east to west about 31 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 33547 (Lithia) and 33556 (Odessa), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Pinellas County (20 miles), Pasco County (24 miles), Manatee County (33 miles) and Polk County (40 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Hillsborough County begin with 336 (58 codes) and 335 (36 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 336, so those three digits alone point to Hillsborough County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 53 of the 94 codes. Together they come to about 1,019 square miles of land (2,638 sq km). The largest is 33547 (Lithia) with 161 square miles, and the smallest is 33550 (Mango) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 9.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Water adds about 62.3 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hillsborough County; a code can reach across its border. 41 codes have no mapped area: 33508 (Brandon), 33509 (Brandon), 33564 (Plant City), 33568 (Riverview), 33571 (Sun City Center) and 36 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Hillsborough County ranks 2nd among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 94 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hillsborough County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • Tampa, FL 33601 (one of 58 codes for Tampa; the street decides which)
  • Plant City, FL 33563 (one of 5 codes for Plant City; the street decides which)
  • Brandon, FL 33508 (one of 4 codes for Brandon; the street decides which)

Lutz and Riverview share a name with a place in another part of Florida; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
