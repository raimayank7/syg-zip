Where Escambia County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Escambia County fall in the north-west of Florida. Its codes spread over about 43 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 32508 (Pensacola) and 32535 (Century), about 43 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Santa Rosa County (13 miles), Baldwin County, Alabama (29 miles), Okaloosa County (41 miles) and Escambia County, Alabama (41 miles).

The first three digits

All 28 codes here begin with the same three digits, 325. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 325 is shared with 3 other counties, Okaloosa County, Santa Rosa County and Walton County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 18 of the 28 codes. Together they come to about 620 square miles of land (1,606 sq km). The largest is 32568 (Mc David) with 205 square miles, and the smallest is 32512 (Pensacola) with 0.07. The middle-sized code here covers 12.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Water adds about 39.3 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Escambia County; a code can reach across its border. 10 codes have no mapped area: 32513 (Pensacola), 32516 (Pensacola), 32520 (Pensacola), 32521 (Pensacola), 32522 (Pensacola) and 5 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Escambia County ranks 18th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 28 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.
