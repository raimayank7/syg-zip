Where Sarasota County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Sarasota County is in the east of Florida. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 31 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 34224 (Englewood) and 34234 (Sarasota), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Manatee County (20 miles), Charlotte County (26 miles), DeSoto County (34 miles) and Hardee County (42 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Sarasota County starts with 342. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 342 is shared with 2 other counties, DeSoto County and Manatee County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 24 of the 34 codes. Together they come to about 450 square miles of land (1,166 sq km). The largest is 34241 (Sarasota) with 70.1 square miles, and the smallest is 34236 (Sarasota) with 3.6. The middle-sized code here covers 11.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Water adds about 57.2 square miles, roughly 11% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sarasota County; a code can reach across its border. 10 codes have no mapped area: 34230 (Sarasota), 34249 (Sarasota), 34272 (Laurel), 34274 (Nokomis), 34276 (Sarasota) and 5 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Sarasota County ranks 14th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 34 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Sarasota County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • Sarasota, FL 34230 (one of 17 codes for Sarasota; the street decides which)
  • North Port, FL 34286 (one of 6 codes for North Port; the street decides which)
  • Venice, FL 34284 (one of 4 codes for Venice; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named Sarasota are also listed under Manatee County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site.
