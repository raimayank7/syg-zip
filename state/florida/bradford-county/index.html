Where Bradford County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Bradford County fall in the north-east of Florida. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 11 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 32058 (Lawtey) and 32622 (Brooker), about 14 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Union County (9.7 miles), Baker County (22 miles), Clay County (22 miles) and Alachua County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Bradford County begin with 320 (4 codes) and 326 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 320 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Baker County, Clay County and Columbia County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 4 of the 5 codes. Together they come to about 310 square miles of land (803 sq km). The largest is 32091 (Starke) with 152 square miles, and the smallest is 32044 (Hampton) with 17.5. The middle-sized code here covers 70.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 9.9 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bradford County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 32042 (Graham). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Bradford County ranks 48th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bradford County
