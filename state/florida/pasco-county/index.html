Where Pasco County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Pasco County is in the east of Florida. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 42 miles, and from east to west about 37 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 33578 (Riverview) and 34679 (Aripeka), about 44 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Hernando County (19 miles), Hillsborough County (24 miles), Pinellas County (31 miles) and Citrus County (41 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Pasco County begin with 346 (19 codes) and 335 (17 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 346 is shared with 2 other counties, Hernando County and Pinellas County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 26 of the 36 codes. Together they come to about 713 square miles of land (1,847 sq km). The largest is 33523 (Dade City) with 105 square miles, and the smallest is 33574 (Saint Leo) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 22.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Water adds about 28.4 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pasco County; a code can reach across its border. 10 codes have no mapped area: 33524 (Crystal Springs), 33526 (Dade City), 33537 (Lacoochee), 33539 (Zephyrhills), 33593 (Trilby) and 5 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Pasco County ranks 13th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 36 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pasco County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • New Port Richey, FL 34652 (one of 5 codes for New Port Richey; the street decides which)
  • Zephyrhills, FL 33539 (one of 4 codes for Zephyrhills; the street decides which)
  • Dade City, FL 33523 (one of 3 codes for Dade City; the street decides which)

Lutz, Riverview and Spring Hill share a name with a place in another part of Florida; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Pasco County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for New Port Richey, FL?

New Port Richey has 5 ZIP codes in Pasco County: 34652, 34653, 34654, 34655, 34656. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Pasco County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Pasco County covers the most land?
