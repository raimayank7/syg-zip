Where Walton County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Walton County is in the north-west of Florida. Its codes spread over about 47 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 32461 (Rosemary Beach) and 32538 (Paxton), about 50 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Okaloosa County (23 miles), Washington County (29 miles), Holmes County (29 miles) and Geneva County, Alabama (42 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Walton County begin with 324 (7 codes) and 325 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 324 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Bay County, Calhoun County and Gulf County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 561 square miles of land (1,453 sq km). The largest is 32433 (Defuniak Springs) with 251 square miles, and the smallest is 32550 (Miramar Beach) with 7.0. The middle-sized code here covers 75.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 32.1 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Walton County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 32422 (Argyle), 32434 (Mossy Head) and 32538 (Paxton). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Walton County ranks 40th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Walton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • Defuniak Springs, FL 32433 (one of 2 codes for Defuniak Springs; the street decides which)
  • Argyle, FL 32422
  • Freeport, FL 32439

Questions about Walton County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Defuniak Springs, FL?

Defuniak Springs has 2 ZIP codes in Walton County: 32433, 32435. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Walton County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 324, on 7 of the 9 codes.

Which ZIP code in Walton County covers the most land?

32433 (Defuniak Springs), at about 251 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
