Where Alachua County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Alachua County fall in the north-east of Florida. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 31 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 32643 (High Springs) and 32654 (Island Grove), about 41 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Bradford County (23 miles), Union County (24 miles), Gilchrist County (30 miles) and Levy County (35 miles).

The first three digits

All 31 codes here begin with the same three digits, 326. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 326 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Bradford County, Clay County and Dixie County, and has 55 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 21 of the 31 codes. Together they come to about 1,068 square miles of land (2,766 sq km). The largest is 32640 (Hawthorne) with 188 square miles, and the smallest is 32610 (Gainesville) with 0.07. The middle-sized code here covers 29.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Water adds about 98.7 square miles, roughly 8% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Alachua County; a code can reach across its border. 10 codes have no mapped area: 32602 (Gainesville), 32604 (Gainesville), 32611 (Gainesville), 32614 (Gainesville), 32627 (Gainesville) and 5 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Alachua County ranks 16th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 31 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Alachua County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • Gainesville, FL 32601 (one of 17 codes for Gainesville; the street decides which)
  • Alachua, FL 32615 (one of 2 codes for Alachua; the street decides which)
  • High Springs, FL 32643 (one of 2 codes for High Springs; the street decides which)

Gainesville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
