Where Volusia County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Volusia County fall in the north-east of Florida. Its codes spread over about 38 miles from north to south and 41 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 32190 (Seville) and 32759 (Oak Hill), about 51 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Seminole County (27 miles), Flagler County (29 miles), Putnam County (39 miles) and Orange County (40 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Volusia County begin with 321 (30 codes) and 327 (17 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 321 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Clay County, Flagler County and Lake County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 27 of the 47 codes. Together they come to about 1,107 square miles of land (2,867 sq km). The largest is 32168 (New Smyrna Beach) with 154 square miles, and the smallest is 32723 (Deland) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 23.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Water adds about 95.2 square miles, roughly 8% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Volusia County; a code can reach across its border. 20 codes have no mapped area: 32105 (Barberville), 32115 (Daytona Beach), 32116 (Daytona Beach), 32120 (Daytona Beach), 32121 (Daytona Beach) and 15 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Volusia County ranks 9th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 47 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Volusia County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters FL and the ZIP code:

  • Daytona Beach, FL 32114 (one of 13 codes for Daytona Beach; the street decides which)
  • Deland, FL 32720 (one of 4 codes for Deland; the street decides which)
  • Deltona, FL 32725 (one of 4 codes for Deltona; the street decides which)

Debary and Mid Florida share a name with a place in another part of Florida; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
