Where Highlands County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Highlands County is in the east of Florida. Its codes spread over about 33 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 33825 (Avon Park) and 33960 (Venus), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hardee County (27 miles), DeSoto County (34 miles), Okeechobee County (34 miles) and Glades County (38 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Highlands County begin with 338 (10 codes) and 339 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 338 is shared with 3 other counties, Hardee County, Osceola County and Polk County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 1,001 square miles of land (2,593 sq km). The largest is 33852 (Lake Placid) with 293 square miles, and the smallest is 33872 (Sebring) with 22.7. The middle-sized code here covers 104 square miles; the middle value for all of Florida is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 91.7 square miles, roughly 8% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Highlands County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 33826 (Avon Park), 33862 (Lake Placid) and 33871 (Sebring). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Highlands County ranks 36th among the 67 counties and county-level areas of Florida (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 1,473 codes in the state list.
