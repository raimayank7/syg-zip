Where Kiowa County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Kiowa County fall in the south of Oklahoma. Its codes spread over about 29 miles from north to south and 29 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 73062 (Mountain View) and 73566 (Snyder), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Greer County (28 miles), Washita County (29 miles), Jackson County (31 miles) and Comanche County (35 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Kiowa County begin with 735 (3 codes), 730 (2 codes) and 736 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 735 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Comanche County, Cotton County and Greer County, and has 55 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,139 square miles of land (2,951 sq km). The largest is 73062 (Mountain View) with 233 square miles, and the smallest is 73566 (Snyder) with 110. The middle-sized code here covers 174 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Kiowa County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Kiowa County ranks 41st among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 765 codes in the state list.
