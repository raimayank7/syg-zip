Where Hughes County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Hughes County fall in the east of Oklahoma. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 26 miles, and from east to west about 18 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 74531 (Calvin) and 74839 (Dustin), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Seminole County (24 miles), Okfuskee County (29 miles), Pittsburg County (30 miles) and Pontotoc County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Hughes County begin with 748 (5 codes) and 745 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 748 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Garvin County, Johnston County and Lincoln County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 877 square miles of land (2,272 sq km). The largest is 74848 (Holdenville) with 258 square miles, and the smallest is 74827 (Atwood) with 37.1. The middle-sized code here covers 107 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hughes County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Hughes County ranks 41st among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 765 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hughes County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OK and the ZIP code:

  • Atwood, OK 74827
  • Calvin, OK 74531
  • Dustin, OK 74839

Atwood is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
