Where Lincoln County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Lincoln County fall in the east of Oklahoma. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 74864 (Prague) and 74875 (Tryon), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Payne County (27 miles), Okfuskee County (33 miles), Pottawatomie County (35 miles) and Logan County (35 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lincoln County begin with 748 (8 codes) and 740 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 748 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Garvin County, Hughes County and Johnston County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 921 square miles of land (2,386 sq km). The largest is 74079 (Stroud) with 187 square miles, and the smallest is 74026 (Davenport) with 9.5. The middle-sized code here covers 76.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lincoln County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lincoln County ranks 18th among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 765 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lincoln County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OK and the ZIP code:

Questions about Lincoln County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Agra, OK?

Do all ZIP codes in Lincoln County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Lincoln County covers the most land?
