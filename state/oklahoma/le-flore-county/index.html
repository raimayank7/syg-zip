Where Le Flore County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Le Flore County fall in the south-east of Oklahoma. Its codes spread over about 55 miles from north to south and 32 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 74549 (Honobia) and 74901 (Arkoma), about 62 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Haskell County (29 miles), Sebastian County, Arkansas (30 miles), Latimer County (33 miles) and Sequoyah County (35 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Le Flore County begin with 749 (16 codes) and 745 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 749 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Adair County, Haskell County and McCurtain County, and has 32 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,516 square miles of land (3,925 sq km). The largest is 74966 (Wister) with 264 square miles, and the smallest is 74947 (Monroe) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 63.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Le Flore County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Le Flore County ranks 3rd among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 765 codes in the state list.
