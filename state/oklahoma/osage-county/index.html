Where Osage County is

The ZIP codes of Osage County are found in the north-east of Oklahoma. Its codes spread over about 43 miles from north to south and 43 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 74127 (Tulsa) and 74633 (Burbank), about 56 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Pawnee County (21 miles), Washington County (27 miles), Tulsa County (32 miles) and Creek County (38 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Osage County begin with 740 (8 codes), 746 (3 codes) and 741 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 740 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Creek County, Lincoln County and Nowata County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,866 square miles of land (4,834 sq km). The largest is 74056 (Pawhuska) with 646 square miles, and the smallest is 74001 (Avant) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 123 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Osage County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Osage County ranks 14th among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 765 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Osage County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OK and the ZIP code:

  • Avant, OK 74001
  • Barnsdall, OK 74002
  • Burbank, OK 74633

Tulsa shares a name with a place in another part of Oklahoma; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
