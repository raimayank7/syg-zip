Where Johnston County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Johnston County fall in the south-east of Oklahoma. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 24 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 73447 (Mannsville) and 73461 (Wapanucka), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Marshall County (23 miles), Coal County (24 miles), Murray County (27 miles) and Pontotoc County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Johnston County begin with 734 (6 codes), 748 (2 codes) and 745 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 734 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Bryan County, Carter County and Garvin County, and has 35 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 674 square miles of land (1,745 sq km). The largest is 74856 (Mill Creek) with 188 square miles, and the smallest is 73455 (Ravia) with 7.7. The middle-sized code here covers 65.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Johnston County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Johnston County ranks 23rd among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 765 codes in the state list.
