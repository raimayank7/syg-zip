Where Oklahoma County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Oklahoma County fall in the east of Oklahoma. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 84 miles, and from east to west about 31 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 73025 (Edmond) and 73039 (Davis), about 87 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cleveland County (18 miles), Canadian County (23 miles), McClain County (32 miles) and Logan County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Oklahoma County begin with 731 (71 codes), 730 (16 codes) and 748 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 731 is shared with 1 other county, Cleveland County, and has 76 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 53 of the 88 codes. Together they come to about 873 square miles of land (2,260 sq km). The largest is 73054 (Luther) with 89.8 square miles, and the smallest is 73066 (Nicoma Park) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 7.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Oklahoma County; a code can reach across its border. 35 codes have no mapped area: 73039 (Davis), 73083 (Edmond), 73101 (Oklahoma City), 73113 (Oklahoma City), 73123 (Oklahoma City) and 30 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Oklahoma has more ZIP codes: Oklahoma County holds 88 of the 765 in the state list, about 11.5%.

Writing an address in Oklahoma County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OK and the ZIP code:

  • Oklahoma City, OK 73101 (one of 71 codes for Oklahoma City; the street decides which)
  • Edmond, OK 73003 (one of 6 codes for Edmond; the street decides which)
  • Arcadia, OK 73007

ZIP codes named Oklahoma City are also listed under Cleveland County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site.
