Where Pottawatomie County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Pottawatomie County fall in the east of Oklahoma. Its codes spread over about 30 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 74826 (Asher) and 74851 (Mcloud), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Seminole County (18 miles), Cleveland County (29 miles), McClain County (32 miles) and Lincoln County (35 miles).

The first three digits

All 11 codes here begin with the same three digits, 748. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 748 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Garvin County, Hughes County and Johnston County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 816 square miles of land (2,113 sq km). The largest is 74873 (Tecumseh) with 137 square miles, and the smallest is 74866 (Saint Louis) with 2.9. The middle-sized code here covers 81.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pottawatomie County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 74802 (Shawnee). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Pottawatomie County ranks 16th among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 765 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pottawatomie County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OK and the ZIP code:

  • Shawnee, OK 74801 (one of 3 codes for Shawnee; the street decides which)
  • Asher, OK 74826
  • Earlsboro, OK 74840

Shawnee is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

What is the ZIP code for Shawnee, OK?

Shawnee has 3 ZIP codes in Pottawatomie County: 74801, 74802, 74804. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Which ZIP code in Pottawatomie County covers the most land?
