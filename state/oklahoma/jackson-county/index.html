Where Jackson County is

The ZIP codes of Jackson County are found in the south of Oklahoma. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 29 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 73537 (Eldorado) and 73549 (Headrick), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Greer County (25 miles), Harmon County (28 miles), Hardeman County, Texas (28 miles) and Kiowa County (31 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 735. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 735 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Comanche County, Cotton County and Greer County, and has 55 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 861 square miles of land (2,230 sq km). The largest is 73521 (Altus) with 186 square miles, and the smallest is 73556 (Martha) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 80.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jackson County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 73522 (Altus). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Jackson County ranks 18th among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 765 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jackson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OK and the ZIP code:

  • Altus, OK 73521 (one of 2 codes for Altus; the street decides which)
  • Altus Afb, OK 73523
  • Blair, OK 73526

Questions about Jackson County ZIP codes
