Where Woodward County is

The ZIP codes of Woodward County are found in the north of Oklahoma. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 25 miles, and from east to west about 26 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 73841 (Fort Supply) and 73853 (Mutual), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Ellis County (25 miles), Dewey County (35 miles), Harper County (35 miles) and Woods County (36 miles).

The first three digits

All 6 codes here begin with the same three digits, 738. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 738 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Beaver County, Dewey County and Ellis County, and has 20 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 1,135 square miles of land (2,939 sq km). The largest is 73801 (Woodward) with 459 square miles, and the smallest is 73841 (Fort Supply) with 41.3. The middle-sized code here covers 137 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Woodward County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 73802 (Woodward). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Woodward County ranks 54th among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 765 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Woodward County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OK and the ZIP code:

  • Woodward, OK 73801 (one of 2 codes for Woodward; the street decides which)
  • Fort Supply, OK 73841
  • Mooreland, OK 73852
