Where Haskell County is

The ZIP codes of Haskell County are found in the east of Oklahoma. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 13 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 74440 (Hoyt) and 74941 (Keota), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Latimer County (22 miles), Sequoyah County (29 miles), Le Flore County (29 miles) and McIntosh County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Haskell County begin with 744 (3 codes), 749 (3 codes) and 745 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 744 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Adair County, Cherokee County and McIntosh County, and has 49 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 606 square miles of land (1,571 sq km). The largest is 74462 (Stigler) with 268 square miles, and the smallest is 74943 (Lequire) with 1.0. The middle-sized code here covers 102 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. Water adds about 50.8 square miles, roughly 8% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Haskell County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 74440 (Hoyt). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Haskell County ranks 41st among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 765 codes in the state list.
