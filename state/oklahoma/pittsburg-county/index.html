Where Pittsburg County is

The ZIP codes of Pittsburg County are found in the south-east of Oklahoma. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 34 miles, and from east to west about 32 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 74561 (Quinton) and 74576 (Wardville), about 46 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Latimer County (25 miles), Hughes County (30 miles), McIntosh County (34 miles) and Pushmataha County (38 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Pittsburg County begin with 745 (13 codes) and 744 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 745 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Atoka County, Coal County and Haskell County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 1,257 square miles of land (3,256 sq km). The largest is 74501 (Mcalester) with 551 square miles, and the smallest is 74546 (Haileyville) with 2.3. The middle-sized code here covers 43.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. Water adds about 57.2 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pittsburg County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 74502 (Mcalester) and 74529 (Blocker). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Pittsburg County ranks 5th among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 765 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pittsburg County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OK and the ZIP code:

  • Mcalester, OK 74501 (one of 2 codes for Mcalester; the street decides which)
  • Alderson, OK 74522
  • Blanco, OK 74528

Questions about Pittsburg County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Mcalester, OK?

Mcalester has 2 ZIP codes in Pittsburg County: 74501, 74502. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Pittsburg County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 745, on 13 of the 16 codes.

Which ZIP code in Pittsburg County covers the most land?

74501 (Mcalester), at about 551 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
