Where Logan County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Logan County fall in the north-east of Oklahoma. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 73056 (Marshall) and 73058 (Meridian), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Payne County (26 miles), Noble County (31 miles), Kingfisher County (31 miles) and Oklahoma County (33 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Logan County starts with 730. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 730 is shared with 15 other counties, such as Blaine County, Caddo County and Canadian County, and has 89 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 874 square miles of land (2,265 sq km). The largest is 73044 (Guthrie) with 265 square miles, and the smallest is 73050 (Langston) with 1.5. The middle-sized code here covers 108 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Logan County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Logan County ranks 28th among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 765 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Logan County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OK and the ZIP code:
