Where Dewey County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Dewey County fall in the north of Oklahoma. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 39 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 73646 (Fay) and 73859 (Vici), about 40 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Custer County (25 miles), Blaine County (32 miles), Woodward County (35 miles) and Major County (41 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Dewey County begin with 736 (6 codes) and 738 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 736 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Beckham County, Custer County and Greer County, and has 34 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,234 square miles of land (3,195 sq km). The largest is 73654 (Leedey) with 317 square miles, and the smallest is 73658 (Oakwood) with 88.8. The middle-sized code here covers 142 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Dewey County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Dewey County ranks 28th among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 765 codes in the state list.
