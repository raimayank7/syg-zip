Where Tulsa County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Tulsa County fall in the north-east of Oklahoma. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 31 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 74021 (Collinsville) and 74043 (Leonard), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Creek County (24 miles), Rogers County (26 miles), Wagoner County (26 miles) and Osage County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Tulsa County begin with 741 (50 codes) and 740 (12 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 741 is shared with 2 other counties, Creek County and Osage County, and has 52 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 37 of the 62 codes. Together they come to about 674 square miles of land (1,747 sq km). The largest is 74063 (Sand Springs) with 130 square miles, and the smallest is 74171 (Tulsa) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 8.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 22.3 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Tulsa County; a code can reach across its border. 25 codes have no mapped area: 74013 (Broken Arrow), 74043 (Leonard), 74101 (Tulsa), 74102 (Tulsa), 74121 (Tulsa) and 20 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Tulsa County ranks 2nd among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 62 of the 765 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Tulsa County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OK and the ZIP code:

  • Tulsa, OK 74101 (one of 50 codes for Tulsa; the street decides which)
  • Broken Arrow, OK 74011 (one of 3 codes for Broken Arrow; the street decides which)
  • Bixby, OK 74008

ZIP codes named Tulsa are also listed under Creek County and Osage County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site.
