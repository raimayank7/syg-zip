Where Stephens County is

The ZIP codes of Stephens County are found in the south of Oklahoma. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 73434 (Foster) and 73529 (Comanche), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Jefferson County (26 miles), Carter County (29 miles), Garvin County (32 miles) and Cotton County (38 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Stephens County begin with 734 (4 codes), 735 (4 codes) and 730 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 734 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Bryan County, Carter County and Garvin County, and has 35 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 1,044 square miles of land (2,705 sq km). The largest is 73533 (Duncan) with 330 square miles, and the smallest is 73425 (Countyline) with 4.7. The middle-sized code here covers 106 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Stephens County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 73534 (Duncan) and 73536 (Duncan). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Stephens County ranks 23rd among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 765 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Stephens County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OK and the ZIP code:

  • Duncan, OK 73533 (one of 3 codes for Duncan; the street decides which)
  • Comanche, OK 73529
  • Countyline, OK 73425

Duncan is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Stephens County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Duncan, OK?

Duncan has 3 ZIP codes in Stephens County: 73533, 73534, 73536. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Stephens County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 734, on 4 of the 9 codes.

Which ZIP code in Stephens County covers the most land?

73533 (Duncan), at about 330 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
