Where Mayes County is

The ZIP codes of Mayes County are found in the north-east of Oklahoma. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 25 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 74337 (Chouteau) and 74349 (Ketchum), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Delaware County (19 miles), Craig County (26 miles), Rogers County (26 miles) and Cherokee County (31 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 743. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 743 is shared with 3 other counties, Craig County, Delaware County and Ottawa County, and has 34 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 731 square miles of land (1,892 sq km). The largest is 74361 (Pryor) with 163 square miles, and the smallest is 74340 (Disney) with 1.1. The middle-sized code here covers 82.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. Water adds about 32.5 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Mayes County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 74362 (Pryor). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Mayes County ranks 14th among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 765 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Mayes County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OK and the ZIP code:

  • Pryor, OK 74361 (one of 2 codes for Pryor; the street decides which)
  • Adair, OK 74330
  • Chouteau, OK 74337
