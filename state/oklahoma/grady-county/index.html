Where Grady County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Grady County fall close to the middle of Oklahoma. Its codes spread over about 37 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 73059 (Minco) and 73082 (Rush Springs), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are McClain County (25 miles), Caddo County (28 miles), Cleveland County (29 miles) and Canadian County (32 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Grady County starts with 730. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 730 is shared with 15 other counties, such as Blaine County, Caddo County and Canadian County, and has 89 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 1,002 square miles of land (2,595 sq km). The largest is 73059 (Minco) with 162 square miles, and the smallest is 73002 (Alex) with 35.5. The middle-sized code here covers 94.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Grady County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 73023 (Chickasha). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Grady County ranks 16th among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 765 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Grady County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OK and the ZIP code:

  • Chickasha, OK 73018 (one of 2 codes for Chickasha; the street decides which)
  • Alex, OK 73002
  • Amber, OK 73004

Questions about Grady County ZIP codes

Chickasha has 2 ZIP codes in Grady County: 73018, 73023. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Grady County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 11 start with 730.

Which ZIP code in Grady County covers the most land?
