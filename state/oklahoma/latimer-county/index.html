Where Latimer County is

The ZIP codes of Latimer County are found in the south-east of Oklahoma. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 4.8 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 74545 (Gowen) and 74563 (Red Oak), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Haskell County (22 miles), Pittsburg County (25 miles), Le Flore County (33 miles) and Pushmataha County (33 miles).

The first three digits

All 4 codes here begin with the same three digits, 745. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 745 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Atoka County, Coal County and Haskell County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 2 of the 4 codes. Together they come to about 524 square miles of land (1,357 sq km). The largest is 74578 (Wilburton) with 317 square miles, and the smallest is 74563 (Red Oak) with 207. The middle-sized code here covers 262 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Latimer County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 74545 (Gowen) and 74559 (Panola). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Latimer County ranks 68th among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 765 codes in the state list.
