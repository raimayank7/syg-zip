Where Cleveland County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Cleveland County is in the east of Oklahoma. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 73051 (Lexington) and 73173 (Oklahoma City), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are McClain County (14 miles), Oklahoma County (18 miles), Pottawatomie County (29 miles) and Grady County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Cleveland County begin with 730 (8 codes) and 731 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 730 is shared with 15 other counties, such as Blaine County, Caddo County and Canadian County, and has 89 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 493 square miles of land (1,276 sq km). The largest is 73051 (Lexington) with 127 square miles, and the smallest is 73070 (Norman) with 0.08. The middle-sized code here covers 21.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 18.9 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cleveland County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Cleveland County ranks 11th among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 765 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cleveland County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OK and the ZIP code:

  • Norman, OK 73019 (one of 6 codes for Norman; the street decides which)
  • Oklahoma City, OK 73139 (one of 5 codes for Oklahoma City; the street decides which)
  • Lexington, OK 73051

Norman is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Oklahoma City shares a name with a place in another part of Oklahoma; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Cleveland County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Norman, OK?

Norman has 6 ZIP codes in Cleveland County: 73019, 73026, 73069, 73070, 73071, 73072. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Cleveland County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 730, on 8 of the 13 codes.

Which ZIP code in Cleveland County covers the most land?

73051 (Lexington), at about 127 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
