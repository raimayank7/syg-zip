Where Seminole County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Seminole County is in the east of Oklahoma. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 74837 (Cromwell) and 74849 (Konawa), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Pottawatomie County (18 miles), Hughes County (24 miles), Okfuskee County (26 miles) and Pontotoc County (29 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 748. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 748 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Garvin County, Hughes County and Johnston County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 564 square miles of land (1,460 sq km). The largest is 74868 (Seminole) with 211 square miles, and the smallest is 74837 (Cromwell) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 109 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Seminole County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 74818 (Seminole). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Seminole County ranks 41st among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 765 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Seminole County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OK and the ZIP code:

  • Seminole, OK 74818 (one of 2 codes for Seminole; the street decides which)
  • Bowlegs, OK 74830
  • Cromwell, OK 74837

Seminole is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
