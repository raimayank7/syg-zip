Oklahoma ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 765 ZIP codes for Oklahoma, about 1.9% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 21st among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 77 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Oklahoma County (88), Tulsa County (62), Le Flore County (19), Garfield County (17) and Comanche County (16). The fewest are in Murray County, with 3.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Oklahoma uses 17 of these prefixes, from 730 to 749. The busiest are 730 (89 codes, most of them for Edmond), 731 (76 codes, most of them for Oklahoma City), 740 (75 codes, most of them for Stillwater) and 735 (55 codes, most of them for Lawton). The first digit 7 is shared with Arkansas, Louisiana, Missouri and Texas.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 664 of the 765 codes. The largest is 73933 (Boise City, Cimarron County) at 961 square miles and the smallest is 74477 (Wagoner) at 0.04; the middle value is 77.4 square miles. The other 101 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 74072 (S Coffeyville) and the southernmost 74747 (Kemp); the one farthest east is 74901 (Arkoma) and farthest west 73946 (Kenton).

Place names and addresses in Oklahoma

Of the 582 place names in the list, 550 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Oklahoma City (76), Tulsa (52), Edmond (6) and Norman (6). 5 place names appear under more than one county, among them Tulsa, Broken Arrow, Claremore and Davis: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters OK and the code, as in Oklahoma City, OK 73101. The name of the county is left out.

Questions about Oklahoma ZIP codes

Oklahoma City, with 76 codes in this list.
