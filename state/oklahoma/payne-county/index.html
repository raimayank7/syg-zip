Where Payne County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Payne County fall in the north-east of Oklahoma. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 74059 (Perkins) and 74085 (Yale), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Pawnee County (24 miles), Logan County (26 miles), Lincoln County (27 miles) and Noble County (27 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 740. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 740 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Creek County, Lincoln County and Nowata County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 746 square miles of land (1,932 sq km). The largest is 74023 (Cushing) with 173 square miles, and the smallest is 74078 (Stillwater) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 97.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Oklahoma is 77.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Payne County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 74076 (Stillwater). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Payne County ranks 18th among the 77 counties and county-level areas of Oklahoma (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 765 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Payne County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OK and the ZIP code:

  • Stillwater, OK 74074 (one of 5 codes for Stillwater; the street decides which)
  • Cushing, OK 74023
  • Glencoe, OK 74032

Stillwater is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
