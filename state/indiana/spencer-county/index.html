Where Spencer County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Spencer County is in the south-west of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 47577 (Saint Meinrad) and 47617 (Hatfield), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Warrick County (18 miles), Perry County (19 miles), Hancock County, Kentucky (19 miles) and Dubois County (21 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Spencer County begin with 475 (10 codes) and 476 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 475 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Daviess County, Dubois County and Knox County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 425 square miles of land (1,102 sq km). The largest is 47635 (Rockport) with 86.8 square miles, and the smallest is 47552 (Lincoln City) with 6.4. The middle-sized code here covers 35.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Spencer County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 47536 (Fulda), 47556 (Mariah Hill) and 47617 (Hatfield). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Spencer County ranks 11th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Spencer County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Chrisney, IN 47611
  • Dale, IN 47523
  • Evanston, IN 47531
