Where Bartholomew County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Bartholomew County fall in the south-east of Indiana. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 13 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 47246 (Hope) and 47247 (Jonesville), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Jennings County (20 miles), Decatur County (21 miles), Brown County (22 miles) and Johnson County (23 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Bartholomew County starts with 472. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 472 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Decatur County, Jackson County and Jefferson County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 387 square miles of land (1,003 sq km). The largest is 47201 (Columbus) with 201 square miles, and the smallest is 47226 (Clifford) with 0.09. The middle-sized code here covers 17.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bartholomew County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 47202 (Columbus). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Bartholomew County ranks 29th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bartholomew County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Columbus, IN 47201 (one of 3 codes for Columbus; the street decides which)
  • Clifford, IN 47226
  • Elizabethtown, IN 47232

Columbus is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 16 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
