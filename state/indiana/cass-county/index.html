Where Cass County is

The ZIP codes of Cass County are found in the north of Indiana. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 46932 (Galveston) and 46978 (Royal Center), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Miami County (13 miles), Carroll County (17 miles), Fulton County (21 miles) and Howard County (22 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Cass County starts with 469. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 469 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Carroll County, Fulton County and Grant County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 428 square miles of land (1,110 sq km). The largest is 46947 (Logansport) with 190 square miles, and the smallest is 46967 (Onward) with 0.09. The middle-sized code here covers 29.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cass County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 46942 (Lake Cicott). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Cass County ranks 29th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cass County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:
