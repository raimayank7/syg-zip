Where Jackson County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Jackson County is in the south of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 13 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 47229 (Crothersville) and 47264 (Norman), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Jennings County (23 miles), Scott County (23 miles), Bartholomew County (24 miles) and Lawrence County (25 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 472. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 472 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Bartholomew County, Decatur County and Jefferson County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 593 square miles of land (1,535 sq km). The largest is 47274 (Seymour) with 215 square miles, and the smallest is 47235 (Freetown) with 42.5. The middle-sized code here covers 59.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jackson County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 47228 (Cortland) and 47249 (Kurtz). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Jackson County ranks 36th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jackson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Brownstown, IN 47220
  • Cortland, IN 47228
  • Crothersville, IN 47229

Brownstown is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
