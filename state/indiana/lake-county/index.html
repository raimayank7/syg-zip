Where Lake County is

The ZIP codes of Lake County are found in the north-west of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 34 miles, and from east to west about 13 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 46377 (Shelby) and 46394 (Whiting), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Porter County (16 miles), Cook County, Illinois (31 miles), Will County, Illinois (32 miles) and LaPorte County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lake County begin with 463 (21 codes) and 464 (11 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 463 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Jasper County, LaPorte County and Newton County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 27 of the 32 codes. Together they come to about 465 square miles of land (1,205 sq km). The largest is 46356 (Lowell) with 113 square miles, and the smallest is 46377 (Shelby) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 9.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 17.3 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lake County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 46308 (Crown Point), 46325 (Hammond), 46355 (Leroy), 46401 (Gary) and 46411 (Merrillville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lake County ranks 4th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 32 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lake County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Gary, IN 46401 (one of 8 codes for Gary; the street decides which)
  • Hammond, IN 46320 (one of 5 codes for Hammond; the street decides which)
  • Crown Point, IN 46307 (one of 2 codes for Crown Point; the street decides which)

Gary is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
