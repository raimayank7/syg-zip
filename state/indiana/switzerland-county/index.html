Where Switzerland County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Switzerland County is in the south-east of Indiana. Its codes spread over about 7.2 miles from north to south and 13 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 47038 (Patriot) and 47043 (Vevay), about 14 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Ohio County (9.9 miles), Gallatin County, Kentucky (10 miles), Carroll County, Kentucky (13 miles) and Boone County, Kentucky (18 miles).

The first three digits

All 5 codes here begin with the same three digits, 470. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 470 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Dearborn County, Franklin County and Ohio County, and has 31 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 4 of the 5 codes. Together they come to about 182 square miles of land (472 sq km). The largest is 47043 (Vevay) with 103 square miles, and the smallest is 47020 (Florence) with 18.9. The middle-sized code here covers 30.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Switzerland County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 47019 (East Enterprise). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Switzerland County ranks 79th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Switzerland County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Bennington, IN 47011
  • East Enterprise, IN 47019
  • Florence, IN 47020

Bennington is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
