Where Sullivan County is

The ZIP codes of Sullivan County are found in the south-west of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 16 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 47838 (Carlisle) and 47850 (Farmersburg), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Crawford County, Illinois (18 miles), Greene County (23 miles), Vigo County (24 miles) and Knox County (25 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Sullivan County starts with 478. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 478 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Clay County, Parke County and Vermillion County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 444 square miles of land (1,150 sq km). The largest is 47882 (Sullivan) with 142 square miles, and the smallest is 47865 (Paxton) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 44.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sullivan County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 47852 (Graysville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Sullivan County ranks 29th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Sullivan County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Carlisle, IN 47838
  • Dugger, IN 47848
  • Fairbanks, IN 47849

Carlisle is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
