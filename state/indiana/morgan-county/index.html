Where Morgan County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Morgan County fall close to the middle of Indiana. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 46158 (Mooresville) and 46166 (Paragon), about 17 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Hendricks County (17 miles), Johnson County (21 miles), Brown County (22 miles) and Putnam County (22 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Morgan County starts with 461. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 461 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Boone County, Brown County and Fayette County, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 350 square miles of land (907 sq km). The largest is 46151 (Martinsville) with 204 square miles, and the smallest is 46125 (Eminence) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 34.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Morgan County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Morgan County ranks 64th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Morgan County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Brooklyn, IN 46111
  • Eminence, IN 46125
  • Martinsville, IN 46151

Brooklyn is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
