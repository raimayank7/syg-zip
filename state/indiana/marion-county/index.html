Where Marion County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Marion County fall close to the middle of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 19 miles, and from east to west about 18 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 46113 (Camby) and 46213 (Indianapolis), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hamilton County (16 miles), Hancock County (19 miles), Hendricks County (20 miles) and Johnson County (21 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Marion County begin with 462 (59 codes) and 461 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 462 is shared with 1 other county, Hamilton County, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 39 of the 62 codes. Together they come to about 428 square miles of land (1,109 sq km). The largest is 46239 (Indianapolis) with 28.3 square miles, and the smallest is 46206 (Indianapolis) with 0.02. The middle-sized code here covers 11.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Marion County; a code can reach across its border. 23 codes have no mapped area: 46207 (Indianapolis), 46209 (Indianapolis), 46211 (Indianapolis), 46213 (Indianapolis), 46230 (Indianapolis) and 18 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Indiana has more ZIP codes: Marion County holds 62 of the 968 in the state list, about 6.4%.

Writing an address in Marion County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Indianapolis, IN 46201 (one of 59 codes for Indianapolis; the street decides which)
  • Beech Grove, IN 46107
  • Camby, IN 46113

ZIP codes named Indianapolis are also listed under Hamilton County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site.
