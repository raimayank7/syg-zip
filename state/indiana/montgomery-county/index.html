Where Montgomery County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Montgomery County is in the west of Indiana. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 47954 (Ladoga) and 47994 (Wingate), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Fountain County (16 miles), Boone County (23 miles), Tippecanoe County (24 miles) and Parke County (26 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Montgomery County starts with 479. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 479 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Benton County, Carroll County and Fountain County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 535 square miles of land (1,385 sq km). The largest is 47933 (Crawfordsville) with 236 square miles, and the smallest is 47916 (Alamo) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 33.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Montgomery County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Montgomery County ranks 23rd among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Montgomery County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Alamo, IN 47916
  • Crawfordsville, IN 47933
  • Darlington, IN 47940

Alamo is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
