Where LaPorte County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of LaPorte County fall in the north-west of Indiana. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 46348 (La Crosse) and 46371 (Rolling Prairie), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Porter County (17 miles), Starke County (19 miles), St. Joseph County (27 miles) and Marshall County (29 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of LaPorte County starts with 463. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 463 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Jasper County, Lake County and Newton County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 553 square miles of land (1,432 sq km). The largest is 46350 (La Porte) with 193 square miles, and the smallest is 46345 (Kingsbury) with 0.10. The middle-sized code here covers 39.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of LaPorte County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 46352 (La Porte) and 46361 (Michigan City). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, LaPorte County ranks 17th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in LaPorte County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • La Porte, IN 46350 (one of 2 codes for La Porte; the street decides which)
  • Michigan City, IN 46360 (one of 2 codes for Michigan City; the street decides which)
  • Hanna, IN 46340
