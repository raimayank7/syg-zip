Where Posey County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Posey County fall in the south-west of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 18 miles, and from east to west about 12 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 47612 (Cynthiana) and 47620 (Mount Vernon), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Vanderburgh County (16 miles), White County, Illinois (20 miles), Wabash County, Illinois (20 miles) and Gibson County (22 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Posey County starts with 476. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 476 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Gibson County, Spencer County and Vanderburgh County, and has 30 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 437 square miles of land (1,133 sq km). The largest is 47620 (Mount Vernon) with 209 square miles, and the smallest is 47612 (Cynthiana) with 16.2. The middle-sized code here covers 53.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Posey County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Posey County ranks 64th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Posey County
