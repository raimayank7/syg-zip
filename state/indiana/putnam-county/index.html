Where Putnam County is

The ZIP codes of Putnam County are found in the west of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 22 miles, and from east to west about 12 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 46120 (Cloverdale) and 46175 (Russellville), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hendricks County (18 miles), Clay County (22 miles), Morgan County (22 miles) and Parke County (22 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 461. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 461 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Boone County, Brown County and Fayette County, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 464 square miles of land (1,201 sq km). The largest is 46135 (Greencastle) with 167 square miles, and the smallest is 46170 (Putnamville) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 39.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Putnam County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Putnam County ranks 46th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Putnam County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Bainbridge, IN 46105
  • Cloverdale, IN 46120
  • Fillmore, IN 46128

Bainbridge is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
