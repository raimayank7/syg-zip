Where Miami County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Miami County is in the north of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 14 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 46919 (Converse) and 46951 (Macy), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cass County (13 miles), Wabash County (17 miles), Howard County (20 miles) and Fulton County (24 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 469. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 469 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Carroll County, Cass County and Fulton County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 394 square miles of land (1,022 sq km). The largest is 46970 (Peru) with 180 square miles, and the smallest is 46971 (Grissom Arb) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 35.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Miami County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 46921 (Deedsville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Miami County ranks 29th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Miami County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Amboy, IN 46911
  • Bunker Hill, IN 46914
  • Converse, IN 46919

Amboy is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
