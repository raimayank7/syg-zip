Where Ripley County is

The ZIP codes of Ripley County are found in the south-east of Indiana. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 47006 (Batesville) and 47017 (Cross Plains), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Dearborn County (14 miles), Decatur County (19 miles), Ohio County (22 miles) and Franklin County (24 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Ripley County starts with 470. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 470 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Dearborn County, Franklin County and Ohio County, and has 31 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 466 square miles of land (1,208 sq km). The largest is 47006 (Batesville) with 106 square miles, and the smallest is 47033 (Morris) with under 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 56.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ripley County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 47039 (Pierceville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Ripley County ranks 23rd among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Ripley County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Batesville, IN 47006
  • Cross Plains, IN 47017
  • Friendship, IN 47021

Batesville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
