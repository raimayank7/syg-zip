Where Tippecanoe County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Tippecanoe County is in the north-west of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 19 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 47930 (Clarks Hill) and 47962 (Montmorenci), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Clinton County (20 miles), Carroll County (22 miles), Montgomery County (24 miles) and Fountain County (26 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Tippecanoe County starts with 479. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 479 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Benton County, Carroll County and Fountain County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 505 square miles of land (1,307 sq km). The largest is 47906 (West Lafayette) with 134 square miles, and the smallest is 47983 (Stockwell) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 25.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Tippecanoe County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 47902 (Lafayette), 47903 (Lafayette), 47962 (Montmorenci) and 47996 (West Lafayette). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Tippecanoe County ranks 9th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Tippecanoe County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Lafayette, IN 47901 (one of 6 codes for Lafayette; the street decides which)
  • West Lafayette, IN 47906 (one of 3 codes for West Lafayette; the street decides which)
  • Battle Ground, IN 47920

Lafayette is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
