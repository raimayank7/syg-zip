Where Clinton County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Clinton County fall in the north of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 18 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 46035 (Colfax) and 46039 (Forest), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Carroll County (18 miles), Tippecanoe County (20 miles), Boone County (21 miles) and Howard County (22 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 460. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 460 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Boone County, Hamilton County and Hancock County, and has 51 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 437 square miles of land (1,133 sq km). The largest is 46041 (Frankfort) with 248 square miles, and the smallest is 46035 (Colfax) with 24.9. The middle-sized code here covers 27.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clinton County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 46067 (Sedalia). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Clinton County ranks 46th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clinton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Colfax, IN 46035
  • Forest, IN 46039
  • Frankfort, IN 46041

Colfax is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
