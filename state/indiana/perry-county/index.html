Where Perry County is

The ZIP codes of Perry County are found in the south of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 18 miles, and from east to west about 12 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 47515 (Bristow) and 47574 (Rome), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Spencer County (19 miles), Crawford County (19 miles), Hancock County, Kentucky (20 miles) and Breckinridge County, Kentucky (23 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 475. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 475 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Daviess County, Dubois County and Knox County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 335 square miles of land (868 sq km). The largest is 47586 (Tell City) with 97.0 square miles, and the smallest is 47514 (Branchville) with 7.0. The middle-sized code here covers 28.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Perry County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Perry County ranks 46th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Perry County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Branchville, IN 47514
  • Bristow, IN 47515
  • Cannelton, IN 47520

Branchville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
