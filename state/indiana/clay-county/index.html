Where Clay County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Clay County is in the west of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 14 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 47837 (Carbon) and 47845 (Coalmont), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Vigo County (15 miles), Owen County (17 miles), Putnam County (22 miles) and Parke County (24 miles).

The first three digits

All 11 codes here begin with the same three digits, 478. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 478 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Parke County, Sullivan County and Vermillion County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 434 square miles of land (1,123 sq km). The largest is 47834 (Brazil) with 154 square miles, and the smallest is 47853 (Harmony) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 33.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clay County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Clay County ranks 23rd among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clay County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Bowling Green, IN 47833
  • Brazil, IN 47834
  • Carbon, IN 47837

Bowling Green is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
