Where Porter County is

The ZIP codes of Porter County are found in the north-west of Indiana. Its codes spread over about 26 miles from north to south and 12 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 46301 (Beverly Shores) and 46341 (Hebron), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lake County (16 miles), LaPorte County (17 miles), Starke County (27 miles) and Jasper County (29 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 463. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 463 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Jasper County, LaPorte County and Lake County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 427 square miles of land (1,105 sq km). The largest is 46341 (Hebron) with 95.5 square miles, and the smallest is 46393 (Wheeler) with 1.7. The middle-sized code here covers 57.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Porter County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 46384 (Valparaiso). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Porter County ranks 29th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Porter County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Valparaiso, IN 46383 (one of 3 codes for Valparaiso; the street decides which)
  • Beverly Shores, IN 46301
  • Boone Grove, IN 46302
