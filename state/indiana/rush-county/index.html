Where Rush County is

The ZIP codes of Rush County are found in the east of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 15 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 46127 (Falmouth) and 46150 (Manilla), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Shelby County (15 miles), Fayette County (17 miles), Henry County (19 miles) and Hancock County (20 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 461. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 461 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Boone County, Brown County and Fayette County, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 379 square miles of land (980 sq km). The largest is 46173 (Rushville) with 226 square miles, and the smallest is 46155 (Mays) with 0.02. The middle-sized code here covers 26.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Rush County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Rush County ranks 46th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Rush County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Arlington, IN 46104
  • Carthage, IN 46115
  • Falmouth, IN 46127

Arlington is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 20 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
