Where Benton County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Benton County fall in the north-west of Indiana. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 47942 (Earl Park) and 47970 (Otterbein), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Warren County (17 miles), Tippecanoe County (26 miles), Iroquois County, Illinois (29 miles) and Fountain County (30 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Benton County starts with 479. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 479 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Carroll County, Fountain County and Jasper County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 442 square miles of land (1,146 sq km). The largest is 47944 (Fowler) with 189 square miles, and the smallest is 47986 (Templeton) with 0.10. The middle-sized code here covers 55.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Benton County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 47984 (Talbot). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Benton County ranks 46th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Benton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Boswell, IN 47921
  • Earl Park, IN 47942
