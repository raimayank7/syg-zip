Where Jay County is

The ZIP codes of Jay County are found in the north-east of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 47326 (Bryant) and 47373 (Redkey), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Blackford County (15 miles), Randolph County (19 miles), Adams County (21 miles) and Wells County (22 miles).

The first three digits

All 6 codes here begin with the same three digits, 473. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 473 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Blackford County, Delaware County and Fayette County, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 400 square miles of land (1,035 sq km). The largest is 47371 (Portland) with 220 square miles, and the smallest is 47381 (Salamonia) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 41.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jay County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Jay County ranks 64th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jay County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Bryant, IN 47326
  • Dunkirk, IN 47336
  • Pennville, IN 47369

Bryant is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
