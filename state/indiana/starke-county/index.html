Where Starke County is

The ZIP codes of Starke County are found in the north of Indiana. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 46374 (San Pierre) and 46531 (Grovertown), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Pulaski County (16 miles), LaPorte County (19 miles), Marshall County (20 miles) and Fulton County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Starke County begin with 465 (3 codes), 463 (2 codes) and 469 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 465 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Elkhart County, Kosciusko County and LaGrange County, and has 52 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 309 square miles of land (800 sq km). The largest is 46534 (Knox) with 120 square miles, and the smallest is 46968 (Ora) with 1.1. The middle-sized code here covers 43.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Starke County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Starke County ranks 64th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Starke County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:
