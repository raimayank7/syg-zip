Where Jasper County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Jasper County is in the north-west of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 30 miles, and from east to west about 15 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 46380 (Tefft) and 47977 (Remington), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Newton County (13 miles), Pulaski County (23 miles), White County (27 miles) and Porter County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Jasper County begin with 463 (3 codes) and 479 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 463 is shared with 5 other counties, such as LaPorte County, Lake County and Newton County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 589 square miles of land (1,524 sq km). The largest is 47978 (Rensselaer) with 264 square miles, and the smallest is 46310 (Demotte) with 63.4. The middle-sized code here covers 86.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jasper County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 46380 (Tefft). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Jasper County ranks 64th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 968 codes in the state list.
