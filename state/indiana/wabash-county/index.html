Where Wabash County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Wabash County fall in the north-east of Indiana. Its codes spread over about 25 miles from north to south and 12 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 46946 (Liberty Mills) and 46984 (Somerset), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Huntington County (16 miles), Miami County (17 miles), Whitley County (24 miles) and Kosciusko County (26 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Wabash County starts with 469. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 469 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Carroll County, Cass County and Fulton County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 430 square miles of land (1,113 sq km). The largest is 46992 (Wabash) with 163 square miles, and the smallest is 46980 (Servia) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 29.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wabash County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Wabash County ranks 29th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 968 codes in the state list.
