Where White County is

The ZIP codes of White County are found in the north-west of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 19 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 47926 (Burnettsville) and 47995 (Wolcott), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Carroll County (19 miles), Pulaski County (21 miles), Tippecanoe County (26 miles) and Cass County (26 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of White County starts with 479. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 479 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Benton County, Carroll County and Fountain County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 531 square miles of land (1,375 sq km). The largest is 47960 (Monticello) with 118 square miles, and the smallest is 47925 (Buffalo) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 54.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of White County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, White County ranks 36th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in White County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:
