Indiana ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 968 ZIP codes for Indiana, about 2.4% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 14th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 92 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Marion County (62), Allen County (54), Vanderburgh County (33), Lake County (32) and St. Joseph County (27). At the other end, 3 have only one or two codes, for example Blackford County, Ohio County and Union County.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Indiana uses 20 of these prefixes, from 460 to 479. The busiest are 469 (71 codes, most of them for Kokomo), 479 (71 codes, most of them for Lafayette), 467 (66 codes, most of them for Albion) and 461 (63 codes, most of them for Greenwood). The first digit 4 is shared with Kentucky, Michigan and Ohio.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 807 of the 968 codes. The largest is 47240 (Greensburg, Decatur County) at 304 square miles and the smallest is 47870 (Prairieton) at under 0.01; the middle value is 32.6 square miles. The other 161 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 46530 (Granger) and the southernmost 47635 (Rockport); the one farthest east is 47003 (West College Corner) and farthest west 47631 (New Harmony).

Place names and addresses in Indiana

Of the 733 place names in the list, 700 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Indianapolis (63), Fort Wayne (43), Evansville (32) and South Bend (16). 1 place name appears under more than one county, among them Indianapolis: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters IN and the code, as in Indianapolis, IN 46201. The name of the county is left out.
