Where Steuben County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Steuben County fall in the north-east of Indiana. Its codes spread over about 14 miles from north to south and 13 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 46742 (Hamilton) and 46776 (Orland), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are DeKalb County (16 miles), LaGrange County (19 miles), Branch County, Michigan (21 miles) and Williams County, Ohio (24 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Steuben County starts with 467. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 467 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Adams County, Allen County and DeKalb County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 331 square miles of land (858 sq km). The largest is 46703 (Angola) with 121 square miles, and the smallest is 46776 (Orland) with 25.0. The middle-sized code here covers 44.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. Water adds about 14.2 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Steuben County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Steuben County ranks 64th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Steuben County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:
