Where Noble County is

The ZIP codes of Noble County are found in the north-east of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 13 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 46763 (Laotto) and 46767 (Ligonier), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are LaGrange County (16 miles), Whitley County (17 miles), DeKalb County (21 miles) and Kosciusko County (25 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Noble County starts with 467. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 467 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Adams County, Allen County and DeKalb County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 395 square miles of land (1,022 sq km). The largest is 46701 (Albion) with 96.8 square miles, and the smallest is 46796 (Wolflake) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 25.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Noble County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Noble County ranks 29th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Noble County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Albion, IN 46701
  • Avilla, IN 46710
  • Cromwell, IN 46732

Albion is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 12 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
