Where Knox County is

The ZIP codes of Knox County are found in the south-west of Indiana. Its codes spread over about 26 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 47524 (Decker) and 47578 (Sandborn), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Daviess County (15 miles), Lawrence County, Illinois (22 miles), Sullivan County (25 miles) and Martin County (26 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 475. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 475 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Daviess County, Dubois County and Martin County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 535 square miles of land (1,385 sq km). The largest is 47591 (Vincennes) with 167 square miles, and the smallest is 47573 (Ragsdale) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 44.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Knox County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Knox County ranks 20th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 968 codes in the state list.
