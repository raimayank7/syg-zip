Where Lawrence County is

The ZIP codes of Lawrence County are found in the south of Indiana. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 47436 (Heltonville) and 47437 (Huron), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Orange County (18 miles), Martin County (20 miles), Monroe County (23 miles) and Jackson County (25 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Lawrence County starts with 474. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 474 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Brown County, Greene County and Monroe County, and has 48 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 487 square miles of land (1,261 sq km). The largest is 47421 (Bedford) with 184 square miles, and the smallest is 47467 (Tunnelton) with 0.02. The middle-sized code here covers 43.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lawrence County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lawrence County ranks 36th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lawrence County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Avoca, IN 47420
  • Bedford, IN 47421
  • Heltonville, IN 47436

Avoca is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
