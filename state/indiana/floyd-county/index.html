Where Floyd County is

The ZIP codes of Floyd County are found in the south-east of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 4.7 miles, and from east to west about 10 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 47124 (Greenville) and 47151 (New Albany), about 11 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Harrison County (12 miles), Clark County (14 miles), Jefferson County, Kentucky (14 miles) and Washington County (21 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Floyd County starts with 471. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 471 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Clark County, Crawford County and Harrison County, and has 55 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 4 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 146 square miles of land (379 sq km). The largest is 47150 (New Albany) with 45.0 square miles, and the smallest is 47124 (Greenville) with 24.1. The middle-sized code here covers 38.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Floyd County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 47146 (Mount Saint Francis) and 47151 (New Albany). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Floyd County ranks 64th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Floyd County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • New Albany, IN 47150 (one of 2 codes for New Albany; the street decides which)
  • Floyds Knobs, IN 47119
  • Georgetown, IN 47122

New Albany is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Floyd County ZIP codes

New Albany has 2 ZIP codes in Floyd County: 47150, 47151. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Yes. All 6 start with 471.

Which ZIP code in Floyd County covers the most land?
