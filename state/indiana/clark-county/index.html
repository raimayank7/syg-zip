Where Clark County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Clark County is in the south-east of Indiana. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 27 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 47104 (Bethlehem) and 47106 (Borden), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Oldham County, Kentucky (13 miles), Floyd County (14 miles), Jefferson County, Kentucky (15 miles) and Scott County (18 miles).

The first three digits

All 20 codes here begin with the same three digits, 471. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 471 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Crawford County, Floyd County and Harrison County, and has 55 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 20 codes. Together they come to about 406 square miles of land (1,050 sq km). The largest is 47111 (Charlestown) with 86.0 square miles, and the smallest is 47104 (Bethlehem) with 4.4. The middle-sized code here covers 24.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clark County; a code can reach across its border. 7 codes have no mapped area: 47131 (Jeffersonville), 47132 (Jeffersonville), 47133 (Jeffersonville), 47134 (Jeffersonville), 47144 (Jeffersonville) and 2 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Clark County ranks 6th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 20 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clark County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Jeffersonville, IN 47130 (one of 8 codes for Jeffersonville; the street decides which)
  • Bethlehem, IN 47104
  • Borden, IN 47106

Jeffersonville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
