Where Henry County is

The ZIP codes of Henry County are found in the east of Indiana. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 47356 (Middletown) and 47387 (Straughn), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Rush County (19 miles), Hancock County (20 miles), Delaware County (20 miles) and Wayne County (20 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Henry County begin with 473 (14 codes) and 461 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 473 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Blackford County, Delaware County and Fayette County, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 400 square miles of land (1,036 sq km). The largest is 47362 (New Castle) with 148 square miles, and the smallest is 47361 (Mount Summit) with 0.05. The middle-sized code here covers 16.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Henry County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Henry County ranks 11th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 968 codes in the state list.
