Where Warrick County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Warrick County is in the south-west of Indiana. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 47630 (Newburgh) and 47637 (Tennyson), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Vanderburgh County (16 miles), Spencer County (18 miles), Gibson County (18 miles) and Pike County (20 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Warrick County starts with 476. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 476 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Gibson County, Posey County and Spencer County, and has 30 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 363 square miles of land (939 sq km). The largest is 47601 (Boonville) with 127 square miles, and the smallest is 47610 (Chandler) with 35.9. The middle-sized code here covers 49.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Warrick County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 47629 (Newburgh). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Warrick County ranks 61st among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Warrick County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Newburgh, IN 47629 (one of 2 codes for Newburgh; the street decides which)
  • Boonville, IN 47601
  • Chandler, IN 47610
