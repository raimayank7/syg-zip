Where Shelby County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Shelby County fall in the east of Indiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 14 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 46144 (Gwynneville) and 47234 (Flat Rock), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Rush County (15 miles), Johnson County (18 miles), Hancock County (19 miles) and Decatur County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Shelby County begin with 461 (7 codes) and 472 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 461 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Boone County, Brown County and Fayette County, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 352 square miles of land (912 sq km). The largest is 46176 (Shelbyville) with 169 square miles, and the smallest is 46144 (Gwynneville) with 1.3. The middle-sized code here covers 33.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Indiana is 32.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Shelby County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Shelby County ranks 46th among the 92 counties and county-level areas of Indiana (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 968 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Shelby County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IN and the ZIP code:

  • Fairland, IN 46126
