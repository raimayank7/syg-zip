Where Sheridan County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Sheridan County is in the north-east of Montana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 37 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 59257 (Redstone) and 59275 (Westby), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Roosevelt County (36 miles), Daniels County (40 miles), Divide County, North Dakota (53 miles) and Williams County, North Dakota (55 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 592. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 592 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Daniels County, Dawson County and McCone County, and has 41 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,764 square miles of land (4,569 sq km). The largest is 59275 (Westby) with 376 square miles, and the smallest is 59256 (Raymond) with 78.2. The middle-sized code here covers 183 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sheridan County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Sheridan County ranks 15th among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 404 codes in the state list.
