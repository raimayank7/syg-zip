Where Park County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Park County fall in the south of Montana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 65 miles, and from east to west about 43 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 59020 (Cooke City) and 59086 (Wilsall), about 72 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Gallatin County (38 miles), Sweet Grass County (39 miles), Stillwater County (55 miles) and Carbon County (66 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Park County starts with 590. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 590 is shared with 15 other counties, such as Big Horn County, Carbon County and Fergus County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,721 square miles of land (7,049 sq km). The largest is 59047 (Livingston) with 781 square miles, and the smallest is 59081 (Silver Gate) with 4.4. The middle-sized code here covers 229 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Park County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Park County ranks 15th among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 404 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Park County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MT and the ZIP code:
