Where Cascade County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Cascade County is in the north of Montana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 47 miles, and from east to west about 54 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 59465 (Neihart) and 59477 (Simms), about 66 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Chouteau County (43 miles), Teton County (44 miles), Judith Basin County (55 miles) and Lewis and Clark County (59 miles).

The first three digits

All 18 codes here begin with the same three digits, 594. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 594 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Chouteau County, Fergus County and Glacier County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 15 of the 18 codes. Together they come to about 2,726 square miles of land (7,060 sq km). The largest is 59421 (Cascade) with 747 square miles, and the smallest is 59414 (Black Eagle) with 1.7. The middle-sized code here covers 78.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cascade County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 59402 (Malmstrom Afb), 59403 (Great Falls) and 59406 (Great Falls). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Cascade County ranks 2nd among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 404 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cascade County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MT and the ZIP code:
