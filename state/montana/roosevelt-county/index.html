Where Roosevelt County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Roosevelt County is in the north-east of Montana. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 66 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 59201 (Wolf Point) and 59212 (Bainville), about 66 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Sheridan County (36 miles), Richland County (42 miles), Daniels County (49 miles) and Williams County, North Dakota (61 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 592. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 592 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Daniels County, Dawson County and McCone County, and has 41 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 3,453 square miles of land (8,943 sq km). The largest is 59201 (Wolf Point) with 1,313 square miles, and the smallest is 59242 (Homestead) with 85.3. The middle-sized code here covers 373 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Roosevelt County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Roosevelt County ranks 22nd among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 404 codes in the state list.
