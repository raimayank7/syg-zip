Where Lake County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Lake County is in the north-west of Montana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 52 miles, and from east to west about 14 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 59821 (Arlee) and 59929 (Proctor), about 54 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Sanders County (43 miles), Flathead County (47 miles), Missoula County (48 miles) and Mineral County (50 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lake County begin with 598 (7 codes) and 599 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 598 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Granite County, Mineral County and Missoula County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,189 square miles of land (3,080 sq km). The largest is 59821 (Arlee) with 320 square miles, and the smallest is 59855 (Pablo) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 49.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 119 square miles, roughly 9% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lake County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lake County ranks 7th among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 404 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lake County
