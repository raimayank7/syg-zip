Where Meagher County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Meagher County fall close to the middle of Montana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 25 miles, and from east to west about 29 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 59053 (Martinsdale) and 59645 (White Sulphur Springs), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Broadwater County (41 miles), Wheatland County (46 miles), Judith Basin County (54 miles) and Sweet Grass County (61 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Meagher County begin with 596 (2 codes) and 590 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 596 is shared with 3 other counties, Broadwater County, Jefferson County and Lewis and Clark County, and has 24 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,416 square miles of land (6,257 sq km). The largest is 59645 (White Sulphur Springs) with 1,631 square miles, and the smallest is 59642 (Ringling) with 278. The middle-sized code here covers 507 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Meagher County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Meagher County ranks 46th among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 3 of the 404 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Meagher County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MT and the ZIP code:
