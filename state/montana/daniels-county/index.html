Where Daniels County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Daniels County is in the north-east of Montana. Its codes spread over about 10 miles from north to south and 29 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 59253 (Peerless) and 59276 (Whitetail), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Sheridan County (40 miles), Roosevelt County (49 miles), Valley County (55 miles) and McCone County (91 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Daniels County starts with 592. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 592 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Dawson County, McCone County and Phillips County, and has 41 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,293 square miles of land (3,349 sq km). The largest is 59263 (Scobey) with 588 square miles, and the smallest is 59276 (Whitetail) with 133. The middle-sized code here covers 286 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Daniels County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Daniels County ranks 38th among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 404 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Daniels County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MT and the ZIP code:
