Where Prairie County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Prairie County fall in the east of Montana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 11 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 59341 (Mildred) and 59349 (Terry), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Custer County (38 miles), Dawson County (40 miles), Fallon County (47 miles) and Wibaux County (47 miles).

The first three digits

All 3 codes here begin with the same three digits, 593. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 593 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Carter County, Custer County and Dawson County, and has 31 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 2 of the 3 codes. Together they come to about 1,669 square miles of land (4,322 sq km). The largest is 59349 (Terry) with 1,305 square miles, and the smallest is 59326 (Fallon) with 363. The middle-sized code here covers 834 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Prairie County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 59341 (Mildred). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Prairie County ranks 46th among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 3 of the 404 codes in the state list.
