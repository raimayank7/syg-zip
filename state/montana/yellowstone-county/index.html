Where Yellowstone County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Yellowstone County is in the south-east of Montana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 31 miles, and from east to west about 58 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 59024 (Custer) and 59044 (Laurel), about 65 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Musselshell County (45 miles), Golden Valley County (46 miles), Stillwater County (47 miles) and Carbon County (49 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Yellowstone County begin with 591 (14 codes) and 590 (9 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 591, so those three digits alone point to Yellowstone County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 23 codes. Together they come to about 2,902 square miles of land (7,516 sq km). The largest is 59101 (Billings) with 591 square miles, and the smallest is 59102 (Billings) with 15.0. The middle-sized code here covers 175 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Yellowstone County; a code can reach across its border. 10 codes have no mapped area: 59103 (Billings), 59104 (Billings), 59107 (Billings), 59108 (Billings), 59111 (Billings) and 5 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Montana has more ZIP codes: Yellowstone County holds 23 of the 404 in the state list, about 5.7%.

Writing an address in Yellowstone County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MT and the ZIP code:

  • Billings, MT 59101 (one of 14 codes for Billings; the street decides which)
  • Acton, MT 59002
  • Ballantine, MT 59006

Billings is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Which ZIP code in Yellowstone County covers the most land?
