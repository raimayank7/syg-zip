Where Carter County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Carter County fall in the south-east of Montana. Its codes spread over about 50 miles from north to south and 47 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 59311 (Alzada) and 59324 (Ekalaka), about 51 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Powder River County (49 miles), Harding County, South Dakota (52 miles), Fallon County (59 miles) and Crook County, Wyoming (61 miles).

The first three digits

All 5 codes here begin with the same three digits, 593. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 593 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Custer County, Dawson County and Fallon County, and has 31 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 3,015 square miles of land (7,808 sq km). The largest is 59324 (Ekalaka) with 1,370 square miles, and the smallest is 59316 (Boyes) with 130. The middle-sized code here covers 459 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Carter County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Carter County ranks 33rd among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 404 codes in the state list.
