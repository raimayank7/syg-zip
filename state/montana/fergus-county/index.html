Where Fergus County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Fergus County fall close to the middle of Montana. Its codes spread over about 57 miles from north to south and 59 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 59032 (Grass Range) and 59424 (Coffee Creek), about 63 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Judith Basin County (38 miles), Petroleum County (48 miles), Wheatland County (51 miles) and Golden Valley County (62 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Fergus County begin with 594 (9 codes) and 590 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 594 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Cascade County, Chouteau County and Glacier County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 4,572 square miles of land (11,842 sq km). The largest is 59471 (Roy) with 1,026 square miles, and the smallest is 59424 (Coffee Creek) with 81.6. The middle-sized code here covers 379 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Fergus County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Fergus County ranks 11th among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 404 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Fergus County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MT and the ZIP code:

  • Buffalo, MT 59418
  • Coffee Creek, MT 59424
  • Denton, MT 59430

Buffalo is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 16 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
