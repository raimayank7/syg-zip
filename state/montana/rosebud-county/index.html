Where Rosebud County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Rosebud County fall in the south-east of Montana. Its codes spread over about 100 miles from north to south and 65 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 59012 (Birney) and 59083 (Sumatra), about 105 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Treasure County (31 miles), Custer County (51 miles), Big Horn County (65 miles) and Powder River County (74 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Rosebud County begin with 590 (6 codes) and 593 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 590 is shared with 15 other counties, such as Big Horn County, Carbon County and Fergus County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 5,152 square miles of land (13,345 sq km). The largest is 59327 (Forsyth) with 2,004 square miles, and the smallest is 59333 (Hathaway) with 8.0. The middle-sized code here covers 304 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Rosebud County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 59004 (Ashland). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Rosebud County ranks 8th among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 404 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Rosebud County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MT and the ZIP code:

  • Ashland, MT 59003 (one of 2 codes for Ashland; the street decides which)
  • Angela, MT 59312
  • Birney, MT 59012

Ashland is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 17 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Rosebud County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Ashland, MT?

Ashland has 2 ZIP codes in Rosebud County: 59003, 59004. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Rosebud County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 590, on 6 of the 11 codes.

Which ZIP code in Rosebud County covers the most land?

59327 (Forsyth), at about 2,004 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
