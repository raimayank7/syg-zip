Where Lewis and Clark County is

The ZIP codes of Lewis and Clark County are found in the west of Montana. Its codes spread over about 60 miles from north to south and 36 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 59410 (Augusta) and 59635 (East Helena), about 63 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Powell County (32 miles), Jefferson County (38 miles), Broadwater County (40 miles) and Deer Lodge County (54 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lewis and Clark County begin with 596 (14 codes) and 594 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 596 is shared with 3 other counties, Broadwater County, Jefferson County and Meagher County, and has 24 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 3,502 square miles of land (9,069 sq km). The largest is 59410 (Augusta) with 1,159 square miles, and the smallest is 59623 (Helena) with under 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 130 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lewis and Clark County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 59604 (Helena), 59620 (Helena), 59624 (Helena) and 59626 (Helena). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lewis and Clark County ranks 5th among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 404 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lewis and Clark County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MT and the ZIP code:

  • Helena, MT 59601 (one of 8 codes for Helena; the street decides which)
  • Augusta, MT 59410
  • Canyon Creek, MT 59633

Helena is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Lewis and Clark County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Helena, MT?

Helena has 8 ZIP codes in Lewis and Clark County: 59601, 59602, 59604, 59620, 59623, 59624, 59625, 59626. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Lewis and Clark County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 596, on 14 of the 15 codes.

Which ZIP code in Lewis and Clark County covers the most land?

59410 (Augusta), at about 1,159 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
