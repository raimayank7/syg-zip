Where Silver Bow County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Silver Bow County fall in the south-west of Montana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 26 miles, and from east to west about 13 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 59707 (Butte) and 59743 (Melrose), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Deer Lodge County (20 miles), Jefferson County (36 miles), Beaverhead County (46 miles) and Madison County (47 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Silver Bow County starts with 597. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 597 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Beaverhead County, Deer Lodge County and Gallatin County, and has 49 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 1,141 square miles of land (2,956 sq km). The largest is 59701 (Butte) with 510 square miles, and the smallest is 59748 (Ramsay) with 21.3. The middle-sized code here covers 210 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Silver Bow County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 59702 (Butte), 59703 (Butte) and 59707 (Butte). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Silver Bow County ranks 18th among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 404 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Silver Bow County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MT and the ZIP code:

  • Butte, MT 59701 (one of 5 codes for Butte; the street decides which)
  • Divide, MT 59727
  • Melrose, MT 59743
