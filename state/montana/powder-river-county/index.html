Where Powder River County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Powder River County fall in the south-east of Montana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 46 miles, and from east to west about 50 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 59062 (Otter) and 59345 (Powderville), about 68 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Carter County (49 miles), Custer County (66 miles), Campbell County, Wyoming (72 miles) and Crook County, Wyoming (73 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Powder River County begin with 593 (4 codes) and 590 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 593 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Carter County, Custer County and Dawson County, and has 31 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,769 square miles of land (7,172 sq km). The largest is 59317 (Broadus) with 1,406 square miles, and the smallest is 59343 (Olive) with 175. The middle-sized code here covers 335 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Powder River County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Powder River County ranks 33rd among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 404 codes in the state list.
