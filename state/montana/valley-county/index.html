Where Valley County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Valley County fall in the north-east of Montana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 60 miles, and from east to west about 45 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 59223 (Fort Peck) and 59250 (Opheim), about 60 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Daniels County (55 miles), Phillips County (68 miles), McCone County (75 miles) and Roosevelt County (79 miles).

The first three digits

All 11 codes here begin with the same three digits, 592. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 592 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Daniels County, Dawson County and McCone County, and has 41 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 5,108 square miles of land (13,230 sq km). The largest is 59230 (Glasgow) with 1,497 square miles, and the smallest is 59231 (Saint Marie) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 327 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Valley County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Valley County ranks 8th among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 404 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Valley County
