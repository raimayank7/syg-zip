Montana ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 404 ZIP codes for Montana, about 1.0% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 39th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 56 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Yellowstone County (23), Cascade County (18), Flathead County (18), Missoula County (16) and Lewis and Clark County (15). At the other end, 4 have only one or two codes, for example Deer Lodge County, Golden Valley County and Petroleum County.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Montana uses 10 of these prefixes, from 590 to 599. The busiest are 590 (75 codes, most of them for Ashland), 594 (64 codes, most of them for Great Falls), 598 (53 codes, most of them for Missoula) and 597 (49 codes, most of them for Bozeman). The first digit 5 is shared with District of Columbia, Iowa, Minnesota, North Dakota, South Dakota and Wisconsin.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 369 of the 404 codes. The largest is 59301 (Miles City, Custer County) at 2,852 square miles and the smallest is 59623 (Helena) at under 0.01; the middle value is 262 square miles. The other 35 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 59484 (Sweet Grass) and the southernmost 59739 (Lima); the one farthest east is 59319 (Capitol) and farthest west 59844 (Heron).

Place names and addresses in Montana

Of the 360 place names in the list, 351 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Billings (14), Helena (8), Missoula (8) and Bozeman (6). In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters MT and the code, as in Billings, MT 59101. The name of the county is left out.
