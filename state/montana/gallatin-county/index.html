Where Gallatin County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Gallatin County fall in the south of Montana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 84 miles, and from east to west about 35 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 59752 (Three Forks) and 59758 (West Yellowstone), about 87 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Madison County (37 miles), Park County (38 miles), Broadwater County (53 miles) and Jefferson County (59 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Gallatin County starts with 597. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 597 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Beaverhead County, Deer Lodge County and Jefferson County, and has 49 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 2,656 square miles of land (6,880 sq km). The largest is 59730 (Gallatin Gateway) with 686 square miles, and the smallest is 59760 (Willow Creek) with 45.6. The middle-sized code here covers 295 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Gallatin County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 59717 (Bozeman), 59719 (Bozeman), 59771 (Bozeman) and 59772 (Bozeman). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Gallatin County ranks 6th among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 404 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Gallatin County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MT and the ZIP code:

  • Bozeman, MT 59715 (one of 6 codes for Bozeman; the street decides which)
  • Belgrade, MT 59714
  • Big Sky, MT 59716

Questions about Gallatin County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Bozeman, MT?

Bozeman has 6 ZIP codes in Gallatin County: 59715, 59717, 59718, 59719, 59771, 59772. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Gallatin County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 13 start with 597.

Which ZIP code in Gallatin County covers the most land?

59730 (Gallatin Gateway), at about 686 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
