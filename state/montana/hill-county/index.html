Where Hill County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Hill County fall in the north of Montana. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 46 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 59501 (Havre) and 59530 (Inverness), about 46 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Liberty County (35 miles), Chouteau County (54 miles), Blaine County (62 miles) and Toole County (72 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 595. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 595 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Blaine County, Chouteau County and Liberty County, and has 23 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,928 square miles of land (7,583 sq km). The largest is 59501 (Havre) with 1,345 square miles, and the smallest is 59530 (Inverness) with 195. The middle-sized code here covers 284 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hill County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Hill County ranks 22nd among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 404 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hill County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MT and the ZIP code:
