Where Powell County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Powell County is in the west of Montana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 43 miles, and from east to west about 33 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 59728 (Elliston) and 59854 (Ovando), about 48 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Granite County (24 miles), Lewis and Clark County (32 miles), Deer Lodge County (34 miles) and Jefferson County (47 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Powell County begin with 597 (5 codes) and 598 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 597 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Beaverhead County, Deer Lodge County and Gallatin County, and has 49 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,371 square miles of land (6,141 sq km). The largest is 59854 (Ovando) with 844 square miles, and the smallest is 59731 (Garrison) with 131. The middle-sized code here covers 252 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Powell County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Powell County ranks 22nd among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 404 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Powell County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MT and the ZIP code:

  • Avon, MT 59713
  • Deer Lodge, MT 59722
  • Elliston, MT 59728

Avon is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 11 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
