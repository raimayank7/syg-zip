Where Toole County is

The ZIP codes of Toole County are found in the north-west of Montana. Its codes spread over about 36 miles from north to south and 41 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 59461 (Lothair) and 59484 (Sweet Grass), about 49 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Liberty County (37 miles), Pondera County (38 miles), Glacier County (56 miles) and Teton County (61 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 594. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 594 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Cascade County, Chouteau County and Fergus County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,803 square miles of land (4,670 sq km). The largest is 59474 (Shelby) with 553 square miles, and the smallest is 59461 (Lothair) with 37.5. The middle-sized code here covers 165 square miles; the middle value for all of Montana is 262. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Toole County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Toole County ranks 18th among the 56 counties and county-level areas of Montana (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 404 codes in the state list.
