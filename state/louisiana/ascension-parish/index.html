Where Ascension Parish is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Ascension Parish is in the south-east of Louisiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 14 miles, and from east to west about 9.8 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 70346 (Donaldsonville) and 70769 (Prairieville), about 15 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are St. James Parish (16 miles), Assumption Parish (19 miles), Livingston Parish (19 miles) and Iberville Parish (22 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Ascension Parish begin with 707 (10 codes) and 703 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 707 is shared with 9 other parishes, such as East Baton Rouge Parish, East Feliciana Parish and Iberville Parish, and has 67 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 302 square miles of land (781 sq km). The largest is 70346 (Donaldsonville) with 64.9 square miles, and the smallest is 70725 (Darrow) with 8.6. The middle-sized code here covers 47.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Water adds about 13.2 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ascension Parish; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 70707 (Gonzales), 70718 (Brittany), 70728 (Duplessis) and 70738 (Burnside). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Ascension Parish ranks 18th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Ascension Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • Gonzales, LA 70707 (one of 2 codes for Gonzales; the street decides which)
  • Brittany, LA 70718
  • Burnside, LA 70738

Questions about Ascension Parish ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Gonzales, LA?

Gonzales has 2 ZIP codes in Ascension Parish: 70707, 70737. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Ascension Parish start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 707, on 10 of the 11 codes.

Which ZIP code in Ascension Parish covers the most land?

70346 (Donaldsonville), at about 64.9 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
