Louisiana ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 719 ZIP codes for Louisiana, about 1.8% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 24th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 64 parishes and county-level areas. The most codes are in Orleans Parish (56), Caddo Parish (47), East Baton Rouge Parish (47), Jefferson Parish (34) and Rapides Parish (30). At the other end, 2 have only one or two codes, for example Red River Parish and St. Helena Parish.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Louisiana uses 13 of these prefixes, from 700 to 714. The busiest are 705 (75 codes, most of them for Lafayette), 714 (68 codes, most of them for Natchitoches), 707 (67 codes, most of them for Denham Springs) and 700 (64 codes, most of them for Metairie). The first digit 7 is shared with Arkansas, Missouri, Oklahoma and Texas.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 539 of the 719 codes. The largest is 71446 (Leesville, Vernon Parish) at 660 square miles and the smallest is 70163 (New Orleans) at under 0.01; the middle value is 45.6 square miles. The other 180 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 71075 (Springhill) and the southernmost 70081 (Pilottown); the one farthest east is 70081 (Pilottown) and farthest west 71007 (Bethany).

Place names and addresses in Louisiana

Of the 479 place names in the list, 437 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to New Orleans (61), Baton Rouge (42), Shreveport (35) and Lafayette (12). 1 place name appears under more than one parish, among them New Orleans: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters LA and the code, as in New Orleans, LA 70112. The name of the parish is left out.
