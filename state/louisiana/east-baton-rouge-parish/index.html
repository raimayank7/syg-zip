Where East Baton Rouge Parish is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, East Baton Rouge Parish is in the south of Louisiana. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 70791 (Zachary) and 70810 (Baton Rouge), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are West Baton Rouge Parish (12 miles), Iberville Parish (16 miles), Livingston Parish (19 miles) and Ascension Parish (22 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of East Baton Rouge Parish begin with 708 (42 codes) and 707 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 708, so those three digits alone point to East Baton Rouge Parish.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 25 of the 47 codes. Together they come to about 445 square miles of land (1,152 sq km). The largest is 70791 (Zachary) with 119 square miles, and the smallest is 70825 (Baton Rouge) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 11.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 14.6 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of East Baton Rouge Parish; a code can reach across its border. 22 codes have no mapped area: 70704 (Baker), 70804 (Baton Rouge), 70821 (Baton Rouge), 70822 (Baton Rouge), 70823 (Baton Rouge) and 17 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, East Baton Rouge Parish ranks 2nd among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 47 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in East Baton Rouge Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:
