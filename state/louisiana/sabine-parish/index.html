Where Sabine Parish is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Sabine Parish fall in the north-west of Louisiana. Its codes spread over about 26 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 71065 (Pleasant Hill) and 71429 (Florien), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Natchitoches Parish (29 miles), Sabine County, Texas (31 miles), De Soto Parish (37 miles) and Red River Parish (37 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Sabine Parish begin with 714 (8 codes) and 710 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 714 is shared with 9 other parishes, such as Allen Parish, Caldwell Parish and Catahoula Parish, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 844 square miles of land (2,186 sq km). The largest is 71429 (Florien) with 251 square miles, and the smallest is 71426 (Fisher) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 80.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Water adds about 138 square miles, roughly 14% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sabine Parish; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 71460 (Negreet). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Sabine Parish ranks 26th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Sabine Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • Belmont, LA 71406
  • Converse, LA 71419
  • Fisher, LA 71426

Belmont is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 13 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
