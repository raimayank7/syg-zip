Where Morehouse Parish is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Morehouse Parish is in the north of Louisiana. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 71250 (Jones) and 71264 (Oak Ridge), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are West Carroll Parish (21 miles), Richland Parish (27 miles), Ouachita Parish (28 miles) and Ashley County, Arkansas (29 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 712. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 712 is shared with 9 other parishes, such as East Carroll Parish, Franklin Parish and Jackson Parish, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 822 square miles of land (2,129 sq km). The largest is 71220 (Bastrop) with 374 square miles, and the smallest is 71223 (Bonita) with 50.7. The middle-sized code here covers 102 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Morehouse Parish; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 71221 (Bastrop). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Morehouse Parish ranks 35th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Morehouse Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • Bastrop, LA 71220 (one of 2 codes for Bastrop; the street decides which)
  • Bonita, LA 71223
  • Collinston, LA 71229
