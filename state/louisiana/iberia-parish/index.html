Where Iberia Parish is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Iberia Parish fall in the south of Louisiana. Its codes spread over about 12 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 70513 (Avery Island) and 70552 (Loreauville), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are St. Martin Parish (18 miles), Lafayette Parish (22 miles), Vermilion Parish (25 miles) and St. Mary Parish (29 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Iberia Parish starts with 705. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 705 is shared with 8 other parishes, such as Acadia Parish, Evangeline Parish and Jefferson Davis Parish, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 362 square miles of land (938 sq km). The largest is 70560 (New Iberia) with 176 square miles, and the smallest is 70513 (Avery Island) with 1.6. The middle-sized code here covers 62.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Water adds about 18.4 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Iberia Parish; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 70562 (New Iberia) and 70569 (Lydia). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Iberia Parish ranks 35th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Iberia Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • New Iberia, LA 70560 (one of 3 codes for New Iberia; the street decides which)
  • Avery Island, LA 70513
  • Jeanerette, LA 70544
