Where Lafourche Parish is

The ZIP codes of Lafourche Parish are found in the south-east of Louisiana. Its codes spread over about 33 miles from north to south and 34 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 70310 (Thibodaux) and 70357 (Golden Meadow), about 44 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Terrebonne Parish (14 miles), St. Charles Parish (21 miles), St. John the Baptist Parish (28 miles) and St. James Parish (29 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 703. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 703 is shared with 5 other parishes, such as Ascension Parish, Assumption Parish and Jefferson Parish, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 962 square miles of land (2,492 sq km). The largest is 70301 (Thibodaux) with 284 square miles, and the smallest is 70310 (Thibodaux) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 76.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Water adds about 192 square miles, roughly 17% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lafourche Parish; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 70302 (Thibodaux) and 70371 (Kraemer). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lafourche Parish ranks 16th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lafourche Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • Thibodaux, LA 70301 (one of 3 codes for Thibodaux; the street decides which)
  • Cut Off, LA 70345
  • Galliano, LA 70354

Questions about Lafourche Parish ZIP codes

Thibodaux has 3 ZIP codes in Lafourche Parish: 70301, 70302, 70310. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Which ZIP code in Lafourche Parish covers the most land?
