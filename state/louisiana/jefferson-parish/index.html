Where Jefferson Parish is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Jefferson Parish is in the south-east of Louisiana. Its codes spread over about 55 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 70065 (Kenner) and 70358 (Grand Isle), about 57 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Orleans Parish (5.7 miles), St. Bernard Parish (13 miles), St. Charles Parish (14 miles) and St. John the Baptist Parish (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Jefferson Parish begin with 700 (28 codes), 701 (5 codes) and 703 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 700 is shared with 5 other parishes, such as Plaquemines Parish, St. Bernard Parish and St. Charles Parish, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 34 codes. Together they come to about 185 square miles of land (478 sq km). The largest is 70072 (Marrero) with 51.8 square miles, and the smallest is 70006 (Metairie) with 2.6. The middle-sized code here covers 7.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 23.5 square miles, roughly 11% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jefferson Parish; a code can reach across its border. 17 codes have no mapped area: 70004 (Metairie), 70009 (Metairie), 70010 (Metairie), 70011 (Metairie), 70033 (Metairie) and 12 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Jefferson Parish ranks 4th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 34 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jefferson Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • Metairie, LA 70001 (one of 12 codes for Metairie; the street decides which)
  • Kenner, LA 70062 (one of 5 codes for Kenner; the street decides which)
  • New Orleans, LA 70121 (one of 5 codes for New Orleans; the street decides which)

New Orleans shares a name with a place in another part of Louisiana; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
