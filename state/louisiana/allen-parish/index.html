Where Allen Parish is

The ZIP codes of Allen Parish are found in the west of Louisiana. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 23 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 70658 (Reeves) and 71463 (Oakdale), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Beauregard Parish (23 miles), Jefferson Davis Parish (28 miles), Evangeline Parish (28 miles) and Vernon Parish (39 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Allen Parish begin with 706 (7 codes) and 714 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 706 is shared with 5 other parishes, such as Beauregard Parish, Calcasieu Parish and Cameron Parish, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 748 square miles of land (1,938 sq km). The largest is 71463 (Oakdale) with 252 square miles, and the smallest is 70644 (Grant) with 11.0. The middle-sized code here covers 49.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Allen Parish; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Allen Parish ranks 29th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Allen Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • Elizabeth, LA 70638
  • Grant, LA 70644
  • Kinder, LA 70648

Elizabeth is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
