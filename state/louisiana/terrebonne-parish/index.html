Where Terrebonne Parish is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Terrebonne Parish is in the south-east of Louisiana. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 70356 (Gibson) and 70377 (Montegut), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lafourche Parish (14 miles), St. James Parish (31 miles), Assumption Parish (32 miles) and St. Charles Parish (33 miles).

The first three digits

All 13 codes here begin with the same three digits, 703. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 703 is shared with 5 other parishes, such as Ascension Parish, Assumption Parish and Jefferson Parish, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 563 square miles of land (1,457 sq km). The largest is 70377 (Montegut) with 84.6 square miles, and the smallest is 70352 (Donner) with 3.9. The middle-sized code here covers 49.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Water adds about 57.3 square miles, roughly 9% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Terrebonne Parish; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 70361 (Houma). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Terrebonne Parish ranks 15th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Terrebonne Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • Houma, LA 70360 (one of 4 codes for Houma; the street decides which)
  • Bourg, LA 70343
  • Chauvin, LA 70344
