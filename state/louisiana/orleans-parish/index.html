Where Orleans Parish is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Orleans Parish fall in the south-east of Louisiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 70118 (New Orleans) and 70129 (New Orleans), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Jefferson Parish (5.7 miles), St. Bernard Parish (8.6 miles), St. Charles Parish (19 miles) and St. John the Baptist Parish (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 56 codes here begin with the same three digits, 701. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 701 is shared with 1 other parish, Jefferson Parish, and has 61 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 20 of the 56 codes. Together they come to about 169 square miles of land (439 sq km). The largest is 70129 (New Orleans) with 90.9 square miles, and the smallest is 70163 (New Orleans) with under 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 4.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 55.5 square miles, roughly 25% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Orleans Parish; a code can reach across its border. 36 codes have no mapped area: 70142, 70143, 70145, 70146, 70150 and 31 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Louisiana has more ZIP codes: Orleans Parish holds 56 of the 719 in the state list, about 7.8%.

Writing an address in Orleans Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • New Orleans, LA 70112 (one of 56 codes for New Orleans; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named New Orleans are also listed under Jefferson Parish, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site.
