Where Catahoula Parish is

The ZIP codes of Catahoula Parish are found in the north of Louisiana. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 71363 (Rhinehart) and 71368 (Sicily Island), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Concordia Parish (20 miles), La Salle Parish (22 miles), Caldwell Parish (25 miles) and Franklin Parish (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Catahoula Parish begin with 713 (4 codes) and 714 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 713 is shared with 8 other parishes, such as Avoyelles Parish, Concordia Parish and Evangeline Parish, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 796 square miles of land (2,063 sq km). The largest is 71343 (Jonesville) with 538 square miles, and the smallest is 71401 (Aimwell) with 16.6. The middle-sized code here covers 85.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Water adds about 49.6 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Catahoula Parish; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 71363 (Rhinehart). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Catahoula Parish ranks 41st among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Catahoula Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:
