Where Winn Parish is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Winn Parish fall in the north-west of Louisiana. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 30 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 71471 (Saint Maurice) and 71473 (Sikes), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Grant Parish (25 miles), Natchitoches Parish (27 miles), Jackson Parish (28 miles) and La Salle Parish (29 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Winn Parish starts with 714. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 714 is shared with 9 other parishes, such as Allen Parish, Caldwell Parish and Catahoula Parish, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 753 square miles of land (1,951 sq km). The largest is 71483 (Winnfield) with 363 square miles, and the smallest is 71410 (Calvin) with 0.8. The middle-sized code here covers 124 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Winn Parish; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 71440 (Joyce) and 71471 (Saint Maurice). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Winn Parish ranks 35th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Winn Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • Atlanta, LA 71404
  • Calvin, LA 71410
  • Dodson, LA 71422

Atlanta is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 10 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
