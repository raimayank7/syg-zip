Where Bossier Parish is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Bossier Parish is in the north-west of Louisiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 36 miles, and from east to west about 13 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 71051 (Elm Grove) and 71064 (Plain Dealing), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Caddo Parish (10.0 miles), Webster Parish (19 miles), De Soto Parish (34 miles) and Bienville Parish (37 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Bossier Parish begin with 711 (6 codes) and 710 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 711 is shared with 1 other parish, Caddo Parish, and has 41 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 784 square miles of land (2,030 sq km). The largest is 71064 (Plain Dealing) with 212 square miles, and the smallest is 71110 (Barksdale Afb) with 17.9. The middle-sized code here covers 70.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bossier Parish; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 71113 (Bossier City), 71171 (Bossier City) and 71172 (Bossier City). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Bossier Parish ranks 18th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bossier Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • Bossier City, LA 71111 (one of 5 codes for Bossier City; the street decides which)
  • Barksdale Afb, LA 71110
  • Benton, LA 71006

Questions about Bossier Parish ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Bossier City, LA?

Bossier City has 5 ZIP codes in Bossier Parish: 71111, 71112, 71113, 71171, 71172. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Bossier Parish start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 711, on 6 of the 11 codes.

Which ZIP code in Bossier Parish covers the most land?

71064 (Plain Dealing), at about 212 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
