Where Grant Parish is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Grant Parish is in the north-west of Louisiana. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 71454 (Montgomery) and 71467 (Pollock), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are La Salle Parish (23 miles), Rapides Parish (25 miles), Winn Parish (25 miles) and Natchitoches Parish (32 miles).

The first three digits

All 6 codes here begin with the same three digits, 714. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 714 is shared with 9 other parishes, such as Allen Parish, Caldwell Parish and Catahoula Parish, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 668 square miles of land (1,730 sq km). The largest is 71467 (Pollock) with 174 square miles, and the smallest is 71407 (Bentley) with 17.7. The middle-sized code here covers 118 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Grant Parish; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Grant Parish ranks 41st among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 719 codes in the state list.
