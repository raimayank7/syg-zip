Where St. Landry Parish is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, St. Landry Parish is in the south of Louisiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 31 miles, and from east to west about 38 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 70535 (Eunice) and 71353 (Melville), about 40 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Evangeline Parish (25 miles), St. Martin Parish (25 miles), Lafayette Parish (25 miles) and Pointe Coupee Parish (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of St. Landry Parish begin with 705 (10 codes), 713 (4 codes) and 707 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 705 is shared with 8 other parishes, such as Acadia Parish, Evangeline Parish and Iberia Parish, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 938 square miles of land (2,430 sq km). The largest is 70570 (Opelousas) with 172 square miles, and the smallest is 71345 (Lebeau) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 71.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of St. Landry Parish; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 70571 (Opelousas). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, St. Landry Parish ranks 12th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in St. Landry Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • Opelousas, LA 70570 (one of 2 codes for Opelousas; the street decides which)
  • Arnaudville, LA 70512
  • Eunice, LA 70535

Questions about St. Landry Parish ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Opelousas, LA?

Opelousas has 2 ZIP codes in St. Landry Parish: 70570, 70571. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in St. Landry Parish start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 705, on 10 of the 15 codes.

Which ZIP code in St. Landry Parish covers the most land?

70570 (Opelousas), at about 172 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
