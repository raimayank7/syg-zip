Where Webster Parish is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Webster Parish fall in the north-west of Louisiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 38 miles, and from east to west about 14 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 71039 (Heflin) and 71075 (Springhill), about 40 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Bossier Parish (19 miles), Claiborne Parish (23 miles), Caddo Parish (28 miles) and Bienville Parish (30 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Webster Parish starts with 710. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 710 is shared with 8 other parishes, such as Bienville Parish, Bossier Parish and Caddo Parish, and has 56 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 719 square miles of land (1,862 sq km). The largest is 71055 (Minden) with 260 square miles, and the smallest is 71021 (Cullen) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 50.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Water adds about 29.0 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Webster Parish; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 71058 (Minden). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Webster Parish ranks 18th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Webster Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • Minden, LA 71055 (one of 2 codes for Minden; the street decides which)
  • Cotton Valley, LA 71018
  • Cullen, LA 71021

Minden is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
