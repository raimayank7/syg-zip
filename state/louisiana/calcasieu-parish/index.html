Where Calcasieu Parish is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Calcasieu Parish is in the south-west of Louisiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 22 miles, and from east to west about 45 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 70646 (Hayes) and 70661 (Starks), about 47 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cameron Parish (27 miles), Beauregard Parish (29 miles), Jefferson Davis Parish (30 miles) and Orange County, Texas (37 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Calcasieu Parish starts with 706. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 706 is shared with 5 other parishes, such as Allen Parish, Beauregard Parish and Cameron Parish, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 16 of the 21 codes. Together they come to about 1,414 square miles of land (3,663 sq km). The largest is 70661 (Starks) with 185 square miles, and the smallest is 70629 (Lake Charles) with under 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 89.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Water adds about 59.4 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Calcasieu Parish; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 70602 (Lake Charles), 70606 (Lake Charles), 70612 (Lake Charles), 70616 (Lake Charles) and 70664 (Sulphur). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Calcasieu Parish ranks 6th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 21 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Calcasieu Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:
