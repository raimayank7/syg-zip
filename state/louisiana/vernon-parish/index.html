Where Vernon Parish is

The ZIP codes of Vernon Parish are found in the west of Louisiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 33 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 70656 (Pitkin) and 71439 (Hornbeck), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Beauregard Parish (33 miles), Allen Parish (39 miles), Sabine Parish (39 miles) and Newton County, Texas (42 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Vernon Parish begin with 714 (9 codes) and 706 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 714 is shared with 9 other parishes, such as Allen Parish, Caldwell Parish and Catahoula Parish, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 1,271 square miles of land (3,293 sq km). The largest is 71446 (Leesville) with 660 square miles, and the smallest is 71475 (Slagle) with 0.07. The middle-sized code here covers 42.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Vernon Parish; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 71443 (Kurthwood) and 71496 (Leesville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Vernon Parish ranks 16th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Vernon Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • Leesville, LA 71446 (one of 2 codes for Leesville; the street decides which)
  • Anacoco, LA 71403
  • Evans, LA 70639

Leesville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Vernon Parish ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Leesville, LA?

Leesville has 2 ZIP codes in Vernon Parish: 71446, 71496. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Vernon Parish start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 714, on 9 of the 12 codes.

Which ZIP code in Vernon Parish covers the most land?

71446 (Leesville), at about 660 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
