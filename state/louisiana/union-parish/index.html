Where Union Parish is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Union Parish is in the north-west of Louisiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 71234 (Downsville) and 71256 (Lillie), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lincoln Parish (21 miles), Union County, Arkansas (27 miles), Claiborne Parish (28 miles) and Ouachita Parish (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 6 codes here begin with the same three digits, 712. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 712 is shared with 9 other parishes, such as East Carroll Parish, Franklin Parish and Jackson Parish, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 901 square miles of land (2,333 sq km). The largest is 71260 (Marion) with 288 square miles, and the smallest is 71256 (Lillie) with 52.4. The middle-sized code here covers 130 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 29.4 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Union Parish; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Union Parish ranks 41st among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Union Parish
