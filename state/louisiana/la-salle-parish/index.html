Where La Salle Parish is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, La Salle Parish is in the north-west of Louisiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 15 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 71342 (Jena) and 71479 (Tullos), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Caldwell Parish (19 miles), Catahoula Parish (22 miles), Grant Parish (23 miles) and Winn Parish (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of La Salle Parish begin with 714 (3 codes) and 713 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 714 is shared with 9 other parishes, such as Allen Parish, Caldwell Parish and Catahoula Parish, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 633 square miles of land (1,640 sq km). The largest is 71465 (Olla) with 250 square miles, and the smallest is 71480 (Urania) with 3.9. The middle-sized code here covers 140 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of La Salle Parish; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, La Salle Parish ranks 50th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in La Salle Parish
