Where Ouachita Parish is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Ouachita Parish is in the north of Louisiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 22 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 71202 (Monroe) and 71280 (Sterlington), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Richland Parish (25 miles), Morehouse Parish (28 miles), Union Parish (30 miles) and Jackson Parish (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 17 codes here begin with the same three digits, 712. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 712 is shared with 9 other parishes, such as East Carroll Parish, Franklin Parish and Jackson Parish, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 587 square miles of land (1,522 sq km). The largest is 71292 (West Monroe) with 154 square miles, and the smallest is 71209 (Monroe) with 0.05. The middle-sized code here covers 59.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Water adds about 22.9 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ouachita Parish; a code can reach across its border. 8 codes have no mapped area: 71207 (Monroe), 71210 (Monroe), 71211 (Monroe), 71213 (Monroe), 71217 (Monroe) and 3 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Ouachita Parish ranks 9th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Ouachita Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • Monroe, LA 71201 (one of 10 codes for Monroe; the street decides which)
  • West Monroe, LA 71291 (one of 3 codes for West Monroe; the street decides which)
  • Calhoun, LA 71225

Monroe is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 20 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
