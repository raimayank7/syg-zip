Where Livingston Parish is

The ZIP codes of Livingston Parish are found in the south-east of Louisiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 70449 (Maurepas) and 70706 (Denham Springs), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Ascension Parish (19 miles), East Baton Rouge Parish (19 miles), Tangipahoa Parish (22 miles) and St. Helena Parish (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Livingston Parish begin with 707 (9 codes) and 704 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 707 is shared with 9 other parishes, such as Ascension Parish, East Baton Rouge Parish and East Feliciana Parish, and has 67 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 633 square miles of land (1,639 sq km). The largest is 70754 (Livingston) with 127 square miles, and the smallest is 70786 (Watson) with 0.05. The middle-sized code here covers 69.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Livingston Parish; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 70727 (Denham Springs). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Livingston Parish ranks 18th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Livingston Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • Denham Springs, LA 70706 (one of 3 codes for Denham Springs; the street decides which)
  • Albany, LA 70711
  • French Settlement, LA 70733

Questions about Livingston Parish ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Denham Springs, LA?

Denham Springs has 3 ZIP codes in Livingston Parish: 70706, 70726, 70727. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Livingston Parish start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 707, on 9 of the 11 codes.

Which ZIP code in Livingston Parish covers the most land?

70754 (Livingston), at about 127 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
