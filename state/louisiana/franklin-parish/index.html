Where Franklin Parish is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Franklin Parish is in the north of Louisiana. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 71219 (Baskin) and 71378 (Wisner), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Richland Parish (20 miles), Catahoula Parish (26 miles), Tensas Parish (26 miles) and Caldwell Parish (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Franklin Parish begin with 712 (5 codes) and 713 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 712 is shared with 9 other parishes, such as East Carroll Parish, Jackson Parish and Lincoln Parish, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 569 square miles of land (1,473 sq km). The largest is 71295 (Winnsboro) with 315 square miles, and the smallest is 71230 (Crowville) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 54.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Franklin Parish; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 71249 (Jigger) and 71324 (Chase). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Franklin Parish ranks 29th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Franklin Parish
