Where Bienville Parish is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Bienville Parish fall in the north-west of Louisiana. Its codes spread over about 25 miles from north to south and 23 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 71001 (Arcadia) and 71068 (Ringgold), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Red River Parish (22 miles), Lincoln Parish (27 miles), Jackson Parish (28 miles) and Webster Parish (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 710. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 710 is shared with 8 other parishes, such as Bossier Parish, Caddo Parish and Claiborne Parish, and has 56 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 918 square miles of land (2,379 sq km). The largest is 71070 (Saline) with 199 square miles, and the smallest is 71045 (Jamestown) with 49.8. The middle-sized code here covers 132 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bienville Parish; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 71080 (Taylor). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Bienville Parish ranks 29th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bienville Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • Arcadia, LA 71001
  • Bienville, LA 71008
  • Castor, LA 71016

Arcadia is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 13 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
