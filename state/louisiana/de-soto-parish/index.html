Where De Soto Parish is

The ZIP codes of De Soto Parish are found in the north-west of Louisiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 71063 (Pelican) and 71078 (Stonewall), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Shelby County, Texas (28 miles), Red River Parish (30 miles), Panola County, Texas (32 miles) and Caddo Parish (33 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of De Soto Parish starts with 710. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 710 is shared with 8 other parishes, such as Bienville Parish, Bossier Parish and Caddo Parish, and has 56 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 867 square miles of land (2,244 sq km). The largest is 71052 (Mansfield) with 320 square miles, and the smallest is 71030 (Gloster) with 40.1. The middle-sized code here covers 89.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of De Soto Parish; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 71050 (Longstreet). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, De Soto Parish ranks 26th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in De Soto Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • Gloster, LA 71030
