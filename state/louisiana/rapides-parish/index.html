Where Rapides Parish is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Rapides Parish is in the west of Louisiana. Its codes spread over about 29 miles from north to south and 42 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 71325 (Cheneyville) and 71427 (Flatwoods), about 43 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Grant Parish (25 miles), Avoyelles Parish (32 miles), Evangeline Parish (37 miles) and La Salle Parish (41 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Rapides Parish begin with 713 (16 codes) and 714 (14 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 713 is shared with 8 other parishes, such as Avoyelles Parish, Catahoula Parish and Concordia Parish, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 20 of the 30 codes. Together they come to about 1,448 square miles of land (3,750 sq km). The largest is 71360 (Pineville) with 195 square miles, and the smallest is 71359 (Pineville) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 60.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Water adds about 45.7 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Rapides Parish; a code can reach across its border. 10 codes have no mapped area: 71306 (Alexandria), 71307 (Alexandria), 71309 (Alexandria), 71315 (Alexandria), 71348 (Libuse) and 5 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Rapides Parish ranks 5th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 30 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Rapides Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • Alexandria, LA 71301 (one of 7 codes for Alexandria; the street decides which)
  • Pineville, LA 71359 (one of 3 codes for Pineville; the street decides which)
  • Ball, LA 71405

Alexandria is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 11 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
