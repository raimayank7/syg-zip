Where Natchitoches Parish is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Natchitoches Parish is in the north-west of Louisiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 51 miles, and from east to west about 28 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 71002 (Ashland) and 71455 (Mora), about 51 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Winn Parish (27 miles), Red River Parish (28 miles), Sabine Parish (29 miles) and Grant Parish (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Natchitoches Parish begin with 714 (14 codes) and 710 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 714 is shared with 9 other parishes, such as Allen Parish, Caldwell Parish and Catahoula Parish, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 16 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 1,125 square miles of land (2,913 sq km). The largest is 71457 (Natchitoches) with 270 square miles, and the smallest is 71066 (Powhatan) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 45.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Water adds about 38.7 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Natchitoches Parish; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 71458 (Natchitoches). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Natchitoches Parish ranks 9th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Natchitoches Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:

  • Natchitoches, LA 71457 (one of 3 codes for Natchitoches; the street decides which)
  • Ashland, LA 71002
  • Campti, LA 71411

Questions about Natchitoches Parish ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Natchitoches, LA?

Natchitoches has 3 ZIP codes in Natchitoches Parish: 71457, 71458, 71497. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Natchitoches Parish start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 714, on 14 of the 17 codes.

Which ZIP code in Natchitoches Parish covers the most land?

71457 (Natchitoches), at about 270 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
