Where Lincoln Parish is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Lincoln Parish fall in the north-west of Louisiana. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 16 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 71227 (Choudrant) and 71275 (Simsboro), about 16 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Jackson Parish (18 miles), Union Parish (21 miles), Claiborne Parish (25 miles) and Bienville Parish (27 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 712. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 712 is shared with 9 other parishes, such as East Carroll Parish, Franklin Parish and Jackson Parish, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 587 square miles of land (1,521 sq km). The largest is 71270 (Ruston) with 207 square miles, and the smallest is 71272 (Ruston) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 106 square miles; the middle value for all of Louisiana is 45.6. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lincoln Parish; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 71273 (Ruston). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lincoln Parish ranks 35th among the 64 parishes of Louisiana (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 719 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lincoln Parish

The name of the parish is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters LA and the ZIP code:
