Where Edgar County is

The ZIP codes of Edgar County are found in the east of Illinois. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 18 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 61924 (Chrisman) and 61933 (Kansas), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Vermillion County, Indiana (20 miles), Clark County (22 miles), Douglas County (24 miles) and Coles County (26 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 619. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 619 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Coles County, Douglas County and Moultrie County, and has 30 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 585 square miles of land (1,516 sq km). The largest is 61944 (Paris) with 263 square miles, and the smallest is 61955 (Vermilion) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 45.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Edgar County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Edgar County ranks 65th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Edgar County
