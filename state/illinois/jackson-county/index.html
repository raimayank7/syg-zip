Where Jackson County is

The ZIP codes of Jackson County are found in the south of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 62902 (Carbondale) and 62916 (Campbell Hill), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Williamson County (19 miles), Perry County (19 miles), Perry County, Missouri (21 miles) and Union County (22 miles).

The first three digits

All 16 codes here begin with the same three digits, 629. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 629 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Alexander County, Franklin County and Gallatin County, and has 83 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 15 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 643 square miles of land (1,666 sq km). The largest is 62966 (Murphysboro) with 111 square miles, and the smallest is 62927 (Dowell) with 1.7. The middle-sized code here covers 37.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. Water adds about 27.5 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jackson County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 62971 (Oraville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Jackson County ranks 26th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jackson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Carbondale, IL 62901 (one of 3 codes for Carbondale; the street decides which)
  • Ava, IL 62907
  • Campbell Hill, IL 62916

Carbondale is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
