Where Warren County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Warren County is in the north-west of Illinois. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 14 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 61453 (Little York) and 61473 (Roseville), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Henderson County (17 miles), Knox County (21 miles), Mercer County (22 miles) and McDonough County (26 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 614. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 614 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Fulton County, Hancock County and Henderson County, and has 73 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 467 square miles of land (1,210 sq km). The largest is 61462 (Monmouth) with 142 square miles, and the smallest is 61435 (Gerlaw) with 17.4. The middle-sized code here covers 42.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Warren County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Warren County ranks 65th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Warren County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Berwick, IL 61417
  • Cameron, IL 61423
  • Gerlaw, IL 61435

Berwick is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
