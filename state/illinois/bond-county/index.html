Where Bond County is

The ZIP codes of Bond County are found in the south of Illinois. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 18 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 62262 (Mulberry Grove) and 62273 (Pierron), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Clinton County (22 miles), Montgomery County (23 miles), Fayette County (24 miles) and Madison County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Bond County begin with 622 (5 codes) and 620 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 622 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Clinton County, Fayette County and Madison County, and has 79 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 415 square miles of land (1,076 sq km). The largest is 62246 (Greenville) with 143 square miles, and the smallest is 62273 (Pierron) with 1.3. The middle-sized code here covers 48.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bond County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Bond County ranks 73rd among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bond County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Donnellson, IL 62019
  • Greenville, IL 62246
  • Mulberry Grove, IL 62262
