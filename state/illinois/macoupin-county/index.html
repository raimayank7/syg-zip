Where Macoupin County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Macoupin County fall close to the middle of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 34 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 62088 (Staunton) and 62683 (Scottville), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Montgomery County (23 miles), Jersey County (24 miles), Greene County (27 miles) and Madison County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Macoupin County begin with 626 (10 codes) and 620 (9 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 626 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Cass County, Logan County and Mason County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 18 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 772 square miles of land (1,999 sq km). The largest is 62626 (Carlinville) with 171 square miles, and the smallest is 62672 (Nilwood) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 39.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Macoupin County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 62683 (Scottville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Macoupin County ranks 20th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.
