Where Pike County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Pike County is in the west of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 32 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 62343 (Hull) and 62361 (Pearl), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Pike County, Missouri (22 miles), Scott County (23 miles), Brown County (23 miles) and Adams County (32 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Pike County starts with 623. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 623 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Adams County, Brown County and Hancock County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 905 square miles of land (2,344 sq km). The largest is 62363 (Pittsfield) with 154 square miles, and the smallest is 62352 (Milton) with 1.0. The middle-sized code here covers 62.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pike County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Pike County ranks 33rd among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pike County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:
