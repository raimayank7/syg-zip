Where Marion County is

The ZIP codes of Marion County are found in the south-east of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 62801 (Centralia) and 62854 (Kinmundy), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Fayette County (23 miles), Jefferson County (25 miles), Washington County (27 miles) and Clinton County (28 miles).

The first three digits

All 11 codes here begin with the same three digits, 628. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 628 is shared with 14 other counties, such as Clay County, Edwards County and Fayette County, and has 90 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 688 square miles of land (1,783 sq km). The largest is 62801 (Centralia) with 134 square miles, and the smallest is 62893 (Walnut Hill) with 20.1. The middle-sized code here covers 39.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Marion County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Marion County ranks 45th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Marion County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Alma, IL 62807
  • Centralia, IL 62801
  • Iuka, IL 62849

Alma is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 10 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
