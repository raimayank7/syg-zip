Where Cook County is

The ZIP codes of Cook County are found in the north-east of Illinois. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 47 miles, and from east to west about 35 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 60179 (Hoffman Estates) and 60475 (Steger), about 52 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are DuPage County (16 miles), Will County (27 miles), Kane County (31 miles) and Lake County, Indiana (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Cook County begin with 606 (83 codes), 604 (42 codes), 600 (34 codes), 601 (27 codes), 605 (7 codes), 602 (6 codes) and 3 less common prefixes. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 606, so those three digits alone point to Cook County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 163 of the 213 codes. Together they come to about 870 square miles of land (2,253 sq km). The largest is 60411 (Chicago Heights) with 30.7 square miles, and the smallest is 60604 (Chicago) with 0.08. The middle-sized code here covers 4.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cook County; a code can reach across its border. 50 codes have no mapped area: 60006 (Arlington Heights), 60009 (Elk Grove Village), 60017 (Des Plaines), 60019 (Des Plaines), 60038 (Palatine) and 45 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Illinois has more ZIP codes: Cook County holds 213 of the 1,575 in the state list, about 13.5%.

Writing an address in Cook County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Chicago, IL 60601 (one of 84 codes for Chicago; the street decides which)
  • Palatine, IL 60038 (one of 7 codes for Palatine; the street decides which)
  • Schaumburg, IL 60159 (one of 7 codes for Schaumburg; the street decides which)

Tinley Park shares a name with a place in another part of Illinois; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
