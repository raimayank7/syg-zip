Where White County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of White County fall in the south-east of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 62844 (Grayville) and 62869 (Norris City), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Posey County, Indiana (20 miles), Hamilton County (21 miles), Edwards County (22 miles) and Gallatin County (23 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 628. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 628 is shared with 14 other counties, such as Clay County, Edwards County and Fayette County, and has 90 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 525 square miles of land (1,360 sq km). The largest is 62821 (Carmi) with 171 square miles, and the smallest is 62861 (Maunie) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 46.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of White County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 62834 (Emma). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, White County ranks 50th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.
