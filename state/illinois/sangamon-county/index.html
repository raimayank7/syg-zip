Where Sangamon County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Sangamon County sits near the middle of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 29 miles from north to south and 33 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 62539 (Illiopolis) and 62662 (Lowder), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Menard County (21 miles), Christian County (23 miles), Logan County (28 miles) and Morgan County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Sangamon County begin with 627 (35 codes), 626 (10 codes) and 625 (9 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 627, so those three digits alone point to Sangamon County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 26 of the 54 codes. Together they come to about 907 square miles of land (2,350 sq km). The largest is 62558 (Pawnee) with 93.7 square miles, and the smallest is 62706 (Springfield) with 0.05. The middle-sized code here covers 26.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sangamon County; a code can reach across its border. 28 codes have no mapped area: 62662 (Lowder), 62705 (Springfield), 62708 (Springfield), 62715 (Springfield), 62716 (Springfield) and 23 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Sangamon County ranks 2nd among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 54 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Sangamon County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Springfield, IL 62701 (one of 35 codes for Springfield; the street decides which)
  • Auburn, IL 62615
  • Buffalo, IL 62515

Springfield is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 23 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
