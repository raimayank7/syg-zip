Where Johnson County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Johnson County fall in the south-east of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 13 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 62908 (Belknap) and 62967 (New Burnside), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Pope County (20 miles), Massac County (21 miles), Pulaski County (22 miles) and Union County (22 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Johnson County starts with 629. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 629 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Alexander County, Franklin County and Gallatin County, and has 83 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 462 square miles of land (1,196 sq km). The largest is 62995 (Vienna) with 120 square miles, and the smallest is 62967 (New Burnside) with 6.5. The middle-sized code here covers 49.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Johnson County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 62909 (Boles). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Johnson County ranks 50th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Johnson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Boles, IL 62909
  • Buncombe, IL 62912
