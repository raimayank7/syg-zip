Where DeKalb County is

The ZIP codes of DeKalb County are found in the north-east of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 33 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 60146 (Kirkland) and 60548 (Sandwich), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Kane County (20 miles), Kendall County (24 miles), Lee County (27 miles) and Ogle County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of DeKalb County begin with 601 (9 codes) and 605 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 601 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Cook County, DuPage County and Kane County, and has 89 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 588 square miles of land (1,523 sq km). The largest is 60115 (Dekalb) with 81.0 square miles, and the smallest is 60112 (Cortland) with 3.7. The middle-sized code here covers 43.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of DeKalb County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, DeKalb County ranks 35th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in DeKalb County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:
