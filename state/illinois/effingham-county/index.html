Where Effingham County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Effingham County is in the south-east of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 62414 (Beecher City) and 62445 (Montrose), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Shelby County (23 miles), Cumberland County (23 miles), Fayette County (24 miles) and Clay County (25 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Effingham County starts with 624. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 624 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Clark County, Clay County and Coles County, and has 61 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 637 square miles of land (1,649 sq km). The largest is 62401 (Effingham) with 104 square miles, and the smallest is 62473 (Watson) with 21.8. The middle-sized code here covers 66.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Effingham County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Effingham County ranks 50th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Effingham County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Altamont, IL 62411
  • Beecher City, IL 62414
  • Dieterich, IL 62424

Altamont is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
