Where Fulton County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Fulton County is in the north-west of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 32 miles from north to south and 23 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 61501 (Astoria) and 61531 (Farmington), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Mason County (23 miles), McDonough County (24 miles), Knox County (31 miles) and Schuyler County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Fulton County begin with 614 (10 codes) and 615 (10 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 614 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Hancock County, Henderson County and Henry County, and has 73 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 932 square miles of land (2,413 sq km). The largest is 61520 (Canton) with 152 square miles, and the smallest is 61524 (Dunfermline) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 43.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Fulton County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Fulton County ranks 18th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 20 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Fulton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Astoria, IL 61501
  • Avon, IL 61415
  • Bryant, IL 61519

Astoria is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
