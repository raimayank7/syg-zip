Where Williamson County is

The ZIP codes of Williamson County are found in the south-east of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 62922 (Creal Springs) and 62949 (Hurst), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Franklin County (13 miles), Jackson County (19 miles), Johnson County (24 miles) and Saline County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Williamson County begin with 629 (10 codes) and 628 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 629 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Alexander County, Franklin County and Gallatin County, and has 83 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 323 square miles of land (836 sq km). The largest is 62959 (Marion) with 143 square miles, and the smallest is 62841 (Freeman Spur) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 20.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. Water adds about 13.0 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Williamson County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Williamson County ranks 45th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Williamson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Carterville, IL 62918
