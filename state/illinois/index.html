Illinois ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 1,575 ZIP codes for Illinois, about 3.8% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 5th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 102 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Cook County (213), Sangamon County (54), DuPage County (53), Peoria County (44) and Champaign County (33). The fewest are in Pope County, with 3.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Illinois uses 29 of these prefixes, from 600 to 629. The busiest are 628 (90 codes, most of them for Albion), 601 (89 codes, most of them for Schaumburg), 604 (83 codes, most of them for Joliet) and 606 (83 codes, most of them for Chicago). The first digit 6 is shared with Kansas, Missouri and Nebraska.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 1,396 of the 1,575 codes. The largest is 61944 (Paris, Edgar County) at 263 square miles and the smallest is 61331 (Lee Center) at 0.03; the middle value is 31.5 square miles. The other 179 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 60075 (Russell) and the southernmost 62914 (Cairo); the one farthest east is 60438 (Lansing) and farthest west 62379 (Warsaw).

Place names and addresses in Illinois

Of the 1,286 place names in the list, 1,227 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Chicago (84), Springfield (35), Peoria (29) and Rockford (14). 3 place names appear under more than one county, among them Aurora, Naperville and Tinley Park: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters IL and the code, as in Chicago, IL 60601. The name of the county is left out.

Questions about Illinois ZIP codes

What ZIP codes does Illinois use?

In this list, Illinois ZIP codes run from 60001 to 62999 and start with 6. A few codes inside that range can belong to a neighbouring state near the border.

Which place in Illinois has the most ZIP codes?

Chicago, with 84 codes in this list.

Type the first letters of the town in the filter above the table on this page, or open its county from the list. Each code opens in the look-up with a map link.
