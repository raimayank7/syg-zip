Where Greene County is

The ZIP codes of Greene County are found in the west of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 62054 (Kane) and 62078 (Patterson), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Scott County (17 miles), Jersey County (20 miles), Calhoun County (21 miles) and Morgan County (25 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Greene County starts with 620. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 620 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Bond County, Calhoun County and Christian County, and has 78 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 587 square miles of land (1,521 sq km). The largest is 62016 (Carrollton) with 135 square miles, and the smallest is 62078 (Patterson) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 59.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Greene County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Greene County ranks 50th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Greene County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Carrollton, IL 62016
  • Eldred, IL 62027
  • Greenfield, IL 62044

Carrollton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
