Where Fayette County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Fayette County is in the south-east of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 62011 (Bingham) and 62838 (Farina), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Marion County (23 miles), Effingham County (24 miles), Bond County (24 miles) and Shelby County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Fayette County begin with 624 (3 codes), 628 (3 codes), 620 (2 codes) and 622 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 624 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Clark County, Clay County and Coles County, and has 61 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 620 square miles of land (1,606 sq km). The largest is 62471 (Vandalia) with 128 square miles, and the smallest is 62011 (Bingham) with 28.8. The middle-sized code here covers 76.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Fayette County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 62247 (Hagarstown). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Fayette County ranks 57th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Fayette County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:
