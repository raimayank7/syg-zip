Where Pulaski County is

The ZIP codes of Pulaski County are found in the south of Illinois. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 12 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 62956 (Karnak) and 62963 (Mound City), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Alexander County (13 miles), Ballard County, Kentucky (15 miles), Union County (19 miles) and Johnson County (22 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 629. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 629 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Alexander County, Franklin County and Gallatin County, and has 83 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 206 square miles of land (534 sq km). The largest is 62992 (Ullin) with 37.7 square miles, and the smallest is 62963 (Mound City) with 4.2. The middle-sized code here covers 26.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pulaski County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 62973 (Perks). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Pulaski County ranks 57th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pulaski County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:
