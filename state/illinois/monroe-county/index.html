Where Monroe County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Monroe County is in the south-west of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 62236 (Columbia) and 62279 (Renault), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are St. Clair County (19 miles), Jefferson County, Missouri (19 miles), Randolph County (25 miles) and St. Louis (city), Missouri (25 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Monroe County starts with 622. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 622 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Bond County, Clinton County and Fayette County, and has 79 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 323 square miles of land (837 sq km). The largest is 62298 (Waterloo) with 143 square miles, and the smallest is 62256 (Maeystown) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 47.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. Water adds about 12.4 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Monroe County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Monroe County ranks 73rd among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Monroe County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Columbia, IL 62236
  • Fults, IL 62244
  • Hecker, IL 62248

Columbia is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 16 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
