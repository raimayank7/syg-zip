Where Carroll County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Carroll County is in the north of Illinois. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 13 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 61078 (Shannon) and 61285 (Thomson), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Whiteside County (22 miles), Stephenson County (25 miles), Jo Daviess County (28 miles) and Clinton County, Iowa (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Carroll County begin with 610 (6 codes) and 612 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 610 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Boone County, Jo Daviess County and Lee County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 479 square miles of land (1,241 sq km). The largest is 61053 (Mount Carroll) with 108 square miles, and the smallest is 61285 (Thomson) with 44.4. The middle-sized code here covers 63.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 21.6 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Carroll County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Carroll County ranks 73rd among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Carroll County
