Where McHenry County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of McHenry County fall in the north-east of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 60021 (Fox River Grove) and 60033 (Harvard), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Lake County (22 miles), Walworth County, Wisconsin (23 miles), Boone County (23 miles) and Kenosha County, Wisconsin (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of McHenry County begin with 600 (15 codes) and 601 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 600 is shared with 2 other counties, Cook County and Lake County, and has 82 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 18 of the 20 codes. Together they come to about 623 square miles of land (1,615 sq km). The largest is 60033 (Harvard) with 117 square miles, and the smallest is 60021 (Fox River Grove) with 2.0. The middle-sized code here covers 22.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of McHenry County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 60001 (Alden) and 60039 (Crystal Lake). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, McHenry County ranks 18th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 20 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in McHenry County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Crystal Lake, IL 60012 (one of 3 codes for Crystal Lake; the street decides which)
  • Mchenry, IL 60050 (one of 2 codes for Mchenry; the street decides which)
  • Alden, IL 60001

Questions about McHenry County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Crystal Lake, IL?

Crystal Lake has 3 ZIP codes in McHenry County: 60012, 60014, 60039. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in McHenry County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 600, on 15 of the 20 codes.

Which ZIP code in McHenry County covers the most land?

60033 (Harvard), at about 117 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
