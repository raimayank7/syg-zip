Where Richland County is

The ZIP codes of Richland County are found in the south-east of Illinois. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 13 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 62425 (Dundas) and 62452 (Parkersburg), about 17 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lawrence County (18 miles), Clay County (20 miles), Edwards County (20 miles) and Jasper County (21 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Richland County begin with 624 (5 codes) and 628 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 624 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Clark County, Clay County and Coles County, and has 61 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 397 square miles of land (1,029 sq km). The largest is 62450 (Olney) with 157 square miles, and the smallest is 62452 (Parkersburg) with 20.9. The middle-sized code here covers 47.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Richland County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Richland County ranks 83rd among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Richland County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Calhoun, IL 62419
  • Claremont, IL 62421
  • Dundas, IL 62425

Calhoun is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
