Where Crawford County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Crawford County fall in the south-east of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 62427 (Flat Rock) and 62478 (West York), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Sullivan County, Indiana (18 miles), Jasper County (20 miles), Clark County (23 miles) and Lawrence County (24 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Crawford County starts with 624. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 624 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Clark County, Clay County and Coles County, and has 61 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 486 square miles of land (1,259 sq km). The largest is 62454 (Robinson) with 124 square miles, and the smallest is 62464 (Stoy) with 1.3. The middle-sized code here covers 48.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Crawford County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Crawford County ranks 65th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Crawford County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Annapolis, IL 62413
  • Flat Rock, IL 62427
  • Hutsonville, IL 62433

Annapolis is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
