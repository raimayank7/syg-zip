Where Marshall County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Marshall County fall in the north of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 14 miles from north to south and 31 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 61377 (Wenona) and 61424 (Camp Grove), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Putnam County (13 miles), Woodford County (19 miles), Stark County (24 miles) and Peoria County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Marshall County begin with 615 (5 codes), 613 (3 codes) and 614 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 615 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Fulton County, Knox County and Mason County, and has 48 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 389 square miles of land (1,006 sq km). The largest is 61570 (Washburn) with 69.0 square miles, and the smallest is 61541 (La Rose) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 51.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. Water adds about 16.2 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Marshall County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Marshall County ranks 57th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Marshall County
