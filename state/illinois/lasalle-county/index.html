Where LaSalle County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of LaSalle County fall in the north-east of Illinois. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 45 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 60531 (Leland) and 61321 (Dana), about 46 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Putnam County (19 miles), Bureau County (28 miles), Grundy County (29 miles) and Marshall County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of LaSalle County begin with 613 (18 codes), 605 (5 codes) and 604 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 613 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Bureau County, Lee County and Livingston County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,235 square miles of land (3,198 sq km). The largest is 61364 (Streator) with 157 square miles, and the smallest is 61371 (Triumph) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 42.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of LaSalle County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, LaSalle County ranks 13th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 24 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.
