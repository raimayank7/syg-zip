Where McLean County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, McLean County is in the north-east of Illinois. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 38 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 61731 (Cropsey) and 61754 (Mc Lean), about 40 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are De Witt County (23 miles), Woodford County (25 miles), Livingston County (33 miles) and Tazewell County (34 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of McLean County starts with 617. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 617 is shared with 7 other counties, such as De Witt County, Ford County and Livingston County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 25 of the 32 codes. Together they come to about 1,276 square miles of land (3,305 sq km). The largest is 61726 (Chenoa) with 94.7 square miles, and the smallest is 61701 (Bloomington) with 11.0. The middle-sized code here covers 43.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of McLean County; a code can reach across its border. 7 codes have no mapped area: 61702 (Bloomington), 61709 (Bloomington), 61710 (Bloomington), 61758 (Merna), 61790 (Normal) and 2 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, McLean County ranks 8th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 32 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in McLean County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Bloomington, IL 61701 (one of 8 codes for Bloomington; the street decides which)
  • Normal, IL 61761 (one of 2 codes for Normal; the street decides which)
  • Anchor, IL 61720

Bloomington is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
