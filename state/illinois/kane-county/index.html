Where Kane County is

The ZIP codes of Kane County are found in the north-east of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 25 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 60110 (Carpentersville) and 60511 (Big Rock), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are DuPage County (16 miles), DeKalb County (20 miles), Kendall County (20 miles) and McHenry County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Kane County begin with 601 (19 codes) and 605 (10 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 601 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Cook County, DeKalb County and DuPage County, and has 89 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 24 of the 29 codes. Together they come to about 539 square miles of land (1,396 sq km). The largest is 60151 (Maple Park) with 82.5 square miles, and the smallest is 60144 (Kaneville) with 0.05. The middle-sized code here covers 14.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Kane County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 60121 (Elgin), 60122 (Elgin), 60147 (Lafox), 60507 (Aurora) and 60568 (Aurora). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Kane County ranks 10th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 29 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Kane County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Aurora, IL 60502 (one of 5 codes for Aurora; the street decides which)
  • Elgin, IL 60120 (one of 5 codes for Elgin; the street decides which)
  • Saint Charles, IL 60174 (one of 2 codes for Saint Charles; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named Aurora are also listed under DuPage County and Will County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Aurora is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 15 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Kane County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Aurora, IL?
