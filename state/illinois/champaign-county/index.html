Where Champaign County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Champaign County fall in the east of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 33 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 61816 (Broadlands) and 61845 (Foosland), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Piatt County (24 miles), Douglas County (24 miles), Vermilion County (24 miles) and Ford County (36 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Champaign County begin with 618 (32 codes) and 609 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 618 is shared with 3 other counties, De Witt County, Piatt County and Vermilion County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 29 of the 33 codes. Together they come to about 1,013 square miles of land (2,624 sq km). The largest is 61822 (Champaign) with 107 square miles, and the smallest is 61815 (Bondville) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 30.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Champaign County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 61803 (Urbana), 61824 (Champaign), 61825 (Champaign) and 61826 (Champaign). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Champaign County ranks 5th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 33 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Champaign County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Champaign, IL 61820 (one of 6 codes for Champaign; the street decides which)
  • Urbana, IL 61801 (one of 3 codes for Urbana; the street decides which)
  • Bondville, IL 61815

Questions about Champaign County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Champaign, IL?

Champaign has 6 ZIP codes in Champaign County: 61820, 61821, 61822, 61824, 61825, 61826. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Champaign County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 618, on 32 of the 33 codes.

Which ZIP code in Champaign County covers the most land?

61822 (Champaign), at about 107 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
