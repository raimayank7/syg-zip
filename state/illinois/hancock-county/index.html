Where Hancock County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Hancock County fall in the west of Illinois. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 25 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 62311 (Augusta) and 62358 (Niota), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Lee County, Iowa (22 miles), Adams County (27 miles), Clark County, Missouri (28 miles) and McDonough County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Hancock County begin with 623 (15 codes) and 614 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 623 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Adams County, Brown County and McDonough County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 15 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 902 square miles of land (2,335 sq km). The largest is 62321 (Carthage) with 168 square miles, and the smallest is 62336 (Ferris) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 46.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hancock County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 62329 (Colusa). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Hancock County ranks 26th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hancock County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Augusta, IL 62311
  • Basco, IL 62313
  • Bowen, IL 62316

Augusta is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 12 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
