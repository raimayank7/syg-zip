Where Christian County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Christian County sits near the middle of Illinois. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 62083 (Rosamond) and 62547 (Mount Auburn), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Sangamon County (23 miles), Montgomery County (24 miles), Macon County (27 miles) and Shelby County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Christian County begin with 625 (12 codes) and 620 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 625 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Logan County, Macon County and Montgomery County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 660 square miles of land (1,709 sq km). The largest is 62568 (Taylorville) with 125 square miles, and the smallest is 62517 (Bulpitt) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 39.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Christian County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Christian County ranks 39th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.
