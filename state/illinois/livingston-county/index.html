Where Livingston County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Livingston County fall in the north-east of Illinois. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 30 miles, and from east to west about 32 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 61311 (Ancona) and 61775 (Strawn), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Ford County (25 miles), Grundy County (26 miles), McLean County (33 miles) and LaSalle County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Livingston County begin with 617 (7 codes), 609 (4 codes), 613 (4 codes) and 604 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 617 is shared with 7 other counties, such as De Witt County, Ford County and Logan County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 930 square miles of land (2,409 sq km). The largest is 61764 (Pontiac) with 155 square miles, and the smallest is 60920 (Campus) with 1.8. The middle-sized code here covers 40.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Livingston County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Livingston County ranks 23rd among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Livingston County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:
