Where Douglas County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Douglas County fall in the east of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 61911 (Arthur) and 61942 (Newman), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Coles County (19 miles), Edgar County (24 miles), Champaign County (24 miles) and Piatt County (25 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Douglas County starts with 619. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 619 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Coles County, Edgar County and Moultrie County, and has 30 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 494 square miles of land (1,280 sq km). The largest is 61953 (Tuscola) with 125 square miles, and the smallest is 61941 (Murdock) with 1.8. The middle-sized code here covers 41.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Douglas County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Douglas County ranks 57th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Douglas County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Arcola, IL 61910
  • Arthur, IL 61911
  • Atwood, IL 61913

Arcola is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
