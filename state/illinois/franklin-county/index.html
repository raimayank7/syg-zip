Where Franklin County is

The ZIP codes of Franklin County are found in the south-east of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 62884 (Sesser) and 62890 (Thompsonville), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Williamson County (13 miles), Jefferson County (21 miles), Hamilton County (23 miles) and Perry County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Franklin County begin with 628 (14 codes) and 629 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 628 is shared with 14 other counties, such as Clay County, Edwards County and Fayette County, and has 90 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 15 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 433 square miles of land (1,122 sq km). The largest is 62890 (Thompsonville) with 103 square miles, and the smallest is 62891 (Valier) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 14.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 22.6 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Franklin County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 62840 (Frankfort Heights). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Franklin County ranks 26th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Franklin County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Benton, IL 62812
  • Buckner, IL 62819
  • Christopher, IL 62822

Benton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 11 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
