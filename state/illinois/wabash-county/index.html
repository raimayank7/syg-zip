Where Wabash County is

The ZIP codes of Wabash County are found in the south-east of Illinois. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 10 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 62410 (Allendale) and 62852 (Keensburg), about 14 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Edwards County (12 miles), Gibson County, Indiana (19 miles), Lawrence County (19 miles) and Posey County, Indiana (20 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Wabash County begin with 628 (4 codes) and 624 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 628 is shared with 14 other counties, such as Clay County, Edwards County and Fayette County, and has 90 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 4 of the 5 codes. Together they come to about 187 square miles of land (484 sq km). The largest is 62863 (Mount Carmel) with 147 square miles, and the smallest is 62852 (Keensburg) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 19.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wabash County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 62855 (Lancaster). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Wabash County ranks 90th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Wabash County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Allendale, IL 62410
  • Bellmont, IL 62811
  • Keensburg, IL 62852

Allendale is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
