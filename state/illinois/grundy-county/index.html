Where Grundy County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Grundy County fall in the north-east of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 60437 (Kinsman) and 60447 (Minooka), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Livingston County (26 miles), Will County (26 miles), Kendall County (27 miles) and Kankakee County (27 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Grundy County starts with 604. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 604 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Cook County, DuPage County and LaSalle County, and has 83 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 428 square miles of land (1,107 sq km). The largest is 60450 (Morris) with 158 square miles, and the smallest is 60474 (South Wilmington) with 5.4. The middle-sized code here covers 39.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Grundy County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Grundy County ranks 57th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.
