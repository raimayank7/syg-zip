Where Perry County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Perry County is in the south of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 11 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 62888 (Tamaroa) and 62997 (Willisville), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Jackson County (19 miles), Washington County (21 miles), Randolph County (22 miles) and Franklin County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Perry County begin with 622 (2 codes), 628 (2 codes) and 629 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 622 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Bond County, Clinton County and Fayette County, and has 79 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 364 square miles of land (942 sq km). The largest is 62274 (Pinckneyville) with 143 square miles, and the smallest is 62997 (Willisville) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 72.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Perry County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Perry County ranks 90th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Perry County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Cutler, IL 62238
  • Du Quoin, IL 62832
  • Pinckneyville, IL 62274

Cutler is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
