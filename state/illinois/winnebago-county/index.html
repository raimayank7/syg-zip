Where Winnebago County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Winnebago County fall in the north of Illinois. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 18 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 61077 (Seward) and 61080 (South Beloit), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Boone County (15 miles), Ogle County (20 miles), Rock County, Wisconsin (24 miles) and Stephenson County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Winnebago County begin with 611 (19 codes) and 610 (9 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 611, so those three digits alone point to Winnebago County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 20 of the 28 codes. Together they come to about 523 square miles of land (1,355 sq km). The largest is 61063 (Pecatonica) with 74.5 square miles, and the smallest is 61112 (Rockford) with 0.8. The middle-sized code here covers 20.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Winnebago County; a code can reach across its border. 8 codes have no mapped area: 61105 (Rockford), 61106 (Rockford), 61110 (Rockford), 61125 (Rockford), 61126 (Rockford) and 3 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Winnebago County ranks 11th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 28 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Winnebago County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Rockford, IL 61101 (one of 14 codes for Rockford; the street decides which)
  • Loves Park, IL 61111 (one of 4 codes for Loves Park; the street decides which)
  • Cherry Valley, IL 61016

Rockford is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
