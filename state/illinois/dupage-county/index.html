Where DuPage County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of DuPage County fall in the north-east of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 60399 (Wood Dale) and 60572 (Aurora), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Kane County (16 miles), Cook County (16 miles), Will County (23 miles) and Kendall County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of DuPage County begin with 601 (28 codes), 605 (23 codes), 603 (1 code) and 604 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 601 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Cook County, DeKalb County and Kane County, and has 89 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 36 of the 53 codes. Together they come to about 342 square miles of land (887 sq km). The largest is 60185 (West Chicago) with 30.9 square miles, and the smallest is 60519 (Eola) with 0.05. The middle-sized code here covers 8.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of DuPage County; a code can reach across its border. 17 codes have no mapped area: 60105 (Bensenville), 60116 (Carol Stream), 60117 (Bloomingdale), 60128 (Carol Stream), 60132 (Carol Stream) and 12 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, DuPage County ranks 3rd among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 53 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in DuPage County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Carol Stream, IL 60116 (one of 6 codes for Carol Stream; the street decides which)
  • Naperville, IL 60540 (one of 5 codes for Naperville; the street decides which)
  • Aurora, IL 60504 (one of 4 codes for Aurora; the street decides which)

Aurora and Naperville share a name with a place in another part of Illinois; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about DuPage County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Carol Stream, IL?

Carol Stream has 6 ZIP codes in DuPage County: 60116, 60128, 60132, 60188, 60197, 60199. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in DuPage County start with the same digits?

No. They use 4 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 601, on 28 of the 53 codes.

Which ZIP code in DuPage County covers the most land?

60185 (West Chicago), at about 30.9 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
