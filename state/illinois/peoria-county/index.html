Where Peoria County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Peoria County is in the north of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 61526 (Edelstein) and 61539 (Kingston Mines), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Tazewell County (16 miles), Stark County (23 miles), Woodford County (24 miles) and Marshall County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Peoria County begin with 616 (30 codes), 615 (13 codes) and 614 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 616 is shared with 1 other county, Tazewell County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 24 of the 44 codes. Together they come to about 535 square miles of land (1,387 sq km). The largest is 61517 (Brimfield) with 68.5 square miles, and the smallest is 61562 (Rome) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 20.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Peoria County; a code can reach across its border. 20 codes have no mapped area: 61601 (Peoria), 61612 (Peoria), 61613 (Peoria), 61629 (Peoria), 61630 (Peoria) and 15 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Peoria County ranks 4th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 44 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.
