Where Alexander County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Alexander County fall in the south of Illinois. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 15 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 62914 (Cairo) and 62957 (Mc Clure), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Pulaski County (13 miles), Scott County, Missouri (15 miles), Union County (21 miles) and Mississippi County, Missouri (23 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 629. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 629 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Franklin County, Gallatin County and Hardin County, and has 83 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 236 square miles of land (611 sq km). The largest is 62988 (Tamms) with 70.3 square miles, and the smallest is 62969 (Olive Branch) with 14.3. The middle-sized code here covers 42.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. Water adds about 19.1 square miles, roughly 7% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Alexander County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 62993 (Unity). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Alexander County ranks 73rd among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Alexander County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Cairo, IL 62914
  • Mc Clure, IL 62957
  • Miller City, IL 62962

Cairo is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
