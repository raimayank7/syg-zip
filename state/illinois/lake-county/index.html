Where Lake County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Lake County fall in the north-east of Illinois. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 30 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 60011 (Barrington) and 60042 (Island Lake), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Kenosha County, Wisconsin (18 miles), McHenry County (22 miles), Racine County, Wisconsin (29 miles) and Cook County (32 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Lake County starts with 600. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 600 is shared with 2 other counties, Cook County and McHenry County, and has 82 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 29 of the 33 codes. Together they come to about 483 square miles of land (1,250 sq km). The largest is 60010 (Barrington) with 74.1 square miles, and the smallest is 60075 (Russell) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 13.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Illinois is 31.5. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 37.6 square miles, roughly 7% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lake County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 60011 (Barrington), 60037 (Fort Sheridan), 60079 (Waukegan) and 60086 (North Chicago). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lake County ranks 5th among the 102 counties and county-level areas of Illinois (equal counts share a rank), with 33 of the 1,575 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lake County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters IL and the ZIP code:

  • Waukegan, IL 60079 (one of 3 codes for Waukegan; the street decides which)
  • Barrington, IL 60010 (one of 2 codes for Barrington; the street decides which)
  • North Chicago, IL 60064 (one of 2 codes for North Chicago; the street decides which)
