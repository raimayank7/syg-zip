Where Sagadahoc County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Sagadahoc County fall in the south-west of Maine. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 11 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 04357 (Richmond) and 04562 (Phippsburg), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Lincoln County (15 miles), Cumberland County (25 miles), Androscoggin County (25 miles) and Kennebec County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Sagadahoc County begin with 045 (5 codes), 040 (2 codes), 042 (1 code) and 043 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 045 is shared with 2 other counties, Knox County and Lincoln County, and has 31 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 254 square miles of land (658 sq km). The largest is 04287 (Bowdoin) with 43.5 square miles, and the smallest is 04548 (Georgetown) with 18.6. The middle-sized code here covers 31.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Maine is 36.7. Water adds about 53.7 square miles, roughly 17% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sagadahoc County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 04565 (Sebasco Estates). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Sagadahoc County ranks 16th among the 16 counties and county-level areas of Maine (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 485 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Sagadahoc County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters ME and the ZIP code:

  • Bath, ME 04530
  • Bowdoin, ME 04287
  • Bowdoinham, ME 04008

Bath is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 10 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Sagadahoc County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Bath, ME?

04530 is the ZIP code listed for Bath in Sagadahoc County.

Do all ZIP codes in Sagadahoc County start with the same digits?

No. They use 4 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 045, on 5 of the 9 codes.

Which ZIP code in Sagadahoc County covers the most land?

04287 (Bowdoin), at about 43.5 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
