Where Lincoln County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Lincoln County is in the south-west of Maine. Its codes spread over about 34 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 04341 (Coopers Mills) and 04852 (Monhegan), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Sagadahoc County (15 miles), Knox County (23 miles), Kennebec County (31 miles) and Androscoggin County (36 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lincoln County begin with 045 (23 codes), 043 (4 codes) and 048 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 045 is shared with 2 other counties, Knox County and Sagadahoc County, and has 31 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 27 of the 28 codes. Together they come to about 454 square miles of land (1,177 sq km). The largest is 04348 (Jefferson) with 75.3 square miles, and the smallest is 04341 (Coopers Mills) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 7.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Maine is 36.7. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 86.6 square miles, roughly 16% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lincoln County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 04549 (Isle Of Springs). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lincoln County ranks 9th among the 16 counties and county-level areas of Maine (equal counts share a rank), with 28 of the 485 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lincoln County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters ME and the ZIP code:
