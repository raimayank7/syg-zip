Maine ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 485 ZIP codes for Maine, about 1.2% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 34th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 16 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Cumberland County (52), Penobscot County (47), Aroostook County (44), Hancock County (43) and York County (42). The fewest are in Sagadahoc County, with 9.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Maine uses 11 of these prefixes, from 039 to 049. The busiest are 040 (77 codes, most of them for Freeport), 049 (70 codes, most of them for New Portland), 044 (68 codes, most of them for Bangor) and 046 (63 codes, most of them for Addison). The first digit 0 is shared with Connecticut, Massachusetts, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, Rhode Island and Vermont.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 426 of the 485 codes. The largest is 04441 (Greenville, Piscataquis County) at 981 square miles and the smallest is 04234 (East Wilton) at 0.01; the middle value is 36.7 square miles. The other 59 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 04741 (Estcourt Station) and the southernmost 03904 (Kittery); the one farthest east is 04631 (Eastport) and farthest west 04261 (Newry).

Place names and addresses in Maine

Of the 457 place names in the list, 442 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Portland (9), Augusta (5), Auburn (3) and Freeport (3). In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters ME and the code, as in Portland, ME 04101. The name of the county is left out.
