Where Waldo County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Waldo County fall in the south of Maine. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 31 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 04848 (Islesboro) and 04922 (Burnham), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Knox County (28 miles), Kennebec County (34 miles), Hancock County (35 miles) and Lincoln County (41 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Waldo County begin with 049 (14 codes), 048 (3 codes), 044 (2 codes) and 043 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 049 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Franklin County, Kennebec County and Penobscot County, and has 70 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 18 of the 20 codes. Together they come to about 740 square miles of land (1,918 sq km). The largest is 04915 (Belfast) with 73.1 square miles, and the smallest is 04848 (Islesboro) with 14.3. The middle-sized code here covers 38.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Maine is 36.7. Water adds about 123 square miles, roughly 14% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Waldo County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 04850 (Lincolnville Center) and 04972 (Sandy Point). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Waldo County ranks 12th among the 16 counties and county-level areas of Maine (equal counts share a rank), with 20 of the 485 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Waldo County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters ME and the ZIP code:
