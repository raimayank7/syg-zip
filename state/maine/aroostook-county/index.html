Where Aroostook County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Aroostook County fall in the north-east of Maine. Its codes spread over about 124 miles from north to south and 80 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 04497 (Wytopitlock) and 04741 (Estcourt Station), about 135 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Piscataquis County (111 miles), Penobscot County (115 miles), Washington County (126 miles) and Somerset County (141 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Aroostook County begin with 047 (42 codes) and 044 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 047 is shared with 1 other county, Penobscot County, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 39 of the 44 codes. Together they come to about 4,020 square miles of land (10,413 sq km). The largest is 04747 (Island Falls) with 310 square miles, and the smallest is 04737 (Clayton Lake) with 2.2. The middle-sized code here covers 76.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Maine is 36.7. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Aroostook County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 04738 (Crouseville), 04741 (Estcourt Station), 04744 (Fort Kent Mills), 04751 (Limestone) and 04775 (Sheridan). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Aroostook County ranks 3rd among the 16 counties and county-level areas of Maine (equal counts share a rank), with 44 of the 485 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Aroostook County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters ME and the ZIP code:

  • Limestone, ME 04750 (one of 2 codes for Limestone; the street decides which)
  • Ashland, ME 04732
  • Benedicta, ME 04733

Limestone is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
