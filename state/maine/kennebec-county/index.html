Where Kennebec County is

The ZIP codes of Kennebec County are found in the south-west of Maine. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 48 miles, and from east to west about 30 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 04350 (Litchfield) and 04926 (China Village), about 53 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Androscoggin County (27 miles), Lincoln County (31 miles), Sagadahoc County (32 miles) and Waldo County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Kennebec County begin with 043 (20 codes), 049 (11 codes) and 042 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 043 is shared with 3 other counties, Lincoln County, Sagadahoc County and Waldo County, and has 26 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 26 of the 34 codes. Together they come to about 857 square miles of land (2,220 sq km). The largest is 04330 (Augusta) with 117 square miles, and the smallest is 04926 (China Village) with 0.05. The middle-sized code here covers 30.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Maine is 36.7. Water adds about 83.7 square miles, roughly 9% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Kennebec County; a code can reach across its border. 8 codes have no mapped area: 04332 (Augusta), 04333 (Augusta), 04336 (Augusta), 04338 (Augusta), 04343 (East Winthrop) and 3 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Kennebec County ranks 6th among the 16 counties and county-level areas of Maine (equal counts share a rank), with 34 of the 485 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Kennebec County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters ME and the ZIP code:

  • Augusta, ME 04330 (one of 5 codes for Augusta; the street decides which)
  • Waterville, ME 04901 (one of 2 codes for Waterville; the street decides which)
  • Albion, ME 04910

Augusta is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 12 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Kennebec County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Augusta, ME?

Augusta has 5 ZIP codes in Kennebec County: 04330, 04332, 04333, 04336, 04338. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Kennebec County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 043, on 20 of the 34 codes.

Which ZIP code in Kennebec County covers the most land?

04330 (Augusta), at about 117 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
