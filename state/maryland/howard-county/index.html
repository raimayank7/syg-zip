Where Howard County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Howard County fall in the north of Maryland. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 41 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 20588 (Dhs) and 21765 (Lisbon), about 42 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Montgomery County (14 miles), Baltimore (city) (17 miles), Prince George's County (21 miles) and Anne Arundel County (21 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Howard County begin with 210 (9 codes), 207 (6 codes), 217 (5 codes), 205 (1 code) and 211 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 210 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Anne Arundel County, Baltimore County and Carroll County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 18 of the 22 codes. Together they come to about 184 square miles of land (477 sq km). The largest is 21042 (Ellicott City) with 37.0 square miles, and the smallest is 20763 (Savage) with 1.1. The middle-sized code here covers 8.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Maryland is 11.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Howard County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 20588 (Dhs), 21041 (Ellicott City), 21150 (Simpsonville) and 21765 (Lisbon). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Howard County ranks 10th among the 24 counties and county-level areas of Maryland (equal counts share a rank), with 22 of the 605 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Howard County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MD and the ZIP code:

  • Columbia, MD 21044 (one of 3 codes for Columbia; the street decides which)
  • Ellicott City, MD 21041 (one of 3 codes for Ellicott City; the street decides which)
  • Annapolis Junction, MD 20701

Columbia is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 16 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Laurel shares a name with a place in another part of Maryland; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Howard County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Columbia, MD?

Columbia has 3 ZIP codes in Howard County: 21044, 21045, 21046. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Howard County start with the same digits?

No. They use 5 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 210, on 9 of the 22 codes.

Which ZIP code in Howard County covers the most land?

21042 (Ellicott City), at about 37.0 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
