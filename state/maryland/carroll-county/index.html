Where Carroll County is

The ZIP codes of Carroll County are found in the north of Maryland. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 21088 (Lineboro) and 21797 (Woodbine), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Frederick County (22 miles), Baltimore County (23 miles), Howard County (25 miles) and Adams County, Pennsylvania (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Carroll County begin with 217 (6 codes), 211 (4 codes) and 210 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 217 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Allegany County, Frederick County and Howard County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 551 square miles of land (1,428 sq km). The largest is 21157 (Westminster) with 77.7 square miles, and the smallest is 21104 (Marriottsville) with 17.1. The middle-sized code here covers 42.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Maryland is 11.4. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Carroll County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 21088 (Lineboro). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Carroll County ranks 18th among the 24 counties and county-level areas of Maryland (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 605 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Carroll County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MD and the ZIP code:

  • Westminster, MD 21157 (one of 2 codes for Westminster; the street decides which)
  • Finksburg, MD 21048
  • Hampstead, MD 21074

Westminster is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Carroll County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Westminster, MD?

Westminster has 2 ZIP codes in Carroll County: 21157, 21158. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Carroll County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 217, on 6 of the 13 codes.

Which ZIP code in Carroll County covers the most land?

21157 (Westminster), at about 77.7 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
