Where Talbot County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Talbot County is in the east of Maryland. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 18 miles, and from east to west about 18 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 21625 (Cordova) and 21671 (Tilghman), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Queen Anne's County (21 miles), Caroline County (23 miles), Dorchester County (23 miles) and Calvert County (24 miles).

The first three digits

All 15 codes here begin with the same three digits, 216. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 216 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Caroline County, Dorchester County and Kent County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 263 square miles of land (681 sq km). The largest is 21601 (Easton) with 107 square miles, and the smallest is 21653 (Newcomb) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 5.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Maryland is 11.4. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 81.4 square miles, roughly 24% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Talbot County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Talbot County ranks 16th among the 24 counties and county-level areas of Maryland (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 605 codes in the state list.
