Where Dorchester County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Dorchester County fall in the south-east of Maryland. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 28 miles, and from east to west about 28 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 21634 (Fishing Creek) and 21659 (Rhodesdale), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Talbot County (23 miles), Wicomico County (23 miles), Calvert County (27 miles) and Somerset County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Dorchester County begin with 216 (14 codes) and 218 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 216 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Caroline County, Kent County and Queen Anne's County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 512 square miles of land (1,325 sq km). The largest is 21613 (Cambridge) with 154 square miles, and the smallest is 21664 (Secretary) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 13.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Maryland is 11.4. Water adds about 103 square miles, roughly 17% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Dorchester County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Dorchester County ranks 15th among the 24 counties and county-level areas of Maryland (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 605 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Dorchester County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MD and the ZIP code:

  • Cambridge, MD 21613
  • Church Creek, MD 21622
  • Crapo, MD 21626

Cambridge is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 12 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
