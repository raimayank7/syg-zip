Where Somerset County is

The ZIP codes of Somerset County are found in the south-east of Maryland. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 21853 (Princess Anne) and 21866 (Tylerton), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Accomack County, Virginia (22 miles), Wicomico County (23 miles), Dorchester County (30 miles) and Worcester County (31 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Somerset County starts with 218. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 218 is shared with 3 other counties, Dorchester County, Wicomico County and Worcester County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 269 square miles of land (697 sq km). The largest is 21853 (Princess Anne) with 115 square miles, and the smallest is 21866 (Tylerton) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 14.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Maryland is 11.4. Water adds about 34.5 square miles, roughly 11% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Somerset County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 21836 (Manokin) and 21857 (Rehobeth). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Somerset County ranks 21st among the 24 counties and county-level areas of Maryland (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 605 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Somerset County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MD and the ZIP code:

  • Westover, MD 21871 (one of 2 codes for Westover; the street decides which)
  • Crisfield, MD 21817
  • Deal Island, MD 21821

Questions about Somerset County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Westover, MD?

Westover has 2 ZIP codes in Somerset County: 21871, 21890. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Somerset County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 11 start with 218.

Which ZIP code in Somerset County covers the most land?

21853 (Princess Anne), at about 115 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
