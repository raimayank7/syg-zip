Where Charles County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Charles County fall in the south of Maryland. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 26 miles, and from east to west about 28 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 20616 (Bryans Road) and 20625 (Cobb Island), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are King George County, Virginia (18 miles), Calvert County (23 miles), City of Alexandria, Virginia (23 miles) and St. Mary's County (27 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Charles County starts with 206. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 206 is shared with 3 other counties, Calvert County, Prince George's County and St. Mary's County, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 21 of the 24 codes. Together they come to about 424 square miles of land (1,099 sq km). The largest is 20646 (La Plata) with 79.8 square miles, and the smallest is 20661 (Mount Victoria) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 14.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Maryland is 11.4. Water adds about 44.1 square miles, roughly 9% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Charles County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 20604 (Waldorf), 20643 (Ironsides) and 20682 (Rock Point). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Charles County ranks 8th among the 24 counties and county-level areas of Maryland (equal counts share a rank), with 24 of the 605 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Charles County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MD and the ZIP code:

  • Waldorf, MD 20601 (one of 4 codes for Waldorf; the street decides which)
  • Bel Alton, MD 20611
  • Benedict, MD 20612
