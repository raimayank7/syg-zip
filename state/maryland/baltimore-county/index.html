Where Baltimore County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Baltimore County fall in the north-east of Maryland. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 35 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 21052 (Fort Howard) and 21053 (Freeland), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Baltimore (city) (8.4 miles), Harford County (18 miles), Howard County (22 miles) and Carroll County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Baltimore County begin with 212 (20 codes), 210 (18 codes) and 211 (15 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 212 is shared with 2 other counties and county-level areas, Anne Arundel County and Baltimore (city), and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 44 of the 53 codes. Together they come to about 566 square miles of land (1,466 sq km). The largest is 21136 (Reisterstown) with 56.2 square miles, and the smallest is 21105 (Maryland Line) with 0.09. The middle-sized code here covers 11.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Maryland is 11.4. Water adds about 28.3 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Baltimore County; a code can reach across its border. 9 codes have no mapped area: 21020 (Boring), 21022 (Brooklandville), 21027 (Chase), 21065 (Hunt Valley), 21092 (Long Green) and 4 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Baltimore County ranks 3rd among the 24 counties and county-level areas of Maryland (equal counts share a rank), with 53 of the 605 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Baltimore County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MD and the ZIP code:

  • Towson, MD 21204 (one of 5 codes for Towson; the street decides which)
  • Baltimore, MD 21250 (one of 2 codes for Baltimore; the street decides which)
  • Hunt Valley, MD 21031 (one of 2 codes for Hunt Valley; the street decides which)

Baltimore shares a name with a place in another part of Maryland; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Baltimore County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Towson, MD?

Towson has 5 ZIP codes in Baltimore County: 21204, 21252, 21284, 21285, 21286. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Baltimore County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 212, on 20 of the 53 codes.

Which ZIP code in Baltimore County covers the most land?

21136 (Reisterstown), at about 56.2 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
