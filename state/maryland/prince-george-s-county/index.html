Where Prince George's County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Prince George's County fall close to the middle of Maryland. Its codes spread over about 36 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 20608 (Aquasco) and 20707 (Laurel), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are District of Columbia, District of Columbia (7.4 miles), Arlington County, Virginia (12 miles), City of Alexandria, Virginia (12 miles) and Falls Church (city), Virginia (16 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Prince George's County begin with 207 (58 codes), 206 (5 codes) and 209 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 207 is shared with 3 other counties, Anne Arundel County, Calvert County and Howard County, and has 79 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 36 of the 64 codes. Together they come to about 482 square miles of land (1,247 sq km). The largest is 20772 (Upper Marlboro) with 72.5 square miles, and the smallest is 20742 (College Park) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 9.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Maryland is 11.4. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Prince George's County; a code can reach across its border. 28 codes have no mapped area: 20697 (Southern Md Facility), 20703 (Lanham), 20704 (Beltsville), 20709 (Laurel), 20717 (Bowie) and 23 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Prince George's County ranks 2nd among the 24 counties and county-level areas of Maryland (equal counts share a rank), with 64 of the 605 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Prince George's County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MD and the ZIP code:

  • Bowie, MD 20715 (one of 7 codes for Bowie; the street decides which)
  • Hyattsville, MD 20781 (one of 7 codes for Hyattsville; the street decides which)
  • Capitol Heights, MD 20731 (one of 5 codes for Capitol Heights; the street decides which)

Laurel and Takoma Park share a name with a place in another part of Maryland; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
