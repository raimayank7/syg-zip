Where Caroline County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Caroline County is in the east of Maryland. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 11 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 21609 (Bethlehem) and 21670 (Templeville), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Queen Anne's County (14 miles), Kent County, Delaware (20 miles), Talbot County (23 miles) and Kent County (26 miles).

The first three digits

All 11 codes here begin with the same three digits, 216. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 216 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Dorchester County, Kent County and Queen Anne's County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 350 square miles of land (907 sq km). The largest is 21629 (Denton) with 83.6 square miles, and the smallest is 21641 (Hillsboro) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 32.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Maryland is 11.4. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Caroline County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 21609 (Bethlehem) and 21670 (Templeville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Caroline County ranks 21st among the 24 counties and county-level areas of Maryland (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 605 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Caroline County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MD and the ZIP code:

  • Bethlehem, MD 21609
  • Denton, MD 21629
  • Federalsburg, MD 21632

Bethlehem is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
