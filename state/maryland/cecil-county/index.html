Where Cecil County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Cecil County fall in the north-east of Maryland. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 21918 (Conowingo) and 21930 (Georgetown), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are New Castle County, Delaware (19 miles), Harford County (20 miles), Kent County (20 miles) and Salem County, New Jersey (31 miles).

The first three digits

All 17 codes here begin with the same three digits, 219. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 219, so those three digits alone point to Cecil County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 15 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 346 square miles of land (897 sq km). The largest is 21921 (Elkton) with 94.7 square miles, and the smallest is 21914 (Charlestown) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 17.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Maryland is 11.4. Water adds about 30.3 square miles, roughly 8% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cecil County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 21916 (Childs) and 21922 (Elkton). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Cecil County ranks 14th among the 24 counties and county-level areas of Maryland (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 605 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cecil County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MD and the ZIP code:

  • Elkton, MD 21921 (one of 2 codes for Elkton; the street decides which)
  • Cecilton, MD 21913
  • Charlestown, MD 21914

Elkton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
