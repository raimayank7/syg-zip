Where St. Mary's County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of St. Mary's County fall in the south-east of Maryland. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 20622 (Charlotte Hall) and 20687 (Scotland), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Westmoreland County, Virginia (13 miles), Calvert County (19 miles), Richmond County, Virginia (24 miles) and Charles County (27 miles).

The first three digits

All 29 codes here begin with the same three digits, 206. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 206 is shared with 3 other counties, Calvert County, Charles County and Prince George's County, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 27 of the 29 codes. Together they come to about 393 square miles of land (1,018 sq km). The largest is 20659 (Mechanicsville) with 97.0 square miles, and the smallest is 20686 (Saint Marys City) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 7.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Maryland is 11.4. Water adds about 86.9 square miles, roughly 18% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of St. Mary's County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 20627 (Compton) and 20635 (Helen). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, St. Mary's County ranks 7th among the 24 counties and county-level areas of Maryland (equal counts share a rank), with 29 of the 605 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in St. Mary's County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MD and the ZIP code:

  • Abell, MD 20606
  • Avenue, MD 20609
  • Bushwood, MD 20618
