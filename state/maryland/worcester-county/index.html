Where Worcester County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Worcester County fall in the south-east of Maryland. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 31 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 21813 (Bishopville) and 21851 (Pocomoke City), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Wicomico County (19 miles), Sussex County, Delaware (27 miles), Somerset County (31 miles) and Accomack County, Virginia (38 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 218. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 218 is shared with 3 other counties, Dorchester County, Somerset County and Wicomico County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 488 square miles of land (1,263 sq km). The largest is 21863 (Snow Hill) with 123 square miles, and the smallest is 21862 (Showell) with 1.1. The middle-sized code here covers 27.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Maryland is 11.4. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 44.1 square miles, roughly 8% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Worcester County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 21843 (Ocean City). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Worcester County ranks 20th among the 24 counties and county-level areas of Maryland (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 605 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Worcester County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MD and the ZIP code:

  • Ocean City, MD 21842 (one of 2 codes for Ocean City; the street decides which)
  • Berlin, MD 21811
  • Bishopville, MD 21813

Questions about Worcester County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Ocean City, MD?

Ocean City has 2 ZIP codes in Worcester County: 21842, 21843. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Worcester County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 12 start with 218.

Which ZIP code in Worcester County covers the most land?

21863 (Snow Hill), at about 123 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
