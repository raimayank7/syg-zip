Where Mono County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Mono County sits near the middle of California. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 71 miles, and from east to west about 52 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 93546 (Mammoth Lakes) and 96133 (Topaz), about 80 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Mineral County, Nevada (51 miles), Mariposa County (57 miles), Tuolumne County (61 miles) and Alpine County (63 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Mono County begin with 935 (5 codes) and 961 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 935 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Inyo County, Kern County and Los Angeles County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,175 square miles of land (3,042 sq km). The largest is 93517 (Bridgeport) with 337 square miles, and the smallest is 96133 (Topaz) with 13.1. The middle-sized code here covers 101 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 81.2 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Mono County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Mono County ranks 52nd among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Mono County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:

  • Benton, CA 93512
  • Bridgeport, CA 93517
  • Coleville, CA 96107

Benton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 11 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
