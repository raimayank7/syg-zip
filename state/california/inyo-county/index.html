Where Inyo County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Inyo County is in the east of California. Its codes spread over about 111 miles from north to south and 125 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 92389 (Tecopa) and 93514 (Bishop), about 167 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Nye County, Nevada (74 miles), Esmeralda County, Nevada (81 miles), Tulare County (85 miles) and Kern County (103 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Inyo County begin with 935 (9 codes) and 923 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 935 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Kern County, Los Angeles County and Mono County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 4,443 square miles of land (11,508 sq km). The largest is 92328 (Death Valley) with 1,774 square miles, and the smallest is 93530 (Keeler) with 1.2. The middle-sized code here covers 311 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Inyo County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 93515 (Bishop) and 93542 (Little Lake). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Inyo County ranks 43rd among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Inyo County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:

  • Bishop, CA 93514 (one of 2 codes for Bishop; the street decides which)
  • Big Pine, CA 93513
  • Darwin, CA 93522

Bishop is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Inyo County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Bishop, CA?

Bishop has 2 ZIP codes in Inyo County: 93514, 93515. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Inyo County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 935, on 9 of the 12 codes.

Which ZIP code in Inyo County covers the most land?

92328 (Death Valley), at about 1,774 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
