Where Solano County is

The ZIP codes of Solano County are found in the west of California. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 26 miles, and from east to west about 31 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 94592 (Vallejo) and 95620 (Dixon), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Contra Costa County (22 miles), Napa County (24 miles), Yolo County (32 miles) and Alameda County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Solano County begin with 945 (11 codes) and 956 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 945 is shared with 3 other counties, Alameda County, Contra Costa County and Napa County, and has 93 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 15 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 796 square miles of land (2,063 sq km). The largest is 95620 (Dixon) with 241 square miles, and the smallest is 95625 (Elmira) with 0.9. The middle-sized code here covers 22.5 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Water adds about 30.2 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Solano County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 95696 (Vacaville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Solano County ranks 35th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Solano County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:

  • Vallejo, CA 94589 (one of 4 codes for Vallejo; the street decides which)
  • Vacaville, CA 95687 (one of 3 codes for Vacaville; the street decides which)
  • Fairfield, CA 94533 (one of 2 codes for Fairfield; the street decides which)

Questions about Solano County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Vallejo, CA?

Vallejo has 4 ZIP codes in Solano County: 94589, 94590, 94591, 94592. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Solano County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 945, on 11 of the 16 codes.

Which ZIP code in Solano County covers the most land?

95620 (Dixon), at about 241 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
