Where Contra Costa County is

The ZIP codes of Contra Costa County are found in the west of California. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 41 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 94505 (Discovery Bay) and 94801 (Richmond), about 41 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Alameda County (13 miles), Solano County (22 miles), City and County of San Francisco (23 miles) and Marin County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Contra Costa County begin with 945 (42 codes) and 948 (10 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 945 is shared with 3 other counties, Alameda County, Napa County and Solano County, and has 93 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 42 of the 52 codes. Together they come to about 622 square miles of land (1,611 sq km). The largest is 94513 (Brentwood) with 78.9 square miles, and the smallest is 94575 (Moraga) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 9.8 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Water adds about 24.7 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Contra Costa County; a code can reach across its border. 10 codes have no mapped area: 94522 (Concord), 94524 (Concord), 94527 (Concord), 94529 (Concord), 94570 (Moraga) and 5 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Contra Costa County ranks 11th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 52 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Contra Costa County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:

  • Concord, CA 94518 (one of 8 codes for Concord; the street decides which)
  • Richmond, CA 94801 (one of 7 codes for Richmond; the street decides which)
  • Walnut Creek, CA 94595 (one of 4 codes for Walnut Creek; the street decides which)

Concord is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 12 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
