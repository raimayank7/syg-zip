Where San Luis Obispo County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, San Luis Obispo County is in the south-west of California. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 60 miles, and from east to west about 50 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 93444 (Nipomo) and 93451 (San Miguel), about 60 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Santa Barbara County (67 miles), Kings County (76 miles), Monterey County (95 miles) and San Benito County (104 miles).

The first three digits

All 35 codes here begin with the same three digits, 934. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 934 is shared with 2 other counties, Monterey County and Santa Barbara County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 24 of the 35 codes. Together they come to about 2,902 square miles of land (7,517 sq km). The largest is 93453 (Santa Margarita) with 691 square miles, and the smallest is 93408 (San Luis Obispo) with 0.02. The middle-sized code here covers 67.1 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of San Luis Obispo County; a code can reach across its border. 11 codes have no mapped area: 93403 (San Luis Obispo), 93406 (San Luis Obispo), 93412 (Los Osos), 93421 (Arroyo Grande), 93423 (Atascadero) and 6 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, San Luis Obispo County ranks 21st among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 35 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.
