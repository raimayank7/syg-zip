California ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 2,593 ZIP codes for California, about 6.3% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 2nd among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 58 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Los Angeles County (508), San Diego County (182), Orange County (146), Sacramento County (132) and San Bernardino County (116). At the other end, 1 has only one or two codes, for example Alpine County.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. California uses 60 of these prefixes, from 900 to 961. The busiest are 900 (95 codes, most of them for Los Angeles), 956 (94 codes, most of them for Auburn), 945 (93 codes, most of them for Concord) and 921 (80 codes, most of them for San Diego). The first digit 9 is shared with Alaska, Hawaii, Oregon and Washington.

The Census Bureau draws an area for 1,802 of the 2,593 codes. The largest is 92328 (Death Valley, Inyo County) at 1,774 square miles and the smallest is 91046 (Verdugo City) at 0.01; the middle value is 15.2 square miles. The other 791 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 95567 (Smith River) and the southernmost 92173 (San Ysidro); the one farthest east is 92267 (Parker Dam) and farthest west 95536 (Ferndale).

Place names and addresses in California

Of the 1,239 place names in the list, 927 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Sacramento (100), Los Angeles (94), San Diego (76) and Fresno (58). 6 place names appear under more than one county, among them Clovis, Fontana, La Quinta and Madera: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters CA and the code, as in Sacramento, CA 94203. The name of the county is left out.
