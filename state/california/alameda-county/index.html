Where Alameda County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Alameda County is in the west of California. Its codes spread over about 26 miles from north to south and 29 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 94539 (Fremont) and 94706 (Albany), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Contra Costa County (13 miles), City and County of San Francisco (15 miles), San Mateo County (19 miles) and Marin County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Alameda County begin with 946 (29 codes), 945 (28 codes) and 947 (12 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 946, so those three digits alone point to Alameda County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 49 of the 69 codes. Together they come to about 806 square miles of land (2,087 sq km). The largest is 94550 (Livermore) with 272 square miles, and the smallest is 94613 (Oakland) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 5.9 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Alameda County; a code can reach across its border. 20 codes have no mapped area: 94537 (Fremont), 94540 (Hayward), 94543 (Hayward), 94557 (Hayward), 94604 (Oakland) and 15 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Alameda County ranks 9th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 69 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Alameda County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:

  • Oakland, CA 94601 (one of 26 codes for Oakland; the street decides which)
  • Berkeley, CA 94701 (one of 11 codes for Berkeley; the street decides which)
  • Hayward, CA 94540 (one of 7 codes for Hayward; the street decides which)

Oakland is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 15 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
