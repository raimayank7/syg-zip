Where Tulare County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Tulare County sits near the middle of California. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 72 miles, and from east to west about 58 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 93260 (Posey) and 93619 (Clovis), about 90 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Kings County (36 miles), Fresno County (44 miles), Kern County (69 miles) and Madera County (73 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Tulare County begin with 932 (33 codes) and 936 (9 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 932 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Fresno County, Kern County and Kings County, and has 72 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 37 of the 42 codes. Together they come to about 3,040 square miles of land (7,872 sq km). The largest is 93265 (Springville) with 390 square miles, and the smallest is 93670 (Yettem) with 0.05. The middle-sized code here covers 65.8 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Tulare County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 93275 (Tulare), 93278 (Visalia), 93279 (Visalia), 93282 (Waukena) and 93290 (Visalia). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Tulare County ranks 16th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 42 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.
