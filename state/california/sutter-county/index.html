Where Sutter County is

The ZIP codes of Sutter County are found in the north-west of California. Its codes spread over about 31 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 95668 (Pleasant Grove) and 95953 (Live Oak), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Yolo County (27 miles), Yuba County (28 miles), Colusa County (30 miles) and Sacramento County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Sutter County begin with 959 (6 codes) and 956 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 959 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Butte County, Colusa County and Glenn County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 521 square miles of land (1,350 sq km). The largest is 95953 (Live Oak) with 116 square miles, and the smallest is 95676 (Robbins) with 1.3. The middle-sized code here covers 45.9 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sutter County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 95992 (Yuba City). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Sutter County ranks 48th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Sutter County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:

  • Yuba City, CA 95991 (one of 3 codes for Yuba City; the street decides which)
  • Live Oak, CA 95953
  • Meridian, CA 95957

Questions about Sutter County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Yuba City, CA?

Yuba City has 3 ZIP codes in Sutter County: 95991, 95992, 95993. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Sutter County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 959, on 6 of the 10 codes.

Which ZIP code in Sutter County covers the most land?

95953 (Live Oak), at about 116 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
