Where San Bernardino County is

The ZIP codes of San Bernardino County are found in the south-east of California. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 144 miles, and from east to west about 205 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 92267 (Parker Dam) and 93558 (Red Mountain), about 210 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Riverside County (38 miles), Orange County (62 miles), Los Angeles County (72 miles) and San Diego County (101 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of San Bernardino County begin with 923 (69 codes), 917 (17 codes), 924 (15 codes), 922 (12 codes) and 935 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 923 is shared with 3 other counties, Imperial County, Inyo County and Riverside County, and has 74 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 92 of the 116 codes. Together they come to about 8,764 square miles of land (22,697 sq km). The largest is 92277 (Twentynine Palms) with 1,599 square miles, and the smallest is 92415 (San Bernardino) with 0.07. The middle-sized code here covers 28.1 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of San Bernardino County; a code can reach across its border. 24 codes have no mapped area: 91729 (Rancho Cucamonga), 91743 (Guasti), 91758 (Ontario), 91785 (Upland), 92248 (La Quinta) and 19 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, San Bernardino County ranks 5th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 116 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in San Bernardino County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:

  • San Bernardino, CA 92401 (one of 15 codes for San Bernardino; the street decides which)
  • Rancho Cucamonga, CA 91701 (one of 5 codes for Rancho Cucamonga; the street decides which)
  • Fontana, CA 92334 (one of 4 codes for Fontana; the street decides which)

Fontana and La Quinta share a name with a place in another part of California; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
