Where Riverside County is

The ZIP codes of Riverside County are found in the south-east of California. Its codes spread over about 38 miles from north to south and 173 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 92225 (Blythe) and 92880 (Corona), about 174 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are San Bernardino County (38 miles), Orange County (52 miles), San Diego County (64 miles) and Los Angeles County (78 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Riverside County begin with 925 (59 codes), 922 (30 codes), 928 (8 codes), 917 (1 code) and 923 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 925, so those three digits alone point to Riverside County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 71 of the 99 codes. Together they come to about 4,128 square miles of land (10,693 sq km). The largest is 92225 (Blythe) with 581 square miles, and the smallest is 92521 (Riverside) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 29.1 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Riverside County; a code can reach across its border. 28 codes have no mapped area: 92202 (Indio), 92226 (Blythe), 92235 (Cathedral City), 92247 (La Quinta), 92255 (Palm Desert) and 23 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Riverside County ranks 7th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 99 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Riverside County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:

  • Riverside, CA 92501 (one of 17 codes for Riverside; the street decides which)
  • Corona, CA 92877 (one of 7 codes for Corona; the street decides which)
  • Moreno Valley, CA 92551 (one of 7 codes for Moreno Valley; the street decides which)

Riverside is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 13 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. La Quinta shares a name with a place in another part of California; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
