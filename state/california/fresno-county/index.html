Where Fresno County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Fresno County fall close to the middle of California. Its codes spread over about 80 miles from north to south and 84 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 93210 (Coalinga) and 93642 (Mono Hot Springs), about 109 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Madera County (32 miles), Kings County (39 miles), Tulare County (44 miles) and Mariposa County (59 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Fresno County begin with 937 (55 codes), 936 (38 codes), 932 (3 codes) and 938 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 937, so those three digits alone point to Fresno County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 57 of the 98 codes. Together they come to about 5,322 square miles of land (13,785 sq km). The largest is 93634 (Lakeshore) with 1,162 square miles, and the smallest is 93741 (Fresno) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 40.7 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Fresno County; a code can reach across its border. 41 codes have no mapped area: 93607 (Burrel), 93613 (Clovis), 93642 (Mono Hot Springs), 93649 (Piedra), 93707 (Fresno) and 36 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Fresno County ranks 8th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 98 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Fresno County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:

  • Fresno, CA 93650 (one of 58 codes for Fresno; the street decides which)
  • Clovis, CA 93611 (one of 3 codes for Clovis; the street decides which)

Clovis shares a name with a place in another part of California; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
