Where Lassen County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Lassen County fall in the north-west of California. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 78 miles, and from east to west about 56 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 96009 (Bieber) and 96109 (Doyle), about 95 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Plumas County (42 miles), Sierra County (65 miles), Modoc County (66 miles) and Washoe County, Nevada (78 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lassen County begin with 961 (13 codes) and 960 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 961 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Alpine County, El Dorado County and Modoc County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 2,292 square miles of land (5,935 sq km). The largest is 96130 (Susanville) with 500 square miles, and the smallest is 96113 (Herlong) with 21.9. The middle-sized code here covers 138 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lassen County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 96127 (Susanville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lassen County ranks 38th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lassen County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:

Questions about Lassen County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Susanville, CA?

Susanville has 2 ZIP codes in Lassen County: 96127, 96130. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Lassen County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 961, on 13 of the 15 codes.

Which ZIP code in Lassen County covers the most land?
