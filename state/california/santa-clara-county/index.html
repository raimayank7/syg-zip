Where Santa Clara County is

The ZIP codes of Santa Clara County are found in the west of California. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 30 miles, and from east to west about 34 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 94304 (Palo Alto) and 95021 (Gilroy), about 45 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Santa Cruz County (21 miles), San Mateo County (27 miles), Alameda County (33 miles) and City and County of San Francisco (42 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Santa Clara County begin with 951 (58 codes), 950 (31 codes), 940 (14 codes) and 943 (6 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 951, so those three digits alone point to Santa Clara County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 58 of the 109 codes. Together they come to about 1,026 square miles of land (2,658 sq km). The largest is 95020 (Gilroy) with 152 square miles, and the smallest is 95053 (Santa Clara) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 6.3 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Santa Clara County; a code can reach across its border. 51 codes have no mapped area: 94023 (Los Altos), 94035 (Mountain View), 94039 (Mountain View), 94042 (Mountain View), 94088 (Sunnyvale) and 46 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Santa Clara County ranks 6th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 109 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Santa Clara County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:

  • San Jose, CA 95025 (one of 58 codes for San Jose; the street decides which)
  • Santa Clara, CA 95050 (one of 7 codes for Santa Clara; the street decides which)
  • Mountain View, CA 94035 (one of 6 codes for Mountain View; the street decides which)

Palo Alto shares a name with a place in another part of California; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
