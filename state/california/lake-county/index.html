Where Lake County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Lake County is in the north-west of California. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 23 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 95467 (Hidden Valley Lake) and 95493 (Witter Springs), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Colusa County (36 miles), Sonoma County (39 miles), Napa County (42 miles) and Mendocino County (45 miles).

The first three digits

All 15 codes here begin with the same three digits, 954. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 954 is shared with 2 other counties, Mendocino County and Sonoma County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 771 square miles of land (1,998 sq km). The largest is 95457 (Lower Lake) with 148 square miles, and the smallest is 95435 (Finley) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 31.8 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 42.0 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lake County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 95424 (Clearlake Park). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lake County ranks 38th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.
