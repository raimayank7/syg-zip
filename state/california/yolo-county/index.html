Where Yolo County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Yolo County is in the north-west of California. Its codes spread over about 35 miles from north to south and 42 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 95612 (Clarksburg) and 95679 (Rumsey), about 53 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Sacramento County (23 miles), Sutter County (27 miles), Solano County (32 miles) and Napa County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Yolo County begin with 956 (17 codes), 957 (3 codes) and 959 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 956 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Alpine County, Amador County and El Dorado County, and has 94 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 18 of the 21 codes. Together they come to about 1,027 square miles of land (2,659 sq km). The largest is 95695 (Woodland) with 146 square miles, and the smallest is 95697 (Yolo) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 40.7 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Yolo County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 95617 (Davis), 95798 (West Sacramento) and 95799 (West Sacramento). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Yolo County ranks 31st among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 21 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Yolo County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:

Questions about Yolo County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for West Sacramento, CA?

West Sacramento has 4 ZIP codes in Yolo County: 95605, 95691, 95798, 95799. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Yolo County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 956, on 17 of the 21 codes.

Which ZIP code in Yolo County covers the most land?

95695 (Woodland), at about 146 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
