Where Shasta County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Shasta County is in the north-west of California. Its codes spread over about 51 miles from north to south and 81 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 96056 (Mcarthur) and 96076 (Platina), about 93 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Tehama County (41 miles), Trinity County (54 miles), Siskiyou County (67 miles) and Glenn County (76 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Shasta County starts with 960. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 960 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Lassen County, Modoc County and Plumas County, and has 80 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 27 of the 33 codes. Together they come to about 3,207 square miles of land (8,307 sq km). The largest is 96051 (Lakehead) with 343 square miles, and the smallest is 96019 (Shasta Lake) with 19.3. The middle-sized code here covers 104 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Shasta County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 96049 (Redding), 96070 (Obrien), 96079 (Shasta Lake), 96089 (Shasta Lake), 96095 (Whiskeytown) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Shasta County ranks 23rd among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 33 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Shasta County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:
