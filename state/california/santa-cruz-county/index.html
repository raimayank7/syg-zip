Where Santa Cruz County is

The ZIP codes of Santa Cruz County are found in the west of California. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 95006 (Boulder Creek) and 95076 (Watsonville), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Santa Clara County (21 miles), San Mateo County (40 miles), Monterey County (41 miles) and San Benito County (42 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Santa Cruz County starts with 950. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 950 is shared with 3 other counties, Monterey County, San Benito County and Santa Clara County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 16 of the 21 codes. Together they come to about 433 square miles of land (1,120 sq km). The largest is 95076 (Watsonville) with 135 square miles, and the smallest is 95041 (Mount Hermon) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 14.6 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Santa Cruz County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 95001 (Aptos), 95061 (Santa Cruz), 95063 (Santa Cruz), 95067 (Scotts Valley) and 95077 (Watsonville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Santa Cruz County ranks 31st among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 21 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Santa Cruz County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:

  • Santa Cruz, CA 95060 (one of 6 codes for Santa Cruz; the street decides which)
  • Aptos, CA 95001 (one of 2 codes for Aptos; the street decides which)
  • Scotts Valley, CA 95066 (one of 2 codes for Scotts Valley; the street decides which)
