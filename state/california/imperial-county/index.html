Where Imperial County is

The ZIP codes of Imperial County are found in the south-east of California. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 98 miles, and from east to west about 168 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 92222 (Bard) and 92331 (Fontana), about 190 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Yuma County, Arizona (72 miles), San Diego County (89 miles), Riverside County (97 miles) and La Paz County, Arizona (99 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Imperial County begin with 922 (17 codes) and 923 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 922 is shared with 2 other counties, Riverside County and San Bernardino County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 15 of the 18 codes. Together they come to about 3,683 square miles of land (9,538 sq km). The largest is 92266 (Palo Verde) with 1,185 square miles, and the smallest is 92222 (Bard) with 2.8. The middle-sized code here covers 152 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Imperial County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 92232 (Calexico), 92244 (El Centro) and 92331 (Fontana). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Imperial County ranks 34th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Imperial County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:

  • Calexico, CA 92231 (one of 2 codes for Calexico; the street decides which)
  • El Centro, CA 92243 (one of 2 codes for El Centro; the street decides which)
  • Bard, CA 92222

Fontana shares a name with a place in another part of California; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Imperial County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Calexico, CA?

Calexico has 2 ZIP codes in Imperial County: 92231, 92232. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Imperial County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 922, on 17 of the 18 codes.

Which ZIP code in Imperial County covers the most land?

92266 (Palo Verde), at about 1,185 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
