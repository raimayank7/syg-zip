Where Modoc County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Modoc County is in the north-west of California. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 45 miles, and from east to west about 57 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 96054 (Lookout) and 96112 (Fort Bidwell), about 70 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lassen County (66 miles), Lake County, Oregon (84 miles), Klamath County, Oregon (92 miles) and Shasta County (100 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Modoc County begin with 961 (7 codes) and 960 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 961 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Alpine County, El Dorado County and Lassen County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,110 square miles of land (5,464 sq km). The largest is 96101 (Alturas) with 597 square miles, and the smallest is 96115 (Lake City) with 112. The middle-sized code here covers 182 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 76.3 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Modoc County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Modoc County ranks 48th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.
