Where Ventura County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Ventura County is in the south of California. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 38 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 93001 (Ventura) and 93063 (Simi Valley), about 39 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Los Angeles County (46 miles), Santa Barbara County (58 miles), Orange County (76 miles) and Kern County (77 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Ventura County begin with 930 (39 codes) and 913 (8 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 930 is shared with 1 other county, Santa Barbara County, and has 42 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 27 of the 47 codes. Together they come to about 1,378 square miles of land (3,569 sq km). The largest is 93040 (Piru) with 372 square miles, and the smallest is 93043 (Port Hueneme Cbc Base) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 22.0 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ventura County; a code can reach across its border. 20 codes have no mapped area: 91319 (Newbury Park), 91358 (Thousand Oaks), 91359 (Westlake Village), 93002 (Ventura), 93005 (Ventura) and 15 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Ventura County ranks 14th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 47 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Ventura County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:
