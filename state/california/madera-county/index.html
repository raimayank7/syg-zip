Where Madera County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Madera County sits near the middle of California. Its codes spread over about 32 miles from north to south and 42 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 93610 (Chowchilla) and 93643 (North Fork), about 42 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Mariposa County (27 miles), Fresno County (32 miles), Merced County (43 miles) and Tuolumne County (60 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Madera County starts with 936. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 936 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Fresno County, Mariposa County and Merced County, and has 65 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 1,397 square miles of land (3,617 sq km). The largest is 93637 (Madera) with 280 square miles, and the smallest is 93669 (Wishon) with 7.1. The middle-sized code here covers 117 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Madera County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 93639 (Madera). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Madera County ranks 43rd among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Madera County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:

  • Madera, CA 93637 (one of 3 codes for Madera; the street decides which)
  • Ahwahnee, CA 93601
  • Bass Lake, CA 93604

ZIP codes named Madera are also listed under Merced County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site.
