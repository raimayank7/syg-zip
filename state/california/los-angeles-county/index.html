Where Los Angeles County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Los Angeles County fall in the south of California. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 98 miles, and from east to west about 66 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 90704 (Avalon) and 93535 (Lancaster), about 99 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Orange County (33 miles), Ventura County (46 miles), San Bernardino County (72 miles) and Riverside County (78 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Los Angeles County begin with 900 (95 codes), 913 (62 codes), 902 (50 codes), 917 (46 codes), 908 (28 codes), 911 (27 codes) and 13 less common prefixes. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 900, so those three digits alone point to Los Angeles County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 294 of the 508 codes. Together they come to about 3,637 square miles of land (9,421 sq km). The largest is 93550 (Palmdale) with 259 square miles, and the smallest is 91046 (Verdugo City) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 4.2 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 274 square miles, roughly 7% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Los Angeles County; a code can reach across its border. 214 codes have no mapped area: 90009 (Los Angeles), 90030 (Los Angeles), 90050 (Los Angeles), 90051 (Los Angeles), 90053 (Los Angeles) and 209 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of California has more ZIP codes: Los Angeles County holds 508 of the 2,593 in the state list, about 19.6%.

Writing an address in Los Angeles County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:

  • Los Angeles, CA 90001 (one of 94 codes for Los Angeles; the street decides which)
  • Long Beach, CA 90801 (one of 27 codes for Long Beach; the street decides which)
  • Pasadena, CA 91101 (one of 25 codes for Pasadena; the street decides which)
