Where Del Norte County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Del Norte County fall in the north-west of California. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 25 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 95548 (Klamath) and 95567 (Smith River), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Josephine County, Oregon (49 miles), Curry County, Oregon (56 miles), Siskiyou County (76 miles) and Jackson County, Oregon (77 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Del Norte County starts with 955. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 955 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Humboldt County, Mendocino County and Siskiyou County, and has 51 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 451 square miles of land (1,168 sq km). The largest is 95531 (Crescent City) with 189 square miles, and the smallest is 95538 (Fort Dick) with 1.9. The middle-sized code here covers 62.5 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Del Norte County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 95532 (Crescent City). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Del Norte County ranks 56th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.
