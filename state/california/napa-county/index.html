Where Napa County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Napa County is in the north-west of California. Its codes spread over about 31 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 94503 (American Canyon) and 94515 (Calistoga), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Sonoma County (23 miles), Solano County (24 miles), Marin County (33 miles) and Yolo County (33 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Napa County starts with 945. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 945 is shared with 3 other counties, Alameda County, Contra Costa County and Solano County, and has 93 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 810 square miles of land (2,097 sq km). The largest is 94558 (Napa) with 402 square miles, and the smallest is 94562 (Oakville) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 27.0 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Water adds about 37.6 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Napa County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 94581 (Napa). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Napa County ranks 43rd among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Napa County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:

  • Napa, CA 94558 (one of 3 codes for Napa; the street decides which)
  • American Canyon, CA 94503
  • Angwin, CA 94508

Which ZIP code in Napa County covers the most land?
