Where Yuba County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Yuba County is in the north-west of California. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 45 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 95961 (Olivehurst) and 95981 (Strawberry Valley), about 49 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Sutter County (28 miles), Butte County (28 miles), Nevada County (30 miles) and Placer County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Yuba County begin with 959 (11 codes) and 956 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 959 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Butte County, Colusa County and Glenn County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 611 square miles of land (1,583 sq km). The largest is 95901 (Marysville) with 200 square miles, and the smallest is 95972 (Rackerby) with 1.1. The middle-sized code here covers 41.9 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Yuba County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Yuba County ranks 43rd among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.
