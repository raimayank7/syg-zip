Where Kern County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Kern County fall in the south of California. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 65 miles, and from east to west about 116 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 93249 (Lost Hills) and 93596 (Boron), about 123 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Tulare County (69 miles), Ventura County (77 miles), Kings County (84 miles) and Santa Barbara County (88 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Kern County begin with 932 (26 codes), 933 (22 codes) and 935 (19 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 932 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Fresno County, Kings County and Santa Barbara County, and has 72 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 51 of the 67 codes. Together they come to about 6,125 square miles of land (15,865 sq km). The largest is 93252 (Maricopa) with 447 square miles, and the smallest is 93528 (Johannesburg) with 2.5. The middle-sized code here covers 92.2 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Kern County; a code can reach across its border. 16 codes have no mapped area: 93216 (Delano), 93302 (Bakersfield), 93303 (Bakersfield), 93380 (Bakersfield), 93383 (Bakersfield) and 11 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Kern County ranks 10th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 67 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.
