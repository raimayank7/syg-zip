Where Butte County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Butte County is in the north-west of California. Its codes spread over about 38 miles from north to south and 42 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 95927 (Chico) and 95930 (Clipper Mills), about 46 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Yuba County (28 miles), Glenn County (30 miles), Tehama County (40 miles) and Colusa County (40 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Butte County starts with 959. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 959 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Colusa County, Glenn County and Nevada County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 20 of the 24 codes. Together they come to about 1,564 square miles of land (4,052 sq km). The largest is 95965 (Oroville) with 311 square miles, and the smallest is 95929 (Chico) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 59.4 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Butte County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 95927 (Chico), 95940 (Feather Falls), 95967 (Paradise) and 95976 (Chico). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Butte County ranks 28th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 24 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Butte County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:
