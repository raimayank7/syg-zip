Where Plumas County is

The ZIP codes of Plumas County are found in the north-west of California. Its codes spread over about 51 miles from north to south and 76 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 95923 (Canyon Dam) and 96103 (Blairsden Graeagle), about 86 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Sierra County (28 miles), Lassen County (42 miles), Nevada County (46 miles) and Butte County (48 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Plumas County begin with 959 (9 codes), 961 (6 codes) and 960 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 959 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Butte County, Colusa County and Glenn County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 15 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 1,169 square miles of land (3,027 sq km). The largest is 96105 (Chilcoot) with 254 square miles, and the smallest is 95923 (Canyon Dam) with 10.2. The middle-sized code here covers 64.0 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Plumas County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 95980 (Storrie). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Plumas County ranks 35th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.
