Where San Mateo County is

The ZIP codes of San Mateo County are found in the west of California. Its codes spread over about 34 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 94016 (Daly City) and 94060 (Pescadero), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are City and County of San Francisco (18 miles), Alameda County (19 miles), Santa Clara County (27 miles) and Contra Costa County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of San Mateo County begin with 940 (31 codes), 944 (5 codes), 941 (1 code) and 943 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 940 is shared with 1 other county, Santa Clara County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 31 of the 38 codes. Together they come to about 437 square miles of land (1,133 sq km). The largest is 94060 (Pescadero) with 73.5 square miles, and the smallest is 94018 (El Granada) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 6.1 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of San Mateo County; a code can reach across its border. 7 codes have no mapped area: 94011 (Burlingame), 94016 (Daly City), 94017 (Daly City), 94026 (Menlo Park), 94064 (Redwood City) and 2 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, San Mateo County ranks 19th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 38 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in San Mateo County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:
