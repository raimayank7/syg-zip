Where Orange County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Orange County fall in the south-east of California. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 34 miles, and from east to west about 28 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 90631 (La Habra) and 92672 (San Clemente), about 39 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Los Angeles County (33 miles), Riverside County (52 miles), San Bernardino County (62 miles) and San Diego County (75 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Orange County begin with 926 (62 codes), 928 (53 codes), 927 (16 codes), 906 (10 codes) and 907 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 926, so those three digits alone point to Orange County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 89 of the 146 codes. Together they come to about 779 square miles of land (2,016 sq km). The largest is 92676 (Silverado) with 69.4 square miles, and the smallest is 90743 (Surfside) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 6.3 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Orange County; a code can reach across its border. 57 codes have no mapped area: 90622 (Buena Park), 90624 (Buena Park), 90632 (La Habra), 90633 (La Habra), 90721 (Los Alamitos) and 52 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Orange County ranks 3rd among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 146 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.
