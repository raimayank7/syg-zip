Where Santa Barbara County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Santa Barbara County fall in the south of California. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 51 miles, and from east to west about 60 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 93014 (Carpinteria) and 93434 (Guadalupe), about 71 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Ventura County (58 miles), San Luis Obispo County (67 miles), Kern County (88 miles) and Los Angeles County (103 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Santa Barbara County begin with 931 (21 codes), 934 (16 codes), 930 (3 codes) and 932 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 931, so those three digits alone point to Santa Barbara County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 24 of the 41 codes. Together they come to about 1,886 square miles of land (4,885 sq km). The largest is 93454 (Santa Maria) with 418 square miles, and the smallest is 93067 (Summerland) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 36.3 square miles; the middle value for all of California is 15.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Santa Barbara County; a code can reach across its border. 17 codes have no mapped area: 93014 (Carpinteria), 93102 (Santa Barbara), 93107 (Santa Barbara), 93116 (Goleta), 93118 (Goleta) and 12 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Santa Barbara County ranks 17th among the 58 counties and county-level areas of California (equal counts share a rank), with 41 of the 2,593 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Santa Barbara County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CA and the ZIP code:
