Where Wayne County is

The ZIP codes of Wayne County are found in the south of Utah. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 42 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 84734 (Hanksville) and 84747 (Loa), about 44 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Piute County (42 miles), Sevier County (45 miles), Garfield County (54 miles) and Emery County (64 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Wayne County starts with 847. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 847 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Beaver County, Garfield County and Iron County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,145 square miles of land (2,967 sq km). The largest is 84775 (Torrey) with 460 square miles, and the smallest is 84715 (Bicknell) with 30.8. The middle-sized code here covers 74.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Utah is 49.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wayne County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Wayne County ranks 19th among the 29 counties and county-level areas of Utah (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 347 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Wayne County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters UT and the ZIP code:

  • Loa, UT 84747
