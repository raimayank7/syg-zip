Where Duchesne County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Duchesne County is in the north-east of Utah. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 43 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 84027 (Fruitland) and 84053 (Neola), about 47 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Uintah County (39 miles), Carbon County (53 miles), Wasatch County (57 miles) and Daggett County (61 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 840. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 840 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Daggett County, Davis County and Morgan County, and has 94 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,159 square miles of land (5,591 sq km). The largest is 84021 (Duchesne) with 606 square miles, and the smallest is 84007 (Bluebell) with 20.7. The middle-sized code here covers 96.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Utah is 49.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Duchesne County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Duchesne County ranks 9th among the 29 counties and county-level areas of Utah (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 347 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Duchesne County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters UT and the ZIP code:

  • Altamont, UT 84001
  • Altonah, UT 84002
  • Bluebell, UT 84007

Altamont is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
