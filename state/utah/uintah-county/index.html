Where Uintah County is

The ZIP codes of Uintah County are found in the north-east of Utah. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 31 miles, and from east to west about 40 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 84008 (Bonanza) and 84085 (Whiterocks), about 50 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Duchesne County (39 miles), Daggett County (42 miles), Rio Blanco County, Colorado (71 miles) and Carbon County (75 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 840. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 840 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Daggett County, Davis County and Duchesne County, and has 94 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 1,879 square miles of land (4,867 sq km). The largest is 84078 (Vernal) with 1,338 square miles, and the smallest is 84008 (Bonanza) with 1.9. The middle-sized code here covers 87.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Utah is 49.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Uintah County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 84079 (Vernal). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Uintah County ranks 13th among the 29 counties and county-level areas of Utah (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 347 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Uintah County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters UT and the ZIP code:

  • Vernal, UT 84078 (one of 2 codes for Vernal; the street decides which)
  • Bonanza, UT 84008
  • Fort Duchesne, UT 84026

Questions about Uintah County ZIP codes

Vernal has 2 ZIP codes in Uintah County: 84078, 84079. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Yes. All 9 start with 840.

Which ZIP code in Uintah County covers the most land?
