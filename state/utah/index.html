Utah ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 347 ZIP codes for Utah, about 0.8% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 41st among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 29 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Salt Lake County (72), Utah County (27), Washington County (22), Cache County (19) and Box Elder County (18). At the other end, 2 have only one or two codes, for example Daggett County and Morgan County.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Utah uses 8 of these prefixes, from 840 to 847. The busiest are 840 (94 codes, most of them for Sandy), 847 (71 codes, most of them for Saint George), 841 (55 codes, most of them for Salt Lake City) and 846 (47 codes, most of them for Provo). The first digit 8 is shared with Arizona, Colorado, Idaho, Nevada, New Mexico, Texas and Wyoming.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 298 of the 347 codes. The largest is 84533 (Lake Powell, San Juan County) at 1,826 square miles and the smallest is 84138 (Salt Lake City) at 0.01; the middle value is 49.9 square miles. The other 49 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 84331 (Portage) and the southernmost 84784 (Hildale); the one farthest east is 84534 (Montezuma Creek) and farthest west 84083 (Wendover).

Place names and addresses in Utah

Of the 251 place names in the list, 234 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Salt Lake City (52), Ogden (13), Provo (6) and Sandy (6). In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters UT and the code, as in Salt Lake City, UT 84101. The name of the county is left out.
