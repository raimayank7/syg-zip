Where Salt Lake County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Salt Lake County is in the north of Utah. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 84096 (Herriman) and 84158 (Salt Lake City), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Davis County (22 miles), Summit County (27 miles), Wasatch County (28 miles) and Morgan County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Salt Lake County begin with 841 (55 codes) and 840 (17 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 841, so those three digits alone point to Salt Lake County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 42 of the 72 codes. Together they come to about 748 square miles of land (1,937 sq km). The largest is 84044 (Magna) with 81.6 square miles, and the smallest is 84138 (Salt Lake City) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 8.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Utah is 49.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 52.8 square miles, roughly 7% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Salt Lake County; a code can reach across its border. 30 codes have no mapped area: 84090 (Sandy), 84091 (Sandy), 84110 (Salt Lake City), 84122 (Salt Lake City), 84125 (Salt Lake City) and 25 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Utah has more ZIP codes: Salt Lake County holds 72 of the 347 in the state list, about 20.7%.

Writing an address in Salt Lake County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters UT and the ZIP code:

  • Salt Lake City, UT 84101 (one of 52 codes for Salt Lake City; the street decides which)
  • Sandy, UT 84070 (one of 6 codes for Sandy; the street decides which)
  • West Jordan, UT 84081 (one of 3 codes for West Jordan; the street decides which)
