Where Weber County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Weber County is in the north of Utah. Its codes spread over about 11 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 84315 (Hooper) and 84317 (Huntsville), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Davis County (16 miles), Morgan County (21 miles), Cache County (38 miles) and Box Elder County (38 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Weber County begin with 844 (11 codes), 843 (3 codes), 842 (2 codes) and 840 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 844, so those three digits alone point to Weber County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 551 square miles of land (1,428 sq km). The largest is 84317 (Huntsville) with 218 square miles, and the smallest is 84408 (Ogden) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 26.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Utah is 49.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Weber County; a code can reach across its border. 7 codes have no mapped area: 84201 (Ogden), 84244 (Ogden), 84402 (Ogden), 84407 (Ogden), 84409 (Ogden) and 2 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Weber County ranks 6th among the 29 counties and county-level areas of Utah (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 347 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Weber County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters UT and the ZIP code:

  • Ogden, UT 84201 (one of 13 codes for Ogden; the street decides which)
  • Eden, UT 84310
  • Hooper, UT 84315

Ogden is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Weber County ZIP codes

Do all ZIP codes in Weber County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Weber County covers the most land?
