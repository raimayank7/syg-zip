Where Warren County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Warren County sits near the middle of Tennessee. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 37394 (Viola) and 38550 (Campaign), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are White County (20 miles), Van Buren County (20 miles), Coffee County (21 miles) and Cannon County (22 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Warren County begin with 373 (3 codes), 371 (2 codes) and 385 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 373 is shared with 16 other counties, such as Bedford County, Bledsoe County and Bradley County, and has 87 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 606 square miles of land (1,571 sq km). The largest is 37110 (Mcminnville) with 374 square miles, and the smallest is 38550 (Campaign) with under 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 98.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Warren County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 37111 (Mcminnville) and 37378 (Smartt). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Warren County ranks 43rd among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Warren County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

Questions about Warren County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Mcminnville, TN?

Mcminnville has 2 ZIP codes in Warren County: 37110, 37111. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Warren County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 373, on 3 of the 7 codes.

Which ZIP code in Warren County covers the most land?

37110 (Mcminnville), at about 374 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
