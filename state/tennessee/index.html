Tennessee ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 785 ZIP codes for Tennessee, about 1.9% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 20th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 95 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Shelby County (77), Davidson County (49), Knox County (35), Hamilton County (33) and Rutherford County (16). At the other end, 12 have only one or two codes, for example Bledsoe County, Clay County and Hancock County.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Tennessee uses 16 of these prefixes, from 370 to 385. The busiest are 373 (87 codes, most of them for Cleveland), 370 (77 codes, most of them for Clarksville), 383 (72 codes, most of them for Jackson) and 378 (67 codes, most of them for Maryville). The first digit 3 is shared with Alabama, Florida, Georgia and Mississippi.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 636 of the 785 codes. The largest is 37110 (Mcminnville, Warren County) at 374 square miles and the smallest is 38503 (Cookeville) at under 0.01; the middle value is 48.5 square miles. The other 149 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 37119 (Mitchellville) and the southernmost 37396 (Whiteside); the one farthest east is 37680 (Laurel Bloomery) and farthest west 38109 (Memphis).

Place names and addresses in Tennessee

Of the 548 place names in the list, 499 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Memphis (61), Nashville (39), Knoxville (31) and Chattanooga (20). In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters TN and the code, as in Memphis, TN 37501. The name of the county is left out.
