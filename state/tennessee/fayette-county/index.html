Where Fayette County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Fayette County fall in the south-west of Tennessee. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 38036 (Gallaway) and 38046 (La Grange), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Haywood County (25 miles), Hardeman County (25 miles), Marshall County, Mississippi (25 miles) and Tipton County (27 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 380. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 380 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Crockett County, Dyer County and Hardeman County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 496 square miles of land (1,285 sq km). The largest is 38068 (Somerville) with 259 square miles, and the smallest is 38046 (La Grange) with 0.09. The middle-sized code here covers 44.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Fayette County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 38010 (Braden), 38045 (Laconia) and 38048 (Macon). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Fayette County ranks 14th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 785 codes in the state list.
