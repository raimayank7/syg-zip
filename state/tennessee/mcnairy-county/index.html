Where McNairy County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, McNairy County is in the south-west of Tennessee. Its codes spread over about 26 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 38359 (Milledgeville) and 38393 (Chewalla), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Hardin County (16 miles), Chester County (21 miles), Alcorn County, Mississippi (21 miles) and Hardeman County (26 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 383. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 383 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Benton County, Carroll County and Chester County, and has 72 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 577 square miles of land (1,495 sq km). The largest is 38375 (Selmer) with 113 square miles, and the smallest is 38359 (Milledgeville) with 6.1. The middle-sized code here covers 65.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of McNairy County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 38393 (Chewalla). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, McNairy County ranks 14th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in McNairy County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Adamsville, TN 38310
  • Bethel Springs, TN 38315
  • Chewalla, TN 38393

Adamsville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
