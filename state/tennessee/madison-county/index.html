Where Madison County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Madison County is in the south-west of Tennessee. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 38378 (Spring Creek) and 38392 (Mercer), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Chester County (19 miles), Crockett County (22 miles), Henderson County (26 miles) and Haywood County (27 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Madison County starts with 383. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 383 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Benton County, Carroll County and Chester County, and has 72 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 592 square miles of land (1,533 sq km). The largest is 38301 (Jackson) with 151 square miles, and the smallest is 38362 (Oakfield) with 8.6. The middle-sized code here covers 71.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Madison County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 38302 (Jackson), 38303 (Jackson), 38308 (Jackson), 38314 (Jackson) and 38378 (Spring Creek). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Madison County ranks 6th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Madison County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Jackson, TN 38301 (one of 6 codes for Jackson; the street decides which)
  • Beech Bluff, TN 38313
  • Denmark, TN 38391

Jackson is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 19 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
