Where Houston County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Houston County fall in the north-west of Tennessee. Its codes spread over about 1.6 miles from north to south and 11 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 37061 (Erin) and 37175 (Stewart), about 11 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Stewart County (13 miles), Humphreys County (20 miles), Benton County (23 miles) and Montgomery County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Houston County begin with 371 (2 codes) and 370 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 371 is shared with 19 other counties, such as Bedford County, Cannon County and Cheatham County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 206 square miles of land (534 sq km). The largest is 37061 (Erin) with 88.8 square miles, and the smallest is 37178 (Tennessee Ridge) with 42.3. The middle-sized code here covers 75.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. Water adds about 8.3 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Houston County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Houston County ranks 77th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 3 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Houston County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Erin, TN 37061
  • Stewart, TN 37175
  • Tennessee Ridge, TN 37178
