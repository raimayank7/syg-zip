Where Stewart County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Stewart County fall in the north-west of Tennessee. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 37028 (Bumpus Mills) and 37050 (Cumberland City), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Houston County (13 miles), Montgomery County (20 miles), Christian County, Kentucky (24 miles) and Trigg County, Kentucky (25 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Stewart County starts with 370. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 370 is shared with 21 other counties, such as Bedford County, Cannon County and Cheatham County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 349 square miles of land (903 sq km). The largest is 37058 (Dover) with 184 square miles, and the smallest is 37028 (Bumpus Mills) with 15.5. The middle-sized code here covers 52.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. Water adds about 15.3 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Stewart County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Stewart County ranks 60th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Stewart County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:
