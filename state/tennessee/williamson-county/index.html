Where Williamson County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Williamson County sits near the middle of Tennessee. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 37014 (Arrington) and 37062 (Fairview), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Davidson County (18 miles), Rutherford County (24 miles), Maury County (26 miles) and Cheatham County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Williamson County begin with 370 (10 codes) and 371 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 370 is shared with 21 other counties, such as Bedford County, Cannon County and Cheatham County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 562 square miles of land (1,455 sq km). The largest is 37064 (Franklin) with 184 square miles, and the smallest is 37014 (Arrington) with 22.0. The middle-sized code here covers 53.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Williamson County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 37024 (Brentwood), 37065 (Franklin) and 37068 (Franklin). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Williamson County ranks 9th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Williamson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Franklin, TN 37064 (one of 5 codes for Franklin; the street decides which)
  • Brentwood, TN 37024 (one of 2 codes for Brentwood; the street decides which)
  • Arrington, TN 37014

Franklin is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 26 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Williamson County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Franklin, TN?

Franklin has 5 ZIP codes in Williamson County: 37064, 37065, 37067, 37068, 37069. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Williamson County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 370, on 10 of the 12 codes.

Which ZIP code in Williamson County covers the most land?

37064 (Franklin), at about 184 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
