Where Rutherford County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Rutherford County fall close to the middle of Tennessee. Its codes spread over about 25 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 37060 (Eagleville) and 37118 (Milton), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cannon County (17 miles), Bedford County (22 miles), Wilson County (23 miles) and Williamson County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Rutherford County begin with 371 (10 codes) and 370 (6 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 371 is shared with 19 other counties, such as Bedford County, Cannon County and Cheatham County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 576 square miles of land (1,492 sq km). The largest is 37129 (Murfreesboro) with 80.0 square miles, and the smallest is 37132 (Murfreesboro) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 53.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Rutherford County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 37063 (Fosterville), 37089 (La Vergne), 37131 (Murfreesboro) and 37133 (Murfreesboro). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Rutherford County ranks 5th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Rutherford County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

Questions about Rutherford County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Murfreesboro, TN?

Murfreesboro has 7 ZIP codes in Rutherford County: 37127, 37128, 37129, 37130, 37131, 37132, 37133. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Rutherford County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Rutherford County covers the most land?
