Where Polk County is

The ZIP codes of Polk County are found in the south-east of Tennessee. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 53 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 37333 (Farner) and 37362 (Old Fort), about 54 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Bradley County (15 miles), Murray County, Georgia (18 miles), McMinn County (20 miles) and Fannin County, Georgia (20 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 373. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 373 is shared with 16 other counties, such as Bedford County, Bledsoe County and Bradley County, and has 87 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 406 square miles of land (1,051 sq km). The largest is 37369 (Reliance) with 108 square miles, and the smallest is 37316 (Conasauga) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 27.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Polk County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Polk County ranks 14th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Polk County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Benton, TN 37307
  • Conasauga, TN 37316
  • Copperhill, TN 37317

Benton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 11 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
