Where Dyer County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Dyer County fall in the north-west of Tennessee. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 38030 (Finley) and 38259 (Trimble), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Lake County (18 miles), Lauderdale County (22 miles), Crockett County (22 miles) and Obion County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Dyer County begin with 380 (7 codes) and 382 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 380 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Crockett County, Fayette County and Hardeman County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 427 square miles of land (1,107 sq km). The largest is 38024 (Dyersburg) with 143 square miles, and the smallest is 38007 (Bogota) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 27.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Dyer County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 38025 (Dyersburg). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Dyer County ranks 26th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Dyer County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Dyersburg, TN 38024 (one of 2 codes for Dyersburg; the street decides which)
  • Bogota, TN 38007
  • Finley, TN 38030

Questions about Dyer County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Dyersburg, TN?

Dyersburg has 2 ZIP codes in Dyer County: 38024, 38025. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Dyer County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 380, on 7 of the 8 codes.

Which ZIP code in Dyer County covers the most land?

38024 (Dyersburg), at about 143 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
