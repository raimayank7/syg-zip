Where Sullivan County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Sullivan County is in the north-east of Tennessee. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 37620 (Bristol) and 37665 (Kingsport), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Bristol (city), Virginia (13 miles), Washington County (16 miles), Scott County, Virginia (16 miles) and Carter County (18 miles).

The first three digits

All 13 codes here begin with the same three digits, 376. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 376 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Carter County, Greene County and Hawkins County, and has 42 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 414 square miles of land (1,072 sq km). The largest is 37620 (Bristol) with 128 square miles, and the smallest is 37665 (Kingsport) with 3.0. The middle-sized code here covers 46.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. Water adds about 16.5 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sullivan County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 37621 (Bristol), 37625 (Bristol), 37662 (Kingsport), 37669 (Kingsport) and 37699 (Piney Flats). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Sullivan County ranks 6th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Sullivan County
