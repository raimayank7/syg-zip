Where Montgomery County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Montgomery County is in the north-west of Tennessee. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 15 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 37171 (Southside) and 37191 (Woodlawn), about 17 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Stewart County (20 miles), Dickson County (21 miles), Christian County, Kentucky (22 miles) and Houston County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Montgomery County begin with 370 (6 codes) and 371 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 370 is shared with 21 other counties, such as Bedford County, Cannon County and Cheatham County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 400 square miles of land (1,036 sq km). The largest is 37043 (Clarksville) with 108 square miles, and the smallest is 37171 (Southside) with 23.9. The middle-sized code here covers 45.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Montgomery County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 37041 (Clarksville) and 37044 (Clarksville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Montgomery County ranks 20th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Montgomery County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Clarksville, TN 37040 (one of 5 codes for Clarksville; the street decides which)
  • Cunningham, TN 37052
  • Palmyra, TN 37142

Clarksville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 12 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Montgomery County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Clarksville, TN?

Clarksville has 5 ZIP codes in Montgomery County: 37040, 37041, 37042, 37043, 37044. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Montgomery County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 370, on 6 of the 9 codes.

Which ZIP code in Montgomery County covers the most land?

37043 (Clarksville), at about 108 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
