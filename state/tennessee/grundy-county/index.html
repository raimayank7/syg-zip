Where Grundy County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Grundy County fall in the south of Tennessee. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 37356 (Monteagle) and 37365 (Palmer), about 17 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Sequatchie County (18 miles), Marion County (20 miles), Warren County (22 miles) and Coffee County (23 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Grundy County starts with 373. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 373 is shared with 16 other counties, such as Bedford County, Bledsoe County and Bradley County, and has 87 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 345 square miles of land (893 sq km). The largest is 37387 (Tracy City) with 74.2 square miles, and the smallest is 37305 (Beersheba Springs) with 16.4. The middle-sized code here covers 45.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Grundy County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Grundy County ranks 26th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Grundy County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Altamont, TN 37301
  • Beersheba Springs, TN 37305
  • Coalmont, TN 37313

Altamont is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
