Where Washington County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Washington County fall in the north-east of Tennessee. Its codes spread over about 18 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 37614 (Johnson City) and 37656 (Fall Branch), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Unicoi County (13 miles), Carter County (15 miles), Sullivan County (16 miles) and Greene County (24 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Washington County starts with 376. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 376 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Carter County, Greene County and Hawkins County, and has 42 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 365 square miles of land (946 sq km). The largest is 37659 (Jonesborough) with 127 square miles, and the smallest is 37614 (Johnson City) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 35.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Washington County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 37602 (Johnson City), 37605 (Johnson City) and 37684 (Mountain Home). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Washington County ranks 12th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Washington County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Johnson City, TN 37601 (one of 6 codes for Johnson City; the street decides which)
  • Fall Branch, TN 37656
  • Jonesborough, TN 37659
