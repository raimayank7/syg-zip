Where Crockett County is

The ZIP codes of Crockett County are found in the west of Tennessee. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 38034 (Friendship) and 38337 (Gadsden), about 17 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Gibson County (17 miles), Lauderdale County (21 miles), Haywood County (21 miles) and Madison County (22 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Crockett County begin with 380 (5 codes) and 383 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 380 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Dyer County, Fayette County and Hardeman County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 300 square miles of land (777 sq km). The largest is 38034 (Friendship) with 103 square miles, and the smallest is 38050 (Maury City) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 47.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Crockett County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 38336 (Fruitvale). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Crockett County ranks 43rd among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Crockett County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Alamo, TN 38001
  • Bells, TN 38006
  • Crockett Mills, TN 38021

Alamo is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
