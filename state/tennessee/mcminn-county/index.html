Where McMinn County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of McMinn County fall in the south-east of Tennessee. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 18 miles, and from east to west about 14 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 37309 (Calhoun) and 37826 (Niota), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Meigs County (12 miles), Monroe County (16 miles), Polk County (20 miles) and Bradley County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of McMinn County begin with 373 (6 codes) and 378 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 373 is shared with 16 other counties, such as Bedford County, Bledsoe County and Bradley County, and has 87 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 403 square miles of land (1,043 sq km). The largest is 37303 (Athens) with 149 square miles, and the smallest is 37331 (Etowah) with 39.4. The middle-sized code here covers 56.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of McMinn County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 37371 (Athens). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, McMinn County ranks 43rd among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in McMinn County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Athens, TN 37303 (one of 2 codes for Athens; the street decides which)
  • Calhoun, TN 37309
  • Englewood, TN 37329

Athens is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 13 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about McMinn County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Athens, TN?

Athens has 2 ZIP codes in McMinn County: 37303, 37371. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in McMinn County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 373, on 6 of the 7 codes.

Which ZIP code in McMinn County covers the most land?

37303 (Athens), at about 149 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
