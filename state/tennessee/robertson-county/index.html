Where Robertson County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Robertson County fall in the north of Tennessee. Its codes spread over about 14 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 37010 (Adams) and 37188 (White House), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Sumner County (20 miles), Simpson County, Kentucky (20 miles), Cheatham County (22 miles) and Davidson County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Robertson County begin with 370 (4 codes) and 371 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 370 is shared with 21 other counties, such as Bedford County, Cannon County and Cheatham County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 467 square miles of land (1,209 sq km). The largest is 37172 (Springfield) with 180 square miles, and the smallest is 37141 (Orlinda) with 20.3. The middle-sized code here covers 51.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Robertson County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 37152 (Ridgetop). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Robertson County ranks 26th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Robertson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Adams, TN 37010
  • Cedar Hill, TN 37032
  • Cross Plains, TN 37049

Adams is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
