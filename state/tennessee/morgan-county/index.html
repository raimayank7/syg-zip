Where Morgan County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Morgan County fall in the north-east of Tennessee. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 37733 (Rugby) and 37840 (Oliver Springs), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Roane County (18 miles), Scott County (20 miles), Anderson County (23 miles) and Fentress County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Morgan County begin with 378 (5 codes) and 377 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 378 is shared with 20 other counties, such as Anderson County, Blount County and Campbell County, and has 67 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 531 square miles of land (1,375 sq km). The largest is 37770 (Lancing) with 136 square miles, and the smallest is 37719 (Coalfield) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 80.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Morgan County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Morgan County ranks 20th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 785 codes in the state list.
