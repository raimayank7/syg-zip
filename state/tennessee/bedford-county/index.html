Where Bedford County is

The ZIP codes of Bedford County are found in the south of Tennessee. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 14 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 37180 (Unionville) and 37360 (Normandy), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Moore County (18 miles), Coffee County (19 miles), Marshall County (19 miles) and Rutherford County (22 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Bedford County begin with 371 (5 codes), 370 (1 code) and 373 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 371 is shared with 19 other counties, such as Cannon County, Cheatham County and Davidson County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 469 square miles of land (1,215 sq km). The largest is 37160 (Shelbyville) with 233 square miles, and the smallest is 37360 (Normandy) with 27.6. The middle-sized code here covers 82.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bedford County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 37161 (Shelbyville) and 37162 (Shelbyville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Bedford County ranks 43rd among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bedford County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Shelbyville, TN 37160 (one of 3 codes for Shelbyville; the street decides which)
  • Bell Buckle, TN 37020
  • Normandy, TN 37360

Shelbyville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Bedford County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Shelbyville, TN?

Shelbyville has 3 ZIP codes in Bedford County: 37160, 37161, 37162. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Bedford County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 371, on 5 of the 7 codes.

Which ZIP code in Bedford County covers the most land?

37160 (Shelbyville), at about 233 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
