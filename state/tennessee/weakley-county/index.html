Where Weakley County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Weakley County fall in the north-west of Tennessee. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 13 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 38226 (Dukedom) and 38230 (Greenfield), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Henry County (23 miles), Gibson County (24 miles), Fulton County, Kentucky (24 miles) and Obion County (24 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Weakley County starts with 382. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 382 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Benton County, Carroll County and Dyer County, and has 32 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 522 square miles of land (1,353 sq km). The largest is 38237 (Martin) with 138 square miles, and the smallest is 38226 (Dukedom) with 23.9. The middle-sized code here covers 60.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Weakley County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 38238 (Martin). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Weakley County ranks 26th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Weakley County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Martin, TN 38237 (one of 2 codes for Martin; the street decides which)
  • Dresden, TN 38225
  • Dukedom, TN 38226

Martin is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
