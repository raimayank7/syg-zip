Where Hawkins County is

The ZIP codes of Hawkins County are found in the north-east of Tennessee. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 19 miles, and from east to west about 31 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 37645 (Mount Carmel) and 37811 (Mooresburg), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hancock County (14 miles), Greene County (18 miles), Lee County, Virginia (22 miles) and Hamblen County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Hawkins County begin with 378 (3 codes), 376 (2 codes) and 377 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 378 is shared with 20 other counties, such as Anderson County, Blount County and Campbell County, and has 67 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 501 square miles of land (1,298 sq km). The largest is 37857 (Rogersville) with 236 square miles, and the smallest is 37645 (Mount Carmel) with 6.2. The middle-sized code here covers 52.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hawkins County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Hawkins County ranks 43rd among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 785 codes in the state list.
