Where Grainger County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Grainger County fall in the north-east of Tennessee. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 37709 (Blaine) and 37881 (Thorn Hill), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Jefferson County (15 miles), Hamblen County (15 miles), Union County (16 miles) and Claiborne County (20 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Grainger County begin with 378 (4 codes) and 377 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 378 is shared with 20 other counties, such as Anderson County, Blount County and Campbell County, and has 67 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 317 square miles of land (821 sq km). The largest is 37861 (Rutledge) with 98.6 square miles, and the smallest is 37848 (Powder Springs) with 8.0. The middle-sized code here covers 48.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. Water adds about 21.8 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Grainger County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Grainger County ranks 54th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Grainger County
