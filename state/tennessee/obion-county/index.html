Where Obion County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Obion County fall in the north-west of Tennessee. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 38254 (Samburg) and 38257 (South Fulton), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Fulton County, Kentucky (14 miles), Lake County (18 miles), Weakley County (24 miles) and Hickman County, Kentucky (24 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Obion County starts with 382. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 382 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Benton County, Carroll County and Dyer County, and has 32 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 494 square miles of land (1,279 sq km). The largest is 38261 (Union City) with 177 square miles, and the smallest is 38254 (Samburg) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 55.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Obion County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 38271 (Woodland Mills) and 38281 (Union City). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Obion County ranks 20th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Obion County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Union City, TN 38261 (one of 2 codes for Union City; the street decides which)
  • Hornbeak, TN 38232
  • Obion, TN 38240

Union City is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
