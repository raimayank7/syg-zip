Where Cumberland County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Cumberland County sits near the middle of Tennessee. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 14 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 37723 (Crab Orchard) and 38578 (Pleasant Hill), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Bledsoe County (25 miles), Rhea County (25 miles), Roane County (26 miles) and Morgan County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Cumberland County begin with 385 (6 codes) and 377 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 385 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Clay County, Fentress County and Jackson County, and has 49 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 554 square miles of land (1,436 sq km). The largest is 38571 (Crossville) with 227 square miles, and the smallest is 38578 (Pleasant Hill) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 74.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cumberland County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 38557 (Crossville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Cumberland County ranks 43rd among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cumberland County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

Questions about Cumberland County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Crossville, TN?

Do all ZIP codes in Cumberland County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Cumberland County covers the most land?
