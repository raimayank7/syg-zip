Where Claiborne County is

The ZIP codes of Claiborne County are found in the north-east of Tennessee. Its codes spread over about 14 miles from north to south and 23 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 37730 (Eagan) and 37773 (Lone Mountain), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Bell County, Kentucky (17 miles), Union County (18 miles), Grainger County (20 miles) and Campbell County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Claiborne County begin with 377 (6 codes) and 378 (6 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 377 is shared with 18 other counties, such as Anderson County, Blount County and Campbell County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 447 square miles of land (1,157 sq km). The largest is 37879 (Tazewell) with 175 square miles, and the smallest is 37730 (Eagan) with 1.5. The middle-sized code here covers 36.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Claiborne County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 37707 (Arthur), 37773 (Lone Mountain), 37824 (New Tazewell) and 37867 (Shawanee). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Claiborne County ranks 9th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Claiborne County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • New Tazewell, TN 37824 (one of 2 codes for New Tazewell; the street decides which)
  • Arthur, TN 37707
  • Clairfield, TN 37715

Questions about Claiborne County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for New Tazewell, TN?

New Tazewell has 2 ZIP codes in Claiborne County: 37824, 37825. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Claiborne County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 377, on 6 of the 12 codes.

Which ZIP code in Claiborne County covers the most land?

37879 (Tazewell), at about 175 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
