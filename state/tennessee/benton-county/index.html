Where Benton County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Benton County is in the north-west of Tennessee. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 7.3 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 38221 (Big Sandy) and 38341 (Holladay), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Humphreys County (15 miles), Henry County (22 miles), Carroll County (23 miles) and Houston County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Benton County begin with 383 (3 codes) and 382 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 383 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Carroll County, Chester County and Crockett County, and has 72 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 414 square miles of land (1,073 sq km). The largest is 38320 (Camden) with 147 square miles, and the smallest is 38333 (Eva) with 24.7. The middle-sized code here covers 121 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 41.9 square miles, roughly 9% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Benton County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Benton County ranks 69th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Benton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Big Sandy, TN 38221
  • Camden, TN 38320
  • Eva, TN 38333

Big Sandy is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
