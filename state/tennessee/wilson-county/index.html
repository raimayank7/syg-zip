Where Wilson County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Wilson County is in the north of Tennessee. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 7.9 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 37121 (Mount Juliet) and 37184 (Watertown), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Trousdale County (18 miles), Sumner County (22 miles), Smith County (22 miles) and Rutherford County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Wilson County begin with 370 (4 codes) and 371 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 370 is shared with 21 other counties, such as Bedford County, Cannon County and Cheatham County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 4 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 553 square miles of land (1,433 sq km). The largest is 37090 (Lebanon) with 166 square miles, and the smallest is 37184 (Watertown) with 110. The middle-sized code here covers 138 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wilson County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 37071 (Gladeville), 37088 (Lebanon), 37121 (Mount Juliet) and 37136 (Norene). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Wilson County ranks 26th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Wilson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Lebanon, TN 37087 (one of 3 codes for Lebanon; the street decides which)
  • Mount Juliet, TN 37121 (one of 2 codes for Mount Juliet; the street decides which)
  • Gladeville, TN 37071

Lebanon is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 18 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Wilson County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Lebanon, TN?

Lebanon has 3 ZIP codes in Wilson County: 37087, 37088, 37090. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Wilson County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 370, on 4 of the 8 codes.

Which ZIP code in Wilson County covers the most land?

37090 (Lebanon), at about 166 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
