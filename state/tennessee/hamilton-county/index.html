Where Hamilton County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Hamilton County fall in the south of Tennessee. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 37308 (Birchwood) and 37350 (Lookout Mountain), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Catoosa County, Georgia (10 miles), Walker County, Georgia (17 miles), Marion County (20 miles) and Dade County, Georgia (20 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Hamilton County begin with 374 (20 codes) and 373 (13 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 374, so those three digits alone point to Hamilton County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 27 of the 33 codes. Together they come to about 642 square miles of land (1,663 sq km). The largest is 37379 (Soddy Daisy) with 106 square miles, and the smallest is 37450 (Chattanooga) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 12.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 42.4 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hamilton County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 37304 (Bakewell), 37384 (Soddy Daisy), 37401 (Chattanooga), 37414 (Chattanooga), 37422 (Chattanooga) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Hamilton County ranks 4th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 33 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hamilton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:
