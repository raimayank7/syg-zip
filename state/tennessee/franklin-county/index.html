Where Franklin County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Franklin County is in the south of Tennessee. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 37345 (Huntland) and 37375 (Sewanee), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Moore County (19 miles), Coffee County (22 miles), Grundy County (23 miles) and Lincoln County (27 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 373. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 373 is shared with 16 other counties, such as Bedford County, Bledsoe County and Bradley County, and has 87 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 552 square miles of land (1,429 sq km). The largest is 37306 (Belvidere) with 118 square miles, and the smallest is 37383 (Sewanee) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 66.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. Water adds about 20.6 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Franklin County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Franklin County ranks 20th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Franklin County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Sewanee, TN 37375 (one of 2 codes for Sewanee; the street decides which)
  • Belvidere, TN 37306
  • Cowan, TN 37318

Questions about Franklin County ZIP codes

Which ZIP code in Franklin County covers the most land?
