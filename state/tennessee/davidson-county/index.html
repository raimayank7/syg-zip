Where Davidson County is

The ZIP codes of Davidson County are found in the north of Tennessee. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 19 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 37013 (Antioch) and 37080 (Joelton), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Cheatham County (17 miles), Williamson County (18 miles), Robertson County (23 miles) and Wilson County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Davidson County begin with 372 (39 codes), 370 (6 codes) and 371 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 372, so those three digits alone point to Davidson County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 30 of the 49 codes. Together they come to about 544 square miles of land (1,409 sq km). The largest is 37072 (Goodlettsville) with 77.6 square miles, and the smallest is 37238 (Nashville) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 14.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 22.7 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Davidson County; a code can reach across its border. 19 codes have no mapped area: 37011 (Antioch), 37070 (Goodlettsville), 37116 (Madison), 37202 (Nashville), 37222 (Nashville) and 14 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Davidson County ranks 2nd among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 49 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Davidson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Nashville, TN 37201 (one of 39 codes for Nashville; the street decides which)
  • Antioch, TN 37011 (one of 2 codes for Antioch; the street decides which)
  • Goodlettsville, TN 37070 (one of 2 codes for Goodlettsville; the street decides which)

Nashville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
