Where Sumner County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Sumner County fall in the north of Tennessee. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 37077 (Hendersonville) and 37186 (Westmoreland), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Trousdale County (17 miles), Simpson County, Kentucky (19 miles), Robertson County (20 miles) and Wilson County (22 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Sumner County begin with 370 (6 codes) and 371 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 370 is shared with 21 other counties, such as Bedford County, Cannon County and Cheatham County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 587 square miles of land (1,520 sq km). The largest is 37148 (Portland) with 132 square miles, and the smallest is 37031 (Castalian Springs) with 38.3. The middle-sized code here covers 87.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sumner County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 37077 (Hendersonville) and 37119 (Mitchellville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Sumner County ranks 20th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Sumner County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Hendersonville, TN 37075 (one of 2 codes for Hendersonville; the street decides which)
  • Bethpage, TN 37022
  • Castalian Springs, TN 37031

Questions about Sumner County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Hendersonville, TN?

Hendersonville has 2 ZIP codes in Sumner County: 37075, 37077. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Sumner County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 370, on 6 of the 9 codes.

Which ZIP code in Sumner County covers the most land?

37148 (Portland), at about 132 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
