Where Bledsoe County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Bledsoe County fall close to the middle of Tennessee. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Rhea County (13 miles), Van Buren County (14 miles), Sequatchie County (21 miles) and White County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The one code here starts with 373. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 373 is shared with 16 other counties, such as Bedford County, Bradley County and Coffee County, and has 87 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for one code here, 37367 (Pikeville): 297 square miles of land. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bledsoe County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Bledsoe County ranks 89th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 1 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bledsoe County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Pikeville, TN 37367
