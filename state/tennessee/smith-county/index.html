Where Smith County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Smith County fall in the north of Tennessee. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 24 miles, and from east to west about 13 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 37057 (Dixon Springs) and 38569 (Lancaster), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Trousdale County (17 miles), DeKalb County (17 miles), Jackson County (17 miles) and Macon County (20 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Smith County begin with 385 (6 codes), 370 (2 codes) and 371 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 385 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Clay County, Cumberland County and Fentress County, and has 49 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 329 square miles of land (853 sq km). The largest is 37145 (Pleasant Shade) with 79.3 square miles, and the smallest is 38552 (Chestnut Mound) with 7.3. The middle-sized code here covers 30.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. Water adds about 10.4 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Smith County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Smith County ranks 14th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Smith County

Questions about Smith County ZIP codes

38547 is the ZIP code listed for Brush Creek in Smith County.

Do all ZIP codes in Smith County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Smith County covers the most land?
