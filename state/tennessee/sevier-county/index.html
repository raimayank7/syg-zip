Where Sevier County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Sevier County fall in the east of Tennessee. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 15 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 37738 (Gatlinburg) and 37764 (Kodak), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Jefferson County (16 miles), Blount County (23 miles), Knox County (23 miles) and Cocke County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Sevier County begin with 378 (6 codes) and 377 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 378 is shared with 20 other counties, such as Anderson County, Blount County and Campbell County, and has 67 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 608 square miles of land (1,574 sq km). The largest is 37738 (Gatlinburg) with 215 square miles, and the smallest is 37863 (Pigeon Forge) with 17.2. The middle-sized code here covers 77.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sevier County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 37864 (Sevierville) and 37868 (Pigeon Forge). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Sevier County ranks 26th among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Sevier County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Pigeon Forge, TN 37863 (one of 2 codes for Pigeon Forge; the street decides which)
  • Gatlinburg, TN 37738

Questions about Sevier County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Sevierville, TN?

Sevierville has 3 ZIP codes in Sevier County: 37862, 37864, 37876. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Sevier County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 378, on 6 of the 8 codes.

Which ZIP code in Sevier County covers the most land?

37738 (Gatlinburg), at about 215 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
