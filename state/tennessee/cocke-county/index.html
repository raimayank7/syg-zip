Where Cocke County is

The ZIP codes of Cocke County are found in the east of Tennessee. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 11 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 37713 (Bybee) and 37753 (Hartford), about 16 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hamblen County (21 miles), Jefferson County (22 miles), Greene County (23 miles) and Madison County, North Carolina (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Cocke County begin with 377 (4 codes) and 378 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 377 is shared with 18 other counties, such as Anderson County, Blount County and Campbell County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 445 square miles of land (1,153 sq km). The largest is 37821 (Newport) with 130 square miles, and the smallest is 37713 (Bybee) with 29.9. The middle-sized code here covers 71.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Tennessee is 48.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cocke County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 37822 (Newport). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Cocke County ranks 43rd among the 95 counties and county-level areas of Tennessee (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 785 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cocke County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TN and the ZIP code:

  • Newport, TN 37821 (one of 2 codes for Newport; the street decides which)
  • Bybee, TN 37713
  • Cosby, TN 37722

Newport is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 18 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Cocke County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Newport, TN?

Newport has 2 ZIP codes in Cocke County: 37821, 37822. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Cocke County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 377, on 4 of the 7 codes.

Which ZIP code in Cocke County covers the most land?

37821 (Newport), at about 130 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
