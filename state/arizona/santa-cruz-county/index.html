Where Santa Cruz County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Santa Cruz County fall in the south-east of Arizona. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 34 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 85611 (Elgin) and 85648 (Rio Rico), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Pima County (44 miles), Cochise County (58 miles), Pinal County (103 miles) and Graham County (112 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Santa Cruz County starts with 856. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 856 is shared with 3 other counties, Cochise County, Pima County and Pinal County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 1,577 square miles of land (4,085 sq km). The largest is 85611 (Elgin) with 382 square miles, and the smallest is 85640 (Tumacacori) with 2.9. The middle-sized code here covers 187 square miles; the middle value for all of Arizona is 73.7. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Santa Cruz County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 85628 (Nogales) and 85662 (Nogales). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Santa Cruz County ranks 12th among the 15 counties and county-level areas of Arizona (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 530 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Santa Cruz County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AZ and the ZIP code:

  • Nogales, AZ 85621 (one of 3 codes for Nogales; the street decides which)
  • Amado, AZ 85645
  • Elgin, AZ 85611

Questions about Santa Cruz County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Nogales, AZ?

Nogales has 3 ZIP codes in Santa Cruz County: 85621, 85628, 85662. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Santa Cruz County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 10 start with 856.

Which ZIP code in Santa Cruz County covers the most land?

85611 (Elgin), at about 382 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
