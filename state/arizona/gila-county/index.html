Where Gila County is

The ZIP codes of Gila County are found in the east of Arizona. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 93 miles, and from east to west about 62 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 85192 (Winkelman) and 85544 (Pine), about 102 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Pinal County (54 miles), Maricopa County (66 miles), Graham County (81 miles) and Pima County (99 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Gila County begin with 855 (12 codes) and 851 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 855 is shared with 2 other counties, Graham County and Greenlee County, and has 24 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 4,046 square miles of land (10,480 sq km). The largest is 85554 (Young) with 828 square miles, and the smallest is 85532 (Claypool) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 315 square miles; the middle value for all of Arizona is 73.7. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Gila County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 85502 (Globe) and 85547 (Payson). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Gila County ranks 10th among the 15 counties and county-level areas of Arizona (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 530 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Gila County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AZ and the ZIP code:

  • Globe, AZ 85501 (one of 2 codes for Globe; the street decides which)
  • Payson, AZ 85541 (one of 2 codes for Payson; the street decides which)
  • Claypool, AZ 85532

Questions about Gila County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Globe, AZ?

Globe has 2 ZIP codes in Gila County: 85501, 85502. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Gila County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 855, on 12 of the 14 codes.

Which ZIP code in Gila County covers the most land?

85554 (Young), at about 828 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
