Where Pinal County is

The ZIP codes of Pinal County are found in the south of Arizona. Its codes spread over about 67 miles from north to south and 84 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 85139 (Maricopa) and 85631 (San Manuel), about 88 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Maricopa County (50 miles), Gila County (54 miles), Pima County (60 miles) and Graham County (95 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Pinal County begin with 851 (26 codes) and 856 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 851 is shared with 2 other counties, Gila County and Maricopa County, and has 31 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 26 of the 30 codes. Together they come to about 3,743 square miles of land (9,694 sq km). The largest is 85132 (Florence) with 601 square miles, and the smallest is 85191 (Valley Farms) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 81.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Arizona is 73.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pinal County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 85117 (Apache Junction), 85130 (Casa Grande), 85144 (San Tan Valley) and 85178 (Apache Junction). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Pinal County ranks 5th among the 15 counties and county-level areas of Arizona (equal counts share a rank), with 30 of the 530 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pinal County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AZ and the ZIP code:

  • Apache Junction, AZ 85117 (one of 4 codes for Apache Junction; the street decides which)
  • Casa Grande, AZ 85122 (one of 4 codes for Casa Grande; the street decides which)
  • San Tan Valley, AZ 85140 (one of 3 codes for San Tan Valley; the street decides which)

Marana shares a name with a place in another part of Arizona; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Pinal County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Apache Junction, AZ?

Do all ZIP codes in Pinal County start with the same digits?
