Where Mohave County is

The ZIP codes of Mohave County are found in the north-west of Arizona. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 177 miles, and from east to west about 91 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 86021 (Colorado City) and 86403 (Lake Havasu City), about 190 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Clark County, Nevada (83 miles), La Paz County (99 miles), Yavapai County (110 miles) and Coconino County (143 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Mohave County begin with 864 (29 codes), 853 (1 code) and 860 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 864 is shared with 1 other county, Coconino County, and has 30 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 24 of the 31 codes. Together they come to about 9,563 square miles of land (24,768 sq km). The largest is 86401 (Kingman) with 1,226 square miles, and the smallest is 86403 (Lake Havasu City) with 11.0. The middle-sized code here covers 288 square miles; the middle value for all of Arizona is 73.7. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Mohave County; a code can reach across its border. 7 codes have no mapped area: 86402 (Kingman), 86405 (Lake Havasu City), 86412 (Hualapai), 86427 (Fort Mohave), 86430 (Bullhead City) and 2 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Mohave County ranks 4th among the 15 counties and county-level areas of Arizona (equal counts share a rank), with 31 of the 530 codes in the state list.
