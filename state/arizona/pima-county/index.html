Where Pima County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Pima County is in the south-east of Arizona. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 70 miles, and from east to west about 123 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 85341 (Lukeville) and 85619 (Mount Lemmon), about 125 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Santa Cruz County (44 miles), Pinal County (60 miles), Cochise County (73 miles) and Graham County (87 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Pima County begin with 857 (55 codes), 856 (12 codes) and 853 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 857, so those three digits alone point to Pima County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 51 of the 69 codes. Together they come to about 7,852 square miles of land (20,338 sq km). The largest is 85634 (Sells) with 2,805 square miles, and the smallest is 85721 (Tucson) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 23.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Arizona is 73.7. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pima County; a code can reach across its border. 18 codes have no mapped area: 85652 (Cortaro), 85702 (Tucson), 85703 (Tucson), 85717 (Tucson), 85720 (Tucson) and 13 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Pima County ranks 2nd among the 15 counties and county-level areas of Arizona (equal counts share a rank), with 69 of the 530 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pima County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AZ and the ZIP code:

  • Tucson, AZ 85701 (one of 54 codes for Tucson; the street decides which)
  • Green Valley, AZ 85614 (one of 2 codes for Green Valley; the street decides which)
  • Ajo, AZ 85321
