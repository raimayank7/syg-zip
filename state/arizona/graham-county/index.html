Where Graham County is

The ZIP codes of Graham County are found in the south-east of Arizona. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 28 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 85530 (Bylas) and 85551 (Solomon), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Greenlee County (39 miles), Gila County (81 miles), Cochise County (82 miles) and Hidalgo County, New Mexico (82 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Graham County starts with 855. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 855 is shared with 2 other counties, Gila County and Greenlee County, and has 24 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 1,664 square miles of land (4,311 sq km). The largest is 85546 (Safford) with 697 square miles, and the smallest is 85535 (Eden) with 3.9. The middle-sized code here covers 86.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Arizona is 73.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Graham County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 85548 (Safford). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Graham County ranks 13th among the 15 counties and county-level areas of Arizona (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 530 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Graham County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AZ and the ZIP code:

  • Safford, AZ 85546 (one of 2 codes for Safford; the street decides which)
  • Bylas, AZ 85530
  • Central, AZ 85531
