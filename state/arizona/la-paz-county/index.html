Where La Paz County is

The ZIP codes of La Paz County are found in the west of Arizona. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 58 miles, and from east to west about 69 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 85328 (Cibola) and 85357 (Wenden), about 86 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Yuma County (80 miles), Mohave County (99 miles), Imperial County, California (99 miles) and Yavapai County (117 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 853. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 853 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Maricopa County, Mohave County and Pima County, and has 81 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 3,320 square miles of land (8,598 sq km). The largest is 85344 (Parker) with 880 square miles, and the smallest is 85371 (Poston) with 9.3. The middle-sized code here covers 394 square miles; the middle value for all of Arizona is 73.7. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of La Paz County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 85359 (Quartzsite). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, La Paz County ranks 13th among the 15 counties and county-level areas of Arizona (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 530 codes in the state list.
