Where Maricopa County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Maricopa County fall close to the middle of Arizona. Its codes spread over about 72 miles from north to south and 105 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 85190 (Tortilla Flat) and 85320 (Aguila), about 108 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Pinal County (50 miles), Gila County (66 miles), Yavapai County (80 miles) and Pima County (108 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Maricopa County begin with 850 (78 codes), 852 (63 codes), 853 (54 codes) and 851 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 850, so those three digits alone point to Maricopa County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 135 of the 198 codes. Together they come to about 6,425 square miles of land (16,640 sq km). The largest is 85354 (Tonopah) with 716 square miles, and the smallest is 85236 (Higley) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 11.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Arizona is 73.7. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Maricopa County; a code can reach across its border. 63 codes have no mapped area: 85001 (Phoenix), 85002 (Phoenix), 85005 (Phoenix), 85010 (Phoenix), 85011 (Phoenix) and 58 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Arizona has more ZIP codes: Maricopa County holds 198 of the 530 in the state list, about 37.4%.

Writing an address in Maricopa County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AZ and the ZIP code:

  • Phoenix, AZ 85001 (one of 77 codes for Phoenix; the street decides which)
  • Mesa, AZ 85201 (one of 19 codes for Mesa; the street decides which)
  • Scottsdale, AZ 85250 (one of 15 codes for Scottsdale; the street decides which)

Phoenix is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
