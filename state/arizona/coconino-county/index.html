Where Coconino County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Coconino County fall in the north of Arizona. Its codes spread over about 170 miles from north to south and 104 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 85931 (Forest Lakes) and 86022 (Fredonia), about 193 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Yavapai County (76 miles), Navajo County (90 miles), Apache County (128 miles) and Kane County, Utah (132 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Coconino County begin with 860 (23 codes), 863 (3 codes), 859 (1 code) and 864 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 860 is shared with 3 other counties, Apache County, Mohave County and Navajo County, and has 37 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 26 of the 28 codes. Together they come to about 13,386 square miles of land (34,669 sq km). The largest is 86020 (Cameron) with 1,248 square miles, and the smallest is 86011 (Flagstaff) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 452 square miles; the middle value for all of Arizona is 73.7. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Coconino County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 86002 (Flagstaff) and 86339 (Sedona). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Coconino County ranks 7th among the 15 counties and county-level areas of Arizona (equal counts share a rank), with 28 of the 530 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Coconino County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AZ and the ZIP code:

  • Flagstaff, AZ 86001 (one of 6 codes for Flagstaff; the street decides which)
  • Sedona, AZ 86336 (one of 3 codes for Sedona; the street decides which)
  • Bellemont, AZ 86015

Sedona shares a name with a place in another part of Arizona; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Coconino County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Flagstaff, AZ?

Do all ZIP codes in Coconino County start with the same digits?
