Where Yuma County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Yuma County is in the south-west of Arizona. Its codes spread over about 26 miles from north to south and 77 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 85333 (Dateland) and 85349 (San Luis), about 81 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Imperial County, California (72 miles), La Paz County (80 miles), Maricopa County (146 miles) and San Diego County, California (157 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 853. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 853 is shared with 5 other counties, such as La Paz County, Maricopa County and Mohave County, and has 81 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 2,599 square miles of land (6,732 sq km). The largest is 85365 (Yuma) with 1,349 square miles, and the smallest is 85336 (Gadsden) with 9.8. The middle-sized code here covers 61.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Arizona is 73.7. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Yuma County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 85366 (Yuma) and 85369 (Yuma). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Yuma County ranks 11th among the 15 counties and county-level areas of Arizona (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 530 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Yuma County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AZ and the ZIP code:

  • Yuma, AZ 85364 (one of 5 codes for Yuma; the street decides which)
  • Dateland, AZ 85333
  • Gadsden, AZ 85336
