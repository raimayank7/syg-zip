Where Yavapai County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Yavapai County sits near the middle of Arizona. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 86 miles, and from east to west about 80 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 85324 (Black Canyon City) and 86337 (Seligman), about 98 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Coconino County (76 miles), Maricopa County (80 miles), Gila County (107 miles) and Mohave County (110 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Yavapai County begin with 863 (29 codes) and 853 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 863 is shared with 1 other county, Coconino County, and has 32 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 26 of the 32 codes. Together they come to about 7,628 square miles of land (19,756 sq km). The largest is 86337 (Seligman) with 1,708 square miles, and the smallest is 86313 (Prescott) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 256 square miles; the middle value for all of Arizona is 73.7. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Yavapai County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 86302 (Prescott), 86304 (Prescott), 86312 (Prescott Valley), 86340 (Sedona), 86341 (Sedona) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Yavapai County ranks 3rd among the 15 counties and county-level areas of Arizona (equal counts share a rank), with 32 of the 530 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Yavapai County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AZ and the ZIP code:

  • Prescott, AZ 86301 (one of 6 codes for Prescott; the street decides which)
  • Prescott Valley, AZ 86312 (one of 3 codes for Prescott Valley; the street decides which)
  • Sedona, AZ 86340 (one of 2 codes for Sedona; the street decides which)

Prescott is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Sedona shares a name with a place in another part of Arizona; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
