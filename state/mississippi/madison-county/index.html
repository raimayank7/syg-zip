Where Madison County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Madison County fall close to the middle of Mississippi. Its codes spread over about 26 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 39045 (Camden) and 39071 (Flora), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hinds County (19 miles), Rankin County (21 miles), Yazoo County (24 miles) and Scott County (35 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Madison County begin with 391 (5 codes) and 390 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 391 is shared with 19 other counties, such as Adams County, Attala County and Carroll County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 637 square miles of land (1,650 sq km). The largest is 39046 (Canton) with 312 square miles, and the smallest is 39157 (Ridgeland) with 30.0. The middle-sized code here covers 109 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. Water adds about 27.5 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Madison County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 39130 (Madison), 39158 (Ridgeland) and 39163 (Sharon). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Madison County ranks 18th among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 531 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Madison County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MS and the ZIP code:

  • Madison, MS 39110 (one of 2 codes for Madison; the street decides which)
  • Ridgeland, MS 39157 (one of 2 codes for Ridgeland; the street decides which)
  • Camden, MS 39045

Madison is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 25 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Madison County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Madison, MS?

Madison has 2 ZIP codes in Madison County: 39110, 39130. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Madison County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 391, on 5 of the 8 codes.

Which ZIP code in Madison County covers the most land?

39046 (Canton), at about 312 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
