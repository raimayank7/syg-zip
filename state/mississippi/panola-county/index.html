Where Panola County is

The ZIP codes of Panola County are found in the north of Mississippi. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 28 miles, and from east to west about 15 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 38658 (Pope) and 38665 (Sarah), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Quitman County (18 miles), Tate County (20 miles), Tunica County (23 miles) and Yalobusha County (29 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 386. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 386 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Benton County, Coahoma County and DeSoto County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 765 square miles of land (1,982 sq km). The largest is 38606 (Batesville) with 251 square miles, and the smallest is 38658 (Pope) with 43.0. The middle-sized code here covers 65.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. Water adds about 37.9 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Panola County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Panola County ranks 25th among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 531 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Panola County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MS and the ZIP code:

  • Batesville, MS 38606
  • Como, MS 38619
  • Courtland, MS 38620

Batesville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
