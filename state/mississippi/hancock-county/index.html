Where Hancock County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Hancock County is in the south of Mississippi. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 13 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 39556 (Kiln) and 39572 (Pearlington), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Pearl River County (22 miles), Harrison County (26 miles), St. Tammany Parish, Louisiana (30 miles) and Stone County (35 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 395. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 395 is shared with 3 other counties, Harrison County, Jackson County and Stone County, and has 41 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 244 square miles of land (632 sq km). The largest is 39556 (Kiln) with 103 square miles, and the smallest is 39576 (Waveland) with 6.5. The middle-sized code here covers 13.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 8.0 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hancock County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 39521 (Bay Saint Louis), 39522 (Stennis Space Center) and 39558 (Lakeshore). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Hancock County ranks 13th among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 531 codes in the state list.
