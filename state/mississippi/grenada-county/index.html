Where Grenada County is

The ZIP codes of Grenada County are found in the north of Mississippi. Its codes spread over about 6.8 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 38929 (Gore Springs) and 38940 (Holcomb), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Yalobusha County (18 miles), Montgomery County (21 miles), Carroll County (22 miles) and Tallahatchie County (27 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Grenada County starts with 389. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 389 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Calhoun County, Carroll County and Holmes County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 3 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 463 square miles of land (1,200 sq km). The largest is 38901 (Grenada) with 242 square miles, and the smallest is 38929 (Gore Springs) with 71.2. The middle-sized code here covers 150 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. Water adds about 19.0 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Grenada County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 38902 (Grenada), 38926 (Elliott) and 38960 (Tie Plant). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Grenada County ranks 31st among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 531 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Grenada County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MS and the ZIP code:

  • Grenada, MS 38901 (one of 2 codes for Grenada; the street decides which)
  • Elliott, MS 38926
  • Gore Springs, MS 38929
