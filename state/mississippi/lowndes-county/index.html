Where Lowndes County is

The ZIP codes of Lowndes County are found in the north-east of Mississippi. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 39743 (Crawford) and 39766 (Steens), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Oktibbeha County (23 miles), Clay County (24 miles), Pickens County, Alabama (25 miles) and Monroe County (26 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 397. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 397 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Calhoun County, Chickasaw County and Choctaw County, and has 35 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 462 square miles of land (1,197 sq km). The largest is 39701 (Columbus) with 191 square miles, and the smallest is 39753 (Mayhew) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 66.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lowndes County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 39703 (Columbus), 39704 (Columbus) and 39710 (Columbus). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lowndes County ranks 9th among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 531 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lowndes County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MS and the ZIP code:

  • Columbus, MS 39701 (one of 6 codes for Columbus; the street decides which)
  • Artesia, MS 39736
  • Crawford, MS 39743

Columbus is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 16 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
