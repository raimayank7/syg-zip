Where Clarke County is

The ZIP codes of Clarke County are found in the east of Mississippi. Its codes spread over about 14 miles from north to south and 12 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 39330 (Enterprise) and 39360 (Shubuta), about 16 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Jasper County (21 miles), Lauderdale County (27 miles), Newton County (29 miles) and Choctaw County, Alabama (31 miles).

The first three digits

All 5 codes here begin with the same three digits, 393. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 393 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Greene County, Jasper County and Kemper County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 733 square miles of land (1,899 sq km). The largest is 39355 (Quitman) with 279 square miles, and the smallest is 39363 (Stonewall) with 23.2. The middle-sized code here covers 128 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clarke County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Clarke County ranks 38th among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 531 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clarke County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MS and the ZIP code:

  • Enterprise, MS 39330
  • Pachuta, MS 39347
  • Quitman, MS 39355

Enterprise is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
