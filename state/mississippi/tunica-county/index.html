Where Tunica County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Tunica County fall in the north-west of Mississippi. Its codes spread over about 30 miles from north to south and 9.1 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 38664 (Robinsonville) and 38670 (Sledge), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Tate County (21 miles), Panola County (23 miles), Quitman County (25 miles) and DeSoto County (27 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Tunica County starts with 386. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 386 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Benton County, Coahoma County and DeSoto County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 540 square miles of land (1,399 sq km). The largest is 38676 (Tunica) with 170 square miles, and the smallest is 38664 (Robinsonville) with 99.2. The middle-sized code here covers 135 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. Water adds about 21.8 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Tunica County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Tunica County ranks 52nd among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 531 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Tunica County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MS and the ZIP code:

  • Dundee, MS 38626
  • Robinsonville, MS 38664
  • Sledge, MS 38670

Dundee is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
