Where Tate County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Tate County fall in the north of Mississippi. Its codes spread over about 4.4 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 38602 (Arkabutla) and 38638 (Independence), about 16 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are DeSoto County (18 miles), Panola County (20 miles), Tunica County (21 miles) and Marshall County (28 miles).

The first three digits

All 4 codes here begin with the same three digits, 386. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 386 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Benton County, Coahoma County and DeSoto County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 2 of the 4 codes. Together they come to about 359 square miles of land (929 sq km). The largest is 38618 (Coldwater) with 198 square miles, and the smallest is 38668 (Senatobia) with 160. The middle-sized code here covers 179 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Tate County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 38602 (Arkabutla) and 38638 (Independence). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Tate County ranks 52nd among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 531 codes in the state list.
