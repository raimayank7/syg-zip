Where Lee County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Lee County is in the north-east of Mississippi. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 38824 (Baldwyn) and 38868 (Shannon), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Itawamba County (19 miles), Pontotoc County (19 miles), Union County (23 miles) and Prentiss County (24 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 388. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 388 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Alcorn County, Calhoun County and Chickasaw County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 599 square miles of land (1,551 sq km). The largest is 38824 (Baldwyn) with 147 square miles, and the smallest is 38879 (Verona) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 72.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lee County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 38802 (Tupelo) and 38803 (Tupelo). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lee County ranks 8th among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 531 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lee County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MS and the ZIP code:

  • Tupelo, MS 38801 (one of 4 codes for Tupelo; the street decides which)
  • Baldwyn, MS 38824
  • Belden, MS 38826

Questions about Lee County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Tupelo, MS?

Tupelo has 4 ZIP codes in Lee County: 38801, 38802, 38803, 38804. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Lee County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 12 start with 388.

Which ZIP code in Lee County covers the most land?

38824 (Baldwyn), at about 147 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
