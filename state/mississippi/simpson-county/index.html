Where Simpson County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Simpson County is in the south of Mississippi. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 39082 (Harrisville) and 39111 (Magee), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Rankin County (23 miles), Smith County (25 miles), Copiah County (26 miles) and Lawrence County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Simpson County begin with 390 (3 codes) and 391 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 390 is shared with 14 other counties, such as Attala County, Claiborne County and Copiah County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 614 square miles of land (1,589 sq km). The largest is 39114 (Mendenhall) with 190 square miles, and the smallest is 39062 (D Lo) with 1.4. The middle-sized code here covers 110 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Simpson County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Simpson County ranks 31st among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 531 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Simpson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MS and the ZIP code:
