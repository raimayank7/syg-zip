Where Pike County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Pike County fall in the south-west of Mississippi. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 5.1 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 39657 (Osyka) and 39666 (Summit), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Walthall County (20 miles), Lincoln County (24 miles), Amite County (25 miles) and St. Helena Parish, Louisiana (31 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 396. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 396 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Amite County, Franklin County and Jefferson County, and has 30 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 525 square miles of land (1,359 sq km). The largest is 39666 (Summit) with 164 square miles, and the smallest is 39635 (Fernwood) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 108 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pike County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 39632 (Chatawa) and 39649 (Mccomb). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Pike County ranks 25th among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 531 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pike County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MS and the ZIP code:

  • Mccomb, MS 39648 (one of 2 codes for Mccomb; the street decides which)
  • Chatawa, MS 39632
  • Fernwood, MS 39635
