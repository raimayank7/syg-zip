Where Webster County is

The ZIP codes of Webster County are found in the north-east of Mississippi. Its codes spread over about 7.4 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 39737 (Bellefontaine) and 39750 (Maben), about 15 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Choctaw County (20 miles), Clay County (22 miles), Calhoun County (22 miles) and Oktibbeha County (23 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Webster County starts with 397. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 397 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Calhoun County, Chickasaw County and Choctaw County, and has 35 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 427 square miles of land (1,106 sq km). The largest is 39744 (Eupora) with 244 square miles, and the smallest is 39771 (Walthall) with 1.0. The middle-sized code here covers 73.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Webster County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Webster County ranks 38th among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 531 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Webster County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MS and the ZIP code:
