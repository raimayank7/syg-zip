Where Harrison County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Harrison County fall in the south-east of Mississippi. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 39533 (Biloxi) and 39571 (Pass Christian), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Stone County (25 miles), Jackson County (26 miles), Hancock County (26 miles) and Pearl River County (37 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Harrison County starts with 395. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 395 is shared with 3 other counties, Hancock County, Jackson County and Stone County, and has 41 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 563 square miles of land (1,457 sq km). The largest is 39574 (Saucier) with 213 square miles, and the smallest is 39534 (Biloxi) with 1.8. The middle-sized code here covers 14.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 17.8 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Harrison County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 39502 (Gulfport), 39505 (Gulfport), 39506 (Gulfport), 39533 (Biloxi) and 39535 (Biloxi). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Harrison County ranks 2nd among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 531 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Harrison County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MS and the ZIP code:

  • Gulfport, MS 39501 (one of 6 codes for Gulfport; the street decides which)
