Where Coahoma County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Coahoma County fall in the north-west of Mississippi. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 38644 (Lula) and 38739 (Dublin), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Quitman County (19 miles), Phillips County, Arkansas (19 miles), Tunica County (28 miles) and Tallahatchie County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Coahoma County begin with 386 (8 codes) and 387 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 386 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Benton County, DeSoto County and Lafayette County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 402 square miles of land (1,042 sq km). The largest is 38614 (Clarksdale) with 243 square miles, and the smallest is 38644 (Lula) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 4.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Coahoma County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 38630 (Farrell), 38669 (Sherard) and 38739 (Dublin). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Coahoma County ranks 9th among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 531 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Coahoma County
