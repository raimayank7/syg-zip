Where Tallahatchie County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Tallahatchie County is in the north-west of Mississippi. Its codes spread over about 25 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 38927 (Enid) and 38950 (Philipp), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Quitman County (21 miles), Yalobusha County (25 miles), Grenada County (27 miles) and Leflore County (28 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Tallahatchie County starts with 389. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 389 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Calhoun County, Carroll County and Grenada County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 577 square miles of land (1,496 sq km). The largest is 38963 (Tutwiler) with 174 square miles, and the smallest is 38957 (Sumner) with 10.8. The middle-sized code here covers 51.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Tallahatchie County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 38958 (Swan Lake). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Tallahatchie County ranks 9th among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 531 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Tallahatchie County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MS and the ZIP code:
