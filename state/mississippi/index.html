Mississippi ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 531 ZIP codes for Mississippi, about 1.3% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 32nd among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 82 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Hinds County (36), Bolivar County (16), Harrison County (16), Rankin County (15) and Washington County (14). At the other end, 5 have only one or two codes, for example George County, Neshoba County and Walthall County.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Mississippi uses 12 of these prefixes, from 386 to 397. The busiest are 386 (64 codes, most of them for Holly Springs), 391 (57 codes, most of them for Vicksburg), 388 (50 codes, most of them for Tupelo) and 387 (46 codes, most of them for Greenville). The first digit 3 is shared with Alabama, Florida, Georgia and Tennessee.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 427 of the 531 codes. The largest is 39452 (Lucedale, George County) at 535 square miles and the smallest is 39269 (Jackson) at 0.01; the middle value is 83.6 square miles. The other 104 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 38671 (Southaven) and the southernmost 39558 (Lakeshore); the one farthest east is 38847 (Golden) and farthest west 39121 (Natchez).

Place names and addresses in Mississippi

Of the 438 place names in the list, 406 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Jackson (27), Meridian (7), Biloxi (6) and Columbus (6). 1 place name appears under more than one county, among them Jackson: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters MS and the code, as in Jackson, MS 39201. The name of the county is left out.
