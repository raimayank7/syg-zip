Where Jackson County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Jackson County is in the south-east of Mississippi. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 39555 (Hurley) and 39566 (Ocean Springs), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Harrison County (26 miles), George County (29 miles), Mobile County, Alabama (35 miles) and Stone County (39 miles).

The first three digits

All 13 codes here begin with the same three digits, 395. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 395 is shared with 3 other counties, Hancock County, Harrison County and Stone County, and has 41 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 599 square miles of land (1,550 sq km). The largest is 39565 (Vancleave) with 275 square miles, and the smallest is 39595 (Pascagoula) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 24.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 23.9 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jackson County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 39552 (Escatawpa), 39555 (Hurley), 39566 (Ocean Springs), 39568 (Pascagoula) and 39569 (Pascagoula). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Jackson County ranks 6th among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 531 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jackson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MS and the ZIP code:

  • Pascagoula, MS 39567 (one of 5 codes for Pascagoula; the street decides which)
  • Moss Point, MS 39562 (one of 2 codes for Moss Point; the street decides which)
  • Ocean Springs, MS 39564 (one of 2 codes for Ocean Springs; the street decides which)
