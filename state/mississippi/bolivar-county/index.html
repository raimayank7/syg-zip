Where Bolivar County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Bolivar County fall in the north-west of Mississippi. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 39 miles, and from east to west about 18 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 38720 (Alligator) and 38772 (Scott), about 43 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Sunflower County (20 miles), Desha County, Arkansas (28 miles), Coahoma County (34 miles) and Washington County (36 miles).

The first three digits

All 16 codes here begin with the same three digits, 387. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 387 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Coahoma County, Humphreys County and Issaquena County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 975 square miles of land (2,526 sq km). The largest is 38725 (Benoit) with 147 square miles, and the smallest is 38733 (Cleveland) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 63.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. Water adds about 35.9 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bolivar County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Bolivar County ranks 2nd among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 531 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bolivar County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MS and the ZIP code:

  • Cleveland, MS 38732 (one of 2 codes for Cleveland; the street decides which)
  • Alligator, MS 38720
  • Benoit, MS 38725

Cleveland is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 18 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
