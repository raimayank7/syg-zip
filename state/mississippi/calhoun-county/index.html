Where Calhoun County is

The ZIP codes of Calhoun County are found in the north-east of Mississippi. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 38913 (Banner) and 39751 (Mantee), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Chickasaw County (22 miles), Webster County (22 miles), Yalobusha County (29 miles) and Grenada County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Calhoun County begin with 389 (6 codes), 388 (2 codes) and 397 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 389 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Carroll County, Grenada County and Holmes County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 573 square miles of land (1,485 sq km). The largest is 38916 (Calhoun City) with 176 square miles, and the smallest is 38839 (Derma) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 63.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Calhoun County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 38955 (Slate Spring). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Calhoun County ranks 13th among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 531 codes in the state list.
