Where DeSoto County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of DeSoto County fall in the north of Mississippi. Its codes spread over about 12 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 38641 (Lake Cormorant) and 38654 (Olive Branch), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Shelby County, Tennessee (17 miles), Tate County (18 miles), Crittenden County, Arkansas (25 miles) and Tunica County (27 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 386. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 386 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Benton County, Coahoma County and Lafayette County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 439 square miles of land (1,137 sq km). The largest is 38632 (Hernando) with 162 square miles, and the smallest is 38672 (Southaven) with 13.0. The middle-sized code here covers 28.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 19.3 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of DeSoto County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 38686 (Walls). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, DeSoto County ranks 13th among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 531 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in DeSoto County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MS and the ZIP code:
