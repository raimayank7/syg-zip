Where Washington County is

The ZIP codes of Washington County are found in the north-west of Mississippi. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 33 miles, and from east to west about 13 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 38744 (Glen Allan) and 38782 (Winterville), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Chicot County, Arkansas (20 miles), Sharkey County (25 miles), Humphreys County (29 miles) and Issaquena County (32 miles).

The first three digits

All 14 codes here begin with the same three digits, 387. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 387 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Bolivar County, Coahoma County and Humphreys County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 779 square miles of land (2,019 sq km). The largest is 38748 (Hollandale) with 261 square miles, and the smallest is 38776 (Stoneville) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 42.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. Water adds about 36.1 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Washington County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 38702 (Greenville), 38704 (Greenville), 38780 (Wayside) and 38782 (Winterville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Washington County ranks 5th among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 531 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Washington County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MS and the ZIP code:

  • Greenville, MS 38701 (one of 4 codes for Greenville; the street decides which)
  • Arcola, MS 38722
  • Avon, MS 38723

Greenville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 23 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
