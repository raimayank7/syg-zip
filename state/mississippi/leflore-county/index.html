Where Leflore County is

The ZIP codes of Leflore County are found in the north-west of Mississippi. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 26 miles, and from east to west about 11 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 38944 (Minter City) and 38946 (Morgan City), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Sunflower County (17 miles), Carroll County (21 miles), Tallahatchie County (28 miles) and Grenada County (31 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 389. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 389 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Calhoun County, Carroll County and Grenada County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 517 square miles of land (1,339 sq km). The largest is 38930 (Greenwood) with 194 square miles, and the smallest is 38946 (Morgan City) with 9.6. The middle-sized code here covers 106 square miles; the middle value for all of Mississippi is 83.6. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Leflore County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 38935 (Greenwood), 38945 (Money) and 38959 (Swiftown). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Leflore County ranks 18th among the 82 counties and county-level areas of Mississippi (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 531 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Leflore County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MS and the ZIP code:

  • Greenwood, MS 38930 (one of 2 codes for Greenwood; the street decides which)
  • Itta Bena, MS 38941
  • Minter City, MS 38944

Greenwood is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 14 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
