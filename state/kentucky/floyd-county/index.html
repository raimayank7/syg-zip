Where Floyd County is

The ZIP codes of Floyd County are found in the south-east of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 29 miles from north to south and 13 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 41602 (Auxier) and 41667 (Weeksbury), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Knott County (16 miles), Magoffin County (20 miles), Pike County (21 miles) and Johnson County (24 miles).

The first three digits

All 33 codes here begin with the same three digits, 416. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 416 is shared with 1 other county, Magoffin County, and has 34 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 421 square miles of land (1,091 sq km). The largest is 41653 (Prestonsburg) with 125 square miles, and the smallest is 41612 (Bypro) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 7.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Floyd County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Floyd County ranks 4th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 33 of the 946 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Floyd County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KY and the ZIP code:

  • Allen, KY 41601
  • Auxier, KY 41602

Allen is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
