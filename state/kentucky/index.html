Kentucky ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 946 ZIP codes for Kentucky, about 2.3% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 16th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 120 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Jefferson County (73), Pike County (41), Fayette County (39), Floyd County (33) and Harlan County (26). At the other end, 20 have only one or two codes, for example Anderson County, Caldwell County and Clark County.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Kentucky uses 27 of these prefixes, from 400 to 427. The busiest are 410 (68 codes, most of them for Covington), 402 (66 codes, most of them for Louisville), 400 (54 codes, most of them for La Grange) and 420 (52 codes, most of them for Paducah). The first digit 4 is shared with Indiana, Michigan and Ohio.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 780 of the 946 codes. The largest is 42633 (Monticello, Wayne County) at 419 square miles and the smallest is 42444 (Poole) at 0.01; the middle value is 25.3 square miles. The other 166 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 41074 (Dayton) and the southernmost 42049 (Hazel); the one farthest east is 41568 (Stopover) and farthest west 42050 (Hickman).

Place names and addresses in Kentucky

Of the 785 place names in the list, 749 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Louisville (66), Lexington (39), Frankfort (9) and London (6). In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters KY and the code, as in Louisville, KY 40201. The name of the county is left out.
