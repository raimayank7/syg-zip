Where Grayson County is

The ZIP codes of Grayson County are found in the south of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 9.0 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 42712 (Big Clifty) and 42721 (Caneyville), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Edmonson County (17 miles), Breckinridge County (22 miles), Hardin County (26 miles) and Butler County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Grayson County begin with 427 (6 codes) and 401 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 427 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Adair County, Cumberland County and Green County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 605 square miles of land (1,567 sq km). The largest is 42754 (Leitchfield) with 189 square miles, and the smallest is 42762 (Millwood) with 9.4. The middle-sized code here covers 105 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Grayson County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 42755 (Leitchfield). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Grayson County ranks 37th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 946 codes in the state list.

Questions about Grayson County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Leitchfield, KY?

Leitchfield has 2 ZIP codes in Grayson County: 42754, 42755. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Grayson County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 427, on 6 of the 7 codes.

Which ZIP code in Grayson County covers the most land?

42754 (Leitchfield), at about 189 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
