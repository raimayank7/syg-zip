Where Campbell County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Campbell County fall in the north-east of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 14 miles from north to south and 8.7 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 41007 (California) and 41074 (Dayton), about 16 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Kenton County (7.0 miles), Hamilton County, Ohio (12 miles), Clermont County, Ohio (13 miles) and Boone County (16 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Campbell County starts with 410. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 410 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Boone County, Bracken County and Carroll County, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 150 square miles of land (388 sq km). The largest is 41001 (Alexandria) with 51.7 square miles, and the smallest is 41085 (Silver Grove) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 5.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Campbell County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 41072 (Newport) and 41099 (Newport). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Campbell County ranks 17th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 946 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Campbell County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KY and the ZIP code:

  • Newport, KY 41071 (one of 4 codes for Newport; the street decides which)
  • Alexandria, KY 41001
  • Bellevue, KY 41073

Newport is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 18 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
