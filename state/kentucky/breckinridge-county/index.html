Where Breckinridge County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Breckinridge County fall close to the middle of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 40152 (Mc Daniels) and 40171 (Union Star), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Meade County (16 miles), Hancock County (22 miles), Grayson County (22 miles) and Perry County, Indiana (23 miles).

The first three digits

All 13 codes here begin with the same three digits, 401. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 401 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Bullitt County, Grayson County and Hardin County, and has 36 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 487 square miles of land (1,262 sq km). The largest is 40143 (Hardinsburg) with 142 square miles, and the smallest is 40178 (Westview) with 8.2. The middle-sized code here covers 30.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Breckinridge County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 40153 (Mc Quady). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Breckinridge County ranks 13th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 946 codes in the state list.
