Where Webster County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Webster County is in the south-west of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 42455 (Sebree) and 42463 (Wheatcroft), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Union County (16 miles), Hopkins County (19 miles), Henderson County (21 miles) and McLean County (24 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 424. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 424 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Caldwell County, Henderson County and Hopkins County, and has 32 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 385 square miles of land (996 sq km). The largest is 42404 (Clay) with 87.7 square miles, and the smallest is 42444 (Poole) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 72.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Webster County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Webster County ranks 37th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 946 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Webster County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KY and the ZIP code:

  • Clay, KY 42404
  • Dixon, KY 42409
  • Poole, KY 42444

Clay is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
