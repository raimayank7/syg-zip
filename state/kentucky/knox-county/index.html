Where Knox County is

The ZIP codes of Knox County are found in the south-east of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 14 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 40771 (Woodbine) and 40997 (Walker), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Bell County (12 miles), Whitley County (15 miles), Laurel County (22 miles) and Clay County (22 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Knox County begin with 409 (15 codes) and 407 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 409 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Bell County, Clay County and Harlan County, and has 35 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 16 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 361 square miles of land (934 sq km). The largest is 40906 (Barbourville) with 137 square miles, and the smallest is 40946 (Green Road) with 1.6. The middle-sized code here covers 9.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Knox County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 40930 (Dewitt). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Knox County ranks 10th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 946 codes in the state list.
