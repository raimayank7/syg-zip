Where Hardin County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Hardin County sits near the middle of Kentucky. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 37 miles, and from east to west about 13 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 40177 (West Point) and 42784 (Upton), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Larue County (22 miles), Meade County (23 miles), Bullitt County (25 miles) and Breckinridge County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Hardin County begin with 427 (8 codes) and 401 (7 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 427 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Adair County, Cumberland County and Grayson County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 661 square miles of land (1,712 sq km). The largest is 42701 (Elizabethtown) with 162 square miles, and the smallest is 42788 (White Mills) with 7.6. The middle-sized code here covers 51.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hardin County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 40122 (Fort Knox), 40159 (Radcliff) and 42702 (Elizabethtown). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Hardin County ranks 12th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 946 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hardin County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KY and the ZIP code:

  • Elizabethtown, KY 42701 (one of 2 codes for Elizabethtown; the street decides which)
  • Fort Knox, KY 40121 (one of 2 codes for Fort Knox; the street decides which)
  • Radcliff, KY 40159 (one of 2 codes for Radcliff; the street decides which)

Elizabethtown is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Hardin County ZIP codes
