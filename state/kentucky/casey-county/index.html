Where Casey County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Casey County fall in the south of Kentucky. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 14 miles, and from east to west about 12 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 42528 (Dunnville) and 42541 (Middleburg), about 14 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lincoln County (16 miles), Pulaski County (20 miles), Russell County (20 miles) and Taylor County (23 miles).

The first three digits

All 6 codes here begin with the same three digits, 425. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 425 is shared with 1 other county, Pulaski County, and has 17 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 340 square miles of land (882 sq km). The largest is 42539 (Liberty) with 220 square miles, and the smallest is 42516 (Bethelridge) with 12.6. The middle-sized code here covers 20.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Casey County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Casey County ranks 48th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 946 codes in the state list.
