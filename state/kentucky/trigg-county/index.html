Where Trigg County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Trigg County fall in the south-west of Kentucky. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 10 miles, and from east to west about 9.1 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 42211 (Cadiz) and 42215 (Cerulean), about 14 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Christian County (15 miles), Caldwell County (23 miles), Lyon County (23 miles) and Stewart County, Tennessee (25 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Trigg County starts with 422. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 422 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Butler County, Christian County and Edmonson County, and has 36 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 341 square miles of land (883 sq km). The largest is 42211 (Cadiz) with 284 square miles, and the smallest is 42215 (Cerulean) with 56.4. The middle-sized code here covers 170 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 18.8 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Trigg County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Trigg County ranks 101st among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 2 of the 946 codes in the state list.
