Where Morgan County is

The ZIP codes of Morgan County are found in the east of Kentucky. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 14 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 41413 (Crockett) and 41425 (Ezel), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Elliott County (17 miles), Wolfe County (19 miles), Menifee County (19 miles) and Magoffin County (19 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Morgan County begin with 414 (7 codes) and 413 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 414 is shared with 1 other county, Magoffin County, and has 10 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 342 square miles of land (886 sq km). The largest is 41472 (West Liberty) with 270 square miles, and the smallest is 41408 (Cannel City) with 5.5. The middle-sized code here covers 20.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Morgan County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 41413 (Crockett), 41451 (Malone) and 41477 (Wrigley). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Morgan County ranks 31st among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 946 codes in the state list.
