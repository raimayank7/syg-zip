Where Grant County is

The ZIP codes of Grant County are found in the north-east of Kentucky. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 11 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 41010 (Corinth) and 41030 (Crittenden), about 17 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Gallatin County (14 miles), Pendleton County (14 miles), Owen County (16 miles) and Harrison County (21 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Grant County starts with 410. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 410 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Boone County, Bracken County and Campbell County, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 333 square miles of land (861 sq km). The largest is 41035 (Dry Ridge) with 102 square miles, and the smallest is 41052 (Jonesville) with 10.5. The middle-sized code here covers 91.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Grant County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 41054 (Mason). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Grant County ranks 48th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 946 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Grant County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KY and the ZIP code:

  • Corinth, KY 41010
  • Crittenden, KY 41030
  • Dry Ridge, KY 41035

Corinth is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
