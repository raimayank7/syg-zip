Where Bell County is

The ZIP codes of Bell County are found in the south-east of Kentucky. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 40913 (Beverly) and 40940 (Frakes), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Knox County (12 miles), Claiborne County, Tennessee (17 miles), Whitley County (24 miles) and Harlan County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Bell County begin with 409 (8 codes) and 408 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 409 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Clay County, Harlan County and Knox County, and has 35 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 386 square miles of land (1,000 sq km). The largest is 40977 (Pineville) with 134 square miles, and the smallest is 40845 (Hulen) with 8.7. The middle-sized code here covers 26.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bell County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 40955 (Ingram). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Bell County ranks 17th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 946 codes in the state list.
