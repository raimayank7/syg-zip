Where Breathitt County is

The ZIP codes of Breathitt County are found in the east of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 10 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 41310 (Bays) and 41390 (Whick), about 17 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Perry County (19 miles), Magoffin County (19 miles), Lee County (21 miles) and Wolfe County (21 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Breathitt County starts with 413. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 413 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Lee County, Morgan County and Owsley County, and has 21 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 451 square miles of land (1,168 sq km). The largest is 41339 (Jackson) with 330 square miles, and the smallest is 41390 (Whick) with 1.6. The middle-sized code here covers 26.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Breathitt County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Breathitt County ranks 37th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 946 codes in the state list.
