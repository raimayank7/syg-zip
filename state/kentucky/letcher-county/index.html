Where Letcher County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Letcher County fall in the south-east of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 41517 (Burdine) and 41819 (Gordon), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Knott County (14 miles), Wise County, Virginia (17 miles), Norton (city), Virginia (18 miles) and Perry County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Letcher County begin with 418 (20 codes), 408 (2 codes) and 415 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 418 is shared with 1 other county, Knott County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 356 square miles of land (922 sq km). The largest is 41858 (Whitesburg) with 87.3 square miles, and the smallest is 41517 (Burdine) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 7.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Letcher County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Letcher County ranks 7th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 24 of the 946 codes in the state list.
