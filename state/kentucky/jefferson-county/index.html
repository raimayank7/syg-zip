Where Jefferson County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Jefferson County is in the north of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 40018 (Eastwood) and 40272 (Louisville), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Floyd County, Indiana (14 miles), Bullitt County (15 miles), Clark County, Indiana (15 miles) and Oldham County (17 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Jefferson County begin with 402 (66 codes), 400 (6 codes) and 401 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 402, so those three digits alone point to Jefferson County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 39 of the 73 codes. Together they come to about 422 square miles of land (1,093 sq km). The largest is 40299 (Louisville) with 54.0 square miles, and the smallest is 40231 (Louisville) with 0.07. The middle-sized code here covers 7.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jefferson County; a code can reach across its border. 34 codes have no mapped area: 40018 (Eastwood), 40027 (Harrods Creek), 40201 (Louisville), 40224 (Louisville), 40225 (Louisville) and 29 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Kentucky has more ZIP codes: Jefferson County holds 73 of the 946 in the state list, about 7.7%.

Writing an address in Jefferson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KY and the ZIP code:

  • Louisville, KY 40201 (one of 66 codes for Louisville; the street decides which)
  • Eastwood, KY 40018
  • Fairdale, KY 40118

Louisville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
