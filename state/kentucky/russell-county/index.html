Where Russell County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Russell County fall in the south of Kentucky. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 6.0 miles, and from east to west about 3.3 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 42629 (Jamestown) and 42642 (Russell Springs), about 6.8 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Adair County (15 miles), Wayne County (17 miles), Clinton County (19 miles) and Casey County (20 miles).

The first three digits

All 2 codes here begin with the same three digits, 426. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 426 is shared with 3 other counties, Clinton County, McCreary County and Wayne County, and has 12 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 241 square miles of land (624 sq km). The largest is 42642 (Russell Springs) with 140 square miles, and the smallest is 42629 (Jamestown) with 101. The middle-sized code here covers 120 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 24.2 square miles, roughly 9% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Russell County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Russell County ranks 101st among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 2 of the 946 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Russell County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KY and the ZIP code:

  • Jamestown, KY 42629
  • Russell Springs, KY 42642

Jamestown is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 17 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
