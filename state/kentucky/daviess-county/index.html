Where Daviess County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Daviess County fall in the west of Kentucky. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 42334 (Curdsville) and 42378 (Whitesville), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are McLean County (17 miles), Hancock County (18 miles), Spencer County, Indiana (24 miles) and Henderson County (25 miles).

The first three digits

All 11 codes here begin with the same three digits, 423. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 423 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Hancock County, McLean County and Muhlenberg County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 497 square miles of land (1,287 sq km). The largest is 42301 (Owensboro) with 173 square miles, and the smallest is 42356 (Maple Mount) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 61.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Daviess County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 42302 (Owensboro), 42304 (Owensboro), 42334 (Curdsville) and 42377 (West Louisville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Daviess County ranks 17th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 946 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Daviess County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KY and the ZIP code:

  • Owensboro, KY 42301 (one of 4 codes for Owensboro; the street decides which)
  • Curdsville, KY 42334
  • Maceo, KY 42355
