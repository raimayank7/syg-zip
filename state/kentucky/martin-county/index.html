Where Martin County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Martin County is in the east of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 9.5 miles from north to south and 11 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 41250 (Pilgrim) and 41262 (Tomahawk), about 12 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Johnson County (17 miles), Mingo County, West Virginia (21 miles), Lawrence County (22 miles) and Wayne County, West Virginia (25 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Martin County starts with 412. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 412 is shared with 2 other counties, Johnson County and Lawrence County, and has 29 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 221 square miles of land (572 sq km). The largest is 41224 (Inez) with 83.7 square miles, and the smallest is 41267 (Warfield) with 6.0. The middle-sized code here covers 15.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Martin County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Martin County ranks 37th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 946 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Martin County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KY and the ZIP code:
