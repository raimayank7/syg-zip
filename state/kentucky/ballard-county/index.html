Where Ballard County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Ballard County fall in the south-west of Kentucky. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 12 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 42022 (Bandana) and 42060 (Lovelaceville), about 14 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are McCracken County (13 miles), Carlisle County (14 miles), Pulaski County, Illinois (15 miles) and Massac County, Illinois (17 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Ballard County starts with 420. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 420 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Calloway County, Carlisle County and Crittenden County, and has 52 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 169 square miles of land (439 sq km). The largest is 42087 (Wickliffe) with 68.2 square miles, and the smallest is 42022 (Bandana) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 46.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Water adds about 7.9 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ballard County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Ballard County ranks 65th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 946 codes in the state list.
