Where Laurel County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Laurel County is in the south-east of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 40724 (Bush) and 40729 (East Bernstadt), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Clay County (20 miles), Jackson County (21 miles), Whitley County (22 miles) and Rockcastle County (22 miles).

The first three digits

All 11 codes here begin with the same three digits, 407. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 407 is shared with 2 other counties, Knox County and Whitley County, and has 19 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 409 square miles of land (1,060 sq km). The largest is 40741 (London) with 207 square miles, and the smallest is 40737 (Keavy) with 8.1. The middle-sized code here covers 75.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Laurel County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 40724 (Bush), 40742 (London), 40743 (London), 40745 (London), 40750 (London) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Laurel County ranks 17th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 946 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Laurel County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KY and the ZIP code:

  • London, KY 40741 (one of 6 codes for London; the street decides which)
  • Bush, KY 40724
  • East Bernstadt, KY 40729

London is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
