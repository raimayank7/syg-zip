Where Oldham County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Oldham County is in the north of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 9.6 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 40056 (Pewee Valley) and 40077 (Westport), about 13 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Clark County, Indiana (13 miles), Jefferson County (17 miles), Henry County (18 miles) and Trimble County (19 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Oldham County starts with 400. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 400 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Bullitt County, Henry County and Jefferson County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 162 square miles of land (420 sq km). The largest is 40031 (La Grange) with 66.9 square miles, and the smallest is 40010 (Buckner) with 1.5. The middle-sized code here covers 19.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Oldham County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 40032 (La Grange). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Oldham County ranks 37th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 946 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Oldham County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KY and the ZIP code:

  • La Grange, KY 40031 (one of 2 codes for La Grange; the street decides which)
  • Buckner, KY 40010
  • Crestwood, KY 40014

La Grange is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
