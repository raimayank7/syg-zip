Where Hopkins County is

The ZIP codes of Hopkins County are found in the south-west of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 42441 (Nebo) and 42464 (White Plains), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Webster County (19 miles), McLean County (22 miles), Muhlenberg County (24 miles) and Caldwell County (24 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 424. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 424 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Caldwell County, Henderson County and Union County, and has 32 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 567 square miles of land (1,468 sq km). The largest is 42431 (Madisonville) with 167 square miles, and the smallest is 42440 (Mortons Gap) with 1.4. The middle-sized code here covers 47.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hopkins County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Hopkins County ranks 24th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 946 codes in the state list.
