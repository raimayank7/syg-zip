Where Taylor County is

The ZIP codes of Taylor County are found in the south of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 2.4 miles from north to south and 8.7 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 42718 (Campbellsville) and 42733 (Elk Horn), about 8.7 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Marion County (16 miles), Green County (17 miles), Adair County (18 miles) and Casey County (23 miles).

The first three digits

All 4 codes here begin with the same three digits, 427. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 427 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Adair County, Cumberland County and Grayson County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 3 of the 4 codes. Together they come to about 321 square miles of land (833 sq km). The largest is 42718 (Campbellsville) with 266 square miles, and the smallest is 42758 (Mannsville) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 55.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 10.8 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Taylor County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 42719 (Campbellsville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Taylor County ranks 75th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 946 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Taylor County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KY and the ZIP code:
