Where Ohio County is

The ZIP codes of Ohio County are found in the south-west of Kentucky. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 18 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 42343 (Fordsville) and 42369 (Rockport), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Butler County (18 miles), Muhlenberg County (21 miles), McLean County (24 miles) and Daviess County (25 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Ohio County starts with 423. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 423 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Daviess County, Hancock County and McLean County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 550 square miles of land (1,423 sq km). The largest is 42320 (Beaver Dam) with 149 square miles, and the smallest is 42370 (Rosine) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 37.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ohio County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Ohio County ranks 17th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 946 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Ohio County
