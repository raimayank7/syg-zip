Where Bullitt County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Bullitt County fall close to the middle of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 12 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 40047 (Mount Washington) and 40150 (Lebanon Junction), about 16 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Jefferson County (15 miles), Nelson County (18 miles), Spencer County (23 miles) and Hardin County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Bullitt County begin with 401 (6 codes) and 400 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 401 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Breckinridge County, Grayson County and Hardin County, and has 36 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 223 square miles of land (577 sq km). The largest is 40165 (Shepherdsville) with 113 square miles, and the smallest is 40110 (Clermont) with 5.0. The middle-sized code here covers 29.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bullitt County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 40129 (Hillview) and 40166 (Shepherdsville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Bullitt County ranks 37th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 946 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bullitt County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters KY and the ZIP code:

  • Shepherdsville, KY 40165 (one of 2 codes for Shepherdsville; the street decides which)
  • Brooks, KY 40109
  • Clermont, KY 40110

Questions about Bullitt County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Shepherdsville, KY?

Shepherdsville has 2 ZIP codes in Bullitt County: 40165, 40166. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Bullitt County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 401, on 6 of the 7 codes.

Which ZIP code in Bullitt County covers the most land?

40165 (Shepherdsville), at about 113 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
