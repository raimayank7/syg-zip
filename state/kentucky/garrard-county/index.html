Where Garrard County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Garrard County fall in the east of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 7.3 miles from north to south and 12 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 40410 (Bryantsville) and 40461 (Paint Lick), about 14 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Jessamine County (16 miles), Madison County (17 miles), Lincoln County (17 miles) and Boyle County (17 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Garrard County starts with 404. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 404 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Boyle County, Estill County and Jackson County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 2 of the 3 codes. Together they come to about 215 square miles of land (557 sq km). The largest is 40444 (Lancaster) with 159 square miles, and the smallest is 40461 (Paint Lick) with 55.7. The middle-sized code here covers 108 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Garrard County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 40410 (Bryantsville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Garrard County ranks 90th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 3 of the 946 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Garrard County
