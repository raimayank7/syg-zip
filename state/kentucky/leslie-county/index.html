Where Leslie County is

The ZIP codes of Leslie County are found in the south-east of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 18 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 40840 (Helton) and 41762 (Sizerock), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Perry County (16 miles), Harlan County (18 miles), Clay County (19 miles) and Owsley County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Leslie County begin with 408 (8 codes), 417 (8 codes) and 409 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 408 is shared with 3 other counties, Bell County, Harlan County and Letcher County, and has 37 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 349 square miles of land (905 sq km). The largest is 41749 (Hyden) with 104 square miles, and the smallest is 40803 (Asher) with 1.5. The middle-sized code here covers 12.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Leslie County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Leslie County ranks 10th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 946 codes in the state list.
