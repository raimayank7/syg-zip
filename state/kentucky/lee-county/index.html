Where Lee County is

The ZIP codes of Lee County are found in the east of Kentucky. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 9.8 miles, and from east to west about 6.2 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 41347 (Lone) and 41397 (Zoe), about 11 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Wolfe County (12 miles), Owsley County (14 miles), Powell County (17 miles) and Estill County (18 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Lee County starts with 413. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 413 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Breathitt County, Morgan County and Owsley County, and has 21 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 2 of the 4 codes. Together they come to about 181 square miles of land (469 sq km). The largest is 41311 (Beattyville) with 170 square miles, and the smallest is 41397 (Zoe) with 10.7. The middle-sized code here covers 90.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lee County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 41347 (Lone) and 41368 (Saint Helens). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lee County ranks 75th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 946 codes in the state list.
