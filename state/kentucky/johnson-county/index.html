Where Johnson County is

The ZIP codes of Johnson County are found in the east of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 41226 (Keaton) and 41265 (Van Lear), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lawrence County (13 miles), Magoffin County (15 miles), Martin County (17 miles) and Elliott County (22 miles).

The first three digits

All 18 codes here begin with the same three digits, 412. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 412 is shared with 2 other counties, Lawrence County and Martin County, and has 29 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 278 square miles of land (719 sq km). The largest is 41219 (Flatgap) with 48.3 square miles, and the smallest is 41268 (West Van Lear) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 12.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Johnson County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Johnson County ranks 8th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 946 codes in the state list.
