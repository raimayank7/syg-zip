Where Hancock County is

The ZIP codes of Hancock County are found in the west of Kentucky. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 8.4 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 42351 (Lewisport) and 42368 (Reynolds Station), about 14 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Daviess County (18 miles), Spencer County, Indiana (19 miles), Perry County, Indiana (20 miles) and Breckinridge County (22 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Hancock County starts with 423. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 423 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Daviess County, McLean County and Muhlenberg County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 3 of the 4 codes. Together they come to about 219 square miles of land (567 sq km). The largest is 42348 (Hawesville) with 104 square miles, and the smallest is 42368 (Reynolds Station) with 44.0. The middle-sized code here covers 70.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hancock County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 42364 (Pellville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Hancock County ranks 75th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 946 codes in the state list.
