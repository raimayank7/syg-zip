Where Crittenden County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Crittenden County fall in the south-west of Kentucky. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 11 miles, and from east to west about 5.6 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 42037 (Dycusburg) and 42064 (Marion), about 12 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Caldwell County (12 miles), Livingston County (13 miles), Lyon County (13 miles) and Hardin County, Illinois (17 miles).

The first three digits

All 3 codes here begin with the same three digits, 420. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 420 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Ballard County, Calloway County and Carlisle County, and has 52 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 2 of the 3 codes. Together they come to about 294 square miles of land (762 sq km). The largest is 42064 (Marion) with 294 square miles, and the smallest is 42037 (Dycusburg) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 147 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Crittenden County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 42033 (Crayne). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Crittenden County ranks 90th among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 3 of the 946 codes in the state list.
