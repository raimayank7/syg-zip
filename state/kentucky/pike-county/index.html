Where Pike County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Pike County is in the south-east of Kentucky. Its codes spread over about 30 miles from north to south and 29 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 41503 (South Williamson) and 41563 (Shelby Gap), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Floyd County (21 miles), Mingo County, West Virginia (21 miles), Dickenson County, Virginia (22 miles) and Buchanan County, Virginia (24 miles).

The first three digits

All 41 codes here begin with the same three digits, 415. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 415 is shared with 1 other county, Letcher County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 36 of the 41 codes. Together they come to about 752 square miles of land (1,947 sq km). The largest is 41501 (Pikeville) with 192 square miles, and the smallest is 41526 (Fords Branch) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 10.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Kentucky is 25.3. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pike County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 41502 (Pikeville), 41520 (Dorton), 41542 (Lookout), 41549 (Myra) and 41561 (Rockhouse). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Pike County ranks 2nd among the 120 counties and county-level areas of Kentucky (equal counts share a rank), with 41 of the 946 codes in the state list.
