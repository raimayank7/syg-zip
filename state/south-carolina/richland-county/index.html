Where Richland County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Richland County is in the north of South Carolina. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 32 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 29044 (Eastover) and 29177 (White Rock), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Lexington County (15 miles), Fairfield County (25 miles), Calhoun County (29 miles) and Kershaw County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Richland County begin with 292 (32 codes), 290 (6 codes) and 291 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 292 is shared with 1 other county, Lexington County, and has 34 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 18 of the 40 codes. Together they come to about 687 square miles of land (1,778 sq km). The largest is 29044 (Eastover) with 137 square miles, and the smallest is 29225 (Columbia) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 21.8 square miles; the middle value for all of South Carolina is 56.0. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Richland County; a code can reach across its border. 22 codes have no mapped area: 29002 (Ballentine), 29147 (State Park), 29202 (Columbia), 29211 (Columbia), 29214 (Columbia) and 17 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of South Carolina has more ZIP codes: Richland County holds 40 of the 534 in the state list, about 7.5%.

Writing an address in Richland County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SC and the ZIP code:

  • Columbia, SC 29201 (one of 32 codes for Columbia; the street decides which)
  • Ballentine, SC 29002

ZIP codes named Columbia are also listed under Lexington County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Columbia is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 16 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
