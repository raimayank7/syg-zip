Where Aiken County is

The ZIP codes of Aiken County are found in the west of South Carolina. Its codes spread over about 25 miles from north to south and 38 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 29137 (Salley) and 29861 (North Augusta), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Edgefield County (19 miles), Richmond County, Georgia (21 miles), Saluda County (25 miles) and Barnwell County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Aiken County begin with 298 (21 codes) and 291 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 298 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Allendale County, Bamberg County and Barnwell County, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 16 of the 23 codes. Together they come to about 825 square miles of land (2,135 sq km). The largest is 29803 (Aiken) with 128 square miles, and the smallest is 29834 (Langley) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 47.9 square miles; the middle value for all of South Carolina is 56.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Aiken County; a code can reach across its border. 7 codes have no mapped area: 29802 (Aiken), 29804 (Aiken), 29808 (Aiken), 29822 (Clearwater), 29839 (Montmorenci) and 2 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Aiken County ranks 5th among the 46 counties and county-level areas of South Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 23 of the 534 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Aiken County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SC and the ZIP code:

  • North Augusta, SC 29841 (one of 3 codes for North Augusta; the street decides which)
  • Bath, SC 29816

Questions about Aiken County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Aiken, SC?

Aiken has 6 ZIP codes in Aiken County: 29801, 29802, 29803, 29804, 29805, 29808. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Aiken County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 298, on 21 of the 23 codes.

Which ZIP code in Aiken County covers the most land?
