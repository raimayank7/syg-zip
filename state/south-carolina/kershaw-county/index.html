Where Kershaw County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Kershaw County fall in the north of South Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 29009 (Bethune) and 29045 (Elgin), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lee County (29 miles), Sumter County (30 miles), Lancaster County (30 miles) and Richland County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Kershaw County begin with 290 (7 codes) and 291 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 290 is shared with 16 other counties, such as Bamberg County, Calhoun County and Chester County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 647 square miles of land (1,675 sq km). The largest is 29020 (Camden) with 233 square miles, and the smallest is 29175 (Westville) with 8.1. The middle-sized code here covers 78.9 square miles; the middle value for all of South Carolina is 56.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Kershaw County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 29021 (Camden). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Kershaw County ranks 22nd among the 46 counties and county-level areas of South Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 534 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Kershaw County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SC and the ZIP code:

  • Camden, SC 29020 (one of 2 codes for Camden; the street decides which)
  • Bethune, SC 29009
  • Cassatt, SC 29032

Camden is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 15 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Kershaw County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Camden, SC?

Camden has 2 ZIP codes in Kershaw County: 29020, 29021. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Kershaw County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 290, on 7 of the 8 codes.

Which ZIP code in Kershaw County covers the most land?

29020 (Camden), at about 233 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
