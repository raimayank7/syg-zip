Where Greenwood County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Greenwood County is in the north-west of South Carolina. Its codes spread over about 29 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 29692 (Ware Shoals) and 29848 (Troy), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Abbeville County (16 miles), Laurens County (19 miles), McCormick County (27 miles) and Lincoln County, Georgia (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Greenwood County begin with 296 (8 codes) and 298 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 296 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Abbeville County, Anderson County and Greenville County, and has 88 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 571 square miles of land (1,479 sq km). The largest is 29666 (Ninety Six) with 122 square miles, and the smallest is 29692 (Ware Shoals) with 48.5. The middle-sized code here covers 82.5 square miles; the middle value for all of South Carolina is 56.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Greenwood County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 29647 (Greenwood), 29648 (Greenwood) and 29695 (Hodges). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Greenwood County ranks 19th among the 46 counties and county-level areas of South Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 534 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Greenwood County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SC and the ZIP code:

  • Greenwood, SC 29646 (one of 4 codes for Greenwood; the street decides which)
  • Hodges, SC 29653 (one of 2 codes for Hodges; the street decides which)
  • Bradley, SC 29819

Greenwood is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 14 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
