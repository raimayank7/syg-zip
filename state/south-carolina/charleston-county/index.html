Where Charleston County is

The ZIP codes of Charleston County are found in the south-east of South Carolina. Its codes spread over about 32 miles from north to south and 48 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 29426 (Adams Run) and 29458 (Mc Clellanville), about 53 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Berkeley County (24 miles), Dorchester County (30 miles), Colleton County (42 miles) and Beaufort County (54 miles).

The first three digits

All 34 codes here begin with the same three digits, 294. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 294 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Berkeley County, Colleton County and Dorchester County, and has 72 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 24 of the 34 codes. Together they come to about 800 square miles of land (2,071 sq km). The largest is 29458 (Mc Clellanville) with 113 square miles, and the smallest is 29424 (Charleston) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 15.3 square miles; the middle value for all of South Carolina is 56.0. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 78.9 square miles, roughly 9% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Charleston County; a code can reach across its border. 10 codes have no mapped area: 29402 (Charleston), 29413 (Charleston), 29415 (North Charleston), 29416 (Charleston), 29417 (Charleston) and 5 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Charleston County ranks 3rd among the 46 counties and county-level areas of South Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 34 of the 534 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Charleston County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SC and the ZIP code:

  • Charleston, SC 29401 (one of 15 codes for Charleston; the street decides which)
  • North Charleston, SC 29405 (one of 4 codes for North Charleston; the street decides which)
  • Mount Pleasant, SC 29464 (one of 3 codes for Mount Pleasant; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named Charleston are also listed under Berkeley County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Charleston is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
