Where Colleton County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Colleton County fall in the south of South Carolina. Its codes spread over about 38 miles from north to south and 37 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 29082 (Lodge) and 29438 (Edisto Island), about 50 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Dorchester County (21 miles), Hampton County (28 miles), Bamberg County (33 miles) and Beaufort County (37 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Colleton County begin with 294 (10 codes), 290 (1 code) and 299 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 294 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Berkeley County, Charleston County and Dorchester County, and has 72 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 1,026 square miles of land (2,657 sq km). The largest is 29488 (Walterboro) with 238 square miles, and the smallest is 29493 (Williams) with 2.5. The middle-sized code here covers 78.3 square miles; the middle value for all of South Carolina is 56.0. Water adds about 49.1 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Colleton County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 29433 (Canadys). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Colleton County ranks 17th among the 46 counties and county-level areas of South Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 534 codes in the state list.
