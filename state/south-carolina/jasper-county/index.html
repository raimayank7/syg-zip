Where Jasper County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Jasper County fall in the south of South Carolina. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 29912 (Coosawhatchie) and 29927 (Hardeeville), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Beaufort County (18 miles), Effingham County, Georgia (22 miles), Hampton County (24 miles) and Chatham County, Georgia (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 6 codes here begin with the same three digits, 299. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 299 is shared with 3 other counties, Beaufort County, Colleton County and Hampton County, and has 39 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 593 square miles of land (1,536 sq km). The largest is 29936 (Ridgeland) with 232 square miles, and the smallest is 29912 (Coosawhatchie) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 66.4 square miles; the middle value for all of South Carolina is 56.0. Water adds about 39.9 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jasper County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Jasper County ranks 31st among the 46 counties and county-level areas of South Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 534 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jasper County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SC and the ZIP code:
