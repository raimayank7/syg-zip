Where Spartanburg County is

The ZIP codes of Spartanburg County are found in the north-west of South Carolina. Its codes spread over about 36 miles from north to south and 27 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 29331 (Cross Anchor) and 29356 (Landrum), about 41 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cherokee County (20 miles), Union County (25 miles), Greenville County (25 miles) and Polk County, North Carolina (26 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Spartanburg County starts with 293. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 293 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Cherokee County, Laurens County and Newberry County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 32 of the 38 codes. Together they come to about 936 square miles of land (2,424 sq km). The largest is 29388 (Woodruff) with 97.2 square miles, and the smallest is 29378 (Una) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 22.4 square miles; the middle value for all of South Carolina is 56.0. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Spartanburg County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 29304 (Spartanburg), 29305 (Spartanburg), 29319 (Spartanburg), 29336 (Fairforest), 29348 (Gramling) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Spartanburg County ranks 2nd among the 46 counties and county-level areas of South Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 38 of the 534 codes in the state list.
