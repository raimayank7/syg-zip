Where Chesterfield County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Chesterfield County is in the north-east of South Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 22 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 29520 (Cheraw) and 29728 (Pageland), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Anson County, North Carolina (21 miles), Darlington County (25 miles), Union County, North Carolina (31 miles) and Marlboro County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Chesterfield County begin with 297 (5 codes), 295 (2 codes) and 291 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 297 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Cherokee County, Chester County and Lancaster County, and has 32 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 756 square miles of land (1,957 sq km). The largest is 29520 (Cheraw) with 132 square miles, and the smallest is 29741 (Ruby) with 37.0. The middle-sized code here covers 102 square miles; the middle value for all of South Carolina is 56.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Chesterfield County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Chesterfield County ranks 22nd among the 46 counties and county-level areas of South Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 534 codes in the state list.
