Where Bamberg County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Bamberg County fall in the south of South Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 8.5 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 29042 (Denmark) and 29081 (Ehrhardt), about 16 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Barnwell County (16 miles), Allendale County (18 miles), Orangeburg County (23 miles) and Hampton County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Bamberg County begin with 290 (3 codes) and 298 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 290 is shared with 16 other counties, such as Calhoun County, Chester County and Clarendon County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 345 square miles of land (893 sq km). The largest is 29003 (Bamberg) with 136 square miles, and the smallest is 29042 (Denmark) with 61.6. The middle-sized code here covers 73.6 square miles; the middle value for all of South Carolina is 56.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bamberg County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Bamberg County ranks 41st among the 46 counties and county-level areas of South Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 534 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bamberg County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SC and the ZIP code:
