Where Beaufort County is

The ZIP codes of Beaufort County are found in the south of South Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 31 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 29915 (Daufuskie Island) and 29940 (Seabrook), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Jasper County (18 miles), Chatham County, Georgia (33 miles), Hampton County (37 miles) and Effingham County, Georgia (37 miles).

The first three digits

All 19 codes here begin with the same three digits, 299. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 299 is shared with 3 other counties, Colleton County, Hampton County and Jasper County, and has 39 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 466 square miles of land (1,206 sq km). The largest is 29910 (Bluffton) with 104 square miles, and the smallest is 29935 (Port Royal) with 2.3. The middle-sized code here covers 20.7 square miles; the middle value for all of South Carolina is 56.0. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 149 square miles, roughly 24% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Beaufort County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 29901 (Beaufort), 29903 (Beaufort), 29914 (Dale), 29925 (Hilton Head Island), 29931 (Lobeco) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Beaufort County ranks 7th among the 46 counties and county-level areas of South Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 534 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Beaufort County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SC and the ZIP code:

  • Beaufort, SC 29901 (one of 6 codes for Beaufort; the street decides which)
  • Hilton Head Island, SC 29925 (one of 4 codes for Hilton Head Island; the street decides which)
  • Bluffton, SC 29910
