Where Lexington County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Lexington County fall close to the middle of South Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 28 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 29036 (Chapin) and 29160 (Swansea), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Richland County (15 miles), Saluda County (26 miles), Fairfield County (30 miles) and Newberry County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lexington County begin with 290 (9 codes), 291 (6 codes) and 292 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 290 is shared with 16 other counties, such as Bamberg County, Calhoun County and Chester County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 922 square miles of land (2,389 sq km). The largest is 29070 (Leesville) with 164 square miles, and the smallest is 29033 (Cayce) with 10.0. The middle-sized code here covers 62.7 square miles; the middle value for all of South Carolina is 56.0. Water adds about 66.4 square miles, roughly 7% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lexington County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 29071 (Lexington), 29171 (West Columbia) and 29228 (Columbia). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lexington County ranks 9th among the 46 counties and county-level areas of South Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 534 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lexington County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SC and the ZIP code:

  • West Columbia, SC 29169 (one of 4 codes for West Columbia; the street decides which)
  • Lexington, SC 29071 (one of 3 codes for Lexington; the street decides which)
  • Columbia, SC 29212 (one of 2 codes for Columbia; the street decides which)

Columbia shares a name with a place in another part of South Carolina; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Lexington County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for West Columbia, SC?

West Columbia has 4 ZIP codes in Lexington County: 29169, 29170, 29171, 29172. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Lexington County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 290, on 9 of the 17 codes.

Which ZIP code in Lexington County covers the most land?

29070 (Leesville), at about 164 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
