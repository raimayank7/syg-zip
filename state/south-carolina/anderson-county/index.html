Where Anderson County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Anderson County is in the north-west of South Carolina. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 27 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 29654 (Honea Path) and 29689 (Townville), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Pickens County (19 miles), Hart County, Georgia (21 miles), Abbeville County (22 miles) and Oconee County (27 miles).

The first three digits

All 16 codes here begin with the same three digits, 296. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 296 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Abbeville County, Greenville County and Greenwood County, and has 88 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 843 square miles of land (2,183 sq km). The largest is 29655 (Iva) with 141 square miles, and the smallest is 29624 (Anderson) with 28.2. The middle-sized code here covers 57.5 square miles; the middle value for all of South Carolina is 56.0. Water adds about 44.2 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Anderson County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 29622 (Anderson), 29623 (Anderson), 29656 (La France) and 29677 (Sandy Springs). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Anderson County ranks 10th among the 46 counties and county-level areas of South Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 534 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Anderson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SC and the ZIP code:

  • Anderson, SC 29621 (one of 6 codes for Anderson; the street decides which)
  • Belton, SC 29627
  • Honea Path, SC 29654

Anderson is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
