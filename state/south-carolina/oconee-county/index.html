Where Oconee County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Oconee County fall in the north-west of South Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 29643 (Fair Play) and 29686 (Tamassee), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Pickens County (16 miles), Stephens County, Georgia (20 miles), Rabun County, Georgia (22 miles) and Hart County, Georgia (26 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Oconee County starts with 296. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 296 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Abbeville County, Anderson County and Greenville County, and has 88 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 629 square miles of land (1,628 sq km). The largest is 29693 (Westminster) with 173 square miles, and the smallest is 29665 (Newry) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 39.2 square miles; the middle value for all of South Carolina is 56.0. Water adds about 54.3 square miles, roughly 8% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Oconee County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 29675 (Richland) and 29679 (Seneca). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Oconee County ranks 14th among the 46 counties and county-level areas of South Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 534 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Oconee County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SC and the ZIP code:

  • Seneca, SC 29672 (one of 3 codes for Seneca; the street decides which)
  • Fair Play, SC 29643
  • Long Creek, SC 29658

Seneca is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
