South Carolina ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 534 ZIP codes for South Carolina, about 1.3% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 31st among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 46 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Richland County (40), Spartanburg County (38), Charleston County (34), Greenville County (32) and Aiken County (23). At the other end, 1 has only one or two codes, for example Calhoun County.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. South Carolina uses 10 of these prefixes, from 290 to 299. The busiest are 296 (88 codes, most of them for Greenville), 294 (72 codes, most of them for Charleston), 295 (66 codes, most of them for Myrtle Beach) and 290 (53 codes, most of them for Lexington). The first digit 2 is shared with District of Columbia, Maryland, North Carolina, Virginia and West Virginia.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 424 of the 534 codes. The largest is 29440 (Georgetown, Georgetown County) at 523 square miles and the smallest is 29225 (Columbia) at 0.03; the middle value is 56.0 square miles. The other 110 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 29356 (Landrum) and the southernmost 29915 (Daufuskie Island); the one farthest east is 29566 (Little River) and farthest west 29658 (Long Creek).

Place names and addresses in South Carolina

Of the 376 place names in the list, 338 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Columbia (34), Greenville (17), Charleston (16) and Spartanburg (8). 5 place names appear under more than one county, among them Charleston, Columbia, Fort Mill and North Charleston: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters SC and the code, as in Columbia, SC 29201. The name of the county is left out.
