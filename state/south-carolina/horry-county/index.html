Where Horry County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Horry County fall in the east of South Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 43 miles, and from east to west about 34 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 29581 (Nichols) and 29587 (Myrtle Beach), about 44 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Marion County (30 miles), Columbus County, North Carolina (33 miles), Georgetown County (34 miles) and Brunswick County, North Carolina (38 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Horry County starts with 295. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 295 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Chesterfield County, Darlington County and Dillon County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 15 of the 20 codes. Together they come to about 1,160 square miles of land (3,004 sq km). The largest is 29526 (Conway) with 175 square miles, and the smallest is 29572 (Myrtle Beach) with 8.9. The middle-sized code here covers 53.0 square miles; the middle value for all of South Carolina is 56.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Horry County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 29528 (Conway), 29578 (Myrtle Beach), 29587 (Myrtle Beach), 29597 (North Myrtle Beach) and 29598 (North Myrtle Beach). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Horry County ranks 6th among the 46 counties and county-level areas of South Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 20 of the 534 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Horry County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SC and the ZIP code:

  • Myrtle Beach, SC 29572 (one of 7 codes for Myrtle Beach; the street decides which)
  • Conway, SC 29526 (one of 3 codes for Conway; the street decides which)
  • North Myrtle Beach, SC 29582 (one of 3 codes for North Myrtle Beach; the street decides which)
