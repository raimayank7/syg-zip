Where Orangeburg County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Orangeburg County fall close to the middle of South Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 25 miles, and from east to west about 54 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 29048 (Eutawville) and 29146 (Springfield), about 54 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Calhoun County (13 miles), Bamberg County (23 miles), Barnwell County (32 miles) and Dorchester County (35 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Orangeburg County begin with 291 (11 codes), 290 (6 codes) and 294 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 291 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Aiken County, Calhoun County and Chesterfield County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 18 codes. Together they come to about 1,186 square miles of land (3,072 sq km). The largest is 29115 (Orangeburg) with 125 square miles, and the smallest is 29117 (Orangeburg) with 0.10. The middle-sized code here covers 64.2 square miles; the middle value for all of South Carolina is 56.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Orangeburg County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 29116 (Orangeburg). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Orangeburg County ranks 8th among the 46 counties and county-level areas of South Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 534 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Orangeburg County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SC and the ZIP code:

  • Orangeburg, SC 29115 (one of 4 codes for Orangeburg; the street decides which)
  • Bowman, SC 29018
  • Branchville, SC 29432

Questions about Orangeburg County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Orangeburg, SC?

Orangeburg has 4 ZIP codes in Orangeburg County: 29115, 29116, 29117, 29118. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Orangeburg County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 291, on 11 of the 18 codes.

Which ZIP code in Orangeburg County covers the most land?

29115 (Orangeburg), at about 125 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
