Where Lee County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Lee County fall in the north-east of South Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 10 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 29010 (Bishopville) and 29080 (Lynchburg), about 16 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Darlington County (18 miles), Sumter County (19 miles), Clarendon County (26 miles) and Florence County (26 miles).

The first three digits

All 3 codes here begin with the same three digits, 290. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 290 is shared with 16 other counties, such as Bamberg County, Calhoun County and Chester County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 328 square miles of land (849 sq km). The largest is 29010 (Bishopville) with 199 square miles, and the smallest is 29046 (Elliott) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 129 square miles; the middle value for all of South Carolina is 56.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lee County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lee County ranks 44th among the 46 counties and county-level areas of South Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 3 of the 534 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lee County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SC and the ZIP code:
