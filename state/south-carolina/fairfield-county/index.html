Where Fairfield County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Fairfield County is in the north of South Carolina. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 24 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 29015 (Blair) and 29130 (Ridgeway), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Chester County (24 miles), Richland County (25 miles), Newberry County (26 miles) and Lexington County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Fairfield County begin with 291 (3 codes) and 290 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 291 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Aiken County, Calhoun County and Chesterfield County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 4 of the 5 codes. Together they come to about 602 square miles of land (1,559 sq km). The largest is 29180 (Winnsboro) with 308 square miles, and the smallest is 29065 (Jenkinsville) with 25.8. The middle-sized code here covers 134 square miles; the middle value for all of South Carolina is 56.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 25.1 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Fairfield County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 29132 (Rion). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Fairfield County ranks 36th among the 46 counties and county-level areas of South Carolina (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 534 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Fairfield County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters SC and the ZIP code:

  • Blair, SC 29015
  • Jenkinsville, SC 29065
  • Ridgeway, SC 29130

Blair is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
