Where Brown County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Brown County fall in the south-west of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 36 miles from north to south and 41 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 45101 (Aberdeen) and 45275 (Cincinnati), about 50 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Clermont County (16 miles), Bracken County, Kentucky (18 miles), Mason County, Kentucky (20 miles) and Adams County (22 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Brown County begin with 451 (11 codes) and 452 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 451 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Adams County, Clermont County and Clinton County, and has 49 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 444 square miles of land (1,150 sq km). The largest is 45121 (Georgetown) with 107 square miles, and the smallest is 45119 (Feesburg) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 37.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Brown County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 45275 (Cincinnati). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Brown County ranks 41st among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Brown County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Aberdeen, OH 45101
  • Cincinnati, OH 45275
  • Decatur, OH 45115

Aberdeen is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Cincinnati shares a name with a place in another part of Ohio; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
