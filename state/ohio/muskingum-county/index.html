Where Muskingum County is

The ZIP codes of Muskingum County are found in the east of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 23 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 43720 (Blue Rock) and 43822 (Frazeysburg), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Perry County (19 miles), Coshocton County (25 miles), Morgan County (26 miles) and Guernsey County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Muskingum County begin with 437 (13 codes) and 438 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 437 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Belmont County, Guernsey County and Licking County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 18 codes. Together they come to about 764 square miles of land (1,977 sq km). The largest is 43701 (Zanesville) with 181 square miles, and the smallest is 43738 (Fultonham) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 41.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Muskingum County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 43702 (Zanesville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Muskingum County ranks 19th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Muskingum County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Zanesville, OH 43701 (one of 2 codes for Zanesville; the street decides which)
  • Adamsville, OH 43802
  • Blue Rock, OH 43720

Questions about Muskingum County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Zanesville, OH?

Zanesville has 2 ZIP codes in Muskingum County: 43701, 43702. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Muskingum County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 437, on 13 of the 18 codes.

Which ZIP code in Muskingum County covers the most land?

43701 (Zanesville), at about 181 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
