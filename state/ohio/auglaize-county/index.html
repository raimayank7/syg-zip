Where Auglaize County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Auglaize County is in the west of Ohio. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 45865 (Minster) and 45896 (Waynesfield), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Shelby County (14 miles), Allen County (17 miles), Mercer County (22 miles) and Logan County (25 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Auglaize County starts with 458. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 458 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Allen County, Hancock County and Hardin County, and has 85 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 455 square miles of land (1,179 sq km). The largest is 45895 (Wapakoneta) with 187 square miles, and the smallest is 45819 (Buckland) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 35.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Auglaize County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Auglaize County ranks 47th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Auglaize County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Buckland, OH 45819
  • Lima, OH 45806
  • Minster, OH 45865

Lima shares a name with a place in another part of Ohio; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
