Where Stark County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Stark County fall in the north-east of Ohio. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 28 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 44640 (Limaville) and 44689 (Wilmot), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Carroll County (20 miles), Summit County (22 miles), Tuscarawas County (23 miles) and Wayne County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Stark County begin with 446 (22 codes) and 447 (20 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 446 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Carroll County, Columbiana County and Harrison County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 34 of the 42 codes. Together they come to about 677 square miles of land (1,755 sq km). The largest is 44601 (Alliance) with 82.0 square miles, and the smallest is 44630 (Greentown) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 16.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Stark County; a code can reach across its border. 8 codes have no mapped area: 44648 (Massillon), 44650 (Maximo), 44701 (Canton), 44711 (Canton), 44735 (Canton) and 3 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Stark County ranks 6th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 42 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Stark County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Canton, OH 44701 (one of 18 codes for Canton; the street decides which)
  • Massillon, OH 44646 (one of 3 codes for Massillon; the street decides which)
  • Alliance, OH 44601

Canton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 16 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
