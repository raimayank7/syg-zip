Where Medina County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Medina County fall in the north-east of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 44233 (Hinckley) and 44235 (Homerville), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Wayne County (19 miles), Summit County (19 miles), Lorain County (21 miles) and Ashland County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Medina County begin with 442 (15 codes) and 441 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 442 is shared with 3 other counties, Portage County, Summit County and Wayne County, and has 52 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 423 square miles of land (1,094 sq km). The largest is 44256 (Medina) with 131 square miles, and the smallest is 44274 (Sharon Center) with 1.1. The middle-sized code here covers 25.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Medina County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 44258 (Medina) and 44282 (Wadsworth). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Medina County ranks 22nd among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Medina County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Medina, OH 44256 (one of 2 codes for Medina; the street decides which)
  • Wadsworth, OH 44281 (one of 2 codes for Wadsworth; the street decides which)
  • Brunswick, OH 44212

Medina is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Strongsville shares a name with a place in another part of Ohio; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Medina County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Medina, OH?

Medina has 2 ZIP codes in Medina County: 44256, 44258. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Medina County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Medina County covers the most land?
