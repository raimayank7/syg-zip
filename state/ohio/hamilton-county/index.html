Where Hamilton County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Hamilton County is in the south-west of Ohio. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 26 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 45030 (Harrison) and 45255 (Cincinnati), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Campbell County, Kentucky (12 miles), Kenton County, Kentucky (13 miles), Boone County, Kentucky (17 miles) and Butler County (18 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Hamilton County begin with 452 (68 codes), 450 (7 codes), 451 (2 codes) and 459 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 452 is shared with 2 other counties, Brown County and Clermont County, and has 70 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 54 of the 78 codes. Together they come to about 412 square miles of land (1,066 sq km). The largest is 45030 (Harrison) with 40.1 square miles, and the smallest is 45267 (Cincinnati) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 5.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hamilton County; a code can reach across its border. 24 codes have no mapped area: 45201 (Cincinnati), 45221 (Cincinnati), 45222 (Cincinnati), 45234 (Cincinnati), 45235 (Cincinnati) and 19 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Ohio has more ZIP codes: Hamilton County holds 78 of the 1,416 in the state list, about 5.5%.

Writing an address in Hamilton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Cincinnati, OH 45201 (one of 69 codes for Cincinnati; the street decides which)
  • Addyston, OH 45001
  • Camp Dennison, OH 45111

ZIP codes named Cincinnati are also listed under Brown County and Clermont County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site.
