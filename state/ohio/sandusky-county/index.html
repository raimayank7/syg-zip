Where Sandusky County is

The ZIP codes of Sandusky County are found in the north-west of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 43464 (Vickery) and 43469 (Woodville), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Ottawa County (15 miles), Seneca County (15 miles), Wood County (22 miles) and Lucas County (29 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Sandusky County starts with 434. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 434 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Erie County, Lucas County and Ottawa County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 361 square miles of land (934 sq km). The largest is 43420 (Fremont) with 136 square miles, and the smallest is 43407 (Burgoon) with 14.7. The middle-sized code here covers 34.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. Water adds about 21.9 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sandusky County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Sandusky County ranks 75th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Sandusky County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Burgoon, OH 43407
  • Clyde, OH 43410
  • Fremont, OH 43420
