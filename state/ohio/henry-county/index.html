Where Henry County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Henry County fall in the north-west of Ohio. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 43516 (Deshler) and 43555 (Ridgeville Corners), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Fulton County (19 miles), Putnam County (22 miles), Defiance County (24 miles) and Wood County (24 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Henry County starts with 435. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 435 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Defiance County, Fulton County and Lucas County, and has 55 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 423 square miles of land (1,096 sq km). The largest is 43545 (Napoleon) with 135 square miles, and the smallest is 43510 (Colton) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 31.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Henry County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 43550 (Okolona). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Henry County ranks 41st among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Henry County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Colton, OH 43510
  • Deshler, OH 43516
  • Grelton, OH 43523

Colton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
