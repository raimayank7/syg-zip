Where Union County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Union County fall in the west of Ohio. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 11 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 43029 (Irwin) and 43344 (Richwood), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Delaware County (20 miles), Champaign County (21 miles), Logan County (22 miles) and Marion County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Union County begin with 430 (8 codes) and 433 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 430 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Champaign County, Clark County and Delaware County, and has 70 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 361 square miles of land (934 sq km). The largest is 43040 (Marysville) with 166 square miles, and the smallest is 43077 (Unionville Center) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 28.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Union County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 43041 (Marysville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Union County ranks 66th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Union County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Marysville, OH 43040 (one of 2 codes for Marysville; the street decides which)
  • Broadway, OH 43007
  • Irwin, OH 43029

Marysville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Union County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Marysville, OH?

Marysville has 2 ZIP codes in Union County: 43040, 43041. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Union County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 430, on 8 of the 9 codes.

Which ZIP code in Union County covers the most land?

43040 (Marysville), at about 166 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
