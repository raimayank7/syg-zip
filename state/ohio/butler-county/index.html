Where Butler County is

The ZIP codes of Butler County are found in the south-west of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 18 miles from north to south and 23 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 45003 (College Corner) and 45071 (West Chester), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hamilton County (18 miles), Warren County (21 miles), Preble County (23 miles) and Franklin County, Indiana (24 miles).

The first three digits

All 21 codes here begin with the same three digits, 450. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 450 is shared with 3 other counties, Hamilton County, Preble County and Warren County, and has 39 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 15 of the 21 codes. Together they come to about 500 square miles of land (1,296 sq km). The largest is 45013 (Hamilton) with 118 square miles, and the smallest is 45055 (Overpeck) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 27.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Butler County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 45004 (Collinsville), 45012 (Hamilton), 45018 (Fairfield), 45061 (Ross), 45063 (Shandon) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Butler County ranks 13th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 21 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Butler County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Hamilton, OH 45011 (one of 4 codes for Hamilton; the street decides which)
  • Fairfield, OH 45014 (one of 2 codes for Fairfield; the street decides which)
  • Middletown, OH 45042 (one of 2 codes for Middletown; the street decides which)

Hamilton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 19 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Butler County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Hamilton, OH?

Hamilton has 4 ZIP codes in Butler County: 45011, 45012, 45013, 45015. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Butler County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 21 start with 450.

Which ZIP code in Butler County covers the most land?

45013 (Hamilton), at about 118 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
