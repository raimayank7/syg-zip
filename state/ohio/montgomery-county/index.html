Where Montgomery County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Montgomery County fall in the south-west of Ohio. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 45354 (Phillipsburg) and 45458 (Dayton), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Greene County (16 miles), Preble County (19 miles), Miami County (21 miles) and Warren County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Montgomery County begin with 454 (41 codes) and 453 (10 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 454 is shared with 1 other county, Greene County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 37 of the 51 codes. Together they come to about 495 square miles of land (1,282 sq km). The largest is 45309 (Brookville) with 65.9 square miles, and the smallest is 45423 (Dayton) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 7.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Montgomery County; a code can reach across its border. 14 codes have no mapped area: 45343 (Miamisburg), 45401 (Dayton), 45412 (Dayton), 45413 (Dayton), 45422 (Dayton) and 9 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Montgomery County ranks 4th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 51 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Montgomery County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Dayton, OH 45401 (one of 41 codes for Dayton; the street decides which)
  • Miamisburg, OH 45342 (one of 2 codes for Miamisburg; the street decides which)
  • Brookville, OH 45309

ZIP codes named Dayton are also listed under Greene County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Dayton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 17 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
