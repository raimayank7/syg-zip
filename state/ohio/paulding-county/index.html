Where Paulding County is

The ZIP codes of Paulding County are found in the north-west of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 45813 (Antwerp) and 45873 (Oakwood), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Defiance County (16 miles), Van Wert County (19 miles), Putnam County (24 miles) and Allen County, Indiana (27 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 458. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 458 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Allen County, Auglaize County and Hancock County, and has 85 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 390 square miles of land (1,011 sq km). The largest is 45879 (Paulding) with 103 square miles, and the smallest is 45855 (Latty) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 43.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Paulding County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Paulding County ranks 66th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.
