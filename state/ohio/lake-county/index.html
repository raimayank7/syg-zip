Where Lake County is

The ZIP codes of Lake County are found in the north-east of Ohio. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 14 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 44057 (Madison) and 44092 (Wickliffe), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Geauga County (15 miles), Cuyahoga County (22 miles), Ashtabula County (29 miles) and Portage County (36 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Lake County starts with 440. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 440 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Ashtabula County, Cuyahoga County and Erie County, and has 65 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 228 square miles of land (591 sq km). The largest is 44077 (Painesville) with 60.5 square miles, and the smallest is 44045 (Grand River) with 0.9. The middle-sized code here covers 30.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lake County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 44061 (Mentor), 44096 (Willoughby) and 44097 (Eastlake). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lake County ranks 47th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lake County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Eastlake, OH 44095 (one of 2 codes for Eastlake; the street decides which)
  • Mentor, OH 44060 (one of 2 codes for Mentor; the street decides which)
  • Willoughby, OH 44094 (one of 2 codes for Willoughby; the street decides which)
