Where Monroe County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Monroe County fall in the south-east of Ohio. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 14 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 43786 (Stafford) and 43931 (Hannibal), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Tyler County, West Virginia (20 miles), Belmont County (22 miles), Noble County (22 miles) and Marshall County, West Virginia (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Monroe County begin with 437 (7 codes), 439 (4 codes) and 457 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 437 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Belmont County, Guernsey County and Licking County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 424 square miles of land (1,097 sq km). The largest is 43793 (Woodsfield) with 105 square miles, and the smallest is 43786 (Stafford) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 46.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Monroe County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 43752 (Laings) and 43757 (Malaga). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Monroe County ranks 41st among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.
