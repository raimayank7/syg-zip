Where Wayne County is

The ZIP codes of Wayne County are found in the north-east of Ohio. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 44287 (West Salem) and 44659 (Mount Eaton), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Holmes County (19 miles), Medina County (19 miles), Ashland County (23 miles) and Summit County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Wayne County begin with 446 (10 codes) and 442 (6 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 446 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Carroll County, Columbiana County and Harrison County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 15 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 610 square miles of land (1,579 sq km). The largest is 44691 (Wooster) with 155 square miles, and the smallest is 44659 (Mount Eaton) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 29.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wayne County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 44636 (Kidron). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Wayne County ranks 22nd among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Wayne County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

Questions about Wayne County ZIP codes

Do all ZIP codes in Wayne County start with the same digits?
