Where Warren County is

The ZIP codes of Warren County are found in the south-west of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 45005 (Franklin) and 45162 (Pleasant Plain), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Clinton County (19 miles), Butler County (21 miles), Montgomery County (23 miles) and Greene County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Warren County begin with 450 (10 codes) and 451 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 450 is shared with 3 other counties, Butler County, Hamilton County and Preble County, and has 39 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 360 square miles of land (934 sq km). The largest is 45036 (Lebanon) with 81.1 square miles, and the smallest is 45032 (Harveysburg) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 26.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Warren County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Warren County ranks 41st among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Warren County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Franklin, OH 45005
  • Harveysburg, OH 45032
  • Kings Mills, OH 45034

Franklin is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 26 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Do all ZIP codes in Warren County start with the same digits?
