Where Clermont County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Clermont County fall in the south-west of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 31 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 45112 (Chilo) and 45140 (Loveland), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Campbell County, Kentucky (13 miles), Brown County (16 miles), Kenton County, Kentucky (20 miles) and Hamilton County (21 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Clermont County begin with 451 (15 codes) and 452 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 451 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Adams County, Brown County and Clinton County, and has 49 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 481 square miles of land (1,245 sq km). The largest is 45103 (Batavia) with 85.3 square miles, and the smallest is 45156 (Neville) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 30.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clermont County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Clermont County ranks 22nd among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clermont County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Amelia, OH 45102
  • Batavia, OH 45103
  • Bethel, OH 45106
