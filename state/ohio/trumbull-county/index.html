Where Trumbull County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Trumbull County is in the north-east of Ohio. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 44425 (Hubbard) and 44439 (Mesopotamia), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Mahoning County (17 miles), Mercer County, Pennsylvania (23 miles), Portage County (26 miles) and Lawrence County, Pennsylvania (27 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Trumbull County starts with 444. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 444 is shared with 3 other counties, Columbiana County, Mahoning County and Portage County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 25 of the 28 codes. Together they come to about 604 square miles of land (1,565 sq km). The largest is 44410 (Cortland) with 65.4 square miles, and the smallest is 44424 (Hartford) with 2.2. The middle-sized code here covers 17.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Trumbull County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 44453 (Orangeville), 44482 (Warren) and 44486 (Warren). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Trumbull County ranks 10th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 28 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Trumbull County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Warren, OH 44481 (one of 6 codes for Warren; the street decides which)
  • Bristolville, OH 44402
  • Brookfield, OH 44403

Warren is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 15 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
