Where Van Wert County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Van Wert County fall in the north-west of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 18 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 45863 (Middle Point) and 45898 (Willshire), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Adams County, Indiana (19 miles), Paulding County (19 miles), Mercer County (24 miles) and Allen County (27 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 458. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 458 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Allen County, Auglaize County and Hancock County, and has 85 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 368 square miles of land (952 sq km). The largest is 45891 (Van Wert) with 134 square miles, and the smallest is 45838 (Elgin) with 0.08. The middle-sized code here covers 29.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Van Wert County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Van Wert County ranks 66th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.
