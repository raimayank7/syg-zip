Where Guernsey County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Guernsey County is in the east of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 43732 (Cumberland) and 43736 (Fairview), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Noble County (15 miles), Muskingum County (27 miles), Monroe County (28 miles) and Belmont County (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 14 codes here begin with the same three digits, 437. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 437 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Belmont County, Licking County and Monroe County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 585 square miles of land (1,514 sq km). The largest is 43725 (Cambridge) with 124 square miles, and the smallest is 43736 (Fairview) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 36.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Guernsey County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Guernsey County ranks 30th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Guernsey County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Buffalo, OH 43722
  • Byesville, OH 43723
  • Cambridge, OH 43725

Buffalo is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 16 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
