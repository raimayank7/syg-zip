Where Lucas County is

The ZIP codes of Lucas County are found in the north-west of Ohio. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 43434 (Harbor View) and 43547 (Neapolis), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Monroe County, Michigan (17 miles), Wood County (19 miles), Ottawa County (25 miles) and Fulton County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lucas County begin with 436 (32 codes), 435 (8 codes) and 434 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 436 is shared with 1 other county, Wood County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 25 of the 42 codes. Together they come to about 264 square miles of land (684 sq km). The largest is 43616 (Oregon) with 38.4 square miles, and the smallest is 43434 (Harbor View) with 0.02. The middle-sized code here covers 7.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 10.4 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lucas County; a code can reach across its border. 17 codes have no mapped area: 43405 (Toledo), 43601 (Toledo), 43603 (Toledo), 43635 (Toledo), 43652 (Toledo) and 12 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lucas County ranks 6th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 42 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lucas County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Toledo, OH 43405 (one of 32 codes for Toledo; the street decides which)
  • Berkey, OH 43504
  • Harbor View, OH 43434

Toledo is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
