Where Scioto County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Scioto County is in the south of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 26 miles from north to south and 28 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 45636 (Haverhill) and 45671 (Rarden), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Greenup County, Kentucky (17 miles), Pike County (19 miles), Lewis County, Kentucky (24 miles) and Jackson County (27 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Scioto County starts with 456. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 456 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Adams County, Gallia County and Jackson County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 684 square miles of land (1,772 sq km). The largest is 45648 (Lucasville) with 139 square miles, and the smallest is 45630 (Friendship) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 42.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Scioto County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Scioto County ranks 27th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Scioto County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Lucasville, OH 45648 (one of 2 codes for Lucasville; the street decides which)
  • Franklin Furnace, OH 45629
  • Friendship, OH 45630
