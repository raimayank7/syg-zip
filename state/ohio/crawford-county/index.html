Where Crawford County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Crawford County is in the north of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 44827 (Crestline) and 44860 (Oceola), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Richland County (19 miles), Wyandot County (22 miles), Morrow County (22 miles) and Marion County (23 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Crawford County starts with 448. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 448 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Ashland County, Erie County and Hancock County, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 371 square miles of land (960 sq km). The largest is 44820 (Bucyrus) with 161 square miles, and the smallest is 44856 (North Robinson) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 35.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Crawford County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 44860 (Oceola). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Crawford County ranks 66th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Crawford County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:
