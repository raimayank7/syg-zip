Where Marion County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Marion County fall close to the middle of Ohio. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 24 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 43332 (La Rue) and 43335 (Martel), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Morrow County (18 miles), Wyandot County (21 miles), Crawford County (23 miles) and Delaware County (23 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 433. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 433 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Hardin County, Logan County and Morrow County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 432 square miles of land (1,119 sq km). The largest is 43302 (Marion) with 195 square miles, and the smallest is 43322 (Green Camp) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 39.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Marion County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 43301 (Marion) and 43335 (Martel). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Marion County ranks 54th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Marion County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Marion, OH 43301 (one of 2 codes for Marion; the street decides which)
  • Caledonia, OH 43314
  • Green Camp, OH 43322

Marion is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 22 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
