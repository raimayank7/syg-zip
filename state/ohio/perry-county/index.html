Where Perry County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Perry County is in the south of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 43076 (Thornville) and 43730 (Corning), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Muskingum County (19 miles), Hocking County (20 miles), Fairfield County (22 miles) and Morgan County (22 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Perry County begin with 437 (10 codes) and 430 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 437 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Belmont County, Guernsey County and Licking County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 439 square miles of land (1,136 sq km). The largest is 43076 (Thornville) with 65.2 square miles, and the smallest is 43761 (Moxahala) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 38.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Perry County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Perry County ranks 47th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Perry County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Corning, OH 43730
  • Crooksville, OH 43731
  • Glenford, OH 43739

Corning is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
