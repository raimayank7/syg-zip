Where Hancock County is

The ZIP codes of Hancock County are found in the north-west of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 45858 (Mc Comb) and 45897 (Williamstown), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Wyandot County (21 miles), Hardin County (21 miles), Putnam County (25 miles) and Allen County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Hancock County begin with 458 (12 codes) and 448 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 458 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Allen County, Auglaize County and Hardin County, and has 85 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 414 square miles of land (1,073 sq km). The largest is 45840 (Findlay) with 166 square miles, and the smallest is 45816 (Benton Ridge) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 21.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hancock County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 45839 (Findlay). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Hancock County ranks 37th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hancock County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Findlay, OH 45839 (one of 2 codes for Findlay; the street decides which)
  • Arcadia, OH 44804
  • Arlington, OH 45814

Questions about Hancock County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Findlay, OH?

Findlay has 2 ZIP codes in Hancock County: 45839, 45840. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Hancock County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 458, on 12 of the 13 codes.

Which ZIP code in Hancock County covers the most land?

45840 (Findlay), at about 166 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
