Where Putnam County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Putnam County is in the north-west of Ohio. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 19 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 45815 (Belmore) and 45876 (Ottoville), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Allen County (16 miles), Henry County (22 miles), Paulding County (24 miles) and Hancock County (25 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Putnam County starts with 458. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 458 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Allen County, Auglaize County and Hancock County, and has 85 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 527 square miles of land (1,365 sq km). The largest is 45875 (Ottawa) with 103 square miles, and the smallest is 45848 (Glandorf) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 26.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Putnam County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 45837 (Dupont). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Putnam County ranks 30th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.
