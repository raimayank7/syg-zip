Where Wood County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Wood County is in the north-west of Ohio. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 43529 (Hoytville) and 43619 (Northwood), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lucas County (19 miles), Sandusky County (22 miles), Henry County (24 miles) and Hancock County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Wood County begin with 434 (18 codes), 435 (9 codes), 436 (1 code), 448 (1 code) and 458 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 434 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Erie County, Lucas County and Ottawa County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 28 of the 30 codes. Together they come to about 611 square miles of land (1,583 sq km). The largest is 43402 (Bowling Green) with 111 square miles, and the smallest is 43565 (Tontogany) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 18.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wood County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 43441 (Lemoyne) and 43552 (Perrysburg). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Wood County ranks 9th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 30 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Wood County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Bowling Green, OH 43402 (one of 2 codes for Bowling Green; the street decides which)
  • Perrysburg, OH 43551 (one of 2 codes for Perrysburg; the street decides which)
  • Bloomdale, OH 44817

Bowling Green is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
