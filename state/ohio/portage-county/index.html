Where Portage County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Portage County fall in the north-east of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 44202 (Aurora) and 44411 (Deerfield), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Summit County (17 miles), Geauga County (23 miles), Trumbull County (26 miles) and Stark County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Portage County begin with 442 (15 codes) and 444 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 442 is shared with 3 other counties, Medina County, Summit County and Wayne County, and has 52 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 439 square miles of land (1,136 sq km). The largest is 44266 (Ravenna) with 91.9 square miles, and the smallest is 44243 (Kent) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 24.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. Water adds about 14.3 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Portage County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 44211 (Brady Lake), 44242 (Kent) and 44265 (Randolph). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Portage County ranks 20th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Portage County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Kent, OH 44240 (one of 3 codes for Kent; the street decides which)
  • Atwater, OH 44201
  • Aurora, OH 44202

Kent is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Portage County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Kent, OH?

Kent has 3 ZIP codes in Portage County: 44240, 44242, 44243. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Portage County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 442, on 15 of the 17 codes.

Which ZIP code in Portage County covers the most land?

44266 (Ravenna), at about 91.9 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
