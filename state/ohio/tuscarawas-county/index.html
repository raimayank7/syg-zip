Where Tuscarawas County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Tuscarawas County is in the east of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 26 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 43832 (Newcomerstown) and 44671 (Sandyville), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Carroll County (19 miles), Harrison County (23 miles), Stark County (23 miles) and Coshocton County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Tuscarawas County begin with 446 (16 codes) and 438 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 446 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Carroll County, Columbiana County and Harrison County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 19 of the 20 codes. Together they come to about 683 square miles of land (1,770 sq km). The largest is 43832 (Newcomerstown) with 101 square miles, and the smallest is 44682 (Tuscarawas) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 32.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Tuscarawas County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 44679 (Stillwater). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Tuscarawas County ranks 15th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 20 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.
