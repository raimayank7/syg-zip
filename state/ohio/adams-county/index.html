Where Adams County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Adams County fall in the south-west of Ohio. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 18 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 45144 (Manchester) and 45660 (Peebles), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Brown County (22 miles), Mason County, Kentucky (23 miles), Lewis County, Kentucky (26 miles) and Highland County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Adams County begin with 456 (7 codes) and 451 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 456 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Gallia County, Jackson County and Lawrence County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 653 square miles of land (1,690 sq km). The largest is 45660 (Peebles) with 203 square miles, and the smallest is 45105 (Bentonville) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 70.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Adams County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Adams County ranks 66th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Adams County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Bentonville, OH 45105
  • Blue Creek, OH 45616
  • Cherry Fork, OH 45618

Bentonville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
