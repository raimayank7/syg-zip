Where Clark County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Clark County fall in the west of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 11 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 43010 (Catawba) and 45341 (Medway), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Champaign County (15 miles), Greene County (16 miles), Madison County (24 miles) and Miami County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Clark County begin with 453 (8 codes), 455 (6 codes) and 430 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 453 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Champaign County, Darke County and Greene County, and has 80 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 386 square miles of land (1,001 sq km). The largest is 45502 (Springfield) with 115 square miles, and the smallest is 45501 (Springfield) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 12.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clark County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Clark County ranks 27th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clark County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Springfield, OH 45501 (one of 6 codes for Springfield; the street decides which)
  • Catawba, OH 43010
  • Donnelsville, OH 45319

Springfield is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 23 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Clark County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Springfield, OH?

Springfield has 6 ZIP codes in Clark County: 45501, 45502, 45503, 45504, 45505, 45506. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Clark County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 453, on 8 of the 15 codes.

Which ZIP code in Clark County covers the most land?

45502 (Springfield), at about 115 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
