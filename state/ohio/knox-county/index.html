Where Knox County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Knox County fall close to the middle of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 28 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 43006 (Brinkhaven) and 43011 (Centerburg), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Licking County (23 miles), Morrow County (23 miles), Richland County (28 miles) and Coshocton County (29 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 430. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 430 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Champaign County, Clark County and Delaware County, and has 70 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 534 square miles of land (1,384 sq km). The largest is 43050 (Mount Vernon) with 154 square miles, and the smallest is 43005 (Bladensburg) with 1.9. The middle-sized code here covers 50.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Knox County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 43048 (Mount Liberty). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Knox County ranks 54th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Knox County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:
