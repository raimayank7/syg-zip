Where Holmes County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Holmes County fall in the east of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 11 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 44628 (Glenmont) and 44690 (Winesburg), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Coshocton County (19 miles), Wayne County (19 miles), Tuscarawas County (25 miles) and Ashland County (27 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 446. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 446 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Carroll County, Columbiana County and Harrison County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 333 square miles of land (863 sq km). The largest is 44654 (Millersburg) with 160 square miles, and the smallest is 44690 (Winesburg) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 22.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Holmes County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 44617 (Charm). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Holmes County ranks 41st among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Holmes County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Berlin, OH 44610
  • Big Prairie, OH 44611
  • Charm, OH 44617

Berlin is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 10 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
