Where Summit County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Summit County fall in the north-east of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 44087 (Twinsburg) and 44216 (Clinton), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Portage County (17 miles), Medina County (19 miles), Stark County (22 miles) and Wayne County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Summit County begin with 443 (28 codes), 442 (16 codes), 440 (3 codes) and 446 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 443, so those three digits alone point to Summit County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 35 of the 48 codes. Together they come to about 442 square miles of land (1,145 sq km). The largest is 44203 (Barberton) with 35.0 square miles, and the smallest is 44232 (Green) with 0.02. The middle-sized code here covers 10.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Summit County; a code can reach across its border. 13 codes have no mapped area: 44210 (Bath), 44222 (Cuyahoga Falls), 44237 (Hudson), 44309 (Akron), 44315 (Akron) and 8 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Summit County ranks 5th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 48 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Summit County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Akron, OH 44301 (one of 27 codes for Akron; the street decides which)
  • Cuyahoga Falls, OH 44221 (one of 3 codes for Cuyahoga Falls; the street decides which)
  • Hudson, OH 44236 (one of 2 codes for Hudson; the street decides which)

Akron is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
