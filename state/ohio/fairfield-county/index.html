Where Fairfield County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Fairfield County fall close to the middle of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 43046 (Millersport) and 43154 (Stoutsville), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Licking County (21 miles), Perry County (22 miles), Hocking County (22 miles) and Pickaway County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Fairfield County begin with 431 (13 codes) and 430 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 431 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Fayette County, Franklin County and Hocking County, and has 49 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 556 square miles of land (1,440 sq km). The largest is 43130 (Lancaster) with 165 square miles, and the smallest is 43157 (Thurston) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 29.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Fairfield County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Fairfield County ranks 30th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Fairfield County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:
