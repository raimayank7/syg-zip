Where Columbiana County is

The ZIP codes of Columbiana County are found in the north-east of Ohio. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 44441 (Negley) and 44634 (Homeworth), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Mahoning County (18 miles), Hancock County, West Virginia (20 miles), Carroll County (22 miles) and Beaver County, Pennsylvania (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Columbiana County begin with 444 (14 codes), 439 (4 codes) and 446 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 444 is shared with 3 other counties, Mahoning County, Portage County and Trumbull County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 20 of the 21 codes. Together they come to about 596 square miles of land (1,543 sq km). The largest is 44460 (Salem) with 107 square miles, and the smallest is 44415 (Elkton) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 21.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Columbiana County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 44492 (West Point). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Columbiana County ranks 13th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 21 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Columbiana County
