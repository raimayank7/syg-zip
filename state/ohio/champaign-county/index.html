Where Champaign County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Champaign County is in the west of Ohio. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 43060 (North Lewisburg) and 45389 (Christiansburg), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Clark County (15 miles), Logan County (16 miles), Union County (21 miles) and Shelby County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Champaign County begin with 430 (9 codes) and 453 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 430 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Clark County, Delaware County and Fairfield County, and has 70 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 388 square miles of land (1,004 sq km). The largest is 43078 (Urbana) with 150 square miles, and the smallest is 43047 (Mingo) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 26.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Champaign County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 43083 (Westville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Champaign County ranks 54th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Champaign County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:
