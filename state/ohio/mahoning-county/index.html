Where Mahoning County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Mahoning County is in the north-east of Ohio. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 44436 (Lowellville) and 44672 (Sebring), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Trumbull County (17 miles), Columbiana County (18 miles), Lawrence County, Pennsylvania (18 miles) and Portage County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Mahoning County begin with 445 (15 codes), 444 (14 codes) and 446 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 445, so those three digits alone point to Mahoning County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 28 of the 32 codes. Together they come to about 349 square miles of land (905 sq km). The largest is 44406 (Canfield) with 54.5 square miles, and the smallest is 44555 (Youngstown) with 0.09. The middle-sized code here covers 7.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Ohio is 22.2. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 12.5 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Mahoning County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 44416 (Ellsworth), 44422 (Greenford), 44501 (Youngstown) and 44513 (Youngstown). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Mahoning County ranks 8th among the 88 counties and county-level areas of Ohio (equal counts share a rank), with 32 of the 1,416 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Mahoning County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Youngstown, OH 44501 (one of 15 codes for Youngstown; the street decides which)
  • Beloit, OH 44609
  • Berlin Center, OH 44401

Youngstown is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
