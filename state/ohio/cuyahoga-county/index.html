Where Cuyahoga County is

The ZIP codes of Cuyahoga County are found in the north-east of Ohio. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 26 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 44040 (Gates Mills) and 44138 (Olmsted Falls), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lake County (22 miles), Lorain County (25 miles), Geauga County (25 miles) and Summit County (28 miles).

Writing an address in Cuyahoga County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters OH and the ZIP code:

  • Cleveland, OH 44101 (one of 40 codes for Cleveland; the street decides which)
  • Euclid, OH 44117 (one of 3 codes for Euclid; the street decides which)
  • Bay Village, OH 44140

Cleveland is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 18 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Chagrin Falls and Strongsville share a name with a place in another part of Ohio; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
