Where Northwest Arctic is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Northwest Arctic is in the north-west of Alaska. Its codes spread over about 121 miles from north to south and 208 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 99750 (Kivalina) and 99751 (Kobuk), about 211 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Nome Census Area (199 miles) and North Slope (249 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Northwest Arctic starts with 997. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 997 is shared with 6 other boroughs and census areas, such as Denali, Fairbanks North Star and Nome Census Area, and has 82 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 4,036 square miles of land (10,453 sq km). The largest is 99752 (Kotzebue) with 3,967 square miles, and the smallest is 99727 (Buckland) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 8.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Alaska is 47.5. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 962 square miles, roughly 19% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Northwest Arctic; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Northwest Arctic ranks 10th among the 30 boroughs and census areas of Alaska (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 273 codes in the state list.
