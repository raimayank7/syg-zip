Where Matanuska-Susitna Borough is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Matanuska-Susitna Borough sits near the middle of Alaska. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 55 miles, and from east to west about 85 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 99667 (Skwentna) and 99674 (Sutton), about 87 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Anchorage Municipality (51 miles), Kenai Peninsula (106 miles), Chugach Census Area (113 miles) and Denali (148 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 996. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 996 is shared with 14 other boroughs and census areas, such as Aleutians East, Aleutians West Census Area and Anchorage Municipality, and has 89 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 12,234 square miles of land (31,686 sq km). The largest is 99667 (Skwentna) with 4,873 square miles, and the smallest is 99694 (Houston) with 26.9. The middle-sized code here covers 854 square miles; the middle value for all of Alaska is 47.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Matanuska-Susitna Borough; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 99629 (Wasilla) and 99687 (Wasilla). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Matanuska-Susitna Borough ranks 7th among the 30 boroughs and census areas of Alaska (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 273 codes in the state list.
