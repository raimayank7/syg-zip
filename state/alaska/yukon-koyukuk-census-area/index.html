Where Yukon-Koyukuk Census Area is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Yukon-Koyukuk Census Area is in the north of Alaska. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 410 miles, and from east to west about 481 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 99602 (Holy Cross) and 99722 (Arctic Village), about 581 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Denali (121 miles), Fairbanks North Star (146 miles), Matanuska-Susitna Borough (234 miles) and Anchorage Municipality (273 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Yukon-Koyukuk Census Area begin with 997 (23 codes), 996 (5 codes) and 995 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 997 is shared with 6 other boroughs and census areas, such as Denali, Fairbanks North Star and Nome Census Area, and has 82 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 44,457 square miles of land (115,143 sq km). The largest is 99740 (Fort Yukon) with 8,051 square miles, and the smallest is 99758 (Minto) with 2.9. The middle-sized code here covers 26.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Alaska is 47.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Yukon-Koyukuk Census Area; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Yukon-Koyukuk Census Area ranks 2nd among the 30 boroughs and census areas of Alaska (equal counts share a rank), with 30 of the 273 codes in the state list.
