Where Copper River Census Area is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Copper River Census Area is in the east of Alaska. Its codes spread over about 54 miles from north to south and 40 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 99566 (Chitina) and 99586 (Gakona), about 61 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Chugach Census Area (100 miles), Southeast Fairbanks Census Area (138 miles), Matanuska-Susitna Borough (150 miles) and Anchorage Municipality (181 miles).

The first three digits

All 4 codes here begin with the same three digits, 995. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 995 is shared with 11 other boroughs and census areas, such as Aleutians East, Aleutians West Census Area and Anchorage Municipality, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 18,557 square miles of land (48,063 sq km). The largest is 99566 (Chitina) with 9,380 square miles, and the smallest is 99586 (Gakona) with 1,847. The middle-sized code here covers 3,665 square miles; the middle value for all of Alaska is 47.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Copper River Census Area; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Copper River Census Area ranks 21st among the 30 boroughs and census areas of Alaska (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 273 codes in the state list.
