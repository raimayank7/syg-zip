Where Denali is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Denali fall in the north of Alaska. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 66 miles, and from east to west about 33 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 99729 (Cantwell) and 99744 (Anderson), about 66 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Fairbanks North Star (81 miles), Yukon-Koyukuk Census Area (121 miles), Matanuska-Susitna Borough (148 miles) and Southeast Fairbanks Census Area (179 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Denali starts with 997. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 997 is shared with 6 other boroughs and census areas, such as Fairbanks North Star, Nome Census Area and North Slope, and has 82 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 6,402 square miles of land (16,580 sq km). The largest is 99743 (Healy) with 3,229 square miles, and the smallest is 99704 (Clear) with 13.8. The middle-sized code here covers 184 square miles; the middle value for all of Alaska is 47.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Denali; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Denali ranks 19th among the 30 boroughs and census areas of Alaska (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 273 codes in the state list.
