Where Prince of Wales-Hyder is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Prince of Wales-Hyder is in the south-east of Alaska. Its codes spread over about 85 miles from north to south and 180 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 99836 (Port Alexander) and 99923 (Hyder), about 180 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Ketchikan Gateway (45 miles), City and Borough of Wrangell (50 miles), Petersburg (89 miles) and Sitka City and Borough (138 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Prince of Wales-Hyder begin with 999 (9 codes) and 998 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 999 is shared with 2 other boroughs and census areas, City and Borough of Wrangell and Ketchikan Gateway, and has 13 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,741 square miles of land (7,099 sq km). The largest is 99921 (Craig) with 2,241 square miles, and the smallest is 99836 (Port Alexander) with 3.5. The middle-sized code here covers 51.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Alaska is 47.5. Water adds about 531 square miles, roughly 16% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Prince of Wales-Hyder; a code can reach across its border.
