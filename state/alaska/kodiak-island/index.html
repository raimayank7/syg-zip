Where Kodiak Island is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Kodiak Island is in the south of Alaska. Its codes spread over about 50 miles from north to south and 76 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 99608 (Karluk) and 99619 (Kodiak), about 77 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Lake and Peninsula (138 miles), Bristol Bay (152 miles), Kenai Peninsula (202 miles) and Dillingham Census Area (220 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Kodiak Island begin with 996 (7 codes) and 995 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 996 is shared with 14 other boroughs and census areas, such as Aleutians East, Aleutians West Census Area and Anchorage Municipality, and has 89 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 4,239 square miles of land (10,978 sq km). The largest is 99615 (Kodiak) with 4,128 square miles, and the smallest is 99550 (Port Lions) with 5.0. The middle-sized code here covers 17.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Alaska is 47.5. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 489 square miles, roughly 10% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Kodiak Island; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 99619 (Kodiak) and 99697 (Kodiak). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Kodiak Island ranks 13th among the 30 boroughs and census areas of Alaska (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 273 codes in the state list.
