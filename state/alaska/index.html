Alaska ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 273 ZIP codes for Alaska, about 0.7% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 46th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 30 boroughs, census areas and county-level areas. The most codes are in Anchorage Municipality (31), Yukon-Koyukuk Census Area (30), Bethel Census Area (27), Fairbanks North Star (16) and Kenai Peninsula (15). At the other end, 6 have only one or two codes, for example City and Borough of Wrangell, Haines and Petersburg.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Alaska uses 5 of these prefixes, from 995 to 999. The busiest are 996 (89 codes, most of them for Wasilla), 997 (82 codes, most of them for Fairbanks), 995 (69 codes, most of them for Anchorage) and 998 (20 codes, most of them for Juneau). The first digit 9 is shared with California, Hawaii, Oregon and Washington.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 245 of the 273 codes. The largest is 99557 (Aniak, Bethel Census Area) at 13,680 square miles and the smallest is 99513 (Anchorage) at 0.01; the middle value is 47.5 square miles. The other 28 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 99723 (Barrow) and the southernmost 99546 (Adak); the one farthest east is 99923 (Hyder) and farthest west 99546 (Adak).

Place names and addresses in Alaska

Of the 228 place names in the list, 221 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Anchorage (25), Fairbanks (10), Juneau (6) and Wasilla (4). In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters AK and the code, as in Anchorage, AK 99501.
