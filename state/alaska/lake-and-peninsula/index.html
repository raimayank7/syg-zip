Where Lake and Peninsula is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Lake and Peninsula is in the south of Alaska. Its codes spread over about 294 miles from north to south and 189 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 99648 (Perryville) and 99653 (Port Alsworth), about 345 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Bristol Bay (34 miles), Dillingham Census Area (91 miles), Kodiak Island (138 miles) and Bethel Census Area (247 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lake and Peninsula begin with 996 (7 codes) and 995 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 996 is shared with 14 other boroughs and census areas, such as Aleutians East, Aleutians West Census Area and Anchorage Municipality, and has 89 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 3,293 square miles of land (8,529 sq km). The largest is 99640 (Nondalton) with 2,686 square miles, and the smallest is 99564 (Chignik) with 10.9. The middle-sized code here covers 26.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Alaska is 47.5. Water adds about 171 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lake and Peninsula; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lake and Peninsula ranks 7th among the 30 boroughs and census areas of Alaska (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 273 codes in the state list.
