Where Nome Census Area is

The ZIP codes of Nome Census Area are found in the north-west of Alaska. Its codes spread over about 190 miles from north to south and 324 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 99684 (Unalakleet) and 99742 (Gambell), about 332 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Kusilvak (168 miles), Northwest Arctic (199 miles) and Bethel Census Area (286 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Nome Census Area begin with 997 (11 codes) and 996 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 997 is shared with 6 other boroughs and census areas, such as Denali, Fairbanks North Star and North Slope, and has 82 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 10,504 square miles of land (27,206 sq km). The largest is 99772 (Shishmaref) with 3,978 square miles, and the smallest is 99784 (White Mountain) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 8.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Alaska is 47.5. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 380 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Nome Census Area; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Nome Census Area ranks 6th among the 30 boroughs and census areas of Alaska (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 273 codes in the state list.
