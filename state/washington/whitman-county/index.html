Where Whitman County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Whitman County is in the east of Washington. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 49 miles, and from east to west about 51 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 99033 (Tekoa) and 99333 (Hooper), about 60 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Latah County, Idaho (31 miles), Garfield County (34 miles), Benewah County, Idaho (38 miles) and Columbia County (48 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Whitman County begin with 991 (19 codes), 990 (2 codes) and 993 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 991 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Adams County, Ferry County and Grant County, and has 67 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 20 of the 22 codes. Together they come to about 2,229 square miles of land (5,773 sq km). The largest is 99143 (Lacrosse) with 331 square miles, and the smallest is 99174 (Steptoe) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 83.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Whitman County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 99104 (Belmont) and 99164 (Pullman). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Whitman County ranks 8th among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 22 of the 716 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Whitman County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WA and the ZIP code:

Questions about Whitman County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Pullman, WA?

Pullman has 2 ZIP codes in Whitman County: 99163, 99164. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Whitman County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 991, on 19 of the 22 codes.

Which ZIP code in Whitman County covers the most land?

99143 (Lacrosse), at about 331 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
