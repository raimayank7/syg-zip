Where Lincoln County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Lincoln County fall in the east of Washington. Its codes spread over about 36 miles from north to south and 47 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 99032 (Sprague) and 99103 (Almira), about 53 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Adams County (45 miles), Spokane County (48 miles), Grant County (50 miles) and Stevens County (54 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lincoln County begin with 991 (9 codes) and 990 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 991 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Adams County, Ferry County and Grant County, and has 67 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,375 square miles of land (6,151 sq km). The largest is 99122 (Davenport) with 601 square miles, and the smallest is 99147 (Lincoln) with 8.8. The middle-sized code here covers 163 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lincoln County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lincoln County ranks 20th among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 716 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lincoln County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WA and the ZIP code:
