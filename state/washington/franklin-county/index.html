Where Franklin County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Franklin County is in the south-east of Washington. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 31 miles, and from east to west about 29 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 99302 (Pasco) and 99335 (Kahlotus), about 41 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Benton County (29 miles), Walla Walla County (32 miles), Adams County (38 miles) and Columbia County (45 miles).

The first three digits

All 6 codes here begin with the same three digits, 993. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 993 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Adams County, Benton County and Columbia County, and has 36 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 1,150 square miles of land (2,978 sq km). The largest is 99301 (Pasco) with 486 square miles, and the smallest is 99330 (Eltopia) with 87.5. The middle-sized code here covers 181 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Franklin County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 99302 (Pasco). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Franklin County ranks 32nd among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 716 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Franklin County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WA and the ZIP code:

  • Connell, WA 99326
  • Eltopia, WA 99330
