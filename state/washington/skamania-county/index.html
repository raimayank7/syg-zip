Where Skamania County is

The ZIP codes of Skamania County are found in the south-west of Washington. Its codes spread over about 7.7 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 98639 (North Bonneville) and 98651 (Underwood), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Hood River County, Oregon (11 miles), Klickitat County (36 miles), Clark County (36 miles) and Clackamas County, Oregon (39 miles).

The first three digits

All 4 codes here begin with the same three digits, 986. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 986 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Clark County, Cowlitz County and Klickitat County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 302 square miles of land (782 sq km). The largest is 98648 (Stevenson) with 144 square miles, and the smallest is 98639 (North Bonneville) with 2.8. The middle-sized code here covers 77.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 12.3 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Skamania County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Skamania County ranks 35th among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 716 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Skamania County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WA and the ZIP code:

  • Carson, WA 98610
  • North Bonneville, WA 98639
  • Stevenson, WA 98648

Carson is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
