Where Thurston County is

The ZIP codes of Thurston County are found in the west of Washington. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 98502 (Olympia) and 98597 (Yelm), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Pierce County (25 miles), Mason County (28 miles), Lewis County (29 miles) and Kitsap County (46 miles).

The first three digits

All 21 codes here begin with the same three digits, 985. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 985 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Cowlitz County, Grays Harbor County and Lewis County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 21 codes. Together they come to about 735 square miles of land (1,903 sq km). The largest is 98512 (Olympia) with 139 square miles, and the smallest is 98556 (Littlerock) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 42.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Water adds about 41.1 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Thurston County; a code can reach across its border. 7 codes have no mapped area: 98504 (Olympia), 98507 (Olympia), 98508 (Olympia), 98509 (Lacey), 98511 (Tumwater) and 2 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Thurston County ranks 10th among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 21 of the 716 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Thurston County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WA and the ZIP code:

  • Olympia, WA 98501 (one of 11 codes for Olympia; the street decides which)
  • Lacey, WA 98503 (one of 2 codes for Lacey; the street decides which)
  • Bucoda, WA 98530
