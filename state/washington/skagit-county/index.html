Where Skagit County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Skagit County fall in the north-west of Washington. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 60 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 98221 (Anacortes) and 98267 (Marblemount), about 60 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Island County (31 miles), Whatcom County (31 miles), Snohomish County (37 miles) and San Juan County (39 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Skagit County starts with 982. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 982 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Island County, King County and San Juan County, and has 76 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,887 square miles of land (4,886 sq km). The largest is 98283 (Rockport) with 468 square miles, and the smallest is 98238 (Conway) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 53.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Water adds about 77.0 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Skagit County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Skagit County ranks 17th among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 716 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Skagit County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WA and the ZIP code:

  • Mount Vernon, WA 98273 (one of 2 codes for Mount Vernon; the street decides which)
  • Anacortes, WA 98221
  • Bow, WA 98232

Mount Vernon is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 15 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
