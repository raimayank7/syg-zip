Where Kitsap County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Kitsap County is in the west of Washington. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 34 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 98322 (Burley) and 98340 (Hansville), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are King County (19 miles), Jefferson County (23 miles), Mason County (28 miles) and Snohomish County (31 miles).

How much ground the codes cover

Water adds about 62.1 square miles, roughly 13% of the mapped area.
