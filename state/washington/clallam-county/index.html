Where Clallam County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Clallam County fall in the north-west of Washington. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 69 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 98357 (Neah Bay) and 98382 (Sequim), about 71 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Jefferson County (57 miles), San Juan County (59 miles), Island County (66 miles) and Mason County (67 miles).

The first three digits

All 11 codes here begin with the same three digits, 983. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 983 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Jefferson County, Kitsap County and Lewis County, and has 81 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 2,358 square miles of land (6,108 sq km). The largest is 98331 (Forks) with 930 square miles, and the smallest is 98350 (La Push) with 1.5. The middle-sized code here covers 207 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 77.4 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clallam County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 98324 (Carlsborg) and 98343 (Joyce). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Clallam County ranks 20th among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 716 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clallam County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WA and the ZIP code:

  • Port Angeles, WA 98362 (one of 2 codes for Port Angeles; the street decides which)
  • Beaver, WA 98305
  • Carlsborg, WA 98324
