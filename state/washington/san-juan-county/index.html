Where San Juan County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of San Juan County fall in the north-west of Washington. Its codes spread over about 14 miles from north to south and 13 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 98261 (Lopez Island) and 98297 (Waldron), about 15 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Whatcom County (28 miles), Island County (36 miles), Skagit County (39 miles) and Jefferson County (46 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 982. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 982 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Island County, King County and Skagit County, and has 76 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 168 square miles of land (435 sq km). The largest is 98250 (Friday Harbor) with 61.1 square miles, and the smallest is 98243 (Deer Harbor) with 3.0. The middle-sized code here covers 7.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 120 square miles, roughly 42% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of San Juan County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, San Juan County ranks 24th among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 716 codes in the state list.
