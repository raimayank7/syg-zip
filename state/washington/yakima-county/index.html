Where Yakima County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Yakima County fall in the south of Washington. Its codes spread over about 41 miles from north to south and 51 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 98930 (Grandview) and 98937 (Naches), about 64 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Kittitas County (45 miles), Benton County (55 miles), Klickitat County (56 miles) and Franklin County (71 miles).

The first three digits

All 25 codes here begin with the same three digits, 989. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 989 is shared with 1 other county, Kittitas County, and has 34 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 21 of the 25 codes. Together they come to about 3,602 square miles of land (9,330 sq km). The largest is 98937 (Naches) with 865 square miles, and the smallest is 98921 (Buena) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 106 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Yakima County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 98904 (Yakima), 98907 (Yakima), 98909 (Yakima) and 98920 (Brownstown). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Yakima County ranks 6th among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 25 of the 716 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Yakima County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WA and the ZIP code:

  • Yakima, WA 98901 (one of 7 codes for Yakima; the street decides which)
  • Brownstown, WA 98920
  • Buena, WA 98921
