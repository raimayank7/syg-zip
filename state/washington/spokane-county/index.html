Where Spokane County is

The ZIP codes of Spokane County are found in the east of Washington. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 50 miles, and from east to west about 37 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 99009 (Elk) and 99018 (Latah), about 50 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Kootenai County, Idaho (29 miles), Benewah County, Idaho (45 miles), Lincoln County (48 miles) and Whitman County (49 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Spokane County begin with 992 (28 codes) and 990 (25 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 992, so those three digits alone point to Spokane County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 41 of the 53 codes. Together they come to about 1,817 square miles of land (4,706 sq km). The largest is 99004 (Cheney) with 334 square miles, and the smallest is 99020 (Marshall) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 23.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Spokane County; a code can reach across its border. 12 codes have no mapped area: 99209 (Spokane), 99210 (Spokane), 99211 (Spokane), 99213 (Spokane), 99214 (Spokane) and 7 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Spokane County ranks 3rd among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 53 of the 716 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Spokane County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WA and the ZIP code:

  • Spokane, WA 99201 (one of 28 codes for Spokane; the street decides which)
  • Airway Heights, WA 99001
  • Chattaroy, WA 99003
