Where Clark County is

The ZIP codes of Clark County are found in the south-west of Washington. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 98629 (La Center) and 98671 (Washougal), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Multnomah County, Oregon (13 miles), Washington County, Oregon (21 miles), Columbia County, Oregon (24 miles) and Clackamas County, Oregon (25 miles).

The first three digits

All 23 codes here begin with the same three digits, 986. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 986 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Cowlitz County, Klickitat County and Pacific County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 19 of the 23 codes. Together they come to about 640 square miles of land (1,659 sq km). The largest is 98675 (Yacolt) with 155 square miles, and the smallest is 98663 (Vancouver) with 4.1. The middle-sized code here covers 15.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 26.6 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clark County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 98622 (Heisson), 98666 (Vancouver), 98668 (Vancouver) and 98687 (Vancouver). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Clark County ranks 7th among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 23 of the 716 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clark County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WA and the ZIP code:
