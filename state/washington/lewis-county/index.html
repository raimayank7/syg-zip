Where Lewis County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Lewis County is in the south-west of Washington. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 79 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 98361 (Packwood) and 98572 (Pe Ell), about 79 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Thurston County (29 miles), Cowlitz County (30 miles), Pierce County (43 miles) and Wahkiakum County (44 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lewis County begin with 985 (17 codes) and 983 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 985 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Cowlitz County, Grays Harbor County and Mason County, and has 77 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 21 of the 22 codes. Together they come to about 2,263 square miles of land (5,861 sq km). The largest is 98361 (Packwood) with 345 square miles, and the smallest is 98544 (Galvin) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 113 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lewis County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 98522 (Adna). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lewis County ranks 8th among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 22 of the 716 codes in the state list.
