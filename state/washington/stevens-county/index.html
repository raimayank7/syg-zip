Where Stevens County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Stevens County fall in the north-east of Washington. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 73 miles, and from east to west about 30 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 99040 (Wellpinit) and 99157 (Northport), about 73 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Pend Oreille County (33 miles), Ferry County (39 miles), Spokane County (49 miles) and Lincoln County (54 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Stevens County begin with 991 (15 codes) and 990 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 991 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Adams County, Ferry County and Grant County, and has 67 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,751 square miles of land (7,124 sq km). The largest is 99114 (Colville) with 719 square miles, and the smallest is 99151 (Marcus) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 89.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Stevens County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Stevens County ranks 13th among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 716 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Stevens County
