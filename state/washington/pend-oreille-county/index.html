Where Pend Oreille County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Pend Oreille County is in the north-east of Washington. Its codes spread over about 48 miles from north to south and 12 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 99153 (Metaline Falls) and 99156 (Newport), about 49 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Stevens County (33 miles), Bonner County, Idaho (37 miles), Boundary County, Idaho (50 miles) and Ferry County (52 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Pend Oreille County starts with 991. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 991 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Adams County, Ferry County and Grant County, and has 67 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,361 square miles of land (3,526 sq km). The largest is 99119 (Cusick) with 373 square miles, and the smallest is 99152 (Metaline) with 4.4. The middle-sized code here covers 253 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pend Oreille County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Pend Oreille County ranks 32nd among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 716 codes in the state list.
