Where Douglas County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Douglas County sits near the middle of Washington. Its codes spread over about 44 miles from north to south and 40 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 98802 (East Wenatchee) and 98830 (Mansfield), about 52 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Chelan County (20 miles), Grant County (34 miles), Kittitas County (52 miles) and Okanogan County (54 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 988. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 988 is shared with 3 other counties, Chelan County, Grant County and Okanogan County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,264 square miles of land (3,274 sq km). The largest is 98830 (Mansfield) with 410 square miles, and the smallest is 98845 (Palisades) with 62.8. The middle-sized code here covers 100 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Douglas County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Douglas County ranks 29th among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 716 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Douglas County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WA and the ZIP code:

  • Bridgeport, WA 98813
  • East Wenatchee, WA 98802
  • Mansfield, WA 98830

Bridgeport is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 13 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
