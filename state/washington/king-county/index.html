Where King County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of King County fall in the west of Washington. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 35 miles, and from east to west about 51 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 98013 (Burton) and 98288 (Skykomish), about 55 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Kitsap County (19 miles), Snohomish County (27 miles), Pierce County (28 miles) and Jefferson County (38 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of King County begin with 980 (59 codes), 981 (59 codes) and 982 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 980 is shared with 2 other counties, Kitsap County and Snohomish County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 86 of the 120 codes. Together they come to about 2,523 square miles of land (6,533 sq km). The largest is 98022 (Enumclaw) with 727 square miles, and the smallest is 98154 (Seattle) with under 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 7.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 103 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of King County; a code can reach across its border. 34 codes have no mapped area: 98009 (Bellevue), 98013 (Burton), 98015 (Bellevue), 98035 (Kent), 98041 (Bothell) and 29 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Washington has more ZIP codes: King County holds 120 of the 716 in the state list, about 16.8%.

Writing an address in King County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WA and the ZIP code:

  • Seattle, WA 98101 (one of 59 codes for Seattle; the street decides which)
  • Bellevue, WA 98004 (one of 7 codes for Bellevue; the street decides which)
  • Kent, WA 98030 (one of 7 codes for Kent; the street decides which)

Bothell and Woodinville share a name with a place in another part of Washington; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
