Where Snohomish County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Snohomish County is in the north-west of Washington. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 34 miles, and from east to west about 45 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 98256 (Index) and 98282 (Camano Island), about 51 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Island County (24 miles), King County (27 miles), Jefferson County (30 miles) and Kitsap County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Snohomish County begin with 982 (26 codes) and 980 (11 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 982 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Island County, King County and San Juan County, and has 76 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 29 of the 37 codes. Together they come to about 1,883 square miles of land (4,877 sq km). The largest is 98241 (Darrington) with 422 square miles, and the smallest is 98207 (Everett) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 17.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Snohomish County; a code can reach across its border. 8 codes have no mapped area: 98046 (Lynnwood), 98082 (Mill Creek), 98206 (Everett), 98213 (Everett), 98259 (North Lakewood) and 3 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Snohomish County ranks 4th among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 37 of the 716 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Snohomish County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WA and the ZIP code:

  • Everett, WA 98201 (one of 7 codes for Everett; the street decides which)
  • Lynnwood, WA 98036 (one of 4 codes for Lynnwood; the street decides which)
  • Snohomish, WA 98290 (one of 3 codes for Snohomish; the street decides which)

Bothell and Woodinville share a name with a place in another part of Washington; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
