Where Benton County is

The ZIP codes of Benton County are found in the south-east of Washington. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 30 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 99337 (Kennewick) and 99345 (Paterson), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Franklin County (29 miles), Morrow County, Oregon (42 miles), Walla Walla County (43 miles) and Umatilla County, Oregon (43 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 993. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 993 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Adams County, Columbia County and Franklin County, and has 36 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,367 square miles of land (3,541 sq km). The largest is 99350 (Prosser) with 600 square miles, and the smallest is 99336 (Kennewick) with 13.4. The middle-sized code here covers 80.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 50.1 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Benton County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Benton County ranks 23rd among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 716 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Benton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WA and the ZIP code:

  • Kennewick, WA 99336 (one of 3 codes for Kennewick; the street decides which)
  • Richland, WA 99352 (one of 2 codes for Richland; the street decides which)
  • Benton City, WA 99320
