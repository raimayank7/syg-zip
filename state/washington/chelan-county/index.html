Where Chelan County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Chelan County fall close to the middle of Washington. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 66 miles, and from east to west about 32 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 98828 (Malaga) and 98852 (Stehekin), about 69 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Douglas County (20 miles), Kittitas County (42 miles), Grant County (52 miles) and Okanogan County (61 miles).

The first three digits

All 14 codes here begin with the same three digits, 988. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 988 is shared with 3 other counties, Douglas County, Grant County and Okanogan County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 2,634 square miles of land (6,823 sq km). The largest is 98826 (Leavenworth) with 1,016 square miles, and the smallest is 98811 (Ardenvoir) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 97.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Chelan County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 98807 (Wenatchee). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Chelan County ranks 17th among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 716 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Chelan County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WA and the ZIP code:

  • Wenatchee, WA 98801 (one of 2 codes for Wenatchee; the street decides which)
  • Ardenvoir, WA 98811
  • Cashmere, WA 98815
