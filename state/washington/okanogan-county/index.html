Where Okanogan County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Okanogan County is in the north-east of Washington. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 65 miles, and from east to west about 66 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 98833 (Mazama) and 99124 (Elmer City), about 78 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Douglas County (54 miles), Ferry County (58 miles), Chelan County (61 miles) and Grant County (77 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Okanogan County begin with 988 (16 codes) and 991 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 988 is shared with 3 other counties, Chelan County, Douglas County and Grant County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 4,907 square miles of land (12,710 sq km). The largest is 98855 (Tonasket) with 632 square miles, and the smallest is 99124 (Elmer City) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 194 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Okanogan County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Okanogan County ranks 12th among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 716 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Okanogan County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WA and the ZIP code:

  • Brewster, WA 98812
  • Carlton, WA 98814
  • Conconully, WA 98819

Brewster is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
