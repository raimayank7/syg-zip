Where Grant County is

The ZIP codes of Grant County are found in the east of Washington. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 83 miles, and from east to west about 43 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 99133 (Grand Coulee) and 99349 (Mattawa), about 91 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Douglas County (34 miles), Adams County (49 miles), Lincoln County (50 miles) and Chelan County (52 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Grant County begin with 988 (9 codes), 991 (4 codes) and 993 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 988 is shared with 3 other counties, Chelan County, Douglas County and Okanogan County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 3,187 square miles of land (8,254 sq km). The largest is 98837 (Moses Lake) with 486 square miles, and the smallest is 98824 (George) with 1.0. The middle-sized code here covers 178 square miles; the middle value for all of Washington is 35.1. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 120 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Grant County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Grant County ranks 15th among the 39 counties and county-level areas of Washington (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 716 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Grant County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WA and the ZIP code:

  • Beverly, WA 99321
  • Coulee City, WA 99115
  • Electric City, WA 99123

Beverly is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
