Where Oneida County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Oneida County fall close to the middle of New York. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 45 miles, and from east to west about 36 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 13313 (Bridgewater) and 13483 (Westdale), about 46 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Madison County (21 miles), Herkimer County (22 miles), Onondaga County (40 miles) and Otsego County (41 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Oneida County begin with 134 (28 codes), 133 (19 codes), 135 (6 codes), 131 (3 codes) and 130 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 134 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Chenango County, Fulton County and Hamilton County, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 47 of the 57 codes. Together they come to about 1,518 square miles of land (3,932 sq km). The largest is 13338 (Forestport) with 229 square miles, and the smallest is 13455 (Sangerfield) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 17.6 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Oneida County; a code can reach across its border. 10 codes have no mapped area: 13341 (Franklin Springs), 13362 (Knoxboro), 13401 (Mc Connellsville), 13442 (Rome), 13449 (Rome) and 5 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Oneida County ranks 10th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 57 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Oneida County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Utica, NY 13501 (one of 6 codes for Utica; the street decides which)
  • Rome, NY 13440 (one of 4 codes for Rome; the street decides which)
  • Alder Creek, NY 13301

Utica is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 11 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Oneida County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Utica, NY?

Utica has 6 ZIP codes in Oneida County: 13501, 13502, 13503, 13504, 13505, 13599. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Oneida County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Oneida County covers the most land?
