Where Wyoming County is

The ZIP codes of Wyoming County are found in the west of New York. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 14037 (Cowlesville) and 14536 (Portageville), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Genesee County (21 miles), Livingston County (23 miles), Erie County (30 miles) and Allegany County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Wyoming County begin with 140 (8 codes), 145 (6 codes), 141 (4 codes) and 144 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 140 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Allegany County, Cattaraugus County and Chautauqua County, and has 65 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 590 square miles of land (1,527 sq km). The largest is 14569 (Warsaw) with 74.7 square miles, and the smallest is 14549 (Silver Lake) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 26.3 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wyoming County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Wyoming County ranks 43rd among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.
