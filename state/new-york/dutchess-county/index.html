Where Dutchess County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Dutchess County is in the south-east of New York. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 38 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 12531 (Holmes) and 12583 (Tivoli), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Ulster County (22 miles), Putnam County (24 miles), Litchfield, Connecticut (25 miles) and Northwest Hills, Connecticut (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Dutchess County begin with 125 (35 codes) and 126 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 125 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Columbia County, Orange County and Putnam County, and has 74 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 34 of the 39 codes. Together they come to about 839 square miles of land (2,174 sq km). The largest is 12571 (Red Hook) with 58.1 square miles, and the smallest is 12527 (Glenham) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 22.6 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Dutchess County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 12506 (Bangall), 12510 (Billings), 12511 (Castle Point), 12537 (Hughsonville) and 12602 (Poughkeepsie). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Dutchess County ranks 17th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 39 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Dutchess County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Poughkeepsie, NY 12601 (one of 4 codes for Poughkeepsie; the street decides which)
  • Amenia, NY 12501
  • Annandale On Hudson, NY 12504

Questions about Dutchess County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Poughkeepsie, NY?

Poughkeepsie has 4 ZIP codes in Dutchess County: 12601, 12602, 12603, 12604. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Dutchess County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 125, on 35 of the 39 codes.

Which ZIP code in Dutchess County covers the most land?

12571 (Red Hook), at about 58.1 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
