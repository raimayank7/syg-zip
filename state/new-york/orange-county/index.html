Where Orange County is

The ZIP codes of Orange County are found in the south-east of New York. Its codes spread over about 30 miles from north to south and 40 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 10997 (West Point) and 12780 (Sparrow Bush), about 40 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Rockland County (23 miles), Putnam County (25 miles), Sussex County, New Jersey (30 miles) and Passaic County, New Jersey (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Orange County begin with 109 (38 codes), 125 (14 codes) and 127 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 109 is shared with 1 other county, Rockland County, and has 65 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 47 of the 56 codes. Together they come to about 811 square miles of land (2,101 sq km). The largest is 10940 (Middletown) with 63.1 square miles, and the smallest is 10979 (Sterling Forest) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 11.3 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Orange County; a code can reach across its border. 9 codes have no mapped area: 10912 (Bellvale), 10949 (Monroe), 10959 (New Milford), 10981 (Sugar Loaf), 10997 (West Point) and 4 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Orange County ranks 12th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 56 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Orange County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Newburgh, NY 12550 (one of 4 codes for Newburgh; the street decides which)
  • Middletown, NY 10940 (one of 2 codes for Middletown; the street decides which)
  • Monroe, NY 10949 (one of 2 codes for Monroe; the street decides which)

Questions about Orange County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Newburgh, NY?

Newburgh has 4 ZIP codes in Orange County: 12550, 12551, 12552, 12555. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Orange County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 109, on 38 of the 56 codes.

Which ZIP code in Orange County covers the most land?

10940 (Middletown), at about 63.1 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
