Where Sullivan County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Sullivan County fall in the south-east of New York. Its codes spread over about 36 miles from north to south and 37 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 12722 (Burlingham) and 12760 (Long Eddy), about 41 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Wayne County, Pennsylvania (28 miles), Pike County, Pennsylvania (28 miles), Orange County (34 miles) and Ulster County (34 miles).

The first three digits

All 56 codes here begin with the same three digits, 127. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 127 is shared with 1 other county, Orange County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 55 of the 56 codes. Together they come to about 1,209 square miles of land (3,131 sq km). The largest is 12758 (Livingston Manor) with 154 square miles, and the smallest is 12722 (Burlingham) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 11.2 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sullivan County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 12727 (Cochecton Center). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Sullivan County ranks 12th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 56 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.
