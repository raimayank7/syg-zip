Where Columbia County is

The ZIP codes of Columbia County are found in the east of New York. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 31 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 12125 (New Lebanon) and 12526 (Germantown), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Berkshire County, Massachusetts (21 miles), Greene County (23 miles), Albany County (26 miles) and Rensselaer County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Columbia County begin with 125 (14 codes), 121 (12 codes) and 120 (6 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 125 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Dutchess County, Orange County and Putnam County, and has 74 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 30 of the 32 codes. Together they come to about 587 square miles of land (1,521 sq km). The largest is 12534 (Hudson) with 88.6 square miles, and the smallest is 12530 (Hollowville) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 16.2 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Columbia County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 12050 (Columbiaville) and 12541 (Livingston). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Columbia County ranks 22nd among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 32 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Columbia County
