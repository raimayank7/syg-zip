Where Erie County is

The ZIP codes of Erie County are found in the west of New York. Its codes spread over about 38 miles from north to south and 31 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 14001 (Akron) and 14061 (Farnham), about 42 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Niagara County (26 miles), Wyoming County (30 miles), Genesee County (34 miles) and Cattaraugus County (37 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Erie County begin with 142 (44 codes), 140 (30 codes) and 141 (13 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 142, so those three digits alone point to Erie County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 67 of the 87 codes. Together they come to about 1,029 square miles of land (2,664 sq km). The largest is 14141 (Springville) with 72.3 square miles, and the smallest is 14112 (North Evans) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 7.7 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Erie County; a code can reach across its border. 20 codes have no mapped area: 14010 (Athol Springs), 14038 (Crittenden), 14110 (North Boston), 14140 (Spring Brook), 14151 (Tonawanda) and 15 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Erie County ranks 4th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 87 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Erie County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Buffalo, NY 14201 (one of 44 codes for Buffalo; the street decides which)
  • Tonawanda, NY 14150 (one of 2 codes for Tonawanda; the street decides which)
  • Akron, NY 14001

Buffalo is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 16 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
