Where Madison County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Madison County fall close to the middle of New York. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 32 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 13037 (Chittenango) and 13364 (Leonardsville), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Oneida County (21 miles), Chenango County (26 miles), Onondaga County (29 miles) and Cortland County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Madison County begin with 130 (7 codes), 134 (7 codes), 133 (6 codes) and 131 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 130 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Cayuga County, Cortland County and Oneida County, and has 56 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 21 of the 23 codes. Together they come to about 603 square miles of land (1,561 sq km). The largest is 13032 (Canastota) with 82.5 square miles, and the smallest is 13484 (West Eaton) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 29.2 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Madison County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 13043 (Clockville) and 13465 (Solsville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Madison County ranks 33rd among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 23 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.
