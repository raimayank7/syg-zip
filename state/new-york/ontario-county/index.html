Where Ontario County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Ontario County fall in the west of New York. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 28 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 14512 (Naples) and 14537 (Port Gibson), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Wayne County (22 miles), Yates County (22 miles), Seneca County (25 miles) and Monroe County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Ontario County begin with 144 (11 codes) and 145 (10 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 144 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Genesee County, Livingston County and Monroe County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 21 codes. Together they come to about 628 square miles of land (1,628 sq km). The largest is 14424 (Canandaigua) with 129 square miles, and the smallest is 14585 (West Bloomfield) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 28.6 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ontario County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 14443 (East Bloomfield), 14461 (Gorham), 14518 (Oaks Corners) and 14547 (Seneca Castle). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Ontario County ranks 39th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 21 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Ontario County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Bloomfield, NY 14469
  • Canandaigua, NY 14424
  • Clifton Springs, NY 14432

Bloomfield is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
