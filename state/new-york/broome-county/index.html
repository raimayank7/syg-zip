Where Broome County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Broome County is in the south of New York. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 34 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 13754 (Deposit) and 13794 (Killawog), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Tioga County (24 miles), Susquehanna County, Pennsylvania (25 miles), Chenango County (30 miles) and Cortland County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Broome County begin with 137 (17 codes), 138 (9 codes) and 139 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 137 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Chenango County, Cortland County and Delaware County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 22 of the 31 codes. Together they come to about 741 square miles of land (1,918 sq km). The largest is 13865 (Windsor) with 107 square miles, and the smallest is 13902 (Binghamton) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 28.6 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Broome County; a code can reach across its border. 9 codes have no mapped area: 13737 (Bible School Park), 13745 (Chenango Bridge), 13749 (Corbettsville), 13761 (Endicott), 13762 (Endwell) and 4 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Broome County ranks 24th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 31 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.
