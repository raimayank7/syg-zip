Where Schuyler County is

The ZIP codes of Schuyler County are found in the west of New York. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 14824 (Cayuta) and 14893 (Wayne), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Chemung County (18 miles), Yates County (19 miles), Tompkins County (22 miles) and Seneca County (25 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 148. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 148 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Allegany County, Chemung County and Livingston County, and has 81 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 280 square miles of land (726 sq km). The largest is 14812 (Beaver Dams) with 76.4 square miles, and the smallest is 14893 (Wayne) with 0.08. The middle-sized code here covers 22.1 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Schuyler County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 14863 (Mecklenburg), 14876 (Reading Center) and 14887 (Tyrone). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Schuyler County ranks 55th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Schuyler County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Alpine, NY 14805
  • Beaver Dams, NY 14812
  • Burdett, NY 14818

Alpine is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
