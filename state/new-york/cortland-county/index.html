Where Cortland County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Cortland County fall close to the middle of New York. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 25 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 13141 (Preble) and 13863 (Willet), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Tompkins County (20 miles), Chenango County (26 miles), Onondaga County (30 miles) and Madison County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Cortland County begin with 130 (5 codes), 131 (3 codes), 137 (2 codes) and 138 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 130 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Cayuga County, Madison County and Oneida County, and has 56 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 496 square miles of land (1,285 sq km). The largest is 13045 (Cortland) with 108 square miles, and the smallest is 13738 (Blodgett Mills) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 38.4 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cortland County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 13056 (East Homer). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Cortland County ranks 55th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cortland County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:
