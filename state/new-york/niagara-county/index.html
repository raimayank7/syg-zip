Where Niagara County is

The ZIP codes of Niagara County are found in the west of New York. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 29 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 14012 (Barker) and 14302 (Niagara Falls), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Erie County (26 miles), Orleans County (34 miles), Genesee County (37 miles) and Wyoming County (47 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Niagara County begin with 141 (11 codes), 140 (7 codes) and 143 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 141 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Cattaraugus County, Chautauqua County and Erie County, and has 41 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 20 of the 23 codes. Together they come to about 529 square miles of land (1,369 sq km). The largest is 14094 (Lockport) with 122 square miles, and the smallest is 14302 (Niagara Falls) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 24.0 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Niagara County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 14095 (Lockport), 14107 (Model City) and 14144 (Stella Niagara). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Niagara County ranks 33rd among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 23 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Questions about Niagara County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Niagara Falls, NY?

Niagara Falls has 5 ZIP codes in Niagara County: 14301, 14302, 14303, 14304, 14305. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Niagara County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 141, on 11 of the 23 codes.

Which ZIP code in Niagara County covers the most land?

14094 (Lockport), at about 122 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
