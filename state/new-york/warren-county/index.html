Where Warren County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Warren County is in the north-east of New York. Its codes spread over about 30 miles from north to south and 29 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 12801 (Glens Falls) and 12856 (North River), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Washington County (24 miles), Hamilton County (35 miles), Saratoga County (36 miles) and Rutland County, Vermont (37 miles).

The first three digits

All 23 codes here begin with the same three digits, 128. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 128 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Essex County, Hamilton County and Saratoga County, and has 67 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 869 square miles of land (2,252 sq km). The largest is 12878 (Stony Creek) with 108 square miles, and the smallest is 12820 (Cleverdale) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 43.3 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 49.3 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Warren County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Warren County ranks 33rd among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 23 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.
