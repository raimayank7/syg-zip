Where Livingston County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Livingston County is in the west of New York. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 14485 (Lima) and 14846 (Hunt), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Wyoming County (23 miles), Genesee County (26 miles), Ontario County (28 miles) and Monroe County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Livingston County begin with 144 (12 codes), 145 (9 codes) and 148 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 144 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Genesee County, Monroe County and Ontario County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 21 of the 23 codes. Together they come to about 658 square miles of land (1,704 sq km). The largest is 14437 (Dansville) with 118 square miles, and the smallest is 14539 (Retsof) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 32.9 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Livingston County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 14488 (Livonia Center) and 14558 (South Lima). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Livingston County ranks 33rd among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 23 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Livingston County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Avon, NY 14414
  • Caledonia, NY 14423
  • Conesus, NY 14435

Avon is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 11 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
