Where St. Lawrence County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of St. Lawrence County fall in the north of New York. Its codes spread over about 57 miles from north to south and 59 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 13623 (Chippewa Bay) and 13683 (Rooseveltown), about 62 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Franklin County (39 miles), Jefferson County (55 miles), Lewis County (58 miles) and Hamilton County (69 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of St. Lawrence County begin with 136 (41 codes) and 129 (6 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 136 is shared with 3 other counties, Franklin County, Jefferson County and Lewis County, and has 88 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 45 of the 47 codes. Together they come to about 2,441 square miles of land (6,321 sq km). The largest is 13642 (Gouverneur) with 189 square miles, and the smallest is 13645 (Hailesboro) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 45.8 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 130 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of St. Lawrence County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 12949 (Lawrenceville) and 13677 (Pyrites). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, St. Lawrence County ranks 14th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 47 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in St. Lawrence County

Questions about St. Lawrence County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Potsdam, NY?

Potsdam has 2 ZIP codes in St. Lawrence County: 13676, 13699. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in St. Lawrence County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 136, on 41 of the 47 codes.

Which ZIP code in St. Lawrence County covers the most land?
