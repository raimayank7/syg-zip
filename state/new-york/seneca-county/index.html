Where Seneca County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Seneca County fall in the west of New York. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 7.5 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 13148 (Seneca Falls) and 14860 (Lodi), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Yates County (15 miles), Cayuga County (17 miles), Schuyler County (25 miles) and Ontario County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Seneca County begin with 145 (3 codes), 131 (2 codes), 148 (2 codes) and 130 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 145 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Genesee County, Livingston County and Monroe County, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 279 square miles of land (722 sq km). The largest is 13165 (Waterloo) with 68.2 square miles, and the smallest is 14588 (Willard) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 39.4 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 9.0 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Seneca County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Seneca County ranks 61st among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Seneca County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Fayette, NY 13065
  • Interlaken, NY 14847
  • Lodi, NY 14860

Fayette is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
