Where Delaware County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Delaware County fall in the east of New York. Its codes spread over about 35 miles from north to south and 46 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 12434 (Grand Gorge) and 13783 (Hancock), about 47 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Otsego County (26 miles), Schoharie County (34 miles), Sullivan County (39 miles) and Chenango County (40 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Delaware County begin with 137 (16 codes), 138 (9 codes), 124 (8 codes) and 121 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 137 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Broome County, Chenango County and Cortland County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,295 square miles of land (3,355 sq km). The largest is 13856 (Walton) with 180 square miles, and the smallest is 13847 (Trout Creek) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 35.7 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Delaware County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Delaware County ranks 20th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 34 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.
