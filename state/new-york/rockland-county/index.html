Where Rockland County is

The ZIP codes of Rockland County are found in the south-east of New York. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 10964 (Palisades) and 10974 (Sloatsburg), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Westchester County (12 miles), Bergen County, New Jersey (14 miles), Passaic County, New Jersey (16 miles) and Bronx County (20 miles).

The first three digits

All 27 codes here begin with the same three digits, 109. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 109 is shared with 1 other county, Orange County, and has 65 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 26 of the 27 codes. Together they come to about 174 square miles of land (450 sq km). The largest is 10980 (Stony Point) with 27.6 square miles, and the smallest is 10968 (Piermont) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 4.8 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Rockland County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 10982 (Tallman). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Rockland County ranks 29th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 27 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.
