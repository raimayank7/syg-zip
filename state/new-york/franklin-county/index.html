Where Franklin County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Franklin County fall in the north-east of New York. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 52 miles, and from east to west about 31 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 12986 (Tupper Lake) and 13655 (Hogansburg), about 53 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Clinton County (33 miles), St. Lawrence County (39 miles), Essex County (50 miles) and Grand Isle County, Vermont (53 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Franklin County begin with 129 (21 codes) and 136 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 129 is shared with 3 other counties, Clinton County, Essex County and St. Lawrence County, and has 74 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 21 of the 22 codes. Together they come to about 1,745 square miles of land (4,519 sq km). The largest is 12986 (Tupper Lake) with 287 square miles, and the smallest is 12915 (Brainardsville) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 45.1 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 74.3 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Franklin County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 12995 (Whippleville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Franklin County ranks 37th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 22 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Franklin County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:
