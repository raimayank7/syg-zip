Where Onondaga County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Onondaga County sits near the middle of New York. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 28 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 13051 (Delphi Falls) and 13137 (Plainville), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cayuga County (22 miles), Oswego County (26 miles), Madison County (29 miles) and Cortland County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Onondaga County begin with 132 (28 codes), 130 (19 codes) and 131 (13 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 132, so those three digits alone point to Onondaga County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 47 of the 60 codes. Together they come to about 806 square miles of land (2,088 sq km). The largest is 13159 (Tully) with 77.3 square miles, and the smallest is 13138 (Pompey) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 11.3 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Onondaga County; a code can reach across its border. 13 codes have no mapped area: 13089 (Liverpool), 13137 (Plainville), 13201 (Syracuse), 13217 (Syracuse), 13218 (Syracuse) and 8 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Onondaga County ranks 9th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 60 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Onondaga County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Syracuse, NY 13201 (one of 28 codes for Syracuse; the street decides which)
  • Liverpool, NY 13088 (one of 3 codes for Liverpool; the street decides which)
  • Apulia Station, NY 13020

Syracuse is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
