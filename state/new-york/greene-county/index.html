Where Greene County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Greene County fall in the east of New York. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 31 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 12124 (New Baltimore) and 12492 (West Kill), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Columbia County (23 miles), Albany County (27 miles), Ulster County (29 miles) and Schoharie County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Greene County begin with 124 (27 codes), 120 (6 codes) and 121 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 124 is shared with 3 other counties, Albany County, Delaware County and Ulster County, and has 83 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 638 square miles of land (1,652 sq km). The largest is 12414 (Catskill) with 62.2 square miles, and the smallest is 12452 (Lexington) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 12.9 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Greene County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Greene County ranks 19th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 36 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Greene County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Acra, NY 12405
  • Ashland, NY 12407
  • Athens, NY 12015
