Where Chemung County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Chemung County fall in the south-west of New York. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 14814 (Big Flats) and 14889 (Van Etten), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Schuyler County (18 miles), Tioga County (23 miles), Bradford County, Pennsylvania (27 miles) and Tompkins County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Chemung County begin with 148 (11 codes) and 149 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 148 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Allegany County, Livingston County and Schuyler County, and has 81 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 15 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 373 square miles of land (966 sq km). The largest is 14845 (Horseheads) with 78.2 square miles, and the smallest is 14905 (Elmira) with 3.7. The middle-sized code here covers 16.8 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Chemung County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 14902 (Elmira). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Chemung County ranks 50th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Chemung County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Elmira, NY 14901 (one of 5 codes for Elmira; the street decides which)
  • Big Flats, NY 14814
  • Breesport, NY 14816

Elmira is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Chemung County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Elmira, NY?

Elmira has 5 ZIP codes in Chemung County: 14901, 14902, 14903, 14904, 14905. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Chemung County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 148, on 11 of the 16 codes.

Which ZIP code in Chemung County covers the most land?

14845 (Horseheads), at about 78.2 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
