Where Genesee County is

The ZIP codes of Genesee County are found in the west of New York. Its codes spread over about 14 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 14013 (Basom) and 14486 (Linwood), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Orleans County (19 miles), Wyoming County (21 miles), Livingston County (26 miles) and Monroe County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Genesee County begin with 140 (9 codes), 144 (4 codes), 141 (2 codes) and 145 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 140 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Allegany County, Cattaraugus County and Chautauqua County, and has 65 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 16 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 529 square miles of land (1,370 sq km). The largest is 14020 (Batavia) with 66.3 square miles, and the smallest is 14056 (East Pembroke) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 33.9 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Genesee County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 14021 (Batavia). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Genesee County ranks 46th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Genesee County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Batavia, NY 14020 (one of 2 codes for Batavia; the street decides which)
  • Alexander, NY 14005
  • Basom, NY 14013

Batavia is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Genesee County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Batavia, NY?

Batavia has 2 ZIP codes in Genesee County: 14020, 14021. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Genesee County start with the same digits?

No. They use 4 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 140, on 9 of the 17 codes.

Which ZIP code in Genesee County covers the most land?

14020 (Batavia), at about 66.3 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
