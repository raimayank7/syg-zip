Where Tioga County is

The ZIP codes of Tioga County are found in the south of New York. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 26 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 13835 (Richford) and 14892 (Waverly), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Tompkins County (23 miles), Chemung County (23 miles), Broome County (24 miles) and Bradford County, Pennsylvania (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Tioga County begin with 138 (7 codes), 137 (4 codes) and 148 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 138 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Broome County, Chenango County and Cortland County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 594 square miles of land (1,539 sq km). The largest is 13827 (Owego) with 90.7 square miles, and the smallest is 13864 (Willseyville) with 25.8. The middle-sized code here covers 43.0 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Tioga County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 13840 (Smithboro) and 13845 (Tioga Center). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Tioga County ranks 53rd among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.
