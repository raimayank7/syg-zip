Where Putnam County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Putnam County fall in the south-east of New York. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 10 miles, and from east to west about 18 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 10524 (Garrison) and 12563 (Patterson), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Westchester County (20 miles), Western Connecticut, Connecticut (21 miles), Fairfield, Connecticut (22 miles) and Rockland County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Putnam County begin with 105 (8 codes) and 125 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 105 is shared with 1 other county, Westchester County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 225 square miles of land (582 sq km). The largest is 10512 (Carmel) with 52.2 square miles, and the smallest is 10542 (Mahopac Falls) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 24.8 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 13.7 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Putnam County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Putnam County ranks 60th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Putnam County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Brewster, NY 10509
  • Carmel, NY 10512
  • Cold Spring, NY 10516

Brewster is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
