Where Tompkins County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Tompkins County fall close to the middle of New York. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 13102 (Mc Lean) and 14867 (Newfield), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Cortland County (20 miles), Schuyler County (22 miles), Tioga County (23 miles) and Seneca County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Tompkins County begin with 148 (10 codes), 130 (4 codes) and 131 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 148 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Allegany County, Chemung County and Livingston County, and has 81 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 484 square miles of land (1,255 sq km). The largest is 14850 (Ithaca) with 125 square miles, and the smallest is 13102 (Mc Lean) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 34.8 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Tompkins County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 14851 (Ithaca) and 14852 (Ithaca). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Tompkins County ranks 51st among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Tompkins County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Ithaca, NY 14850 (one of 4 codes for Ithaca; the street decides which)
  • Brooktondale, NY 14817
  • Dryden, NY 13053

Questions about Tompkins County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Ithaca, NY?

Ithaca has 4 ZIP codes in Tompkins County: 14850, 14851, 14852, 14853. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Tompkins County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 148, on 10 of the 15 codes.

Which ZIP code in Tompkins County covers the most land?

14850 (Ithaca), at about 125 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
