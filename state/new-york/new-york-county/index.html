Where New York County is

The ZIP codes of New York County are found in the south-east of New York. Its codes spread over about 11 miles from north to south and 4.9 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 10034 (New York) and 10041 (New York), about 12 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hudson County, New Jersey (4.7 miles), Kings County (7.6 miles), Bronx County (8.2 miles) and Queens County (8.7 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of New York County begin with 101 (65 codes), 100 (52 codes) and 102 (29 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 101, so those three digits alone point to New York County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 69 of the 146 codes. Together they come to about 22.2 square miles of land (57.4 sq km). The largest is 10035 (New York) with 1.4 square miles, and the smallest is 10169 (New York) with under 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 0.3 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of New York County; a code can reach across its border. 77 codes have no mapped area: 10008, 10041, 10043, 10045, 10055 and 72 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of New York has more ZIP codes: New York County holds 146 of the 2,154 in the state list, about 6.8%.

Writing an address in New York County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • New York, NY 10001 (one of 146 codes for New York; the street decides which)
