Where Chautauqua County is

The ZIP codes of Chautauqua County are found in the west of New York. Its codes spread over about 39 miles from north to south and 35 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 14081 (Irving) and 14724 (Clymer), about 47 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Warren County, Pennsylvania (29 miles), Cattaraugus County (34 miles), Erie County, Pennsylvania (40 miles) and Erie County (48 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Chautauqua County begin with 147 (31 codes), 140 (4 codes) and 141 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 147 is shared with 2 other counties, Allegany County and Cattaraugus County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 36 of the 38 codes. Together they come to about 1,104 square miles of land (2,860 sq km). The largest is 14701 (Jamestown) with 92.6 square miles, and the smallest is 14756 (Maple Springs) with 0.02. The middle-sized code here covers 27.1 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Chautauqua County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 14702 (Jamestown) and 14758 (Niobe). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Chautauqua County ranks 18th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 38 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Chautauqua County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Jamestown, NY 14701 (one of 2 codes for Jamestown; the street decides which)
  • Ashville, NY 14710
  • Bemus Point, NY 14712

Jamestown is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 17 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Chautauqua County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Jamestown, NY?

Jamestown has 2 ZIP codes in Chautauqua County: 14701, 14702. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Chautauqua County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 147, on 31 of the 38 codes.

Which ZIP code in Chautauqua County covers the most land?

14701 (Jamestown), at about 92.6 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
