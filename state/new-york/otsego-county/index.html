Where Otsego County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Otsego County fall close to the middle of New York. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 36 miles, and from east to west about 34 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 13333 (East Springfield) and 13849 (Unadilla), about 44 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Delaware County (26 miles), Chenango County (30 miles), Schoharie County (30 miles) and Madison County (36 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Otsego County begin with 138 (9 codes), 133 (8 codes), 134 (8 codes), 121 (3 codes), 137 (3 codes) and 120 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 138 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Broome County, Chenango County and Cortland County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,005 square miles of land (2,602 sq km). The largest is 13820 (Oneonta) with 107 square miles, and the smallest is 13747 (Colliersville) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 22.9 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Otsego County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Otsego County ranks 22nd among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 32 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.
