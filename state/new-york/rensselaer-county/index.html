Where Rensselaer County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Rensselaer County fall in the east of New York. Its codes spread over about 33 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 12133 (North Hoosick) and 12156 (Schodack Landing), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Albany County (15 miles), Schenectady County (25 miles), Saratoga County (27 miles) and Columbia County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Rensselaer County begin with 121 (17 codes) and 120 (14 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 121 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Albany County, Columbia County and Delaware County, and has 73 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 28 of the 31 codes. Together they come to about 697 square miles of land (1,805 sq km). The largest is 12138 (Petersburg) with 89.6 square miles, and the smallest is 12082 (Grafton) with 0.02. The middle-sized code here covers 21.4 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Rensselaer County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 12089 (Hoosick), 12133 (North Hoosick) and 12181 (Troy). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Rensselaer County ranks 24th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 31 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Rensselaer County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Troy, NY 12180 (one of 3 codes for Troy; the street decides which)
  • Stephentown, NY 12168 (one of 2 codes for Stephentown; the street decides which)
  • Averill Park, NY 12018

ZIP codes named Troy are also listed under Albany County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Troy is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 19 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Rensselaer County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Troy, NY?

Troy has 3 ZIP codes in Rensselaer County: 12180, 12181, 12182. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Rensselaer County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 121, on 17 of the 31 codes.

Which ZIP code in Rensselaer County covers the most land?

12138 (Petersburg), at about 89.6 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
