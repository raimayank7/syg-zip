Where Cattaraugus County is

The ZIP codes of Cattaraugus County are found in the west of New York. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 34 miles, and from east to west about 37 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 14168 (Versailles) and 14770 (Portville), about 48 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are McKean County, Pennsylvania (32 miles), Allegany County (32 miles), Chautauqua County (34 miles) and Wyoming County (36 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Cattaraugus County begin with 147 (21 codes), 141 (7 codes) and 140 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 147 is shared with 2 other counties, Allegany County and Chautauqua County, and has 69 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 29 of the 33 codes. Together they come to about 1,293 square miles of land (3,348 sq km). The largest is 14779 (Salamanca) with 139 square miles, and the smallest is 14168 (Versailles) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 45.1 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cattaraugus County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 14133 (Sandusky), 14730 (East Randolph), 14751 (Leon) and 14766 (Otto). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Cattaraugus County ranks 21st among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 33 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.
