Where Yates County is

The ZIP codes of Yates County are found in the west of New York. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 14544 (Rushville) and 14878 (Rock Stream), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Seneca County (15 miles), Schuyler County (19 miles), Ontario County (22 miles) and Steuben County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Yates County begin with 144 (4 codes), 148 (4 codes) and 145 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 144 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Genesee County, Livingston County and Monroe County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 360 square miles of land (933 sq km). The largest is 14527 (Penn Yan) with 135 square miles, and the smallest is 14441 (Dresden) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 23.1 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Yates County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 14857 (Lakemont). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Yates County ranks 57th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Yates County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Bellona, NY 14415
  • Branchport, NY 14418
  • Dresden, NY 14441

Questions about Yates County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Bellona, NY?

14415 is the ZIP code listed for Bellona in Yates County.

Do all ZIP codes in Yates County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 144, on 4 of the 11 codes.

Which ZIP code in Yates County covers the most land?

14527 (Penn Yan), at about 135 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
