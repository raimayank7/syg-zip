Where Richmond County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Richmond County fall in the south-east of New York. Its codes spread over about 8.6 miles from north to south and 8.8 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 10301 (Staten Island) and 10307 (Staten Island), about 12 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Union County, New Jersey (10 miles), Kings County (11 miles), Hudson County, New Jersey (11 miles) and Essex County, New Jersey (14 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Richmond County starts with 103. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 103, so those three digits alone point to Richmond County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 55.6 square miles of land (144 sq km). The largest is 10314 (Staten Island) with 13.0 square miles, and the smallest is 10311 (Staten Island) with 0.05. The middle-sized code here covers 3.5 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Richmond County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 10313. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Richmond County ranks 53rd among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Richmond County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Staten Island, NY 10301 (one of 14 codes for Staten Island; the street decides which)
