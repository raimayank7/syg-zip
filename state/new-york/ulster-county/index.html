Where Ulster County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Ulster County fall in the south-east of New York. Its codes spread over about 37 miles from north to south and 30 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 12441 (Highmount) and 12542 (Marlboro), about 45 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Dutchess County (22 miles), Greene County (29 miles), Sullivan County (34 miles) and Orange County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Ulster County begin with 124 (47 codes) and 125 (10 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 124 is shared with 3 other counties, Albany County, Delaware County and Greene County, and has 83 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 54 of the 57 codes. Together they come to about 942 square miles of land (2,439 sq km). The largest is 12446 (Kerhonkson) with 67.4 square miles, and the smallest is 12471 (Rifton) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 8.3 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ulster County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 12402 (Kingston), 12568 (Plattekill) and 12588 (Walker Valley). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Ulster County ranks 10th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 57 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Ulster County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Kingston, NY 12401 (one of 2 codes for Kingston; the street decides which)
  • Accord, NY 12404
  • Bearsville, NY 12409

Kingston is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 17 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Ulster County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Kingston, NY?

Kingston has 2 ZIP codes in Ulster County: 12401, 12402. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Ulster County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 124, on 47 of the 57 codes.

Which ZIP code in Ulster County covers the most land?

12446 (Kerhonkson), at about 67.4 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
