Where Westchester County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Westchester County is in the south-east of New York. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 31 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 10560 (North Salem) and 10705 (Yonkers), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Rockland County (12 miles), Western Connecticut, Connecticut (18 miles), Bronx County (19 miles) and Bergen County, New Jersey (19 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Westchester County begin with 105 (58 codes), 107 (10 codes), 106 (8 codes) and 108 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 105 is shared with 1 other county, Putnam County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 74 of the 81 codes. Together they come to about 429 square miles of land (1,112 sq km). The largest is 10536 (Katonah) with 26.6 square miles, and the smallest is 10503 (Ardsley On Hudson) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 3.5 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 21.6 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Westchester County; a code can reach across its border. 7 codes have no mapped area: 10521 (Croton On Hudson), 10551 (Mount Vernon), 10587 (Shenorock), 10602 (White Plains), 10610 (White Plains) and 2 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Westchester County ranks 5th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 81 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Westchester County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • White Plains, NY 10601 (one of 7 codes for White Plains; the street decides which)
  • Yonkers, NY 10701 (one of 6 codes for Yonkers; the street decides which)
  • Mount Vernon, NY 10550 (one of 4 codes for Mount Vernon; the street decides which)

White Plains is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Westchester County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for White Plains, NY?

White Plains has 7 ZIP codes in Westchester County: 10601, 10602, 10603, 10605, 10606, 10607, 10610. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Westchester County start with the same digits?

No. They use 4 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 105, on 58 of the 81 codes.

Which ZIP code in Westchester County covers the most land?
