Where Schenectady County is

The ZIP codes of Schenectady County are found in the east of New York. Its codes spread over about 10 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 12141 (Quaker Street) and 12309 (Schenectady), about 17 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Albany County (15 miles), Saratoga County (19 miles), Montgomery County (22 miles) and Rensselaer County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Schenectady County begin with 123 (11 codes), 120 (3 codes) and 121 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 123, so those three digits alone point to Schenectady County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 232 square miles of land (601 sq km). The largest is 12053 (Delanson) with 56.4 square miles, and the smallest is 12008 (Alplaus) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 15.6 square miles; the middle value for all of New York is 12.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Schenectady County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 12141 (Quaker Street), 12301 (Schenectady), 12325 (Schenectady) and 12345 (Schenectady). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Schenectady County ranks 46th among the 62 counties and county-level areas of New York (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 2,154 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Schenectady County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NY and the ZIP code:

  • Schenectady, NY 12301 (one of 11 codes for Schenectady; the street decides which)
  • Alplaus, NY 12008
  • Delanson, NY 12053
