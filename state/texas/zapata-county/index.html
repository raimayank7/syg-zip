Where Zapata County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Zapata County fall in the south of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 78067 (San Ygnacio) and 78564 (Lopeno), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Jim Hogg County (33 miles), Starr County (41 miles), Webb County (43 miles) and Brooks County (69 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Zapata County begin with 780 (2 codes) and 785 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 780 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Atascosa County, Bandera County and Bexar County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 753 square miles of land (1,950 sq km). The largest is 78076 (Zapata) with 552 square miles, and the smallest is 78564 (Lopeno) with 19.8. The middle-sized code here covers 181 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 44.6 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Zapata County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Zapata County ranks 183rd among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 3 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.
