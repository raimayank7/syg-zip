Where Willacy County is

The ZIP codes of Willacy County are found in the south-east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 58 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 78574 (Mission) and 78598 (Port Mansfield), about 61 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Hidalgo County (25 miles), Cameron County (27 miles), Kenedy County (39 miles) and Brooks County (49 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Willacy County starts with 785. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 785 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Cameron County, Hidalgo County and Starr County, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 473 square miles of land (1,225 sq km). The largest is 78580 (Raymondville) with 257 square miles, and the smallest is 78590 (San Perlita) with 1.0. The middle-sized code here covers 6.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 74.0 square miles, roughly 14% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Willacy County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Willacy County ranks 88th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.
