Where DeWitt County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, DeWitt County is in the south-east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 28 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 77967 (Hochheim) and 78141 (Nordheim), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Goliad County (25 miles), Victoria County (30 miles), Karnes County (31 miles) and Gonzales County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of DeWitt County begin with 779 (5 codes) and 781 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 779 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Calhoun County, Goliad County and Jackson County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 795 square miles of land (2,060 sq km). The largest is 77954 (Cuero) with 379 square miles, and the smallest is 77974 (Meyersville) with 28.5. The middle-sized code here covers 77.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of DeWitt County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 77967 (Hochheim) and 77989 (Thomaston). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, DeWitt County ranks 88th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.
