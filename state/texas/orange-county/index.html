Where Orange County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Orange County fall in the east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 13 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 77631 (Orange) and 77670 (Vidor), about 17 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Jefferson County (15 miles), Jasper County (32 miles), Calcasieu Parish, Louisiana (37 miles) and Hardin County (37 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Orange County starts with 776. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 776 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Chambers County, Galveston County and Hardin County, and has 36 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 4 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 385 square miles of land (998 sq km). The largest is 77662 (Vidor) with 140 square miles, and the smallest is 77611 (Bridge City) with 19.4. The middle-sized code here covers 113 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 31.1 square miles, roughly 7% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Orange County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 77626 (Mauriceville), 77631 (Orange), 77639 (Orangefield) and 77670 (Vidor). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Orange County ranks 77th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Orange County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Orange, TX 77630 (one of 3 codes for Orange; the street decides which)
  • Vidor, TX 77662 (one of 2 codes for Vidor; the street decides which)
  • Bridge City, TX 77611

Orange is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
