Where Bowie County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Bowie County fall in the north-east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 13 miles, and from east to west about 33 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 75504 (Texarkana) and 75559 (De Kalb), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Miller County, Arkansas (21 miles), Little River County, Arkansas (21 miles), Cass County (27 miles) and Sevier County, Arkansas (38 miles).

The first three digits

All 13 codes here begin with the same three digits, 755. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 755 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Cass County, Marion County and Morris County, and has 28 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 832 square miles of land (2,156 sq km). The largest is 75559 (De Kalb) with 292 square miles, and the smallest is 75569 (Nash) with 2.3. The middle-sized code here covers 78.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Water adds about 37.1 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bowie County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 75504 (Texarkana), 75505 (Texarkana), 75507 (Texarkana) and 75599 (Texarkana). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Bowie County ranks 32nd among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bowie County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Texarkana, TX 75501 (one of 6 codes for Texarkana; the street decides which)
  • De Kalb, TX 75559
  • Hooks, TX 75561
