Where Hardin County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Hardin County fall in the east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 26 miles, and from east to west about 29 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 77376 (Votaw) and 77656 (Silsbee), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Liberty County (22 miles), Tyler County (29 miles), Jefferson County (30 miles) and Jasper County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Hardin County begin with 776 (5 codes), 773 (2 codes) and 775 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 776 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Chambers County, Galveston County and Jasper County, and has 36 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 885 square miles of land (2,292 sq km). The largest is 77625 (Kountze) with 318 square miles, and the smallest is 77663 (Village Mills) with 17.9. The middle-sized code here covers 57.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hardin County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Hardin County ranks 61st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hardin County

Questions about Hardin County ZIP codes

Do all ZIP codes in Hardin County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Hardin County covers the most land?
