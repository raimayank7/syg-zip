Where McLennan County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, McLennan County is in the east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 36 miles, and from east to west about 34 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 76557 (Moody) and 76654 (Leroy), about 40 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Falls County (24 miles), Hill County (30 miles), Limestone County (34 miles) and Bosque County (36 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of McLennan County begin with 767 (17 codes), 766 (13 codes) and 765 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 767, so those three digits alone point to McLennan County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 24 of the 32 codes. Together they come to about 1,313 square miles of land (3,401 sq km). The largest is 76657 (Mc Gregor) with 139 square miles, and the smallest is 76798 (Waco) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 54.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of McLennan County; a code can reach across its border. 8 codes have no mapped area: 76684 (Ross), 76702 (Waco), 76703 (Waco), 76714 (Waco), 76715 (Waco) and 3 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, McLennan County ranks 8th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 32 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in McLennan County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Waco, TX 76701 (one of 16 codes for Waco; the street decides which)
  • Axtell, TX 76624
  • Bruceville, TX 76630

Waco is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
