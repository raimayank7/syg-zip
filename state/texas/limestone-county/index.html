Where Limestone County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Limestone County is in the east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 30 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 76653 (Kosse) and 76686 (Tehuacana), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Freestone County (20 miles), Falls County (33 miles), Navarro County (33 miles) and McLennan County (34 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 766. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 766 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Bosque County, Ellis County and Falls County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 831 square miles of land (2,153 sq km). The largest is 76642 (Groesbeck) with 248 square miles, and the smallest is 76686 (Tehuacana) with 1.8. The middle-sized code here covers 132 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Limestone County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Limestone County ranks 88th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Limestone County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Coolidge, TX 76635
  • Groesbeck, TX 76642
  • Kosse, TX 76653

Coolidge is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
