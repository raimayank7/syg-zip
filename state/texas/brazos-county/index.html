Where Brazos County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Brazos County is in the east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 26 miles, and from east to west about 16 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 77808 (Bryan) and 77866 (Millican), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Burleson County (21 miles), Grimes County (23 miles), Robertson County (25 miles) and Washington County (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 16 codes here begin with the same three digits, 778. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 778 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Burleson County, Grimes County and Lee County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 536 square miles of land (1,388 sq km). The largest is 77808 (Bryan) with 254 square miles, and the smallest is 77843 (College Station) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 11.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Brazos County; a code can reach across its border. 7 codes have no mapped area: 77805 (Bryan), 77806 (Bryan), 77841 (College Station), 77842 (College Station), 77844 (College Station) and 2 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Brazos County ranks 25th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.
