Where Goliad County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Goliad County fall in the south-east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 22 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 77960 (Fannin) and 78107 (Berclair), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are DeWitt County (25 miles), Victoria County (28 miles), Bee County (29 miles) and Karnes County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Goliad County begin with 779 (3 codes) and 781 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 779 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Calhoun County, DeWitt County and Jackson County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 780 square miles of land (2,021 sq km). The largest is 77963 (Goliad) with 579 square miles, and the smallest is 77993 (Weesatche) with 18.5. The middle-sized code here covers 91.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Goliad County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Goliad County ranks 151st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.
