Where Austin County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Austin County is in the south-east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 27 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 77485 (Wallis) and 78931 (Bleiblerville), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Waller County (19 miles), Washington County (23 miles), Colorado County (25 miles) and Fayette County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Austin County begin with 774 (5 codes) and 789 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 774 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Brazoria County, Colorado County and Fort Bend County, and has 83 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 745 square miles of land (1,929 sq km). The largest is 77474 (Sealy) with 207 square miles, and the smallest is 77473 (San Felipe) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 96.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Austin County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 77452 (Kenney). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Austin County ranks 61st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Austin County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:
