Where Jasper County is

The ZIP codes of Jasper County are found in the east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 38 miles from north to south and 10 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 75951 (Jasper) and 77615 (Evadale), about 39 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Newton County (18 miles), Tyler County (26 miles), Hardin County (32 miles) and Orange County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Jasper County begin with 759 (2 codes) and 776 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 759 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Angelina County, Cherokee County and Nacogdoches County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 969 square miles of land (2,508 sq km). The largest is 75951 (Jasper) with 373 square miles, and the smallest is 77615 (Evadale) with 1.2. The middle-sized code here covers 297 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jasper County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Jasper County ranks 151st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jasper County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:
