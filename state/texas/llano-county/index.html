Where Llano County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Llano County sits near the middle of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 22 miles, and from east to west about 35 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 76831 (Castell) and 78657 (Horseshoe Bay), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Burnet County (26 miles), San Saba County (30 miles), Mason County (32 miles) and Lampasas County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Llano County begin with 786 (6 codes) and 768 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 786 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Bastrop County, Blanco County and Burnet County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 939 square miles of land (2,432 sq km). The largest is 78643 (Llano) with 712 square miles, and the smallest is 76831 (Castell) with 8.2. The middle-sized code here covers 34.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Water adds about 33.1 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Llano County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Llano County ranks 77th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Llano County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Bluffton, TX 78607
  • Buchanan Dam, TX 78609
  • Castell, TX 76831

Bluffton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
