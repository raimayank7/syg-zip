Where Bee County is

The ZIP codes of Bee County are found in the south-east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 33 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 78145 (Pawnee) and 78391 (Tynan), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Live Oak County (19 miles), Goliad County (29 miles), Karnes County (34 miles) and McMullen County (39 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Bee County begin with 781 (7 codes) and 783 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 781 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Bexar County, Comal County and DeWitt County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 764 square miles of land (1,979 sq km). The largest is 78102 (Beeville) with 526 square miles, and the smallest is 78145 (Pawnee) with 3.2. The middle-sized code here covers 21.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bee County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Bee County ranks 61st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bee County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Beeville, TX 78102 (one of 2 codes for Beeville; the street decides which)
  • Mineral, TX 78125
  • Normanna, TX 78142

Questions about Bee County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Beeville, TX?

Beeville has 2 ZIP codes in Bee County: 78102, 78104. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Bee County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 781, on 7 of the 9 codes.

Which ZIP code in Bee County covers the most land?

78102 (Beeville), at about 526 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
