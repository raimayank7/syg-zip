Where Randall County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Randall County fall in the north-west of Texas. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 79091 (Umbarger) and 79110 (Amarillo), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Potter County (16 miles), Deaf Smith County (25 miles), Armstrong County (27 miles) and Oldham County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Randall County begin with 791 (6 codes) and 790 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 791 is shared with 1 other county, Potter County, and has 27 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 661 square miles of land (1,712 sq km). The largest is 79015 (Canyon) with 328 square miles, and the smallest is 79016 (Canyon) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 33.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Randall County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 79114 (Amarillo). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Randall County ranks 61st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Randall County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Amarillo, TX 79109 (one of 6 codes for Amarillo; the street decides which)
  • Canyon, TX 79015 (one of 2 codes for Canyon; the street decides which)
  • Umbarger, TX 79091

ZIP codes named Amarillo are also listed under Potter County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site.

Questions about Randall County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Amarillo, TX?

Amarillo has 6 ZIP codes in Randall County: 79109, 79110, 79114, 79118, 79119, 79121. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Randall County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 791, on 6 of the 9 codes.

Which ZIP code in Randall County covers the most land?

79015 (Canyon), at about 328 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
