Where Hopkins County is

The ZIP codes of Hopkins County are found in the north-east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 75433 (Cumby) and 75481 (Sulphur Bluff), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Delta County (20 miles), Franklin County (22 miles), Rains County (24 miles) and Wood County (26 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 754. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 754 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Camp County, Collin County and Delta County, and has 84 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 747 square miles of land (1,934 sq km). The largest is 75482 (Sulphur Springs) with 314 square miles, and the smallest is 75420 (Brashear) with 35.2. The middle-sized code here covers 62.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Water adds about 25.4 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hopkins County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 75483 (Sulphur Springs). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Hopkins County ranks 61st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hopkins County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Sulphur Springs, TX 75482 (one of 2 codes for Sulphur Springs; the street decides which)
  • Brashear, TX 75420
  • Como, TX 75431

Sulphur Springs is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
