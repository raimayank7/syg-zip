Where Gregg County is

The ZIP codes of Gregg County are found in the north-east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 14 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 75641 (Easton) and 75647 (Gladewater), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Upshur County (15 miles), Rusk County (23 miles), Harrison County (29 miles) and Smith County (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 15 codes here begin with the same three digits, 756. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 756 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Camp County, Cass County and Harrison County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 466 square miles of land (1,207 sq km). The largest is 75662 (Kilgore) with 127 square miles, and the smallest is 75641 (Easton) with 1.7. The middle-sized code here covers 51.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Gregg County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 75606 (Longview), 75607 (Longview), 75608 (Longview), 75615 (Longview), 75660 (Judson) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Gregg County ranks 28th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Gregg County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:
