Where Hidalgo County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Hidalgo County fall in the south-east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 37 miles, and from east to west about 40 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 78563 (Linn) and 78579 (Progreso), about 41 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Willacy County (25 miles), Cameron County (38 miles), Starr County (39 miles) and Brooks County (56 miles).

The first three digits

All 30 codes here begin with the same three digits, 785. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 785 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Cameron County, Starr County and Willacy County, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 28 of the 30 codes. Together they come to about 1,418 square miles of land (3,674 sq km). The largest is 78541 (Edinburg) with 290 square miles, and the smallest is 78558 (La Blanca) with 0.8. The middle-sized code here covers 25.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hidalgo County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 78502 (Mcallen) and 78505 (Mcallen). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Hidalgo County ranks 11th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 30 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hidalgo County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Mcallen, TX 78501 (one of 5 codes for Mcallen; the street decides which)
  • Edinburg, TX 78539 (one of 4 codes for Edinburg; the street decides which)
  • Mission, TX 78572 (one of 2 codes for Mission; the street decides which)

Mission shares a name with a place in another part of Texas; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
