Where Pecos County is

The ZIP codes of Pecos County are found in the west of Texas. Its codes spread over about 40 miles from north to south and 71 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 79730 (Coyanosa) and 79781 (Sheffield), about 80 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Crane County (27 miles), Upton County (33 miles), Ward County (49 miles) and Ector County (59 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Pecos County starts with 797. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 797 is shared with 15 other counties, such as Andrews County, Borden County and Crane County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 2,525 square miles of land (6,539 sq km). The largest is 79735 (Fort Stockton) with 1,238 square miles, and the smallest is 79743 (Imperial) with 93.2. The middle-sized code here covers 473 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pecos County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 79740 (Girvin). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Pecos County ranks 101st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pecos County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Fort Stockton, TX 79735
  • Girvin, TX 79740
