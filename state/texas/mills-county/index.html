Where Mills County is

The ZIP codes of Mills County are found in the east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 76864 (Mullin) and 76880 (Star), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Comanche County (27 miles), Hamilton County (28 miles), San Saba County (30 miles) and Brown County (31 miles).

The first three digits

All 4 codes here begin with the same three digits, 768. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 768 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Brown County, Coleman County and Concho County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 670 square miles of land (1,735 sq km). The largest is 76844 (Goldthwaite) with 375 square miles, and the smallest is 76880 (Star) with 5.3. The middle-sized code here covers 145 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Mills County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Mills County ranks 151st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Mills County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:
