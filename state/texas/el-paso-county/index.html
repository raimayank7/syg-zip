Where El Paso County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of El Paso County fall in the west of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 38 miles, and from east to west about 37 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 79821 (Anthony) and 79853 (Tornillo), about 49 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Doña Ana County, New Mexico (48 miles), Hudspeth County (57 miles), Otero County, New Mexico (82 miles) and Luna County, New Mexico (84 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of El Paso County begin with 885 (76 codes), 799 (63 codes) and 798 (6 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 885, so those three digits alone point to El Paso County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 31 of the 145 codes. Together they come to about 1,031 square miles of land (2,671 sq km). The largest is 79938 (El Paso) with 390 square miles, and the smallest is 79920 (El Paso) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 12.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of El Paso County; a code can reach across its border. 114 codes have no mapped area: 79910 (El Paso), 79913 (El Paso), 79914 (El Paso), 79917 (El Paso), 79923 (El Paso) and 109 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.
