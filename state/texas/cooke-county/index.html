Where Cooke County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Cooke County fall in the north-east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 76241 (Gainesville) and 76263 (Rosston), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Love County, Oklahoma (24 miles), Montague County (27 miles), Denton County (31 miles) and Wise County (32 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 762. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 762 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Clay County, Denton County and Grayson County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 691 square miles of land (1,789 sq km). The largest is 76240 (Gainesville) with 369 square miles, and the smallest is 76241 (Gainesville) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 16.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 23.1 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cooke County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Cooke County ranks 77th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cooke County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Gainesville, TX 76240 (one of 2 codes for Gainesville; the street decides which)
  • Era, TX 76238
  • Lindsay, TX 76250

Gainesville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
