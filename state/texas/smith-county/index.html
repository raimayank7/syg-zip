Where Smith County is

The ZIP codes of Smith County are found in the east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 75771 (Lindale) and 75789 (Troup), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Rusk County (29 miles), Gregg County (30 miles), Upshur County (32 miles) and Wood County (32 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Smith County starts with 757. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 757 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Anderson County, Cherokee County and Henderson County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 22 codes. Together they come to about 1,011 square miles of land (2,618 sq km). The largest is 75789 (Troup) with 184 square miles, and the smallest is 75711 (Tyler) with 0.02. The middle-sized code here covers 52.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Smith County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 75710 (Tyler), 75712 (Tyler), 75713 (Tyler), 75798 (Tyler) and 75799 (Tyler). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Smith County ranks 18th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 22 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Smith County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:
