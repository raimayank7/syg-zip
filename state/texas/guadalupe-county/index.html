Where Guadalupe County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Guadalupe County fall in the south-east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 28 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 78154 (Schertz) and 78670 (Staples), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Comal County (17 miles), Caldwell County (24 miles), Wilson County (27 miles) and Gonzales County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Guadalupe County begin with 781 (7 codes) and 786 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 781 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Bee County, Bexar County and Comal County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 601 square miles of land (1,556 sq km). The largest is 78155 (Seguin) with 355 square miles, and the smallest is 78123 (Mc Queeney) with 3.7. The middle-sized code here covers 32.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Guadalupe County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 78115 (Geronimo) and 78156 (Seguin). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Guadalupe County ranks 61st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Guadalupe County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Seguin, TX 78155 (one of 2 codes for Seguin; the street decides which)
  • Cibolo, TX 78108
  • Geronimo, TX 78115

Questions about Guadalupe County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Seguin, TX?

Seguin has 2 ZIP codes in Guadalupe County: 78155, 78156. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Guadalupe County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 781, on 7 of the 9 codes.

Which ZIP code in Guadalupe County covers the most land?

78155 (Seguin), at about 355 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
