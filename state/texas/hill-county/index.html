Where Hill County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Hill County is in the east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 28 miles, and from east to west about 33 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 76627 (Blum) and 76648 (Hubbard), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Ellis County (29 miles), McLennan County (30 miles), Navarro County (32 miles) and Bosque County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Hill County begin with 766 (14 codes) and 760 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 766 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Bosque County, Ellis County and Falls County, and has 58 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 968 square miles of land (2,507 sq km). The largest is 76645 (Hillsboro) with 187 square miles, and the smallest is 76628 (Brandon) with 5.7. The middle-sized code here covers 53.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hill County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Hill County ranks 28th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hill County
