Where Lamar County is

The ZIP codes of Lamar County are found in the north-east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 32 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 75411 (Arthur City) and 75434 (Cunningham), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Delta County (20 miles), Choctaw County, Oklahoma (26 miles), Red River County (29 miles) and Fannin County (35 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Lamar County starts with 754. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 754 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Camp County, Collin County and Delta County, and has 84 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 879 square miles of land (2,276 sq km). The largest is 75462 (Paris) with 193 square miles, and the smallest is 75434 (Cunningham) with 13.0. The middle-sized code here covers 64.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lamar County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 75425 (Chicota) and 75461 (Paris). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lamar County ranks 31st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lamar County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Paris, TX 75460 (one of 3 codes for Paris; the street decides which)
  • Arthur City, TX 75411
  • Blossom, TX 75416

Paris is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 11 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
