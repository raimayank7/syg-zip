Where Medina County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Medina County is in the south of Texas. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 28 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 78056 (Mico) and 78886 (Yancey), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Frio County (28 miles), Bexar County (31 miles), Bandera County (32 miles) and Atascosa County (39 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Medina County begin with 780 (6 codes) and 788 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 780 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Atascosa County, Bandera County and Bexar County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,220 square miles of land (3,161 sq km). The largest is 78861 (Hondo) with 491 square miles, and the smallest is 78039 (La Coste) with 20.7. The middle-sized code here covers 73.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Medina County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Medina County ranks 61st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Medina County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:
