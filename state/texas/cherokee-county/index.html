Where Cherokee County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Cherokee County fall in the east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 38 miles, and from east to west about 28 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 75759 (Cuney) and 75976 (Wells), about 47 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Rusk County (30 miles), Anderson County (33 miles), Nacogdoches County (34 miles) and Smith County (36 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Cherokee County begin with 757 (7 codes) and 759 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 757 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Anderson County, Henderson County and Nacogdoches County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 885 square miles of land (2,291 sq km). The largest is 75766 (Jacksonville) with 318 square miles, and the smallest is 75764 (Gallatin) with 1.3. The middle-sized code here covers 31.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cherokee County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 75772 (Maydelle). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Cherokee County ranks 61st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cherokee County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Alto, TX 75925
  • Cuney, TX 75759
  • Gallatin, TX 75764

Alto is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
