Where Val Verde County is

The ZIP codes of Val Verde County are found in the south of Texas. Its codes spread over about 32 miles from north to south and 40 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 78843 (Laughlin Afb) and 78871 (Langtry), about 50 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Kinney County (40 miles), Maverick County (65 miles), Edwards County (70 miles) and Real County (75 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Val Verde County starts with 788. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 788 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Bandera County, Dimmit County and Edwards County, and has 36 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 2,136 square miles of land (5,532 sq km). The largest is 78840 (Del Rio) with 1,046 square miles, and the smallest is 78843 (Laughlin Afb) with 1.8. The middle-sized code here covers 125 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 72.7 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Val Verde County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 78847 (Del Rio). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Val Verde County ranks 88th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Val Verde County
