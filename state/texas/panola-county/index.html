Where Panola County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Panola County is in the east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 75669 (Long Branch) and 75685 (Panola), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Shelby County (24 miles), Harrison County (26 miles), Rusk County (28 miles) and De Soto Parish, Louisiana (32 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Panola County starts with 756. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 756 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Camp County, Cass County and Gregg County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 734 square miles of land (1,902 sq km). The largest is 75633 (Carthage) with 325 square miles, and the smallest is 75669 (Long Branch) with 55.0. The middle-sized code here covers 118 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Panola County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 75637 (Clayton) and 75685 (Panola). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Panola County ranks 88th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.
