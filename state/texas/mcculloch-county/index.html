Where McCulloch County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of McCulloch County fall close to the middle of Texas. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 76836 (Doole) and 76887 (Voca), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Mason County (32 miles), Concho County (35 miles), Menard County (35 miles) and San Saba County (36 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of McCulloch County starts with 768. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 768 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Brown County, Coleman County and Concho County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,245 square miles of land (3,226 sq km). The largest is 76825 (Brady) with 479 square miles, and the smallest is 76836 (Doole) with 72.8. The middle-sized code here covers 149 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of McCulloch County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, McCulloch County ranks 101st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in McCulloch County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:
