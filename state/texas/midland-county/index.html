Where Midland County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Midland County fall in the west of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 11 miles, and from east to west about 7.9 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 79704 (Midland) and 79707 (Midland), about 12 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Ector County (22 miles), Martin County (25 miles), Glasscock County (32 miles) and Andrews County (38 miles).

The first three digits

All 11 codes here begin with the same three digits, 797. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 797 is shared with 15 other counties, such as Andrews County, Borden County and Crane County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 858 square miles of land (2,223 sq km). The largest is 79706 (Midland) with 690 square miles, and the smallest is 79703 (Midland) with 6.5. The middle-sized code here covers 71.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Midland County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 79702, 79704, 79708, 79710, 79711 and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Midland County ranks 46th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Midland County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Midland, TX 79701 (one of 11 codes for Midland; the street decides which)

Midland is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 11 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
