Where Dallas County is

The ZIP codes of Dallas County are found in the north-east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 29 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 75054 (Grand Prairie) and 75098 (Wylie), about 41 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Rockwall County (24 miles), Collin County (26 miles), Tarrant County (28 miles) and Denton County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Dallas County begin with 752 (66 codes), 750 (41 codes), 753 (39 codes) and 751 (19 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 752, so those three digits alone point to Dallas County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 90 of the 165 codes. Together they come to about 957 square miles of land (2,478 sq km). The largest is 75159 (Seagoville) with 59.4 square miles, and the smallest is 75270 (Dallas) with under 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 8.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 50.6 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Dallas County; a code can reach across its border. 75 codes have no mapped area: 75011 (Carrollton), 75014 (Irving), 75015 (Irving), 75016 (Irving), 75017 (Irving) and 70 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Dallas County ranks 2nd among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 165 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Dallas County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Dallas, TX 75201 (one of 105 codes for Dallas; the street decides which)
  • Irving, TX 75014 (one of 12 codes for Irving; the street decides which)
  • Garland, TX 75040 (one of 9 codes for Garland; the street decides which)

Dallas is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Carrollton shares a name with a place in another part of Texas; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
