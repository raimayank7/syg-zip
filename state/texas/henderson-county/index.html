Where Henderson County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Henderson County fall in the east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 40 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 75143 (Kemp) and 75758 (Chandler), about 40 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Van Zandt County (27 miles), Anderson County (28 miles), Smith County (33 miles) and Kaufman County (37 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Henderson County begin with 757 (7 codes) and 751 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 757 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Anderson County, Cherokee County and Nacogdoches County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,015 square miles of land (2,630 sq km). The largest is 75143 (Kemp) with 182 square miles, and the smallest is 75782 (Poynor) with 1.6. The middle-sized code here covers 88.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Water adds about 68.7 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Henderson County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Henderson County ranks 46th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Henderson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Athens, TX 75751 (one of 2 codes for Athens; the street decides which)
  • Brownsboro, TX 75756
  • Chandler, TX 75758

Athens is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 13 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Henderson County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Athens, TX?

Athens has 2 ZIP codes in Henderson County: 75751, 75752. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Henderson County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 757, on 7 of the 11 codes.

Which ZIP code in Henderson County covers the most land?

75143 (Kemp), at about 182 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
