Where Comanche County is

The ZIP codes of Comanche County are found in the east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 23 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 76452 (Energy) and 76474 (Sidney), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hamilton County (23 miles), Mills County (27 miles), Erath County (28 miles) and Brown County (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 6 codes here begin with the same three digits, 764. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 764 is shared with 14 other counties, such as Brown County, Callahan County and Eastland County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 704 square miles of land (1,824 sq km). The largest is 76442 (Comanche) with 434 square miles, and the smallest is 76452 (Energy) with 23.7. The middle-sized code here covers 68.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Comanche County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 76468 (Proctor). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Comanche County ranks 101st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Comanche County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • De Leon, TX 76444
  • Energy, TX 76452
