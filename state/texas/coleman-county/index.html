Where Coleman County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Coleman County sits near the middle of Texas. Its codes spread over about 38 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 76873 (Rockwood) and 79519 (Goldsboro), about 42 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Brown County (31 miles), Runnels County (33 miles), Concho County (35 miles) and McCulloch County (39 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Coleman County begin with 768 (8 codes) and 795 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 768 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Brown County, Concho County and Edwards County, and has 53 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,339 square miles of land (3,468 sq km). The largest is 76834 (Coleman) with 440 square miles, and the smallest is 76884 (Valera) with 15.5. The middle-sized code here covers 91.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Coleman County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Coleman County ranks 54th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Coleman County
