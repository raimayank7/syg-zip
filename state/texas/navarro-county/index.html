Where Navarro County is

The ZIP codes of Navarro County are found in the east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 32 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 75105 (Chatfield) and 76639 (Dawson), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Ellis County (25 miles), Freestone County (27 miles), Hill County (32 miles) and Limestone County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Navarro County begin with 751 (7 codes) and 766 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 751 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Collin County, Dallas County and Ellis County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 865 square miles of land (2,240 sq km). The largest is 75144 (Kerens) with 174 square miles, and the smallest is 75153 (Powell) with 24.5. The middle-sized code here covers 69.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Water adds about 56.4 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Navarro County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 75151 (Corsicana). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Navarro County ranks 41st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Navarro County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Corsicana, TX 75109 (one of 3 codes for Corsicana; the street decides which)
  • Barry, TX 75102
  • Blooming Grove, TX 76626

Questions about Navarro County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Corsicana, TX?

Corsicana has 3 ZIP codes in Navarro County: 75109, 75110, 75151. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Navarro County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 751, on 7 of the 12 codes.

Which ZIP code in Navarro County covers the most land?

75144 (Kerens), at about 174 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
