Where Van Zandt County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Van Zandt County is in the north-east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 75169 (Wills Point) and 75754 (Ben Wheeler), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Rains County (17 miles), Wood County (25 miles), Henderson County (27 miles) and Kaufman County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Van Zandt County begin with 751 (5 codes) and 757 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 751 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Collin County, Dallas County and Ellis County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 766 square miles of land (1,983 sq km). The largest is 75169 (Wills Point) with 215 square miles, and the smallest is 75127 (Fruitvale) with 26.7. The middle-sized code here covers 116 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Van Zandt County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Van Zandt County ranks 88th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.
