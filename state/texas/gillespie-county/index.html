Where Gillespie County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Gillespie County sits near the middle of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 37 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 78631 (Harper) and 78671 (Stonewall), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Blanco County (29 miles), Kerr County (29 miles), Kendall County (32 miles) and Llano County (34 miles).

The first three digits

All 5 codes here begin with the same three digits, 786. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 786 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Bastrop County, Blanco County and Burnet County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,292 square miles of land (3,346 sq km). The largest is 78624 (Fredericksburg) with 742 square miles, and the smallest is 78675 (Willow City) with 43.4. The middle-sized code here covers 159 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Gillespie County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Gillespie County ranks 127th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.
