Where Ector County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Ector County is in the west of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 20 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 79758 (Gardendale) and 79759 (Notrees), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Midland County (22 miles), Andrews County (32 miles), Crane County (33 miles) and Winkler County (40 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Ector County starts with 797. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 797 is shared with 15 other counties, such as Andrews County, Borden County and Crane County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 883 square miles of land (2,286 sq km). The largest is 79766 (Odessa) with 305 square miles, and the smallest is 79776 (Penwell) with 3.1. The middle-sized code here covers 50.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ector County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 79760 (Odessa) and 79768 (Odessa). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Ector County ranks 32nd among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Ector County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Odessa, TX 79760 (one of 9 codes for Odessa; the street decides which)
  • Gardendale, TX 79758
  • Goldsmith, TX 79741

Odessa is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
