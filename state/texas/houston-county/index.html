Where Houston County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Houston County is in the east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 30 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 75851 (Lovelady) and 75858 (Ratcliff), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Trinity County (20 miles), Cherokee County (36 miles), Anderson County (40 miles) and Leon County (40 miles).

The first three digits

All 6 codes here begin with the same three digits, 758. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 758 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Anderson County, Freestone County and Leon County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,333 square miles of land (3,452 sq km). The largest is 75835 (Crockett) with 469 square miles, and the smallest is 75849 (Latexo) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 255 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Houston County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Houston County ranks 101st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Houston County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Crockett, TX 75835
  • Grapeland, TX 75844
  • Kennard, TX 75847

Crockett is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
