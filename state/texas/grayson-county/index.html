Where Grayson County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Grayson County fall in the north-east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 28 miles, and from east to west about 28 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 75495 (Van Alstyne) and 76245 (Gordonville), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Marshall County, Oklahoma (27 miles), Bryan County, Oklahoma (29 miles), Fannin County (31 miles) and Collin County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Grayson County begin with 750 (7 codes), 762 (6 codes) and 754 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 750 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Collin County, Dallas County and Denton County, and has 84 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 18 codes. Together they come to about 1,088 square miles of land (2,817 sq km). The largest is 76273 (Whitesboro) with 207 square miles, and the smallest is 76268 (Southmayd) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 58.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Water adds about 48.5 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Grayson County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 75091 (Sherman). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Grayson County ranks 23rd among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Grayson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Sherman, TX 75090 (one of 3 codes for Sherman; the street decides which)
  • Denison, TX 75020 (one of 2 codes for Denison; the street decides which)
  • Bells, TX 75414

Sherman is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Grayson County ZIP codes

Do all ZIP codes in Grayson County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Grayson County covers the most land?
