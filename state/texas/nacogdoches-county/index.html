Where Nacogdoches County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Nacogdoches County is in the east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 32 miles from north to south and 30 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 75788 (Sacul) and 75944 (Etoile), about 44 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Angelina County (24 miles), Shelby County (30 miles), San Augustine County (31 miles) and Cherokee County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Nacogdoches County begin with 759 (11 codes) and 757 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 759 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Angelina County, Cherokee County and Jasper County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 1,032 square miles of land (2,674 sq km). The largest is 75961 (Nacogdoches) with 280 square miles, and the smallest is 75962 (Nacogdoches) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 72.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Water adds about 34.9 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Nacogdoches County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 75958 (Martinsville), 75963 (Nacogdoches) and 75978 (Woden). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Nacogdoches County ranks 32nd among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Nacogdoches County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:
