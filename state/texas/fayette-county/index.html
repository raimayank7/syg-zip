Where Fayette County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Fayette County fall in the east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 38 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 78941 (Flatonia) and 78946 (Ledbetter), about 42 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lee County (26 miles), Bastrop County (29 miles), Colorado County (30 miles) and Austin County (34 miles).

The first three digits

All 13 codes here begin with the same three digits, 789. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 789 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Austin County, Bastrop County and Colorado County, and has 28 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 1,077 square miles of land (2,790 sq km). The largest is 78945 (La Grange) with 248 square miles, and the smallest is 78938 (Ellinger) with 7.8. The middle-sized code here covers 93.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Fayette County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 78952 (Plum), 78960 (Warda) and 78961 (Round Top). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Fayette County ranks 32nd among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Fayette County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:
