Where Titus County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Titus County is in the north-east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 6.0 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 75493 (Winfield) and 75558 (Cookville), about 15 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Franklin County (10 miles), Camp County (15 miles), Morris County (16 miles) and Red River County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Titus County begin with 754 (3 codes) and 755 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 754 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Camp County, Collin County and Delta County, and has 84 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 3 of the 4 codes. Together they come to about 351 square miles of land (910 sq km). The largest is 75455 (Mount Pleasant) with 307 square miles, and the smallest is 75493 (Winfield) with 4.0. The middle-sized code here covers 40.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Water adds about 12.6 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Titus County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 75456 (Mount Pleasant). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Titus County ranks 151st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Titus County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Mount Pleasant, TX 75455 (one of 2 codes for Mount Pleasant; the street decides which)
  • Cookville, TX 75558
  • Winfield, TX 75493

Mount Pleasant is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 10 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Titus County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Mount Pleasant, TX?

Mount Pleasant has 2 ZIP codes in Titus County: 75455, 75456. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Titus County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 754, on 3 of the 4 codes.

Which ZIP code in Titus County covers the most land?

75455 (Mount Pleasant), at about 307 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
