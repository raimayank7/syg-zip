Where Travis County is

The ZIP codes of Travis County are found in the east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 31 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 78653 (Manor) and 78669 (Spicewood), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hays County (22 miles), Williamson County (22 miles), Bastrop County (30 miles) and Caldwell County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Travis County begin with 787 (70 codes), 786 (8 codes) and 733 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 787 is shared with 1 other county, Williamson County, and has 72 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 48 of the 80 codes. Together they come to about 897 square miles of land (2,322 sq km). The largest is 78669 (Spicewood) with 122 square miles, and the smallest is 78712 (Austin) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 11.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Travis County; a code can reach across its border. 32 codes have no mapped area: 73301 (Austin), 73344 (Austin), 78651 (Mc Neil), 78691 (Pflugerville), 78708 (Austin) and 27 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Travis County ranks 6th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 80 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Travis County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Austin, TX 73301 (one of 72 codes for Austin; the street decides which)
  • Pflugerville, TX 78660 (one of 2 codes for Pflugerville; the street decides which)
  • Del Valle, TX 78617

ZIP codes named Austin are also listed under Williamson County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Austin is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Travis County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Austin, TX?

Austin has 72 ZIP codes in Travis County: 73301, 73344, 78701, 78702, 78703, 78704, 78705, 78708, 78709, 78710 and 62 more in the table above. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Travis County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 787, on 70 of the 80 codes.

Which ZIP code in Travis County covers the most land?

78669 (Spicewood), at about 122 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
