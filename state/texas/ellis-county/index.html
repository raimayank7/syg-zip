Where Ellis County is

The ZIP codes of Ellis County are found in the east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 26 miles from north to south and 33 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 75155 (Rice) and 76065 (Midlothian), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Navarro County (25 miles), Hill County (29 miles), Johnson County (31 miles) and Dallas County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Ellis County begin with 751 (10 codes), 760 (3 codes) and 766 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 751 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Collin County, Dallas County and Henderson County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 983 square miles of land (2,547 sq km). The largest is 75119 (Ennis) with 264 square miles, and the smallest is 75101 (Bardwell) with 0.8. The middle-sized code here covers 57.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ellis County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 75120 (Ennis) and 75168 (Waxahachie). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Ellis County ranks 25th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Ellis County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Ennis, TX 75119 (one of 2 codes for Ennis; the street decides which)

Questions about Ellis County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Waxahachie, TX?

Waxahachie has 3 ZIP codes in Ellis County: 75165, 75167, 75168. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Ellis County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 751, on 10 of the 16 codes.

Which ZIP code in Ellis County covers the most land?

75119 (Ennis), at about 264 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
