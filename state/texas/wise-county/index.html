Where Wise County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Wise County is in the north-east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 28 miles, and from east to west about 24 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 76071 (Newark) and 76431 (Chico), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Parker County (29 miles), Denton County (30 miles), Montague County (32 miles) and Cooke County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Wise County begin with 760 (4 codes), 762 (4 codes) and 764 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 760 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Ellis County, Hill County and Hood County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 883 square miles of land (2,288 sq km). The largest is 76234 (Decatur) with 314 square miles, and the smallest is 76267 (Slidell) with 1.7. The middle-sized code here covers 84.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wise County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 76246 (Greenwood). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Wise County ranks 54th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.
