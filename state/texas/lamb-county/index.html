Where Lamb County is

The ZIP codes of Lamb County are found in the north-west of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 22 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 79031 (Earth) and 79369 (Spade), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hale County (28 miles), Bailey County (29 miles), Hockley County (31 miles) and Castro County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lamb County begin with 793 (5 codes) and 790 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 793 is shared with 14 other counties, such as Bailey County, Cochran County and Crosby County, and has 48 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,150 square miles of land (2,979 sq km). The largest is 79371 (Sudan) with 338 square miles, and the smallest is 79369 (Spade) with 6.3. The middle-sized code here covers 105 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lamb County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lamb County ranks 77th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lamb County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Amherst, TX 79312
  • Earth, TX 79031
  • Fieldton, TX 79326

Amherst is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
