Where Coryell County is

The ZIP codes of Coryell County are found in the east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 25 miles, and from east to west about 30 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 76525 (Evant) and 76561 (Oglesby), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Bell County (30 miles), Lampasas County (34 miles), Hamilton County (35 miles) and Bosque County (36 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 765. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 765 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Bell County, Falls County and Hamilton County, and has 55 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 1,047 square miles of land (2,711 sq km). The largest is 76528 (Gatesville) with 486 square miles, and the smallest is 76598 (Gatesville) with 0.03. The middle-sized code here covers 66.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Coryell County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 76526 (Flat) and 76558 (Mound). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Coryell County ranks 41st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.
