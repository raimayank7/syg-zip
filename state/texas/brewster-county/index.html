Where Brewster County is

The ZIP codes of Brewster County are found in the west of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 74 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 79832 (Alpine) and 79834 (Big Bend National Park), about 77 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Presidio County (40 miles), Jeff Davis County (62 miles), Terrell County (76 miles) and Reeves County (91 miles).

The first three digits

All 6 codes here begin with the same three digits, 798. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 798 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Culberson County, El Paso County and Hudspeth County, and has 22 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 3,061 square miles of land (7,928 sq km). The largest is 79830 (Alpine) with 1,165 square miles, and the smallest is 79832 (Alpine) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 613 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Brewster County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 79831 (Alpine). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Brewster County ranks 101st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Brewster County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Alpine, TX 79830 (one of 3 codes for Alpine; the street decides which)
  • Big Bend National Park, TX 79834
  • Marathon, TX 79842

Alpine is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
