Where Newton County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Newton County is in the east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 52 miles from north to south and 10 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 75977 (Wiergate) and 77614 (Deweyville), about 52 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Jasper County (18 miles), Beauregard Parish, Louisiana (31 miles), Tyler County (36 miles) and Sabine County (39 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Newton County begin with 759 (5 codes) and 776 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 759 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Angelina County, Cherokee County and Jasper County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 750 square miles of land (1,942 sq km). The largest is 75966 (Newton) with 268 square miles, and the smallest is 77614 (Deweyville) with 10.1. The middle-sized code here covers 110 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Newton County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Newton County ranks 101st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.
