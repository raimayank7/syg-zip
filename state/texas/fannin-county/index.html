Where Fannin County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Fannin County fall in the north-east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 75452 (Leonard) and 75488 (Telephone), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Delta County (28 miles), Hunt County (28 miles), Bryan County, Oklahoma (28 miles) and Grayson County (31 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Fannin County starts with 754. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 754 is shared with 12 other counties, such as Camp County, Collin County and Delta County, and has 84 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 866 square miles of land (2,242 sq km). The largest is 75446 (Honey Grove) with 178 square miles, and the smallest is 75413 (Bailey) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 48.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Fannin County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 75443 (Gober). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Fannin County ranks 28th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Fannin County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Bailey, TX 75413
  • Bonham, TX 75418
  • Dodd City, TX 75438

Bailey is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
