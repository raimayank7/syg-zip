Where Karnes County is

The ZIP codes of Karnes County are found in the south-east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 78113 (Falls City) and 78151 (Runge), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Wilson County (22 miles), DeWitt County (31 miles), Goliad County (32 miles) and Bee County (34 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 781. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 781 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Bee County, Bexar County and Comal County, and has 46 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,116 square miles of land (2,891 sq km). The largest is 78119 (Kenedy) with 329 square miles, and the smallest is 78144 (Panna Maria) with 2.2. The middle-sized code here covers 111 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Karnes County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Karnes County ranks 77th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Karnes County
