Where Rusk County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Rusk County fall in the east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 30 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 75666 (Laird Hill) and 75681 (Mount Enterprise), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Gregg County (23 miles), Panola County (28 miles), Smith County (29 miles) and Cherokee County (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 13 codes here begin with the same three digits, 756. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 756 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Camp County, Cass County and Gregg County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 781 square miles of land (2,023 sq km). The largest is 75654 (Henderson) with 198 square miles, and the smallest is 75680 (Minden) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 85.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Rusk County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 75653 (Henderson) and 75666 (Laird Hill). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Rusk County ranks 32nd among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Rusk County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Henderson, TX 75652 (one of 3 codes for Henderson; the street decides which)
  • Joinerville, TX 75658
  • Laird Hill, TX 75666

Henderson is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 14 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
