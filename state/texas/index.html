Texas ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 2,600 ZIP codes for Texas, about 6.3% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 1st among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 254 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Harris County (232), Dallas County (165), El Paso County (145), Tarrant County (97) and Bexar County (96). At the other end, 42 have only one or two codes, for example Andrews County, Armstrong County and Baylor County.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Texas uses 52 of these prefixes, from 733 to 885. The busiest are 770 (98 codes, most of them for Houston), 782 (85 codes, most of them for San Antonio), 750 (84 codes, most of them for Irving) and 754 (84 codes, most of them for Greenville). By first digit, the codes split into 2,524 starting with 7 and 76 starting with 8. Those first digits are shared with Arizona, Arkansas, Colorado, Idaho, Louisiana, Missouri, Nevada, New Mexico, Oklahoma, Utah and Wyoming.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 1,990 of the 2,600 codes. The largest is 76943 (Ozona, Crockett County) at 2,078 square miles and the smallest is 75270 (Dallas) at under 0.01; the middle value is 47.8 square miles. The other 610 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 73960 (Texhoma) and the southernmost 78522 (Brownsville); the one farthest east is 75932 (Burkeville) and farthest west 79821 (Anthony).

Place names and addresses in Texas

Of the 1,469 place names in the list, 1,312 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Houston (178), El Paso (137), Dallas (105) and San Antonio (83). 10 place names appear under more than one county, among them Amarillo, Austin, Azle and Baytown: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters TX and the code, as in Houston, TX 77001. The name of the county is left out.

Questions about Texas ZIP codes

What ZIP codes does Texas use?

In this list, Texas ZIP codes run from 73301 to 88595 and start with 7 or 8. A few codes inside that range can belong to a neighbouring state near the border.

Which place in Texas has the most ZIP codes?

Houston, with 178 codes in this list.
