Where Wharton County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Wharton County is in the south-east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 33 miles, and from east to west about 26 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 77432 (Danevang) and 77454 (Lissie), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Matagorda County (29 miles), Colorado County (31 miles), Fort Bend County (32 miles) and Jackson County (37 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 774. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 774 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Austin County, Brazoria County and Colorado County, and has 83 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,111 square miles of land (2,878 sq km). The largest is 77437 (El Campo) with 353 square miles, and the smallest is 77467 (Pierce) with 1.9. The middle-sized code here covers 18.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wharton County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Wharton County ranks 41st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.
