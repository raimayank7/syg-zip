Where Colorado County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Colorado County fall in the south-east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 30 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 77460 (Nada) and 78951 (Oakland), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Austin County (25 miles), Fayette County (30 miles), Lavaca County (31 miles) and Wharton County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Colorado County begin with 774 (6 codes) and 789 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 774 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Austin County, Brazoria County and Fort Bend County, and has 83 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 857 square miles of land (2,220 sq km). The largest is 78934 (Columbus) with 221 square miles, and the smallest is 77460 (Nada) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 29.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Colorado County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Colorado County ranks 46th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Colorado County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

Questions about Colorado County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Alleyton, TX?

78935 is the ZIP code listed for Alleyton in Colorado County.

Do all ZIP codes in Colorado County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 774, on 6 of the 11 codes.

Which ZIP code in Colorado County covers the most land?

78934 (Columbus), at about 221 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
