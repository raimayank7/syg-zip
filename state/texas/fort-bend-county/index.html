Where Fort Bend County is

The ZIP codes of Fort Bend County are found in the south-east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 33 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 77451 (Kendleton) and 77545 (Fresno), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Harris County (26 miles), Brazoria County (28 miles), Waller County (32 miles) and Wharton County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Fort Bend County begin with 774 (21 codes) and 775 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 774 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Austin County, Brazoria County and Colorado County, and has 83 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 19 of the 22 codes. Together they come to about 723 square miles of land (1,874 sq km). The largest is 77461 (Needville) with 151 square miles, and the smallest is 77451 (Kendleton) with 6.9. The middle-sized code here covers 20.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Fort Bend County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 77487 (Sugar Land), 77496 (Sugar Land) and 77497 (Stafford). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Fort Bend County ranks 18th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 22 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Fort Bend County

Questions about Fort Bend County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Sugar Land, TX?

Sugar Land has 5 ZIP codes in Fort Bend County: 77478, 77479, 77487, 77496, 77498. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Fort Bend County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Fort Bend County covers the most land?

77461 (Needville), at about 151 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
