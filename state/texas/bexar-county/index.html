Where Bexar County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Bexar County fall in the south-east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 39 miles, and from east to west about 48 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 78023 (Helotes) and 78150 (Jbsa Randolph), about 51 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Comal County (28 miles), Guadalupe County (30 miles), Medina County (31 miles) and Wilson County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Bexar County begin with 782 (84 codes), 780 (6 codes) and 781 (6 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 782 is shared with 1 other county, Comal County, and has 85 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 71 of the 96 codes. Together they come to about 1,254 square miles of land (3,247 sq km). The largest is 78023 (Helotes) with 89.8 square miles, and the smallest is 78150 (Jbsa Randolph) with 0.06. The middle-sized code here covers 10.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bexar County; a code can reach across its border. 25 codes have no mapped area: 78054 (Macdona), 78206 (San Antonio), 78241 (San Antonio), 78246 (San Antonio), 78265 (San Antonio) and 20 more.
