Where Parker County is

The ZIP codes of Parker County are found in the north-east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 27 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 76008 (Aledo) and 76490 (Whitt), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hood County (27 miles), Wise County (29 miles), Palo Pinto County (30 miles) and Tarrant County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Parker County begin with 760 (8 codes) and 764 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 760 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Ellis County, Hill County and Hood County, and has 71 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 791 square miles of land (2,049 sq km). The largest is 76087 (Weatherford) with 186 square miles, and the smallest is 76439 (Dennis) with 0.05. The middle-sized code here covers 84.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Parker County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 76098 (Azle) and 76485 (Peaster). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Parker County ranks 41st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Parker County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Weatherford, TX 76085 (one of 4 codes for Weatherford; the street decides which)
  • Aledo, TX 76008
  • Azle, TX 76098

Azle shares a name with a place in another part of Texas; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Parker County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Weatherford, TX?

Weatherford has 4 ZIP codes in Parker County: 76085, 76086, 76087, 76088. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Parker County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 760, on 8 of the 12 codes.

Which ZIP code in Parker County covers the most land?

76087 (Weatherford), at about 186 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
