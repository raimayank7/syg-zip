Where Anderson County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Anderson County is in the east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 28 miles, and from east to west about 28 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 75832 (Cayuga) and 75839 (Elkhart), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Henderson County (28 miles), Cherokee County (33 miles), Freestone County (35 miles) and Houston County (40 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Anderson County begin with 758 (11 codes) and 757 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 758 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Freestone County, Houston County and Leon County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 984 square miles of land (2,549 sq km). The largest is 75803 (Palestine) with 325 square miles, and the smallest is 75884 (Tennessee Colony) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 108 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Anderson County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 75802 (Palestine), 75832 (Cayuga), 75880 (Tennessee Colony) and 75882 (Palestine). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Anderson County ranks 32nd among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Anderson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Palestine, TX 75801 (one of 4 codes for Palestine; the street decides which)
  • Tennessee Colony, TX 75861 (one of 4 codes for Tennessee Colony; the street decides which)
  • Cayuga, TX 75832

Palestine is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
