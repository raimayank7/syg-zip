Where Hudspeth County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Hudspeth County fall in the west of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 50 miles, and from east to west about 37 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 79837 (Dell City) and 79839 (Fort Hancock), about 57 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Culberson County (49 miles), El Paso County (57 miles), Otero County, New Mexico (91 miles) and Eddy County, New Mexico (93 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Hudspeth County starts with 798. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 798 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Brewster County, Culberson County and El Paso County, and has 22 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,686 square miles of land (6,956 sq km). The largest is 79837 (Dell City) with 1,087 square miles, and the smallest is 79839 (Fort Hancock) with 327. The middle-sized code here covers 636 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hudspeth County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Hudspeth County ranks 151st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.
