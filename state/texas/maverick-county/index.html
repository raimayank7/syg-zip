Where Maverick County is

The ZIP codes of Maverick County are found in the south of Texas. Its codes spread over about 29 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 78860 (El Indio) and 78877 (Quemado), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Kinney County (42 miles), Zavala County (44 miles), Dimmit County (49 miles) and Val Verde County (65 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Maverick County starts with 788. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 788 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Bandera County, Dimmit County and Edwards County, and has 36 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 3 of the 4 codes. Together they come to about 769 square miles of land (1,992 sq km). The largest is 78852 (Eagle Pass) with 483 square miles, and the smallest is 78877 (Quemado) with 47.4. The middle-sized code here covers 239 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Maverick County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 78853 (Eagle Pass). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Maverick County ranks 151st among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Maverick County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Eagle Pass, TX 78852 (one of 2 codes for Eagle Pass; the street decides which)
