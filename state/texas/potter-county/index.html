Where Potter County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Potter County is in the north-west of Texas. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 79012 (Bushland) and 79111 (Amarillo), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Randall County (16 miles), Oldham County (29 miles), Armstrong County (33 miles) and Hutchinson County (36 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Potter County begin with 791 (21 codes) and 790 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 791 is shared with 1 other county, Randall County, and has 27 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 22 codes. Together they come to about 417 square miles of land (1,081 sq km). The largest is 79108 (Amarillo) with 183 square miles, and the smallest is 79101 (Amarillo) with 1.6. The middle-sized code here covers 6.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Potter County; a code can reach across its border. 12 codes have no mapped area: 79105 (Amarillo), 79116 (Amarillo), 79117 (Amarillo), 79120 (Amarillo), 79159 (Amarillo) and 7 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Potter County ranks 18th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 22 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Potter County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:
