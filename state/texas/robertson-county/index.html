Where Robertson County is

The ZIP codes of Robertson County are found in the east of Texas. Its codes spread over about 29 miles from north to south and 17 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 76629 (Bremond) and 77867 (Mumford), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Brazos County (25 miles), Milam County (30 miles), Falls County (32 miles) and Burleson County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Robertson County begin with 778 (6 codes) and 766 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 778 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Brazos County, Burleson County and Grimes County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 818 square miles of land (2,118 sq km). The largest is 77856 (Franklin) with 373 square miles, and the smallest is 77882 (Wheelock) with 2.1. The middle-sized code here covers 131 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Robertson County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 77870 (New Baden). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Robertson County ranks 88th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.
