Where Floyd County is

The ZIP codes of Floyd County are found in the north of Texas. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 79221 (Aiken) and 79231 (Dougherty), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Briscoe County (25 miles), Motley County (25 miles), Hale County (28 miles) and Crosby County (29 miles).

The first three digits

All 5 codes here begin with the same three digits, 792. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 792 is shared with 13 other counties, such as Briscoe County, Childress County and Collingsworth County, and has 32 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 4 of the 5 codes. Together they come to about 906 square miles of land (2,346 sq km). The largest is 79235 (Floydada) with 499 square miles, and the smallest is 79231 (Dougherty) with 10.7. The middle-sized code here covers 198 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Floyd County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 79221 (Aiken). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Floyd County ranks 127th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Floyd County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Dougherty, TX 79231
