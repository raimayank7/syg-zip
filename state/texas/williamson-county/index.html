Where Williamson County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Williamson County is in the east of Texas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 25 miles, and from east to west about 38 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 76578 (Thrall) and 78642 (Liberty Hill), about 39 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Travis County (22 miles), Burnet County (31 miles), Bell County (31 miles) and Milam County (40 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Williamson County begin with 786 (19 codes), 765 (6 codes) and 787 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 786 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Bastrop County, Blanco County and Burnet County, and has 75 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 20 of the 27 codes. Together they come to about 1,174 square miles of land (3,039 sq km). The largest is 76574 (Taylor) with 149 square miles, and the smallest is 78674 (Weir) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 55.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Texas is 47.8. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Williamson County; a code can reach across its border. 7 codes have no mapped area: 78627 (Georgetown), 78630 (Cedar Park), 78646 (Leander), 78673 (Walburg), 78680 (Round Rock) and 2 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Williamson County ranks 13th among the 254 counties and county-level areas of Texas (equal counts share a rank), with 27 of the 2,600 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Williamson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters TX and the ZIP code:

  • Round Rock, TX 78664 (one of 6 codes for Round Rock; the street decides which)
  • Georgetown, TX 78626 (one of 4 codes for Georgetown; the street decides which)
  • Austin, TX 78717 (one of 2 codes for Austin; the street decides which)

Austin and Leander share a name with a place in another part of Texas; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Williamson County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Round Rock, TX?

Round Rock has 6 ZIP codes in Williamson County: 78664, 78665, 78680, 78681, 78682, 78683. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Williamson County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 786, on 19 of the 27 codes.

Which ZIP code in Williamson County covers the most land?

76574 (Taylor), at about 149 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
