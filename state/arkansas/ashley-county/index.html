Where Ashley County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Ashley County is in the south of Arkansas. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 28 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 71635 (Crossett) and 71658 (Montrose), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Chicot County (23 miles), Morehouse Parish, Louisiana (29 miles), Drew County (31 miles) and West Carroll Parish, Louisiana (32 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 716. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 716 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Bradley County, Chicot County and Cleveland County, and has 40 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,050 square miles of land (2,720 sq km). The largest is 71646 (Hamburg) with 377 square miles, and the smallest is 71642 (Fountain Hill) with 69.2. The middle-sized code here covers 93.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ashley County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Ashley County ranks 42nd among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 706 codes in the state list.
