Where Monroe County is

The ZIP codes of Monroe County are found in the east of Arkansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 19 miles, and from east to west about 15 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 72021 (Brinkley) and 72134 (Roe), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Prairie County (19 miles), Lee County (26 miles), Arkansas County (26 miles) and Phillips County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Monroe County begin with 720 (3 codes) and 721 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 720 is shared with 18 other counties, such as Arkansas County, Cleburne County and Conway County, and has 81 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 604 square miles of land (1,565 sq km). The largest is 72069 (Holly Grove) with 273 square miles, and the smallest is 72108 (Monroe) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 93.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Monroe County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Monroe County ranks 61st among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Monroe County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:

  • Brinkley, AR 72021
  • Clarendon, AR 72029
  • Holly Grove, AR 72069

Questions about Monroe County ZIP codes

Do all ZIP codes in Monroe County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 720, on 3 of the 5 codes.

Which ZIP code in Monroe County covers the most land?
