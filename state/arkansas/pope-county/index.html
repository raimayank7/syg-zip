Where Pope County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Pope County fall in the north-west of Arkansas. Its codes spread over about 38 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 72823 (Atkins) and 72856 (Pelsor), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Johnson County (24 miles), Conway County (24 miles), Perry County (31 miles) and Yell County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Pope County begin with 728 (10 codes) and 726 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 728 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Franklin County, Johnson County and Logan County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 862 square miles of land (2,232 sq km). The largest is 72837 (Dover) with 244 square miles, and the smallest is 72801 (Russellville) with 8.0. The middle-sized code here covers 98.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pope County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 72811 (Russellville) and 72812 (Russellville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Pope County ranks 16th among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pope County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:

  • Russellville, AR 72801 (one of 4 codes for Russellville; the street decides which)
  • Atkins, AR 72823
  • Dover, AR 72837

Russellville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Pope County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Russellville, AR?

Russellville has 4 ZIP codes in Pope County: 72801, 72802, 72811, 72812. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Pope County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 728, on 10 of the 11 codes.

Which ZIP code in Pope County covers the most land?

72837 (Dover), at about 244 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
