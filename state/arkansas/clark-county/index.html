Where Clark County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Clark County is in the south-west of Arkansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 30 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 71772 (Whelen Springs) and 71921 (Amity), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hot Spring County (24 miles), Nevada County (25 miles), Pike County (29 miles) and Hempstead County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Clark County begin with 719 (6 codes) and 717 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 719 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Garland County, Hot Spring County and Howard County, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 960 square miles of land (2,486 sq km). The largest is 71923 (Arkadelphia) with 382 square miles, and the smallest is 71999 (Arkadelphia) with 0.07. The middle-sized code here covers 1.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Clark County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 71920 (Alpine). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Clark County ranks 22nd among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Clark County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:

  • Arkadelphia, AR 71923 (one of 3 codes for Arkadelphia; the street decides which)
  • Alpine, AR 71920
  • Amity, AR 71921

Questions about Clark County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Arkadelphia, AR?

Arkadelphia has 3 ZIP codes in Clark County: 71923, 71998, 71999. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Clark County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 719, on 6 of the 10 codes.

Which ZIP code in Clark County covers the most land?

71923 (Arkadelphia), at about 382 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
