Where St. Francis County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of St. Francis County fall in the east of Arkansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 37 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 72346 (Heth) and 72392 (Wheatley), about 39 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Lee County (14 miles), Cross County (22 miles), Woodruff County (28 miles) and Crittenden County (32 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of St. Francis County starts with 723. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 723 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Crittenden County, Cross County and Desha County, and has 65 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 765 square miles of land (1,982 sq km). The largest is 72348 (Hughes) with 170 square miles, and the smallest is 72322 (Caldwell) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 85.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. Water adds about 25.5 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of St. Francis County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 72336 (Forrest City). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, St. Francis County ranks 16th among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in St. Francis County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:

  • Forrest City, AR 72335 (one of 2 codes for Forrest City; the street decides which)
  • Caldwell, AR 72322
  • Colt, AR 72326

Questions about St. Francis County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Forrest City, AR?

Forrest City has 2 ZIP codes in St. Francis County: 72335, 72336. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in St. Francis County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 11 start with 723.

Which ZIP code in St. Francis County covers the most land?

72348 (Hughes), at about 170 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
