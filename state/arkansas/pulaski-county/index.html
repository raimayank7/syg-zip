Where Pulaski County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Pulaski County fall close to the middle of Arkansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 28 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 72135 (Roland) and 72180 (Woodson), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Saline County (20 miles), Lonoke County (23 miles), Faulkner County (28 miles) and Perry County (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Pulaski County begin with 722 (24 codes), 721 (17 codes) and 720 (6 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 722 is shared with 1 other county, Boone County, and has 25 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 30 of the 47 codes. Together they come to about 874 square miles of land (2,264 sq km). The largest is 72206 (Little Rock) with 112 square miles, and the smallest is 72180 (Woodson) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 11.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 51.3 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pulaski County; a code can reach across its border. 17 codes have no mapped area: 72078 (Jacksonville), 72115 (North Little Rock), 72124 (North Little Rock), 72164 (Sweet Home), 72190 (North Little Rock) and 12 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Arkansas has more ZIP codes: Pulaski County holds 47 of the 706 in the state list, about 6.7%.

Writing an address in Pulaski County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:

  • Little Rock, AR 72201 (one of 24 codes for Little Rock; the street decides which)
  • North Little Rock, AR 72114 (one of 9 codes for North Little Rock; the street decides which)
  • Jacksonville, AR 72076 (one of 2 codes for Jacksonville; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named Little Rock are also listed under Boone County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Little Rock is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
