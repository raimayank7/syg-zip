Where Newton County is

The ZIP codes of Newton County are found in the north-west of Arkansas. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 72628 (Deer) and 72685 (Western Grove), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Boone County (16 miles), Searcy County (26 miles), Madison County (30 miles) and Marion County (36 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 726. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 726 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Baxter County, Boone County and Carroll County, and has 55 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 605 square miles of land (1,568 sq km). The largest is 72628 (Deer) with 141 square miles, and the smallest is 72683 (Vendor) with 15.9. The middle-sized code here covers 42.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Newton County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Newton County ranks 22nd among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Newton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:

  • Compton, AR 72624
  • Deer, AR 72628
  • Hasty, AR 72640

Compton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
