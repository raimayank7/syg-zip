Where Perry County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Perry County fall in the west of Arkansas. Its codes spread over about 5.3 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 72016 (Bigelow) and 72025 (Casa), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Conway County (21 miles), Faulkner County (29 miles), Pope County (31 miles) and Saline County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Perry County begin with 720 (4 codes) and 721 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 720 is shared with 18 other counties, such as Arkansas County, Cleburne County and Conway County, and has 81 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 451 square miles of land (1,169 sq km). The largest is 72126 (Perryville) with 203 square miles, and the smallest is 72001 (Adona) with 22.2. The middle-sized code here covers 65.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Perry County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Perry County ranks 51st among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Perry County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:

  • Adona, AR 72001
  • Bigelow, AR 72016
  • Casa, AR 72025

Questions about Perry County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Adona, AR?

72001 is the ZIP code listed for Adona in Perry County.

Do all ZIP codes in Perry County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 720, on 4 of the 6 codes.

Which ZIP code in Perry County covers the most land?

72126 (Perryville), at about 203 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
