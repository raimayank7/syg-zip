Where Jefferson County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Jefferson County fall in the south of Arkansas. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 36 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 72132 (Redfield) and 72133 (Reydell), about 40 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Lincoln County (24 miles), Grant County (29 miles), Lonoke County (31 miles) and Cleveland County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Jefferson County begin with 716 (7 codes), 721 (6 codes) and 720 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 716 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Ashley County, Bradley County and Chicot County, and has 40 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 855 square miles of land (2,214 sq km). The largest is 72004 (Altheimer) with 183 square miles, and the smallest is 71659 (Moscow) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 57.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. Water adds about 38.1 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jefferson County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 71611 (Pine Bluff), 71612 (White Hall) and 71613 (Pine Bluff). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Jefferson County ranks 8th among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jefferson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:

  • Pine Bluff, AR 71601 (one of 4 codes for Pine Bluff; the street decides which)
  • White Hall, AR 71602 (one of 2 codes for White Hall; the street decides which)
  • Altheimer, AR 72004

Questions about Jefferson County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Pine Bluff, AR?

Pine Bluff has 4 ZIP codes in Jefferson County: 71601, 71603, 71611, 71613. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Jefferson County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 716, on 7 of the 15 codes.

Which ZIP code in Jefferson County covers the most land?

72004 (Altheimer), at about 183 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
