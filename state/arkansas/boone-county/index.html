Where Boone County is

The ZIP codes of Boone County are found in the north-west of Arkansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 119 miles, and from east to west about 52 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 72260 (Little Rock) and 72662 (Omaha), about 128 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Newton County (16 miles), Searcy County (20 miles), Marion County (20 miles) and Baxter County (35 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Boone County begin with 726 (9 codes) and 722 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 726 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Baxter County, Carroll County and Marion County, and has 55 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 766 square miles of land (1,984 sq km). The largest is 72601 (Harrison) with 366 square miles, and the smallest is 72615 (Bergman) with 0.10. The middle-sized code here covers 84.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Boone County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 72260 (Little Rock) and 72602 (Harrison). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Boone County ranks 22nd among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Boone County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:

  • Harrison, AR 72601 (one of 2 codes for Harrison; the street decides which)
  • Alpena, AR 72611
  • Bergman, AR 72615

Harrison is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 11 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Little Rock shares a name with a place in another part of Arkansas; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Boone County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Harrison, AR?

Harrison has 2 ZIP codes in Boone County: 72601, 72602. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Boone County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 726, on 9 of the 10 codes.

Which ZIP code in Boone County covers the most land?

72601 (Harrison), at about 366 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
