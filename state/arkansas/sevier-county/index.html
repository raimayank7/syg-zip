Where Sevier County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Sevier County fall in the south-west of Arkansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 24 miles, and from east to west about 12 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 71823 (Ben Lomond) and 71841 (Gillham), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Little River County (17 miles), Howard County (17 miles), Polk County (31 miles) and Hempstead County (35 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Sevier County starts with 718. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 718 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Columbia County, Hempstead County and Howard County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 546 square miles of land (1,414 sq km). The largest is 71846 (Lockesburg) with 229 square miles, and the smallest is 71823 (Ben Lomond) with 1.3. The middle-sized code here covers 68.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. Water adds about 23.6 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sevier County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Sevier County ranks 61st among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Sevier County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:
