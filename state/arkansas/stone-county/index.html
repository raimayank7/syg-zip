Where Stone County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Stone County fall in the north of Arkansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 72555 (Marcella) and 72663 (Onia), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cleburne County (22 miles), Izard County (22 miles), Van Buren County (26 miles) and Searcy County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Stone County begin with 725 (4 codes), 726 (3 codes) and 720 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 725 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Baxter County, Cleburne County and Fulton County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 472 square miles of land (1,221 sq km). The largest is 72560 (Mountain View) with 299 square miles, and the smallest is 72555 (Marcella) with 12.8. The middle-sized code here covers 34.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Stone County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 72657 (Timbo). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Stone County ranks 35th among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Stone County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:

Questions about Stone County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Timbo, AR?

Timbo has 2 ZIP codes in Stone County: 72657, 72680. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Stone County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 725, on 4 of the 8 codes.

Which ZIP code in Stone County covers the most land?

72560 (Mountain View), at about 299 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
