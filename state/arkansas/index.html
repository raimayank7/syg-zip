Arkansas ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 706 ZIP codes for Arkansas, about 1.7% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 26th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 75 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Pulaski County (47), Washington County (23), Benton County (22), Sebastian County (20) and Mississippi County (19). The fewest are in Cleveland County, with 3.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Arkansas uses 14 of these prefixes, from 716 to 729. The busiest are 720 (81 codes, most of them for Conway), 721 (68 codes, most of them for North Little Rock), 724 (66 codes, most of them for Jonesboro) and 723 (65 codes, most of them for Blytheville). The first digit 7 is shared with Louisiana, Missouri, Oklahoma and Texas.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 615 of the 706 codes. The largest is 71953 (Mena, Polk County) at 684 square miles and the smallest is 72387 (Vanndale) at 0.01; the middle value is 58.9 square miles. The other 91 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 72733 (Gateway) and the southernmost 71749 (Junction City); the one farthest east is 72310 (Armorel) and farthest west 72747 (Maysville).

Place names and addresses in Arkansas

Of the 603 place names in the list, 564 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Little Rock (25), Fort Smith (13), North Little Rock (9) and Hot Springs National Park (5). 2 place names appear under more than one county, among them Benton and Little Rock: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters AR and the code, as in Little Rock, AR 72201. The name of the county is left out.
