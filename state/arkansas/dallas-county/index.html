Where Dallas County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Dallas County is in the south of Arkansas. Its codes spread over about 18 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 71742 (Fordyce) and 71763 (Sparkman), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cleveland County (21 miles), Grant County (23 miles), Calhoun County (23 miles) and Ouachita County (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 4 codes here begin with the same three digits, 717. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 717 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Calhoun County, Clark County and Columbia County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 607 square miles of land (1,572 sq km). The largest is 71763 (Sparkman) with 242 square miles, and the smallest is 71748 (Ivan) with 3.5. The middle-sized code here covers 180 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Dallas County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Dallas County ranks 69th among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Dallas County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:

  • Carthage, AR 71725
  • Fordyce, AR 71742
  • Ivan, AR 71748

Carthage is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
