Where White County is

The ZIP codes of White County are found in the north of Arkansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 33 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 72020 (Bradford) and 72045 (El Paso), about 39 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Cleburne County (26 miles), Woodruff County (29 miles), Prairie County (32 miles) and Faulkner County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of White County begin with 720 (10 codes) and 721 (9 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 720 is shared with 18 other counties, such as Arkansas County, Cleburne County and Conway County, and has 81 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 1,193 square miles of land (3,089 sq km). The largest is 72143 (Searcy) with 302 square miles, and the smallest is 72178 (West Point) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 49.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of White County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 72145 (Searcy) and 72149 (Searcy). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, White County ranks 5th among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in White County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:

  • Searcy, AR 72143 (one of 3 codes for Searcy; the street decides which)
  • Bald Knob, AR 72010
  • Beebe, AR 72012

Questions about White County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Searcy, AR?

Searcy has 3 ZIP codes in White County: 72143, 72145, 72149. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in White County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 720, on 10 of the 19 codes.

Which ZIP code in White County covers the most land?

72143 (Searcy), at about 302 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
