Where Cleburne County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Cleburne County is in the north of Arkansas. Its codes spread over about 18 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 72131 (Quitman) and 72523 (Concord), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Stone County (22 miles), Van Buren County (25 miles), White County (26 miles) and Independence County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Cleburne County begin with 725 (6 codes), 721 (3 codes) and 720 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 725 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Baxter County, Fulton County and Independence County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 571 square miles of land (1,478 sq km). The largest is 72131 (Quitman) with 129 square miles, and the smallest is 72546 (Ida) with 3.3. The middle-sized code here covers 45.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. Water adds about 39.6 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Cleburne County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 72545 (Heber Springs). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Cleburne County ranks 16th among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Cleburne County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:

  • Heber Springs, AR 72543 (one of 2 codes for Heber Springs; the street decides which)
  • Concord, AR 72523
  • Drasco, AR 72530

Questions about Cleburne County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Heber Springs, AR?

Heber Springs has 2 ZIP codes in Cleburne County: 72543, 72545. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Cleburne County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 725, on 6 of the 11 codes.

Which ZIP code in Cleburne County covers the most land?

72131 (Quitman), at about 129 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
