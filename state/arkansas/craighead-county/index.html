Where Craighead County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Craighead County fall in the north-east of Arkansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 34 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 72419 (Caraway) and 72427 (Egypt), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Poinsett County (18 miles), Greene County (21 miles), Lawrence County (31 miles) and Mississippi County (33 miles).

The first three digits

All 15 codes here begin with the same three digits, 724. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 724 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Clay County, Greene County and Jackson County, and has 66 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 730 square miles of land (1,891 sq km). The largest is 72404 (Jonesboro) with 128 square miles, and the smallest is 72427 (Egypt) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 50.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Craighead County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 72402 (Jonesboro) and 72403 (Jonesboro). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Craighead County ranks 8th among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Craighead County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:

  • Jonesboro, AR 72401 (one of 5 codes for Jonesboro; the street decides which)
  • Bay, AR 72411
  • Black Oak, AR 72414

Jonesboro is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
