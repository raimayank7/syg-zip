Where Lee County is

The ZIP codes of Lee County are found in the east of Arkansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 11 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 72320 (Brickeys) and 72368 (Moro), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are St. Francis County (14 miles), Monroe County (26 miles), Phillips County (26 miles) and Tunica County, Mississippi (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 6 codes here begin with the same three digits, 723. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 723 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Crittenden County, Cross County and Desha County, and has 65 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 522 square miles of land (1,352 sq km). The largest is 72360 (Marianna) with 288 square miles, and the smallest is 72352 (La Grange) with 0.07. The middle-sized code here covers 42.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lee County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lee County ranks 51st among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lee County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:
