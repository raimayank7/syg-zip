Where Franklin County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Franklin County is in the north-west of Arkansas. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 72820 (Alix) and 72933 (Charleston), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Logan County (16 miles), Crawford County (24 miles), Johnson County (25 miles) and Sebastian County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Franklin County begin with 729 (4 codes) and 728 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 729 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Crawford County, Logan County and Scott County, and has 43 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 520 square miles of land (1,347 sq km). The largest is 72949 (Ozark) with 318 square miles, and the smallest is 72928 (Branch) with 23.4. The middle-sized code here covers 37.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Franklin County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 72820 (Alix). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Franklin County ranks 51st among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Franklin County
