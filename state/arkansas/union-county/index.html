Where Union County is

The ZIP codes of Union County are found in the south of Arkansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 22 miles, and from east to west about 43 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 71747 (Huttig) and 71758 (Mount Holly), about 47 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Union Parish, Louisiana (27 miles), Calhoun County (29 miles), Ouachita County (29 miles) and Bradley County (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 717. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 717 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Calhoun County, Clark County and Columbia County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 1,034 square miles of land (2,677 sq km). The largest is 71730 (El Dorado) with 457 square miles, and the smallest is 71759 (Norphlet) with 0.9. The middle-sized code here covers 77.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Union County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 71731 (El Dorado) and 71750 (Lawson). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Union County ranks 22nd among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Union County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:
