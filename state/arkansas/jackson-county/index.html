Where Jackson County is

The ZIP codes of Jackson County are found in the north-east of Arkansas. Its codes spread over about 31 miles from north to south and 13 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 72169 (Tupelo) and 72471 (Swifton), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Independence County (22 miles), Lawrence County (30 miles), Cross County (30 miles) and Woodruff County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Jackson County begin with 720 (4 codes), 724 (3 codes) and 721 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 720 is shared with 18 other counties, such as Arkansas County, Cleburne County and Conway County, and has 81 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 514 square miles of land (1,332 sq km). The largest is 72112 (Newport) with 328 square miles, and the smallest is 72075 (Jacksonport) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 15.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jackson County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 72043 (Diaz). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Jackson County ranks 28th among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jackson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:
