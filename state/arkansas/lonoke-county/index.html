Where Lonoke County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Lonoke County fall close to the middle of Arkansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 35 miles, and from east to west about 16 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 72007 (Austin) and 72072 (Humnoke), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Prairie County (22 miles), Pulaski County (23 miles), Jefferson County (31 miles) and White County (36 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lonoke County begin with 720 (8 codes) and 721 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 720 is shared with 18 other counties, such as Arkansas County, Cleburne County and Conway County, and has 81 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 845 square miles of land (2,188 sq km). The largest is 72046 (England) with 233 square miles, and the smallest is 72083 (Keo) with 3.7. The middle-sized code here covers 84.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. Water adds about 35.0 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lonoke County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 72037 (Coy). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lonoke County ranks 28th among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lonoke County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:

  • Austin, AR 72007
  • Cabot, AR 72023
  • Carlisle, AR 72024

Austin is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Do all ZIP codes in Lonoke County start with the same digits?
