Where Lafayette County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Lafayette County fall in the south-west of Arkansas. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 16 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 71826 (Bradley) and 71827 (Buckner), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Columbia County (19 miles), Miller County (19 miles), Nevada County (31 miles) and Hempstead County (33 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Lafayette County starts with 718. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 718 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Columbia County, Hempstead County and Howard County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 538 square miles of land (1,393 sq km). The largest is 71845 (Lewisville) with 203 square miles, and the smallest is 71839 (Garland City) with 43.1. The middle-sized code here covers 106 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lafayette County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lafayette County ranks 61st among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lafayette County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:

  • Bradley, AR 71826
  • Buckner, AR 71827
  • Garland City, AR 71839

Bradley is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
