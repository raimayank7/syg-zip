Where Arkansas County is

The ZIP codes of Arkansas County are found in the east of Arkansas. Its codes spread over about 29 miles from north to south and 31 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 72073 (Humphrey) and 72166 (Tichnor), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Monroe County (26 miles), Phillips County (29 miles), Lincoln County (31 miles) and Prairie County (35 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Arkansas County begin with 720 (7 codes) and 721 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 720 is shared with 18 other counties, such as Cleburne County, Conway County and Faulkner County, and has 81 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,121 square miles of land (2,904 sq km). The largest is 72160 (Stuttgart) with 319 square miles, and the smallest is 72140 (Saint Charles) with 14.3. The middle-sized code here covers 96.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. Water adds about 52.4 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Arkansas County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Arkansas County ranks 22nd among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Arkansas County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:

  • Almyra, AR 72003
  • Casscoe, AR 72026
  • Crocketts Bluff, AR 72038

Questions about Arkansas County ZIP codes

Do all ZIP codes in Arkansas County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Arkansas County covers the most land?
