Where Columbia County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Columbia County is in the south-west of Arkansas. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 19 miles, and from east to west about 15 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 71752 (Mc Neil) and 71861 (Taylor), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lafayette County (19 miles), Nevada County (32 miles), Ouachita County (33 miles) and Claiborne Parish, Louisiana (34 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Columbia County begin with 717 (5 codes) and 718 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 717 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Calhoun County, Clark County and Dallas County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 6 codes. Together they come to about 831 square miles of land (2,151 sq km). The largest is 71753 (Magnolia) with 311 square miles, and the smallest is 71752 (Mc Neil) with 38.4. The middle-sized code here covers 150 square miles; the middle value for all of Arkansas is 58.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Columbia County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 71754 (Magnolia). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Columbia County ranks 51st among the 75 counties and county-level areas of Arkansas (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 706 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Columbia County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters AR and the ZIP code:

  • Magnolia, AR 71753 (one of 2 codes for Magnolia; the street decides which)
  • Emerson, AR 71740
  • Mc Neil, AR 71752

Magnolia is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 11 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Columbia County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Magnolia, AR?

Magnolia has 2 ZIP codes in Columbia County: 71753, 71754. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Columbia County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 717, on 5 of the 6 codes.

Which ZIP code in Columbia County covers the most land?

71753 (Magnolia), at about 311 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
