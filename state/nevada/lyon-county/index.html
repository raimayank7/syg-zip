Where Lyon County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Lyon County fall in the west of Nevada. Its codes spread over about 57 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 89408 (Fernley) and 89430 (Smith), about 57 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Storey County (19 miles), Carson City (city) (22 miles), Douglas County (27 miles) and Washoe County (36 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 894. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 894 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Churchill County, Douglas County and Humboldt County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,961 square miles of land (5,079 sq km). The largest is 89447 (Yerington) with 854 square miles, and the smallest is 89428 (Silver City) with 1.5. The middle-sized code here covers 211 square miles; the middle value for all of Nevada is 34.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lyon County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lyon County ranks 8th among the 17 counties and county-level areas of Nevada (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 254 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lyon County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NV and the ZIP code:

  • Dayton, NV 89403
  • Fernley, NV 89408
  • Silver City, NV 89428

Dayton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 17 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
