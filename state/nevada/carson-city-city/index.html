Where Carson City (city) is

The ZIP codes of Carson City (city) are found in the west of Nevada. Its codes spread over about 4.6 miles from north to south and 1.7 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 89702 (Carson City) and 89706 (Carson City), about 4.6 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Storey County (11 miles), Douglas County (12 miles), Lyon County (22 miles) and Washoe County (28 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Carson City (city) starts with 897. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 897 is shared with 2 other counties, Douglas County and Washoe County, and has 11 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 3 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 114 square miles of land (294 sq km). The largest is 89703 (Carson City) with 44.3 square miles, and the smallest is 89706 (Carson City) with 34.5. The middle-sized code here covers 34.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Nevada is 34.5. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Carson City (city); a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 89702, 89711, 89712, 89713, 89714 and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Carson City (city) ranks 5th among the 17 counties and county-level areas of Nevada (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 254 codes in the state list.
