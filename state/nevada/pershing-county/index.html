Where Pershing County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Pershing County is in the north-west of Nevada. Its codes spread over about 25 miles from north to south and 23 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 89418 (Imlay) and 89419 (Lovelock), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Churchill County (68 miles), Lander County (68 miles), Humboldt County (74 miles) and Washoe County (97 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Pershing County starts with 894. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 894 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Churchill County, Douglas County and Humboldt County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 666 square miles of land (1,725 sq km). The largest is 89419 (Lovelock) with 413 square miles, and the smallest is 89418 (Imlay) with 253. The middle-sized code here covers 333 square miles; the middle value for all of Nevada is 34.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pershing County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Pershing County ranks 14th among the 17 counties and county-level areas of Nevada (equal counts share a rank), with 2 of the 254 codes in the state list.
