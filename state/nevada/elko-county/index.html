Where Elko County is

The ZIP codes of Elko County are found in the north-east of Nevada. Its codes spread over about 107 miles from north to south and 112 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 89832 (Owyhee) and 89883 (West Wendover), about 134 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Eureka County (92 miles), Twin Falls County, Idaho (105 miles), Lander County (113 miles) and Jerome County, Idaho (118 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Elko County starts with 898. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 898 is shared with 2 other counties, Eureka County and Lander County, and has 18 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 6,756 square miles of land (17,497 sq km). The largest is 89835 (Wells) with 1,836 square miles, and the smallest is 89826 (Jarbidge) with 24.0. The middle-sized code here covers 237 square miles; the middle value for all of Nevada is 34.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Elko County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 89802 (Elko) and 89803 (Elko). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Elko County ranks 3rd among the 17 counties and county-level areas of Nevada (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 254 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Elko County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NV and the ZIP code:

  • Elko, NV 89801 (one of 3 codes for Elko; the street decides which)
  • Carlin, NV 89822
  • Deeth, NV 89823
