Where Nye County is

The ZIP codes of Nye County are found in the south of Nevada. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 190 miles, and from east to west about 109 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 89061 (Pahrump) and 89409 (Gabbs), about 219 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Esmeralda County (73 miles), Inyo County, California (74 miles), Lincoln County (105 miles) and Clark County (109 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Nye County begin with 890 (10 codes) and 894 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 890 is shared with 3 other counties, Clark County, Esmeralda County and Lincoln County, and has 61 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 5,173 square miles of land (13,399 sq km). The largest is 89049 (Tonopah) with 2,456 square miles, and the smallest is 89022 (Manhattan) with 15.1. The middle-sized code here covers 215 square miles; the middle value for all of Nevada is 34.5. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Nye County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 89023 (Mercury) and 89041 (Pahrump). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Nye County ranks 4th among the 17 counties and county-level areas of Nevada (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 254 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Nye County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters NV and the ZIP code:

  • Pahrump, NV 89041 (one of 4 codes for Pahrump; the street decides which)
  • Amargosa Valley, NV 89020
  • Beatty, NV 89003

Questions about Nye County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Pahrump, NV?

Pahrump has 4 ZIP codes in Nye County: 89041, 89048, 89060, 89061. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Nye County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 890, on 10 of the 11 codes.

Which ZIP code in Nye County covers the most land?

89049 (Tonopah), at about 2,456 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
