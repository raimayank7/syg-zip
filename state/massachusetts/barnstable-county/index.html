Where Barnstable County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Barnstable County fall in the south-east of Massachusetts. Its codes spread over about 36 miles from north to south and 36 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 02543 (Woods Hole) and 02652 (North Truro), about 46 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Dukes County (29 miles), Nantucket County (33 miles), Plymouth County (35 miles) and Bristol County (43 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Barnstable County begin with 026 (40 codes) and 025 (15 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 026, so those three digits alone point to Barnstable County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 48 of the 55 codes. Together they come to about 401 square miles of land (1,039 sq km). The largest is 02542 (Buzzards Bay) with 28.5 square miles, and the smallest is 02553 (Monument Beach) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 7.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Massachusetts is 11.3. Water adds about 99.1 square miles, roughly 20% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Barnstable County; a code can reach across its border. 7 codes have no mapped area: 02541 (Falmouth), 02574 (West Falmouth), 02634 (Centerville), 02651 (North Eastham), 02661 (South Harwich) and 2 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Barnstable County ranks 5th among the 14 counties and county-level areas of Massachusetts (equal counts share a rank), with 55 of the 685 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Barnstable County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MA and the ZIP code:

  • Buzzards Bay, MA 02532 (one of 2 codes for Buzzards Bay; the street decides which)
  • Centerville, MA 02632 (one of 2 codes for Centerville; the street decides which)
  • Falmouth, MA 02540 (one of 2 codes for Falmouth; the street decides which)
