Where Norfolk County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Norfolk County is in the east of Massachusetts. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 23 miles, and from east to west about 34 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 02019 (Bellingham) and 02025 (Cohasset), about 36 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Suffolk County (11 miles), Middlesex County (19 miles), Plymouth County (22 miles) and Bristol County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Norfolk County begin with 020 (21 codes), 021 (11 codes), 024 (8 codes), 023 (3 codes), 022 (1 code) and 027 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 020 is shared with 2 other counties, Bristol County and Plymouth County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 39 of the 45 codes. Together they come to about 393 square miles of land (1,018 sq km). The largest is 02038 (Franklin) with 26.6 square miles, and the smallest is 02457 (Babson Park) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 10.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Massachusetts is 11.3. Water adds about 17.7 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Norfolk County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 02027 (Dedham), 02070 (Sheldonville), 02185 (Braintree), 02187 (Milton Village), 02269 (Quincy) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Norfolk County ranks 8th among the 14 counties and county-level areas of Massachusetts (equal counts share a rank), with 45 of the 685 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Norfolk County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MA and the ZIP code:

  • Quincy, MA 02169 (one of 4 codes for Quincy; the street decides which)
  • Braintree, MA 02184 (one of 2 codes for Braintree; the street decides which)
  • Brookline, MA 02445 (one of 2 codes for Brookline; the street decides which)

Quincy is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 10 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Norfolk County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Quincy, MA?

Quincy has 4 ZIP codes in Norfolk County: 02169, 02170, 02171, 02269. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Norfolk County start with the same digits?

No. They use 6 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 020, on 21 of the 45 codes.

Which ZIP code in Norfolk County covers the most land?

02038 (Franklin), at about 26.6 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
