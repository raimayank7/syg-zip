Where Suffolk County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Suffolk County is in the north-east of Massachusetts. Its codes spread over about 12 miles from north to south and 12 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 02137 (Readville) and 02151 (Revere), about 14 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Norfolk County (11 miles), Middlesex County (15 miles), Essex County (21 miles) and Plymouth County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Suffolk County begin with 021 (36 codes) and 022 (17 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 021 is shared with 2 other counties, Middlesex County and Norfolk County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 35 of the 53 codes. Together they come to about 56.4 square miles of land (146 sq km). The largest is 02151 (Revere) with 5.7 square miles, and the smallest is 02203 (Boston) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 1.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Massachusetts is 11.3. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 7.2 square miles, roughly 11% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Suffolk County; a code can reach across its border. 18 codes have no mapped area: 02112 (Boston), 02117 (Boston), 02123 (Boston), 02137 (Readville), 02196 (Boston) and 13 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Suffolk County ranks 6th among the 14 counties and county-level areas of Massachusetts (equal counts share a rank), with 53 of the 685 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Suffolk County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MA and the ZIP code:

  • Boston, MA 02108 (one of 35 codes for Boston; the street decides which)
  • Dorchester, MA 02121 (one of 3 codes for Dorchester; the street decides which)
  • Allston, MA 02134

ZIP codes named Boston are also listed under Middlesex County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Boston is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Suffolk County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Boston, MA?

Boston has 35 ZIP codes in Suffolk County: 02108, 02109, 02110, 02111, 02112, 02113, 02114, 02115, 02116, 02117 and 25 more in the table above. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Suffolk County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 021, on 36 of the 53 codes.

Which ZIP code in Suffolk County covers the most land?

02151 (Revere), at about 5.7 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
