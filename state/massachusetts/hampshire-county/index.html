Where Hampshire County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Hampshire County fall in the north-west of Massachusetts. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 38 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 01082 (Ware) and 01243 (Middlefield), about 39 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Franklin County (16 miles), Hampden County (16 miles), Berkshire County (28 miles) and Capitol Region, Connecticut (39 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Hampshire County begin with 010 (28 codes) and 012 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 010 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Berkshire County, Franklin County and Hampden County, and has 70 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 26 of the 29 codes. Together they come to about 561 square miles of land (1,453 sq km). The largest is 01007 (Belchertown) with 54.9 square miles, and the smallest is 01063 (Northampton) with 0.09. The middle-sized code here covers 17.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Massachusetts is 11.3. Water adds about 20.9 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hampshire County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 01004 (Amherst), 01059 (North Amherst) and 01061 (Northampton). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Hampshire County ranks 12th among the 14 counties and county-level areas of Massachusetts (equal counts share a rank), with 29 of the 685 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hampshire County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MA and the ZIP code:

  • Amherst, MA 01002 (one of 3 codes for Amherst; the street decides which)
  • Northampton, MA 01060 (one of 3 codes for Northampton; the street decides which)
  • Belchertown, MA 01007

Amherst is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Hampshire County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Amherst, MA?

Amherst has 3 ZIP codes in Hampshire County: 01002, 01003, 01004. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Hampshire County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 010, on 28 of the 29 codes.

Which ZIP code in Hampshire County covers the most land?

01007 (Belchertown), at about 54.9 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
