Where Dukes County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Dukes County is in the south-east of Massachusetts. Its codes spread over about 6.7 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 02539 (Edgartown) and 02713 (Cuttyhunk), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Barnstable County (29 miles), Newport County, Rhode Island (33 miles), Nantucket County (34 miles) and Bristol County (35 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Dukes County begin with 025 (6 codes) and 027 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 025 is shared with 3 other counties, Barnstable County, Nantucket County and Plymouth County, and has 28 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 93.6 square miles of land (242 sq km). The largest is 02539 (Edgartown) with 26.8 square miles, and the smallest is 02557 (Oak Bluffs) with 3.2. The middle-sized code here covers 17.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Massachusetts is 11.3. Water adds about 59.6 square miles, roughly 39% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Dukes County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 02552 (Menemsha). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Dukes County ranks 13th among the 14 counties and county-level areas of Massachusetts (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 685 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Dukes County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MA and the ZIP code:
