Where Middlesex County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Middlesex County is in the north of Massachusetts. Its codes spread over about 33 miles from north to south and 39 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 01431 (Ashby) and 02212 (Boston), about 44 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Suffolk County (15 miles), Norfolk County (19 miles), Essex County (21 miles) and Worcester County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Middlesex County begin with 018 (30 codes), 024 (28 codes), 017 (25 codes), 021 (15 codes), 014 (11 codes) and 022 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 018 is shared with 1 other county, Essex County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 82 of the 111 codes. Together they come to about 821 square miles of land (2,125 sq km). The largest is 01450 (Groton) with 32.8 square miles, and the smallest is 01718 (Acton) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 8.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Massachusetts is 11.3. Water adds about 29.2 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Middlesex County; a code can reach across its border. 29 codes have no mapped area: 01470 (Groton), 01471 (Groton), 01472 (West Groton), 01703 (Framingham), 01704 (Framingham) and 24 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Massachusetts has more ZIP codes: Middlesex County holds 111 of the 685 in the state list, about 16.2%.

Writing an address in Middlesex County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MA and the ZIP code:

  • Cambridge, MA 02138 (one of 6 codes for Cambridge; the street decides which)
  • Framingham, MA 01701 (one of 5 codes for Framingham; the street decides which)
  • Lowell, MA 01850 (one of 5 codes for Lowell; the street decides which)

Cambridge is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 12 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Boston shares a name with a place in another part of Massachusetts; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Middlesex County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Cambridge, MA?

Cambridge has 6 ZIP codes in Middlesex County: 02138, 02139, 02140, 02141, 02142, 02238. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Middlesex County start with the same digits?

No. They use 6 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 018, on 30 of the 111 codes.

Which ZIP code in Middlesex County covers the most land?

01450 (Groton), at about 32.8 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
