Where Worcester County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Worcester County fall in the north of Massachusetts. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 45 miles, and from east to west about 37 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 01368 (Royalston) and 01504 (Blackstone), about 56 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Windham, Connecticut (22 miles), Middlesex County (30 miles), Northeastern Connecticut, Connecticut (33 miles) and Providence County, Rhode Island (35 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Worcester County begin with 015 (50 codes), 016 (17 codes), 014 (15 codes), 010 (8 codes), 017 (6 codes) and 013 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 015 is shared with 1 other county, Hampden County, and has 51 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 85 of the 99 codes. Together they come to about 1,482 square miles of land (3,837 sq km). The largest is 01331 (Athol) with 54.2 square miles, and the smallest is 01525 (Linwood) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 15.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Massachusetts is 11.3. Water adds about 53.1 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Worcester County; a code can reach across its border. 14 codes have no mapped area: 01441 (Westminster), 01467 (Still River), 01508 (Charlton City), 01509 (Charlton Depot), 01526 (Manchaug) and 9 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Worcester County ranks 2nd among the 14 counties and county-level areas of Massachusetts (equal counts share a rank), with 99 of the 685 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Worcester County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MA and the ZIP code:

  • Worcester, MA 01601 (one of 15 codes for Worcester; the street decides which)
  • Shrewsbury, MA 01545 (one of 2 codes for Shrewsbury; the street decides which)
  • Westminster, MA 01441 (one of 2 codes for Westminster; the street decides which)

Worcester is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
