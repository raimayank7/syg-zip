Where Bristol County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Bristol County is in the south-east of Massachusetts. Its codes spread over about 37 miles from north to south and 23 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 02357 (North Easton) and 02791 (Westport Point), about 37 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Bristol County, Rhode Island (11 miles), Newport County, Rhode Island (20 miles), Providence County, Rhode Island (21 miles) and Plymouth County (21 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Bristol County begin with 027 (39 codes), 023 (4 codes) and 020 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 027 is shared with 3 other counties, Dukes County, Norfolk County and Plymouth County, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 35 of the 44 codes. Together they come to about 553 square miles of land (1,433 sq km). The largest is 02790 (Westport) with 53.9 square miles, and the smallest is 02357 (North Easton) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 13.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Massachusetts is 11.3. Water adds about 41.4 square miles, roughly 7% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bristol County; a code can reach across its border. 9 codes have no mapped area: 02334 (Easton), 02712 (Chartley), 02714 (Dartmouth), 02722 (Fall River), 02741 (New Bedford) and 4 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Bristol County ranks 9th among the 14 counties and county-level areas of Massachusetts (equal counts share a rank), with 44 of the 685 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bristol County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MA and the ZIP code:
