Where Montrose County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Montrose County fall in the south-west of Colorado. Its codes spread over about 28 miles from north to south and 81 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 81220 (Cimarron) and 81411 (Bedrock), about 82 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are San Miguel County (27 miles), Ouray County (35 miles), Delta County (38 miles) and Dolores County (47 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Montrose County begin with 814 (9 codes) and 812 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 814 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Delta County, Gunnison County and Ouray County, and has 26 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 2,215 square miles of land (5,738 sq km). The largest is 81403 (Montrose) with 506 square miles, and the smallest is 81411 (Bedrock) with 101. The middle-sized code here covers 219 square miles; the middle value for all of Colorado is 101. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Montrose County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 81402 (Montrose). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Montrose County ranks 14th among the 64 counties and county-level areas of Colorado (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 645 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Montrose County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CO and the ZIP code:

  • Montrose, CO 81401 (one of 3 codes for Montrose; the street decides which)
  • Bedrock, CO 81411
  • Cimarron, CO 81220

Montrose is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 13 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Montrose County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Montrose, CO?

Do all ZIP codes in Montrose County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 814, on 9 of the 10 codes.

Which ZIP code in Montrose County covers the most land?
