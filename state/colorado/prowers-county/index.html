Where Prowers County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Prowers County fall in the south-east of Colorado. Its codes spread over about 9.6 miles from north to south and 31 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 81047 (Holly) and 81092 (Wiley), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Kiowa County (34 miles), Bent County (34 miles), Hamilton County, Kansas (35 miles) and Greeley County, Kansas (45 miles).

The first three digits

All 5 codes here begin with the same three digits, 810. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 810 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Baca County, Bent County and Crowley County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,965 square miles of land (5,090 sq km). The largest is 81052 (Lamar) with 675 square miles, and the smallest is 81043 (Hartman) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 492 square miles; the middle value for all of Colorado is 101. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Prowers County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Prowers County ranks 39th among the 64 counties and county-level areas of Colorado (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 645 codes in the state list.
