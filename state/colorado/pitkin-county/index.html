Where Pitkin County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Pitkin County fall in the west of Colorado. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 8.6 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 81642 (Meredith) and 81654 (Snowmass), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Lake County (28 miles), Eagle County (31 miles), Gunnison County (40 miles) and Garfield County (43 miles).

The first three digits

All 6 codes here begin with the same three digits, 816. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 816 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Eagle County, Garfield County and Mesa County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 646 square miles of land (1,673 sq km). The largest is 81611 (Aspen) with 306 square miles, and the smallest is 81615 (Snowmass Village) with 25.3. The middle-sized code here covers 79.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Colorado is 101. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pitkin County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Pitkin County ranks 29th among the 64 counties and county-level areas of Colorado (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 645 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pitkin County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CO and the ZIP code:

  • Aspen, CO 81611 (one of 2 codes for Aspen; the street decides which)
  • Meredith, CO 81642
  • Snowmass, CO 81654
