Where Gunnison County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Gunnison County is in the west of Colorado. Its codes spread over about 46 miles from north to south and 47 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 81241 (Pitkin) and 81434 (Somerset), about 52 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Chaffee County (39 miles), Pitkin County (40 miles), Lake County (46 miles) and Hinsdale County (53 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Gunnison County begin with 812 (9 codes) and 814 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 812 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Chaffee County, Custer County and Fremont County, and has 32 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 3,526 square miles of land (9,134 sq km). The largest is 81230 (Gunnison) with 1,697 square miles, and the smallest is 81225 (Crested Butte) with 28.8. The middle-sized code here covers 262 square miles; the middle value for all of Colorado is 101. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Gunnison County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 81231 (Gunnison). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Gunnison County ranks 14th among the 64 counties and county-level areas of Colorado (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 645 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Gunnison County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CO and the ZIP code:

Questions about Gunnison County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Crested Butte, CO?

Do all ZIP codes in Gunnison County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Gunnison County covers the most land?
