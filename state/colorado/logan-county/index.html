Where Logan County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Logan County is in the north-east of Colorado. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 31 miles, and from east to west about 33 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 80726 (Crook) and 80741 (Merino), about 39 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Cheyenne County, Nebraska (37 miles), Phillips County (41 miles), Sedgwick County (43 miles) and Kimball County, Nebraska (44 miles).

The first three digits

All 8 codes here begin with the same three digits, 807. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 807 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Morgan County, Phillips County and Sedgwick County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,780 square miles of land (4,610 sq km). The largest is 80751 (Sterling) with 476 square miles, and the smallest is 80722 (Atwood) with 66.3. The middle-sized code here covers 188 square miles; the middle value for all of Colorado is 101. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Logan County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Logan County ranks 20th among the 64 counties and county-level areas of Colorado (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 645 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Logan County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CO and the ZIP code:

  • Atwood, CO 80722
  • Crook, CO 80726
  • Fleming, CO 80728

Atwood is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
