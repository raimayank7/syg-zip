Where Lincoln County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Lincoln County fall in the east of Colorado. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 43 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 80818 (Genoa) and 80823 (Karval), about 43 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Elbert County (41 miles), Kit Carson County (47 miles), Washington County (53 miles) and Cheyenne County (54 miles).

The first three digits

All 6 codes here begin with the same three digits, 808. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 808 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Cheyenne County, El Paso County and Elbert County, and has 41 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,132 square miles of land (5,521 sq km). The largest is 80821 (Hugo) with 756 square miles, and the smallest is 80826 (Limon) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 327 square miles; the middle value for all of Colorado is 101. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lincoln County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lincoln County ranks 29th among the 64 counties and county-level areas of Colorado (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 645 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lincoln County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CO and the ZIP code:

  • Arriba, CO 80804
  • Genoa, CO 80818
