Where Mesa County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Mesa County fall in the west of Colorado. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 44 miles, and from east to west about 56 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 81522 (Gateway) and 81624 (Collbran), about 69 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Delta County (40 miles), Montrose County (52 miles), Garfield County (54 miles) and Grand County, Utah (58 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Mesa County begin with 815 (15 codes) and 816 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 815, so those three digits alone point to Mesa County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 18 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 4,282 square miles of land (11,090 sq km). The largest is 81630 (De Beque) with 1,257 square miles, and the smallest is 81501 (Grand Junction) with 8.7. The middle-sized code here covers 125 square miles; the middle value for all of Colorado is 101. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Mesa County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 81502 (Grand Junction). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Mesa County ranks 9th among the 64 counties and county-level areas of Colorado (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 645 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Mesa County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CO and the ZIP code:

  • Grand Junction, CO 81501 (one of 7 codes for Grand Junction; the street decides which)
  • Clifton, CO 81520
  • Collbran, CO 81624

Grand Junction is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Mesa County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Grand Junction, CO?

Grand Junction has 7 ZIP codes in Mesa County: 81501, 81502, 81503, 81504, 81505, 81506, 81507. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Mesa County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 815, on 15 of the 19 codes.

Which ZIP code in Mesa County covers the most land?

81630 (De Beque), at about 1,257 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
