Where Delta County is

The ZIP codes of Delta County are found in the west of Colorado. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 81416 (Delta) and 81428 (Paonia), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Montrose County (38 miles), Mesa County (40 miles), Garfield County (48 miles) and Ouray County (50 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 814. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 814 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Gunnison County, Montrose County and Ouray County, and has 26 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 1,506 square miles of land (3,901 sq km). The largest is 81416 (Delta) with 571 square miles, and the smallest is 81410 (Austin) with 34.1. The middle-sized code here covers 190 square miles; the middle value for all of Colorado is 101. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Delta County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 81414 (Cory) and 81420 (Lazear). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Delta County ranks 17th among the 64 counties and county-level areas of Colorado (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 645 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Delta County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CO and the ZIP code:

  • Austin, CO 81410
  • Cedaredge, CO 81413
  • Cory, CO 81414

Austin is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
