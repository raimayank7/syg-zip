Where Gilpin County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Gilpin County fall in the north of Colorado. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 8.0 miles, and from east to west about 2.1 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 80427 (Central City) and 80474 (Rollinsville), about 8.0 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Clear Creek County (12 miles), Boulder County (19 miles), Jefferson County (21 miles) and Broomfield County (27 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Gilpin County starts with 804. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 804 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Boulder County, Clear Creek County and Eagle County, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 130 square miles of land (336 sq km). The largest is 80422 (Black Hawk) with 107 square miles, and the smallest is 80474 (Rollinsville) with 8.3. The middle-sized code here covers 14.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Colorado is 101. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Gilpin County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Gilpin County ranks 53rd among the 64 counties and county-level areas of Colorado (equal counts share a rank), with 3 of the 645 codes in the state list.
