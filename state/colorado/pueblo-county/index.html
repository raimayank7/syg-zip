Where Pueblo County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Pueblo County is in the south-east of Colorado. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 32 miles, and from east to west about 39 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 81025 (Boone) and 81069 (Rye), about 43 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Custer County (36 miles), Huerfano County (42 miles), Fremont County (43 miles) and Crowley County (44 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Pueblo County starts with 810. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 810 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Baca County, Bent County and Crowley County, and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 2,131 square miles of land (5,519 sq km). The largest is 81025 (Boone) with 550 square miles, and the smallest is 81003 (Pueblo) with 12.2. The middle-sized code here covers 145 square miles; the middle value for all of Colorado is 101. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pueblo County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 81002 (Pueblo), 81009 (Pueblo), 81010 (Pueblo), 81011 (Pueblo) and 81012 (Pueblo). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Pueblo County ranks 10th among the 64 counties and county-level areas of Colorado (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 645 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pueblo County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CO and the ZIP code:

  • Pueblo, CO 81001 (one of 12 codes for Pueblo; the street decides which)
  • Avondale, CO 81022
  • Beulah, CO 81023
