Where Elbert County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Elbert County fall in the east of Colorado. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 22 miles, and from east to west about 36 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 80107 (Elizabeth) and 80830 (Matheson), about 40 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are El Paso County (36 miles), Douglas County (37 miles), Arapahoe County (39 miles) and Lincoln County (41 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Elbert County begin with 801 (4 codes) and 808 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 801 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Adams County, Arapahoe County and Douglas County, and has 44 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,184 square miles of land (3,065 sq km). The largest is 80101 (Agate) with 365 square miles, and the smallest is 80835 (Simla) with 136. The middle-sized code here covers 170 square miles; the middle value for all of Colorado is 101. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Elbert County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Elbert County ranks 29th among the 64 counties and county-level areas of Colorado (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 645 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Elbert County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CO and the ZIP code:

  • Agate, CO 80101
  • Elbert, CO 80106
  • Elizabeth, CO 80107
