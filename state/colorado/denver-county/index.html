Where Denver County is

The ZIP codes of Denver County are found in the north of Colorado. Its codes spread over about 10.0 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 80235 (Denver) and 80249 (Denver), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Adams County (9.5 miles), Jefferson County (11 miles), Arapahoe County (11 miles) and Broomfield County (16 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Denver County starts with 802. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 802 is shared with 2 other counties, Adams County and Jefferson County, and has 67 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 33 of the 55 codes. Together they come to about 171 square miles of land (444 sq km). The largest is 80249 (Denver) with 47.7 square miles, and the smallest is 80293 (Denver) with under 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 3.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Colorado is 101. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Denver County; a code can reach across its border. 22 codes have no mapped area: 80201, 80208, 80217, 80243, 80244 and 17 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Denver County ranks 2nd among the 64 counties and county-level areas of Colorado (equal counts share a rank), with 55 of the 645 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Denver County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CO and the ZIP code:

  • Denver, CO 80201 (one of 55 codes for Denver; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named Denver are also listed under Adams County and Jefferson County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Denver is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
