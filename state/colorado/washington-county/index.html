Where Washington County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Washington County fall in the north-east of Colorado. Its codes spread over about 36 miles from north to south and 34 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 80743 (Otis) and 80757 (Woodrow), about 43 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Yuma County (38 miles), Morgan County (42 miles), Kit Carson County (49 miles) and Lincoln County (53 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Washington County begin with 807 (4 codes) and 808 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 807 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Logan County, Morgan County and Phillips County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,222 square miles of land (5,755 sq km). The largest is 80720 (Akron) with 987 square miles, and the smallest is 80801 (Anton) with 141. The middle-sized code here covers 262 square miles; the middle value for all of Colorado is 101. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Washington County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Washington County ranks 29th among the 64 counties and county-level areas of Colorado (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 645 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Washington County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CO and the ZIP code:

  • Akron, CO 80720
  • Anton, CO 80801
  • Cope, CO 80812

Akron is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
