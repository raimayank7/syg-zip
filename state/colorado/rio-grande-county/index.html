Where Rio Grande County is

The ZIP codes of Rio Grande County are found in the south of Colorado. Its codes spread over about 6.9 miles from north to south and 28 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 81135 (Homelake) and 81154 (South Fork), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Alamosa County (25 miles), Conejos County (34 miles), Saguache County (36 miles) and Mineral County (36 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Rio Grande County starts with 811. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 811 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Alamosa County, Archuleta County and Conejos County, and has 32 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 3 of the 4 codes. Together they come to about 1,364 square miles of land (3,533 sq km). The largest is 81132 (Del Norte) with 702 square miles, and the smallest is 81144 (Monte Vista) with 287. The middle-sized code here covers 375 square miles; the middle value for all of Colorado is 101. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Rio Grande County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 81135 (Homelake). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Rio Grande County ranks 45th among the 64 counties and county-level areas of Colorado (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 645 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Rio Grande County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CO and the ZIP code:
