Where Fremont County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Fremont County fall in the south of Colorado. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 42 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 81222 (Coaldale) and 81240 (Penrose), about 42 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Custer County (19 miles), Teller County (37 miles), Saguache County (42 miles) and Pueblo County (43 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Fremont County starts with 812. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 812 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Chaffee County, Custer County and Gunnison County, and has 32 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 1,436 square miles of land (3,718 sq km). The largest is 81212 (Canon City) with 672 square miles, and the smallest is 81232 (Hillside) with 0.8. The middle-sized code here covers 57.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Colorado is 101. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Fremont County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 81215 (Canon City) and 81290 (Florence). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Fremont County ranks 13th among the 64 counties and county-level areas of Colorado (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 645 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Fremont County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CO and the ZIP code:

  • Canon City, CO 81212 (one of 2 codes for Canon City; the street decides which)
  • Florence, CO 81226 (one of 2 codes for Florence; the street decides which)
  • Coal Creek, CO 81221

Questions about Fremont County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Canon City, CO?

Canon City has 2 ZIP codes in Fremont County: 81212, 81215. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Fremont County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 11 start with 812.

Which ZIP code in Fremont County covers the most land?

81212 (Canon City), at about 672 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
