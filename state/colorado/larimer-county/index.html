Where Larimer County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Larimer County fall in the north of Colorado. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 39 miles, and from east to west about 37 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 80513 (Berthoud) and 80545 (Red Feather Lakes), about 50 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Weld County (29 miles), Boulder County (35 miles), Broomfield County (42 miles) and Gilpin County (52 miles).

The first three digits

All 24 codes here begin with the same three digits, 805. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 805 is shared with 2 other counties, Boulder County and Weld County, and has 42 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 24 codes. Together they come to about 2,274 square miles of land (5,889 sq km). The largest is 80512 (Bellvue) with 480 square miles, and the smallest is 80547 (Timnath) with 5.6. The middle-sized code here covers 71.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Colorado is 101. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Larimer County; a code can reach across its border. 7 codes have no mapped area: 80511 (Estes Park), 80522 (Fort Collins), 80523 (Fort Collins), 80527 (Fort Collins), 80539 (Loveland) and 2 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Larimer County ranks 8th among the 64 counties and county-level areas of Colorado (equal counts share a rank), with 24 of the 645 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Larimer County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters CO and the ZIP code:

  • Fort Collins, CO 80521 (one of 9 codes for Fort Collins; the street decides which)
  • Loveland, CO 80537 (one of 3 codes for Loveland; the street decides which)
  • Estes Park, CO 80511 (one of 2 codes for Estes Park; the street decides which)
