Where El Paso County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, El Paso County is in the east of Colorado. Its codes spread over about 34 miles from north to south and 64 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 80819 (Green Mountain Falls) and 80840 (Usaf Academy), about 65 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Teller County (24 miles), Elbert County (36 miles), Douglas County (41 miles) and Pueblo County (47 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of El Paso County begin with 809 (53 codes), 808 (11 codes) and 801 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 809, so those three digits alone point to El Paso County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 43 of the 66 codes. Together they come to about 2,719 square miles of land (7,043 sq km). The largest is 80833 (Rush) with 478 square miles, and the smallest is 80939 (Colorado Springs) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 18.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Colorado is 101. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of El Paso County; a code can reach across its border. 23 codes have no mapped area: 80841 (Usaf Academy), 80901 (Colorado Springs), 80912 (Colorado Springs), 80931 (Colorado Springs), 80932 (Colorado Springs) and 18 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Colorado has more ZIP codes: El Paso County holds 66 of the 645 in the state list, about 10.2%.
