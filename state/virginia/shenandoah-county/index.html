Where Shenandoah County is

The ZIP codes of Shenandoah County are found in the north of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 22641 (Strasburg) and 22844 (New Market), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Page County (18 miles), Hardy County, West Virginia (20 miles), Warren County (20 miles) and Rappahannock County (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Shenandoah County begin with 226 (7 codes) and 228 (6 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 226 is shared with 7 other counties and county-level areas, such as Clarke County, Fauquier County and Frederick County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 507 square miles of land (1,313 sq km). The largest is 22824 (Edinburg) with 85.5 square miles, and the smallest is 22641 (Strasburg) with 4.8. The middle-sized code here covers 47.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Shenandoah County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 22626 (Fishers Hill). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Shenandoah County ranks 22nd among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Shenandoah County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Strasburg, VA 22641 (one of 2 codes for Strasburg; the street decides which)
  • Basye, VA 22810
  • Edinburg, VA 22824

Strasburg is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Shenandoah County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Strasburg, VA?

Strasburg has 2 ZIP codes in Shenandoah County: 22641, 22657. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Shenandoah County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 226, on 7 of the 13 codes.

Which ZIP code in Shenandoah County covers the most land?

22824 (Edinburg), at about 85.5 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
