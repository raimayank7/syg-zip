Where Bedford County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Bedford County fall in the south of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 29 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 24101 (Hardy) and 24536 (Coleman Falls), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Botetourt County (21 miles), Lynchburg (city) (22 miles), Roanoke (city) (22 miles) and Campbell County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Bedford County begin with 241 (6 codes), 245 (6 codes) and 240 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 241 is shared with 14 other counties and county-level areas, such as Botetourt County, Craig County and Floyd County, and has 52 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 732 square miles of land (1,897 sq km). The largest is 24523 (Bedford) with 230 square miles, and the smallest is 24570 (Lowry) with 0.9. The middle-sized code here covers 47.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Water adds about 23.6 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bedford County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 24178 (Villamont). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Bedford County ranks 22nd among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bedford County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Bedford, VA 24523
  • Big Island, VA 24526
  • Coleman Falls, VA 24536

Bedford is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 11 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
