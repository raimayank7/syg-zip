Where Pulaski County is

The ZIP codes of Pulaski County are found in the south-west of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 12 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 24132 (Parrott) and 24324 (Draper), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Radford (city) (7.7 miles), Giles County (17 miles), Montgomery County (20 miles) and Floyd County (20 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Pulaski County begin with 241 (3 codes), 243 (3 codes) and 240 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 241 is shared with 14 other counties and county-level areas, such as Bedford County, Botetourt County and Craig County, and has 52 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 337 square miles of land (873 sq km). The largest is 24084 (Dublin) with 114 square miles, and the smallest is 24129 (New River) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 39.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pulaski County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 24126 (Newbern). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Pulaski County ranks 58th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pulaski County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Belspring, VA 24058
  • Draper, VA 24324
  • Dublin, VA 24084
