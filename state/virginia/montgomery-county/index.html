Where Montgomery County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Montgomery County fall in the south of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 27 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 24023 (Roanoke) and 24149 (Riner), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Radford (city) (12 miles), Floyd County (15 miles), Roanoke County (17 miles) and Salem (city) (17 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Montgomery County begin with 240 (7 codes) and 241 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 240 is shared with 11 other counties and county-level areas, such as Bedford County, Botetourt County and Floyd County, and has 76 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 349 square miles of land (903 sq km). The largest is 24060 (Blacksburg) with 131 square miles, and the smallest is 24061 (Blacksburg) with 1.1. The middle-sized code here covers 51.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Montgomery County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 24023 (Roanoke), 24062 (Blacksburg), 24063 (Blacksburg), 24068 (Christiansburg) and 24111 (Mc Coy). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Montgomery County ranks 31st among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Montgomery County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Blacksburg, VA 24060 (one of 4 codes for Blacksburg; the street decides which)
  • Christiansburg, VA 24068 (one of 2 codes for Christiansburg; the street decides which)
  • Mc Coy, VA 24111

Roanoke shares a name with a place in another part of Virginia; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Montgomery County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Blacksburg, VA?

Blacksburg has 4 ZIP codes in Montgomery County: 24060, 24061, 24062, 24063. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Montgomery County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 240, on 7 of the 11 codes.

Which ZIP code in Montgomery County covers the most land?

24060 (Blacksburg), at about 131 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
