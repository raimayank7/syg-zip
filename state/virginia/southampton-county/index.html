Where Southampton County is

The ZIP codes of Southampton County are found in the south-east of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 23828 (Branchville) and 23866 (Ivor), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Franklin (city) (11 miles), Sussex County (18 miles), Greensville County (22 miles) and Northampton County, North Carolina (22 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Southampton County starts with 238. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 238 is shared with 14 other counties and county-level areas, such as Brunswick County, Chesterfield County and Colonial Heights (city), and has 59 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 484 square miles of land (1,254 sq km). The largest is 23837 (Courtland) with 110 square miles, and the smallest is 23878 (Sedley) with 36.6. The middle-sized code here covers 45.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Southampton County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Southampton County ranks 58th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Southampton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:
