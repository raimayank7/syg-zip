Where Patrick County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Patrick County fall in the south of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 14 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 24053 (Ararat) and 24082 (Critz), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Floyd County (20 miles), Stokes County, North Carolina (21 miles), Carroll County (21 miles) and Henry County (21 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Patrick County begin with 241 (5 codes) and 240 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 241 is shared with 14 other counties and county-level areas, such as Bedford County, Botetourt County and Craig County, and has 52 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 450 square miles of land (1,166 sq km). The largest is 24171 (Stuart) with 199 square miles, and the smallest is 24082 (Critz) with 10.6. The middle-sized code here covers 40.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Patrick County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 24177 (Vesta). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Patrick County ranks 58th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Patrick County
