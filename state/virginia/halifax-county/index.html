Where Halifax County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Halifax County fall in the south of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 24 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 24577 (Nathalie) and 24598 (Virgilina), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Charlotte County (23 miles), Pittsylvania County (27 miles), Person County, North Carolina (27 miles) and Danville (city) (30 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Halifax County starts with 245. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 245 is shared with 11 other counties and county-level areas, such as Albemarle County, Amherst County and Appomattox County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 769 square miles of land (1,992 sq km). The largest is 24577 (Nathalie) with 187 square miles, and the smallest is 24539 (Crystal Hill) with 10.0. The middle-sized code here covers 75.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Halifax County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 24535 (Cluster Springs). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Halifax County ranks 35th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Halifax County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Alton, VA 24520
  • Clover, VA 24534
  • Cluster Springs, VA 24535

Alton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
