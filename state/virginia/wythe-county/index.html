Where Wythe County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Wythe County is in the south-west of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 9.7 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 24313 (Barren Springs) and 24368 (Rural Retreat), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Galax (city) (16 miles), Grayson County (18 miles), Bland County (20 miles) and Carroll County (23 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Wythe County starts with 243. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 243 is shared with 9 other counties and county-level areas, such as Bland County, Carroll County and Floyd County, and has 40 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 525 square miles of land (1,359 sq km). The largest is 24382 (Wytheville) with 190 square miles, and the smallest is 24313 (Barren Springs) with 7.6. The middle-sized code here covers 35.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Wythe County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Wythe County ranks 47th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.
