Where City of Suffolk is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, City of Suffolk is in the south-east of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 22 miles, and from east to west about 40 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 23437 (Suffolk) and 23453 (Virginia Beach), about 41 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Isle of Wight County (12 miles), Portsmouth (city) (12 miles), Chesapeake (city) (14 miles) and Norfolk (city) (17 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of City of Suffolk starts with 234. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 234 is shared with 4 other counties and county-level areas, such as Accomack County, Isle of Wight County and Northampton County, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 408 square miles of land (1,057 sq km). The largest is 23434 (Suffolk) with 207 square miles, and the smallest is 23436 (Suffolk) with 1.6. The middle-sized code here covers 24.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Water adds about 29.9 square miles, roughly 7% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of City of Suffolk; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 23439 (Suffolk). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, City of Suffolk ranks 47th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in City of Suffolk

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Suffolk, VA 23432 (one of 8 codes for Suffolk; the street decides which)
  • Virginia Beach, VA 23453

Virginia Beach shares a name with a place in another part of Virginia; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
