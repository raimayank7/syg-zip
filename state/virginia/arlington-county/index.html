Where Arlington County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Arlington County fall in the north-east of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 4.3 miles from north to south and 6.0 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 22213 (Arlington) and 22242 (Arlington), about 6.7 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are City of Alexandria (4.2 miles), Falls Church (city) (4.3 miles), District of Columbia, District of Columbia (4.3 miles) and Fairfax County (8.6 miles).

The first three digits

All 28 codes here begin with the same three digits, 222. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 222, so those three digits alone point to Arlington County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 28 codes. Together they come to about 26.0 square miles of land (67.5 sq km). The largest is 22207 (Arlington) with 6.5 square miles, and the smallest is 22214 (Arlington) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 2.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Arlington County; a code can reach across its border. 17 codes have no mapped area: 22210 (Arlington), 22212 (Arlington), 22215 (Arlington), 22216 (Arlington), 22217 (Arlington) and 12 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Arlington County ranks 6th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 28 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Arlington County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Arlington, VA 22201 (one of 27 codes for Arlington; the street decides which)
  • Fort Myer, VA 22211

Arlington is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 20 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
