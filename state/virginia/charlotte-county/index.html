Where Charlotte County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Charlotte County is in the south of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 29 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 23963 (Red House) and 23964 (Red Oak), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Prince Edward County (21 miles), Halifax County (23 miles), Lunenburg County (24 miles) and Appomattox County (24 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 239. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 239 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Appomattox County, Brunswick County and Buckingham County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 567 square miles of land (1,469 sq km). The largest is 23947 (Keysville) with 145 square miles, and the smallest is 23976 (Wylliesburg) with 8.7. The middle-sized code here covers 49.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Charlotte County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Charlotte County ranks 35th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Charlotte County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:
