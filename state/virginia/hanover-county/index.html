Where Hanover County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Hanover County fall in the east of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 23015 (Beaverdam) and 23111 (Mechanicsville), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are City of Richmond (15 miles), Henrico County (16 miles), Goochland County (23 miles) and King William County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Hanover County begin with 230 (5 codes) and 231 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 230 is shared with 16 other counties, such as Amelia County, Buckingham County and Charles City County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 547 square miles of land (1,416 sq km). The largest is 23015 (Beaverdam) with 87.2 square miles, and the smallest is 23146 (Rockville) with 32.3. The middle-sized code here covers 68.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hanover County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 23162 (Studley). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Hanover County ranks 35th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hanover County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Mechanicsville, VA 23111 (one of 2 codes for Mechanicsville; the street decides which)
  • Ashland, VA 23005
  • Beaverdam, VA 23015

Mechanicsville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Glen Allen shares a name with a place in another part of Virginia; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Hanover County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Mechanicsville, VA?

Mechanicsville has 2 ZIP codes in Hanover County: 23111, 23116. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Hanover County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 230, on 5 of the 10 codes.

Which ZIP code in Hanover County covers the most land?

23015 (Beaverdam), at about 87.2 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
