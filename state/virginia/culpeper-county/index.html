Where Culpeper County is

The ZIP codes of Culpeper County are found in the north-east of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 22713 (Boston) and 22736 (Richardsville), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Orange County (16 miles), Madison County (17 miles), Rappahannock County (19 miles) and Fauquier County (20 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 227. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 227 is shared with 3 other counties, Fauquier County, Madison County and Rappahannock County, and has 37 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 433 square miles of land (1,121 sq km). The largest is 22701 (Culpeper) with 172 square miles, and the smallest is 22741 (Stevensburg) with 6.5. The middle-sized code here covers 23.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Culpeper County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Culpeper County ranks 27th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Culpeper County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Boston, VA 22713
  • Brandy Station, VA 22714
  • Culpeper, VA 22701

Boston is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
