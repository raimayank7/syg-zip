Where Campbell County is

The ZIP codes of Campbell County are found in the south of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 19 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 24538 (Concord) and 24571 (Lynch Station), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lynchburg (city) (14 miles), Appomattox County (21 miles), Amherst County (24 miles) and Bedford County (25 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 245. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 245 is shared with 11 other counties and county-level areas, such as Albemarle County, Amherst County and Appomattox County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 565 square miles of land (1,464 sq km). The largest is 24554 (Gladys) with 126 square miles, and the smallest is 24517 (Altavista) with 32.6. The middle-sized code here covers 67.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Campbell County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 24513 (Lynchburg) and 24576 (Naruna). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Campbell County ranks 35th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Campbell County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Altavista, VA 24517
  • Brookneal, VA 24528
  • Concord, VA 24538

Lynchburg shares a name with a place in another part of Virginia; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
