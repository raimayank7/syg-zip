Where Fairfax County is

The ZIP codes of Fairfax County are found in the north-east of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 20172 (Herndon) and 22308 (Alexandria), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Fairfax (city) (2.6 miles), Falls Church (city) (4.9 miles), Arlington County (8.6 miles) and City of Alexandria (9.5 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Fairfax County begin with 221 (28 codes), 220 (23 codes), 201 (15 codes) and 223 (8 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 221 is shared with 1 other county, Prince William County, and has 37 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 41 of the 74 codes. Together they come to about 372 square miles of land (963 sq km). The largest is 22079 (Lorton) with 28.4 square miles, and the smallest is 22035 (Fairfax) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 7.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 15.4 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Fairfax County; a code can reach across its border. 33 codes have no mapped area: 20122 (Centreville), 20153 (Chantilly), 20172 (Herndon), 20192 (Herndon), 20195 (Reston) and 28 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

No other part of Virginia has more ZIP codes: Fairfax County holds 74 of the 1,214 in the state list, about 6.1%.

Writing an address in Fairfax County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Springfield, VA 22150 (one of 9 codes for Springfield; the street decides which)
  • Alexandria, VA 22303 (one of 8 codes for Alexandria; the street decides which)
  • Fairfax, VA 22032 (one of 6 codes for Fairfax; the street decides which)

Springfield is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 23 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Alexandria, Chantilly and Fairfax share a name with a place in another part of Virginia; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Fairfax County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Springfield, VA?

Springfield has 9 ZIP codes in Fairfax County: 22150, 22151, 22152, 22153, 22156, 22158, 22159, 22160, 22161. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Fairfax County start with the same digits?

Which ZIP code in Fairfax County covers the most land?
