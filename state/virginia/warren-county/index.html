Where Warren County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Warren County is in the north-east of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 13 miles, and from east to west about 12 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 22642 (Linden) and 22649 (Middletown), about 14 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Rappahannock County (15 miles), Clarke County (15 miles), Winchester (city) (19 miles) and Shenandoah County (20 miles).

The first three digits

All 4 codes here begin with the same three digits, 226. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 226 is shared with 7 other counties and county-level areas, such as Clarke County, Fauquier County and Frederick County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 3 of the 4 codes. Together they come to about 203 square miles of land (525 sq km). The largest is 22630 (Front Royal) with 122 square miles, and the smallest is 22642 (Linden) with 28.4. The middle-sized code here covers 52.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Warren County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 22649 (Middletown). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Warren County ranks 91st among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Warren County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Bentonville, VA 22610
  • Front Royal, VA 22630
  • Linden, VA 22642

Bentonville is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Middletown shares a name with a place in another part of Virginia; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
