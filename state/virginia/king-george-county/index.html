Where King George County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, King George County is in the east of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 11 miles, and from east to west about 9.4 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 22448 (Dahlgren) and 22451 (Dogue), about 12 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Caroline County (17 miles), Charles County, Maryland (18 miles), Fredericksburg (city) (20 miles) and Stafford County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of King George County begin with 224 (4 codes) and 225 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 224 is shared with 10 other counties and county-level areas, such as Caroline County, Essex County and Fredericksburg (city), and has 37 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 2 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 180 square miles of land (466 sq km). The largest is 22485 (King George) with 176 square miles, and the smallest is 22448 (Dahlgren) with 4.1. The middle-sized code here covers 89.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 8.1 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of King George County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 22451 (Dogue), 22481 (Jersey), 22526 (Ninde), 22544 (Rollins Fork) and 22547 (Sealston). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, King George County ranks 68th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.
