Where Carroll County is

The ZIP codes of Carroll County are found in the south-west of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 16 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 24352 (Laurel Fork) and 24381 (Woodlawn), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Galax (city) (12 miles), Surry County, North Carolina (20 miles), Patrick County (21 miles) and Wythe County (23 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 243. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 243 is shared with 9 other counties and county-level areas, such as Bland County, Floyd County and Galax (city), and has 40 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 343 square miles of land (888 sq km). The largest is 24343 (Hillsville) with 126 square miles, and the smallest is 24351 (Lambsburg) with 11.4. The middle-sized code here covers 38.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Carroll County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Carroll County ranks 68th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Carroll County
