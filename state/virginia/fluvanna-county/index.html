Where Fluvanna County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Fluvanna County is in the east of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 12 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 22974 (Troy) and 23022 (Bremo Bluff), about 15 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Charlottesville (city) (18 miles), Louisa County (19 miles), Cumberland County (19 miles) and Goochland County (21 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Fluvanna County begin with 230 (3 codes) and 229 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 230 is shared with 16 other counties, such as Amelia County, Buckingham County and Charles City County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 240 square miles of land (621 sq km). The largest is 22963 (Palmyra) with 125 square miles, and the smallest is 23055 (Fork Union) with 14.0. The middle-sized code here covers 36.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Fluvanna County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Fluvanna County ranks 81st among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Fluvanna County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:
