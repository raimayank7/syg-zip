Where Virginia Beach (city) is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Virginia Beach (city) fall in the south-east of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 12 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 23457 (Virginia Beach) and 23459 (Virginia Beach), about 21 miles from each other in a straight line. The first three digits

All 19 codes here begin with the same three digits, 234. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 234 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Accomack County, City of Suffolk and Isle of Wight County, and has 63 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 227 square miles of land (588 sq km). The largest is 23457 (Virginia Beach) with 67.9 square miles, and the smallest is 23461 (Virginia Beach) with 1.3. The middle-sized code here covers 15.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Water adds about 56.3 square miles, roughly 20% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Virginia Beach (city); a code can reach across its border. 8 codes have no mapped area: 23450, 23458, 23463, 23465, 23466 and 3 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.
