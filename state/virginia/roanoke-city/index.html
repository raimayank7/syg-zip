Where Roanoke (city) is

The ZIP codes of Roanoke (city) are found in the south of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 4.9 miles from north to south and 3.6 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 24012 (Roanoke) and 24014 (Roanoke), about 4.9 miles from each other in a straight line. The first three digits

All 36 codes here begin with the same three digits, 240. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 240 is shared with 11 other counties, such as Bedford County, Botetourt County and Floyd County, and has 76 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 36 codes. Together they come to about 66.7 square miles of land (173 sq km). The largest is 24014 (Roanoke) with 27.3 square miles, and the smallest is 24022 (Roanoke) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 3.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.
