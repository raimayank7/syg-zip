Where Madison County is

The ZIP codes of Madison County are found in the north-east of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 22743 (Syria) and 22989 (Woodberry Forest), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Greene County (14 miles), Orange County (14 miles), Culpeper County (17 miles) and Page County (21 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Madison County begin with 227 (14 codes) and 229 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 227 is shared with 3 other counties, Culpeper County, Fauquier County and Rappahannock County, and has 37 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 15 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 254 square miles of land (659 sq km). The largest is 22727 (Madison) with 106 square miles, and the smallest is 22725 (Leon) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 4.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Madison County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 22748 (Wolftown). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Madison County ranks 16th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Madison County
