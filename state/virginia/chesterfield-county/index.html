Where Chesterfield County is

The ZIP codes of Chesterfield County are found in the south-east of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 23120 (Moseley) and 23836 (Chester), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are City of Richmond (9.3 miles), Henrico County (11 miles), Colonial Heights (city) (14 miles) and Petersburg (city) (17 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Chesterfield County begin with 232 (5 codes), 231 (4 codes) and 238 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 232 is shared with 2 other counties, City of Richmond and Henrico County, and has 48 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 407 square miles of land (1,055 sq km). The largest is 23838 (Chesterfield) with 98.8 square miles, and the smallest is 23114 (Midlothian) with 12.1. The middle-sized code here covers 25.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Chesterfield County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 23297 (Richmond). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Chesterfield County ranks 22nd among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Chesterfield County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Richmond, VA 23234 (one of 5 codes for Richmond; the street decides which)
  • Midlothian, VA 23112 (one of 3 codes for Midlothian; the street decides which)
  • Chester, VA 23831 (one of 2 codes for Chester; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named Richmond are also listed under City of Richmond and Henrico County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Richmond is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 14 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Chesterfield County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Richmond, VA?

Richmond has 5 ZIP codes in Chesterfield County: 23234, 23235, 23236, 23237, 23297. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Chesterfield County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 232, on 5 of the 13 codes.

Which ZIP code in Chesterfield County covers the most land?

23838 (Chesterfield), at about 98.8 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
