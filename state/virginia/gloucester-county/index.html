Where Gloucester County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Gloucester County is in the south-east of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 13 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 23107 (Maryus) and 23190 (Woods Cross Roads), about 19 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are York County (10.0 miles), Mathews County (11 miles), Williamsburg (city) (14 miles) and James City County (15 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Gloucester County begin with 231 (8 codes) and 230 (7 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 231 is shared with 18 other counties and county-level areas, such as Amelia County, Buckingham County and Charles City County, and has 61 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 4 of the 15 codes. Together they come to about 203 square miles of land (526 sq km). The largest is 23061 (Gloucester) with 158 square miles, and the smallest is 23062 (Gloucester Point) with 1.5. The middle-sized code here covers 21.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Water adds about 70.4 square miles, roughly 26% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Gloucester County; a code can reach across its border. 11 codes have no mapped area: 23001 (Achilles), 23003 (Ark), 23018 (Bena), 23107 (Maryus), 23131 (Ordinary) and 6 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Gloucester County ranks 19th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 15 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.
