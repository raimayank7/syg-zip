Where Washington County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Washington County is in the south-west of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 9.7 miles from north to south and 28 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 24270 (Mendota) and 24340 (Glade Spring), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Bristol (city) (16 miles), Johnson County, Tennessee (19 miles), Russell County (20 miles) and Smyth County (22 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Washington County begin with 242 (5 codes) and 243 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 242 is shared with 9 other counties and county-level areas, such as Bristol (city), Buchanan County and Dickenson County, and has 50 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 415 square miles of land (1,075 sq km). The largest is 24210 (Abingdon) with 144 square miles, and the smallest is 24327 (Emory) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 59.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Washington County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 24212 (Abingdon). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Washington County ranks 58th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Washington County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Abingdon, VA 24210 (one of 3 codes for Abingdon; the street decides which)
  • Damascus, VA 24236
  • Emory, VA 24327

Questions about Washington County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Abingdon, VA?

Abingdon has 3 ZIP codes in Washington County: 24210, 24211, 24212. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Washington County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 242, on 5 of the 8 codes.

Which ZIP code in Washington County covers the most land?

24210 (Abingdon), at about 144 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
