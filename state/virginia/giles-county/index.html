Where Giles County is

The ZIP codes of Giles County are found in the south-west of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 9.4 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 24093 (Glen Lyn) and 24128 (Newport), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Radford (city) (16 miles), Pulaski County (17 miles), Monroe County, West Virginia (17 miles) and Montgomery County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Giles County begin with 241 (7 codes) and 240 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 241 is shared with 14 other counties and county-level areas, such as Bedford County, Botetourt County and Craig County, and has 52 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 380 square miles of land (985 sq km). The largest is 24134 (Pearisburg) with 104 square miles, and the smallest is 24093 (Glen Lyn) with 5.3. The middle-sized code here covers 34.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Giles County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Giles County ranks 47th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Giles County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:
