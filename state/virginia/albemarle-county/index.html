Where Albemarle County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Albemarle County sits near the middle of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 31 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 22940 (Free Union) and 24562 (Howardsville), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Charlottesville (city) (5.9 miles), Waynesboro (city) (18 miles), Greene County (21 miles) and Fluvanna County (21 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Albemarle County begin with 229 (15 codes) and 245 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 229 is shared with 8 other counties and county-level areas, such as Augusta County, Charlottesville (city) and Fluvanna County, and has 49 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 634 square miles of land (1,643 sq km). The largest is 24590 (Scottsville) with 162 square miles, and the smallest is 22946 (Keene) with 1.9. The middle-sized code here covers 46.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Albemarle County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 22909 (Charlottesville), 22924 (Batesville), 22945 (Ivy) and 22987 (White Hall). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Albemarle County ranks 15th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Albemarle County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Charlottesville, VA 22901 (one of 3 codes for Charlottesville; the street decides which)
  • Batesville, VA 22924
  • Covesville, VA 22931

ZIP codes named Charlottesville are also listed under Charlottesville (city), so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site.

Questions about Albemarle County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Charlottesville, VA?

Charlottesville has 3 ZIP codes in Albemarle County: 22901, 22909, 22911. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Albemarle County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 229, on 15 of the 17 codes.

Which ZIP code in Albemarle County covers the most land?

24590 (Scottsville), at about 162 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
