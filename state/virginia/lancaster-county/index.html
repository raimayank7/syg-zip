Where Lancaster County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Lancaster County fall in the east of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 8.9 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 22523 (Morattico) and 22578 (White Stone), about 15 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Middlesex County (8.3 miles), Northumberland County (13 miles), Richmond County (16 miles) and Mathews County (22 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lancaster County begin with 225 (8 codes) and 224 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 225 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Caroline County, Essex County and King George County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 151 square miles of land (390 sq km). The largest is 22503 (Lancaster) with 86.7 square miles, and the smallest is 22480 (Irvington) with 2.5. The middle-sized code here covers 18.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Water adds about 62.2 square miles, roughly 29% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lancaster County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 22507 (Lively), 22513 (Merry Point), 22517 (Mollusk), 22523 (Morattico) and 22528 (Nuttsville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Lancaster County ranks 35th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lancaster County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Irvington, VA 22480
  • Kilmarnock, VA 22482
  • Lancaster, VA 22503

Irvington is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Lancaster County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Irvington, VA?

Do all ZIP codes in Lancaster County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 225, on 8 of the 10 codes.

Which ZIP code in Lancaster County covers the most land?

22503 (Lancaster), at about 86.7 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
