Where Louisa County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Louisa County fall in the east of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 11 miles, and from east to west about 15 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 23024 (Bumpass) and 23170 (Trevilians), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Goochland County (19 miles), Orange County (19 miles), Fluvanna County (19 miles) and Spotsylvania County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Louisa County begin with 230 (2 codes) and 231 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 230 is shared with 16 other counties, such as Amelia County, Buckingham County and Charles City County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 3 of the 4 codes. Together they come to about 454 square miles of land (1,177 sq km). The largest is 23093 (Louisa) with 203 square miles, and the smallest is 23024 (Bumpass) with 108. The middle-sized code here covers 143 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 20.5 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Louisa County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 23170 (Trevilians). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Louisa County ranks 91st among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Louisa County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Louisa, VA 23093
