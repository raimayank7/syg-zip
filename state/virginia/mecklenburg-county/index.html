Where Mecklenburg County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Mecklenburg County fall in the south-east of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 17 miles, and from east to west about 33 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 23950 (La Crosse) and 24580 (Nelson), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Vance County, North Carolina (20 miles), Lunenburg County (20 miles), Warren County, North Carolina (21 miles) and Charlotte County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Mecklenburg County begin with 239 (8 codes) and 245 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 239 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Appomattox County, Brunswick County and Buckingham County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 665 square miles of land (1,722 sq km). The largest is 23924 (Chase City) with 141 square miles, and the smallest is 24580 (Nelson) with 21.1. The middle-sized code here covers 51.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Water adds about 54.5 square miles, roughly 8% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Mecklenburg County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Mecklenburg County ranks 35th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Mecklenburg County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:
