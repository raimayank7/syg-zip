Where Northumberland County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Northumberland County is in the east of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 16 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 22435 (Callao) and 22539 (Reedville), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Lancaster County (13 miles), Richmond County (15 miles), Middlesex County (21 miles) and Westmoreland County (23 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Northumberland County begin with 224 (4 codes) and 225 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 224 is shared with 10 other counties and county-level areas, such as Caroline County, Essex County and Fredericksburg (city), and has 37 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 176 square miles of land (455 sq km). The largest is 22473 (Heathsville) with 114 square miles, and the smallest is 22432 (Burgess) with 3.2. The middle-sized code here covers 16.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Water adds about 23.7 square miles, roughly 12% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Northumberland County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 22456 (Edwardsville), 22530 (Ophelia) and 22579 (Wicomico Church). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Northumberland County ranks 58th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Northumberland County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Callao, VA 22435
  • Edwardsville, VA 22456
