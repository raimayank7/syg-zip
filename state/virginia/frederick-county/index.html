Where Frederick County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Frederick County fall in the north-east of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 22625 (Cross Junction) and 22645 (Middletown), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Winchester (city) (3.3 miles), Clarke County (13 miles), Berkeley County, West Virginia (20 miles) and Warren County (20 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 226. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 226 is shared with 7 other counties and county-level areas, such as Clarke County, Fauquier County and Page County, and has 33 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 9 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 439 square miles of land (1,137 sq km). The largest is 22602 (Winchester) with 113 square miles, and the smallest is 22656 (Stephenson) with 8.8. The middle-sized code here covers 41.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Frederick County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 22622 (Brucetown). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Frederick County ranks 35th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Frederick County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Winchester, VA 22602 (one of 2 codes for Winchester; the street decides which)
  • Brucetown, VA 22622
  • Clear Brook, VA 22624

ZIP codes named Winchester are also listed under Winchester (city), so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Winchester is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 12 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Frederick County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Winchester, VA?

Winchester has 2 ZIP codes in Frederick County: 22602, 22603. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Frederick County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 10 start with 226.

Which ZIP code in Frederick County covers the most land?

22602 (Winchester), at about 113 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
