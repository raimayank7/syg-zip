Virginia ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 1,214 ZIP codes for Virginia, about 3.0% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 8th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 133 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Fairfax County (74), Accomack County (40), Roanoke (city) (36), Loudoun County (35) and City of Richmond (30). At the other end, 20 have only one or two codes, for example Buena Vista (city), Charles City County and Colonial Heights (city).

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Virginia uses 29 of these prefixes, from 201 to 246. The busiest are 201 (80 codes, most of them for Manassas), 240 (76 codes, most of them for Roanoke), 245 (64 codes, most of them for Lynchburg) and 234 (63 codes, most of them for Virginia Beach). The first digit 2 is shared with District of Columbia, Maryland, North Carolina, South Carolina and West Virginia.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 903 of the 1,214 codes. The largest is 24426 (Covington, Covington (city)) at 364 square miles and the smallest is 22908 (Charlottesville) at 0.01; the middle value is 28.9 square miles. The other 311 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 22625 (Cross Junction) and the southernmost 24580 (Nelson); the one farthest east is 23336 (Chincoteague Island) and farthest west 24248 (Ewing).

Place names and addresses in Virginia

Of the 837 place names in the list, 763 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Roanoke (41), Richmond (39), Arlington (27) and Alexandria (21). 18 place names appear under more than one county, among them Roanoke, Fredericksburg, Richmond and Alexandria: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters VA and the code, as in Roanoke, VA 24001. The name of the county is left out.
