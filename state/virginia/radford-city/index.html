Where Radford (city) is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Radford (city) is in the south-west of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 1.3 miles from north to south and under a mile from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 24141 (Radford) and 24142 (Radford), about 1.4 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Pulaski County (7.7 miles), Montgomery County (12 miles), Giles County (16 miles) and Floyd County (16 miles).

The first three digits

All 3 codes here begin with the same three digits, 241. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 241 is shared with 14 other counties and county-level areas, such as Bedford County, Botetourt County and Craig County, and has 52 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 2 of the 3 codes. Together they come to about 65.7 square miles of land (170 sq km). The largest is 24141 (Radford) with 65.6 square miles, and the smallest is 24142 (Radford) with 0.1. The middle-sized code here covers 32.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Water adds about 3.6 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Radford (city); a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 24143. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.
