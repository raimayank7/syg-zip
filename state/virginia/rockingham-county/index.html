Where Rockingham County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Rockingham County is in the north of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 38 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 22811 (Bergton) and 24441 (Grottoes), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are City of Harrisonburg (3.4 miles), Page County (22 miles), Greene County (25 miles) and Staunton (city) (26 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Rockingham County begin with 228 (18 codes) and 244 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 228 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Augusta County, City of Harrisonburg and Page County, and has 31 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 18 of the 20 codes. Together they come to about 912 square miles of land (2,362 sq km). The largest is 22827 (Elkton) with 159 square miles, and the smallest is 22846 (Penn Laird) with 11.1. The middle-sized code here covers 35.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Rockingham County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 22833 (Lacey Spring) and 22848 (Pleasant Valley). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Rockingham County ranks 9th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 20 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Rockingham County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:
