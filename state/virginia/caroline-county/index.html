Where Caroline County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Caroline County fall in the east of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 18 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 22538 (Rappahannock Academy) and 22546 (Ruther Glen), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Spotsylvania County (17 miles), King George County (17 miles), Fredericksburg (city) (18 miles) and Hanover County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Caroline County begin with 225 (7 codes) and 224 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 225 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Essex County, King George County and Lancaster County, and has 45 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 10 codes. Together they come to about 509 square miles of land (1,318 sq km). The largest is 22546 (Ruther Glen) with 164 square miles, and the smallest is 22538 (Rappahannock Academy) with 24.3. The middle-sized code here covers 86.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Caroline County; a code can reach across its border. 4 codes have no mapped area: 22428 (Bowling Green), 22446 (Corbin), 22501 (Ladysmith) and 22552 (Sparta). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Caroline County ranks 35th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Caroline County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Bowling Green, VA 22427 (one of 2 codes for Bowling Green; the street decides which)
  • Corbin, VA 22446
  • Ladysmith, VA 22501

Bowling Green is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Caroline County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Bowling Green, VA?

Bowling Green has 2 ZIP codes in Caroline County: 22427, 22428. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Caroline County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 225, on 7 of the 10 codes.

Which ZIP code in Caroline County covers the most land?

22546 (Ruther Glen), at about 164 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
