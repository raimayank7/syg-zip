Where Pittsylvania County is

The ZIP codes of Pittsylvania County are found in the south of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 34 miles, and from east to west about 26 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 24069 (Cascade) and 24563 (Hurt), about 39 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Danville (city) (14 miles), Caswell County, North Carolina (26 miles), Halifax County (27 miles) and Martinsville (city) (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Pittsylvania County begin with 245 (10 codes), 241 (2 codes) and 240 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 245 is shared with 11 other counties and county-level areas, such as Albemarle County, Amherst County and Appomattox County, and has 64 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 834 square miles of land (2,161 sq km). The largest is 24531 (Chatham) with 197 square miles, and the smallest is 24139 (Pittsville) with 24.3. The middle-sized code here covers 51.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Pittsylvania County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Pittsylvania County ranks 22nd among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Pittsylvania County
