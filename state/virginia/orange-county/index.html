Where Orange County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Orange County is in the east of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 12 miles from north to south and 29 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 22508 (Locust Grove) and 22923 (Barboursville), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Madison County (14 miles), Culpeper County (16 miles), Louisa County (19 miles) and Greene County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Orange County begin with 229 (5 codes), 225 (3 codes) and 224 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 229 is shared with 8 other counties and county-level areas, such as Albemarle County, Augusta County and Charlottesville (city), and has 49 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 428 square miles of land (1,110 sq km). The largest is 22960 (Orange) with 122 square miles, and the smallest is 22957 (Montpelier Station) with 1.4. The middle-sized code here covers 45.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Orange County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Orange County ranks 47th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Orange County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Burr Hill, VA 22433
  • Gordonsville, VA 22942

Questions about Orange County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Barboursville, VA?

22923 is the ZIP code listed for Barboursville in Orange County.

Do all ZIP codes in Orange County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 229, on 5 of the 9 codes.

Which ZIP code in Orange County covers the most land?

22960 (Orange), at about 122 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
