Where Fauquier County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Fauquier County is in the north-east of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 37 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 20130 (Paris) and 22720 (Goldvein), about 40 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Manassas (city) (17 miles), Rappahannock County (17 miles), Manassas Park (city) (20 miles) and Culpeper County (20 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Fauquier County begin with 201 (16 codes), 227 (6 codes) and 226 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 201 is shared with 6 other counties and county-level areas, such as Fairfax County, Loudoun County and Manassas (city), and has 80 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 16 of the 24 codes. Together they come to about 661 square miles of land (1,713 sq km). The largest is 20115 (Marshall) with 98.6 square miles, and the smallest is 20130 (Paris) with 11.1. The middle-sized code here covers 36.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Fauquier County; a code can reach across its border. 8 codes have no mapped area: 20116 (Marshall), 20128 (Orlean), 20138 (Calverton), 20139 (Casanova), 20140 (Rectortown) and 3 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Fauquier County ranks 7th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 24 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Fauquier County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Warrenton, VA 20186 (one of 3 codes for Warrenton; the street decides which)
  • Marshall, VA 20115 (one of 2 codes for Marshall; the street decides which)
  • Upperville, VA 20184 (one of 2 codes for Upperville; the street decides which)

Warrenton is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Fauquier County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Warrenton, VA?

Warrenton has 3 ZIP codes in Fauquier County: 20186, 20187, 20188. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Fauquier County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 201, on 16 of the 24 codes.

Which ZIP code in Fauquier County covers the most land?

20115 (Marshall), at about 98.6 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
