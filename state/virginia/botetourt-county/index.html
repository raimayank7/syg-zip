Where Botetourt County is

The ZIP codes of Botetourt County are found in the south of Virginia. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 28 miles, and from east to west about 15 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 24019 (Roanoke) and 24438 (Glen Wilton), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Roanoke (city) (17 miles), Salem (city) (19 miles), Roanoke County (20 miles) and Craig County (20 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Botetourt County begin with 240 (8 codes), 241 (2 codes) and 244 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 240 is shared with 11 other counties and county-level areas, such as Bedford County, Floyd County and Franklin County, and has 76 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 570 square miles of land (1,476 sq km). The largest is 24085 (Eagle Rock) with 166 square miles, and the smallest is 24077 (Cloverdale) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 57.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Botetourt County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 24050 (Roanoke), 24130 (Oriskany) and 24438 (Glen Wilton). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Botetourt County ranks 31st among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Botetourt County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Roanoke, VA 24019 (one of 2 codes for Roanoke; the street decides which)
  • Blue Ridge, VA 24064
  • Buchanan, VA 24066

ZIP codes named Roanoke are also listed under Montgomery County, Roanoke (city) and Roanoke County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Roanoke is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Botetourt County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Roanoke, VA?

Roanoke has 2 ZIP codes in Botetourt County: 24019, 24050. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Botetourt County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 240, on 8 of the 11 codes.

Which ZIP code in Botetourt County covers the most land?

24085 (Eagle Rock), at about 166 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
