Where Prince Edward County is

The ZIP codes of Prince Edward County are found in the south-east of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 14 miles from north to south and 15 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 23942 (Green Bay) and 23960 (Prospect), about 18 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Nottoway County (18 miles), Charlotte County (21 miles), Appomattox County (22 miles) and Amelia County (22 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 239. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 239 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Appomattox County, Brunswick County and Buckingham County, and has 38 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 425 square miles of land (1,100 sq km). The largest is 23901 (Farmville) with 193 square miles, and the smallest is 23909 (Farmville) with 0.10. The middle-sized code here covers 59.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Prince Edward County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Prince Edward County ranks 68th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Prince Edward County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Farmville, VA 23901 (one of 2 codes for Farmville; the street decides which)
  • Green Bay, VA 23942
  • Hampden Sydney, VA 23943
