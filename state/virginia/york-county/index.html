Where York County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of York County fall in the south-east of Virginia. Its codes spread over about 15 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 23090 (Lightfoot) and 23693 (Yorktown), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Newport News (city) (6.8 miles), Gloucester County (10.0 miles), Poquoson (city) (10 miles) and Williamsburg (city) (13 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of York County begin with 236 (6 codes) and 230 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 236 is shared with 3 other counties and county-level areas, Hampton (city), Newport News (city) and Poquoson (city), and has 30 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 5 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 60.9 square miles of land (158 sq km). The largest is 23692 (Yorktown) with 19.4 square miles, and the smallest is 23696 (Seaford) with 5.6. The middle-sized code here covers 11.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Virginia is 28.9. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 21.4 square miles, roughly 26% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of York County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 23090 (Lightfoot) and 23694 (Lackey). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, York County ranks 68th among the 133 counties and county-level areas of Virginia (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,214 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in York County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters VA and the ZIP code:

  • Yorktown, VA 23690 (one of 4 codes for Yorktown; the street decides which)
  • Lackey, VA 23694
  • Lightfoot, VA 23090

Yorktown is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about York County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Yorktown, VA?

Yorktown has 4 ZIP codes in York County: 23690, 23691, 23692, 23693. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in York County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 236, on 6 of the 7 codes.

Which ZIP code in York County covers the most land?

23692 (Yorktown), at about 19.4 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
