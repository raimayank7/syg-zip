Where Sheridan County is

The ZIP codes of Sheridan County are found in the north of Wyoming. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 60 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 82831 (Arvada) and 82838 (Parkman), about 63 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Johnson County (51 miles), Big Horn County, Montana (57 miles), Washakie County (66 miles) and Big Horn County (68 miles).

The first three digits

All 12 codes here begin with the same three digits, 828. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 828 is shared with 1 other county, Johnson County, and has 14 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,680 square miles of land (6,942 sq km). The largest is 82831 (Arvada) with 595 square miles, and the smallest is 82842 (Story) with 9.0. The middle-sized code here covers 127 square miles; the middle value for all of Wyoming is 163. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sheridan County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Sheridan County ranks 5th among the 23 counties and county-level areas of Wyoming (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 195 codes in the state list.
