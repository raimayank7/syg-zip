Where Natrona County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Natrona County fall in the east of Wyoming. Its codes spread over about 61 miles from north to south and 59 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 82620 (Alcova) and 82635 (Edgerton), about 66 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Converse County (66 miles), Johnson County (73 miles), Washakie County (90 miles) and Fremont County (102 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Natrona County starts with 826. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 826 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Carbon County, Converse County and Fremont County, and has 21 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 2,654 square miles of land (6,873 sq km). The largest is 82604 (Casper) with 1,048 square miles, and the smallest is 82644 (Mills) with 0.8. The middle-sized code here covers 101 square miles; the middle value for all of Wyoming is 163. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Natrona County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 82602 (Casper) and 82605 (Casper). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Natrona County ranks 3rd among the 23 counties and county-level areas of Wyoming (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 195 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Natrona County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WY and the ZIP code:

  • Casper, WY 82601 (one of 5 codes for Casper; the street decides which)
  • Alcova, WY 82620
  • Arminto, WY 82630
