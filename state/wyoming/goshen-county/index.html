Where Goshen County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Goshen County fall in the south-east of Wyoming. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 59 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 82219 (Jay Em) and 82221 (Lagrange), about 61 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Platte County (29 miles), Scotts Bluff County, Nebraska (35 miles), Banner County, Nebraska (45 miles) and Sioux County, Nebraska (51 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 822. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 822 is shared with 3 other counties, Converse County, Niobrara County and Platte County, and has 20 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 2,084 square miles of land (5,398 sq km). The largest is 82240 (Torrington) with 665 square miles, and the smallest is 82218 (Huntley) with 1.0. The middle-sized code here covers 162 square miles; the middle value for all of Wyoming is 163. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Goshen County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Goshen County ranks 10th among the 23 counties and county-level areas of Wyoming (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 195 codes in the state list.
