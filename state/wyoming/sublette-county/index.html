Where Sublette County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Sublette County fall in the west of Wyoming. Its codes spread over about 40 miles from north to south and 40 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 82922 (Bondurant) and 82923 (Boulder), about 55 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lincoln County (50 miles), Teton County (64 miles), Fremont County (73 miles) and Teton County, Idaho (83 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Sublette County begin with 829 (4 codes) and 831 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 829 is shared with 2 other counties, Sweetwater County and Uinta County, and has 21 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 3,970 square miles of land (10,282 sq km). The largest is 82923 (Boulder) with 999 square miles, and the smallest is 82922 (Bondurant) with 300. The middle-sized code here covers 687 square miles; the middle value for all of Wyoming is 163. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Sublette County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Sublette County ranks 16th among the 23 counties and county-level areas of Wyoming (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 195 codes in the state list.
