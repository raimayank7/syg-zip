Where Uinta County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Uinta County fall in the south-west of Wyoming. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 42 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 82930 (Evanston) and 82936 (Lonetree), about 44 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Rich County, Utah (52 miles), Daggett County, Utah (53 miles), Summit County, Utah (56 miles) and Morgan County, Utah (59 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 829. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 829 is shared with 2 other counties, Sublette County and Sweetwater County, and has 21 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 1,400 square miles of land (3,625 sq km). The largest is 82930 (Evanston) with 957 square miles, and the smallest is 82933 (Fort Bridger) with 77.1. The middle-sized code here covers 83.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Wyoming is 163. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Uinta County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 82931 (Evanston). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Uinta County ranks 12th among the 23 counties and county-level areas of Wyoming (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 195 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Uinta County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WY and the ZIP code:

  • Evanston, WY 82930 (one of 2 codes for Evanston; the street decides which)
  • Fort Bridger, WY 82933
  • Lonetree, WY 82936
