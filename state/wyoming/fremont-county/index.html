Where Fremont County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Fremont County fall in the west of Wyoming. Its codes spread over about 69 miles from north to south and 101 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 82310 (Jeffrey City) and 82513 (Dubois), about 113 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hot Springs County (49 miles), Sublette County (73 miles), Washakie County (76 miles) and Natrona County (102 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Fremont County begin with 825 (10 codes), 826 (2 codes) and 823 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 825, so those three digits alone point to Fremont County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 6,310 square miles of land (16,343 sq km). The largest is 82520 (Lander) with 1,493 square miles, and the smallest is 82515 (Hudson) with 8.8. The middle-sized code here covers 416 square miles; the middle value for all of Wyoming is 163. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Fremont County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 82524 (Saint Stephens). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Fremont County ranks 4th among the 23 counties and county-level areas of Wyoming (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 195 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Fremont County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WY and the ZIP code:

  • Arapahoe, WY 82510
  • Crowheart, WY 82512
  • Dubois, WY 82513

Arapahoe is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
