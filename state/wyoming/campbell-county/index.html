Where Campbell County is

The ZIP codes of Campbell County are found in the north-east of Wyoming. Its codes spread over about 72 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 82725 (Recluse) and 82732 (Wright), about 73 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Crook County (54 miles), Johnson County (57 miles), Weston County (60 miles) and Powder River County, Montana (72 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Campbell County starts with 827. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 827 is shared with 2 other counties, Crook County and Weston County, and has 18 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 3,926 square miles of land (10,168 sq km). The largest is 82718 (Gillette) with 1,597 square miles, and the smallest is 82732 (Wright) with 76.9. The middle-sized code here covers 421 square miles; the middle value for all of Wyoming is 163. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Campbell County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 82717 (Gillette). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Campbell County ranks 12th among the 23 counties and county-level areas of Wyoming (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 195 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Campbell County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WY and the ZIP code:

  • Gillette, WY 82716 (one of 3 codes for Gillette; the street decides which)
  • Recluse, WY 82725
  • Rozet, WY 82727
