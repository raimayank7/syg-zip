Where Converse County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Converse County fall in the east of Wyoming. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 54 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 82224 (Lost Springs) and 82637 (Glenrock), about 57 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Niobrara County (39 miles), Platte County (50 miles), Natrona County (66 miles) and Sioux County, Nebraska (71 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Converse County begin with 822 (2 codes) and 826 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 822 is shared with 3 other counties, Goshen County, Niobrara County and Platte County, and has 20 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 3,523 square miles of land (9,124 sq km). The largest is 82633 (Douglas) with 2,575 square miles, and the smallest is 82224 (Lost Springs) with 102. The middle-sized code here covers 423 square miles; the middle value for all of Wyoming is 163. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Converse County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Converse County ranks 18th among the 23 counties and county-level areas of Wyoming (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 195 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Converse County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters WY and the ZIP code:

  • Douglas, WY 82633
  • Glenrock, WY 82637
  • Lost Springs, WY 82224

Douglas is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
