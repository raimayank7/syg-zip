Where Midland County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Midland County is in the south-east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 17 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 48618 (Coleman) and 48674 (Midland), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Gladwin County (9.3 miles), Bay County (18 miles), Saginaw County (26 miles) and Isabella County (27 miles).

The first three digits

All 11 codes here begin with the same three digits, 486. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 486 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Arenac County, Bay County and Clare County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 410 square miles of land (1,061 sq km). The largest is 48640 (Midland) with 105 square miles, and the smallest is 48620 (Edenville) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 82.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Midland County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 48641 (Midland), 48667 (Midland), 48670 (Midland), 48674 (Midland) and 48686 (Midland). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Midland County ranks 36th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Midland County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Midland, MI 48640 (one of 7 codes for Midland; the street decides which)
  • Coleman, MI 48618
  • Edenville, MI 48620

Midland is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 11 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
