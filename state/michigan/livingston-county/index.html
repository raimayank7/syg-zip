Where Livingston County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Livingston County fall in the south-east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 21 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 48139 (Hamburg) and 48816 (Cohoctah), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Washtenaw County (23 miles), Shiawassee County (27 miles), Oakland County (29 miles) and Genesee County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Livingston County begin with 481 (6 codes), 488 (5 codes) and 483 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 481 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Monroe County, Oakland County and Washtenaw County, and has 82 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 455 square miles of land (1,180 sq km). The largest is 48836 (Fowlerville) with 94.0 square miles, and the smallest is 48143 (Lakeland) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 36.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Water adds about 18.3 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Livingston County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 48844 (Howell). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Livingston County ranks 31st among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Livingston County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Howell, MI 48843 (one of 3 codes for Howell; the street decides which)
  • Brighton, MI 48114 (one of 2 codes for Brighton; the street decides which)
  • Cohoctah, MI 48816

Questions about Livingston County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Howell, MI?

Howell has 3 ZIP codes in Livingston County: 48843, 48844, 48855. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Livingston County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 481, on 6 of the 12 codes.

Which ZIP code in Livingston County covers the most land?

48836 (Fowlerville), at about 94.0 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
