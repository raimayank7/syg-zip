Where St. Joseph County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of St. Joseph County fall in the south of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 16 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 49066 (Leonidas) and 49099 (White Pigeon), about 23 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are LaGrange County, Indiana (21 miles), Branch County (22 miles), Cass County (23 miles) and Kalamazoo County (24 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of St. Joseph County starts with 490. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 490 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Allegan County, Barry County and Berrien County, and has 92 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 513 square miles of land (1,330 sq km). The largest is 49093 (Three Rivers) with 106 square miles, and the smallest is 49075 (Nottawa) with 0.05. The middle-sized code here covers 53.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Water adds about 21.0 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of St. Joseph County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, St. Joseph County ranks 39th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in St. Joseph County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Centreville, MI 49032
  • Colon, MI 49040
