Where Marquette County is

The ZIP codes of Marquette County are found in the north-west of Michigan. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 53 miles, and from east to west about 40 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 49808 (Big Bay) and 49819 (Arnold), about 54 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Dickinson County (39 miles), Alger County (41 miles), Baraga County (44 miles) and Iron County (49 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Marquette County starts with 498. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 498 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Alger County, Delta County and Dickinson County, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 1,797 square miles of land (4,654 sq km). The largest is 49849 (Ishpeming) with 241 square miles, and the smallest is 49833 (Little Lake) with 14.2. The middle-sized code here covers 184 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 138 square miles, roughly 7% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Marquette County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 49819 (Arnold) and 49865 (National Mine). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Marquette County ranks 28th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Marquette County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Arnold, MI 49819
  • Big Bay, MI 49808
  • Champion, MI 49814

Arnold is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
