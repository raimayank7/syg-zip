Where Shiawassee County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Shiawassee County is in the south-east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 19 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 48460 (New Lothrop) and 48882 (Shaftsburg), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Genesee County (22 miles), Clinton County (23 miles), Ingham County (25 miles) and Livingston County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Shiawassee County begin with 488 (7 codes) and 484 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 488 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Barry County, Clinton County and Eaton County, and has 88 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 527 square miles of land (1,366 sq km). The largest is 48867 (Owosso) with 136 square miles, and the smallest is 48476 (Vernon) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 41.0 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Shiawassee County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 48882 (Shaftsburg). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Shiawassee County ranks 31st among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Shiawassee County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Bancroft, MI 48414
  • Byron, MI 48418
  • Corunna, MI 48817

Bancroft is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
