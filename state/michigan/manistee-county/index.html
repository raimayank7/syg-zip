Where Manistee County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Manistee County sits near the middle of Michigan. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 21 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 49625 (Copemish) and 49634 (Filer City), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Benzie County (21 miles), Mason County (25 miles), Wexford County (26 miles) and Lake County (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 10 codes here begin with the same three digits, 496. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 496 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Antrim County, Benzie County and Grand Traverse County, and has 70 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 558 square miles of land (1,445 sq km). The largest is 49660 (Manistee) with 171 square miles, and the smallest is 49634 (Filer City) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 51.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Water adds about 30.7 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Manistee County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Manistee County ranks 39th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Manistee County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Arcadia, MI 49613
  • Bear Lake, MI 49614
  • Brethren, MI 49619

Arcadia is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 13 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
