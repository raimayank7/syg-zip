Where Delta County is

The ZIP codes of Delta County are found in the north of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 25 miles from north to south and 31 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 49835 (Garden) and 49880 (Rock), about 34 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Menominee County (33 miles), Alger County (38 miles), Dickinson County (44 miles) and Schoolcraft County (45 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Delta County starts with 498. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 498 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Alger County, Dickinson County and Keweenaw County, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 1,247 square miles of land (3,229 sq km). The largest is 49878 (Rapid River) with 456 square miles, and the smallest is 49894 (Wells) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 76.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 72.5 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Delta County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Delta County ranks 39th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 10 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.
