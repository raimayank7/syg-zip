Where Kalamazoo County is

The ZIP codes of Kalamazoo County are found in the south of Michigan. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 18 miles, and from east to west about 19 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 49012 (Augusta) and 49087 (Schoolcraft), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Calhoun County (23 miles), St. Joseph County (24 miles), Van Buren County (25 miles) and Barry County (27 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Kalamazoo County starts with 490. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 490 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Allegan County, Barry County and Berrien County, and has 92 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 18 of the 24 codes. Together they come to about 555 square miles of land (1,438 sq km). The largest is 49009 (Kalamazoo) with 102 square miles, and the smallest is 49074 (Nazareth) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 28.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Kalamazoo County; a code can reach across its border. 6 codes have no mapped area: 49003 (Kalamazoo), 49005 (Kalamazoo), 49019 (Kalamazoo), 49041 (Comstock), 49077 (Oshtemo) and 1 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Kalamazoo County ranks 8th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 24 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Kalamazoo County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Kalamazoo, MI 49001 (one of 10 codes for Kalamazoo; the street decides which)
  • Portage, MI 49002 (one of 3 codes for Portage; the street decides which)
  • Augusta, MI 49012
