Where Branch County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Branch County is in the south-east of Michigan. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 18 miles, and from east to west about 20 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 49089 (Sherwood) and 49255 (Montgomery), about 25 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Steuben County, Indiana (21 miles), Calhoun County (21 miles), St. Joseph County (22 miles) and Hillsdale County (24 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Branch County begin with 490 (5 codes) and 492 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 490 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Allegan County, Barry County and Berrien County, and has 92 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 489 square miles of land (1,267 sq km). The largest is 49036 (Coldwater) with 154 square miles, and the smallest is 49089 (Sherwood) with 37.8. The middle-sized code here covers 70.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Branch County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Branch County ranks 62nd among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Branch County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Bronson, MI 49028
  • Coldwater, MI 49036
  • Montgomery, MI 49255

Bronson is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
