Where Saginaw County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Saginaw County fall in the south-east of Michigan. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 25 miles, and from east to west about 29 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 48637 (Merrill) and 48734 (Frankenmuth), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Bay County (22 miles), Midland County (26 miles), Genesee County (29 miles) and Tuscola County (30 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Saginaw County begin with 486 (17 codes), 487 (4 codes) and 484 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 486 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Arenac County, Bay County and Clare County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 18 of the 23 codes. Together they come to about 795 square miles of land (2,060 sq km). The largest is 48601 (Saginaw) with 104 square miles, and the smallest is 48724 (Carrollton) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 37.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Saginaw County; a code can reach across its border. 5 codes have no mapped area: 48605 (Saginaw), 48606 (Saginaw), 48608 (Saginaw), 48663 (Saginaw) and 48787 (Frankenmuth). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Saginaw County ranks 9th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 23 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Saginaw County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Saginaw, MI 48601 (one of 10 codes for Saginaw; the street decides which)
  • Frankenmuth, MI 48734 (one of 2 codes for Frankenmuth; the street decides which)
  • Birch Run, MI 48415

ZIP codes named Saginaw are also listed under Gladwin County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Saginaw is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
