Where Iosco County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Iosco County is in the east of Michigan. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 25 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 48750 (Oscoda) and 48770 (Whittemore), about 26 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Arenac County (21 miles), Ogemaw County (21 miles), Alcona County (25 miles) and Oscoda County (35 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Iosco County starts with 487. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 487 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Alcona County, Arenac County and Bay County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 482 square miles of land (1,247 sq km). The largest is 48750 (Oscoda) with 104 square miles, and the smallest is 48743 (Long Lake) with 2.2. The middle-sized code here covers 81.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 29.1 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Iosco County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 48764 (Tawas City). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Iosco County ranks 53rd among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Iosco County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:
