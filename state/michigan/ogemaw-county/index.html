Where Ogemaw County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Ogemaw County fall in the east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 25 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 48610 (Alger) and 48761 (South Branch), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Arenac County (21 miles), Iosco County (21 miles), Oscoda County (26 miles) and Roscommon County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Ogemaw County begin with 486 (4 codes) and 487 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 486 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Arenac County, Bay County and Clare County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 701 square miles of land (1,815 sq km). The largest is 48661 (West Branch) with 264 square miles, and the smallest is 48635 (Lupton) with 53.1. The middle-sized code here covers 89.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ogemaw County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Ogemaw County ranks 62nd among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Ogemaw County
