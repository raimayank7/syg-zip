Where Lapeer County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Lapeer County is in the south-east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 48003 (Almont) and 48760 (Silverwood), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Genesee County (24 miles), Tuscola County (27 miles), Sanilac County (33 miles) and St. Clair County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Lapeer County begin with 484 (9 codes), 487 (2 codes) and 480 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 484 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Genesee County, Huron County and Oakland County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 575 square miles of land (1,488 sq km). The largest is 48446 (Lapeer) with 122 square miles, and the smallest is 48440 (Hadley) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 37.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lapeer County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lapeer County ranks 31st among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lapeer County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:
