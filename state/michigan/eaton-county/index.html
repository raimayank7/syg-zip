Where Eaton County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Eaton County is in the south-east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 48917 (Lansing) and 49021 (Bellevue), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Ingham County (18 miles), Barry County (24 miles), Clinton County (27 miles) and Ionia County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Eaton County begin with 488 (7 codes), 490 (3 codes) and 489 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 488 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Barry County, Clinton County and Gratiot County, and has 88 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 11 of the 12 codes. Together they come to about 690 square miles of land (1,787 sq km). The largest is 48813 (Charlotte) with 168 square miles, and the smallest is 48876 (Potterville) with 13.5. The middle-sized code here covers 57.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Eaton County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 48908 (Lansing). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Eaton County ranks 31st among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 12 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Eaton County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Lansing, MI 48908 (one of 2 codes for Lansing; the street decides which)
  • Bellevue, MI 49021
  • Charlotte, MI 48813

ZIP codes named Lansing are also listed under Ingham County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Lansing is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Eaton County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Lansing, MI?

Lansing has 2 ZIP codes in Eaton County: 48908, 48917. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Eaton County start with the same digits?

No. They use 3 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 488, on 7 of the 12 codes.

Which ZIP code in Eaton County covers the most land?

48813 (Charlotte), at about 168 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
