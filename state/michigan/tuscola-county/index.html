Where Tuscola County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Tuscola County is in the south-east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 27 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 48735 (Gagetown) and 48746 (Millington), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Lapeer County (27 miles), Saginaw County (30 miles), Bay County (31 miles) and Huron County (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Tuscola County begin with 487 (13 codes) and 484 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 487 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Alcona County, Arenac County and Bay County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 14 codes. Together they come to about 885 square miles of land (2,291 sq km). The largest is 48723 (Caro) with 132 square miles, and the smallest is 48435 (Fostoria) with 26.1. The middle-sized code here covers 55.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Tuscola County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 48758 (Richville). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Tuscola County ranks 26th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 14 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Tuscola County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Akron, MI 48701
  • Caro, MI 48723
  • Cass City, MI 48726

Akron is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
