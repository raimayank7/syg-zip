Where Barry County is

The ZIP codes of Barry County are found in the south-east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 24 miles from north to south and 18 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 49060 (Hickory Corners) and 49325 (Freeport), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Eaton County (24 miles), Kalamazoo County (27 miles), Calhoun County (28 miles) and Allegan County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Barry County begin with 490 (6 codes), 493 (2 codes) and 488 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 490 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Allegan County, Berrien County and Branch County, and has 92 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 8 of the 9 codes. Together they come to about 482 square miles of land (1,248 sq km). The largest is 49058 (Hastings) with 148 square miles, and the smallest is 49060 (Hickory Corners) with 19.7. The middle-sized code here covers 51.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Water adds about 19.2 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Barry County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 49035 (Cloverdale). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Barry County ranks 47th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Barry County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Cloverdale, MI 49035
  • Delton, MI 49046
  • Dowling, MI 49050

Cloverdale is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
