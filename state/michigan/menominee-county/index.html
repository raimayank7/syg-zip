Where Menominee County is

The ZIP codes of Menominee County are found in the north-west of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 49 miles from north to south and 13 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 49858 (Menominee) and 49873 (Perronville), about 49 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Marinette County, Wisconsin (26 miles), Dickinson County (31 miles), Delta County (33 miles) and Door County, Wisconsin (39 miles).

The first three digits

All 13 codes here begin with the same three digits, 498. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 498 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Alger County, Delta County and Dickinson County, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 12 of the 13 codes. Together they come to about 962 square miles of land (2,491 sq km). The largest is 49887 (Stephenson) with 206 square miles, and the smallest is 49863 (Nadeau) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 67.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Menominee County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 49845 (Harris). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Menominee County ranks 28th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 13 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.
