Where Monroe County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Monroe County is in the south-east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 23 miles from north to south and 22 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 48179 (South Rockwood) and 49267 (Ottawa Lake), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Lucas County, Ohio (17 miles), Washtenaw County (27 miles), Lenawee County (28 miles) and Ottawa County, Ohio (32 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Monroe County begin with 481 (16 codes) and 492 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 481 is shared with 4 other counties, such as Livingston County, Oakland County and Washtenaw County, and has 82 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 18 codes. Together they come to about 567 square miles of land (1,468 sq km). The largest is 48160 (Milan) with 77.9 square miles, and the smallest is 48177 (Samaria) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 32.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Water adds about 17.6 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Monroe County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 48110 (Azalia). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Monroe County ranks 15th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Monroe County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Monroe, MI 48161 (one of 2 codes for Monroe; the street decides which)
  • Azalia, MI 48110
  • Carleton, MI 48117

Monroe is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 20 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Monroe County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Monroe, MI?

Monroe has 2 ZIP codes in Monroe County: 48161, 48162. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Monroe County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 481, on 16 of the 18 codes.

Which ZIP code in Monroe County covers the most land?

48160 (Milan), at about 77.9 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
