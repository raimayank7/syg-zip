Where Oakland County is

The ZIP codes of Oakland County are found in the south-east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 27 miles from north to south and 29 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 48178 (South Lyon) and 48367 (Leonard), about 38 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Macomb County (19 miles), Wayne County (21 miles), Livingston County (29 miles) and Washtenaw County (33 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Oakland County begin with 483 (57 codes), 480 (24 codes), 481 (2 codes), 482 (2 codes) and 484 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 483 is shared with 2 other counties, Livingston County and Macomb County, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 67 of the 87 codes. Together they come to about 904 square miles of land (2,342 sq km). The largest is 48442 (Holly) with 85.9 square miles, and the smallest is 48069 (Pleasant Ridge) with 0.6. The middle-sized code here covers 9.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Small code areas usually go with towns and suburbs where homes stand close together. Water adds about 41.0 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Oakland County; a code can reach across its border. 20 codes have no mapped area: 48007 (Troy), 48012 (Birmingham), 48037 (Southfield), 48068 (Royal Oak), 48086 (Southfield) and 15 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Oakland County ranks 2nd among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 87 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Oakland County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Farmington, MI 48331 (one of 6 codes for Farmington; the street decides which)
  • Southfield, MI 48033 (one of 6 codes for Southfield; the street decides which)
  • Troy, MI 48007 (one of 6 codes for Troy; the street decides which)

Farmington is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 17 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
