Where Newaygo County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Newaygo County is in the south of Michigan. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 10 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 49309 (Bitely) and 49327 (Grant), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Muskegon County (25 miles), Oceana County (25 miles), Mecosta County (27 miles) and Lake County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Newaygo County begin with 493 (5 codes) and 494 (2 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 493 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Allegan County, Barry County and Isabella County, and has 49 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 663 square miles of land (1,716 sq km). The largest is 49349 (White Cloud) with 197 square miles, and the smallest is 49312 (Brohman) with 26.3. The middle-sized code here covers 110 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 20.7 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Newaygo County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 49413 (Fremont). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Newaygo County ranks 56th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Newaygo County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Fremont, MI 49412 (one of 2 codes for Fremont; the street decides which)
  • Bitely, MI 49309
  • Brohman, MI 49312

Fremont is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Newaygo County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Fremont, MI?

Fremont has 2 ZIP codes in Newaygo County: 49412, 49413. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Newaygo County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 493, on 5 of the 7 codes.

Which ZIP code in Newaygo County covers the most land?

49349 (White Cloud), at about 197 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
