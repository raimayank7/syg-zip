Where Mason County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Mason County sits near the middle of Michigan. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 12 miles, and from east to west about 16 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 49431 (Ludington) and 49458 (Walhalla), about 16 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Lake County (21 miles), Oceana County (23 miles), Manistee County (25 miles) and Newaygo County (38 miles).

The first three digits

All 6 codes here begin with the same three digits, 494. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 494 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Allegan County, Kent County and Lake County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 386 square miles of land (1,001 sq km). The largest is 49411 (Free Soil) with 110 square miles, and the smallest is 49458 (Walhalla) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 71.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Water adds about 24.9 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Mason County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Mason County ranks 62nd among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 6 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Mason County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Custer, MI 49405
  • Fountain, MI 49410
  • Free Soil, MI 49411

Custer is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 5 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
