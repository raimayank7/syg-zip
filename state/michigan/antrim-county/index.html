Where Antrim County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Antrim County fall in the east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 21 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 49611 (Alba) and 49629 (Elk Rapids), about 22 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Kalkaska County (18 miles), Charlevoix County (22 miles), Grand Traverse County (25 miles) and Leelanau County (28 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Antrim County begin with 496 (8 codes) and 497 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 496 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Benzie County, Grand Traverse County and Kalkaska County, and has 70 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 385 square miles of land (998 sq km). The largest is 49659 (Mancelona) with 175 square miles, and the smallest is 49627 (Eastport) with 1.6. The middle-sized code here covers 27.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Water adds about 52.5 square miles, roughly 12% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Antrim County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Antrim County ranks 47th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Antrim County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Alba, MI 49611
  • Alden, MI 49612
  • Bellaire, MI 49615

Alba is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
