Where Montcalm County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Montcalm County fall in the south-east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 33 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 48811 (Carson City) and 49329 (Howard City), about 35 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Ionia County (23 miles), Isabella County (25 miles), Gratiot County (26 miles) and Mecosta County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Montcalm County begin with 488 (13 codes) and 493 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 488 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Barry County, Clinton County and Eaton County, and has 88 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 18 codes. Together they come to about 790 square miles of land (2,046 sq km). The largest is 48838 (Greenville) with 93.1 square miles, and the smallest is 48852 (Mcbrides) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 40.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Montcalm County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 48812 (Cedar Lake). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Montcalm County ranks 15th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 18 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.
