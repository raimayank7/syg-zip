Where Benzie County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Benzie County fall close to the middle of Michigan. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 15 miles, and from east to west about 18 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 49628 (Elberta) and 49650 (Lake Ann), about 20 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Manistee County (21 miles), Grand Traverse County (25 miles), Leelanau County (26 miles) and Wexford County (29 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Benzie County starts with 496. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 496 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Antrim County, Grand Traverse County and Kalkaska County, and has 70 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 295 square miles of land (765 sq km). The largest is 49683 (Thompsonville) with 86.6 square miles, and the smallest is 49628 (Elberta) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 38.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Water adds about 35.0 square miles, roughly 11% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Benzie County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Benzie County ranks 56th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Benzie County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Elberta, MI 49628
