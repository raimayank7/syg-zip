Where Houghton County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Houghton County is in the north-west of Michigan. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 54 miles, and from east to west about 27 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 49917 (Copper City) and 49961 (Sidnaw), about 56 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Baraga County (24 miles), Keweenaw County (26 miles), Ontonagon County (42 miles) and Marquette County (61 miles).

The first three digits

All 17 codes here begin with the same three digits, 499. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 499 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Baraga County, Gogebic County and Iron County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 982 square miles of land (2,542 sq km). The largest is 49965 (Toivola) with 192 square miles, and the smallest is 49921 (Dodgeville) with 0.5. The middle-sized code here covers 49.5 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Water adds about 60.0 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Houghton County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Houghton County ranks 18th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.
