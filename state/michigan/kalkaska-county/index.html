ZIP codes in Kalkaska County, Michigan

Where Kalkaska County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Kalkaska County is in the east of Michigan. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 14 miles, and from east to west about 8.5 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 49676 (Rapid City) and 49680 (South Boardman), about 14 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Grand Traverse County (17 miles), Antrim County (18 miles), Crawford County (27 miles) and Missaukee County (31 miles).

The first three digits

All 3 codes here begin with the same three digits, 496. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 496 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Antrim County, Benzie County and Grand Traverse County, and has 70 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 316 square miles of land (817 sq km). The largest is 49646 (Kalkaska) with 219 square miles, and the smallest is 49676 (Rapid City) with 41.0. The middle-sized code here covers 55.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Water adds about 20.3 square miles, roughly 6% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Kalkaska County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Kalkaska County ranks 80th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 3 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Kalkaska County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Kalkaska, MI 49646
  • Rapid City, MI 49676
  • South Boardman, MI 49680
