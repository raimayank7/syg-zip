Where Gladwin County is

The ZIP codes of Gladwin County are found in the south-east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 41 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 48624 (Gladwin) and 48638 (Saginaw), about 48 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Midland County (9.3 miles), Bay County (18 miles), Arenac County (28 miles) and Clare County (30 miles).

The first three digits

All 4 codes here begin with the same three digits, 486. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 486 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Arenac County, Bay County and Clare County, and has 60 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 532 square miles of land (1,378 sq km). The largest is 48624 (Gladwin) with 341 square miles, and the smallest is 48638 (Saginaw) with 6.5. The middle-sized code here covers 92.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Gladwin County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Gladwin County ranks 76th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 4 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Gladwin County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Beaverton, MI 48612
  • Gladwin, MI 48624
  • Rhodes, MI 48652

Saginaw shares a name with a place in another part of Michigan; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.
