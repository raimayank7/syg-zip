Where Ottawa County is

The ZIP codes of Ottawa County are found in the south of Michigan. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 26 miles, and from east to west about 23 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 49403 (Conklin) and 49434 (Macatawa), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Kent County (21 miles), Muskegon County (25 miles), Allegan County (25 miles) and Newaygo County (41 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Ottawa County begin with 494 (19 codes) and 495 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 494 is shared with 7 other counties, such as Allegan County, Kent County and Lake County, and has 57 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 17 of the 20 codes. Together they come to about 640 square miles of land (1,658 sq km). The largest is 49423 (Holland) with 77.9 square miles, and the smallest is 49434 (Macatawa) with 0.09. The middle-sized code here covers 35.3 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Water adds about 36.9 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ottawa County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 49422 (Holland), 49427 (Jamestown) and 49429 (Jenison). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Ottawa County ranks 11th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 20 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Ottawa County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Holland, MI 49422 (one of 3 codes for Holland; the street decides which)
  • Jenison, MI 49428 (one of 2 codes for Jenison; the street decides which)
  • Allendale, MI 49401

Holland is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 9 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name. Grand Rapids shares a name with a place in another part of Michigan; there the ZIP code tells the two apart.

Questions about Ottawa County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Holland, MI?

Holland has 3 ZIP codes in Ottawa County: 49422, 49423, 49424. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Ottawa County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 494, on 19 of the 20 codes.

Which ZIP code in Ottawa County covers the most land?

49423 (Holland), at about 77.9 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
