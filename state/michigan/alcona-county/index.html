Where Alcona County is

The ZIP codes of Alcona County are found in the east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 19 miles from north to south and 25 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 48728 (Curran) and 48738 (Greenbush), about 28 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Alpena County (22 miles), Iosco County (25 miles), Oscoda County (31 miles) and Montmorency County (37 miles).

The first three digits

All 9 codes here begin with the same three digits, 487. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 487 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Arenac County, Bay County and Huron County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 623 square miles of land (1,614 sq km). The largest is 48728 (Curran) with 130 square miles, and the smallest is 48738 (Greenbush) with 12.6. The middle-sized code here covers 67.9 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Water adds about 22.2 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Alcona County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Alcona County ranks 47th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 9 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Alcona County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:
