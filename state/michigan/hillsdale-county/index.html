Where Hillsdale County is

The ZIP codes of Hillsdale County are found in the south-east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 20 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 49232 (Camden) and 49281 (Somerset), about 27 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Jackson County (23 miles), Branch County (24 miles), Williams County, Ohio (25 miles) and Lenawee County (26 miles).

The first three digits

All 16 codes here begin with the same three digits, 492. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 492 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Branch County, Calhoun County and Ingham County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 13 of the 16 codes. Together they come to about 553 square miles of land (1,433 sq km). The largest is 49242 (Hillsdale) with 90.6 square miles, and the smallest is 49258 (Mosherville) with 0.01. The middle-sized code here covers 42.7 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Hillsdale County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 49239 (Frontier), 49257 (Moscow) and 49281 (Somerset). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Hillsdale County ranks 21st among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 16 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Hillsdale County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Allen, MI 49227
  • Camden, MI 49232
  • Frontier, MI 49239

Allen is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 7 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
