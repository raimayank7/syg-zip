Where Lenawee County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Lenawee County fall in the south-east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 22 miles from north to south and 28 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 49233 (Cement City) and 49276 (Riga), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Fulton County, Ohio (21 miles), Hillsdale County (26 miles), Monroe County (28 miles) and Jackson County (29 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Lenawee County starts with 492. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 492 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Branch County, Calhoun County and Hillsdale County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 816 square miles of land (2,113 sq km). The largest is 49221 (Adrian) with 120 square miles, and the smallest is 49289 (Weston) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 32.4 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Lenawee County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Lenawee County ranks 12th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Lenawee County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Addison, MI 49220
  • Adrian, MI 49221
  • Blissfield, MI 49228

Addison is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 6 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
