Where Genesee County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Genesee County is in the south-east of Michigan. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 22 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 48430 (Fenton) and 48463 (Otisville), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Shiawassee County (22 miles), Lapeer County (24 miles), Saginaw County (29 miles) and Livingston County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Genesee County begin with 485 (20 codes) and 484 (16 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. No other county-level area in this list uses 485, so those three digits alone point to Genesee County.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 25 of the 36 codes. Together they come to about 731 square miles of land (1,893 sq km). The largest is 48430 (Fenton) with 83.2 square miles, and the smallest is 48411 (Atlas) with 0.3. The middle-sized code here covers 24.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Genesee County; a code can reach across its border. 11 codes have no mapped area: 48480 (Grand Blanc), 48501 (Flint), 48531 (Flint), 48550 (Flint), 48551 (Flint) and 6 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Genesee County ranks 5th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 36 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Genesee County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Flint, MI 48501 (one of 17 codes for Flint; the street decides which)
  • Burton, MI 48509 (one of 3 codes for Burton; the street decides which)
  • Grand Blanc, MI 48439 (one of 2 codes for Grand Blanc; the street decides which)
