Where St. Clair County is

The ZIP codes of St. Clair County are found in the south-east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 37 miles from north to south and 26 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 48028 (Harsens Island) and 48097 (Yale), about 39 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Macomb County (26 miles), Lapeer County (33 miles), Sanilac County (36 miles) and Oakland County (40 miles).

The first three digits

All 23 codes here begin with the same three digits, 480. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 480 is shared with 3 other counties, Lapeer County, Macomb County and Oakland County, and has 79 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 21 of the 23 codes. Together they come to about 736 square miles of land (1,907 sq km). The largest is 48097 (Yale) with 76.2 square miles, and the smallest is 48040 (Marysville) with 6.9. The middle-sized code here covers 36.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Water adds about 39.9 square miles, roughly 5% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of St. Clair County; a code can reach across its border. 2 codes have no mapped area: 48004 (Anchorville) and 48061 (Port Huron). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, St. Clair County ranks 9th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 23 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in St. Clair County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Port Huron, MI 48060 (one of 2 codes for Port Huron; the street decides which)
  • Algonac, MI 48001
  • Allenton, MI 48002

Questions about St. Clair County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Port Huron, MI?

Port Huron has 2 ZIP codes in St. Clair County: 48060, 48061. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in St. Clair County start with the same digits?

Yes. All 23 start with 480.

Which ZIP code in St. Clair County covers the most land?

48097 (Yale), at about 76.2 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
