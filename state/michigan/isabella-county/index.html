Where Isabella County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Isabella County is in the south-east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 11 miles from north to south and 24 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 48883 (Shepherd) and 49310 (Blanchard), about 24 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Clare County (21 miles), Gratiot County (23 miles), Montcalm County (25 miles) and Mecosta County (25 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Isabella County begin with 488 (7 codes) and 493 (1 code). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 488 is shared with 9 other counties, such as Barry County, Clinton County and Eaton County, and has 88 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 7 of the 8 codes. Together they come to about 461 square miles of land (1,193 sq km). The largest is 48858 (Mount Pleasant) with 174 square miles, and the smallest is 48859 (Mount Pleasant) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 51.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Isabella County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 48804 (Mount Pleasant). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Isabella County ranks 53rd among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 8 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Isabella County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Mount Pleasant, MI 48804 (one of 3 codes for Mount Pleasant; the street decides which)
  • Blanchard, MI 49310
  • Rosebush, MI 48878

Mount Pleasant is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 10 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Isabella County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Mount Pleasant, MI?

Mount Pleasant has 3 ZIP codes in Isabella County: 48804, 48858, 48859. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Isabella County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 488, on 7 of the 8 codes.

Which ZIP code in Isabella County covers the most land?

48858 (Mount Pleasant), at about 174 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
