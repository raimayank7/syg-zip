Where Dickinson County is

The ZIP codes of Dickinson County are found in the north-west of Michigan. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 27 miles, and from east to west about 16 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 49815 (Channing) and 49892 (Vulcan), about 30 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Florence County, Wisconsin (25 miles), Menominee County (31 miles), Iron County (32 miles) and Marinette County, Wisconsin (38 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Dickinson County starts with 498. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 498 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Alger County, Delta County and Keweenaw County, and has 68 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 851 square miles of land (2,205 sq km). The largest is 49892 (Vulcan) with 154 square miles, and the smallest is 49852 (Loretto) with 0.2. The middle-sized code here covers 76.2 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Dickinson County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Dickinson County ranks 36th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Dickinson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:
