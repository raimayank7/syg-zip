Where Alpena County is

Going by the map points of its ZIP codes, Alpena County is in the east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 49753 (Lachine) and 49766 (Ossineke), about 17 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Alcona County (22 miles), Montmorency County (27 miles), Presque Isle County (27 miles) and Oscoda County (33 miles).

The first three digits

All 5 codes here begin with the same three digits, 497. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 497 is shared with 10 other counties, such as Antrim County, Charlevoix County and Cheboygan County, and has 76 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for every code here. Together they come to about 559 square miles of land (1,447 sq km). The largest is 49707 (Alpena) with 215 square miles, and the smallest is 49744 (Herron) with 37.3. The middle-sized code here covers 103 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 52.2 square miles, roughly 9% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Alpena County; a code can reach across its border.

By number of ZIP codes, Alpena County ranks 72nd among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 5 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Alpena County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Herron, MI 49744
