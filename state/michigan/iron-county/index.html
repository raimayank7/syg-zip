Where Iron County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Iron County fall in the north-west of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 13 miles from north to south and 14 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 49903 (Amasa) and 49915 (Caspian), about 15 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Florence County, Wisconsin (17 miles), Dickinson County (32 miles), Forest County, Wisconsin (37 miles) and Baraga County (42 miles).

The first three digits

All 7 codes here begin with the same three digits, 499. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 499 is shared with 5 other counties, such as Baraga County, Gogebic County and Houghton County, and has 47 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 6 of the 7 codes. Together they come to about 1,034 square miles of land (2,679 sq km). The largest is 49935 (Iron River) with 457 square miles, and the smallest is 49902 (Alpha) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 86.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Large code areas usually go with open country, where one post office serves a wide district. Water adds about 41.6 square miles, roughly 4% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Iron County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 49964 (Stambaugh). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Iron County ranks 56th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 7 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Iron County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Alpha, MI 49902
  • Amasa, MI 49903
  • Caspian, MI 49915

Alpha is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 4 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
