Where Ingham County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Ingham County fall in the south-east of Michigan. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 22 miles, and from east to west about 21 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 48906 (Lansing) and 49285 (Stockbridge), about 29 miles from each other in a straight line. Measured between the middle points of their codes, the closest neighbours are Eaton County (18 miles), Clinton County (20 miles), Shiawassee County (25 miles) and Livingston County (31 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Ingham County begin with 489 (20 codes), 488 (12 codes) and 492 (3 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 489 is shared with 1 other county, Eaton County, and has 22 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 19 of the 35 codes. Together they come to about 610 square miles of land (1,580 sq km). The largest is 48854 (Mason) with 113 square miles, and the smallest is 48825 (East Lansing) with 0.7. The middle-sized code here covers 20.1 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Ingham County; a code can reach across its border. 16 codes have no mapped area: 48805 (Okemos), 48826 (East Lansing), 48901 (Lansing), 48909 (Lansing), 48913 (Lansing) and 11 more. Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Ingham County ranks 6th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 35 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Ingham County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Lansing, MI 48901 (one of 20 codes for Lansing; the street decides which)
  • East Lansing, MI 48823 (one of 4 codes for East Lansing; the street decides which)
  • Okemos, MI 48805 (one of 2 codes for Okemos; the street decides which)

ZIP codes named Lansing are also listed under Eaton County, so the same town name can turn up on more than one page of this site. Lansing is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 8 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
