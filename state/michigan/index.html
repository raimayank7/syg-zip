Michigan ZIP codes in brief

This list holds 1,159 ZIP codes for Michigan, about 2.8% of the 40,977 on this site, which puts it 9th among the 51 state lists by size. They are spread over 83 counties and county-level areas. The most codes are in Wayne County (94), Oakland County (87), Kent County (46), Macomb County (41) and Genesee County (36). At the other end, 1 has only one or two codes, for example Luce County.

Prefixes and first digits

The first three digits of a ZIP code name a mail sorting area. Michigan uses 20 of these prefixes, from 480 to 499. The busiest are 490 (92 codes, most of them for Kalamazoo), 488 (88 codes, most of them for East Lansing), 481 (82 codes, most of them for Ann Arbor) and 480 (79 codes, most of them for Southfield). The first digit 4 is shared with Indiana, Kentucky and Ohio.

Largest, smallest and farthest

The Census Bureau draws an area for 992 of the 1,159 codes. The largest is 49868 (Newberry, Luce County) at 707 square miles and the smallest is 48243 (Detroit) at 0.01; the middle value is 37.0 square miles. The other 167 have no mapped area and are normally codes for post office boxes or single buildings. By map point, the northernmost code is 49918 (Copper Harbor) and the southernmost 49232 (Camden); the one farthest east is 48061 (Port Huron) and farthest west 49938 (Ironwood).

Place names and addresses in Michigan

Of the 881 place names in the list, 804 have a single ZIP code. The longest lists belong to Detroit (46), Grand Rapids (26), Lansing (22) and Flint (17). 3 place names appear under more than one county, among them Grand Rapids, Lansing and Saginaw: there the ZIP code, not the name, tells you which is meant. In an address, write the city, a comma, the letters MI and the code, as in Detroit, MI 48201. The name of the county is left out.

Questions about Michigan ZIP codes

What ZIP codes does Michigan use?

In this list, Michigan ZIP codes run from 48001 to 49971 and start with 4. A few codes inside that range can belong to a neighbouring state near the border.

Which place in Michigan has the most ZIP codes?

Detroit, with 46 codes in this list.
