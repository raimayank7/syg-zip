Where Bay County is

The ZIP codes of Bay County are found in the south-east of Michigan. From the northernmost code to the southernmost is about 28 miles, and from east to west about 17 miles. The two codes farthest apart are 48613 (Bentley) and 48747 (Munger), about 32 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Gladwin County (18 miles), Midland County (18 miles), Saginaw County (22 miles) and Arenac County (27 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Bay County begin with 487 (6 codes) and 486 (5 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 487 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Alcona County, Arenac County and Huron County, and has 54 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 10 of the 11 codes. Together they come to about 418 square miles of land (1,083 sq km). The largest is 48650 (Pinconning) with 95.2 square miles, and the smallest is 48710 (University Center) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 35.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Water adds about 30.2 square miles, roughly 7% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Bay County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 48707 (Bay City). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Bay County ranks 36th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 11 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Bay County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Bay City, MI 48706 (one of 3 codes for Bay City; the street decides which)
  • Auburn, MI 48611
  • Bentley, MI 48613

Bay City is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 3 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.

Questions about Bay County ZIP codes

What is the ZIP code for Bay City, MI?

Bay City has 3 ZIP codes in Bay County: 48706, 48707, 48708. Which one applies depends on the street address.

Do all ZIP codes in Bay County start with the same digits?

No. They use 2 different three-digit beginnings; the most common is 487, on 6 of the 11 codes.

Which ZIP code in Bay County covers the most land?

48650 (Pinconning), at about 95.2 square miles in the Census Bureau's 2024 figures.
