Where Allegan County is

The ZIP codes of Allegan County are found in the south of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 32 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 49080 (Plainwell) and 49406 (Douglas), about 33 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Van Buren County (23 miles), Ottawa County (25 miles), Barry County (28 miles) and Kalamazoo County (29 miles).

The first three digits

The codes of Allegan County begin with 493 (7 codes), 494 (6 codes) and 490 (4 codes). The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 493 is shared with 8 other counties, such as Barry County, Isabella County and Kent County, and has 49 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 14 of the 17 codes. Together they come to about 734 square miles of land (1,902 sq km). The largest is 49010 (Allegan) with 157 square miles, and the smallest is 49335 (Moline) with 0.4. The middle-sized code here covers 49.8 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Allegan County; a code can reach across its border. 3 codes have no mapped area: 49311 (Bradley), 49314 (Burnips) and 49416 (Glenn). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Allegan County ranks 18th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 17 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Allegan County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:
