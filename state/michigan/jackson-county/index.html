Where Jackson County is

On the map, the ZIP codes of Jackson County fall in the south-east of Michigan. Its codes spread over about 20 miles from north to south and 27 miles from east to west. The two codes farthest apart are 49263 (Norvell) and 49284 (Springport), about 31 miles from each other in a straight line. The nearest neighbours on the ZIP code map, measured between the middle points of their codes, are Hillsdale County (23 miles), Lenawee County (29 miles), Ingham County (32 miles) and Washtenaw County (33 miles).

The first three digits

Every ZIP code of Jackson County starts with 492. The first three digits of a ZIP code name the mail sorting area it belongs to. The prefix 492 is shared with 6 other counties, such as Branch County, Calhoun County and Hillsdale County, and has 62 codes in all.

How much ground the codes cover

The Census Bureau draws an area for 18 of the 19 codes. Together they come to about 691 square miles of land (1,790 sq km). The largest is 49201 (Jackson) with 155 square miles, and the smallest is 49263 (Norvell) with 0.04. The middle-sized code here covers 30.6 square miles; the middle value for all of Michigan is 37.0. Water adds about 24.1 square miles, roughly 3% of the mapped area. These areas follow ZIP code delivery routes, so their total is not the official size of Jackson County; a code can reach across its border. One code has no mapped area: 49204 (Jackson). Codes like these are normally for post office boxes or one large building.

By number of ZIP codes, Jackson County ranks 12th among the 83 counties and county-level areas of Michigan (equal counts share a rank), with 19 of the 1,159 codes in the state list.

Writing an address in Jackson County

The name of the county is not written in a United States address. The last line is the postal city, the letters MI and the ZIP code:

  • Jackson, MI 49201 (one of 4 codes for Jackson; the street decides which)
  • Brooklyn, MI 49230
  • Clarklake, MI 49234

Jackson is also the name of a place with a ZIP code in 19 other states, which is why the two state letters and the code matter more than the town name.
