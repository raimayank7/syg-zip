Contact

Found a mistake, a page that does not work, or have an idea for ZIP Codes? We read every message.

The message goes to us by email through FormSubmit, a form delivery service. If sending fails, use the form on the main ShareYourGroup website.

What to put in your message

  1. The address of the page. Copy it from the top of your browser, for example https://zip.shareyourgroup.com/... It tells us at once which site and which page you mean.
  2. What is wrong, or what you would like. One or two plain sentences are enough. If a fact is wrong, tell us where you saw the right one, so we can check it.
  3. What you were using. For a page that looks broken, the kind of phone or computer and the browser help us see the same thing.

Good reasons to write

  • A fact, figure, name, code or spelling that is wrong or out of date.
  • A button, search box or tool that does not work, or a page that does not fit your screen.
  • Something missing that you came here to find.
  • Text in Hindi, Punjabi or another language that reads wrongly.
  • You own something shown here and want it credited, corrected or taken down.
  • An advertisement that is unsuitable or covers the page.

What we cannot do

This is an independent information site. We are not a government office, a bank, a school, an airline or any organisation named on these pages, and we hold no records about you. We cannot look into your own application, account, payment, result or complaint with them. For that, please contact the organisation itself through its official website or office.

Your details

Give only what is needed to answer you, such as a name and an email address. What you send is used to deal with your message and for nothing else. Please do not send passwords, card or bank details, identity numbers or anything else private. More on this is in the privacy policy.
